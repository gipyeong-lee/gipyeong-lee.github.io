Understand the mathematical and security assumptions of NIST Post-Quantum standards, identify and classify cryptographic assets in a fixed local test system, experimentally evaluate ML-KEM and ML-DSA, and establish a priority-based migration roadmap.

Learners will be able to understand quantum threat models and utilize practical cryptographic asset identification tools and OQS-based test frameworks to author a report evaluating cryptographic agility in operational environments.
