Why (2026-09-18, AdSense Policy Center "Consent requirement: narrow coverage" — "TC string missing from some EEA/UK/CH ad requests"): with only the AdSense-served Google CMP, adsbygoogle.js fired the first ad requests BEFORE it loaded fundingchoicesmessages.
