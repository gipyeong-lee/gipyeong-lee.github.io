Imagine this: You are struggling with what to have for dinner tonight and ask ChatGPT, "Can you give me a simple recipe for Mexican food?" After the AI provides a heartfelt recipe, a suggestion pops up at the bottom of the chat window: "Get a discount coupon for a taco meal kit that goes perfectly with this dish."

ChatGPT, the conversational AI we use every day, is facing a new change. OpenAI is significantly expanding its advertising business within ChatGPT, evolving from a simple information retrieval tool into a new type of advertising platform.

### Why Does This Matter?

Until now, ChatGPT has felt like an "ad-free zone." However, ads are now entering our everyday conversational environment. This means the context of the conversations users have with AI can be leveraged as highly valuable information for companies.

What is particularly noteworthy is that companies are experimenting with "Sponsored Agents" that allow users to talk directly to an AI, moving beyond simple banner ads. It is similar to having a consultation with a smart store clerk who greets you when you enter a shop. Getting recommendations for relevant products or services while obtaining the information you need will soon become a more routine experience.

### Understanding It Simply: What Are Sponsored Agents?

In simple terms, you can think of a "Sponsored Agent" as an AI professional consultant provided by a specific brand.

To put it in perspective: if traditional internet ads were like "flyers" you happened to encounter while walking down the street, Sponsored Agents are "brand-specialized guides" that possess knowledge related to the information you are seeking and can carry on a conversation. They can grasp the context of the user's questions, provide information from the brand that fits the situation, and engage in a dialogue. [Source: OpenAI ChatGPT Ads: New Sponsored Agents for Brands](https://theaicronicle.com/en/news/companies/openai-chatgpt-ads-expansion-sponsored-agents)

OpenAI is designing these ads so they do not infringe upon the "answers" the chatbot writes itself. [Source: OpenAI expands ChatGPT ad business with Brazil next in line](https://valorinternational.globo.com/business/news/2026/06/24/openai-expands-chatgpt-ad-business-with-brazil-next-in-line.ghtml) In other words, the AI’s responses remain the AI's, while ads are offered naturally within the flow of the conversation.

### Current Status: How Widespread Is It?

OpenAI's advertising strategy is expanding very rapidly. When initial tests were conducted with a small number of companies in the US earlier this year, there was a high barrier to entry, with costs ranging from at least $200,000 to $250,000. [Source: OpenAI Expands Access to ChatGPT Ads](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/openai-expands-access-chatgpt-ads-robert-j-ricci-pysce)

However, the situation has changed. ChatGPT ads are now available in over 40 countries worldwide. [Source: OpenAI Expands ChatGPT Ads Self-Service Access to India and Europe](https://www.unite.ai/openai-expands-chatgpt-ads-self-service-access-to-india-and-europe/) Recently, the service scope has broadened to 31 European markets, as well as South Korea, Japan, and India. [Source: OpenAI expands ChatGPT ads to 31 European markets...](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/openai-expands-chatgpt-ads-to-31-european-markets-heres-where-india-stands-108257.htm)

An "Ads Manager" dashboard is also provided for businesses. Marketers can use this to create campaigns in real-time, track performance, and execute budgets efficiently. [Source: OpenAI expands ChatGPT ads and tests dedicated Ads Manager](https://en.walaw.press/articles/openai_expands_chatgpt_ads_and_tests_dedicated_ads_manager/GQSFSGLWPPRG) Regarding the critical issue of data privacy, OpenAI has clearly stated that "user conversation data is not shared with advertisers." [Source: OpenAI expands ChatGPT ad business with Brazil next in line](https://valorinternational.globo.com/business/news/2026/06/24/openai-expands-chatgpt-ad-business-with-brazil-next-in-line.ghtml)

### What Will Happen in the Future?

In the future, you will receive more personalized recommendations based on what you ask the AI. OpenAI is providing advertisers with more sophisticated targeting tools and creative AI campaign creation tools. [Source: OpenAI Tests Sponsored Agents and Rolls Out AI Tools for ChatGPT Ads](https://www.unite.ai/openai-tests-sponsored-agents-and-rolls-out-ai-tools-for-chatgpt-ads/)

The part readers should be most wary of is the "boundary between ads and information." Having the discernment to clearly distinguish whether content recommended by the AI is pure information or a recommendation influenced by an advertiser's intent will become increasingly important. OpenAI also says they will clearly label such ads as "sponsored." [Source: OpenAI expands ChatGPT ad business with Brazil next in line](https://valorinternational.globo.com/business/news/2026/06/24/openai-expands-chatgpt-ad-business-with-brazil-next-in-line.ghtml)

### MindTickleBytes AI Reporter’s Perspective

ChatGPT is transforming from a simple answer generator into a massive marketing platform. It is a double-edged sword that could become a convenient, personalized recommendation tool for users, or conversely, an annoying distraction. Ultimately, the key to success will be how naturally and valuably the technology integrates advertising.
