Imagine a challenge that has plagued the world's brightest mathematicians for the past 80 years. If you solve it, you could win a $1 million prize. One day, a common AI model presents a certificate, claiming, "I have solved this." Did AI really solve in an instant what human mathematicians have pondered for decades?

Recently, OpenAI announced that an unreleased internal AI model of theirs has solved the "Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness" problem, one of the seven Millennium Prize Problems in mathematics [[Reference 13](https://officechai.com/ai/openai-shares-solution-to-navier-stokes-problem-created-by-a-model-significantly-more-capable-than-astra/)]. However, the global mathematical community is responding with suspicion and concern rather than surprise.

## Why Is This Important?

The "Navier–Stokes equations" are a set of laws that describe the flow of fluids (substances that can flow, like water or air). They are core formulas essential for calculating how airplanes fly or how powerful typhoons move. Surprisingly, it has never been mathematically proven whether solutions to these equations always exist and are smooth [[Reference 13](https://officechai.com/ai/openai-shares-solution-to-navier-stokes-problem-created-by-a-model-significantly-more-capable-than-astra/)].

If AI truly achieved this proof, it would signify more than just a mathematical milestone; it would mean that AI could completely change the way humanity understands complex natural phenomena. It could open new horizons for science. However, the discovery is currently mired in issues regarding research credit and academic ethics [[Reference 7](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/openai-model-tackles-80-year-old-navier-stokes-problem-but-has-it-really-been-solved-110108.htm), [Reference 9](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/has-openai-solved-one-of-math-s-toughest-problems/story)].

## An Easy Explanation

Think of it this way: a mathematical proof is like assembling a massive, "perfect puzzle." Mathematicians have been piecing together tens of thousands of puzzle pieces one by one for decades. OpenAI's AI model is as if it found the final piece of the puzzle in one go and brought back the completed picture [[Reference 9](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/has-openai-solved-one-of-math-s-toughest-problems/story)].

However, the mathematical community does not simply accept these results. This is because a rigorous verification process—checking if the AI simply "looked at the answer key" or if there are any logical errors—is absolutely necessary. In other words, finding a puzzle piece is one thing, but proving that the picture is actually correct is another dimension entirely. Mathematicians emphasize that for this result to be recognized as an official answer, it must pass human, meticulous, and strict verification [[Reference 7](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/openai-model-tackles-80-year-old-navier-stokes-problem-but-has-it-really-been-solved-110108.htm)].

## Current Situation

The current situation reads like a drama. Beyond the excitement of a mathematical mystery being solved, there have been a series of whistleblowing incidents and exposés. Tristan Buckmaster, a mathematician at New York University (NYU), claimed that OpenAI exerted undue pressure on him regarding research credit [[Reference 4](https://genztech.blog/p/buckmaster-openai-navier-stokes-dispute/), [Reference 14](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/openai-fought-dirty-on-career-making-math-problem-says-nyu-mathematician/)].

There are even reports that OpenAI suggested to Professor Buckmaster that he "publish the paper without the name of co-researcher Alpöge," sparking significant public outcry within academia [[Reference 6](https://www.wired.com/story/openai-navier-stokes-math-discovery-academics/)]. Many experts, including mathematician and philosopher Dr. Samuel Allen Alexander, are closely investigating these allegations and monitoring the situation [[Reference 5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VaJlOhX8Ss4)].

## How Far Have We Come, and Where Are We Going?

Moving forward, this issue will likely unfold in two major directions. The first is determining the mathematical truth. Human mathematicians will meticulously determine whether the proof provided by AI has the logical completeness required to qualify for the $1 million prize [[Reference 9](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/has-openai-solved-one-of-math-s-toughest-problems/story), [Reference 14](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/openai-fought-dirty-on-career-making-math-problem-says-nyu-mathematician/)].

The second is the issue of research ethics for AI companies. Whether OpenAI's actions will harmonize with or conflict with the long-standing customs of academia will be a key point to watch. Regardless of how advanced technology becomes, the value of scientific discovery can change depending on how that technology is handled. We will soon know for sure whether this mathematical discovery was a true scientific breakthrough or merely overblown marketing.
