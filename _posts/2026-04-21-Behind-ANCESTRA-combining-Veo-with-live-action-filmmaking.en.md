When we watch a movie at the theater, there are times when we are captivated by breathtaking scenes and wonder, "How on earth did they shoot that?" This is especially true when encountering the mysterious expanse of outer space or surreal landscapes that look like they're from a dream. In the past, creating just a few seconds of such scenes required hundreds of graphic designers working through the night for months or building impossibly large physical sets. However, the formula for filmmaking as we know it is fundamentally changing.

At the peak of this transformation stands **'ANCESTRA'**. This film is not just an experimental work that stitches together curious video fragments created by artificial intelligence (AI). It is a work where 'live-action filming,' capturing the delicate breath of real actors, and the fantastical visual effects created by Google's most advanced video AI, 'Veo,' are perfectly fused like a single organism [Behind “ANCESTRA”: combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/ancestra-behind-the-scenes/).

Until now, the 'AI videos' we encountered were mostly short, fragmented clips created by typing a few lines of commands at a computer. This work made its grand debut on the massive stage of the **Tribeca Festival**, one of the world's most prestigious film festivals, garnering global attention [Behind "ANCESTRA": combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://aipulselab.tech/news/behind-ancestra-combining-veo-with-live-action-filmmaking-52e6b9).

Much like how the film industry transitioned from the era of film cameras to embrace the new tool of digital cameras, a major turning point has been established in the history of filmmaking.

Furthermore, this project offers a very meaningful answer to the public concern that "AI will take away the jobs of human artists." Surprisingly, to complete this single film, **more than 200 experts from various fields** put their heads together and joined forces [Behind “ANCESTRA:” combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://aihub.hkuspace.hku.hk/2025/06/13/behind-ancestra-combining-veo-with-live-action-filmmaking/). ## Easy to Understand: The 'Fantastic Blending' of AI and Live-Action

The film production team utilized **Veo**, Google's state-of-the-art video generation model. Veo is an AI that can instantly create high-definition video that looks like actual footage based on text commands [Google Veo AI Powers Cinematic Innovation in ANCESTRA](https://www.imaginepro.ai/blog/2025/6/behind-ancestra-combining-veo-with-live-action-filmmaking-google-blog). Rather than just generating video, the team employed innovative technologies to seamlessly merge live-action footage performed by real actors with virtual images created by AI, as if they were a single photograph.

Let's compare this complex process to familiar scenes from our daily lives.

### 1. Precise Motion Matching: The Ultimate Dance Partner
Imagine this: you've captured the elegant dance moves of a real actor on camera, and you want a mysterious galactic aurora to surround the actor and move as one with their body. In the past, numerous technicians had to manually edit every single frame. However, Google DeepMind developed a magical feature called **'Precise Motion Matching'** [Behind “ANCESTRA”: combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/ancestra-behind-the-scenes/).

This is like the AI becoming the ultimate dance partner that reads the actor's movements down to the slightest tremor in real-time and breathes in perfect sync with that rhythm. Thanks to this, the actual footage and the AI-generated visual effects could achieve perfect harmony without the slightest misalignment.

### 2. Personalization: A 'Dedicated Brush' Created Just for Me
Every movie has its own unique color, texture, and atmosphere according to the director's intention. To consistently maintain the specific dreamlike atmosphere of 'ANCESTRA,' the production team deeply trained the AI on the film's artistic style beforehand. This is technically referred to as **'Personalization'** technology [Behind “ANCESTRA”: combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/ancestra-behind-the-scenes/).

In simple terms, instead of just asking a painter to "draw any nice picture," it's like giving the AI a dedicated brush and palette, saying, "Draw this using only these specific colors and this unique texture that fits the concept of this work." Thanks to this process, the AI-generated images could blend naturally into the aesthetic flow of the entire film without standing out.

Director Eliza McNitt expressed this wondrous experience as follows: **"Veo is another lens that allows me to imagine the surrounding universe that I cannot see with my own eyes."** [Google Veo AI Powers Cinematic Innovation in ANCESTRA](https://www.imaginepro.ai/blog/2025/6/behind-ancestra-combining-veo-with-live-action-filmmaking-google-blog). While a standard camera lens captures the reality before our eyes, the AI lens has become a tool that prints the abstract imagination residing deep within the director's mind onto a clear screen.
