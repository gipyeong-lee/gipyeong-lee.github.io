Imagine you hear that an innovative tech company you've been watching for a long time is finally going public. The company has achieved an astonishing performance, with revenue growing by 1,300% in just one year. But when you look under the hood, you discover that while it is raking in astronomical amounts of money, it is simultaneously posting losses of unimaginable scale. How should investors view this company's future?

Recently, AI industry giant Anthropic has officially confirmed its plans for a Nasdaq listing, drawing intense market attention. However, behind the flashy growth metrics, cold warning sirens are sounding from financial experts.

## Why Does This Matter?

Anthropic's IPO is more than just a company entering the stock market. It is a symbolic event that reflects both the bubble controversy and the investment fever currently rampant in the AI market. Financial research firms like [New Constructs](https://www.newconstructs.com/anthropic-is-the-most-ridiculous-ipo-of-2026/) have labeled this IPO 'an unprecedented test of investor gullibility,' warning that it could pose significant risks to the broader financial market [Source 8, Source 11].

Some analysts argue that as AI companies like Anthropic monopolize IPO market attention, other healthy software firms are being left in an 'oxygen-deprived' state, unable to even secure listing opportunities [Source 5]. This suggests that capital is being excessively funneled into the specific domain of AI technology development, beyond just a stock market imbalance.

## Understanding the Context

Anthropic’s current state can be easily compared to a **'giant monster that eats endlessly but grows just as quickly.'**

Developing an AI model is similar to the process of building and running a massive library. As the library grows, more books (data) must be purchased, and the costs for electricity and management (computing power and labor) to run it snowball. While Anthropic is selling a massive amount of 'revenue' books, the actual operating costs of the library are far higher, leading to massive losses.

In fact, Anthropic recorded a phenomenal revenue of $16.3 billion in the first half of 2026 [Source 7, Source 10]. This is a 1,300% growth compared to the same period last year [Source 2, Source 3]. However, the net loss incurred during the same period reached $80.7 billion [Source 10]. While revenue grew exponentially, the cost structure rose even more steeply.

Investors hope this 'library' will eventually become a major attraction that generates massive profits, but critical experts raise strong doubts about whether this business model is sustainable [Source 4, Source 16].

## Current Situation

On October 26, Anthropic filed its registration statement (S-1) with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) for its IPO [Source 3, Source 10]. The ticker symbol 'ANT' has been confirmed for the Nasdaq.

Market assessments are sharply divided. Optimists focus on the phenomenal revenue growth rate, while pessimists point to the following concerns [Source 16]:

* **Massive Financial Losses**: The scale of losses, which far exceeds revenue, acts as a long-term financial burden for investors [Source 10, Source 16].
* **Unstable Structure**: It is evaluated as lacking stable profit-generating capabilities due to a high dependence on revenue from specific sectors [Source 16].
* **Weak Moat**: There is criticism that its technological edge is not solid compared to competitors, making it vulnerable to being caught at any time [Source 16].

While some optimistic forecasts suggest a market capitalization ranging from $144 billion to $2 trillion, experts present the harsh prerequisite that the company would need to achieve double the profits of Nvidia by 2035 to justify such valuations [Source 14, Source 15].

## What Happens Next?

Anthropic's IPO will serve as a testing ground for how future AI companies will convince investors to fund them. If Anthropic proves it can maintain stable growth after going public, investment in AI companies will likely accelerate further. Conversely, if the company’s valuation fails to overcome the market's bubble controversy, the possibility of a significant shock to the entire AI investment market cannot be ruled out [Source 11]. Investors must coolly watch how efficiently this company can reduce its losses and whether it can prove the market's claimed astronomical future value with actual results.

## AI Perspective

Anthropic's IPO is a point where technological wonder meets the cold reality of finance. The world is watching whether an era where astronomical valuations are continuously justified simply because AI technology is superior will persist, or if it will collide with the wall of 'profitability.'
