Imagine this: You are sitting comfortably on your sofa, looking at a virtual space on your smartphone. You say to the screen, "Make it rain here," and immediately, the sky clouds over and you begin to hear the sound of rain. When you say, "Add more people," new characters appear and react to your conversation. It’s not just watching a pre-determined video; it’s a world where your words become reality.

The technology turning this dream-like story into reality is "Genie 4," a next-generation world model (an AI model that implements virtual worlds by learning the world's physical laws and ways of interaction) announced by Google DeepMind.

## Why is this important?

Until now, the AI video generation technologies we have encountered mainly focused on creating pre-determined results. They were at the level of "creating" a pretty video according to the text entered by the user. However, Genie 4 has gone one step further. This technology goes beyond AI simply "drawing" a screen; it makes the world inside the screen "understand" its own rules and "interact" with us in real-time [Source 1, Source 8].

Simply put, if we have been mere spectators our whole lives, a door has now opened for us to become "creators" who craft stories together with AI. In various fields such as game development, film directing, and education, an era is coming where the worlds we imagine will instantly unfold into digital environments.

## Easy Understanding: What is Genie 4?

To easily understand Genie 4, let’s compare it to a **"smart film director."**

If existing AI was an artist with excellent painting skills, Genie 4 is a director who instantly adjusts everything on set, from camera work and lighting to background music and actors' performances. Genie 4 is built on Google's latest Gemini model, allowing it to grasp the physical rules of how the world works and the natural movements of people much more precisely than before [Source 2, Source 6, Source 9].

To put it more concretely, Genie 4 is like a massive "virtual playground." When you give a command in natural language, this model instantly changes the scene on the screen and generates ambient sounds or dialogue that fit the situation [Source 1, Source 8]. For example, in a scene where someone throws an apple, it naturally implements the physical movement of the apple falling in a curve through the air. It can even minutely express the reaction of a ball bouncing off a wall or the texture of the ground changing on a rainy day [Source 6, Source 8].

## Current Status: How far have we come?

Currently, Genie 4 is not a service available for everyone to use immediately. Google DeepMind has released this technology in the form of a "Research Preview" (a trial version released limitedly before an official launch) so that verified partners and researchers can use it first [Source 3].

The main functions of Genie 4 that we have confirmed today are as follows:

*   **Real-time Steering:** You can change camera angles, switch weather, or introduce new characters with natural language commands [Source 1, Source 9].
*   **Native Audio Generation:** It instantly generates ambient sounds, background music, and character dialogue tailored to the scene, increasing immersion [Source 1, Source 8].
*   **Physical Laws and Movement:** It implements much more realistic application of physical laws (gravity, collisions, etc.) and natural human movement compared to previous generation models [Source 6, Source 8].

Although it is still being tested in a limited environment, this will serve as an important milestone showing how deeply AI understands the mechanics of the world we live in.

## What will happen in the future?

What will happen if world models like Genie 4 develop further in the future? Experts predict that an era will come where AI predicts the world and actively acts within it [Source 10].

In the immediate future, an environment will be created where we can play and enjoy the content we imagine in real-time, just like a high-spec game. In the distant future, AI will go beyond simply creating digital worlds and integrate more deeply into simulating or researching problems in the real world. The real-time audio and physical law generation technology shown by Genie 4 is also a signal that we are ready to change from just "watching" AI to "living and interacting" with it within the world.
