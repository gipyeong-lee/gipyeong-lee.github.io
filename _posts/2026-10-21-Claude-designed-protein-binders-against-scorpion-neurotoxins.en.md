Imagine you're stung by a scorpion in a distant country, and the antivenom you need is unavailable or too expensive to obtain locally. In reality, about 1.2 million people around the world are stung by scorpions every year, leading to over 3,250 deaths. To solve this tragic problem, which mainly occurs in areas with low medical accessibility, Artificial Intelligence (AI) has emerged as a new savior.

Recently, Anthropic announced that it used its AI model, Claude, to design new proteins capable of neutralizing lethal scorpion neurotoxins. [Source 1](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-scorpion-toxin-binder-design), [Source 16](https://www.blockchain.news/ainews/claude-designs-protein-binders-for-scorpion-neurotoxins)

## Why is this important?

Currently, the development and manufacturing process for scorpion antivenoms is extremely complex and costly. They are particularly difficult to obtain in regions with fragile medical infrastructure. This research demonstrated that AI could dramatically accelerate the "protein design" process, which is the first step in therapeutic development. [Source 12](https://aidailypost.com/news/claude-science-designs-scorpion-venom-protein), [Source 16](https://www.blockchain.news/ainews/claude-designs-protein-binders-for-scorpion-neurotoxins) In other words, a path has been opened to develop cheaper and more accessible antivenoms.

## Easy to Understand: Protein design is making a "custom key"

Protein design is easy to understand as the process of making a "key" that attaches precisely to a specific disease or toxin. If the toxin is a "lock," you are creating a protein molecule shaped exactly right for that keyhole to paralyze the toxin's function.

To use an analogy, just as we use filters in photo apps to screen out only specific colors, AI finds the most efficient combination of protein structures that stick (bind) only to the toxin among countless combinations. This is called designing "protein binders" (proteins designed to bind to a specific target). In this study, Claude targeted five different scorpion toxins and designed custom binding proteins for each. [Source 2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Anthropic/scorpion-toxin-binder-design), [Source 12](https://aidailypost.com/news/claude-science-designs-scorpion-venom-protein), [Source 15](https://claudecertified.com/blog/claude-protein-binders-scorpion-toxin-2026-10-20)

## Current Situation

Anthropic researchers tasked Claude with designing binders for 15 targets in total, and it successfully found binding agents for 14 of them. External laboratory validation confirmed that 17 of the designed proteins met the criteria for actual toxin binding. [Source 3](https://www.adaptyvbio.com/blog/anthropic-1), [Source 13](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/claude-scorpion-toxin-protein-binders-anthropic-2026), [Source 17](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20261021-claude-designed-protein-against-scorpion-neurotoxins)

The success rate is particularly remarkable. While the traditional method performed by human researchers usually yields a 10-15% success rate, the proteins designed by Claude recorded a much higher efficiency of 22-35%. This means that AI can become a powerful assistant in the field of protein biotechnology. [Source 5](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/anthropic-says-claude-designed-binders-against-14-of-15-protein-targets-beating), [Source 9](https://aiweekly.co/node/10348)

## What happens next?

Anthropic went beyond simply suggesting the possibility by releasing relevant protocols so that researchers can utilize them directly. [Source 14](https://blockchain.news/news/anthropic-claude-scorpion-neurotoxin-binders), [Source 7](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/deniz-kavi_agents-now-do-de-novo-protein-design-but-activity-7496581896878059520-OXiw) Of course, this does not mean that an antivenom ready to be used in hospitals has been completed, but it suggests that the AI era, which speeds up new drug development, has begun in earnest. AI will play a key role in opening a new chapter in treating incurable diseases by conquering protein structures that cause various illnesses, not just scorpion toxins.
