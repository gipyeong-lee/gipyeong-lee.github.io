Imagine this: in the middle of a deep, transparent blue ocean, a dolphin leisurely approaches you. It then lets out its characteristic clear cry, warmly greeting you with something like, "Hello! The current feels great today!" While it sounds like a scene from a science fiction movie, artificial intelligence (AI) technology is now bringing this magical imagination to the threshold of reality.

Today, we meet a very special AI announced by Google: **'DolphinGemma.'** This AI analyzes the mysterious cries of dolphins in the ocean to map out the hidden 'language map' within them. [DolphinGemma: How Google AI is helping decode dolphin communication](https://blog.google/technology/ai/dolphingemma/)

## Why is this important?

We have known for a long time that dolphins possess high intelligence and live in complex, tight-knit social groups. We have long suspected that their 'clicks' and smooth 'whistles' are not just noise, but important messages meant for each other.

However, human senses and brains alone were insufficient to perfectly analyze the vast patterns of sound pouring out incessantly from beneath the waves. [SETI Tech On Earth: DolphinGemma: How Google AI Is Helping Decode ...](https://astrobiology.com/2025/04/seti-tech-on-earth-dolphingemma-how-google-ai-is-helping-decode-dolphin-communication.html)

**To use an analogy,** it was like being in a library full of books from an unknown ancient civilization and having to read tens of thousands of them without a dictionary. DolphinGemma has taken on the role of the 'smartest linguist' to decode this massive cipher. If we can properly understand the structure of dolphin communication, humanity will take its first step toward true communion with a non-human intelligent life form for the first time in Earth's history. [Google Launches DolphinGemma AI to Decode and Generate Dolphin Language](https://theaitrack.com/dolphingemma-ai-decodes-dolphin-communication/)

## Easy Understanding: How does DolphinGemma work?

DolphinGemma is a **Large Language Model (LLM)** born from 'Gemma,' Google's latest open AI model. Just as a typical LLM learns human language by reading countless books and internet sentences, this AI focused its studies exclusively on the 'sounds of dolphins' heard from the ocean. [DolphinGemma: How Google AI is helping decode dolphin communication | Research](https://research.gatech.edu/dolphingemma-how-google-ai-helping-decode-dolphin-communication)

Let's break down this fascinating process with three simple analogies.

**1. It's like a smartphone's 'Auto-complete' feature**
When we type "I am currently..." in a messenger, the AI suggests the rest of the sentence, like "going home" or "eating lunch," right? DolphinGemma does the same. It watches the flow of sounds made by dolphins and predicts what sound will come next. [Google Uses DolphinGemma AI to Decode Dolphin Communication](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/google-uses-dolphingemma-ai-to-decode-dolphin-communication/490753) If the AI can accurately guess the dolphin's next sound, it serves as powerful evidence that the AI has begun to understand the 'grammar' and 'rules' hidden within the dolphin language.

**2. It's like reading a massive diary written over 40 years**
Just as good textbooks are needed for studying, DolphinGemma learned from a vast amount of **bioacoustic** data recorded directly in the field by the 'Wild Dolphin Project (WDP)' over a long period of 40 years. [Google Launches DolphinGemma AI to Decode and Generate Dolphin Language](https://theaitrack.com/dolphingemma-ai-decodes-dolphin-communication/) By analyzing decades of sound data, it is grasping the context of what situations and emotions lead dolphins to make certain sounds.

**3. It's a task of putting together sound puzzle pieces one by one**
Dolphins use a combination of diverse sounds, including 'Clicks,' 'Whistles,' and 'Burst pulses.' [DolphinGemma: How Google AI is helping decode dolphin communication | Research](https://research.gatech.edu/dolphingemma-how-google-ai-helping-decode-dolphin-communication) **Simply put,** DolphinGemma is finding repeating rules and specific sequences among scattered sound puzzle pieces, closely analyzing how logically similar its structure is to human language. [DolphinGemma: How Google AI is helping decode dolphin communication](https://blog.google/technology/ai/dolphingemma/)

Beyond just listening and analyzing sounds, we have now come closer to the stage where AI can directly generate sound patterns similar to those of dolphins. This is expected to drastically increase the speed of research in the wild. ## What kind of future awaits us?

Of course, it's still difficult to start chatting with dolphins in real-time tomorrow. This is because DolphinGemma is currently in a crucial foundational stage of grasping the fundamental principles of "what rules and order they use to make sounds," rather than accurately translating "what they are saying." [Google Launches AI That Talks to Dolphins - Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/dolphingemma-google-ai-dolphins-2059955)

However, once this foundation is laid, we will witness an amazing sight. Scientists will use the structure of sounds discovered by DolphinGemma to reveal how similar dolphin communication systems are to human language, or if they have a unique worldview we never imagined. **Imagine this.** One day in the future, dolphins might warn us of dangers caused by marine pollution, or we might send a message in their language telling them to stay calm while we help an injured dolphin.
