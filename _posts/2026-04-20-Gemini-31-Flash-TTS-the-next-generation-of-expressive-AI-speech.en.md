Imagine this. Late at night, an artificial intelligence (AI) is reading a fairy tale to a child. But instead of just reading the text dryly, it lowers its voice and whispers urgently when a scary wolf appears, then changes back to a bright and cheerful singing voice when a cute rabbit comes out. Or, at a complex customer service center, an AI agent immediately senses the annoyance in my voice and responds in a calm, warm tone that sounds sincerely apologetic.

The **TTS (Text-to-Speech)** we've encountered so far has felt somewhat stiff and mechanical. While it could read sentences perfectly, it couldn't express the 'emotions' or 'atmosphere' contained within them. But now, that thick wall is crumbling. On April 15, 2026, Google DeepMind unveiled **'Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS,'** a next-generation AI model that allows you to conduct voices like a film director [Source 7](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/gemini-3-1-flash-tts-google-ai-voice-model-2026), [Source 10](https://datanorth.ai/news/google-gemini-3-1-flash-tts-release).

## Why is this important?

It's not just a matter of "the voice sounding a bit better." It means the very way we exchange information and communicate with machines in our daily lives could completely change.

1.  **The Beginning of Human-like Communication**: AI is no longer just a robot that unilaterally lists information, but is one step closer to being a 'friendly companion' that speaks with emotions appropriate to the situation [Source 11](https://siliconangle.com/2026/04/15/googles-gemini-3-1-flash-tts-offers-unparalleled-control-ai-voices/).
2.  **A Tool for Everyone to Become a Creator**: YouTubers and podcast producers can now create high-quality audio content instantly by giving detailed acting instructions to AI, without having to hire expensive recording equipment or professional voice actors [Source 1](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-1-flash-tts/).
3.  **Breaking Down Global Communication Barriers**: Supporting more than 70 languages is a huge advantage, as it means people all over the world can enjoy services using this natural voice [Source 12](https://the-decoder.com/google-ships-its-most-expressive-gemini-3-1-text-to-speech-model-yet-with-70-language-support/).

## Easy Understanding: Giving 'Acting Lessons' to an AI Voice

The most amazing thing about Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS is its **'Controllability.'** To use an **analogy**, it's similar to a seasoned film director giving acting lessons to a rookie actor.

If previous TTS technology was a passive method where you said "read this script" and just waited for the result, Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS allows the director to give detailed orders to the actor, such as **"This is a very sad scene, so please speak a little slower than usual and with a slightly trembling voice"** [Source 7](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/gemini-3-1-flash-tts-google-ai-voice-model-2026).

How is such magic possible? It's thanks to a core technology called **'Audio Tags'** [Source 6](https://aitoolly.com/ai-news/article/2026-04-16-google-deepmind-unveils-gemini-31-flash-tts-a-new-era-of-expressive-ai-speech-control).

*   **What are Audio Tags?**: Just as you might add salt or sugar to taste when cooking, these are a kind of 'secret signal' inserted between text to adjust the feel of the voice.
*   **What can be adjusted?**: Everything from vocal style to pace, emotional delivery, and tone can be set in very fine units [Source 10](https://datanorth.ai/news/google-gemini-3-1-flash-tts-release), [Source 13](https://www.c-sharpcorner.com/news/google-unveils-gemini-31-flashtts-the-next-generation-of-expressive-ai-speech).

**Simply put**, for the same sentence "Hello," if you add an 'energetic' tag, it becomes a high-energy greeting, and if you add a 'calm' tag, it becomes a polite greeting like a hotel concierge. Google has made it so that users can freely guide the style and intonation of audio using the language we normally use (natural language) [Source 2](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/speech-generation).

## Current Status: What can it do?

Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS is currently released in 'Public Preview,' allowing developers and companies worldwide to try it out first [Source 7](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/gemini-3-1-flash-tts-google-ai-voice-model-2026). Let's look at the key features again.

*   **Support for 70+ Languages**: Not only Korean but billions of people worldwide can experience this innovative technology in their native language [Source 15](https://algo-mania.com/en/blog/news/gemini-3-1-flash-tts-revolutionizes-artificial-intelligence-voice-synthesis/).
*   **Harmony of Diverse Voices**: In addition to solo narration, it supports 'multi-speaker' functions where multiple characters talk to each other, allowing for the creation of content like audio dramas [Source 10](https://datanorth.ai/news/google-gemini-3-1-flash-tts-release).
*   **Strict Safety Measures**: To prevent AI voices from being misused for voice phishing, all generated audio includes a **watermark** in an inaudible range that contains information saying 'This voice was created by AI' [Source 13](https://www.c-sharpcorner.com/news/google-unveils-gemini-31-flashtts-the-next-generation-of-expressive-ai-speech).
*   **Tools for Use**: You can experience this technology directly through Google AI Studio, Vertex AI, and the recently released video editing tool Google Vids [Source 4](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/gemini-31-flash-tts-our-latest-text-to-speech-model-available-tlsde), [Source 10](https://datanorth.ai/news/google-gemini-3-1-flash-tts-release).

## Future Outlook: The Era of 'Audible AI'

Experts see this model as a key to evolving the concept of 'AI assistants' to the next level.

It goes beyond simply finding the right answer to the questions we ask, opening a 'voice-first' era where AI can grasp acoustic nuances in conversation and respond naturally in real-time [Source 9](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models/gemini-3.1-flash-live-preview).

**Imagine for a moment.** When we talk to an AI with a tired and sad voice, the AI immediately detects the trembling in our voice and responds in the warmest tone of comfort in the world. Google is confident that this model will be the most powerful weapon for developers looking to create the next generation of AI voice apps [Source 16](https://onmine.io/gemini-3-1-flash-tts-the-next-generation-of-expressive-ai-speech/). The day when the voice of a robot, which felt only cold, becomes a real 'voice' that sincerely shares its heart with us is not far off.
