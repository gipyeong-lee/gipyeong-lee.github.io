Imagine this: You don't have to wonder whether a message from an unknown number is a scam. What if your smartphone sent you a friendly warning saying, "This conversation is suspicious!" before you even replied? It's like having a very smart security detective sitting next to your chat window at all times.

On October 14, 2026, Google DeepMind and the Singaporean government’s Government Technology Agency (GovTech) announced a strategic partnership to make this dream-like security technology a reality. Revealed at the "Singapore International Cyber Week 2026," this technology is emerging as a new hope for protecting the personal information and assets of users worldwide. [Source 14](https://deepmind.google/blog/strengthening-singapores-ai-future-a-new-national-partnership/), [Source 15](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/owen-larter-3340b224_google-deepmind-singapore-national-ai-activity-7462730290722045952-uWwz), [Source 16](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/), [Source 18](https://ground.news/article/govtech-and-google-deepmind-developing-real-time-detection-of-ai-scams-on-devices)

### Why is this important?

Scam crimes are disrupting the daily lives of modern people beyond mere financial loss. In Singapore, the scale of the damage is immense, with total losses from various scam cases exceeding 4 billion Singapore dollars since 2019. [Source 15](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/owen-larter-3340b224_google-deepmind-singapore-national-ai-activity-7462730290722045952-uWwz)

Most existing security technologies take an approach of sending messages to a server for analysis. However, this comes with concerns about privacy infringement or the risk of data leaks. The technology announced this time was designed with 'privacy' as the top priority so that users can use it with peace of mind. The biggest innovative point of this technology is that it ensures scam prevention while relieving users of the worry that AI companies or the government might peek at their private conversations. [Source 14](https://deepmind.google/blog/strengthening-singapores-ai-future-a-new-national-partnership/), [Source 17](https://cryptobriefing.com/deepmind-govtech-singapore-ai-scam-tool/)

### Easy to understand: A little detective inside your phone

The core of this technology is 'on-device' AI. Simply put, while common cloud-based AI searches for information in a 'large central library,' on-device AI carries all necessary information in your 'small pocket notebook' and checks it instantly.

It’s easier to compare it this way: It’s like putting a very fine sieve inside your smartphone. When words or patterns with malicious intent enter your messages, the 'sieve' filters them out immediately. Google DeepMind has utilized a lightweight classifier and a fine-tuned 'Gemma' AI model to compress a massive and complex dictionary to fit the smartphone's performance, embedding it directly within the device. [Source 17](https://cryptobriefing.com/deepmind-govtech-singapore-ai-scam-tool/), [Source 18](https://ground.news/article/govtech-and-google-deepmind-developing-real-time-detection-of-ai-scams-on-devices)

As soon as a user receives a message, this 'little detective (Gemma model)' reads the content very quickly inside the phone to check if it smells like a scam. You can rest easy because not a single character of your private conversation data travels over the internet to an external server. [Source 14](https://deepmind.google/blog/strengthening-singapores-ai-future-a-new-national-partnership/), [Source 15](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/owen-larter-3340b224_google-deepmind-singapore-national-ai-activity-7462730290722045952-uWwz)

### Current Status

Currently, this technology is being developed into a real-time scam detection model through a strong collaboration between Google DeepMind and the Singaporean government. [Source 13](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/), [Source 17](https://cryptobriefing.com/deepmind-govtech-singapore-ai-scam-tool/) To respond to evolving criminal tactics while protecting user privacy, they are focusing on maximizing the smartphone device's own computational power to catch the subtle linguistic manipulations used by scammers. [Source 15](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/owen-larter-3340b224_google-deepmind-singapore-national-ai-activity-7462730290722045952-uWwz), [Source 17](https://cryptobriefing.com/deepmind-govtech-singapore-ai-scam-tool/) Of course, since no technology is perfect, the work is at a stage where continuous improvements are being made while considering technical limitations.

### What lies ahead?

This announcement signals a change in security standards not just for Singapore, but for every smartphone user around the world. The paradigm will move from an 'era of sending data to a central location for analysis' to an 'era where my device protects me directly.'

We will soon be in an environment where our smartphones automatically block or warn us about the countless scam messages targeting us in our daily lives. As shown by this example from Google DeepMind, we can look forward to a future where, as technology advances, our privacy is protected more deeply and security becomes smarter. [Source 8](https://ground.news/article/govtech-and-google-deepmind-developing-real-time-detection-of-ai-scams-on-devices), [Source 16](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/)
