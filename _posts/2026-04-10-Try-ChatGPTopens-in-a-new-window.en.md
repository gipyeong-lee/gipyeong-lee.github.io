### Technical Background: From GPT-3 to GPT-4o, and the Paradigm Shift of 'Search'

The foundation of ChatGPT's remarkable performance lies in the history of model advancement. In May 2024, OpenAI reached a pinnacle in AI technology by announcing 'GPT-4o,' a true multimodal model that processes audio, vision, and text simultaneously in real-time. [Everything you need to know about ChatGPT](https://botpress.com/blog/chatgpt) This was more than just an improvement in performance; it was an event that completely changed the physical way humans and machines interact. Compared to its predecessor, GPT-3, GPT-4 is much more precisely optimized, and the version released in November 2023 gained the ability to analyze vast amounts of information at once through a significantly expanded context window.
