Imagine this: You turn on your screen this morning, ready to ask your AI assistant, Claude (a next-generation AI assistant developed by Anthropic), about that English homework you were working on yesterday. But instead of your usual screen, a message simply states, "Access blocked due to a violation of usage rules." If you are an adult, you are likely bewildered. Recently, an increasing number of users have reported experiencing this. Why on earth is this happening?

### Why is this important?

AI has become more than just a tool in our daily lives; it is a reliable assistant that performs information retrieval, learning, and task support. However, this powerful technology is not unconditionally open to everyone. In particular, Anthropic enforces a very strict age restriction policy for the Claude service. This is not just because users are "too young," but a necessary measure to protect minors from the potential risks associated with generative AI and to create an environment for responsible technology use. If you are an adult but get interrupted in your learning or work flow because you were misidentified as a minor, understanding the policy background will be a great help in knowing how to use AI moving forward.

### Easy to understand: How does AI know my age?

To put it simply, Claude is like an observant instructor. In the past, you could just enter your date of birth when signing up for a service, but now, Claude carefully examines the "context" of the conversations it shares with users.

1. **Conversation Analysis:** Claude uses an advanced classification system to detect specific expressions in conversations that suggest school homework, entrance exam concerns, or being a minor. Just as a photo app uses an AI filter to locate a person's face, Claude infers the user's age range through a context filter of the conversation.
2. **Smart Identification:** Anthropic continues to advance technology that analyzes the subject or tone of conversations. Thanks to this, even if you registered as an adult with a fake age during sign-up, your account will be immediately suspended if clues revealing you are under 18 are detected during use [Source: [2026 Edition] Is Claude Off-Limits for Children?](https://note.com/matsuzaka01/n/naa801134ddaa?hl=en).

Metaphorically, just as a "soldier is deployed to the field only after completing basic training," AI is putting up a shield, judging that there are areas that should be handled somewhat cautiously for minors [Source: [2026 Edition] Is Claude Off-Limits for Children?](https://note.com/matsuzaka01/n/naa801134ddaa?hl=en).

### Current situation: What if you are under 18?

Currently, Anthropic's official stance is very clear. The minimum age for using Claude is 18 [Source: [Parent Guide](https://www.aitoolsforkids.com/blog/how-old-to-use-claude-ai), [Is Claude Safe for Kids?](https://littleaimaster.com/ai-tools/claude)]. Because the service itself is not designed for minors, accounts confirmed to belong to minors are immediately deactivated [Source: [2026 Edition] Is Claude Off-Limits for Children?](https://note.com/matsuzaka01/n/naa801134ddaa?hl=en).

However, technology is not always 100% perfect. Occasionally, "unfair situations" occur where adult users are incorrectly classified as minors and have their accounts locked [Source: [Anthropic under scrutiny](https://www.digit.in/news/general/anthropic-under-scrutiny-as-claude-flags-users-as-minors-here-is-how-to-unlock-your-account.html)]. Fortunately, in such cases, Anthropic provides an opportunity to appeal. When your account is suspended, you can click the age verification link provided in the notification to certify your age and recover your account. Note that this verification link expires 30 days after being sent, so if you see the email, it is best to take action as quickly as possible [Source: [Anthropic flags adult Claude users as minors](https://www.medianama.com/2026/04/223-claude-users-accounts-suspended-flagged-minors/)].

### What will happen in the future?

Moving forward, AI services will continue to refine their age-verification technologies. Beyond simply asking for your age, we are likely to see an increase in attempts to verify "digital maturity" based on behavior. Users will now become aware that, when talking to AI, their casual information can serve as a metric for age determination.

What is important is that when we use AI, we should not just chase convenience but cultivate a "digital citizenship" that checks the policies and safety guides of each service. Claude's policy this time makes us think seriously about what the minimum promises are that AI must keep to settle in as a member of our society.
