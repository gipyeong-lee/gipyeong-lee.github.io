Imagine you are browsing the internet and find a truly amazing picture. Suddenly, a thought occurs: "Was this really drawn by a person, or was it created by artificial intelligence (AI)?" As AI technology has advanced rapidly, distinguishing between the real and the fake is becoming increasingly difficult. To solve this curiosity, Anthropic (the AI company behind Claude) has stepped up and released a tool.

## Why is this verification important?

A significant portion of the content we see and hear every day is now created with the help of AI. However, knowing which information was generated by AI and what was completed by a person is more important than you might think. It acts as a "digital compass," helping us make better judgments when encountering news materials, works of art, or educational content. Knowing the transparency of a source is the most reliable way to ensure we do not get lost in the digital sea.

## Easy to Understand: Digital 'Hallmarks'

When you generate an image file (.png, .jpg, .svg, etc.) using Claude, Claude leaves a tiny, invisible "digital tag" inside the file. This is called "Content Credential."

To use a simple analogy, it is similar to a pottery master carving a very small signature on the bottom of their work. It is not noticeable in everyday life, but when checked, you can clearly tell whose hands brought the pottery into existence.

This tag follows an international technical standard called 'C2PA.' [Source Title](https://claude.com/check-content) C2PA is an open industry standard already widely used by camera manufacturers and the latest image editing software. [Source Title](https://claude.com/check-files) It is a technology that creates a kind of 'digital family tree' by including an encrypted signature in the file's metadata (data containing information about the file), recording where the file came from.

The official 'Claude Content Checker' tool released by Anthropic acts as a reader that decodes this digital signature. [Source Title](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16266773-how-claude-marks-ai-generated-content)

## How can we verify this right now?

Currently, anyone can visit the [Claude Content Checker](https://claude.com/check-content) page provided by Anthropic and upload files for free to verify them. [Source Title](https://www.itechpost.com/articles/237212/20260902/anthropics-claude-content-checker-tool-now-availableheres-how-use-detector.htm)

The greatest advantage of this tool is that it can be used with "peace of mind." Because the tool runs directly within the user's browser, the files you upload are not transmitted to or stored on an external server. [Source Title](https://www.itechpost.com/articles/237212/20260902/anthropics-claude-content-checker-tool-now-availableheres-how-use-detector.htm) The file remains safely on your computer while only the inspection is performed.

However, there are points to keep in mind. This checker provides clear proof only for specific file formats (.png, .jpg, .svg) generated directly by Claude. [Source Title](https://claude.com/check-files) Also, you must remember that this digital tag can be erased in the process of editing the file or converting it to another path. [Source Title](https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/anthropics-content-checker-tool-is-here-with-one-big-catch/)

## How should we prepare for the future?

In the future, recording source information on digital content will become a matter-of-course cultural practice. Just as camera manufacturers already utilize this technology to protect the integrity of photos, various digital content creation tools beyond AI will competitively introduce these 'provenance verification' features in the future.

We must learn 'digital literacy'—not to unconditionally reject content made by AI, but to transparently verify and utilize its origins. Checking if there is a hidden digital tag when sharing or downloading files will become a very simple but powerful habit for finding the truth in the digital world.
