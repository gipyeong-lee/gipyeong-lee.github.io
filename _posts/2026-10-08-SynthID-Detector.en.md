## Why is this important?

AI-generated content on the internet is becoming more sophisticated by the day. It is now difficult even for experts to tell the difference. The spread of images containing misinformation or manipulated videos can cause confusion in our daily lives.

Simply put, if we believe and act on information without knowing it is fake, unexpected problems can arise. In this situation, verifying "where this content came from" goes beyond simple curiosity and becomes a safety device for finding reliable information. Google is introducing various verification features to ensure users can safely enjoy the digital environment; currently, the authentication features built into Google Search, the Gemini app, and Chrome process over 1 million requests daily. ## Understanding it simply: Digital Watermark

Does the term 'digital watermark' sound a bit difficult? Let's use a very simple analogy.

When we look at banknotes, we can check for hidden patterns by holding them up to the light, right? SynthID is similar. When an AI model creates a photo or audio, it embeds a very subtle 'trace' that is invisible to human eyes and ears but detectable by machines. This is what we professionally call a 'digital watermark'. [SynthID— Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/synthid/)

This is on a different level from the old method of stamping a large logo on an image. [ExplainingSynthID](https://ppc.land/explaining-synthid/) Existing logos were easily erased by cropping or editing the photo slightly, but invisible marks embedded directly into the file, like SynthID, do not disappear easily even if the photo is modified. [SynthID— Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/synthid/) 

It is like leaving a subtle mark on the texture of the paper itself, rather than using a stamp we use in daily life. Simply put, it is as if the AI has attached a tiny 'digital name tag' to the back of its work. The SynthID Detector acts as a 'detector' that finds and informs us of this name tag. [SynthIDDetector: Identify Content Created With Google's AI Tools](https://www.chromastudio.ai/synthid-detector)
