Imagine a scientist who analyzes thousands of cell data points day and night, searching for clues to their research by reading complex papers. That same scientist can now build a data processing pipeline (a series of automated processes for data analysis) that used to take days, finishing it in just a few minutes with the help of AI. It sounds like something out of a movie, but it is a change currently happening in many laboratories.

### Why does this matter?

Scientific research is like assembling a giant puzzle. The process of forming hypotheses, collecting data, and analyzing it to find meaning is extremely time-consuming. To use an analogy, it is similar to flipping through every single book in a massive library to find one specific line of information. What if AI could take over the repetitive and tedious tasks that humans previously had to perform manually?

Scientists gain the ability to spend more time on creative work, such as asking more important 'questions' and interpreting new phenomena. This means that the speed of solving problems directly connected to our lives, such as treating diseases or developing new materials, could accelerate dramatically [Reference 3](https://claude.com/product/claude-science).

### Easy to Understand: The AI Workbench, Claude Science

'Claude Science' is, in a nutshell, a professional **AI workbench for scientific research**. To put it simply, it is like having a very meticulous and smart colleague in the lab.

1. **An assistant for data analysis**: This AI performs the analysis process itself without the need for a human to manually connect complex pipelines, and it meticulously tracks every step [Reference 3](https://claude.com/product/claude-science). It is similar to a photo editing program that automatically applies various filters across multiple steps.
2. **Knowledge across all fields**: Claude Science supports almost all scientific fields, including biology, chemistry, and medicine, as well as computer science, engineering, physics, and even social sciences [Reference 4](https://claude.com/docs/claude-science/overview).
3. **Integration with academic evidence**: Recently, technology has been introduced that integrates AI's analysis capabilities with reliable scholarly literature, such as scientific papers. This allows AI to go beyond simply providing answers, enabling it to offer analysis results that strictly adhere to scientific standards [Reference 15](https://beesway.net/en/industry-first-wiley-launches-ai-gateway-for-scientific-discovery) [Reference 16](http://wisdomsuperman.com/en/industry-first-wiley-launches-ai-gateway-for-scientific-discovery).

### Current Situation: How are scientists using it?

According to a recent report released by Anthropic, an analysis of over 200,000 research sessions as of November 2026 revealed interesting insights [Reference 7](https://explainx.ai/blog/claude-scientists-research-sessions-207000-anthropic-report-2026).

The most common use cases for scientists were **data analysis and academic writing** [Reference 7](https://explainx.ai/blog/claude-scientists-research-sessions-207000-anthropic-report-2026). Conversely, AI utilization was relatively low in the research's core stages, such as 'idea generation' or 'data collection' [Reference 7](https://explainx.ai/blog/claude-scientists-research-sessions-207000-anthropic-report-2026). This shows that AI is currently achieving significant results in the role of a 'skilled assistant' that organizes and analyzes research findings.

Additionally, Anthropic is operating an 'AI for Science' program in partnership with scientific research institutions to produce high-level research outcomes. Here, they support scientists by strictly evaluating the value, impact, and technical feasibility of the research [Reference 6](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11199177-anthropic-s-ai-for-science-program).

### What will happen in the future?

Researchers are now entering a new research environment where they can combine Claude's analytical power with verified scientific literature data to perform everything from statistical analysis to complex code generation without any bottlenecks [Reference 15](https://beesway.net/en/industry-first-wiley-launches-ai-gateway-for-scientific-discovery).

Going forward, AI will move beyond being a simple analysis tool and become more deeply involved in the processes of forming hypotheses and designing experiments. As humanity takes on more complex and difficult problems, AI will become a reliable partner in that journey. We should keep a close watch on how AI will accelerate the pace of scientific discovery [Reference 2](https://www.anthropic.com/research/accelerating-scientific-research).

## MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Perspective

The meeting of AI and science is about much more than just 'finishing work faster.' The essence of scientific research is the process of inquiry itself—asking questions and finding the answers. If AI can now lighten the repetitive and tedious burden of that process, humanity will be able to focus more on deeper and more essential questions. AI is becoming not a replacement for the scientist, but the most intellectual companion on the journey toward scientific truth.
