Imagine this: You wake up in the morning and say to your AI, "Organize my tasks for today, create a report, and level up my game character in the remaining time." Suddenly, your computer screen begins to move on its own. It's as if an invisible person has taken hold of the mouse and is clicking away. Scenes like this are no longer just stories from a movie.

On September 3, 2026, OpenAI released 'GPT-6 Astra,' a new intelligent model poised to bring this world closer [GPT-6 Astra: A Complete Guide to OpenAI's Agentic Model](https://vc.ru/ai/3123553-gpt-6-astra-rukovodstvo-po-agentnoi-modeli-openai). This model doesn't stop at generating text; it possesses the ability to 'directly' operate a computer just like a person.

### Why does this matter?

The AI we have been using (like ChatGPT) acted more like a smart assistant that provided information. When we asked it to "make a table," it would only draw the table in 'text.' However, GPT-6 Astra is 'Actionable AI.' This model can open web browsers, execute Excel (spreadsheets), and even directly manipulate professional tools like CAD (Computer-Aided Design) programs and terminal windows [GPT-6 Astra: A Complete Guide to OpenAI's Agentic Model](https://vc.ru/ai/3123553-gpt-6-astra-rukovodstvo-po-agentnoi-modeli-openai).

This has the potential to change the very way we work. An era is approaching where we can entrust tedious and repetitive computer tasks to AI, allowing us to focus on more important creative problem-solving.

### In simple terms: AI with 'digital hands'

To use a simple analogy: if existing AI was a 'food critic' who knew recipes very well, GPT-6 Astra is a 'chef' who knows how to go into the kitchen, use a knife, and control the heat.

The process of a computer understanding words is similar to putting together a giant puzzle. If the Transformer (the AI architecture that identifies relationships between words in a sentence) learned how to understand those relationships, GPT-6 Astra has added new learning to this: 'how to look at a computer screen and issue commands.' It is a method of 'seeing' where the buttons are on the screen and 'manipulating' them with its 'hands'—knowing which keys to press to move a character in a game [GPT-6 Astra: A Complete Guide to OpenAI's Agentic Model](https://vc.ru/ai/3123553-gpt-6-astra-rukovodstvo-po-agentnoi-modeli-openai).

### How far have we come: AI diving into the gaming world

The capabilities of GPT-6 Astra are already being proven. In the 'Agents' Last Exam,' which evaluates the complex practical abilities of AI agents, GPT-6 Astra recorded a score of 59.3%, surpassing previous powerful models like GPT-5.6 Sol (53.6%) and Claude Opus 5 (55.5%) [GPT-6 Astra API - Try OpenAI GPT-6 on Kie AI](https://kie.ai/gpt-6-astra).

This ability has been clearly demonstrated in recent game-play tests. AI is learning on its own while playing games, and is active in various fields, such as implementing artistic robotic arm manipulation techniques [GPT-6 Astra Pushing the Limits! Robotic Arm, Games, and...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H1LAUftg4LM). However, along with technological advancement, important questions are also being raised.

Expert Emollick noted that GPT-6 Astra experienced two failures before succeeding in the game, raising a sharp question: "Failure is okay in a game, but can we afford to let AI experience two failures in a real business environment where real data and money are involved?" [In all seriousness, this is a startling achievement for GPT-6 Astra](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/emollick_in-all-seriousness-this-is-a-startling-achievement-activity-7509079714129563648-m2uC). This suggests that as AI technology advances, the 'issue of responsibility' we need to solve will become even more critical.

### What should we prepare for in the future?

GPT-6 Astra currently boasts incredible scores: 100% in ExploitBench (cybersecurity), 98% in FrontierMath (mathematics), and 99.9% in ARC-AGI-3 (intelligence evaluation) [GPT-6 Astra - AI Model: Try it for free on Felo AI](https://felo.ai/ru/tools/gpt-6-astra).

In the future, AI will manipulate our computer environments more precisely. We are entering an era where AI goes beyond waiting for commands, instead handling complex software itself to complete tasks on our behalf. We might need to prepare for a manager role where we no longer tell AI 'what to do' but simply present the 'goal we want to achieve.' Now that AI has evolved into an agent that can handle tools, we must possess the wisdom to manage and control the mistakes it might make.
