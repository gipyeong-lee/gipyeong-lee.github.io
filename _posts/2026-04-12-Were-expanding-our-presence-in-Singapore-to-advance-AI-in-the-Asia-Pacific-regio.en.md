Imagine this. You ask your favorite artificial intelligence (AI), "Lunch today is a bit 'geosigi', any spicy recommendations?" and the AI perfectly understands the subtle nuances of the uniquely Korean expression 'geosigi' and suggests a great restaurant. Or, in a complex market in Southeast Asia, the AI perfectly interprets the unique dialects and idioms used by local vendors.

Until now, the AI we've encountered has been somewhat biased toward English and Western cultures. However, AI has now begun to study the hearts and cultures of Asians in earnest. This is because **Google DeepMind**, considered the world's leading AI research lab, has established a new research base in Singapore, the heart of the Asia-Pacific region. [Google DeepMind opens Singapore research lab for Asia-Pacific AI. — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/were-expanding-our-presence-in-singapore-to-advance-AI-in-the-asia-pacific-region/)

## Why is this important?

This is more than just the opening of another research lab. It is a signal fire that will trigger massive waves of change in the economy and daily lives of the Asia-Pacific region.

First, the **economic impact** is enormous. Currently, about 70% of people in Southeast Asia already use generative AI tools (AI that creates text or images on its own) weekly, showing a high level of technology adoption. [Turning AI momentum in Southeast Asia into tremendous economic...](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/around-the-globe/google-asia/turning-ai-momentum-in-southeast-asia-into-tremendous-economic-growth/) If this high enthusiasm leads to technological innovation, it is expected to add $270 billion (approx. 370 trillion KRW) in economic value to the Southeast Asian economy alone. This is a staggering amount, equivalent to the annual budget of many countries. [Turning AI momentum in Southeast Asia into tremendous economic...](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/around-the-globe/google-asia/turning-ai-momentum-in-southeast-asia-into-tremendous-economic-growth/)

Second, **education for all** begins. Google has decided to invest $27 million (approx. 37 billion KRW) to provide AI education to marginalized communities, small businesses, and workers. [Google set to train communities in Asia-Pacific region in AI with...](https://mspoweruser.com/google-set-to-train-communities-in-asia-pacific-region-in-ai-with-a-27m-investment/) The goal is to set the stage so that the benefits of technology do not stay only with large corporations but are evenly distributed to small shop owners and young people just starting their careers.

## Easy Understanding: AI's 'Local Adaptation Training'

To put Google DeepMind's work in Singapore into a simple analogy, it's like **'teaching a brilliant international student local language habits and social cues (nunchi).'**

Gemini (Google's state-of-the-art AI model) is already a very smart AI. However, there was still room for improvement in perfectly understanding the many languages and thousands of complex cultural contexts of Asia. The Singapore lab focuses precisely on this point. [We’re expanding our presence in Singapore to advance AI in the...](https://nowletus.com/news/were-expanding-our-presence-in-singapore-to-advance-ai-in-the-asia-pacific-region-now1226.html)

1. **Linguistic and Cultural Inclusivity**: Beyond simple translation, they research technology to understand cultural taboos, etiquette, and subtle differences in speech unique to the Asian region. They aim to grasp the hidden intentions within Korea's honorific culture or the various dialects of Southeast Asia. [Google DeepMind is opening a new AI research lab in Singapore to advance AI in Asia Pacific.](https://blog.google/around-the-globe/google-asia/google-deepmind-is-opening-a-new-ai-research-lab-in-singapore-to-advance-ai-in-asia-pacific/)

2. **From Lab to Field**: While most labs focus solely on publishing papers, this place is different. Google has brought researchers and software engineers together in one place. It's like placing the dining table (user services) right next to the kitchen (the lab). This speeds up the process so that research results can be immediately applied to the smartphone apps or cloud services we use. [Google boosts AI research, skills & safety in Singapore](https://itbrief.asia/story/google-boosts-ai-research-skills-safety-in-singapore)

## Current Situation: Why Did Singapore Become an 'AI Hotspot'?

Why Singapore? Singapore currently has the most robust AI and data-related infrastructure in the Asia-Pacific region. The vacancy rate for data centers (huge computer facilities that store and process vast amounts of information) in Singapore is only 1.4%, the lowest in the region, indicating explosive demand. [More Mid-Market Hosts Are Moving Into the APAC Gold Rush](https://www.hostingadvice.com/blog/more-mid-market-hosts-are-moving-into-the-apac-gold-rush/)

Furthermore, not only Google but also OpenAI, the creator of ChatGPT, is putting effort into this region, such as hiring regional leads in Singapore. [IT Support Singapore, APAC Regional Lead | OpenAI](https://openai.com/careers/it-support-singapore-apac-regional-lead-singapore/) The Asian market is so competitive that statistics show 60% of companies expanding into the region fail in their first year. [Hidden Pitfalls to Avoid When Expanding a Local Business to the...](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/hidden-pitfalls-avoid-when-expanding-local-business-apac-benny-neo-s336e) Google DeepMind's decision to establish a lab directly here reflects the urgency and determination that success is difficult without a deep understanding of local culture.

Google's AI education fund is already making practical changes, supporting 49 organizations selected through the Asian Venture Philanthropy Network (AVPN) in its first phase in 2024. [Google set to train communities in Asia-Pacific region in AI with...](https://mspoweruser.com/google-set-to-train-communities-in-asia-pacific-region-in-ai-with-a-27m-investment/)

## What Does the Future Hold?

The establishment of the Google DeepMind Singapore lab promises two futures for us.

First, we will encounter **smarter and kinder AI**. Gemini, which understands the subtle nuances of our language and provides answers that consider Asian sensibilities, will integrate more deeply into your smartphone. The day is not far off when you won't have to ask "Do you know what I mean?" when talking to an AI. [Google DeepMind is opening a new AI research lab in Singapore to advance AI in Asia Pacific.](https://blog.google/around-the-globe/google-asia/google-deepmind-is-opening-a-new-ai-research-lab-in-singapore-to-advance-ai-in-asia-pacific/)

Second, it will be an **AI that is safer and better at dealing with risks**. As the Asia-Pacific region is a hub for global manufacturing and logistics, the risk of cyberattacks is also high. [Why Asia Pacific businesses must prepare... | Asian Business Review](https://asianbusinessreview.com/economy/commentary/why-asia-pacific-businesses-must-prepare-new-era-geopolitical-cyber-threats) Considering these regional characteristics, the local lab will also serve as a sentinel, building more secure and reliable AI systems against hacking or personal information leaks.

Ultimately, this expansion is a declaration that technology will go beyond being a tool for calculating numbers and become a bridge connecting cultures between people. We can look forward to seeing what warm changes this small movement starting in Singapore will bring to the daily lives of Korean users.
