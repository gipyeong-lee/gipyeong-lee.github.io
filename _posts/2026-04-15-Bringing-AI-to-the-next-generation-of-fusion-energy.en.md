## How to Bring the Heart of the Sun to Earth

**Imagine this.** What if the electricity powering your living room lights was generated using the same principle as the Sun floating far away in space? The Sun, which brightens the world every morning, is actually a massive 'natural energy factory.' In its core, hydrogen nuclei fuse together under extreme heat and pressure, releasing enormous amounts of energy—a process called **nuclear fusion (the reaction where light atomic nuclei combine to form heavier nuclei, releasing energy)** [Bringing AI to the next generation of fusion energy - ONMINE](https://onmine.io/bringing-ai-to-the-next-generation-of-fusion-energy/).

Scientists have long dreamed of bringing this solar principle to power plants on Earth. If successful, we would gain access to virtually 'limitless clean energy' with zero carbon emissions and minimal concerns about radioactive waste [Bringing AI to the next generation of Fusion Energy](https://dataforcee.us/2025/10/16/bringing-ai-to-the-next-generation-of-fusion-energy/). However, the path to making this dream a reality has been anything but easy. It requires enduring temperatures far hotter than the Sun's core and managing volatile energy with absolute precision.

Recently, Artificial Intelligence (AI) has stepped in as a 'magic hand' to help solve this seemingly impossible puzzle.

## Why Does This Matter?

Why is the world so enthusiastic about nuclear fusion, and why is AI necessary now? The primary reason is that fusion is the 'ultimate solution' to humanity's energy problems.

1. **A Sustainable Future**: Unlike fossil fuels, nuclear fusion does not emit carbon dioxide. Furthermore, unlike conventional nuclear power (fission), it does not produce high-level radioactive waste that lasts for tens of thousands of years, making it extremely safe [Bringing AI to the next generation of fusion energy - ONMINE](https://onmine.io/bringing-ai-to-the-next-generation-of-fusion-energy/).
2. **Infinite Fuel**: Deuterium, the primary fuel for fusion, can be extracted from seawater, meaning resources are virtually inexhaustible [Bringing AI to the next generation of Fusion Energy](https://dataforcee.us/2025/10/16/bringing-ai-to-the-next-generation-of-fusion-energy/). **To put it in perspective**, the fuel from just one cup of seawater could provide enough electricity for a household for decades.
3. **Explosive Investment**: Capital is moving quickly to capture this potential. Total investment in the fusion industry has skyrocketed more than eightfold in five years, from $1.7 billion (approx. 2 trillion KRW) in 2020 to $15 billion (approx. 20 trillion KRW) as of September 2025 [Why the AI Industry Is Betting on Fusion Energy | TIME](https://time.com/7328213/nuclear-fusion-energy-ai/).

However, to succeed in fusion, we must confine and control **plasma (an ionized gaseous state where nuclei and electrons are separated)** at temperatures exceeding 100 million degrees Celsius—far hotter than the Sun [Bringing AI to the next generation of fusion energy - ONMINE](https://onmine.io/bringing-ai-to-the-next-generation-of-fusion-energy/). This process is so complex and occurs in timeframes shorter than a blink of an eye that human calculation alone cannot keep up. This is exactly where AI proves its value.

## Easy Understanding: How Does AI Tame the 'Artificial Sun'?

What happens inside a fusion reactor is **essentially like "using invisible magnetic forces to keep a very hot, wild mass of jelly suspended in mid-air."** If this jelly (plasma) loses balance for even a moment and touches the wall, the device melts or the reaction stops.

### 1. A 0.001-Second Battle: Real-Time Control
Google DeepMind recently partnered with Commonwealth Fusion Systems (CFS) to tackle this challenge [Bringing AI to the next generation of fusion energy](https://deepmind.google/blog/bringing-ai-to-the-next-generation-of-fusion-energy/). DeepMind's AI system monitors the fluctuating state of the plasma inside the reactor in real-time and instantly decides how to adjust the magnetic fields to keep the plasma stable [Plasma Under Control: How AI is Driving Fusion's Next Leap](https://www.cleanenergy-platform.com/insight/plasma-under-control-how-ai-is-driving-fusions-next-leap/).

**Imagine** a circus performer spinning thousands of plates at once. AI predicts which way each plate is tilting and moves its fingertips in 0.001 seconds to prevent them from falling. This real-time control system is essential for next-generation fusion reactors to operate safely for long periods [AI makes fusion energy smarter and safer with real-time plasma monitoring](https://www.thebrighterside.news/post/ai-makes-fusion-energy-smarter-and-safer-with-real-time-plasma-monitoring/).

### 2. Infinite Practice in the Virtual World (Simulation)
The Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory (PPPL) introduced a new computing platform called 'STELLAR-AI' [PPPL launches STELLAR-AI platform to accelerate fusion energy research](https://www.pppl.gov/news/2026/pppl-launches-stellar-ai-platform-accelerate-fusion-energy-research). This platform combines AI and high-performance computing to **simulate (conducting mock experiments of real situations on a computer)** fusion reactions in a virtual environment.

While a single real-world fusion experiment is incredibly costly and time-consuming, AI can perform tens of thousands of virtual experiments in its place. This allows researchers to identify which materials resist high heat or which structures are most efficient, dramatically accelerating the pace of research [Machine Learning Applications Enabling Fusion Energy: Recent ...](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10894-025-00509-z). It’s like a pilot undergoing thousands of hours of training in a flight simulator before ever taking to the actual sky.

## Current Status: How Far Have We Come?

Beyond mere theory, tangible results are now being reported one after another.

- **DeepMind and CFS Partnership**: In October 2025, DeepMind officially announced a research collaboration with CFS, a global fusion leader [Bringing artificial intelligence to the next generation of fusion energy](https://aisckool.com/bringing-artificial-intelligence-to-the-next-generation-of-fusion-energy-2/). This is an attempt to apply DeepMind's previous breakthrough research on AI plasma control to commercial-scale devices [Bringing AI to the next generation of fusion energy](https://deepmind.google/blog/bringing-ai-to-the-next-generation-of-fusion-energy/).
- **Toward Net Energy Gain**: CFS is currently building a device called 'SPARC.' Their goal is to demonstrate **'Net Energy Gain'**—producing more energy than was put in—by the end of 2026 [Top 10 Fusion Energy Startups to Watch in 2026](https://www.naturetechmemos.com/p/top-10-fusion-energy-startups-to-watch-in-2026).
- **Supply Chain Establishment**: Beyond technical development, a supply chain for producing specialized materials and components (such as capacitors) for fusion plants is already being established [Minh Q. Tran on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/minhtran_fusionenergy-innovation-activity-7368542042894913536-4kpc).

Experts agree that "computing tools are making fusion research more agile and precise" [AI ignites innovation in fusion - iter.org](https://www.iter.org/node/20687/ai-ignites-innovation-fusion). While fusion might not power your home tomorrow, it is clear that AI is bringing that day much closer.

## What’s Next?

We have exciting milestones ahead of us.

First is the **SPARC experiment in 2026**. If the SPARC device produces energy as planned, nuclear fusion will be elevated from science fiction to a 'commercially viable energy business' [Top 10 Fusion Energy Startups to Watch in 2026](https://www.naturetechmemos.com/p/top-10-fusion-energy-startups-to-watch-in-2026).
