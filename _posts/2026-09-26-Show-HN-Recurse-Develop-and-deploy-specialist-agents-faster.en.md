Imagine this: You wake up in the morning and tell your artificial intelligence (AI) assistant, "Organize today's meeting materials, summarize the latest three industry news stories, and create a report." With older AI, it might have stuttered trying to handle all these complex processes alone. But now, an era is approaching where your AI assistant will intelligently call upon a 'meeting material analysis expert' and a 'news search specialist agent,' dividing roles to process the task in an instant.

Among developers, technology for efficiently creating and connecting AI agents is a hot topic. [Related Tools](https://agentskills.io/home) Today, we will easily explore how this technology will change our lives and the concepts of 'Recursive' (problems solving and improving itself) development and collaboration that have been emphasized recently.

## Why are specialized agents needed?

The AI we have used until now usually tried to appear as an 'all-around problem solver' capable of handling anything. However, in actual work environments, experts with deep specialized knowledge in specific fields are much more necessary. Today's development environment is evolving in a direction that makes it easier to create these **Expert AI Agents** and enables them to work as if they were a team.

Through this, companies have become able to build much more complex AI systems faster, and we can receive more accurate and reliable help in our daily tasks. A world is opening where we don't have to manage AI one by one, but rather where appropriate agents form teams on their own, collaborate, and solve problems.

## Easy to Understand: AI Cooperatives, 'Agent Swarms'

The method where multiple AI agents possess specialized abilities and join forces is called an **'Agent Swarm.'** [What is an Agent Swarm?](https://builtin.com/articles/agent-swarm) Simply put, think of it as a 'cooperative' for AIs.

To use an analogy, it's like a project at a company where a planner, developer, and designer gather to exercise their respective expertise to complete a single product. This method operates as follows:

1. **Task Division**: The entire job is broken down into smaller sub-tasks.
2. **Expert Allocation**: Each task is delivered to the specific agent best suited to solve it.
3. **Knowledge Sharing**: Agents share the results they have processed to create even smarter outcomes.

Recently, 'recursive development loops,' where AI agents autonomously write code, strengthen security, and improve systems themselves, are gaining attention through tools like Claude Code. [The Power of Recursive Development](https://dev.to/hamzasajid-dev/agents-building-agents-the-recursive-power-of-claude-code-1efd) This is an important technological stepping stone toward a future where AI creates or improves other AIs.

## How far have we come? The new challenge of security

Of course, this technology doesn't only have a rosy future. Recently, security problems are growing as the number of AI agents increases. According to one report, more than 17,000 attack behaviors targeting AI agents have been observed. [AI Agent Security Problems](https://www.docker.com/blog/ai-agent-security-systems-problem/) This means that it is difficult to maintain security just by having humans review AI actions one by one. Therefore, system-level security devices that can safely control AI and immediately detect abnormal behavior have become more important than ever.

## What will the future landscape look like?

In the near future, countless specialized agents will be hidden in the background of every service we use. Developers will add new agents faster, and apps will become increasingly smarter. AI will soon move beyond being a 'chatbot' that is simply a conversation partner and become a 'digital assistant team' that reliably handles your work for you.
