Imagine this: behind the smartphones or laptops we use every day, a "factory of intelligence" of unimaginable scale is running 24/7 without a break. The recent earnings report released by Anthropic, a leader in the field of artificial intelligence (AI), ahead of its initial public offering (IPO), vividly proves that global resources are being poured into building this factory of intelligence.

Anthropic recently disclosed massive financial figures in materials released ahead of its US stock market debut. While third-quarter revenue grew 1,100% year-over-year to $17 billion, the company simultaneously recorded a massive net loss of $80.7 billion [[Source 1](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/business/2026/11/03/anthropic-says-revenue-surged-1-100-ahead-of-ipo), [Source 4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6JujAGXBLgg)]. What caught the market's eye most was the investment plan, with Anthropic committing to spend $895.4 billion (approximately $900 billion) [[Source 6](https://weeklysilicon.com/story/2026-11-02-16194-anthropic-reveals-900b-ai-spending-commitments-ahead-of-ipo)]. Why on earth would they spend such a colossal amount of money, and for what?

## Why does this matter?

This announcement signals that the nature of the AI industry has changed. AI has moved beyond the stage of simply creating smart software and has become a "capital-intensive industry" where survival depends on exclusively securing the world's power, semiconductors, and physical data center infrastructure [[Source 6](https://weeklysilicon.com/story/2026-11-02-16194-anthropic-reveals-900b-ai-spending-commitments-ahead-of-ipo)].

Anthropic is one of the most technologically advanced AI companies today, with clients including the entire Fortune 10 and major global banks [[Source 5](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-eyes-40-trillion-market-as-revenue-surges-ahead-of-ipo/)]. The fact that such a leading company has promised such astronomical investments is a strong signal that it intends to secure more computing power than its competitors to win the AI technology race. This implies that it will become increasingly difficult for smaller companies with less capital to enter the AI market.

## In simple terms: The "Mega Factory" Theory of AI

For AI to accurately answer the questions we ask, it must learn from trillions of pieces of data. Massive "computing resources" are essential for this.

To use an analogy: if past AI was at the level of baking cookies in a small bakery, current AI has entered the stage of building a massive automated factory to meet global demand. To run the factory, machinery (servers), buildings (data centers), and vast amounts of energy (power) must be continuously injected.

The $900 billion promised by Anthropic is the "future cost" secured in advance to continuously operate this factory and expand its facilities [[Source 7](https://news.bloombergtax.com/financial-accounting/anthropic-ipo-filing-reveals-nearly-900-billion-in-future-debt)]. About $447.5 billion of this is scheduled to be executed before December of this year [[Source 8](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/anthropic-reveals-amended-s-1-outlining-huge-spending-commitments-rising-sales-153635825.html)]. It is a strategy to secure a "dedicated lane" by signing contracts in advance so that competitors cannot seize key resources.

## How far have they come?

Anthropic is the first company dealing with cutting-edge AI technology to aim for a large-scale stock market debut [[Source 5](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-eyes-40-trillion-market-as-revenue-surges-ahead-of-ipo/)]. While growth is explosive—revenue increased nearly 12-fold in the third quarter alone—the infrastructure investment costs mentioned earlier are so high that the scale of the loss is also significant [[Source 2](https://invezz.com/news/2026/11/02/anthropic-revenue-surges-12-fold-in-q3-with-895b-in-purchase-commitments/), [Source 9](https://www.techmeme.com/261026/p22)].

In addition, Anthropic emphasizes "safety" just as much as technological advancement. In this IPO filing, they officially warned that AI technology could pose fatal or existential risks to humanity [[Source 3](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/anthropic-releases-ipo-filing-ahead-highly-anticipated-stock-debut-rcna605676)]. This is a point that demonstrates the company's philosophy that responsible development is as important as the speed of technology.

## We are curious about what comes next

Experts believe this IPO has the potential to be the largest in history [[Source 4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6JujAGXBLgg)]. Investors are watching to see if Anthropic can overcome its current massive losses and demonstrate a sustainable and stable revenue model through the infrastructure it has secured.

There are two key points to watch in the future. First, how much more sophisticated and efficient the next-generation AI models will be, born from the computing power that Anthropic has secured through astronomical costs. Second, how the company will manage the globally surging power demand and data infrastructure issues while allaying social concerns. The world is watching to see how this factory of intelligence will change our lives.
