Imagine this: You are relaxing at a cafe with a friend, enjoying a cup of coffee. On your PC, an AI agent is performing a complex data analysis task overnight. But what happens if the agent suddenly hits an unexpected error and halts? Since you aren't sitting in front of your PC, you might lose precious hours without even knowing that the work has stopped.

Wouldn't it be great if you could manage and resolve all these situations in real-time with just your smartphone? A new tool that solves this exact problem, **Pocketty**, has recently been released.

### Why is this important?

As the era of AI agents begins, we have started actively using AI that makes its own decisions and performs complex tasks. However, because agents are machines and not humans, they sometimes run into logical problems they cannot solve on their own or unexpected system errors. The industry refers to this as being "blocked." In other words, if the user doesn't personally intervene to fix the issue, the progress of the work grinds to a complete halt.

In a typical development environment, accessing a server requires opening a laptop and going through complex authentication procedures. However, Pocketty provides a powerful connection that allows you to check the status of your AI agent and respond immediately anytime, anywhere, using the iPhone or iPad you carry in your hand every day. ### Easy to understand: An 'emergency contact' in your hand

To understand this app, let's compare it to a **'smart home door lock.'** If a valuable package arrives at your house but you don't unlock the door, the delivery person has to wait outside indefinitely. In this case, a smart door lock sends an instant notification to your smartphone saying, "There is someone at the door!" You can then open the app and unlock the door remotely right then and there.

Pocketty is like a 'door lock notification system' for your AI agent. [Source: Show HN: Pocketty – iPhone SSH terminal that pings you when an agent is blocked](https://decohack.com/hn/) When an agent hits a 'wall' during a task or finishes everything perfectly, Pocketty immediately sends a notification to your iPhone. You can then launch the app, open the actual terminal screen, and enter commands to resolve the issue as if you were sitting right in front of your PC.
