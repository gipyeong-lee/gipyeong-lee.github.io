Imagine this: you're building a new smartphone application or creating your own business website from scratch. You've spent all night crafting complex programming code, only for your screen to suddenly fill with unexplained error messages. Your eyes are tired, your head is throbbing, and then, a reliable and friendly senior developer sits next to you, lightly pats your shoulder, and says, "Don't worry, I'll fix that part for you in the blink of an eye!" along with the correct code and a clear explanation. How reassuring would that be? This dream is now a reality. Google's recently announced new artificial intelligence model, **Gemini 3.7 Flash**, has returned as our smartest and most helpful virtual colleague.

### Why is this important?

The past world of artificial intelligence (AI) was somewhat polarized. There were 'expensive expert AIs' that were highly intelligent but prohibitively costly to use. On the other hand, there were 'basic assistant AIs' that were affordable but gave nonsensical answers to complex questions. This meant that general users and developers were constantly in a precarious balancing act between performance and their wallet.

However, the newly introduced Gemini 3.7 Flash perfectly resolves this dilemma. This model exhibits exceptionally fast and precise performance in areas requiring sophisticated knowledge, such as coding and advanced data analysis [Source 10].

This is particularly welcome news for those building websites or enjoying learning development. It's not just about finding and fixing bugs in complex source code; it excels as an 'AI Agent' that can independently formulate logical plans and complete tasks through multiple steps [Source 4, Source 10].

What's truly astounding is that despite possessing such advanced intelligence, the cost of use has been brought down to an incredibly reasonable level [Source 7, Source 11]. This opens up a wonderful environment where AI can be embraced not just as a tool to occasionally check out of curiosity, but as a true colleague that closely assists our work every day from morning to night [Source 11].

### Easy to Understand: 'Solid Fundamentals, Deeper Application'

There are broadly two ways to teach AI and make it smarter. One is to have it study an immense volume of books from scratch, relearning the basics (pretraining). The other is to intensively train an already solid student on techniques for solving problems faster and more deeply (inference optimization).

In this analogy, Gemini 3.7 Flash corresponds to the second method. It's like giving a top-ranked student extra tutoring to strengthen their ability to think deeply, enabling them to solve highly advanced essay questions without hesitation.

While Google's previous AI model, **Gemini 3.6 Flash**, possessed broad and robust 'fundamental knowledge,' the new 3.7 Flash has maintained that knowledge base intact while meticulously refining its internal, sophisticated inference algorithms [Source 12]. It hasn't learned entirely new languages from scratch; instead, it has innovatively upgraded its 'thinking system' to utilize its vast existing knowledge more wisely.

Simply put, it's akin to a veteran photographer who, without buying an expensive new camera, precisely adjusts the focus and filters of their existing camera to achieve even clearer and more brilliant photographs. By making the core mechanism (transformer architecture) that captures the intricate relationships between numerous words more flexible and intelligent, it has become adept at understanding even complex, convoluted programming languages [Source 2, Source 12].

### Current Situation: How Much Better Is It?

Even if you're unfamiliar with technical jargon or complex computer terminology, you can immediately grasp the improvement from its enhanced performance metrics.

In **FrontierCode 1.1 Main**, a standardized test rigorously evaluating the practical programming problem-solving abilities of global AIs, the previous model received a somewhat disappointing score of 34.4 out of 100. However, Gemini 3.7 Flash achieved an excellent score of 43.6, demonstrating remarkable growth [Source 9].

But that's not all. In the **DeepSWE v1.1** test, which requires solving the most challenging and head-scratching practical software engineering tasks faced by developers in the field, its performance dramatically surged from 49% to an impressive 65.3% [Source 9]. It has evolved from the level of a junior developer barely getting half the problems right to that of a seasoned mid-level developer confidently solving two out of every three problems perfectly. Tulsee Doshi, a Senior Director of Technical Safety and Performance Development at Google, emphasized in an official announcement that "compared to the previous generation of models, we have achieved a dramatic and unimaginable improvement, especially in practical coding capabilities" [Source 9].

This powerful Gemini 3.7 Flash is rapidly being integrated into Google's diverse user environments. Users subscribed to Google's personal AI paid service, 'Gemini Spark,' can experience this intelligent AI's capabilities immediately without any complex installation [Source 3]. Simultaneously, it's being rapidly deployed as an API (programming interface) for business and professional developers, allowing countless software development companies worldwide to freely integrate this agile brain into their smartphone apps or websites to create amazing services [Source 5, Source 14].

### What Happens Next?

No matter how advanced a new technology is, the realistic concern often arises: "Is it too expensive for only large corporations to use?" Google has taken a very bold and groundbreaking step in this regard.

To ensure that countless students, developers, and businesses worldwide can achieve peak efficiency without worrying about costs, they are guaranteeing an unbelievably low and attractive introductory price of **just $0.75 per 1 million input tokens (text units recognized by AI)** until December 31, 2026 [Source 6, Source 14]. This level of overwhelming cost-effectiveness and strong merit is remarkable, even when compared to competing models with similar performance currently available in the AI market [Source 7, Source 11].

Going forward, such practical models, branded with the 'Flash' title (light, economical, and extremely fast), will play a central role in the core infrastructure of the IT development field and real-world business operations.

Small and medium-sized venture companies, often enduring sleepless nights due to chronic shortages of specialized personnel and development delays, can accelerate their development speed manifold with the perfect support of an affordable AI colleague. Furthermore, general individual users will be able to fully enjoy a smart era of solo entrepreneurship, easily completing tasks like analyzing complex administrative documents, analyzing data spreadsheets, translating, and even practical coding with the help of a much more sophisticated and precise personal AI assistant, all while drastically reducing the burden of high monthly subscription fees.
