## Easy Understanding: How does AI learn dolphin language?

Decoding dolphin conversation is like deciphering a very complex foreign language sentence that has been encrypted. Google used two key weapons to solve this difficult problem.

### 1. 40 Years of Records: Dolphin 'Big Data'
To learn a language, a large amount of conversational content is needed. Dr. Denise L. Herzing and her research team at the Wild Dolphin Project have been observing Atlantic spotted dolphins and collecting a vast amount of vocal data spanning **40 years**. [Google Is Training a New A.I. Model to Decode Dolphin Chatter—and...](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/google-is-training-a-new-ai-model-to-decode-dolphin-chatter-and-potentially-talk-back-180986434/) [Google Uses DolphinGemma AI to Decode Dolphin Communication](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/google-uses-dolphingemma-ai-to-decode-dolphin-communication/490753) Because of this treasure trove of data, the AI was able to learn what sounds dolphins make in what situations. [Google Uses DolphinGemma AI to Decode Dolphin Communication](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/google-uses-dolphingemma-ai-to-decode-dolphin-communication/490753) It's like handing over 40 years' worth of dolphin diaries to an AI and having it read them.

### 2. 'SoundStream' Technology: Dividing Sound into Digital Pieces
Dolphin sounds are so fast and high-pitched that they are difficult for the human ear to fully hear. Google used a tokenizer (a tool that breaks down data for easy analysis) called **'SoundStream'**, which divides sound into tiny digital fragments. [Introducing DolphinGemma: Analyzing dolphins' natural, complex communication is a monumental task](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/dolphingemma/)

To use an analogy, it's like breaking a very long sentence into individual words to look them up in a dictionary; the complex dolphin sound waves are divided into "digital word" fragments that the AI can easily understand. Through this, the AI has grasped the structure of dolphin sounds and can even create new sound sequences (continuous sound segments) similar to those of dolphins. [SETI Tech On Earth: DolphinGemma: How Google AI Is Helping Decode ...](https://astrobiology.com/2025/04/seti-tech-on-earth-dolphingemma-how-google-ai-is-helping-decode-dolphin-communication.html)
