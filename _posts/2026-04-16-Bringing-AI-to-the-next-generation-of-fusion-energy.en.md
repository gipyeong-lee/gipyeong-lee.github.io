## Easy Understanding: An AI Reporter's Story of the 'Sun on Earth'

### What is Nuclear Fusion?
Nuclear fusion is exactly the same way the sun produces light and heat. It is a phenomenon where light atomic nuclei (e.g., hydrogen) merge together under extreme heat and pressure to become a heavier atomic nucleus (helium), releasing the remaining energy in the process. [Int'l body promoting next-generation fusion energy joins Osaka expo](https://english.kyodonews.net/articles/-/53727)

**To use an analogy:** It is like two small water droplets merging to become one large droplet, with a massive 'energy spark' of power flying out at the moment of combination. On the other hand, the nuclear power we currently use is 'nuclear fission,' which is like breaking a large glass to create fragments. Unlike fission, which leaves behind broken fragments (radioactive waste), fusion is a 'merging' process, so there is far less concern about residue and it is cleaner. [Why the AI Industry Is Betting on Fusion Energy | TIME](https://time.com/7328213/nuclear-fusion-energy-ai/)

### But why can't we use it yet?
The biggest problem is maintaining a state called 'plasma.' To merge atoms, a temperature of over 100 million degrees Celsius is required, and in this state, matter becomes 'plasma'—moving much more actively and irregularly than a gas. [Why the AI Industry Is Betting on Fusion Energy | TIME](https://time.com/7328213/nuclear-fusion-energy-ai/)

Imagine having to suspend a hot, slippery 'liquid monster' over 100 million degrees in mid-air using magnetic force and shaping it without a second's rest. If it touches the wall even slightly, the reactor could melt or the spark could go out. This plasma is so unstable that it is incredibly difficult to control with human calculation alone. ### This is where AI comes in: Google's 'TORAX'
Google DeepMind is pouring its AI expertise into controlling this tricky plasma. The core of this partnership is Google's **'TORAX (AI-based software for plasma simulation and control)'**. [Bringing AI to the next generation of fusion energy - Popular News...](https://allheadline.com/bringing-ai-to-the-next-generation-of-fusion-energy/)

TORAX performs tens of thousands of simulations in a virtual space before operating the actual device to find the most stable way to maintain the plasma. [Bringing AI to the next generation of fusion energy - Popular News...](https://allheadline.com/bringing-ai-to-the-next-generation-of-fusion-energy/) Just as an experienced racing driver manipulates the steering wheel in 0.001-second increments to navigate a sharp curve, AI acts as a 'super controller' that predicts and adjusts the movement of the plasma in real-time.
