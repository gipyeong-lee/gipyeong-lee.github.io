Imagine this: every morning, you sit in front of your computer and say, "I want my AI agent to handle the complex tasks I need to do today." Like a skilled assistant, the AI then writes code, inputs terminal commands, and summarizes necessary documents on its own.

We are rapidly entering the 'Agent Era'—where AI goes beyond simply answering questions to thinking and acting for itself (AI that receives user instructions, utilizes tools, and performs tasks on its own). However, the general chat windows we use today are too narrow and stifling for these 'digital colleagues' to work over long periods. Today, we introduce 'AgentsDock,' a new workspace designed to allow for deeper collaboration with AI.

## Why does this matter?

The AI chat tools we have used until now have typically been one-off methods where you have a conversation and finish. But 'agents' are different. They need to continue working overnight like real colleagues or resolve complex projects that require dozens of steps.

It is difficult to properly manage these 'action-oriented' AIs with only standard text editors or web browsers. That is why AgentsDock was born. This tool started with the idea of providing a 'dedicated workspace' where AI agents can work on their own like humans. This is a significant change that will completely transform the quality of collaboration with AI, not just for developers and researchers, but for general users trying to automate complex tasks. [Source: AgentsDock: An IDE designed for agentic AI research | Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49678435)

## Easy to understand: A 'Digital Desk' for AI

To understand AgentsDock, let's use a very simple analogy.

If a standard AI chatbot we commonly use is like reading something on a 'whiteboard' and asking for an opinion, AgentsDock is your **'personal dedicated digital desk.'** Everything the AI needs to work is already prepared there.

1. **Persistent chat sessions**: The AI remembers what it was doing and doesn't forget.
2. **Integrated terminal**: The AI can issue commands directly in your computer environment and execute code.
3. **Task management**: It can load complex media files or perform pre-configured scheduled jobs. [Source: AgentsDock - A dock for all your agents](https://agentsdock.net/)

In short, think of it as a solid infrastructure that helps AI enter your computer, find its own dedicated spot, and handle work.

## Where and how is it used?

Currently, AgentsDock operates mainly around professional AI agents like Claude Code or Codex. These agents demonstrate very powerful capabilities in development environments, such as modifying code on their own and executing terminal commands. [Source: AgentsDock - A dock for all your agents](https://agentsdock.net/)

Of course, it might still feel a bit unfamiliar to general users. Because it is a self-hosted method that requires you to build your own server, it requires a certain level of technical understanding. However, conversely, this means that AI agents can learn and work safely in your own space without leaking your valuable data to the outside. It keeps the principle of 'my data is safe inside my computer.'

## What will happen in the future?

Tools like AgentsDock will continue to evolve. It has great potential to become the standard for 'agent collaboration environments' that goes beyond tools just for developers—capable of writing complex company reports or generating project proposals by crossing through dozens of files on its own.

What we should pay attention to going forward is how much easier these 'agent environments' will become to use. If the installation process becomes simpler and the interface becomes more intuitive, you might eventually have an AI colleague living in your computer who always handles routine tasks for you.
