Imagine a world swept up in a new technology craze, where astonishing artificial intelligence (AI) services pour out every single day. People are pouring money into related stocks and companies as if hunting for gold. Yet right outside the venue where this dazzling festival is taking place, angry citizens have taken to the streets with placards, shouting, "This is a massive bubble, and ordinary folks like us will pay the price later."

This isn't a scene from a movie. It actually unfolded on the streets of New York on the day Anthropic, one of the giants in the artificial intelligence (AI) industry, made its glamorous debut on the U.S. Nasdaq stock exchange with a record-breaking scale [Source 3, Source 15, Source 17]. What on earth is the story hidden behind this listing that has shaken the entire world?

---

## Why Does This Matter?

You might think, "Isn't this just about wealthy folks in Silicon Valley who have nothing to do with me?" However, this event is deeply intertwined with our wallets and our post-retirement lives.

Simply put, behind the smartphone AI services we use every day, billions of dollars are changing hands, and that money is tied up in the stock prices of mega tech companies. What happens if this bubble pops like a fragile bowl? In New York City alone, the pensions of roughly 750,000 ordinary municipal workers are intricately entangled with Anthropic stock [Source 14].

In other words, if tech companies collapse, that shock wave will cascade through massive pension funds holding their shares, ultimately reaching the retirement funds of ordinary citizens. This is why cries of "No Bailout" — demanding that the government must not bail them out with tax money if the AI bubble bursts — erupted onto the streets [Source 1, Source 4]. To use an analogy, it is like a colossal cruise ship speeding head-on toward an iceberg while carrying the secure assets of all of us aboard as passengers.

---

## Easy to Understand: The Massive Shadow Behind the Glamorous Stage

Anthropic's Nasdaq listing can be perfectly likened to **"a skyscraper built with a framework raised far higher than its foundation."**

The chatbots and AI models we frequently encounter look unimaginably smart and sophisticated on the surface, but maintaining and training them requires astronomical costs that defy imagination. It is structured much like a luxury hotel boasting a gorgeous exterior while racking up tens of millions of won in deficits every single day for maintenance.

According to reports, Anthropic recorded a staggering net loss of $80 billion (over 100 trillion won) in the first half of 2026 alone [Source 2]. Compared to the $1.3 billion loss recorded in the same period of the previous year, this is an explosive increase of more than sixfold [Source 2]. Nevertheless, the company entered the stock market evaluated at a corporate value of $2 trillion [Source 5].

To put it simply, **a venture startup yet to turn a stable profit has been recognized with a national-budget-level valuation purely on global expectations.** Because of this, warnings that "this is the pinnacle of an all-time bubble" have continued to echo across the market [Source 7].

---

## Current Status: Record IPO and Arrests on the Street

Anthropic's recent Nasdaq listing was the hottest event in AI industry history. The key details are as follows:

* **Record-Breaking Capital Raising**: Anthropic raised approximately $81.3 billion through its debut on Nasdaq, setting the record for the largest initial public offering (IPO) in U.S. history [Source 17]. In contrast to its major competitor OpenAI, which delayed its IPO timeline to 2027, Anthropic chose a head-on breakthrough [Source 10].
* **Frenzied Stock Surge**: As soon as trading commenced, shares skyrocketed over 34% from the offering price to hit $111.50 [Source 17]. Institutional competition was so fierce that more than half of the institutions that bid to buy shares could not even receive them [Source 5].
* **Street Protests and Arrests**: However, the scene outside the Nasdaq market in New York was entirely different. Protesters criticized Anthropic for "inflating the AI bubble excessively to make a quick buck before the bubble bursts" [Source 3]. During the protests, fierce clashes broke out, resulting in at least four participants being arrested by police [Source 15].

Protesters gathered at the scene, veterans who lived through the 2008 financial crisis, and civic groups all agree that today's AI craze bears an eerie resemblance to the dot-com bubble or subprime mortgage crisis of the past [Source 14]. This sharp observation highlights the fragility of the real economy lurking behind immediate stock gains.

---

## What Happens Next?

This listing is a monumental test facing the entire AI industry [Source 6]. There are two key points to watch moving forward.

First, **high-intensity regulations and pressure from political circles**. Political factions, including Democrats, are preparing powerful roadblocks targeting the AI industry [Source 4, Source 6]. Specifically, these include putting an indefinite halt to the new construction of large-scale data centers that consume endless electric power, legally banning the development of superintelligence with prison sentences of up to 20 years for violations, and upholding the principle of **"absolutely no bailouts for failed AI companies"** [Source 4]. It reflects a resolve by the state to curb the moral hazard of tech firms.

Second, **investors' cold assessment of fundamentals** [Source 9]. While it currently commands attention with dazzling stock gains, if companies fail to cover their massive losses and prove viable business models, market sentiment could turn ice-cold at any moment [Source 2, Source 7]. AI researchers and safety experts also repeatedly warn that companies are bogged down in immediate tech development competition rather than safety and rigorous verification [Source 8].
