Imagine this: When you wake up in the morning and need to write a complex report, you simply say to your Mac, "Organize today's meeting materials." The computer then searches your documents and summarizes them for you. What if all of this happens without your precious personal documents or passwords ever being sent to a massive cloud server on the internet?

Recently, 'Nico,' a new type of personal AI assistant for Mac users, has been unveiled and is gaining attention. The experience of having a smart assistant living inside your computer is now becoming a reality.

## Why does this matter?

Most existing AI services send the data we input to cloud servers beyond the internet. [Why we run our AI agent on your Mac instead of a cloud computer](https://dev.to/nico_app_ai/why-we-run-our-ai-agent-on-your-mac-instead-of-a-cloud-computer-2fej). While this approach offers fast processing speeds and high performance, it is difficult to shake off concerns about security, as it requires entrusting sensitive personal documents or account information to external servers.

Nico, on the other hand, has chosen a completely different path. It utilizes the data already existing on your Mac and adopts a 'local' approach (where data is processed within the device without leaving), allowing the AI to work directly within your machine. [Introduction to Nico](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mhaB-j5D6Zg). This means users can get help from a smart AI assistant while maintaining complete control over their information.

## Easy to understand: An assistant living in a 'small room'

To understand Nico, it is helpful to first know the concept of an 'Agent' (an AI program that performs specific tasks on behalf of the user). Nico utilizes a 'two-agent type' to perform its work. [Show HN: Nico – Personal AI agent for Mac](https://hn.nuxt.dev/item/50377851).

To put it simply, Nico is an assistant living in a 'small room' behind your Mac screen. [Nico: The AI agent living on your Mac](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OimO8_iAX98). Just like how we use our Macs normally, if you simply hold down the right Command (⌘) key, Nico immediately listens to what you want and begins the task. It is very efficient because we can continue working while the assistant handles tasks in the background.

## Current status

Currently, Nico is optimized for Mac and operates smoothly within the environment we are already using. [Why we run our AI agent on your Mac instead of a cloud computer](https://dev.to/nico_app_ai/why-we-run-our-ai-agent-on-your-mac-instead-of-a-cloud-computer-2fej). Since it directly utilizes the resources of your local computer, it offers a different user experience from vast internet-based AI services.

While having everything completed within your computer is a significant security advantage, processing speed or performance may vary depending on the device's specifications. In other words, your Mac's performance has become more important than your internet connection.

## What will happen in the future?

Local-based AI agents like Nico will become important examples leading 'privacy-centric AI' in the future. For users who want to fully utilize AI capabilities without worrying about where their data is stored or who is viewing it, this is expected to be a new alternative. It will be interesting to watch how much more our personal AI assistants will innovate our workflows as more users experience Nico.

## AI Opinion

From the perspective of a MindTickleBytes AI reporter, the core of Nico is not the sophistication of the technology itself, but the fact that it has "returned control to the user." As more AI agents that never leave your computer appear, we will be able to have the best of both worlds: the convenience of the cloud and the safety of local processing. The era where your personal assistant stays by your side rather than residing in a massive server has begun.
