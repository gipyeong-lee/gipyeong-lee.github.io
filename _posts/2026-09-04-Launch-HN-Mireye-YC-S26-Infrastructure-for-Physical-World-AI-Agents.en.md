Imagine this: You wake up in the morning and tell the AI on your smartphone, "Find the best restaurant near me, make a reservation, adjust my schedule today so I don't disrupt my robot vacuum's path, and set the route to the restaurant in advance."

Until now, the AI we have encountered has been like a "desk-bound" scholar who memorizes vast amounts of data to provide clever answers. But now, it is time for AI to become a "street-smart" solver that can grasp information, make decisions, and take actions directly within real-world spaces. Recently, a startup called **Mireye**, selected for Y Combinator’s (YC) S26 batch, has stepped up to play a crucial role at the center of this revolutionary change.

## Why It Matters

Most AI agents we currently use (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) are highly intelligent based on their training data. To use an analogy, they are like genius chefs who have perfectly memorized tens of thousands of cookbooks but have never once stepped into an actual kitchen.

When we give AI the authority to control the real world, there are two major barriers. First, it is difficult for AI to obtain accurate data on what is happening in reality right now (real-time location, surrounding environmental information, etc.). Second, there is the security issue of how to prevent the danger if an AI's decision leads to incorrect actions in reality. Mireye is building infrastructure—essentially an "operating system for the real world"—to solve these two problems [Source 11, Source 2].

## The Explainer

Simply put, what Mireye does is provide AI agents with "eyes, hands, and safety mechanisms to perceive and move in the physical world."

1. **Eyes and Hands (Data and Tools):** Mireye supplies real-time information and data from the physical world to AI through a single API (Application Programming Interface, a passage for different systems to communicate). For example, it helps AI instantly grasp and utilize map information or current environmental signals at a specific location [Source 8, Source 11].
2. **Communication Standard (MCP):** What is important here is the "MCP (Model Context Protocol, a standard spec for AI models to communicate with external data)" tool. This is like a standard language that allows AI to access real-world data. If an AI agent asks, "Mireye, what is the situation near here right now?", Mireye cleans up the data and brings it over in a standardized language [Source 8, Source 10].
3. **Safety Mechanisms (Security):** The real world is far more dangerous than the internet. A single mistake can lead to physical harm. Mireye is designed so that whenever an AI agent controls a real-world system, it must authenticate execution permissions in real-time using encryption for all critical tasks. It is like stamping a digital seal for final verification every time you make an important approval [Source 1].

## Where We Stand

Currently, Mireye is providing infrastructure that allows AI developers to easily connect the abilities of the physical world to their own agents (Claude, ChatGPT, Kimi, Gemini, Cursor, etc.) [Source 8].

Developers can refer to Mireye's official documentation (docs.mireye.ai) to apply the technology to their AI services, and the company is putting effort into expanding the ecosystem by offering 5,000 credits for free for initial testing [Source 10]. However, we are still at the starting point where AI agents directly control physical systems in reality. How safely and diversely infrastructure like Mireye can be connected to various physical assets will be the key factor in this field moving forward [Source 11, Source 1].

## What's Next

With the emergence of Mireye, AI agents will soon move beyond being "desk-bound" scholars and quickly enter the "street-smart" stage [Source 11]. Through infrastructure like Mireye, the AI agents we use will interpret real-world signals in real-time and make more sophisticated decisions that physically implement human intent in reality.

Before long, we will move past the level where AI simply writes emails for us and enter a daily life where we live alongside agents that are on the ground, such as checking front door locks or optimizing the layout of physical spaces.
