Imagine this: You wake up in the morning, turn on your smartphone, and say to your AI, "Record a short news briefing in my voice and send it to my friend." A moment later, an AI with the exact tone and cadence of your voice tells your friend about today's weather and schedule, sounding just as if you had spoken it yourself.

This scene, once seen only in sci-fi movies, is now ready to become part of our daily lives. On September 23, 2026, Google officially unveiled its new AI models: **Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS (Text-to-Speech)** and the Flash-Lite TTS model [[Ref 1](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/gemini-3-8-tts-says-hello), [Ref 11](https://techora.ru/news/google-vypustila-gemini-3-8-tts-2026-09-23)].

### Why is this important?

Until now, the voices produced by AI often felt unnatural, and the sensation that a "machine is reading" could never be fully eliminated. However, Gemini 3.8 TTS has moved beyond simply converting text to sound. Its core feature is **Voice Cloning**, which allows you to clone your voice using only a short 30-second audio sample [[Ref 11](https://techora.ru/news/google-vypustila-gemini-3-8-tts-2026-09-23)].

This brings massive changes for content creators and everyday users. For example, when creating an audiobook, you can clone the author's voice, or when studying a foreign language, you can listen to sentences spoken naturally in your own voice. Furthermore, because it supports over 100 languages, it is expected to play a significant role in lowering language barriers [[Ref 11](https://techora.ru/news/google-vypustila-gemini-3-8-tts-2026-09-23)].

### Simply Put: The Arrival of 'Digital Voice Actors'

Let's compare this technology to a "digital voice actor." Traditional voice actors would read scripts and add emotion to their performances. If previous Google TTS models were voice actors who could only read scripts "accurately," Gemini 3.8 TTS is a voice actor who knows how to "act."

How is this possible? The AI is trained on vast amounts of human voice data, and then you input a 30-second "sample" of your own voice. By doing this, the AI identifies your "voiceprint," such as pitch, breath, and speaking speed.

On top of this, an **emotion control** feature has been added [[Ref 11](https://techora.ru/news/google-vypustila-gemini-3-8-tts-2026-09-23)]. If you instruct the script to "say this sadly" or "read this happily," the AI adjusts its tone to match the context. Just as you can change the mood of a photo with a filter app, you can now select the "mood" of a voice.

### Where can I use it now?

Currently, the Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Flash-Lite TTS models are available through an API for developers and via Google's AI development environment, AI Studio [[Ref 11](https://techora.ru/news/google-vypustila-gemini-3-8-tts-2026-09-23)]. Anyone can input natural language prompts to create new voices or replicate existing ones [[Ref 2](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-8-text-to-speech/)]. According to Google's blog announcement, this model was developed under the leadership of Leland Rechis and Alan Cowen, and it possesses significantly more sophisticated conversation generation capabilities [[Ref 1](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/gemini-3-8-tts-says-hello)].

### What's next?

Going forward, AI will no longer convey information in a "robotic voice." We are rapidly approaching an era where a personalized assistant can read your schedule in the voice of your friend, family member, or a favorite celebrity.

However, as voice cloning technology advances, concerns regarding its potential misuse in voice phishing or the creation of fake information persist. It is a time that requires both the enjoyment of convenience and a simultaneous demand for social consensus and the development of security technologies to use the technology responsibly.
