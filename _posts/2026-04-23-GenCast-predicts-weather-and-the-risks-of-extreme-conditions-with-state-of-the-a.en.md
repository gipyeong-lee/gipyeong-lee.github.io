## Easy understanding: How does GenCast work?

The traditional way of forecasting weather is called **'Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP).'** [Generative Artificial Intelligence and Its Implications for Weather and...](https://www.genre.com/int/knowledge/publications/2025/september/gen-ai-and-its-implications-for-weather-and-climate-risk-management-en) This method involves solving complex physical laws and mathematical equations with computers to calculate how the atmospheric state will change. However, this method requires an enormous amount of calculation and takes a long time even on supercomputers.

In contrast, GenCast applies **'Generative AI'** technology to the weather. Let's explain this with an analogy.

### 1. Scenarios from 50 experts: 'Ensemble Model'
While traditional models struggle to reach a single conclusion like "there is a 70% chance of rain tomorrow," GenCast uses an **'Ensemble Model'** approach. This involves creating **more than 50 different prediction scenarios** simultaneously. [GenCast predicts weather and the risks of extreme conditions...](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jeffsternberg_gencast-predicts-weather-and-the-risks-of-activity-7270150157164249089-Orpp)

**Simply put**, it's like asking 50 weather experts the same question at once. One expert might say it will rain, while another might say it will just be cloudy. By aggregating these 50 answers, you can get much richer and more accurate probabilistic information, such as "it's very likely to rain, but if the temperature is high, it could turn into a shower." [GenCast as a High-Resolution (0.25°) AI Ensemble Model...](https://hub.baai.ac.cn/view/41562)

### 2. An AI that studied a massive 'Weather Photo Album'
How did GenCast acquire this ability? This model was trained using an enormous amount of meteorological data accumulated over decades by the **European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF)**. [r/singularity on Reddit: [Google Deepmind] GenCast predicts weather and the risks of...](https://www.reddit.com/r/singularity/comments/1h6kvfl/google_deepmind_gencast_predicts_weather_and_the/)

**By analogy**, this data is like a massive photo album recording the Earth's weather changes in 4D (time and space). By looking at these records, the AI learned patterns on its own, such as "when the air flows like this, a storm like this comes a few days later." In particular, because GenCast examines the Earth by dividing it into a **very dense resolution of 0.25 degrees (a precision that sees an area the size of thousands of soccer fields as a single point)**, it can capture even very subtle weather changes. [GenCast as a High-Resolution (0.25°) AI Ensemble Model...](https://hub.baai.ac.cn/view/41562)

## Current Status: How accurate is it?

The performance figures are even more surprising. According to Google DeepMind's announcement, GenCast went head-to-head with ECMWF's 'ENS' model, one of the world's best traditional forecasting models. As a result, it outperformed the existing model with a **staggering 97.2% probability** in both daily weather and extreme weather predictions. [Google Reveals New A.I. Model That Predicts Weather Better Than the Best Traditional Forecasts](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/google-reveals-new-ai-model-that-predicts-weather-better-than-the-best-traditional-forecasts-180985608/)

Particularly noteworthy is the **'15-day advance prediction.'** With existing technology, the reliability of forecasts drops sharply beyond 10 days, but GenCast identified risk factors 15 days ahead with accuracy exceeding national standards.
