Imagine this. It's a busy morning, and you hand over a massive meeting document of over 50 pages to your AI assistant, saying, "Summarize the key points for me." In the past, the AI tools we used often stumbled when processing such vast information, losing content mid-way or becoming frustratedly slow. But now, the situation seems to have changed completely. Anthropic unveiled the much more powerful artificial intelligence models, 'Claude Fable 5.1' and 'Claude Mythos 5.1', on September 1st [Source 20](https://letsdatascience.com/news/anthropic-releases-claude-fable-51-and-mythos-51-0e33494c).

This update goes beyond a mere incremental improvement in AI intelligence; it appears set to make the way we utilize AI in our daily lives smarter and more efficient.

## Why It Matters

What if the AI assistant we use every day could master both 'comprehension' and 'speed' simultaneously? This is particularly welcome news for those who primarily perform knowledge-based tasks like coding or writing complex reports. The newly released Claude Fable 5.1 is designed to allow general users to utilize the full potential of AI more safely and efficiently [Source 15](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5), [Source 20](https://letsdatascience.com/news/anthropic-releases-claude-fable-51-and-mythos-51-0e33494c).

The true value of AI goes beyond just writing well. The key lies in the ability to grasp long documents at once and concentrate precisely on the situation the user desires. The capability to process vast amounts of information simultaneously while allowing us to adjust the AI's 'power' as we want during conversation is the most powerful weapon this model possesses [Source 18](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/fable-5-1/whats-new-fable-5-1), [Source 20](https://letsdatascience.com/news/anthropic-releases-claude-fable-51-and-mythos-51-0e33494c).

## The Explainer

To use an analogy for the core technology of the Claude 5.1 series, it is like a **'smart filter in a photo app.'**

Just as we select the optimal filter for our photos depending on the situation, Claude Fable 5.1 allows users to adjust the AI's effort level in real-time during a conversation [Source 18](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/fable-5-1/whats-new-fable-5-1). When you need to write complex, error-free code, you can turn on 'maximum concentration mode' to have the AI work meticulously; for repetitive tasks like simple summaries or checking schedules, you can set it to 'normal mode' to handle them lightly and quickly.

Simply put, whereas you used to have to input new commands every time you gave instructions to the AI, you can now freely command the AI's capabilities without breaking the context of the conversation [Source 18](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/fable-5-1/whats-new-fable-5-1).

Furthermore, the context window (the amount of information the AI can remember and analyze at once) reaches a massive 1 million tokens [Source 17](https://x.com/i/trending/2094590203176571209), [Source 20](https://letsdatascience.com/news/anthropic-releases-claude-fable-51-and-mythos-51-0e33494c). This means that even if you provide materials equivalent to dozens of books, the AI will not miss the overall context and will understand it thoroughly. It's essentially like having a personal assistant with incredible memory.

## Where We Stand

Anthropic currently operates two main versions of the model:

*   **Claude Fable 5.1**: A model that the general public can use safely. It is equipped with Safety Classifiers that prevent the generation of harmful information, allowing for peace of mind in daily tasks [Source 14](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/fable-5/introducing-claude-fable-5-and-claude-mythos-5), [Source 20](https://letsdatascience.com/news/anthropic-releases-claude-fable-51-and-mythos-51-0e33494c).
*   **Claude Mythos 5.1**: A model specially designed for high-level professional tasks. It is currently provided on a limited basis to specific targets through trusted-access programs [Source 20](https://letsdatascience.com/news/anthropic-releases-claude-fable-51-and-mythos-51-0e33494c).

## What's Next

Moving forward, AI will evolve beyond just getting smarter toward 'understanding user intent more deeply.' In particular, this beta feature of adjusting task intensity during conversation will serve as an important milestone in opening the era of 'Agents' (programs that autonomously perform tasks)—where AI will figure out the difficulty of a task and exert concentration on its own without us needing to be specific [Source 12](https://thecode.media/vyshla-claude-fable-51-mestami-v-2-raza-moshnee-predshestvennika/), [Source 18](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/fable-5-1/whats-new-fable-5-1). We are about to welcome a convenient daily life where we achieve better results with less effort.
