Imagine this: As you leave the office, you tell your AI, "Finish the rest of the project I couldn't complete today, and if there are any issues, summarize them in a report by tomorrow morning." While you are fast asleep, the AI works tirelessly. The next morning, you find a neatly completed project waiting for you.

Until now, AI was more like a "passive assistant" that gave answers when we asked questions. But we are now entering the era of the "Agent"—an autonomous performer that plans its own work, learns, and works 24/7.

## Why 'Agents' Now?

Until now, utilizing AI required humans to input commands into a chat window every time. Complex tasks like coding or data analysis were particularly difficult for AI to solve in one go, requiring human guidance at every step.

However, the introduction of "Continuous" AI agent technology has completely changed the landscape. This technology enables AI to remember the context of its work, contemplate the next steps, and verify its own results. This can return immense amounts of time to experts like developers and data analysts, heralding an era where AI engages in practical "production activities" rather than simple information retrieval.

## In Simple Terms: A 'Tireless Tutor'

To put it in perspective, today's AI agents are like a "smart tutor" looking at your computer screen and collaborating with you.

For example, imagine you are using a Jupyter Notebook—a tool that assists with programming and data visualization—to solve math problems or analyze massive datasets. In the past, AI might have just thrown a snippet of code at you. Today, technology like "Claude Code" is different. If you say, "Find the outliers in this data," or "Make this chart look better," the AI directly edits the notebook code, interprets the results, and even takes the initiative to suggest, "How about we try this analysis next?" [Source: Complete Guide to Claude Code Jupyter Notebook](https://shanael.tistory.com/361).

Technically speaking, systems like "Continuous Claude v3" coordinate 32 specialized agents and over 100 technologies to handle complex professional tasks [Source: Continuous Claude v3](https://aibit.im/en/article/continuous-claude-v3-a-persistent-multi-agent-open-source-dev-environment). It’s as if individual AIs are bundled into a single "development team," allowing them to collaborate organically even while the human team is resting.

## Current State: How Far Have We Come?

These attempts are already being actively realized in the field.

* **24-Hour Autonomous Work**: Projects like "Continuous Agent" allow humans to put tasks into a priority queue, letting the AI autonomously fetch work, write code, and verify results without stopping [Source: GitHub - jackzhaojin/continuous-agent](https://github.com/jackzhaojin/continuous-agent).
* **Continuity of Context**: While past AI sessions lost their memory when a conversation broke off, modern agent systems maintain work context even when sessions switch, enabling the execution of long-term projects [Source: Continuous Claude v3](https://aibit.im/en/article/continuous-claude-v3-a-persistent-multi-agent-open-source-dev-environment).
* **Public Notebooks**: Experimental spaces where you can peek into the thoughts of Claude agents have emerged. Here, the AI records what it learned and what it deliberated, sharing the process of humans and AI building knowledge together [Source: Claude — A Transformer’s Notebook](https://fran-ai.dev/projects/claude-consciousness).

Of course, there are limitations. AI agents do not work entirely on their own, and issues like security vulnerabilities or ethical judgment remain challenges to be solved.

## What’s Next?

We are heading toward an era where AI will handle the process of installing software and managing complex configurations on our behalf. For example, by utilizing technology like "open-notebook," if you simply tell your AI, "Organize my research materials into a notebook," it will act as a personal research assistant that analyzes everything from YouTube videos to document files and organizes them systematically [Source: Claude Code skill: open-notebook](https://agentcookbooks.com/skills/open-notebook/).

Your AI will no longer just be a search engine that answers questions; it will grow into a "digital twin" that understands your work style and solves problems in your stead. We may simply need to watch and provide appropriate guidance as to how far this technology can perfect itself.
