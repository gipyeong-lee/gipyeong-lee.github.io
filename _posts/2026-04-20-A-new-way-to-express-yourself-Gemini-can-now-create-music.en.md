On a lazy afternoon, looking at the rain pitter-pattering outside the window, has a melody ever suddenly flashed through your mind? Or have you ever wanted to create a unique short congratulatory song as a birthday gift for a loved one or a dear friend?

In the past, this would have required studying complex composition programs all night or being proficient with an instrument. But the world has changed. Now, a smartphone in your pocket is all you need. Google has announced that it has integrated **'Lyria 3'**, its most advanced music generation model to date, into its AI assistant Gemini. [A new way to express yourself: Gemini can now create music](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/lyria-3/) Now, we can have our own emotionally resonant music with just a few lines of text or a single photo.

## Why is this important to us?

Until now, you've likely seen AI write text, solve difficult math problems, and create stunning paintings. However, creating high-quality 'songs' that include music, lyrics, and vocals has been a very high technical barrier. This is because it requires implementing the harmony of instruments, precision of rhythm, and above all, a voice that touches human emotions.

The changes this update will bring to our lives can be summarized into three main points:

1. **Democratization of Creation (Everyone becomes a composer)**: It’s okay if you don’t know music theory or can't play a single instrument. You can just enter a request as if talking to a friend, like \"Create an exciting 8-bit game music for me.\" The barrier to creation has completely crumbled. [Lyria — Gemini AI music & song generator](https://gemini.google/overview/music-generation/)
2. **A New Means of Self-Expression (Speak through music)**: Even when posting a photo on Instagram, you can now express your mood or the atmosphere of the photo directly through music, rather than just using background music created by others. [A new way to express yourself: Gemini can now create music](https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/lifestyle/2026/02/19/a-new-way-to-express-yourself-gemini-can-now-create-music/)
3. **Professional-Level Sound (A treat for your ears)**: Lyria 3 supports 'High-fidelity' (high-quality sound very similar to the original). It provides clean sound that feels like it was made in a recording studio, rather than noisy, mechanical sounds. [Lyria 3 in Gemini: How AI Music Works](https://aigptjournal.com/create/music/lyria-3-gemini-ai-music/)

## Easy Understanding: How does Lyria 3 work?

To use a metaphor, Lyria 3 is a **'genius composer and singer who reads your mind and performs on the spot.'**

Imagine this: You show Gemini a beautiful sunset photo you took on your way home from work yesterday. Then you ask, \"Create a calm acoustic song that matches this photo.\" **Simply put**, the AI analyzes the warm orange colors and the tranquil atmosphere in the photo as if it were a person. It then composes a guitar melody (accompaniment) that fits that mood perfectly, writes emotional lyrics, sings the song itself, and completes a 30-second track in an instant. [Create music with Gemini: Lyria 3 turns prompts into tracks](https://www.gend.co/blog/gemini-create-music-lyria-3/)

Behind this magical process are some incredible technologies:

- **Combining Eyes and Ears (Multimodal Functionality)**: It creates music inspired not only by text prompts (commands or descriptions given to the AI) but also by photos or videos. It converts visual information into auditory information. [A new way to express yourself: Gemini can now create music](https://aionpulse.com/news/article/01KHRR4BXBAGT923A7CW3JH9Z4/)
- **Fantastic Harmony of Lyrics and Vocals**: It's more than just providing background music (BGM). It can include natural-sounding vocals that feel like a real person breathing and singing, along with context-appropriate lyrics. [Lyria — Gemini AI music & song generator](https://gemini.google/overview/music-generation/)
- **CD-Quality Clean Sound**: Lyria 3 generates music in 44.1 kHz stereo audio. This means **clarity at the level of CD quality** that we commonly hear, and you can clearly feel the difference when listening with earphones. [Generate music with Lyria 3 | Gemini API | Google AI for Developers](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/music-generation/)

Music created this way shows a natural flow between notes, as if it were meticulously crafted by a professional. [“A fun, unique way to express yourself”: Google adds AI music creation ...](https://www.musicradar.com/music-tech/a-fun-unique-way-to-express-yourself-google-adds-ai-music-creation-app-lyria-3-to-its-gemini-assistant)

## Current Situation: Can I make my song right now?

Currently, Lyria 3 is being offered as a beta service (a test service before official release) within the Gemini app. Here are a few points to keep in mind before trying it out yourself:

- **The Beauty of 30 Seconds**: Currently, the length of generated tracks is limited to **30 seconds** at a time. [Gemini Now Lets You Generate AI Music for Free | Lifehacker](https://lifehacker.com/tech/gemini-now-lets-you-generate-ai-music-for-free) This is because the focus is on capturing and expressing small moments of daily life rather than creating grand symphonies. [A new way to express yourself: Gemini can now create music](https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/lifestyle/2026/02/19/a-new-way-to-express-yourself-gemini-can-now-create-music/)
- **Eligibility and Language**: It is available only to adult users aged 18 and over. Starting with 8 languages including Korean, it is expected to gradually expand worldwide. [Lyria 3 in Gemini: How AI Music Works](https://aigptjournal.com/create/music/lyria-3-gemini-ai-music/)
- **Opportunity Open to Everyone**: Basically, all Gemini users can create music for free, but paid subscribers (such as Plus or Pro) have higher usage limits, allowing them to create more often and more tracks. [Gemini now lets you generate AI music for free - MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/technology/gemini-now-lets-you-generate-ai-music-for-free/ar-AA1WDKdQ)
- **Album Art for Visual Pleasure**: Once a song is completed, it also creates a cool cover image that matches the mood of the music. It will look much more impressive when you share it with friends, right? [A new way to express yourself: Gemini can now create music](https://aionpulse.com/news/article/01KHRR4BXBAGT923A7CW3JH9Z4/)
