Imagine this: This morning, you told your AI assistant, "Summarize last week's meeting, email it to the team, and let me know in advance if there are any conflicts with next week's schedule." The AI doesn't just search; it understands the context of the meeting, drafts the email in your usual tone, and perfectly catches complex scheduling conflicts. It's as if you had a seasoned assistant sitting at the desk next to you.

There is some surprising news that has recently shaken the global tech industry. Jensen Huang, the CEO of Nvidia, the synonym for AI chips, declared on his X (formerly Twitter) account that "AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) has arrived" [[Source 3](https://news.aibase.com/news/30856), [Source 4](https://www.cnbctv18.com/technology/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-says-agi-has-arrived-credits-openais-gpt-6-astra-19985159.htm)]. The protagonist he pointed to is none other than OpenAI's latest artificial intelligence model, 'GPT-6 Astra' [[Source 1](https://www.aol.com/articles/nvidias-jensen-huang-says-agi-225932000.html), [Source 12](https://www.timesnownews.com/technology-science/agi-has-arrived-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-makes-massive-claim-about-openais-gpt-6-astra-article-156100828)].

## Why is this important?

The declaration that "AGI has arrived" goes beyond simply meaning a new product is out. AGI (Artificial General Intelligence, an AI capable of performing any intellectual task at or above human level) is on a completely different level from existing AI specialized in specific fields. Simply put, it means we are gaining a "thinking colleague" that learns, reasons, and solves complex problems on its own, rather than a "simple tool" like the calculators or translators we encounter in daily life.

To use an analogy, if existing AI was an "honor student who is only good at certain subjects," AGI is becoming a "genius all-rounder" who integrates human-like reasoning across all fields, including humanities, science, and the arts. If AGI is truly realized, the way we work will change completely. It is because AI can perform at a level comparable to humans in many work areas, such as programming, data analysis, and creative writing. This is an opportunity to explosively increase individual productivity, and at the same time, it is an event that raises fundamental questions about jobs and social structures.

## Easy to Understand: How to Grow an AI's 'Brain'

How was this massive AI created? It's easy to understand with this analogy. Just as we raise a child by teaching them various subjects like math, language, and etiquette, AI also needs to be trained on vast amounts of data.

The recently released GPT-6 Astra was trained using an enormous scale of hardware known as Nvidia's 'Grace Blackwell NVLink72' system, consisting of over 100,000 units [[Source 2](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/nvidia-s-jensen-huang-claims-agi-has-arrived-citing-openai-s-new-model), [Source 12](https://www.timesnownews.com/technology-science/agi-has-arrived-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-makes-massive-claim-about-openais-gpt-6-astra-article-156100828)]. Initially, it was expected that about 300,000 units would be needed, but they managed to finish training with about 100,000 units by increasing technical efficiency [[Source 2](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/nvidia-s-jensen-huang-claims-agi-has-arrived-citing-openai-s-new-model)].

Imagine this hardware as a 'camera filter for the latest smartphone.' Just as a more sophisticated filter makes a photo more vivid and beautiful, Nvidia's hardware acted as a highly sophisticated 'filter' that helps AI grasp subtle patterns and contexts within vast data more accurately, just like a human.

## Current Situation: 'True' AGI or Marketing?

However, there is a point we must address here. Jensen Huang confidently declared that AGI has arrived, but the developer, OpenAI, has never officially called GPT-6 Astra 'AGI' [[Source 10](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/jensen-huang-says-arrived-213937665.html?fr=sycsrp_catchall), [Source 16](https://www.gncrypto.news/news/huang-openai-gpt-6-astra-agi-arrived/)]. OpenAI's leaders have been extremely cautious about referring to their models as AGI.

Opinions are sharply divided among experts as well. Some see Jensen Huang's remarks as praise for innovative achievements, while others take a cautious stance, saying it is just a repetition of the industry's 'AGI-adjacent' expressions that have existed in the past [[Source 11](https://explainx.ai/blog/jensen-huang-agi-has-arrived-gpt-6-astra-nvidia-september-2026), [Source 14](https://www.digit.in/news/general/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-says-agi-has-arrived-credits-openais-gpt-6-astra-for-it.html)]. A heated discussion is underway about whether AI has truly reached human-level intelligence or if it is still in the process of moving toward that direction [[Source 14](https://www.digit.in/news/general/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-says-agi-has-arrived-credits-openais-gpt-6-astra-for-it.html)].

## What will happen in the future?

What matters is the 'speed' of technology rather than the definition of terms. What Jensen Huang showed through this declaration is the fact that artificial intelligence is getting very close to human intelligence levels, and the technical maturity of the hardware and software to support it is higher than ever before.

In the future, we will see AI frequently go beyond simply searching for information to understand complex situations and making real-time judgments. In a world where today's announced technology becomes tomorrow's taken-for-granted daily life, we are now at a point where we need to think about how to treat and utilize our 'smart colleagues' called AI.

## MindTickleBytes AI Reporter's View
Jensen Huang's declaration is a celebration of the technological pinnacle and, at the same time, symbolically presents the destination the AI industry aims to reach. While more scientific verification is needed to attach the AGI label, one thing is certain: we have entered an era where we coexist with intelligent tools of a dimension completely different from before. How we will face the changes that will unfold before us will be a task for all of us.
