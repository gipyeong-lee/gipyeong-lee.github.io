Imagine arriving at the office in the morning and turning on your computer. There are dozens of tasks to handle today. But instead of clicking, coding, and summarizing documents yourself, your "digital assistant" has already started all of it. We have entered an era that goes beyond just understanding "do this," into an era where AI thinks for itself and operates computers to finish the work. This is the story of "Claude Agent."

### Why It Matters

Until now, the AI we have known was primarily a "smart chatbot." It answered questions and wrote text. But now, AI is transforming from a "tool" into a "working colleague." Claude Agent goes beyond providing information; it reasons through complex problems autonomously and completes tasks on behalf of the user. [Source: AI agents | Claude by Anthropic](https://claude.com/solutions/agents), [Source: Introducing Claude 4 \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-4)

This means more than just faster work speeds; it means humans can shift away from repetitive, tedious tasks and focus on more creative endeavors. If AI takes care of your complex work, you can use that time to generate new ideas or immerse yourself in human interaction.

### The Explainer: The New Employee Analogy

To understand Claude Agent, you need to know the concepts of "Skill" and "Context." [Source: [AI Utilization] Understanding the Basic Structure of Claude Code — Agent · Skill · Context Concepts Explained](https://tech.ktcloud.com/entry/2026-04-ktcloud-claude-agent-ai활용-개념정리)

Think of it this way: what if you hired a new employee? To make that new employee work well, they need three things:

1. **Agent**: The new employee themselves. They are the subject that assesses the situation and acts on their own.
2. **Skill**: The technologies they possess. These are tools to perform specific tasks, such as "using Excel," "writing emails," or "creating report templates." [Source: [AI] How to Use Claude Skills (Claude Skills, Agent Skill)](https://innovation123.tistory.com/296)
3. **Context**: The "rules of our company" that this employee must refer to when working, such as our company's workflow and project history.

Claude Agent combines these three elements to operate the computer for you. It is like the perfect colleague who, without you needing to watch over their shoulder, uses provided skills and adheres to company rules (context) to handle tasks autonomously. [Source: Anthropic says Claude can now use your computer to finish ...](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/24/anthropic-claude-ai-agent-use-computer-finish-tasks.html)

### Where We Stand

Claude Agent is already active in many fields, changing the workplace.

*   **Software Development**: Developers now use Claude Agent to assign Jira tasks and receive drafts for Pull Requests automatically. [Source: Introducing Claude Agent for Jira - Inside Atlassian](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/company-news/claude-agent-for-jira) It is also integrated into JetBrains IDEs to support coding tasks. [Source: Introducing Claude Agent in JetBrains IDEs - The JetBrains Blog](https://blog.jetbrains.com/ai/2025/09/introducing-claude-agent-in-jetbrains-ides/)
*   **Daily Work Automation**: Since March 2026, it has been capable of operating a user's computer directly to handle repetitive tasks like clicking and typing. [Source: Anthropic says Claude can now use your computer to finish ...](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/24/anthropic-claude-ai-agent-use-computer-finish-tasks.html)
*   **Enterprise Environments**: Claude models can also be used in Microsoft 365 Copilot Studio, allowing companies to create custom agents tailored to their needs. [Source: Claude is now available in Microsoft 365 Copilot | Claude](https://claude.com/blog/claude-now-available-in-microsoft-365-copilot)

There are limitations, of course. Research shows that stuffing too many skills and instructions into an agent at once can actually cause its performance to drop by about 40%. [Source: Agent Skill Open Standard](https://goddaehee.tistory.com/553) To delegate work efficiently, it is crucial to properly categorize and provide relevant skills. [Source: A Closer Look at Claude Agent Skills](https://junheedot.tistory.com/entry/Claude-Agent-Skills-톺아보기-기술-블로그-글쓰기-도우미-Skill-만들기)

### What's Next

The AI of the future will be more than just a "smart conversational partner"; it will become an "executor that understands my intent." Claude Agent will become even more refined and will solve increasingly complex, long-term tasks on its own. [Source: Release notes | Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12138966-release-notes)

We will spend more time deciding "what problems to solve" rather than "how to work." While AI clicks through your computer and organizes your files, you should focus on the valuable thinking that only you can do. That will be the greatest gift of the agent era.
