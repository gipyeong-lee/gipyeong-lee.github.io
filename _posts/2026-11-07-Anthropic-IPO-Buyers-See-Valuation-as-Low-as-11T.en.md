Imagine you are standing in line to buy tickets for a concert by a popular singer you have been eyeing. Initially, there were rumors that ticket prices would skyrocket to 2 million won. However, as the performance date approaches and the surrounding atmosphere settles, people have started revising their expectations, saying, "Well, wouldn't 1 million won be reasonable?" The current market sentiment surrounding Anthropic's Initial Public Offering (IPO) is exactly like this.

Anthropic is one of the most notable companies in the AI industry today. However, ahead of its IPO (issuing shares publicly to outside investors to go public), news is circulating that the market atmosphere is not what it used to be. The expectation that its corporate value would reach $2 trillion is now being adjusted down to the $1 trillion level. What exactly has changed the market's mind?

### Why does this matter?

Anthropic's listing is more than just a company debuting on the stock market. It is a kind of 'report card' that will determine whether the massive amount of investment flowing into current AI technology can actually prove its practical value.

Investors have moved away from the vague expectation that "AI will change the world" and have started asking the realistic question, "So, how much can this company actually earn?" Therefore, the outcome of this IPO will be an important metric that determines the scale and direction of capital flowing throughout the AI industry. [Source: Anthropic's IPO Reaction Is Worse Than You Think - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q-5Lpcp7Pj0)

### In simple terms: The 'expensive tuition' of AI companies

Developing and operating AI models is like launching a massive rocket. Just as enormous fuel is required to send a rocket into space, Anthropic is spending astronomical computing costs to research and operate its AI.

Metaphorically, it is like forcing a toddler who has just taken their first steps to undergo high-intensity training equivalent to that of an Olympic athlete. While the technology is growing dazzlingly fast, the operating costs (operating losses) that must be borne are equally massive. In fact, Anthropic recorded an operating loss of $693 million in the first half of 2026 alone. [Source: Anthropic IPO filing reveals rapid growth as investors weigh an 80 billion loss](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/anthropic-ipo-filing-reveals-rapid-growth-as-investors-weigh-an-80-billion-loss-202610270748)

Investors, while acknowledging Anthropic's rapid revenue growth, are carefully weighing when these massive losses and cost structures will convert into stable profits. [Source: We Finally Got Anthropic’s S-1. There’s One Huge Problem. - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hi7ikOyTHa8)

### Where do things stand?

Anthropic submitted its S-1, the official registration document for listing, last October. [Source: Anthropic IPO filing reveals rapid growth as investors weigh an 80 billion loss](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/anthropic-ipo-filing-reveals-rapid-growth-as-investors-weigh-an-80-billion-loss-202610270748) Initially, the market expected the company to be valued between $1.5 trillion and $2 trillion when entering the Nasdaq. [Source: Anthropic's $1.5 Trillion IPO Valuation Is Facing Pushback](https://www.ibtimes.com/anthropics-15-trillion-ipo-valuation-facing-pushback-november-listing-could-delayed-3809491)

However, investor sentiment has withered due to the deepening sell-off in the broader tech market recently. [Source: Is Anthropic About To Delay The Biggest IPO... | ZeroHedge](https://www.zerohedge.com/ai/anthropic-about-delay-biggest-ipo-ever) Many potential investors are now discussing a valuation in the $1.2 trillion to $1.3 trillion range, and some are raising the possibility of it dropping to the $1 trillion level. [Source: Anthropic IPO buyers see valuation down as low as 1.2 trillion](https://www.moneycontrol.com/world/anthropic-ipo-buyers-see-valuation-down-as-low-as-1-2-trillion-article-14073758.html)

Interestingly, despite this, Anthropic is being evaluated at a relatively higher value than its competitor, OpenAI. While Anthropic has filed to go public with a valuation of approximately $965 billion, OpenAI is being valued at around $852 billion. The fact that this evaluation has emerged even though Anthropic focuses on B2B services, compared to OpenAI's overwhelming number of individual users, is evidence that the market holds Anthropic's technical potential in such high regard.
