Imagine this: You wake up in the morning and say to your smartphone AI, "How's the weather today?" or "Summarize my meeting schedule," and the response appears in almost 0.1 seconds. Surprisingly, the cost of using this service is hundreds of times cheaper than before. Does it sound like a dream? A new AI model, 'Jev,' recently introduced in the AI industry, has declared that it will make these changes a reality.

## Why It Matters

Until now, the powerful artificial intelligence (AI) we use, the so-called 'frontier models' (cutting-edge large language models), have always had two major challenges: 'cost' and 'speed.'

To put it simply, existing smart AIs were like operating complex machines in a giant factory 24 hours a day. Every time you asked a question, massive amounts of electricity and computing resources were consumed. Because of this, service fees were inevitably expensive, and there were many cases where we had to wait a long time to get an answer.

However, if costs become up to 400 times cheaper and speeds become 200 times faster, as Jev suggests, AI will no longer be a 'special service to be used sparingly' in our daily lives, but a tool that we use anytime, anywhere, like the air we breathe. It has become possible to provide opportunities for developers to implement more diverse and free services, and provide users with a much more immediate AI experience. [Source 1, Source 7]

## The Explainer

How does Jev achieve such amazing efficiency? Simply put, it's because it changed AI's 'way of thinking.'

It's easy to understand with an analogy. If existing cutting-edge AIs were like 'model students' who logically think and review from beginning to end to solve difficult math problems, Jev is like an 'experienced expert' who instantly retrieves the knowledge they already have to answer repetitive or clearly structured questions.

In the industry, this is called 'System One' querying. [Source 4] Just as in neuroscience, where the process by which humans make instinctive and quick judgments without deep thought is called 'System 1,' AI also does not have to spend much time on structured questions where complex logical processes can be skipped, and immediately outputs results. As a result, Jev has maintained the level of 'frontier artificial intelligence' that requires complex thinking processes, while being able to process responses at a much faster speed. [Source 4]

## Where We Stand

Currently, Jev is receiving hot attention from the industry as a 'new frontier model.' [Source 1, Source 3] However, there are also points to be aware of when looking at market reactions. There are many opinions that one should not blindly believe that it is 200 times better than all AIs simply by looking at the speed figures.

For example, directly comparing the speed of complex tasks that require logical thinking (Chain-of-thought, the process where AI thinks by reasoning step-by-step on its own) with simple command processing speed can be like comparing 'apples and oranges.' [Source 1] If AI skips complex analysis and only performs simple structured tasks, it is bound to be faster. Therefore, Jev is currently evaluated not as an absolute No. 1 in all aspects, but as a very innovative option that shows overwhelming efficiency in 'specific types of tasks.'

## What's Next

The future AI market will move in earnest to a competition of 'who is more efficient' beyond simply 'who is smarter.' As models specialized in specific tasks like Jev continue to emerge, companies will more actively use 'AI routing' (a method of automatically selecting and using the optimal AI model as needed) to choose the most suitable and economical model according to the type or difficulty of the question. [Source 9]

We will soon meet immediate AI assistants without latency at every touchpoint of our daily lives, including smartphones, home appliances, and business software. It is a time when we look forward to what kind of amazing changes the 'era of efficiency' opened by Jev will bring to our lives.
