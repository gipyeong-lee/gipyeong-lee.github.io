Imagine this: there is a complex assignment that even the world's top mathematicians have struggled to solve for decades, or nearly 100 years. What if someone—or something—solved it in just a few days? And what if that "something" wasn't a person, but an artificial intelligence (AI)?

This is exactly what has been happening in the AI industry recently. Surprising news has emerged that "Millennium Problems," long considered the massive walls of mathematics, are being conquered one by one by artificial intelligence. [Source 1](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/09/business/openai-millennium-problems-navier-stokes-hnk)

## Why is this important?

The "Millennium Prize Problems" are seven of the most difficult and significant unsolved challenges in mathematics, selected in the year 2000. [Source 6](https://abc17news.com/money/cnn-business-consumer/2026/09/09/openai-says-it-has-solved-one-of-maths-millennium-problems/) These problems are evaluated as the "Holy Grail," testing the intellectual limits of humanity far beyond mere math problems.

This event is a major milestone that could completely shake up how we view AI. It is because artificial intelligence has moved beyond understanding human language and creating art; it has begun to tackle mathematical challenges that describe the laws of the universe and nature. Some experts evaluate that AI now shows it can dramatically expand human intellectual capacity as a practical "research partner," rather than just a simple tool. [Source 10](https://www.thenationalherald.com/openai-says-ai-has-solved-a-1-million-millennium-problem-one-of-mathematics-greatest-unsolved-problems/)

## Understanding it Simply: An Analogy

Put simply, if what we have demanded of AI until now was the role of an "assistant that organizes encyclopedia content," it has now begun to show the potential as a "genius scientist's tool" that explores truths humanity hasn't reached in centuries.

The "Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness problem" solved by OpenAI's AI this time concerns a highly complex equation that explains the flow of fluids like water and air. [Source 9](https://www.livemint.com/global/openai-says-it-has-solved-a-millennium-prize-problem-a-holy-grail-of-math-11788915936036.html)

Think of the process of solving complex mathematical equations as a "giant puzzle" made of tens of millions of pieces. While human mathematicians have been fitting these puzzle pieces together one by one for decades, AI chose the method of simulating countless possibilities at ultra-high speeds. OpenAI announced that it solved this 90-year-old challenge in just 88 hours. [Source 3](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cy7zygy3rl2o) Although there is cautious evaluation that this result fulfills only a portion of the entire proof process, the consensus is that it is a monumental achievement that will remain in human history. [Source 3](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cy7zygy3rl2o)

## Current Situation

The even more surprising news is that this is not the end. Immediately after solving the Navier-Stokes problem, OpenAI officially stated that it had already made "substantial progress" on another Millennium Problem. [Source 2](https://officechai.com/ai/after-navier-stokes-openai-says-it-has-made-substantial-progress-on-another-millenium-prize-problem/)

The global mathematics and science communities are deeply stirred by this news. With the rapid development of artificial intelligence, voices are rising that the very way humans challenge mathematical problems must completely change in the future. [Source 15](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-11/racing-to-solve-maths-problems-pointless-tristan-buckmaster-says/107141628)

## What Happens Next?

Experts predict that this achievement will not stop at just solving math problems.

First, there will be **leaps in foundational science**. Mathematics is the root of all science, including physics, engineering, and biology. By AI solving these challenges, that technology can dramatically increase accuracy in fields directly linked to our daily lives, such as drug development, more precise weather forecasting, and safer airplane design.

Second, the **role of AI will evolve**. We will now define AI not just as a simple information retrieval tool, but as a "knowledge partner" with whom we explore new knowledge together.

Attention is now focused on which Millennium Problem OpenAI will target next, and how these discoveries of digital intelligence will change the research environment for humanity.
