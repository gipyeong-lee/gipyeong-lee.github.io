When you wake up in the morning, the artificial intelligence (AI) in your smartphone tells you today's weather, and during work, it summarizes complex emails in an instant. AI has now become an inseparable part of our lives. But did you know that this grand era of AI actually began with a dream in a small non-profit research lab in 2015?

Today, I would like to share the untold story of the birth of 'OpenAI', which stands at the pinnacle of current AI technology, and the mindset with which it prepared to change the world.

## Why does this matter?

We are currently standing at the threshold of a massive shift called 'Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)'. AGI refers to AI that perfectly mimics human intellectual capabilities and can perform most of the intellectual tasks that a human can do. The very reason OpenAI was founded was to safely develop this AGI for the benefit of humanity [Source: OpenAI - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI).

This is important for the general public because technology is becoming a tool that goes beyond simply creating 'smart machines' to determining our jobs, our way of life, and even our safety. OpenAI began with the philosophy that technology with such immense power should not be monopolized by a few, but rather used for the benefit of all humanity [Source: OpenAI, founded 2015: ChatGPT, GPT, Sam Altman — IT History](https://history.itlibra.com/en/organizations/openai).

## Easy to understand: A laboratory solving an AI puzzle

To use a metaphor, the beginning of OpenAI is like a basic science laboratory that gathered to solve a very difficult 'puzzle'.

Imagine this. The brightest minds from around the world have gathered to solve a very difficult puzzle that will determine the future of humanity. The finished version of that puzzle is 'artificial intelligence that benefits all of humanity'. In December 2015, renowned tech leaders like Sam Altman, Elon Musk, Ilya Sutskever, and Greg Brockman joined forces to complete this puzzle [Source: OpenAI, founded 2015: ChatGPT, GPT, Sam Altman — IT History](https://history.itlibra.com/en/organizations/openai). They secured a staggering $1 billion in commitments to begin full-scale research [Source: Full History Of OpenAI In Timeline From 2015 - Popular Timelines](https://populartimelines.com/timeline/OpenAI/full).

In simple terms, they started as a 'non-profit organization' whose primary goal was not to make money from the beginning. While typical companies prioritize profit generation, they believed that the mission of 'developing safe AGI' was more valuable. By doing so, they demonstrated a strong will to ensure that AI technology would not be biased toward the interests of a specific company, and that the safety of the technology would be considered a top priority [Source: OpenAI - Namuwiki](https://namu.wiki/w/OpenAI).

## Current situation: From a non-profit lab to an AI giant

The OpenAI of today looks very different from its appearance in 2015. Headquartered in San Francisco, this company started as a non-profit research lab, transitioned to a structure combining for-profit models, and has grown at an incredible pace [Source: OpenAI - Namuwiki](https://namu.wiki/w/OpenAI); [Source: OpenAI History: From Research Lab to AI Giant - chatai.guide](https://chatai.guide/openai/openai-history/).

By releasing 'ChatGPT', which we are all familiar with, they opened the AI era wide, and now they have established themselves as a giant AI company building the future infrastructure of humanity beyond just a research institution. Since 2019, they have introduced a 'capped-profit' structure, simultaneously pursuing the original purpose of research and the practical necessity of commercial operation [Source: OpenAI History: From Research Lab to AI Giant - chatai.guide](https://chatai.guide/openai/openai-history/).

## What will happen in the future?

The path OpenAI will take in the future is still directed toward the goal at the time of its founding: 'Safe AGI'. Of course, the various concerns arising from the transformation from a non-profit organization to a giant corporation and the debates over technological safety are still continuing today.

In particular, as the area where AI thinks and judges for itself expands, the key task will be how to control it so that it does not harm humanity and how to align it with human values (alignment, ensuring AI conforms to human intent and ethics) [Source: OpenAI’s technology explained](https://cdn.openai.com/global-affairs/openai-technology-and-data-explainer.pdf). We must continue to keep a close eye on what positive impact the AI ethical guidelines and technological achievements presented by OpenAI will have on our actual lives in the future.

## MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Perspective

From starting with the ideals of a non-profit organization to becoming the current giant AI company, OpenAI has been constantly contemplating the 'harmony with humanity', which is more difficult than the technology itself. The speed of technological advancement is truly astonishing, but it is an important time when the wisdom of humans who use that technology must eventually catch up to the speed of technological development.
