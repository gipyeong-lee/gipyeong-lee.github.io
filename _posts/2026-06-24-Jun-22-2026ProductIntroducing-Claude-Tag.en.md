Imagine this: you are busy at work, and you need to ask your teammates to organize meeting materials via messenger, or you urgently need to request a code review. What if you had an ‘AI teammate’ in your messenger chat room who could help you with these tasks just like a human? Anthropic recently unveiled a new tool that turns this imagination into reality. It is ‘Claude Tag,’ an AI agent that operates within Slack, the collaboration messenger we use every day.

### Why is this important?

Every day, we check emails amid countless messenger notifications, modify complex code, and hunt for scattered sales figures. These simple, repetitive tasks often steal time from the planning or creative thinking we truly need to focus on. ‘Claude Tag’ is important precisely for this reason. Beyond being a chatbot that simply answers questions, it provides an assistant that directly ‘acts’ in the space where we are already working. [Source: Anthropic releases Claude Tag, a virtual employee that works within slack | Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/23/anthropic-claude-tag-virtual-employee-tool-slack/)

### Easy to understand: A smart new hire inside Slack

In short, Claude Tag can be likened to a ‘smart new hire working inside Slack.’

While a typical AI is like an encyclopedia that ‘answers when asked,’ Claude Tag can be directly invited to Slack channels as a teammate. If you tag @CLAUDE.md during a meeting and request, "Organize last month's sales figures," it analyzes the relevant data and provides the result in a neat format. [Source: Anthropic introduced “Claude Tag,” a new AI agent Slack integration. | The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/954921/anthropic-introduced-claude-tag-a-new-ai-agent-slack-integration)

Let’s use a more concrete analogy. When you apply a filter in a photo app, the AI automatically optimizes the colors without you having to adjust the values yourself, right? Claude Tag is the same. If you are a developer, you can request it to modify and merge code; if you are a planner, you can ask it to organize complex data. In other words, the AI automatically handles tasks within the familiar environment of Slack. [Source: Anthropic introduced “Claude Tag,” a new AI agent Slack integration. | The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/954921/anthropic-introduced-claude-tag-a-new-ai-agent-slack-integration)

### Current status

Currently, Claude Tag is provided as a ‘Research Preview’ version. It is not open to all users, but is available to Claude Enterprise and Claude Team customers first. [Source: Sorry, Slackbot. Claude is taking your job - Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2199619/anthropic-announces-claude-tag-for-slack/) [Source: Anthropic’s Claude Tag is learning your company, one Slack message at a time | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/23/anthropics-claude-tag-is-learning-your-company-one-slack-message-at-a-time/)

An interesting fact is that Anthropic is already actively using this tool internally. The Anthropic product team reportedly has Claude Tag write 65% of the total code they produce. They even used Claude Tag's own help to build Claude Tag itself. [Source: Anthropic launches Claude Tag enterprise collaborative tool for agentic workflows - 9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/23/anthropic-launches-cla-collaborative-tool-for-agentic-workflows/)

### What’s next?

Anthropic plans to gradually expand the scope of this ‘always-on’ AI teammate. [Source: Sorry, Slackbot. Claude is taking your job - Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2199619/anthropic-announces-claude-tag-for-slack/) If more companies adopt this in the future, the Slack messenger window will evolve beyond a simple communication space into a ‘digital office’ where AI and humans collaborate in real-time to complete work. The day when the hassle of visiting websites to ask questions and copying/pasting answers disappears is not far off.
