## Easy to Understand: 'Lyria 3', the AI Composer in Your Hand

The secret to Gemini's amazing ability lies in **Lyria 3**, a music generation model developed ambitiously by Google DeepMind. Google confidently introduces it as the most advanced music technology in its history. [Create custom soundtracks with Lyria 3 in the Gemini app](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/02/create-custom-soundtracks-with-lyria-3.html)

To use an analogy, Lyria 3 is like a **'genius music interpreter'** that transforms our clumsy descriptions into grand symphonies or sophisticated pop songs.

For instance, even if you make a quirky request like "Express the comical situation of a single sock wandering around the house to find its pair as an R&B slow jam," Lyria 3 doesn't panic. It accurately captures the humorous feeling of the situation and the characteristically slow, groovy rhythm of R&B, producing it like a real song. [A New Way to Express Yourself: Gemini Can Now Create Music](https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/lifestyle/2026/02/19/a-new-way-to-express-yourself-gemini-can-now-create-music/)

The biggest feature of Lyria 3 is **High-fidelity**. Here, high-fidelity refers to high-quality sound that is very similar to the original. It’s not a sound that feels forced together by a machine, but shows a smooth and natural flow, as if a real performer played the instruments and sang in a studio. [“A fun, unique way to express yourself”: Google adds AI music ...](https://www.musicradar.com/music-tech/a-fun-unique-way-to-express-yourself-google-adds-ai-music-creation-app-lyria-3-to-its-gemini-assistant)

A particularly interesting point is that it can compose music by recognizing not just text, but also photos or videos. If you show it a peaceful forest photo, it plays a serene melody; if you show it a photo of a complex city nightscape, it plays sophisticated City Pop. Just as a painter gets inspiration from a landscape to fill a canvas, Lyria 3 gains musical inspiration from visual materials. ## Current Status: What can you do, and what should you watch out for?

If you turn on the Gemini app right now, you can encounter the following features:

1.  **30 Seconds of Magic**: Currently, Gemini generates custom tracks of approximately 30 seconds at a time. While 30 seconds might feel short, it is more than enough time for creating ringtones or background music for short-form videos. [A new way to express yourself: Gemini can now create music](https://ainews.cx/articles/a-new-way-to-express-yourself-gemini-can-now-create-music)
2.  **Custom Cover Art**: It’s not just the music that comes out. The AI even designs a unique album cover (cover art) that perfectly suits the mood of the song you created. It can make you feel as if you’ve officially released a track. [A new way to express yourself: Gemini can now create music](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/lyria-3/)
3.  **Invisible Digital Stigma**: To prevent AI-generated music from being misused for fake news or infringing on copyrights, Google applied **SynthID** technology. This is a watermark that is completely inaudible to the human ear but can be immediately identified as AI-generated through a dedicated detection system. It serves as a kind of security device for a safe creative ecosystem. [A new way to express yourself: Gemini can now create music](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/lyria-3/)
4.  **Style Mimicry is Possible, Cloning is Prohibited**: Requests like "Sing with IU's voice" may be rejected. This is because acts that steal a specific singer's identity are restricted. However, it is possible to request "Create an acoustic pop song with a similar vibe to someone." It creates entirely new creations that capture the unique texture or genre characteristics of a specific artist. [Google adds music-generation capabilities to the Gemini app](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/18/google-adds-music-generation-capabilities-to-the-gemini-app/)

Currently, this feature is being provided as a **Beta** version (an experimental stage presented in advance to improve completeness before official release), and surprisingly, it is currently free to use. [Gemini now lets you generate AI music for free - MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/technology/gemini-now-lets-you-generate-ai-music-for-free/ar-AA1WDKdQ)
