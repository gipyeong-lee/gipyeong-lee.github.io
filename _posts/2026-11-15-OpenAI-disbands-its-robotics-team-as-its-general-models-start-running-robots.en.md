# Why OpenAI Disbanded Its Robotics Team: 'General Intelligence Models Now Run Robots Directly'

Imagine waking up in the morning, walking into the kitchen, and telling your smartphone or home assistant, "Make me a quick toast and coffee this morning." Without any separate complex programming, the home robot perfectly understands your voice and intent, takes bread out of the refrigerator, puts it in the toaster, and starts brewing coffee. This is the moment when the long-standing dream of robotics—the "thinking machine"—steps closer into our lives.

Yet, OpenAI, the most talked-about company in the artificial intelligence (AI) industry recently, has made a surprising announcement. It is dissolving its standalone robotics team and fully reassigning the researchers within it to core research groups and applied teams [OpenAI disbands its robotics team as its general models start running robots](https://fortune.com/2026/11/13/openai-disbands-robotics-team-as-its-general-models-start-running-robots/). The dedicated department building robots under Sam Altman's leadership has disappeared.

Does a company that claimed to lead the robotics era suddenly getting rid of its robotics team mean it is giving up on robotics research? Absolutely not. This move is instead a massive turning point showing that the very way AI handles robotics has fundamentally changed [Exclusive: OpenAI disbands its stand-alone robotics team as ...](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/articles/exclusive-openai-disbands-stand-alone-170530559.html).

## Why It Matters

Why is this news important for the general public? Until now, robotics technology had a form where the "brain" and the "body" operated completely separately. Building a single robotic arm required mechanical engineers, sensor specialists, and a dedicated team researching specialized AI exclusively for that robot. The costs were astronomical, and the pace of development was slow.

However, OpenAI's latest decision suggests that the general-purpose AI models we use on our smartphones or computers can now become the brain of a robot [OpenAI Disbands Its Robotics Team: What It Means — CASRAI](https://casrai.org/news/openai-disbands-robotics-team-2026). To use an analogy, it is like how playing the piano used to require a separate mechanical device to memorize sheet music, but now a genius musician who reads human thought can flexibly press the instrument's keys themselves. In other words, an era has opened where the giant general AI we casually use to chat with chatbots directly controls industrial robots in factories or service robots at home [OpenAI reorganises its robotics team as GPT-6 Astra runs robots](https://madrobot.blog/2026/11/13/openai-disbands-robotics-team-gpt-6-astra/).

The ripple effects this change will bring to our daily lives are enormous. First, it lays the groundwork for robots to break away from being "dumb machines" that only repeat specific tasks and evolve into "true assistants" that understand the context and situations spoken by humans and respond flexibly. Second, as fragmented robotics research merges with world-class large language and multimodal model research, the pace of robotics commercialization could accelerate beyond imagination [OpenAI Disbands Robotics Team After General Purpose Models ...](https://officechai.com/ai/openai-disbands-robotics-team-after-general-purpose-models-begin-running-robots/).

## The Explainer

To easily understand this event, we can compare it to a school education system. 

**In simple terms:** Past robotics research methods were like setting up a separate special-purpose school called the "Robotics Department" to educate students. Students at that school only learned how to make robots move, and it was difficult for them to deeply learn general common sense, language, or visual information of the general world. In contrast, OpenAI's new strategy is like handing the robot control stick directly to an all-around top-tier prodigy (a general-purpose frontier AI model) who has mastered all knowledge.

Initially, OpenAI researchers assumed that training videos based on technologies like the video generation model "Sora" would allow them into robotics [OpenAI disbands its standalone robotics team as its general ...](https://mlq.ai/news/openai-disbands-its-standalone-robotics-team-as-its-general-models-start-running-robots-fortune-reports/). However, through repeated research, it was proven that a general-purpose frontier model that understands human language, logic, vision, and hearing altogether is far more powerful in controlling robots than a domain-trapped video model [OpenAI disbands its standalone robotics team as its general ...](https://mlq.ai/news/openai-disbands-its-standalone-robotics-team-as-its-general-models-start-running-robots-fortune-reports/).

Let's break down the key concepts used here one by one:

- **General-purpose frontier model:** Refers to top-performance, cutting-edge AI models that learn all the world's data—including text, images, audio, and video—to think and judge like humans. (In this reorganization, models like **GPT-6 Astra** have typically begun directly operating robots [OpenAI reorganises its robotics team as GPT-6 Astra runs robots](https://madrobot.blog/2026/11/13/openai-disbands-robotics-team-gpt-6-astra/)).
- **Reassignment:** Eliminating the standalone robotics team does not mean abandoning research, but rather deeply absorbing robotics experts into the main model research organization under the leadership of core leaders like Mark Chen and Greg Brockman [OpenAI reorganises its robotics team as GPT-6 Astra runs robots](https://madrobot.blog/2026/11/13/openai-disbands-robotics-team-gpt-6-astra/). In other words, it is a structure where the people building robots and the people building brains work in the same team from the very beginning [OpenAI Disbands Robotics Team After General Purpose Models ...](https://officechai.com/ai/openai-disbands-robotics-team-after-general-purpose-models-begin-running-robots/).

## Where We Stand

In fact, this is not OpenAI's first encounter with robotics. Historically, OpenAI has already dissolved an AI robotics team once before [OpenAI shuts down robotics team because it doesn't have enough...](https://www.theregister.com/software/2021/07/18/openai-shuts-down-robotics-team-because-it-doesnt-have-enough-data-yet/748487). Back in 2021, robotics research was temporarily halted due to a lack of sufficient data to train physical robots and limitations in applying machine learning to physical machinery [OpenAI shuts down robotics team because it doesn't have enough...](https://www.theregister.com/software/2021/07/18/openai-shuts-down-robotics-team-because-it-doesnt-have-enough-data-yet/748487), [Why OpenAI decided to abandon robotics research](https://www.therobotreport.com/openai-abandons-robotics-research/). (At the time, it gained fame by developing a model that solved a Rubik's cube with one hand [Why OpenAI decided to abandon robotics research](https://www.therobotreport.com/openai-abandons-robotics-research/)).

Over the next few years, it resumed robotics hiring and research, driving toward the goal of "general-purpose robots operating with human-level intelligence in dynamic, real-world environments" [New OpenAI job listings reveal the company's robotics... | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/10/new-openai-job-listings-reveal-its-robotics-plans/), [OpenAI Enters Robotics and Physical AI | VIVE Blog](https://blog.vive.com/us/openai-enters-robotics-and-physical-ai/). However, after acutely feeling the shortage of data and the limitations of specialized models, OpenAI has judged that the performance of massive general-purpose AI models absorbing data from all directions is now fully matured.

OpenAI's current stance is clear. Through an official statement, a spokesperson emphasized that robotics remains "a core part of OpenAI's long-term vision" [OpenAI disbands its standalone robotics team as its general ...](https://mlq.ai/news/openai-disbands-its-standalone-robotics-team-as-its-general-models-start-running-robots-fortune-reports/). However, the way robotics is researched has evolved from the past isolated island-like dedicated department approach into a form fully fused and integrated with company-wide core AI model development organizations [OpenAI dissolves its dedicated robotics team — Grafy News](https://grafy.ai/news/openai-dissolves-its-dedicated-robotics-team-to-fold-researchers-into-general-ai).

Of course, cautious views also exist, noting that success in simulation environments (such as DROID tasks) does not immediately translate to real-world robot success on factory floors or in homes, and the cost, speed, and energy advantages of specialized models cannot be completely ignored [OpenAI folds its robotics researchers into research and ...](https://dev.to/delimitter/openai-folds-its-robotics-researchers-into-research-and-applied-teams-as-its-general-models-start-51ik).
