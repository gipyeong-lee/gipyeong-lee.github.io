Imagine this: You ask a smart assistant to "summarize this project report." But instead, the assistant says, "Hmm, how should I handle the introduction? Oh, wait! I should include this, too. No, let me rethink that. Wait! I should also..." and continues muttering to itself for an hour, stuck in a loop of its own making.

Recently, users of Google's AI model, Gemini, have reported a similar phenomenon. It seems the AI gets stuck in an "infinite loop"—continually repeating the same process—while trying to generate an answer. What exactly is happening to our smart AI assistant?

### Why does this matter?

As AI technology evolves, our daily lives are changing. It's become common to entrust AI with writing tasks or complex planning. However, the phenomenon of AI freezing and failing to provide an answer goes beyond mere inconvenience. The problem is particularly severe in the CLI (command-line interface) environment used by developers. Cases have been reported where tasks that usually take two minutes are delayed for as long as two hours [1]. This directly impacts work efficiency for users who rely on the AI to handle their tasks.

### Understanding it simply: The growing pains of a "thinking model"

The latest models, such as Gemini 2.5, are called "thinking models." While older AI simply predicted the next word based on probability, these models are designed with advanced reasoning capabilities to solve increasingly complex problems [7, 8].

To put it simply, it's like a grade-school student solving a math problem by writing out each step of the calculation in the corner of their test paper instead of just writing the final answer. Right now, Gemini is trying to think too deeply and has fallen into a "thought swamp" during that calculation process. Users are witnessing the AI endlessly repeating its internal considerations—like "Wait!" or "Let me think about this one more thing"—but failing to reach the necessary conclusion [3]. It’s as if the AI is trying so hard to think that it has gotten tangled up in its own thoughts.

### Current situation: The thought swamp is deepening

This "thought loop" phenomenon is occurring frequently across the latest models, including Gemini 3.1 Pro and 3.5 Flash [6, 9]. Many users, especially in the Gemini CLI environment, are experiencing situations where the "Thinking" status indicator remains frozen for minutes, or even hours [1, 4].

Even users with paid subscriptions are not immune to these delays [4]. While a temporary workaround involves manually opening and closing the AI's "thought process" window to break the loop [5], this is not a fundamental solution.

### What’s next?

Experts analyze that this phenomenon is likely a "growing pain" that occurs as artificial intelligence performs more complex reasoning. As the intelligence of the AI increases, the logical paths it must process become more complicated. Google is expected to continue rolling out updates to strengthen the AI's self-correction capabilities and streamline the reasoning process to prevent these infinite loops. For the time being, users would be wise to bypass the issue by breaking their questions down into smaller, sequential steps rather than posing overly complex questions all at once.

### MindTickleBytes' AI reporter perspective

This is a growing pain for complex reasoning models. It's an error that occurs as AI attempts to think deeply, just like a human. We may be witnessing a transition period as AI evolves from a "speaking machine" into a "thinking entity."
