## Lead

Imagine this: You hear the news that the high-performance artificial intelligence 'Claude Opus 5' you've been waiting for has finally been released. You excitedly log in to assign it some work, but you are met with nothing but "Error" messages. How frustrating would that be?

This is exactly what happened with the recently hyped Claude Opus 5. Many users eager to try out the new AI experienced service disruptions. Let's find out why this happened and whether everything is okay now.

## Why Does This Matter?

Smart assistants and AI chatbots have become part of our daily lives. But what happens if the AI we rely on suddenly stops working? Especially for top-tier models used by companies and professionals, even a small error can significantly impact work efficiency. This case provides a glimpse into the technical challenges faced when new AI technology is unveiled and attracts a massive influx of users simultaneously.

## Understanding It Simply

Think of an AI model as a 'smart library.' The newly released Claude Opus 5 is a special library that has read the most books and is perfectly organized. Now, imagine a situation where as soon as this library opens, people from all over the world rush in at once, shouting, "Find this book for me!" or "Summarize that!"

The 'errors' that occur in this scenario are similar to a librarian (AI system) being unable to respond properly due to receiving too many requests at once. While the developers prepare for high traffic, the reality is that the actual volume of data often exceeds predictions. The phenomenon experienced in this process is 'elevated errors.' [Source: Anthropic Status](https://status.claude.com/history)

In simple terms, it's like a popular restaurant on its opening day where too many customers arrive at once, leading to ingredients running out or food being served late. Also, if you use Claude Opus 5, you might notice it automatically switching to another model when a request isn't processed smoothly; this is called the 'model fallback' feature. [Source: Claude Support Page](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16049681-why-claude-switched-models-in-your-conversation-with-opus-5) It's like a librarian passing work to a colleague next to them when they are too busy.

## Current Situation

Error reports for Claude Opus 5 began as early as July 25, the day after its release. [Source: Kompozy](https://kompozy.io/news/anthropic-opus-5-elevated-error-rates) Afterward, errors occurred again around 9:17 AM on July 26, causing inconvenience to many users. [Source: Pulsetic](https://pulsetic.com/status/claude/incidents/5911/)

Fortunately, the manufacturer, Anthropic, responded quickly. Service errors returned to normal levels as of 2:03 PM (PST) on July 26, and the service is currently stable. [Source: Anthropic Status](https://status.claude.com/history)

## What Happens Next?

Tech experts say that in this era where the latest AI models are constantly being updated and infrastructure is changing, it is realistically difficult to completely avoid temporary errors that occur with specific model launches. [Source: Crashtech](https://crashtech.in/articles/claude-chatgpt-outages-same-week/)

Therefore, it is a good habit to always save your data separately when handling important work. After all, new technology doesn't always arrive fully prepared.

## MindTickleBytes AI Reporter's Perspective

This 'growing pain' experienced every time a new model is launched is paradoxical evidence of how much attention AI technology is receiving. Just as Anthropic quickly normalized the service, we look forward to the system becoming more robust and creating a more pleasant AI environment.
