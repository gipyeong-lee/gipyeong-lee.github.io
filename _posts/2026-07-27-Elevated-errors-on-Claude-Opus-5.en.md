Imagine this: You hear the news that everyone's been waiting for—a brand-new AI model has launched. You’re excited, ready to hand off a complex project, but the screen just coldly displays a message saying "An error has occurred." It’s a lot like arriving at a popular, newly opened restaurant only to find a long line but no food coming out. This is exactly what happened with the latest AI model you were trying to use, 'Claude Opus 5'. [Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 hit by elevated error rates one day after launch](https://kompozy.io/news/anthropic-opus-5-elevated-error-rates)

It’s natural to feel flustered when you run into this while trying to use a new tool with such high expectations. In this article, we’ll explain clearly what the identity of the errors in Claude Opus 5 was, why it happened, and how to respond if you face a similar situation in the future.

## Why It Matters

The latest AI models are like reliable digital assistants that dramatically increase our work efficiency. However, even the highest-performing AI can experience temporary downtime due to technical issues, which can cause significant inconvenience when you can't complete work by a crucial deadline. In fact, this time, [Anthropic’s Claude Opus 5 recorded high error rates, causing many users frustration](https://kompozy.io/news/anthropic-opus-5-elevated-error-rates).

As AI technology advances, the time we spend relying on AI in our daily lives and work continues to increase. Therefore, understanding service stability and being able to respond without panic during unexpected error situations is now a new form of 'digital literacy' required for modern individuals.

## The Explainer

To make this error easier to understand, let’s use another analogy. Imagine you’ve gone to a famous newly opened restaurant to order a trending limited-edition menu item. However, because orders for existing popular menu items were also pouring in at the same time, the entire kitchen system became temporarily paralyzed due to overload.

The problem with Claude Opus 5 was very similar. This error was not an internal defect unique to the Opus 5 model itself. It was a 'multi-model API incident'—a system outage that also affected other models that share the 'Claude API' (the pathway for talking to AI), such as 'Mythos 5', 'Fable 5', and 'Claude Haiku 4.5'. [Reports of high error rates for several models including Claude Opus 5](https://status.claude.com/)

In short, it wasn’t that one specific car broke down, but rather that too many cars rushed to major toll booths on the highway, causing a temporary traffic jam. Fortunately, Anthropic quickly recognized this issue and performed system maintenance.

## Where We Stand

The most important news is that this issue is now fully resolved. Anthropic announced officially that the error rate for Claude Opus 5 has completely returned to previous baseline levels. [Claude Opus 5 errors recovered to normal levels](https://status.claude.com/history)

Therefore, those using Claude Opus 5 right now can use the AI service smoothly just as before. If you occasionally experience slight slowness or minor errors, it is likely due to temporary network environments or device overload rather than a full service outage, so we recommend waiting a moment and trying again. [Anthropic's Claude Opus 5-related errors resolved](https://kompozy.io/news/anthropic-opus-5-elevated-error-rates)

## What's Next

AI technology is advancing extremely rapidly at this very moment, and building a perfect system in that process is technically quite difficult. As users, if we remember these two things, we won't have to panic in the future either.

First, **utilize the service status page.** Large-scale AI services like Claude operate dedicated pages that provide real-time status updates. Make it a habit to bookmark the [Claude status page](https://status.claude.com/) or [real-time AI service status monitoring pages](https://claudestatus.com/) and check them first when an unknown error occurs.

Second, **learn flexible coping methods.** If you are performing professional work using tools like Claude Code, it is good to know how to immediately switch to another model when a specific model is overloaded. For example, if you enter the `/model` command in the chat window to switch to another stable model like Sonnet, you can avoid errors and continue your work seamlessly.
