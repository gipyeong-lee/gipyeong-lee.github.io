Imagine you are looking for a restaurant to go to tonight. Instead of just searching for 'good restaurant,' you tell your AI assistant, "Log in, check my personal coupons, and book the earliest available time." The search engines you use today only list 'restaurant lists,' but in the future, AI will press buttons on websites, fill in information, and bring back the final result just like a human would. This is the world of the recently highlighted **'Agentic Search' (technology where AI actively searches for information by acting on its own).**

### Why is this important?

Until now, we had to ask questions to search engines, click through numerous links ourselves, and find the answers manually. But now, we are moving into an era where Artificial Intelligence (AI) provides more accurate and efficient results [Source: Mistral](https://mistral.ai/).

Agentic Search goes beyond simple search to handle the complex tasks we perform online on our behalf. While the past was at the level of simply scraping web pages, AI can now autonomously collect information that requires logging in or data that needs to be checked by flipping through multiple pages [Source: Firecrawl](https://www.firecrawl.dev/). This is a change that will drastically reduce the unnecessary time we waste in front of computers, going beyond mere technological advancement.

### Understanding it easily: The Librarian Metaphor

It is easy to understand Agentic Search by comparing it to a 'librarian.'

A typical search engine is like a librarian who only brings you a 'card catalog with book titles.' You have to look at the list, find where the book is, and figure out what the content is yourself. In contrast, Agentic Search is like a 'skilled librarian who understands the content and goes directly to the library shelves to find the information.'

This librarian does the following:

1. **Ability to act**: If the archives door is locked, they find the key (login), walk up the stairs (navigate pages), and write down the necessary information on a memo (extract data).
2. **Ability to connect**: The AI system performs multi-step flows itself, such as clicking buttons on web pages or filling out forms [Source: Firecrawl](https://www.firecrawl.dev/).
3. **Smart search**: Using tools such as a 'Vector Search Engine' (search technology that converts the meaning of text into numbers to determine similarity), it selects only the most contextually important materials from vast data [Source: Qdrant](https://qdrant.tech/).

Simply put, 'navigating' the digital space directly like a human is the core of Agentic Search.

### Current Situation

Currently, Agentic Search technology is developing rapidly. Companies like Mistral are releasing models for more accurate and efficient information retrieval [Source: Mistral](https://mistral.ai/), and platforms like Google are integrating experiences where AI plans and assists in research directly into search results [Source: Google I/O 2024](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/generative-ai-google-search-may-2024/).

However, there are also things to be aware of. Although AI has become smarter, it only assists in researching information, and there is still the possibility of a 'lie by omission' where the AI delivers information incorrectly or omits important details [Source: Era of Light](https://eraoflight.com/2026/08/23/total-freedom-vs-total-slavery-and-the-race-for-ai-supremacy/). Therefore, the process of us finally understanding and reviewing the information the AI brings is still very important.

### What will happen in the future?

In the future, most of the search tasks we do are likely to be handled by 'agents.' For example, when planning a trip, instead of simply asking, "Find me accommodation," if you command, "Connect to hotel sites, apply coupons, and book the best room according to my budget and preferences," the AI will handle it automatically.

Of course, discussions about technical safety issues or controllability that arise as AI develops will continue [Source: Situational Awareness](https://situational-awareness.ai/). But what is clear is that the way we get the results we want from the sea of information will change from 'simple search' to 'Agentic Search.'
