Imagine this: you sit down this morning, ready to hand off some work to your AI assistant, 'Claude,' as you usually do, when suddenly you are met with a message saying, "You've reached your usage limit." Frustrating, isn't it? To prepare for situations like this, many people purchase 'Usage Credits.' However, there have been recent changes to the credit management policy. Did you know that the credits you purchased without much thought could suddenly disappear one day?

Today at MindTickleBytes, we are breaking down the newly changed credit policies and the precautions you must take for those of you who regularly use Claude.

## Why is this important?

As AI technology advances, conversational AI like Claude has become an indispensable tool in our daily lives. Users on Pro or Max plans purchase extra credits after hitting their base quota to ensure their workflow remains uninterrupted.

The issue here is 'money.' Credits are essentially a prepaid voucher you buy with your own cash. If your purchased credits now have an expiration date and you neglect them without knowing, your valuable money is essentially evaporating into thin air. This policy change is critical information, especially for those who tend to buy large amounts of credits in advance, to ensure cost-effective service usage.

## Keep it simple: Think of them like 'Prepaid Recharge Cards'

Claude's usage credits are similar to buying an 'additional ride pass' for an amusement park—you bought the general admission ticket, but once you've ridden too many rides and hit your limit, you buy these extra passes to keep going [Source 1](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12429409-manage-usage-credits-for-paid-claude-plans), [Source 3](https://www.ssdnodes.com/learn/claude-usage-credits-explained).

Simply put, credits are a safety net that prevents your AI work from being 'interrupted.'

*   **Base Plan (General Admission):** There are session limits that reset every 5 hours and weekly usage limits. Buying credits will not increase these.
*   **Usage Credits (Additional Ride Pass):** These are supplementary vouchers that allow you to continue using features like Claude or 'Claude Code' after you've exhausted your base limit.

However, since there may now be expiration dates on this safety net, you should check your credits just as carefully as you would check the expiration date on groceries you buy at the store.

## Current status: What do I need to know?

In principle, Claude's usage credits do not expire [Source 8](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12429409-manage-usage-credits-for-paid-claude-plans). However, there are exceptions.

First, **it depends on the specific region and timing.** For example, in certain countries like Japan, credits purchased after September 10, 2026, are set to expire 6 months after the purchase date [Source 8](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12429409-manage-usage-credits-for-paid-claude-plans), [Source 12](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12429409-manage-usage-credits-for-paid-claude-plans). The new policies announced recently may also have different expiration dates depending on the time of purchase and your region of residence, so caution is required.

Second, **you will receive a reminder email.** Fortunately, Anthropic does not let your credits just vanish. 7 days before your credits expire, Anthropic will send a "Your credits are about to expire" notification email to your registered address [Source 8](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12429409-manage-usage-credits-for-paid-claude-plans), [Source 12](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12429409-manage-usage-credits-for-paid-claude-plans). You can also immediately check your credit expiration date by visiting the 'Usage' page in your Settings [Source 12](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12429409-manage-usage-credits-for-paid-claude-plans).

## What to expect in the future?

Moving forward, rather than thinking "it's fine to just buy a bunch," you will need a strategy of "purchasing smaller amounts tailored to my usage patterns." Especially when purchasing credits with a 12-month expiration date, it is wise to estimate whether you will actually do that much AI work within a year.

If you are using Claude for business or a team, the team owner may purchase credits and distribute them to members, so be sure to check whether your account is a personal or team account [Source 10](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12005970-manage-usage-credits-for-team-and-seat-based-enterprise-plans). Most importantly, **"build the habit of checking your account settings page periodically."** Simply visiting the 'Usage' page at least once a month to check the status of your credits will be enough to save you money.
