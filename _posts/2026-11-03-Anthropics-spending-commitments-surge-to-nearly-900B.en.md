Imagine you opened a small local bakery, and in just a few months, your customer base increased 12-fold. There’s a long line every morning, and revenue is hitting the roof. Then, news breaks that you’ve signed a contract to spend hundreds of billions of dollars over the next few years to build a network of massive baking factories worldwide.

This is exactly what is happening right now with **Anthropic**, one of the leading companies in the global artificial intelligence (AI) market. Ahead of its initial public offering (IPO) on Wall Street, Anthropic released official filing documents revealing staggering financial figures alongside impressive growth [Anthropic says revenue surged 1,100% ahead of IPO](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/business/2026/11/03/anthropic-says-revenue-surged-1-100-ahead-of-ipo).

Anthropic’s commitment to securing computing resources and infrastructure has reached a staggering **$895.4 billion (approximately $900 billion)** [Anthropic Files Updated S1: Committed Spend Jumps To $900BN](https://www.zerohedge.com/markets/anthropic-files-updated-its-s1-committed-spend-jumps-900bn-only-google-has-more).

Why would an AI company experiencing explosive 1,100% revenue growth tie up such a massive amount of money in infrastructure contracts? Let’s break down the meaning behind this shocking bill in plain language.

## Why does this matter?

When using AI services on smartphones or for work, we don't always feel what's happening behind the screen. But every time we throw a question at an AI, tens of thousands of advanced computing processors in a massive data center somewhere are busy consuming electricity and calculating the answer.

Simply put, to make the AI’s 'brain'—its massive model—smarter, you need equally massive 'power plants' and 'server factories.' Anthropic's disclosure is important for several reasons:

1. **The fundamental power determining AI performance**: To develop advanced AI and provide services to people, enormous computing servers and infrastructure (data centers and high-speed network systems that process AI calculations) are essential. Anthropic's infrastructure spending indicates the scale of AI processing power they intend to secure.
2. **Wall Street's attention ahead of the IPO**: Anthropic is scheduled to list on the New York Stock Exchange later this month [Anthropic reveals $900B AI spending commitments ahead of IPO](https://hoton.ai/en/news/anthropic-reveals-900b-ai-spending-commitments-ahead-of-ipo-1st3s7u). Investors are scrutinizing how much the company is earning and how much it is investing for the future.
3. **Proving the scale of the big tech technology war**: This isn't just Anthropic's story. Big tech companies have also committed to spending $650 billion on AI infrastructure in 2026 alone, raising funds by issuing $108 billion in bonds [Anthropic went from $380B to $900B. In 3 months.](https://founderscrowd.beehiiv.com/p/anthropic-went-from-380b-to-900b-in-3-months).

Ultimately, this announcement is not just a financial report; it is an event that proves the scale of capital investment in the battle for AI dominance has reached unimaginable levels.

## Understanding the figures

Let’s look at what the numbers Anthropic announced actually mean, using analogies to simplify complex financial and technical terms.

### 1. What are 'Purchase Commitments'?

Think of it like signing a long-term lease agreement to rent a luxury supercar every month for 10 years. You haven't paid the entire car price today, but because you signed the contract, you are obligated to pay the monthly rent for the duration of the agreement.

Anthropic's reported **"purchase commitments and leases"** work just like this [Anthropic Files Updated S1: Committed Spend Jumps To $900BN](https://www.zerohedge.com/markets/anthropic-files-updated-its-s1-committed-spend-jumps-900bn-only-google-has-more). Anthropic has entered into long-term contracts with cloud service providers and data center companies to secure a stable supply of computing resources and infrastructure equipment.

* **Total Commitment**: The total commitment amounts to approximately $895.4 billion (about $900 billion) [Anthropic Files Updated S1: Committed Spend Jumps To $900BN](https://www.zerohedge.com/markets/anthropic-files-updated-its-s1-committed-spend-jumps-900bn-only-google-has-more).
* **Usage**: Almost all of this amount is focused on **computing and infrastructure** required for training AI models and operating services [Anthropic Files Updated S1: Committed Spend Jumps To $900BN](https://www.zerohedge.com/markets/anthropic-files-updated-its-s1-committed-spend-jumps-900bn-only-google-has-more).

### 2. From $380 Billion to $900 Billion in 3 Months?

Even more surprising is the speed at which this commitment amount has increased. Just three months ago, Anthropic's spending commitments were $380 billion [Anthropic went from $380B to $900B. In 3 months.](https://founderscrowd.beehiiv.com/p/anthropic-went-from-380b-to-900b-in-3-months). That’s an increase of more than $500 billion in just one quarter [Anthropic went from $380B to $900B. In 3 months.](https://founderscrowd.beehiiv.com/p/anthropic-went-from-380b-to-900b-in-3-months).

Using the analogy, it’s like a company that had contracted to build 38 baking factories across the country suddenly expanding its contracts to 90 factories in a single quarter because customers were flooding in.

### 3. When is the money paid?

Anthropic is not paying the $900 billion all at once tomorrow. According to the company's filing, **approximately $450 billion** of the total commitments will become due by the end of 2030 [Anthropic's new IPO filing puts its computing bill near $900 billion](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/anthropics-new-ipo-filing-puts-its-computing-bill-near-900-billion-151830444.html). It is effectively a long-term infrastructure subscription fee spread out over the next 4–5 years.

## Current situation

So, what is Anthropic's current management status and financial performance? The filings released ahead of the IPO contain figures that are a mix of highs and lows.

### A dazzling report card of 1,100% revenue growth

Anthropic announced that its revenue this quarter surged **more than 1,100%** compared to the previous period [Anthropic says revenue surged 1,100% ahead of IPO](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/business/2026/11/03/anthropic-says-revenue-surged-1-100-ahead-of-ipo). This means demand for Anthropic's AI services is exploding among both corporate and individual users.

### Back to operating loss after hitting profitability

However, there is one point to note. While Anthropic recorded its first-ever profit in the previous quarter, it has returned to an **operating loss** in the third quarter [Anthropic's new IPO filing puts its computing bill near $900 billion](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/anthropics-new-ipo-filing-puts-its-computing-bill-near-900-billion-151830444.html).

Why did they return to a loss despite a 1,100% revenue increase? It is due to the aforementioned computing infrastructure costs. The spending on server and data center usage to develop (train) smarter AI models and process the exploding number of real-time user inquiries (inference) was greater than the revenue growth rate.
