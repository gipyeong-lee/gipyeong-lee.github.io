Imagine this: You have released hundreds of papers, confident that you have solved challenges that have remained unsolved for decades. But what if a significant portion of those results were not actually correct or contained errors? A similar, fascinating event recently occurred in the field of Artificial Intelligence (AI). Massive mathematical research results written by AI were released to the world, and human experts from around the globe stepped in to verify and correct them directly.

## Why is this important?

It is incredibly encouraging for AI to solve advanced mathematical problems that were once considered the domain of humans. However, the situation changes if the answer provided by AI 'looks correct but is wrong.' In scientific research, logical errors by AI can lead to catastrophic results. This incident is a prime example of why we should not unconditionally trust AI-generated output, and why the 'peer review' process, where fellow scholars meticulously examine papers, remains crucial.

## In Simple Terms

On October 6, 2026, OpenAI released the results of 722 mathematical research papers generated by its internal advanced AI model onto the open-source platform GitHub [Source 3, Source 10]. The project originally tasked the AI with solving about 4,000 mathematical challenges and then compiled and announced the 722 most complete results [Source 13].

However, immediately after the release, mathematicians and developers from all over the world began reviewing these papers, much like diligent teachers grading homework. As a result, within a single day, a situation occurred where 3 papers, including those with fundamental mistakes such as sign errors, were retracted [Source 6, Source 10].

To put it simply, the AI is like a 'genius writer' churning out hundreds of books at an incredible speed. But it lacks a bit of meticulousness. Right now, mathematicians from around the world have taken on the role of 'editors,' reading the books written by this author one by one to catch typos and logical leaps. This work is the 'oai-math-revised' project currently active on GitHub [Source 1, Source 2]. Members of the community are not just pointing out errors; they are putting their heads together to suggest solutions for more accurate mathematical proofs.

## Current Situation

Currently, a total of 719 papers remain under review. About 300 of them are written in a specialized format called 'Lean' [Source 10]. Lean is a language that allows machines to automatically determine whether there are any logical flaws in a proof based on mathematical principles. In other words, an environment has been established where machines can catch errors based on mathematical principles without humans having to read every single line. Revision work is continuously ongoing, including the 14 papers in which errors were discovered [Source 10].

However, experts remain cautious. One group of experts evaluated that OpenAI's mathematical proof methods have not yet reached the rigorous standards required by the mathematics community [Source 17].

## What will happen next?

In the future, AI will continue to pose more complex and difficult mathematical challenges. Through this incident, we caught a glimpse of a new future where AI and humans collaborate. The 'mutually complementary' relationship has become important, where AI handles massive calculations and logical reasoning, and humans verify and refine whether the results are mathematically perfect. What we should focus on going forward is how AI will learn from the community's feedback to produce more accurate results next time, and whether the machine verification system, Lean, can establish itself as an essential standard for future mathematical research.

## MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Perspective

Mathematical results produced by AI open up tremendous possibilities, but we cannot 100% trust the logic used to reach those conclusions. This 'oai-math-revised' project is an important milestone showing how we should handle and grow together with imperfect AI. Do not forget that as important as technological advancement is the human intellect that doubts and verifies the answers provided by that technology.
