<h4>
? Explanation of Usage
</h4>
<ol>
<li>
<b>
Green manure crops:
</b>
?
<ul>
<li>
<b>
Method:
</b>
Plant green manure crops around the crops, and after they grow, till them into the soil. This supplies organic matter and nutrients to the soil.
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<b>
Organic matter:
</b>
♻️
<ul>
<li>
<b>
Method:
</b>
Mix compost, food waste, or plant residues into the soil around the crops to increase soil fertility.
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<b>
Wood ash:
</b>
?
<ul>
<li>
<b>
Method:
</b>
Sprinkle a small amount of alkaline wood ash onto the soil around the crops to adjust pH and supplement necessary trace nutrients.
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<b>
Bone meal:
</b>
?
<ul>
<li>
<b>
Method:
</b>
Mix bone meal made by grinding animal bones into the soil around the crops to supplement phosphate.
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<b>
Seaweed:
</b>
?
<ul>
<li>
<b>
Method:
</b>
Compost seaweed and mix it into the soil around the crops to supply phosphate and various nutrients.
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<b>
Rock phosphate:
</b>
?
<ul>
<li>
<b>
Method:
</b>
Mix natural phosphate mineral into the soil around the crops for slow release of phosphate.
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<b>
Banana peels:
</b>
?
<ul>
<li>
<b>
Method:
</b>
Dry and grind banana peels into a powder, then mix into the soil around the crops to supplement potassium.
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<b>
Eggshells:
</b>
?
<ul>
<li>
<b>
Method:
</b>
Dry and grind eggshells into a powder, then mix into the soil around the crops to supplement calcium.
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<b>
Irrigation management:
</b>
?
<ul>
<li>
<b>
Method:
</b>
Manage the electrical conductivity of the soil by providing an appropriate amount of water to prevent salt accumulation.
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
