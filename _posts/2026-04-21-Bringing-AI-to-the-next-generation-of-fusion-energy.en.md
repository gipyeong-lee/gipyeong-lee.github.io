## 1. Nuclear Fusion: Why is it so important?

Most of the energy we use today comes from burning fossil fuels like oil and coal or relying on conventional nuclear power. However, fossil fuels are the primary cause of global warming, and nuclear power leaves behind the headache of radioactive waste. In contrast, **Nuclear Fusion** generates massive amounts of energy by merging very light atoms together—the exact same way the sun produces light and heat. ## 2. How to Handle a 100-Million-Degree 'Angry Fireball'

To trigger nuclear fusion, fuel must be turned into a state called **Plasma**. Plasma is a state where it is so hot that the nuclei and electrons of atoms are separated, floating freely like a gas. At this stage, the temperature must be over **100 million degrees Celsius**—several times hotter than the core of the sun. [Google DeepMind is bringing AI to the next generation of fusion...](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/trent-bauserman-8b0a168b_google-deepmind-is-bringing-ai-to-the-next-activity-7384721019829272578-8Lr1)

There is no container on Earth that can withstand 100 million degrees. Therefore, scientists use the power of strong magnets to suspend and confine this hot plasma in mid-air.

**Let's use an analogy:**
> Imagine you are trying to suspend a 100-million-degree piece of hot jelly in mid-air using magnets to ensure it never touches the walls of its container. However, this jelly is like a wild beast, changing its shape thousands of times a second and trying to burst out. If it touches the wall even slightly, the fireball cools down instantly, and the energy-making process stops.

It is nearly impossible for human hands or conventional computer programs to track and control these minute, rapid movements individually. This is exactly where **Google DeepMind's** AI steps in as a relief pitcher. [Plasma Under Control: How AI is Driving Fusion's Next Leap](https://www.cleanenergy-platform.com/insight/plasma-under-control-how-ai-is-driving-fusions-next-leap)
