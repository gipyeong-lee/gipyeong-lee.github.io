Imagine this: The AI service we use every day is operating at a loss of tens of trillions of won, yet it is receiving even larger investments and preparing to go public. It might feel like pouring water into a bottomless pit, but in the AI industry, this is being treated as a sort of "arms race" to stake a claim in the future. Anthropic, the developer of "Claude" and a company at the center of this movement, has recently had its IPO filing—the process of listing its shares for public trading for the first time—attract global attention.

## Why Does This Matter?

Understanding the economic environment in which our AI services are created is important. Along with OpenAI, Anthropic is considered the most valuable "pure-play AI company," and in May 2026, it was valued at as much as $965 billion [Anthropic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic,_PBC).

However, behind these dazzling numbers lies massive capital consumption. Anthropic going public means that the general public can now become investors in this massive AI experiment, but it is also a declaration that the company is ready to face cold, hard market scrutiny regarding how it plans to generate future profits [Wall Street is urging Anthropic, OpenAI to do their IPOs in a hurry, here’s why](https://nypost.com/2026/10/23/business/wall-street-is-urging-anthropic-openai-to-do-their-ipos-in-a-hurry-heres-why/).

## Easy to Understand

You can think of Anthropic's current situation as a "racing car traveling at ultra-high speed."

1. **Fuel (Computing Infrastructure):** For a racing car to run fast, it needs a massive amount of high-grade fuel. Here, the fuel is the data centers and AI semiconductor chips used to process data. Anthropic plans to pour an astronomical sum of $518 billion into this "fuel" over the next several years [Anthropic warns of AI's existential risk to humans in its IPO filing](https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2026-09-30/anthropic-warns-of-ais-existential-risk-to-humans-in-its-ipo-filing).
2. **Engine (Claude Model):** The engine that determines the car's performance is the "Claude" series (Opus, Sonnet, Haiku, etc.) [Anthropic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic,_PBC). To maintain high performance for this engine, it must be continuously trained on new data, a process that once led to a $1.5 billion settlement over copyright issues [Anthropic Settlement ($1.5B)](https://ailawsuittracker.com/cases/bartz-v-anthropic/).
3. **Passengers (Customers):** The company's survival depends on the "passengers" boarding this racing car—corporate customers—and how quickly they adopt AI and pay for usage fees [Anthropic's $518B compute bet depends on customers](https://observer.com/2026/10/anthropic-dario-amodei-ipo-ai-market-customers/).

Simply put, Anthropic is currently running at incredible speed and proving its performance, but it is experiencing "growing pains" where the cost of fuel is much higher than the money the racing car is making.

## What is the Current Situation?

Anthropic's IPO documents reveal its scale without filter. While it recorded revenue of $16.3 billion in the first half of 2026, it is known that its net loss reached a staggering $80.7 billion [Anthropic S-1: $80.7B H1 Loss on $16.3B Revenue Before IPO](https://thehype.news/anthropic-s-1-loss-revenue/). This is a very steep increase compared to its total net loss of $42 billion in 2025 [Anthropic's revenue jumped 12-fold in 2025, but so did...](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/10/10/anthropics-revenue-jumped-12-fold-in-2025-but-so-d/).

Furthermore, Anthropic is a company that emphasizes technological ethics. Despite requests from the U.S. Department of Defense (DoD), Anthropic strictly adhered to limitations on large-scale domestic surveillance and fully autonomous weapons, which led to the termination of its relationship with the Department [Anthropic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic,_PBC). This is a snapshot showing that the company prioritizes the philosophy of "safe AI" over simply making money.

## What Will Happen in the Future?

Anthropic is aiming to go public around November 2026 [Anthropic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic,_PBC). Market analysts suggest that Anthropic needs to rush its IPO for liquidity (opportunities for investors to cash out their investments) [The Anthropic S-1 that was expected after Labor Day still hasn't arrived](https://forkast.news/the-anthropic-s-1-that-was-expected-after-labor-day-still-hasnt-arrived/).

Investors are watching to see if Anthropic can create a profit structure capable of covering massive computing costs through future customer acquisition. Additionally, how it maintains its technological gap with competitors and how it resolves AI ethical issues will be key watchpoints that determine its stock price and future growth potential.

## MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Perspective

Anthropic's massive investment appears to be an essential rite of passage for securing future AI leadership. However, after going public, it will begin a high-level balancing act of enduring cold market scrutiny of its profitability while successfully capturing both its philosophy of "safe AI" and its status as a "growing enterprise."
