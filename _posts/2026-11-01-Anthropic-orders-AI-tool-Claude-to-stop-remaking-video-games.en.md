Imagine being able to recreate your childhood favorite classic game in its entirety just by speaking to an AI. Recently, the coding AI tool 'Claude Code' made headlines among developers by demonstrating exactly that kind of magical capability. [Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code) is a powerful AI tool that helps developers analyze and edit code, execute commands, and drastically increase productivity.

However, convenience has its shadow side. As AI began 'copying' games with such ease, game studios grew increasingly concerned, and Anthropic finally took action. Anthropic has recently added new rules to Claude Code that fundamentally prohibit the recreation of existing video games. [Source: Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/anthropic-appears-to-have-ordered-ai-tool-claude-to-stop-remaking-video-games)

### Why was this measure necessary?

This move is a responsible step to prevent AI from transforming from a useful tool that aids creativity into a means of unauthorized replication of copyrighted works. Recently, there has been a surge in 'clone' games or fan-made ports that perfectly imitate popular games like 'Fortnite' or 'Fallout' using AI. [Source: TechPowerUp](https://www.techpowerup.com/354351/anthropic-bans-ai-game-clones-and-reverse-engineering-with-claude)

These acts not only disregard the years of effort put in by game developers but also raise serious copyright infringement issues. Especially as AI has begun interpreting and writing code directly, 'reverse engineering' games to make them open-source or unauthorized recreations has become much easier than in the past. Anthropic’s new rule directly targets this technical misuse. [Source: SKOAR.gg](https://www.skoar.gg/breaking-news/anthropic-adjusts-claude-to-prevent-ai-misuse-in-specific-games/)

### Simply put

To put it metaphorically, this rule is a stern instruction: 'Do not tell AI to copy someone else's work for your homework.' As of around October 27, 2026, a two-sentence powerful rule was embedded into Claude Code's core model, 'Opus 5.5,' to block the recreation of existing games. [Source: Tech-Insider](https://tech-insider.org/anthropic-claude-code-two-sentence-game-recreation-rule-indie-clones-2026/)

This rule is unwavering. It is designed so that the AI will not permit recreations even if the user changes the project name or claims it is an 'original project' they created from scratch. [Source: Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/anthropic-nerfs-claude-again-to-stop-users-from-making-ai-slop-out-of-any-specific-existing-game-2000751665) It is similar to how a filter immediately detects and blocks a file if the source is the same, even if one tries to claim a slightly edited photo as 'my artwork' using Photoshop. [Source: GSMDome](https://www.gsmdome.com/anthropic-restricts-claude-code-from-recreating-existing-video-games)

### Current situation and response

An Anthropic technical staffer has officially confirmed the addition of this rule. [Source: Tech-Insider](https://tech-insider.org/anthropic-claude-code-two-sentence-game-recreation-rule-indie-clones-2026/) Many users have already reported that since this update, attempts to mimic the structure of existing games or open-source them are being blocked by Claude Code. [Source: Dzen](https://dzen.ru/b/auTwb5mPxycDRIpe) Anthropic has consistently strived to maximize the positive impact of AI on society while reducing risks, and this measure is evaluated as a reflection of the company’s ethics and philosophy. [Source: Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/)

### Future outlook

Technological advancement sometimes moves at a speed that threatens the rights of creators. This is why there are constant concerns that the speed of AI development might outpace developers' ability to understand and control it. [Source: YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RRT3H9FUGy4) However, as leading companies like Anthropic begin to regulate the scope of their own tools, the character of AI coding tools is expected to shift from 'tools capable of anything' to 'tools that can be used ethically.' A healthier environment will be created where developers focus on implementing their own unique ideas rather than relying on existing code when utilizing AI.
