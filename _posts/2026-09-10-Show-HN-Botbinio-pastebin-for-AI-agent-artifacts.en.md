Imagine this: You ask an AI agent (intelligent software that makes its own judgments and performs tasks) to "summarize today's meeting notes and organize the next steps." The AI instantly produces perfect results. However, when you try to show this data to friends or colleagues, you’re left wondering where and how to store and send it.

Copying it into a simple notepad ruins the formatting, and sending it via email or messenger every time makes it impossible to manage. Just as developers use 'Pastebin' (a tool to temporarily store text and share it via a link) to share code, we have reached an era where we need a dedicated space for AI-created results.

### Why is this important?

AI agents have moved beyond being simple conversation partners to a stage where they can search the web, write documents, and handle complex tasks on their own. The problem lies in the countless 'fragments of data' they create in the process.

Experts call these 'artifacts' (work results). These artifacts are often not just discarded text but contain the core information of a project. If these results could be easily stored and shared via a single link on the web, work efficiency would increase exponentially. This is precisely why platforms like [Botbin.io](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49639496) are attracting attention.

### Simply put: An AI 'sticky note' box

Is the term 'pastebin' unfamiliar to you? To put it simply, it’s like copying just the necessary pages from a library book and posting them on a bulletin board instead of borrowing the whole book.

[Botbin.io](https://modernorange.io/item/49639496) is a 'digital bulletin board' where you can temporarily or permanently store the results left behind by AI agents as they perform tasks. While [existing pastebin services](https://pastebin.com/) mainly focus on sharing code for developers, [Botbin.io](https://vue-hackernews-ssr-5cavbdjcta-ew.a.run.app/item/49636340) is designed to handle the various text data poured out by AI agents in a more professional way.

By analogy, just as we take photos and post them on Instagram, we can now post results created by AI agents on an 'Instagram for AI' and share them with people via links. It offers the convenience of resolving everything with a single link, without the need to download and attach complex files.

### Where are we currently?

The AI agent market is currently developing at a very rapid pace, from [voice agents](https://ansar.agency/explore) to [automated content creation tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2VWDtjS7jFA). However, the platforms that systematically manage those results are still in their early stages.

[Botbin.io](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49639496) is a new type of service that has emerged amidst this trend. While it may not be able to perfectly automate all tasks, it plays a critical role in anchoring valuable information created by AI so that it doesn't become fragmented and disappear. It’s like dropping an anchor in a flood of information.

### What will happen in the future?

In the future, collaboration between AI agents will become a daily routine. This involves a method where agent B receives and processes what agent A has organized. In this scenario, storage platforms like Botbin.io are highly likely to serve as 'central data repositories' and 'intersections' where agents exchange information.

Before long, we will reach a day when we can say to an AI agent, "Neatly organize the results of today's work and create a Botbin link for them."
