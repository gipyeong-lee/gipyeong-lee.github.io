In the past, you would have had to sit tethered to your desk, staring intently at the monitor until the code ran perfectly without errors. Until recently, executing thousands of lines of code or running heavy tasks was strictly "space-dependent."

Developers and data professionals often found themselves unable to leave their seats, anxious that the program might stop midway. Even when going for a meal or moving to a meeting room, it was a common, bittersweet sight to see people carefully carrying laptops with screens wide open, fearing the work would stop if the power went out or the device slept. The anxiety that "if the laptop turns off, my work ends" kept us tied to our desks.

But now, the chains of these physical constraints are finally being broken. You can now leave heavy workloads to your desktop computer and hop on the subway home with a light heart. Simply pull out your smartphone, open the ChatGPT app, check how far the AI has progressed with the coding in real-time, and easily instruct it: "Modify the logic in this part like this and run it again." The scene from a sci-fi movie where a genius scientist issues commands into thin air has become a reality. This is because OpenAI has officially integrated 'Codex,' its AI coding tool for desktops, into the mobile ChatGPT app. [OpenAI’s Codex is now in the ChatGPT mobile app](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/930763/openai-codex-chatgpt-ios-android-app-preview) Users can now remotely direct the AI running on their desktops using nothing but the smartphone in their pockets.

## What Exactly is Codex?

At the center of this massive change is 'Codex,' which is on a completely different level from the simple chatbots we ask daily questions to. First appearing in February as a desktop-exclusive app for professional developers, Codex is a powerful, cloud-based 'AI coding agent' capable of handling complex software engineering tasks simultaneously. [OpenAI brings Codex coding tool to ChatGPT mobile app | Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/openai-brings-codex-coding-tool-chatgpt-mobile-app-2026-05-14/)

An 'agent' here refers to intelligent software that performs specific goals on behalf of the user. In simple terms, it's a reliable 'virtual AI assistant' that perfectly understands your technical instructions and makes its own judgments to write and modify actual code. This update is a core part of OpenAI's strategy to create a practical 'worker' that goes beyond simple text responses and delves deep into our actual development and work environments. ## The Explainer

So, how can the small smartphone in your pocket handle the heavy task of analyzing hundreds of thousands of lines of code? Let's use an analogy to help you understand.

Imagine you are the **'General Manager'** of a famous restaurant. The kitchen (your office desktop computer) is equipped with top-of-the-line cooking utensils, and a genius **'Sous Chef (Codex)'** is on standby, cooking everything perfectly. In the past, to check if this sous chef was doing a good job, you also had to stand in the small kitchen filled with heat and noise all day.

But now, you've been given a magical **'Walkie-Talkie (ChatGPT mobile app).'** You can now sit on a cool terrace, enjoy a coffee, and receive reports on the kitchen's status through the walkie-talkie. You only need to give instructions like, "Flip the steak you're grilling in 5 minutes, and add a little more wine to the sauce." The actual hard labor is handled by the sous chef (host computer) staying in the kitchen.

Similarly, the updated ChatGPT app does not use the performance of the smartphone itself. The Codex program continues to run inside a high-performance laptop you left on at the office or a **'host computer'** like a Mac Mini at home. [Now in preview: Codex in the ChatGPT mobile app - Codex - OpenAI Developer Community](https://community.openai.com/t/now-in-preview-codex-in-the-chatgpt-mobile-app/1380940) The smartphone merely acts as a thin glass window, or 'remote control,' to remotely access that computer and operate the massive machinery. [OpenAI brings Codex control to ChatGPT for iPhone and Android](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/14/openai-brings-codex-control-to-chatgpt-for-iphone-and-android/)

Two key technologies are hidden in this process:

1.  **Live state synchronization**: The smartphone app doesn't just exchange messages; it brings the 'vivid current state' of where Codex has written the code on the desktop directly to your screen. [OpenAI brings Codex to the ChatGPT mobile app - The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/openai-codex-chatgpt-mobile/)
2.  **Secure relay layer**: When accessing your computer via external Wi-Fi, hacking is the biggest concern. OpenAI has built a sturdy defense called the 'secure relay layer.' To use an analogy, it's like creating a solid 'secret encrypted passage' between the smartphone and the computer that no one can peek into. Thanks to this, you can safely access your computer from anywhere in the world. [OpenAI brings Codex to ChatGPT mobile app for iOS and Android](https://www.testingcatalog.com/openai-brings-codex-to-chatgpt-mobile-app-for-ios-and-android/)

Of course, competitor Anthropic has also introduced a similar feature called 'Dispatch,' but experts evaluate OpenAI's method—bringing the entire real-time desktop environment to mobile—as being one step further advanced. [OpenAI brings Codex to the ChatGPT mobile app - The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/openai-codex-chatgpt-mobile/)

## What Specifically Can You Do with Your Smartphone?

The authority you can exercise within the touch screen is much more powerful than you might think. [Now in preview: Codex in the ChatGPT mobile app - Codex - OpenAI Developer Community](https://community.openai.com/t/now-in-preview-codex-in-the-chatgpt-mobile-app/1380940) [GoogleNews-OpenAIlinksChatGPTmobileappwithCodexfor...](https://news.google.com/stories/CAAqNggKIjBDQklTSGpvSmMzUnZjbmt0TXpZd1NoRUtEd2pjcXUtTUVSSEFtOXN3Uzd3YW95Z0FQAQ?hl=en-MY&gl=MY&ceid=MY:en)

*   **Start new work**: Had a brilliant idea while walking after lunch? Instruct the AI to organize it immediately via your smartphone. Your office computer far away will start working instantly.
*   **Review outputs**: Scrutinize the code or analysis results the AI wrote overnight while on the subway. You can leisurely check for any logical errors.
*   **Steer execution**: If the AI is heading in the wrong direction, provide immediate feedback. You can correct its course in real-time by saying, "This method is inefficient; try a different algorithm."
*   **Approve next steps**: This is the most crucial feature. When the AI needs to make a significant decision, it doesn't just push forward; it sends you a notification requesting 'approval.' You simply check the content and approve with a single touch.

## What's Next?

This update signals a massive upheaval in the way we work. Experts say we have finally entered the era of **'long-running agent supervision.'** [OpenAI brings Codex to ChatGPT mobile app for iOS and Android](https://www.testingcatalog.com/openai-brings-codex-to-chatgpt-mobile-app-for-ios-and-android/)

If past AI was a 'sprinter' answering short questions, it is now evolving into a 'marathoner' performing large projects that take several days. As projects get longer, it becomes difficult for humans to keep watching the monitor. At this point, remote supervision via smartphone becomes an essential element for survival.
