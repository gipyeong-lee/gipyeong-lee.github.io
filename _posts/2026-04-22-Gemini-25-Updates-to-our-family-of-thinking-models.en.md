Imagine this. When you ask a very difficult math problem or a tangled travel plan, instead of the AI throwing an answer in just a second, it says, **"Hmm, wait a moment. Let me check one more time if this method I thought of is really correct."**

Not like a student who writes down the answer as soon as they get the test paper, but like a top student who meticulously writes down the solution process on scratch paper and double-checks it themselves. While previous AIs focused on 'immediately' finding the most plausible answer to the questions we asked, Google's newly introduced **Gemini 2.5** has opened the era of 'thinking models' that review their own logic before speaking [Gemini 2.5: Updates to our family of thinking models](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/gemini-2-5-thinking-model-updates/). Now, AI is evolving beyond simply being smooth-talking to actually 'thinking' like a human.

## Why is this important?

Why should we bother giving AI 'time to think'? Think about when you write an important report at work or write sophisticated programming code. We know from experience that the second thought, which is reviewed once more with "Wait, is this really the best?", is much more accurate and has fewer mistakes than the first idea that comes to mind intuitively.

Gemini 2.5 has officially implemented this 'review process' inside the AI. Through this, it has drastically reduced the 'hallucination' phenomenon where AI tells plausible lies. Especially in fields like mathematics, coding, and scientific reasoning where logical thinking is essential, it shows a level of sophistication that is on a different dimension from previous models [Gemini 2.5: Our newest Gemini model with thinking - The Keyword](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/gemini-model-thinking-updates-march-2025/).

This change will change the way we treat AI itself. This is because it becomes the core driving force for building an **'Agent (an intelligent assistant that performs tasks on behalf of the user)'** system that deeply understands the user's intention and judges and performs complex tasks on its own, going beyond a search bar level assistant that simply answers questions [Gemini 2.5: Pushing the Frontier with Advanced Reasoning ...](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/gemini/gemini_v2_5_report.pdf).

## Easy Understanding: What is AI 'Thinking'?

### 1. The 'Solution Process' Before Answering (Reasoning)
If existing AI was a way of shouting "The answer is A!" as soon as it received a question, **Gemini 2.5 takes logical steps as if organizing its thoughts in a notepad first before generating an answer.** This is professionally called **'Reasoning'** [Gemini 2.5: Updates to our family of thinking models](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/gemini-2-5-thinking-model-updates/).

In simple terms, instead of just writing the answer when solving a descriptive problem, it meticulously goes through the intermediate process of "checking condition 1, applying formula A, and then checking if the result is common sense." Thanks to this process, Gemini 2.5 can produce results that are much more persuasive and have fewer errors.

### 2. Adjusting the 'Thinking Budget'
The most interesting thing about Gemini 2.5 is that you can leave it to the AI to decide **'how much energy to spend thinking deeply about this problem.'** This is called a **'Thinking budget'** [Gemini 2.5: Updates to our family of thinking models](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/gemini-2-5-thinking-model-updates/).

For example, for a light question like "Recommend today's lunch menu," it thinks briefly and answers immediately. However, for a difficult question like "Analyze the weaknesses of our company's marketing strategy for next year," it invests more 'thinking budget' to get a deep answer. It's the same principle as the time we spend choosing a lunch menu being different from the time we spend agonizing over signing a house contract.

### 3. AI with Five Senses (Multimodal)
Gemini 2.5 is a **natively multimodal** model from birth. Here, multimodal refers to the ability to understand and process text, images, video, and audio simultaneously [Gemini 2.5: Pushing the Frontier with Advanced Reasoning ...](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/gemini/gemini_v2_5_report.pdf).

It's not just at the level of matching objects in a photo. You can ask it to summarize the key points of a long hour-long lecture video, or look at a complex design drawing image and find logical design flaws. It's easy to understand it as a form where the eyes, ears, and thinking brain are perfectly combined into one.

## Imagine: The Future Created by Gemini 2.5

Shall we draw a scenario? You are lost in an unfamiliar city while traveling abroad, but your budget is limited and you only have two hours left until the next train.

At this time, if you explain the situation to Gemini 2.5, the AI starts 'thinking' instead of immediately listing nearby restaurants. It takes into account 'the distance from the current location to the train station', 'the types of food you can eat with the remaining budget', and 'the average waiting time for the food'. Then, it suggests the most rational route and menu. This is the power of 'reasoning' beyond a simple answer.

## Current Status: Members of the Gemini 2.5 Family

On June 17, 2025, Google officially released the major models of the Gemini 2.5 series [Gemini (language model) - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_(language_model)). Each model is divided into three, like team members with different roles.

- **Gemini 2.5 Pro**: The 'genius type' of this family. It has achieved state-of-the-art (SoTA) scores in coding and complex scientific reasoning benchmarks (performance measurement standards). Enterprise solution experts evaluate it as "the most advanced and capable model in existence" [Expanding Gemini 2.5 Flash and Pro capabilities - Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/expanding-gemini-2-5-flash-and-pro-capabilities). In particular, when using a mode called **'Deep Think'**, it demonstrates overwhelming thinking power when solving complex challenges.
- **Gemini 2.5 Flash**: The 'fast and smart multi-player.' With an excellent balance of speed and performance, it is most suitable for processing large-scale data or running real-time conversational services and AI agents [Gemini 2.5 Flash | Gemini API | Google AI for Developers](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models/gemini-2.5-flash).
- **Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite**: The 'youngest with the best cost-performance.' While maintaining performance, it drastically lowers operating costs, shining when simple and repetitive tasks need to be processed in large quantities [Gemini 2.5: Updates to our family of thinking models (Engineering.fyi)](https://www.engineering.fyi/article/gemini-2-5-updates-to-our-family-of-thinking-models).

## Special Benefits for Students

Google also held a special event to distribute this powerful technology to educational sites. It provided a **'Google AI Pro' 1-year free upgrade benefit** to students aged 18 and older in five major countries, including South Korea [Gemini Apps’ release updates & improvements](https://gemini.google/release-notes/). Through this, students received great help in their studies, such as analyzing complex papers or generating study quizzes using the performance of Gemini 2.5. (The benefit was provided until October 6, 2025.)

## What Happens Next?

Google plans to **include this 'thinking ability' as a standard in all future AI models** [Gemini 2.5: Our newest Gemini model with thinking - The Keyword](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/gemini-model-thinking-updates-march-2025/).

The goal is not just to make a smarter chatbot. It is an essential stepping stone to the era of 'autonomous AI agents' that sort emails for us, coordinate schedules, and manage complex projects. Now, AI is evolving from a passive tool that only does what it's told to an active partner that judges situations on its own and thinks about the best path. Gemini 2.5 will be the clearest signpost to that 'thinking future.'
