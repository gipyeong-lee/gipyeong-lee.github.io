Imagine this: You arrive at the office in the morning and send a single message to your AI: "Organize the decisions made in yesterday's team meeting and send them by email." A few minutes later, the AI has drafted a clean version, taking into account the Slack conversations you had yesterday, relevant documents in your Gmail, and the project priorities established the previous day. While the AI we’ve known so far has been limited to 'searching' for vast amounts of information or writing text, we are now seeing the emergence of 'colleagues who share the context of work'—AI that deeply understands the intricate details of our company and works alongside us like a teammate.

The star of this shift is **Almanac**, selected for the Summer 2026 batch (YC S26) of Y Combinator, the world-renowned gateway for startups. Almanac operates not just as a chatbot that finds information, but as a 'smart assistant' that knows every bit of the company's history. [Source 1](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49511007), [Source 4](https://ai-minor.com/blog/ja/2026-09-01-1788195919503-launch_hn__almanac__yc_s26____ai_that_knows_your_c/)

### Why It Matters

The generative AI we use daily is convenient, but it often forgets previous context once a conversation ends. In particular, it often provides generic answers because it lacks knowledge of complex internal company affairs or the subtle decision-making processes between teams. Almanac is different. It learns and remembers 'the company's own context,' including who the employees are, projects currently in progress, and the team's decision-making style. [Source 4](https://ai-minor.com/blog/ja/2026-09-01-1788195919503-launch_hn__almanac__yc_s26____ai_that_knows_your_c/), [Source 9](https://www.getreadyforagents.com/news/almanac-company-context-agent/)

How will this change the daily lives of office workers? The biggest change is the automation of 'reporting' and 'management.' Users simply need to command via Slack or iMessage: "Process expenses," "Organize meeting minutes," or "Review this code." [Source 3](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/y-combinator_almanac-yc-s26-is-the-agent-with-a-company-activity-7493692848073269248-H01t), [Source 6](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/almanac) This is because the AI directly uses your work tool accounts to actually perform the work. This will gift us time to step away from simple, repetitive administrative tasks and focus on much more creative and valuable deep thinking.

### The Explainer

To understand Almanac better, let’s use a metaphor. If existing AI chatbots are 'librarians in an internet library,' Almanac is a 'seasoned assistant who has worked with us in the company for years.'

*   **Librarian (Existing AI):** Learned in encyclopedia knowledge, but doesn't know who made what decision in our company Slack room yesterday.
*   **Seasoned Assistant (Almanac):** Knows company culture, understands who is responsible for which tasks, and meticulously remembers even my personal work style.

Almanac operates while always switched on, running on its own dedicated computer. [Source 5](https://usealmanac.com/), [Source 7](https://zeli.app/story/49511007) It is the same principle as an actual employee sitting at their desk, staying logged into Slack, Gmail, and Google Docs, and checking for new updates throughout the day. Because of this, Almanac doesn't miss what happens at the company even when you're away, recording events, summarizing necessary documents, and building a self-sustaining shared knowledge layer for the organization. [Source 7](https://zeli.app/story/49511007), [Source 8](https://www.linkedin.com/company/codealmanac)

### Where We Stand

Currently, Almanac has reached a stage where it can skillfully perform various practical tasks such as analyzing user feedback, meeting management, coding support, recruitment, and expense settlement upon the user's instructions. [Source 3](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/y-combinator_almanac-yc-s26-is-the-agent-with-a-company-activity-7493692848073269248-H01t) In particular, it provides a shared knowledge layer for technical teams, acting as a reliable partner that helps coding agents within the team write code more efficiently. [Source 8](https://www.linkedin.com/company/codealmanac)

Of course, Almanac isn't capable of handling everything. It has limitations in strategic decision-making that requires high-level human judgment or in areas where AI access is restricted for security reasons. Almanac adopts a structure where the user delegates tasks and the AI reports the results. Therefore, now is the time when 'management ability'—where the user guides the AI agent's actions in the right direction—has become more important than just knowing how to use AI well. [Source 5](https://usealmanac.com/)

### What's Next

Looking ahead, AI agents are expected to go beyond individual services and play a pivotal role as 'hubs' that connect an organization's entire information set. The founder who developed Almanac has even described the service as "Hermes with a brain," implying it knows everything about the company. [Source 1](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49511007)

In the near future, each of us will have an agent like this by our side, allowing us to handle vast amounts of work as if we had multiple actual teammates. We are entering an era where your agent will communicate with your colleagues' agents to set up meeting times and coordinate project deadlines with one another. Perhaps we now need to reduce the time spent worrying about 'what to do' and focus on 'how to delegate work effectively to our AI assistant.'
