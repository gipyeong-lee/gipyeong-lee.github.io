## Why Does This Matter?

To the general public, artificial intelligence is a "smart assistant," but to corporations, it is an "ultra-costly advancement game." ## Easy to Understand: The Delicate Tug-of-War Between AI and Capital

### 1. A Unique Mechanism Called Five Stock Classes
When typical companies go public, they issue a relatively simple stock structure. However, Anthropic's initial public offering (IPO) drew immense attention by including five different classes of stock [Source 15]. 

To make this easier to understand, let's think about a school festival. When preparing for a festival, students are assigned roles: general participants, the student council leading the planning, and a supervisory committee responsible for safeguarding the safety and direction of the festival until the end. By designing different voting rights and profit distribution structures, they have built sophisticated safeguards to ensure the company does not abandon AI safety in pursuit of short-term profits.

### 2. Technological Leap: The Riemann Hypothesis and the Potential of AI
Aside from governance, there is another piece of news that has astonished academic circles and the tech industry worldwide: a mathematician at Anthropic announced the proof of the "Riemann hypothesis" (an unsolved 19th-century mathematical hypothesis concerning the distribution of prime numbers), one of mathematics' greatest unsolved problems, using AI [Source 19]. 

The Riemann hypothesis is a riddle regarding the regularity of prime numbers that has plagued mathematicians since the 19th century. By analogy, it is like finding a single cosmic equation that perfectly explains the locations of countless stars scattered across the universe. Using the powerful reasoning capabilities of AI, they found a path through this massive labyrinth that would be difficult for the human brain to solve even in a lifetime. Of course, verification will take considerable time, but this is a decisive scene showing that AI is evolving beyond a mere sentence generator into a tool that expands the horizons of human knowledge [Source 19].

## Current Situation: A Stage Where Glory and Concern Intersect

On November 19, 2026, Anthropic's historic initial public offering (IPO) took place at the Nasdaq MarketSite in New York, USA [Source 17]. Co-founders Chairwoman Daniela Amodei and Chief Executive Officer (CEO) Dario Amodei stood side by side to open a new chapter for the company [Source 17]. 

Anthropic operates as a Public Benefit Corporation (PBC), balancing public interest with shareholder returns, and reaches users worldwide through powerful reasoning and creative Claude models such as Fable, Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku [Source 5]. Furthermore, the company has continued to emphasize safety by demonstrating live demos of "Constitutional Classifiers" (technology that filters and judges harmful content according to a constitutional set of ethical principles that AI must follow) to prevent AI from operating in dangerous or harmful ways [Source 18].

However, not all eyes are fixed on celebration. Some have raised sharp criticisms regarding the financial pressures facing the AI industry. Renowned journalist and author Ed Zitron pointed out regarding Anthropic's fundraising strategy, "Seeing them head toward the already stressed junk bond market [high-risk, high-yield bonds issued by companies with low credit ratings] makes me concerned about where they are headed next" [Source 2]. Because endless, massive capital is required for technology development, macroeconomic environments and capital market pressures remain factors that can trip up a company at any time.
