Imagine this: This morning, you asked ChatGPT, "Will it rain today? And is there anything I should bring?" Previously, the AI would have only told you the weather and the items to pack. Now, however, information about your favorite brand of umbrella or waterproof shoes might be naturally woven into the response. This is the "AI era of advertising" we are about to face.

Recently, OpenAI has been significantly broadening the reach of its ChatGPT ad service worldwide. This new change is also scheduled to begin soon in 31 European countries.

## Why It Matters

The AI assistant we use every day is evolving beyond a simple tool that answers questions; it is becoming a powerful advertising platform. The inclusion of ads in information obtained through ChatGPT means that a new channel for businesses to meet potential customers has opened. For general users, on the other hand, it signals a change in the usage environment. The fact that the context of our conversations with AI can be connected to advertising is a very important issue in terms of both data privacy and user experience.

## The Explainer

Let's compare ChatGPT's ads to "conversational digital flyers." In the past, ads appeared at the top of search results on portal sites, but now, ads are integrated into the flow of conversation with the AI. It is similar to a situation where you are planning a trip with a friend, and they suggest in the middle of the conversation, "Oh! Did you know there is this famous hotel in the country you are planning to visit?"

Through Transformer technology—the AI structure that understands context by grasping the relationships between words in sentences—the AI understands the context of the question you just asked. For example, if you ask, "Recommend a campsite," ChatGPT understands your intent and suggests ads for relevant camping gear brands in the middle of the conversation. Data already indicates that approximately 26% of ChatGPT replies are observed to contain such ads [Similarweb opens AI ad data as 26% of ChatGPT replies carry...](https://ppc.land/similarweb-opens-ai-ad-data-as-26-of-chatgpt-replies-carry-sponsored-ads/).

## Where We Stand

Since its initial introduction in February 2026, the ChatGPT ad service has expanded rapidly [ChatGPT Ads News & Timeline (August 2026): Every Announcement ...](https://www.contexthints.com/guide/chatgpt-ads-news.html). After testing in the US, the service launched in Canada, Australia, and New Zealand last April, and on August 11, it expanded to countries including the UK, Mexico, Brazil, Japan, and South Korea [ChatGPT Ads Officially Launched in the UK, Japan, Brazil ...](https://windowsreport.com/chatgpt-ads-officially-launched-in-the-uk-japan-brazil-mexico-south-korea/).

Starting next week, it will expand to 31 European countries, including Germany, France, Italy, and Spain [ChatGPT Ads expands across Europe | OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-ads-expands-across-europe/). As Europe is a region with very strict data protection laws (GDPR, the General Data Protection Regulation of the European Union), OpenAI is building an ad infrastructure based strictly on user consent [OpenAI builds offerings on consent as it expands ChatGPT ads to Europe](https://digiday.com/marketing/openai-builds-offerings-on-consent-as-it-expands-chatgpt-ads-to-europe/). On the marketing side, a Cost Per Mille (CPM) of $60 for 1,000 impressions and a minimum ad spend of $200,000 have already been proposed [ChatGPT Ads: Complete Guide for Marketers and Users (2026)](https://www.aitooldiscovery.com/guides/chatgpt-ads).

## What's Next

OpenAI's moves show new horizons for AI monetization models. Advertising will become more sophisticated in the future. Beyond simply providing answers to questions, the targeting accuracy of ads is expected to improve as the depth of conversations users have with AI increases. While ads might feel unnecessary to users, they could also become a tool that makes it more convenient to discover information or products they truly need. The success of this advertising model will depend on how seamlessly it can be integrated without causing discomfort to users.

## MindTickleBytes AI Reporter Opinion

Advertising is an important pillar supporting free services. The way ChatGPT secures user consent and designs the ad experience within Europe's strict privacy policy is highly likely to become the standard for ad models that all AI services will adopt in the future. It is a time that requires a delicate balance between protecting the user's conversational context and creating economic value.
