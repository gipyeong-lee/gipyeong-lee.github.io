Imagine this: An engineer is struggling in front of a complex mechanical blueprint. In the past, it would have taken hours to compare drawings and write test code one by one, but now they say to their intelligent AI assistant 'Claude' sitting next to them, "Look at this blueprint, write test code to verify the connection status between components, and run it." Surprisingly, the AI completes the task without losing context over the long duration.

This is no longer a story of the future. Recently, Anthropic (the AI company that created Claude) is evolving Claude not just as a tool for chatting, but as a 'colleague' that assists with practical tasks in real industrial settings. [Introduction to Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ugvHCXCOmm4)

## Why is this important?

The most notable point is that AI is not just staying in the digital space of a chat window, but has deeply entered the physical work environment. Companies like UST are using Claude to analyze engineering design blueprints and pinouts (circuit connection information), and are tasking it with designing and executing tests based on this. [UST's Claude Use Case](https://www.anthropic.com/news/ust-claude)

Moving beyond an AI that simply writes text, it has become capable of accurately understanding engineering context and performing complex tasks that take hours from start to finish. This means an environment has been created where engineers can escape simple repetitive tasks and focus on more creative problem-solving.

## Easy to Understand

To use a simple analogy for Claude's recent changes, it's as if a 'smart pen pal' has been promoted to an 'experienced project manager.'

1. **Engineer's Assistant (Claude Code)**: If it was just a friend who was good at talking at first, it now understands the technical language of complex 'blueprints.' It's like having a very smart research assistant who looks at blueprints and pre-tests things for you. [UST's Claude Use Case](https://www.anthropic.com/news/ust-claude)

2. **Work Health Checkup (Reflect Dashboard)**: The recently added 'Reflect' feature is like a fitness app or a smartwatch. It analyzes and shows activity trends and work types so you can see when, how, and for what purpose you are using AI. [Introduction to Reflect Dashboard](https://www.testingcatalog.com/anthropic-adds-usage-reflection-dashboard-to-claude-for-all-users/) It even cares for the user's work health by allowing you to set 'break reminders' that notify you, "You have used AI too much today, take a break." [Technical Analysis](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/anthropics-new-claude-feature-is-quietly-selling-you-on-ai/)

## Current Status

Claude is growing into an all-encompassing assistant spanning mobile and web. In particular, the 'Claude Cowork' feature is now accessible directly from the sidebar of iOS, iPadOS, and Android apps, as well as the website (claude.ai). [Claude Cowork Expansion News](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/07/anthropic-expanding-claude-cowork-to-mobile-and-web-details-here/) Anthropic is also holding an event to double the Cowork usage limit until August 5, 2026, to make it easier for users to experience this feature. [Usage Limit Expansion Info](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/07/anthropic-expanding-claude-cowork-to-mobile-and-web-details-here/)

However, this ironically also shows how much we are relying on AI for our work. While the analysis dashboard is useful, it also makes us reflect on how much of our daily work we are entrusting to AI. [Psychological Aspects of AI Usage](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/anthropics-new-claude-feature-is-quietly-selling-you-on-ai/)

## What will happen in the future?

The concept of 'Coworking' with AI will become more natural. Beyond just asking questions, understanding the entire context of projects, managing files, and researching will become daily routines. [Learn Claude Cowork](https://anthropic.skilljar.com/introduction-to-claude-cowork)

Users have now reached a point where, beyond just how well they utilize AI, they must consider 'how to coexist more healthily with AI' while checking their work habits through data.

## MindTickleBytes AI Reporter's View

AI is changing from a human tool to a partner. The expansion into professional domains, as seen in the UST case, is very encouraging. However, as AI enters our lives deeply, as shown by the 'Reflect' dashboard, the ability to check one's digital balance rather than being dragged around by machines will become more important than ever.
