# AI Unlocking the Secrets of Science: CERN and Mistral Join Forces

## AI Opening a New Era of Science, with CERN and Mistral AI at the Center

Imagine scientists working to discover new planets by analyzing data from countless stars every day, exploring the secrets of the deep ocean, and striving to find answers to the fundamental questions of the universe. However, all these processes come with the massive challenge of processing vast amounts of data and identifying complex patterns. What if this process could be significantly accelerated thanks to artificial intelligence (AI)?

Recently, CERN (the European Organization for Nuclear Research), Europe's leading particle physics research laboratory, and French AI leader Mistral AI have joined forces. Their goal is to accelerate the advancement of scientific research, particularly in particle physics, using AI technology. All eyes in the global scientific community are on what kind of astonishing synergy will be created when CERN—a massive laboratory where the world’s top physicists gather to uncover the secrets of the universe using cutting-edge equipment—meets Mistral AI, a pioneer in AI technology that mimics human intelligence. This collaboration has the potential to fundamentally change how humanity understands the world. ## Why Is This Important? – The Power of AI to Decipher Big Science Data

Modern science, particularly particle physics research conducted at facilities like CERN’s Large Hadron Collider (LHC), generates an unimaginably large amount of data. Tens of millions of particle collisions occur every second at the LHC, and all of this collision data is like a treasure map containing the secrets hidden by the universe. However, reading this 'treasure map' and finding hidden clues is beyond human capability alone.

Put simply, it is similar to a situation where you need to find a specific word in a library where thousands of books are falling at once. AI excels at finding complex patterns that would take humans decades to identify and quickly sensing meaningful signals within vast amounts of data. The partnership between CERN and Mistral AI focuses on this very point. It aims to dramatically accelerate the scientific discovery process by leveraging AI in areas where existing specialized algorithms or human analytical capabilities have had limitations. This goes beyond just increasing research speed; it could play a decisive role in discovering new physical laws or fundamental principles of the universe that we were previously unaware of. ## Understanding: What Is a 'Large Physics Model'?

At the heart of this collaboration is the development of a new AI model called the 'Large Physics Model' (LPM). The LPM is set to become the first foundation model specifically designed for the field of particle physics. A foundation model refers to AI that has been trained on vast amounts of data to possess general-purpose capabilities, allowing it to be applied to various fields rather than being limited to a specific task. Just as learning a language allows you to read and write various texts, the LPM is essentially learning the language of physics. ### Training AI with Billions of Simulations

The LPM is scheduled to be pre-trained based on billions of simulated collision data points. [Source: CERN and Mistral sign agreement to build AI for particle ...](https://superpowerdaily.com/posts/cern-and-mistral-sign-agreement-to-build-ai-for-particle-physics-research) [Source: CERN and Mistral AI Plan a Large Physics Model for the LHC](https://www.supercomputing.news/ai/cern-mistral-large-physics-model-lhc-reconstruction)

Metaphorically, it is like an AI detective that has looked at billions of puzzle piece pictures beforehand to learn their characteristics in order to solve a complex puzzle. By learning such various collision scenarios, the LPM will help analyze the complex collision data occurring at the actual LHC, quickly identifying which patterns could lead to new discoveries or which results differ from existing theories. This is no different from implementing the intuition gained by a genius physicist through decades of research experience into AI.

### Current Status: The Journey to Find Hidden Truths Within Data

Until now, scientists have explored the secrets of the universe based on CERN's cutting-edge equipment, sophisticated analysis tools, and their own exceptional insights. However, as the scale of experimental data grows exponentially, there are moments when human analytical capabilities reach their limits. Finding rare but decisive clues within vast data is comparable to finding a single grain of sand with a specific composition in a vast desert.

In this situation, the emergence of AI, especially specialized foundation models like the LPM, is very important. It will help researchers significantly shorten the time required for data analysis, allowing them to focus on more creative and essential research, such as theory development or new experimental design. Just as Mistral AI is expanding the application scope of AI technology by collaborating with various industry partners, its partnership with CERN will allow AI to demonstrate its true value in the new territory of science. ## What Comes Next? – The Endless Possibilities of Converging AI and Science

The collaboration between CERN and Mistral AI is not limited to specific research projects. It will serve as an important precedent showing how AI can change the paradigm of scientific research. If the development of the LPM proceeds successfully, we will be able to see AI-based 'foundation models' in many more scientific fields in the future.

For example, these AI models have the potential to revolutionize research processes in all fields that handle vast amounts of data, such as climate change modeling, drug discovery, and materials science. The results combining the strengths of Mistral AI’s platform with CERN’s scientific expertise will also contribute to Europe strengthening its technological independence and solidifying its position as an AI leader.
