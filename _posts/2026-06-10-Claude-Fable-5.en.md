Imagine this: Suppose you've just hired a new 'genius assistant' who effortlessly handles everything from highly complex math problems and cutting-edge software programming to analyzing convoluted legal documents. This assistant is so intelligent that it can perfectly understand and summarize hundreds of pages of documents and complex images you casually throw at it in just a few seconds.

However, this seemingly perfect assistant has a very unique and critical weakness, or rather, a distinctive trait. The moment you ask, "How do I make an explosive?" or "Tell me how to secretly hack a competitor's security network," this genius assistant suddenly zips its lips. It then subtly pushes forward an 'older assistant' standing behind it—someone more experienced but rather conservative and strictly by-the-book—forcing them to answer you instead.

This isn't a story about robots from a sci-fi movie. It is the actual reality of the latest artificial intelligence we face today. This is the hidden story behind **'Claude Fable 5'**, the newly unveiled artificial intelligence from Anthropic, widely considered ChatGPT's strongest rival. Let's take a closer look at just how smart this new AI is and why it chose such a unique approach.

---

## Why It Matters

Recently, Anthropic surprised the public by unveiling its new AI model, 'Claude Fable 5' [Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 is a version of Mythos the public can access today](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/09/anthropics-claude-fable-5-is-a-version-of-mythos-the-public-can-access-today/). The reason this news has the IT industry and tech experts buzzing isn't just the fact that a 'new version was released,' but rather the special 'pedigree' this model possesses.

Previously, the highest-tier AI Anthropic provided to general users was named 'Opus'. But in truth, deep within Anthropic's labs, a legendary tier called **'Mythos'**, boasting a level of intelligence one step above Opus, existed secretly. 

Because this Mythos technology was so powerful and impactful, since April 2025, it had been provided secretly under the code name 'Project Glasswing' only to cybersecurity defenders protecting critical national infrastructure or a very small group of experts [Anthropic brings Mythos to the masses with Claude Fable 5, its most powerful generally available model ever | VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-brings-mythos-to-the-masses-with-claude-fable-5-its-most-powerful-generally-available-model-ever).

The newly announced 'Claude Fable 5' is a model that refines the capabilities of this formidable 'Mythos' tier so that the general public can use it safely for the first time [The Release of Claude Fable 5 and How Automatic Model Switching Works During Conversations](https://tali.kr/claude-fable5-model-switch). 

To use a simple analogy, it's like this. Until now, there was a state-of-the-art 'sports science training center (Mythos)' available only to elite, national-level athletes competing in the Olympics. But now, that training center has opened its doors wide to the public (Fable 5), allowing ordinary citizens like us to use those incredible training machines firsthand at our local neighborhood gym. A colossal brain that assists humans in 'knowledge work'—tasks that require mental effort such as drafting proposals, analyzing data, and coding—has finally taken a massive step into our everyday lives.

## The Explainer

So, what specific capabilities does the publicly released Claude Fable 5 possess? 

This AI goes far beyond simply reading our text and writing smooth responses. It can simultaneously take in massive texts, complex images, and tricky file inputs provided by the user and analyze them comprehensively [ClaudeFable5- API Pricing & Providers | OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/anthropic/claude-fable-5). It specializes in autonomously judging situations to design complex software architectures or autonomously organizing intertwined knowledge information. 

Furthermore, it comes packed with powerful new tools for developers, such as a Vision feature that deeply understands photos and illustrations, a Memory tool that intelligently retrieves the context of past conversations with users, and Task budgets that allow it to autonomously adjust how much computational resources to use when performing complex tasks [Introducing Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 - Claude API Docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/introducing-claude-fable-5-and-claude-mythos-5). 

However, the true value of this innovative technology lies in the **'Guardrails'** hidden behind the model's specs themselves.

Claude Fable 5 is designed to firmly refuse to answer questions about 'High-risk areas' that could pose a major threat to humanity, such as exploiting cybersecurity blind spots or learning how to manufacture lethal biological weapons [Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 is a version of Mythos the public can access today](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/09/anthropics-claude-fable-5-is-a-version-of-mythos-the-public-can-access-today/). 

What's interesting is how it refuses. It doesn't just pop up a cold error message saying, "I cannot answer due to regulations," and cut off the conversation. When the system detects signs of danger in the question, it quickly intercepts it behind the scenes and tosses (routes) it to **'Opus 4.8'**, an older model whose safety has already been thoroughly vetted [Claude Fable 5 & Claude Mythos 5 Full Benchmark Breakdown](https://www.vellum.ai/blog/claude-fable-5-and-mythos-5-benchmarks-explained). 

As an analogy, imagine this situation. You go to a top-tier, 3-star Michelin restaurant and ask the genius chef (Fable 5) to cook for you. This chef creates unimaginably perfect dishes, from steaks to elaborate desserts. But the moment you make a dangerous request to the chef, like "Cook me a poisonous pufferfish without removing the toxins," the kitchen's emergency alarm goes off. The genius chef immediately steps back, and in their place, a seasoned, conservative chef (Opus 4.8) who has stubbornly stuck to safe, orthodox cooking for decades steps up to serve you according to regulations [Anthropic releases its first Mythos-class model Claude Fable | The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/news/946725/anthropic-releases-claude-fable-5-mythos).

Because the AI's capabilities have become overly powerful, an intelligent mechanism was established where the AI puts the 'brakes' on its own brilliance to prevent the uncontrollable fallout that could occur if those capabilities were maliciously exploited.

## Where We Stand

Claude Fable 5's overwhelming prowess is already clearly proven by objective figures. According to the provisional leaderboard of BenchLM.ai, a famous AI benchmark site, Claude Fable 5 scored an impressive 96 out of 100, proudly securing 2nd place among the 123 artificial intelligence models evaluated [ClaudeFable5Benchmarks 2026: Scores, Rankings... | BenchLM.ai](https://benchlm.ai/models/claude-fable). It has firmly established itself at the very top on a global stage where numerous formidable AIs compete.

Some users might worry about the user experience deteriorating, thinking, "If it switches to an older model upon detecting danger, won't it feel choppy or frustrating to use?" However, according to Anthropic's meticulous test results, in 95% of user conversation sessions with this AI, Fable 5 handled everything entirely on its own without relying on the older model (Opus 4.8) at all [Anthropic releases its first Mythos-class model Claude Fable | The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/news/946725/anthropic-releases-claude-fable-5-mythos). In other words, 95 times out of 100 everyday questions, you can fully enjoy the genius AI's capabilities comfortably and smoothly without the cumbersome process of model switching.

Currently, Claude Fable 5 is provided via the Claude API, helping general developers and companies integrate it into their services [ClaudeFable\ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude/fable). Additionally, it is now officially available on 'Amazon Bedrock,' the AI platform of Amazon, a powerhouse in the enterprise cloud market [Claude Fable 5 from Anthropic now available on Amazon Bedrock](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/claude-fable-5-anthropic-available-amazon-bedrock). 

One unique aspect is the pricing policy for enterprises. For companies that are highly reluctant to let their sensitive data pass to overseas servers, an option can be set that forces data processing to occur only within the United States (US-only inference). However, if this safe, dedicated network is selected, the data costs based on usage (input and output token costs) are charged 1.1 times higher than the base price [ClaudeFable\ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude/fable). (It's like paying about a 10% security premium.)

There is a downside, however. No matter how excellent and powerful Fable 5 is, it is ultimately just a version that softly refines the power of the original 'Mythos' technology for the public. The original 'Claude Mythos 5' itself, which possesses the raw, maximum performance and potential, remains tightly hidden behind trusted controls and is still secretly provided only to a very limited number of experts whose safety has been vetted [Anthropic launches Claude Fable 5 with trusted controls | ETIH EdTech News — EdTech Innovation Hub](https://www.edtechinnovationhub.com/news/anthropic-opens-access-to-claude-fable-5-while-keeping-mythos-5-behind-trusted-controls).

## What's Next

The emergence of Claude Fable 5 throws a very important and new talking point at our society. Just a few years ago, humanity's concern was limited to, "How can we make artificial intelligence as smart as humans?" But now, the era has changed. Our question has completely evolved to, "How do we control and safely use in everyday life the immense capabilities of an AI that has become too smart, surpassing humans?" [[In-Depth Analysis] Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5: The Emergence of AI 'Too Powerful' That Required Separate Safeguards](https://ttj.kr/tech-news/심층분석-claude-fable-5와-mythos-5-너무-강력해서-안전장치를-따로-단-ai가-등장했어요). 

Fable 5's unique 'model switching (routing)' method, which autonomously grasps a question's risk level and hands over difficult or dangerous topics to an older model, is quite shocking. This technology is highly likely to become the 'new safety standard' that countless hyper-scale AIs appearing alongside us in the future must adopt. The method intelligently combines the most innovative and smart brain (Mythos) with conservative brakes (Opus) that stop slowly but surely. This is the most realistic compromise to protect the bottom line of human safety without artificially slowing down the dazzling pace of AI development. 

Before long, we might think we are simply conversing with a single AI app on our smartphones on the surface. But behind that invisible screen, depending on the weight and danger of the questions we throw at it, we will face an intriguing era where multiple diverse AI models take turns, like passing a baton in a relay race, to complete the answers.
