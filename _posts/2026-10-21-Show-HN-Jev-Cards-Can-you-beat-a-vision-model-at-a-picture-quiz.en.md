Imagine this: You’ve started a quiz game where you have to find objects hidden in complex pictures. The moment you blink, the artificial intelligence (AI) has already picked the correct answer and moved on to the next question. In just 0.25 seconds. Is something like this really possible?

Recently, a new web quiz game called 'Jev Cards' has appeared on online communities and is generating buzz. It’s not just a game to compete for scores with other people; it’s a game where you compete in real-time against 'Jev,' the latest vision AI model. [Hacker News - kherrick.github.io](https://kherrick.github.io/hacker-news/)

### Why is this game drawing attention?

Until now, the AI we have experienced has primarily been 'language models' that write long texts or have conversations. However, the technology showcased by 'Jev Cards' is on a different level. This is because, instead of spending time crafting complex sentences, this AI is specialized in looking at the image in front of it and making lightning-fast decisions about "what is where."

How will our daily lives change if this technology advances? We are rapidly approaching an era where AI acts as our 'eyes'—instantly identifying and purchasing products from a single photo, or helping autonomous vehicles judge unexpected situations on the road faster than humans can. [ShowHN:JevPlays Pokémon Red - Toksick Magazine](https://toksickmagazine.com/internet-culture/show-hn-jev-plays-pok-mon-red/)

### In simple terms: What is a 'System 1' AI?

'Jev' uses an approach that its developer, TypeSafe AI, calls a 'System 1' decision model.

To use an analogy, if existing AIs perform 'slow thinking' like a graduate student who carefully organizes their thoughts before writing an answer, Jev implements 'fast intuition' like a veteran athlete reacting instinctively and immediately.

*   **Existing AI:** Looks at an image, describes the situation, thinks logically, organizes the information, and outputs the answer as a sentence.
*   **Jev AI:** Immediately chooses the statistically most likely answer as soon as it sees the image and spits out the 'decision value' directly.

This AI doesn't need to parse text (the process where a computer analyzes sentences to understand them). It generates results just like we snap puzzle pieces into place the moment we see them, which is why it is extremely fast. [Jevvs LLMs: Where a DecisionModelBeats... | APIMaster.AI](https://apimaster.ai/blog/jev-vs-llm), [Generating levels in real time with theJevmodel- Sprite Fusion](https://www.spritefusion.com/blog/generating-game-level-in-real-time-with-jev)

### How fast and accurate is it?

According to TypeSafe AI, Jev is up to 200 times faster than traditional language models, and the cost is about 400 times cheaper. [System One Jev (Fully Explained): Does this really beat ...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIlQsncfVYs) In fact, independent test results showed that while document processing costs for existing LLMs are over $1–$3 per 1,000 requests, Jev is at the $0.22 level. [Jevvs LLMs: Where a DecisionModelBeats... | APIMaster.AI](https://apimaster.ai/blog/jev-vs-llm)

Of course, there are things to keep in mind. AI is not the best in every single aspect. According to recent benchmarks from Red Hat, other open-source AI models performed similarly to or better than Jev in areas like safety guidelines or complex security tasks. In other words, while Jev has overwhelming strengths in fields where 'speed and structural decisions' are important, it is not yet the perfect answer for every AI task. [Jev's decision models don't beat · 75 HN comments | Zeli](https://zeli.app/story/49933476)

### How far will play with AI go?

The release of this 'Jev Cards' game shows how deeply AI can enter the realm of entertainment we enjoy, rather than just remaining a smart assistant. Developers can create their own quiz cards, and users can compete against the AI or play games in real-time with friends. [Hacker News - kherrick.github.io](https://hacker-news-kherrick-github-io/), [Jev Cards — Jev App · JevList](https://jevlist.ai/projects/jev-cards)

Moving forward, it appears that fast and lightweight AI models like this will appear more frequently as 'digital assistants' that play games with us inside web browsers or handle complex tasks in real-time. How about a round of picture quizzes with Jev on your way home today? You'll have to keep your eyes wide open if you don't want to lose to the AI!
