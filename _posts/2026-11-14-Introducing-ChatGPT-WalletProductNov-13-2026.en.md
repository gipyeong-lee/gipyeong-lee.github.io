Imagine this: in the middle of a busy workday, you tell your AI, "Order the daily necessities I need for this month." The AI then finds the products you usually prefer, adds them to your cart, and completes the payment in one go. This scene, which seemed like something out of a movie, has now become a reality.

On November 13, 2026, OpenAI announced 'ChatGPT Wallet,' a new payment feature that allows AI agents (AI that performs specific tasks on behalf of a user) to make purchases for the user [Source 14](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/openai-chatgpt-wallet-dot-purchases.html), [Source 15](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/). AI is now evolving beyond the level of merely answering questions into a partner that directly assists with our economic activities.

## Why is this important?

The hassles of daily life are expected to decrease significantly. Previously, even if AI recommended products, the final payment step had to be handled by a human. However, as 'agents' (AI that understands the user's intent and achieves goals) can now execute payments, an environment has been created where repetitive shopping or booking tasks can be completely entrusted to AI [Source 15](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/), [Source 16](https://www.unite.ai/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-for-agent-assisted-purchases/).

In particular, this feature was built through partnerships with major payment providers such as American Express, Mastercard, and Visa, ensuring reliability [Source 10](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openai-introduces-chatgpt-wallet-visa-162102250.html), [Source 16](https://www.unite.ai/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-for-agent-assisted-purchases/), [Source 17](https://investfuture.ru/articles/open-ai-zapuszkaet-chat-gpt-wallet-dlya-pokupok-s-poschyu-ii). Thanks to this, users can enjoy the convenience of AI while worrying less about security.

## Easy to understand: The principle of a 'meal ticket' system

The core of ChatGPT Wallet is a 'Payment Token' system. Simply put, it's similar to using a 'meal ticket' instead of paying with actual cash in a company cafeteria.

The AI does not directly see or use your actual credit card number. Instead, it uses a secure 'payment token' connected between OpenAI and the card company [Source 10](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openai-introduces-chatgpt-wallet-visa-162102250.html), [Source 16](https://www.unite.ai/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-for-agent-assisted-purchases/). Because stores and AI agents only utilize this token information required for payment, the risk of the actual card number being exposed to the outside is fundamentally blocked.

Also, no matter how smart the AI is, spending money requires caution, so all payments are finalized only with the user's 'biometric authorization' [Source 17](https://investfuture.ru/articles/open-ai-zapuszkaet-chat-gpt-wallet-dlya-pokupok-s-poschyu-ii). It works on the same principle as recognizing your fingerprint or face when making a payment on your smartphone.

## Current status

ChatGPT Wallet is currently in the initial release (limited rollout) stage for OpenAI Pro subscribers aged 18 or older residing in the U.S. [Source 10](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openai-introduces-chatgpt-wallet-visa-162102250.html), [Source 15](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/).

If the existing ChatGPT was a 'smart intern' that merely searched for information or wrote code, it has now taken one step further into an agent that executes actual economic transactions with user authorization [Source 8](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openai-introduces-chatgpt-wallet-visa-162102250.html), [Source 16](https://www.unite.ai/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-for-agent-assisted-purchases/). However, as the service is currently limited to the U.S., it is too early for users around the world to experience it immediately [Source 10](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openai-introduces-chatgpt-wallet-visa-162102250.html), [Source 15](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/).

## What will happen in the future?

This announcement clearly shows how OpenAI intends to utilize AI in the future. It is interpreted as part of the process of expanding beyond a model that simply generates text into a 'Computer Use' model that acts directly within the user's environment [Source 15](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/), [Source 16](https://www.unite.ai/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-for-agent-assisted-purchases/).

It won't be long before these agents completely automate travel bookings, grocery shopping, subscription management, and more. Users will live in an era where they save time thinking about what to do and executing it, allowing them to focus on more creative and valuable tasks.

With the launch of ChatGPT Wallet, AI has been elevated from a 'consultant' to an 'agent' in the digital world.
