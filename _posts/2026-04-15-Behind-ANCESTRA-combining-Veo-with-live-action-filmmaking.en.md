Imagine this. What if a filmmaker could bring the fantastic dream scenes they only imagined to life as vivid, realistic videos with just a few words (prompts) and a single photo, instead of filming them directly with a camera? A world where directors use AI as a 'digital brush' to layer textures onto a film, much like a painter adding color to a canvas, has come closer to us.

A remarkable example where such a magical imagination became reality has emerged. It is the short film **'ANCESTRA'**, which debuted at the world-renowned **Tribeca Festival** [Behind "ANCESTRA": combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/ancestra-behind-the-scenes/). This film goes beyond simply listing AI-generated videos; it undertook the challenge of perfectly merging live-action footage (video captured with an actual camera) performed by real actors with surreal videos created by **'Veo'**, Google DeepMind's state-of-the-art video generation AI [Behind "ANCESTRA": combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://aipulselab.tech/news/behind-ancestra-combining-veo-with-live-action-filmmaking-52e6b9).

Today, we want to tell you the detailed behind-the-scenes story of this special film that broke the boundaries between technology and art, opening a new chapter in storytelling.

## Why is this important?

Until now, the AI videos we've encountered have mostly stayed within the realm of short YouTube clips or experimental media art. While they drew reactions like "Wow, that's amazing," they were somewhat lacking when it came to entering the grammar of traditional cinema. However, 'ANCESTRA' is in a different league. This is because it is a major project with the formal participation of master **Darren Aronofsky**, well-known for 'Black Swan' and 'The Whale,' and the talented visual artist and director **Eliza McNitt** [Behind "ANCESTRA": combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://itconsultingroup.com/behind-ancestra-combining-veo-with-live-action-filmmaking/).

The implications of this project are significant. AI has begun to play the role of a powerful **'collaborator'** that infinitely expands the creativity of actual masters, rather than just being a 'subsidiary tool' for filmmaking. Regarding the goal of this collaboration, Google DeepMind emphasized, "By putting the world's best generative AI technology into the hands of the best filmmakers, we are pushing the boundaries of storytelling and visual technology" [Behind "ANCESTRA": combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://onmine.io/behind-ancestra-combining-veo-with-live-action-filmmaking/).

In simple terms, AI hasn't pushed aside human directors to take their place; instead, it has become a reliable partner that helps bring to life areas of imagination that directors wanted to express but had to give up due to technical or financial limitations.

## Easy Understanding: An Enchanting Encounter Between AI and Live-Action Film

What story does 'ANCESTRA' tell? This film is a touching human drama depicting a mother's noble and deep love for a child born with a hole in their heart [Behind "ANCESTRA:" combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://article.wn.com/view/2025/06/13/Behind_ANCESTRA_combining_Veo_with_liveaction_filmmaking/).

An interesting fact is that this story was inspired by director Eliza McNitt's own real-life experience when she was born [Behind "ANCESTRA:" combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://article.wn.com/view/2025/06/13/Behind_ANCESTRA_combining_Veo_with_liveaction_filmmaking/). The director wanted to describe a mother's love not as a simple emotion, but as a mysterious life force, as if the universe were being created, and chose AI as the optimal tool to realize those unrealistic and dreamlike images.

**To use an analogy**, the filmmaking process is like making a delicious 'pizza.'
- The passionate performances of the real actors and the camera filming are like the process of making a chewy and solid **'dough (base).'**
- The fantastic video generated by AI is like the **'rich and colorful toppings'** added on top.

When these two are exquisitely combined, we experience a film with a new sense that we've never tasted before. To achieve this, the production team utilized three of Google's AIs step-by-step.

1.  **Gemini:** Acted as the 'brain' to design the overall tone and manner (atmosphere and emotional line) of the film and refine the flow of the script.
2.  **Imagen:** Generated high-definition images first that could provide visual inspiration based on the abstract ideas conceived by the director.
3.  **Veo:** As the protagonist of this project, it created high-quality video sequences (continuous scenes) that could be directly inserted into the actual film, based on the previously generated images and colors [Behind "ANCESTRA": combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/ancestra-behind-the-scenes/).

The AI video created this way was layered over the real actors' performances. For example, it became possible to produce scenes where streaks of light like the Milky Way, created by AI, wrap around a realistic scene of a mother holding her child [The Making of ANCESTRA | Darren Aronofsky x Google DeepMind](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WLDARkKs-T4).

## Behind-the-Scenes: Why 200 Experts Dedicated Themselves

You might think, "If AI does it all, didn't the director just whip it up in their room?" But the actual site was quite the opposite. To complete a single film like 'ANCESTRA,' a **large-scale army of over 200 people** was deployed [Combining Generative AI With Live-action Filmmaking](https://aifuturethinkers.com/combining-generative-ai-with-live-action-filmmaking/). This figure is comparable to the number of people involved in making a small-to-medium-sized feature film.

This included veterans of traditional filmmaking methods—camera staff, lighting teams, and actors—as well as senior researchers from Google DeepMind, AI engineers, and visual effects (VFX) artists who would naturally blend AI video into live-action scenes [Combining Generative AI With Live-action Filmmaking](https://aifuturethinkers.com/combining-generative-ai-with-live-action-filmmaking/).

**Why was so much help needed?**
While AI's results can be excellent on their own, they need to 'perform' and 'breathe' within the overall context of a film. Adjusting the flow of light generated by AI to match the subtle movements of an actor and eliminating the sense of heterogeneity between the actual filmed footage and the AI video still requires human artistic sense and technical expertise [Behind ANCESTRA: combining generative AI with live-action...](https://www.kifinity.com/post/behind-ancestra-combining-veo-with-live-action-filmmaking-deepmind-google-0f021f92). In other words, AI is not a 'vending machine' that makes a movie with the push of a button, but rather a 'new instrument' in an orchestra played together by many masters.

## How Far Have We Come: The Link Between Present and Future

'ANCESTRA' is the first step in an ambitious series of three short films planned by Google DeepMind and the production company **Primordial Soup** [Behind "ANCESTRA:" combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://article.wn.com/view/2025/06/13/Behind_ANCESTRA_combining_Veo_with_liveaction_filmmaking/).

Of course, we are not at a stage where AI can immediately produce every Hollywood movie. However, this project has proven very important technical progress. In particular, the demonstration of the possibility of **'motion matching'** technology—where the AI model 'Veo' generates or combines video in real-time according to the movement of live-action footage—has made a significant impact on the film industry [Google Veo AI Powers Cinematic Innovation in ANCESTRA](https://www.imaginepro.ai/blog/2025/6/behind-ancestra-combining-veo-with-live-action-filmmaking-google-blog).

AI is no longer a strange and fearful technology for directors. It is becoming a reliable partner that can most vividly and beautifully visualize the 'invisible world' that existed only in their hearts.

## What changes will occur in the future?

Experts expect 'ANCESTRA' to be the signal flare that opens the era of **'personalized storytelling'** [Behind "ANCESTRA": combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://aipulselab.tech/news/behind-ancestra-combining-veo-with-live-action-filmmaking-52e6b9).

In the near future, it might become commonplace for the colors of fantastic scenes in a movie to vary slightly according to the audience's taste, or for independent film directors to create blockbuster-scale space backgrounds with the help of AI, even without huge sets.

However, we must not forget that no matter how brilliantly technology advances, at its center is always the **'human story.'** Just as director Eliza McNitt created a film inspired by her own birth myth, AI will remain as a 'digital candle' that makes those precious human stories shine even more brightly.
