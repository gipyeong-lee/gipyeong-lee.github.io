Imagine this: You're having a busy day, using Codex (an AI-based code generation tool) to write complex programs for your work, and simultaneously using the conversational AI ChatGPT to draft project proposals. Suddenly, a warning pops up: "Usage limit reached." You have to decide whether to use more of Codex or more of ChatGPT. This dilemma is about to become a bit simpler.

OpenAI has decided to merge the usage limits for ChatGPT and Codex into a single shared Credit Pool (token allowance) starting December 2026 [[Source: OpenAI Combines ChatGPT, Coding Tool, and Browser in...](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/theedgesg_openai-plans-desktop-app-fusing-chat-coding-activity-7440630007250563072-_qCy)] [[Source: OpenAI to merge ChatGPT and Codex token allowances in ...](https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-unifies-chatgpt-codex-token-allowance/)].

## Why is this an important change?

This change directly impacts both general users and enterprise clients. Until now, users on ChatGPT Plus, Pro, and Business plans had to manage strictly limited usage separately for each service. However, from now on, you will be able to freely utilize both services within a single, unified "usage entitlement" without distinction [[Source: ChatGPT & Codex Shared Limits: December 2026 Changes](https://aiidelist.com/blog/chatgpt-codex-shared-usage-limits-december-2026)].

This goes beyond simple system integration; it heralds a fundamental shift in how we work with AI. Previously, tools were used like separate apps, but now it signifies a faster entry into OpenAI's envisioned environment centered around "Agents" (AI that performs tasks independently based on user instructions) [[Source: ChatGPT + Codex Limits Merge in December: What Changes ...](https://explainx.ai/blog/chatgpt-codex-usage-limits-merge-shared-token-allowance-december-2026)]. This means you'll no longer have to face inconveniences like asking, "Why is my ChatGPT limit still available but my Codex limit is gone?"

If these Transformer-based technologies (AI structures that analyze relationships between words in a sentence to understand context) were confined within separate limits, they can now bring their agent capabilities into the main ChatGPT experience, demonstrating integrated power according to user needs [[Source: ChatGPT + Codex Limits Merge in December: What Changes ...](https://explainx.ai/blog/chatgpt-codex-usage-limits-merge-shared-token-allowance-december-2026)].

## An Analogy for Better Understanding

This integration process can be likened to "two different restaurant coupons." Until now, you had separate "Korean restaurant coupons" and "Western restaurant coupons." If you used up your Korean restaurant coupon, you couldn't use more even if you had Western restaurant coupons left. But now, they are changing into a "Unified OpenAI Coupon" that can be used anywhere. You can freely use your remaining balance within any service you choose.

## What We Know So Far

OpenAI aims to strengthen agent functionalities across its services through this integration. According to experts, Codex and ChatGPT are rapidly converging in terms of their interfaces and how agents are utilized [[Source: ChatGPT and Codex: What We Know - ai.joaoqueiros.com](https://www.ai.joaoqueiros.com/blog/chatgpt-codex-merge-devday-interface-usage-limits)].

However, not all services will change. As far as has been confirmed, the mobile ChatGPT app appears to maintain its existing usage patterns, separate from this integration policy [[Source: OpenAI Combines ChatGPT, Coding Tool, and Browser in...](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/theedgesg_openai-plans-desktop-app-fusing-chat-coding-activity-7440630007250563072-_qCy)] [[Source: ChatGPT and Codex: What We Know - ai.joaoqueiros.com](https://www.ai.joaoqueiros.com/blog/chatgpt-codex-merge-devday-interface-usage-limits)]. Users will manage their allocated credits integrally according to their subscription plan, which will contribute to more efficient use of computing resources and smoother transitions between tools [[Source: OpenAI Shared Usage Limits: ChatGPT and Codex Quota](https://helpfulinfoforyou.com/2026/10/openai-shared-usage-limits-chatgpt-and-codex-quota/)].

## Future Outlook

Starting in December, integrated token limits will apply based on the nature of your subscription plan (Plus, Pro, Business). This means whether you perform a lot of coding tasks or extensive document writing through conversational AI, you can flexibly allocate your overall AI resources according to your work patterns [[Source: ChatGPT & Codex Shared Limits: December 2026 Changes](https://aiidelist.com/blog/chatgpt-codex-shared-usage-limits-december-2026)].

As OpenAI emphasizes agent capabilities, the boundaries between Codex and ChatGPT are expected to blur further. Users will be able to perform coding, document writing, and information retrieval all within a single integrated application, signaling a crucial evolution of AI from a mere Q&A tool to an active assistant capable of performing real tasks.

## MindTickleBytes AI Reporter's Perspective

This integration appears to be a strategic choice by OpenAI to piece together technological components and offer greater value to users. Users will now face an environment where they can focus more on their "objectives" rather than being constrained by technical limitations such as tool usage limits.
