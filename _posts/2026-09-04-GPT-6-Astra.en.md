Imagine this: You wake up in the morning, open your smartphone, and say to your AI assistant, "Analyze the technical challenges from yesterday's meeting and write code that I can apply today." While AI used to be at the level of simply finding information, it is now becoming a "real colleague" that solves complex problems in professional work environments.

On September 3, 2026, OpenAI unveiled its new flagship model at the pinnacle of this shift: 'GPT-6 Astra' [Source: GPT-6 Astra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-6_Astra), [Source: OpenAI releasing major upgrade to ChatGPT and Codex...](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/03/openai-releasing-major-upgrade-to-chatgpt-and-codex-with-gpt-6-astra-details-here/). What does this model mean for us?

## Why is this important?

GPT-6 Astra goes beyond being a smarter AI; it signifies that AI has reached a level where it can practically be deployed in professional tasks. In particular, this model combines 'computer-use' capabilities (the ability for AI to perform tasks in a computer environment by directly operating a mouse or keyboard like a human) with targeted training for professional environments, making it optimized to assist with complex practical work [Source: OpenAI releasing major upgrade to ChatGPT and Codex...](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/03/openai-releasing-major-upgrade-to-chatgpt-and-codex-with-gpt-6-astra-details-here/).

OpenAI Chairman Greg Brockman evaluated it as a "generational leap," mentioning that it could be considered an event marking the arrival of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI, a state where artificial intelligence mimics or surpasses human intellectual abilities, enabling it to learn and solve problems on its own) in the near future [Source: GPT-6 Astra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-6_Astra). Simply put, AI is evolving from a mere question-and-answer machine into a "practical assistant" that truly understands and executes my workflow.

## Making it easy to understand

To understand GPT-6 Astra, here are two analogies:

First, **'alignment'** (the technology of adjusting AI to act in accordance with human intentions and values) is like adding "specialized professional training" on top of "basic puppy training." Compared to the previous model, 'GPT-5.6 Sol', GPT-6 Astra has been adjusted much more meticulously, from the composition phase of the data it learns to the reinforcement learning phase [Source: GPT-6 Astra System Card](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-astra). In other words, it is designed so that the AI behaves more safely and accurately in the direction humans want. By analogy, it has cultivated the ability to judge for itself what the right action is depending on the situation, rather than just being a dog that only obeys commands.

Second, **'cybersecurity capability'** is like an "AI that has become a security expert." GPT-6 Astra recorded a perfect score of 100% on ExploitBench (a benchmark for testing software vulnerabilities, a kind of security proficiency test) [Source: В Codex начали разворачивать GPT-6 Astra, релиз близок](https://techora.ru/news/v-codex-начали-разворачивать-gpt-6-2026-09-03), [Source: OpenAI Wows With The 'AGI Era' GPT-6 Astra...](https://wccftech.com/openai-wows-with-the-agi-era-gpt-6-astra-with-preliminary-benchmarks-showing-remarkable-performance-gains-as-sam-altman-says-releases-will-now-be-paced-by-safety-considerations-and-not-capabilit/). This shows that the AI has a very high talent for identifying and defending against system vulnerabilities. However, because it has become so smart, OpenAI has classified this as a 'Critical' level cybersecurity risk and is paying special attention to it [Source: GPT-6 Astra](https://www.securitylab.ru/news/576888.php).

## Current status

As of September 3, 2026, GPT-6 Astra has been released in a limited preview form [Source: GPT-6 Astra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-6_Astra), [Source: OpenAI releasing major upgrade to ChatGPT and Codex...](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/03/openai-releasing-major-upgrade-to-chatgpt-and-codex-with-gpt-6-astra-details-here/). Because it possesses powerful cybersecurity features, access is initially granted only to a small number of verified security testers [Source: GPT-6 Astra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-6_Astra), [Source: OpenAI's Astra Is Here...](https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/openai-gpt-6-astra-release-ai-agi-chatgpt/). It will be expanded to ChatGPT Plus subscribers and API users in stages later.

The benchmark results are amazing. It recorded a phenomenal 98.6% on the ARC-AGI-3 (a difficult test measuring AGI performance) [Source: OpenAI Wows With The 'AGI Era' GPT-6 Astra...](https://wccftech.com/openai-wows-with-the-agi-era-gpt-6-astra-with-preliminary-benchmarks-showing-remarkable-performance-gains-as-sam-altman-says-releases-will-now-be-paced-by-safety-considerations-and-not-capabilit/). However, not all the numbers are rosy. There are also survey results showing that 'monitorability'—the ability to observe and control the model's behavior during actual use—is somewhat lower than in previous models, so OpenAI is continuing to conduct research [Source: GPT-6 Astra | HackerNews](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49554643).

## What will happen in the future?

Going forward, we will see AI go beyond being a simple information provider to directly controlling systems, strengthening security, and completing complex projects from start to finish. Following this release, OpenAI has stated that it will prioritize "safety considerations" over performance improvements and adjust the pace of development [Source: OpenAI Wows With The 'AGI Era' GPT-6 Astra...](https://wccftech.com/openai-wows-with-the-agi-era-gpt-6-astra-with-preliminary-benchmarks-showing-remarkable-performance-gains-as-sam-altman-says-releases-will-now-be-paced-by-safety-considerations-and-not-capabilit/).

Simply put, we have entered a phase where, rather than competitively increasing AI's "intelligence," we are focusing on "regulating" that powerful force so that it can safely integrate into our lives. It is time for us, as users, to look beyond what AI "can do" and pay attention to what "risks must be managed."
