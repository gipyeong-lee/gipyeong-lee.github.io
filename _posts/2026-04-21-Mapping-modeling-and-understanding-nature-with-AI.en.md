## What if AI Could Hear the 'Whispers' of the Earth? Smart Guardians Protecting Nature

**Imagine this.** You are walking slowly through a lush, deep forest. Somewhere, you hear the clear chirping of an unknown bird, and beneath your feet, a mysterious, shyly growing plant catches your eye. Usually, we just pass by, feeling that it is "beautiful" or "refreshing." But what if there was an entity that could instantly analyze the secrets hidden in all these sounds and landscapes?

An entity that kindly informs you, "That bird song you hear belongs to an endangered species with only a few individuals left in the world. Also, forest density in this area has decreased by 5% compared to last year, so caution is needed."

This is no longer a story from a science fiction movie. As artificial intelligence (AI) technology advances rapidly, AI is now playing a significant role as a reliable 'helper' in diagnosing and protecting the health of the Earth's ecosystem. Today, through the latest research from Google DeepMind and Google Research, we will easily explain the fascinating process of how AI understands and protects the Earth's nature. [Mapping, modeling, and understanding nature with AI](https://deepmind.google/blog/mapping-modeling-and-understanding-nature-with-ai/)

### Why is this important?

Until now, the methods we used to protect nature required immense time and effort. Scientists had to manually conduct surveys in harsh field conditions or check thousands of satellite photos one by one. However, the speed at which ecosystems are changing due to climate change is much faster than the speed of our response. The Earth is too vast, and the changes are too rapid for humans to respond to everything individually.

This is where the need for AI becomes starkly clear. Some experts suggest that AI can **'democratize and scale action'** to protect nature. [Mapping, modeling and understanding nature with artificial ...](https://aisckool.com/mapping-modeling-and-understanding-nature-with-artificial-intelligence/)

In simple terms, it means that local communities lacking expensive equipment or high-level expertise can easily participate in nature conservation through AI tools, and ecosystems on a global scale can be monitored 24/7 without gaps. [AI for Nature: How AI Can Democratize and Scale Action on ...](https://www.wri.org/research/ai-nature-how-ai-can-democratize-and-scale-action-nature)

### Easy Understanding: AI as an 'Interpreter' and 'Virtual Satellite' of Nature

Let's look at how AI helps nature specifically through two analogies.

#### 1. An 'Interpreter' that translates the language of nature
Just as we learn foreign languages, AI models learn from vast amounts of wildlife sounds, plant photos, and environmental data. Through this, AI has become able to accurately identify "This is the cry of a certain animal" or "This is a certain plant" just by looking at recorded audio or images. In other words, it has acquired the ability to identify species. [Mapping,modeling,andunderstandingnaturewithAI- aiobserver.co](https://aiobserver.co/mapping-modeling-and-understanding-nature-with-ai/)

For example, if a microphone installed in a forest captures a rare bird song, AI analyzes that sound and provides decisive help in identifying what creatures live in that area. This plays a very important role in mapping **Biodiversity (the variety of living species)** in a specific region. [Mapping,modeling,andunderstandingnaturewithAI– digitado](https://www.digitado.com.br/mapping-modeling-and-understanding-nature-with-ai/)

#### 2. A 'Virtual Satellite' scanning the Earth in real-time
AI goes beyond simply seeing the current state and performs modeling work to predict what will happen in the future. The research team explains that AI can function like a **'Virtual satellite.'** [Mapping, modeling, and understanding nature with AI](https://deepmind.google/blog/mapping-modeling-and-understanding-nature-with-ai/)

To use an analogy, it is like having eyes smarter than a satellite orbiting high in the sky. According to reports, AI maps **Deforestation (the phenomenon of forests disappearing)** in real-time or analyzes complex weather data to predict the possibility of floods with very high precision. [Mapping, modeling, and understanding nature with AI](https://news-tech.io/en/news/deepmind-blog-mapping-modeling-and-understanding-nature-with-ai/) It performs a function similar to a 'smartwatch' that checks the health of the large organism that is our Earth in real-time.

According to research, AI integrates data from various sources such as satellites and weather to accurately classify the location and change process of wetlands. *   **Real-time Deforestation Monitoring**: AI models are designed to track minute changes in forests in real-time. By quickly identifying situations where trees are being cut down illegally or forests are being damaged, it helps ensure that immediate protection activities can take place. *   **Predicting Extreme Weather Events**: AI simulates **Extreme events** such as droughts or floods in advance.
