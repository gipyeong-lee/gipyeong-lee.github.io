We generate images with AI every day. But have you ever felt frustrated by it? "I asked you to change the color of the shirt in the picture, so why did the entire image change?" or "Why does it take so long to generate a single image?"

If you've had these concerns, we have good news. On September 8, 2026, OpenAI announced **'ChatGPT Images 2.5'**, an even smarter and faster image generation technology. [Source OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Images 2.5: 50% Lower Latency...](https://www.datastudios.org/post/openai-chatgpt-images-2-5-flare-sunburst-api-pricing) [Source ChatGPT Images 2.5: What Is New, Access and Pricing](https://felloai.com/chatgpt-images-2-5/)

## Why does this matter?

More than just saying "the technology has improved," what truly matters is that the **quality of our workflow and experience** has changed.

Previously, when continuing long conversations with AI to edit images, features emphasized in earlier parts of the chat would gradually blur or disappear. Also, slow generation speeds could disrupt the creative flow. This 2.5 version focuses on technically resolving these user frustrations. Now, you can get the images you want faster and more accurately. [Source OpenAI's ChatGPT Images 2.5 Cuts Generation Time by 50% and Fixes Edits | AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-s-chatgpt-images-2-5-cuts-generation-time-by-50-and-fixes-edits)

## It’s become easier and smarter

How has ChatGPT Images 2.5 changed specifically? Let's use a daily-life analogy.

* **50% Faster Speed**: If the previous model was a chef who took 10 minutes to take an order and prepare the ingredients, it’s now like an expert chef who can whip up a dish in just 5 minutes. The waiting time has been cut in half. [Source OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Images 2.5 and Sketch | Let's Data ...](https://letsdatascience.com/news/openai-launches-chatgpt-images-25-and-sketch-40be4950)
* **Sketch Feature**: It’s like bringing in a 'sketchbook.' Previously, you might have felt frustrated that the AI couldn't 100% understand your intent through words alone. Now, you can sketch out a rough shape to give the AI a direct hint: "Draw it like this." As a result, the AI can grasp user intent much more accurately. [Source ChatGPT Images 2.5: Faster generation and editing](https://tbreak.com/openai-chatgpt-images-2-5-faster-generation-precise-editing/) [Source OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Images 2.5 and Sketch | Let's Data ...](https://letsdatascience.com/news/openai-launches-chatgpt-images-25-and-sketch-40be4950)

Furthermore, texture and lighting representation have become much more natural. This brings out the detail in the finished work, allowing you to experience quality that looks like it was created by a professional. [Source OpenAI's ChatGPT Images 2.5 Cuts Generation Time by 50% and Fixes Edits | AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-s-chatgpt-images-2-5-cuts-generation-time-by-50-and-fixes-edits)

## Can I use it right now?

Yes, absolutely. ChatGPT Images 2.5 is currently available to users of ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex (the AI programming assistant tool) across desktop, mobile, and web environments. [Source Introducing GPT Images 2.5 in the API and ChatGPT](https://community.openai.com/t/introducing-gpt-images-2-5-in-the-api-and-chatgpt/1395897)

This update goes beyond mere image generation. It has evolved into a tool that supports the entire creative process—users can provide sketches, communicate by commenting on images, utilize templates, and share prompts. The new API models for developers, 'Flare' and 'Sunburst,' were also released, allowing even more services and apps to experience this powerful image generation technology. [Source OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Images 2.5 and Sketch | Let's Data ...](https://letsdatascience.com/news/openai-launches-chatgpt-images-25-and-sketch-40be4950) [Source ChatGPT Images 2.5: What Is New, Access and Pricing](https://felloai.com/chatgpt-images-2-5/)

## What’s next?

Conversations with AI will become increasingly focused on 'context.' A standout aspect of this update is how accurately it remembers and reflects the editing instructions given by the user, even during long, ongoing conversations.
