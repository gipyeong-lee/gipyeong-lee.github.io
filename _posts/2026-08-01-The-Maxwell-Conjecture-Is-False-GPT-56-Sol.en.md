Imagine this: There is a complex problem that mathematicians around the world have been pondering for decades without a solution. What if a brilliant new tool appeared, presented a counterexample, saying "this problem is incorrect," or provided a perfect proof in just one hour? While it may sound like a story from the distant future, artificial intelligence (AI) is making this amazing sight a reality today.

Unveiled by OpenAI on June 26, 2026, [GPT-5.6 Sol](https://macpdf.com/ru/blog/articles/chatgpt-gpt-5-6-sol-ne-v-selector-modeley/chatgpt-gpt-5-6-sol-ne-v-selector-modeley.html) is attracting attention as a "genius assistant" to the scientific community, going beyond a simple conversational chatbot. This model was made available to the general public via ChatGPT and API starting July 9, allowing anyone to utilize it [Source](https://www.comss.ru/page.php?id=21210).

## Why is this important?

Until now, AI has mainly played the role of a "secretary," summarizing information created by humans or helping with simple coding. However, GPT-5.6 Sol has opened new possibilities for scientific inquiry by solving mathematical conundrums that humans could not crack for decades on its own. This signifies that we have entered an era where AI can solve scientific homework that humanity has long struggled with, faster and more accurately. However, the fact that such a powerful AI can sometimes be utilized in unexpected directions, such as threatening system security, presents a significant social challenge regarding how carefully and responsibly we must handle this technology.

## Simplified: Mathematical proof is like finding a way through a maze

Shall we compare mathematical proof to finding a way through a "hugely massive maze"? Human mathematicians start finding a path from the entrance of the maze one by one, and if they encounter a dead end, they repeatedly turn back. The 'Cycle Double Cover Conjecture' (a hypothesis that cycles can be chosen so that every edge of a graph is covered an even number of times), which had not been solved for 50 years, was in a state where the maze was so vast and complex that it was impossible to find the exit with human eyes [Source](https://3dnews.ru/1145241/gpt56-sol-za-chas-dokazala-matematicheskuyu-gipotezu-nad-kotoroy-uchyonie-bilis-bolee-50-let).

GPT-5.6 Sol is like a "drone flying in the sky" that looks down on the entire maze at once. This AI examines countless possibilities simultaneously and instantly finds shortcuts or logical errors that humans might not have thought of. For example, regarding 'Maxwell's Conjecture,' a long-held belief in the mathematical community, the AI found a very sophisticated "counterexample" (concrete evidence showing the hypothesis is wrong), proving that what humans had taken for granted for years was actually incorrect [Source](https://modernorange.io/item/49121313).

In other words, just like instantly putting together scattered puzzle pieces to complete one perfect picture, AI is excellent at quickly finding logical solutions within complex data.

## Current situation: How far have we come?

Currently, GPT-5.6 Sol is performing tasks at an expert level, not only in solving math problems but also in advanced programming and complex data analysis [Source](https://rugpt.io/gpt-5-6-sol). It does not just process text; it shows amazing versatility in various fields, such as immediately converting user ideas into interactive, beautiful websites or combining them with video generation tools to create creative media [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kxs23pJ-CoQ).

However, shadows exist behind the bright side of technology. The fact that the GPT-5.6 Sol model was involved in the infrastructure attack incident on the famous AI platform 'Hugging Face' was recently revealed, and concerns that the power of AI technology could be abused have become a reality [Source](https://habr.com/ru/companies/bothub/news/1061656/).

## What will happen in the future?

In the future, a way for AI and human scientists to collaborate closely will become a matter of course. It is a structure where humans ask more creative and fundamental questions, while AI handles complex verification and massive calculations, dramatically increasing the speed of research. This resolution of mathematical conundrums is just the beginning. Before long, AI will produce more results in key areas that will change human life, such as new drug development or efficient energy design. It is a time when we must watch over its safety with both anticipation and caution, wondering what kind of old common sense AI will overturn.

## MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter's Perspective

While AI is solving math problems and making the world smarter, the fact that models can be utilized for malicious attacks at the same time clearly shows that building a social safety net for "safe use" is as urgent as technological development. The tool itself is value-neutral, but this is a point in time when the responsibility and technical control of the humans holding that tool have become more important than ever.
