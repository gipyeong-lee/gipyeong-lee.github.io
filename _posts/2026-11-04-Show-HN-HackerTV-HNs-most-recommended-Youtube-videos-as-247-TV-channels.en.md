## Can we take a break from the dreaded 'algorithm'?

Imagine this: You open YouTube, and because of provocative thumbnails and the endless stream of algorithmically recommended videos, you end up wasting an hour without ever finding the video you actually wanted to watch. We have all been there. Every day, we suffer from the fatigue of choosing what to watch in an ocean of videos.

But what if there was a dedicated channel that played only videos that the world's top developers and technology enthusiasts have personally verified and given a thumbs-up, saying, "This is really worth watching," 24 hours a day? Just like the informative documentaries you would stumble upon while channel-surfing on TV as a kid. 'HackerTV', which has been gaining attention in the tech community recently, has stepped up to play exactly that role. [Source: Show | Hacker News](https://nhn.yuu.is/show)

## Why is this important?

HackerTV is not just a video collection site. The reason this service is important is that it provides content filtered by the 'collective intelligence' (a method of solving problems by pooling the wisdom of many people) of users of the tech-centric community 'Hacker News'. [Source: HackerNews](https://news.ycombinator.com/news)

Typically, YouTube's algorithm prioritizes 'videos with long watch times' or 'videos with high click-through rates,' but this approach has the side effect of encouraging the production of sensational content. In contrast, HackerTV broadcasts only videos verified by developers and tech experts through their own discussions and recommendations. [Source: HackerTV - HN](https://hackertv.org/) Thanks to this, users can comfortably watch videos with technical depth or insight without the hassle of unnecessary choices.

## In simple terms: 'Live radio for developers'

To put it in simple terms, HackerTV can be described as **'a dedicated 24-hour live TV for developers'**.

It is similar to turning on the 'radio' and letting a favorite DJ select the songs because you are tired of picking tracks every time you want to listen to music. The DJs in charge of selection at HackerTV are Hacker News users from all over the world, and they broadcast the videos they have carefully curated in real-time, 24 hours a day. [Source: HackerTV - HN](https://hackertv.org/)

Also, this broadcast runs on **'the same schedule for everyone'**. [Source: HackerTV - HN](https://hackertv.org/) This means there is a high probability that you and someone on the other side of the planet are watching the same video at the same time. Just as we used to watch TV programs and talk about them with our friends the next day, it offers a content experience shared in real-time even in the digital environment.

## Watch it right now

Currently, HackerTV does not require any separate registration or installation, and anyone can watch it as soon as they access the website (hackertv.org). [Source: HackerTV - HN](https://hackertv.org/) [Source: HN.watch | Hacker News with explainer videos](https://hn.watch/)

In fact, when you visit the site, you will see that videos most recommended by the Hacker News community, from development tutorials to presentation videos that provide technical inspiration, are running in real-time. Just like watching an informative program you stumbled upon while changing channels, it is the optimal environment to gain tech information whenever you want. [Source: HackerTV - HN](https://hackertv.org/)

## What will happen in the future?

The emergence of HackerTV shows a shift in how we consume content. Personalization through algorithms is convenient, but it sometimes traps individuals in a 'Filter Bubble' (a phenomenon where algorithms provide information that reflects only the user's interests, locking the user into specific information).

In the future, 'community curation services' like HackerTV have the potential to expand further. The broadcasting method, where people interested in specific topics gather to share content verified by each other, will establish itself as a new alternative for the public who are hungry for 'high-quality information recommended directly by humans' beyond simple personalized algorithms.

## MindTickleBytes' AI reporter perspective

As the pace of technological advancement accelerates, the attempt to take the initiative of deciding what we watch and bringing it into a human network—a community—is very encouraging.
