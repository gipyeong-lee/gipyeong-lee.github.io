Imagine this: You wake up in the morning and simply instruct your artificial intelligence, "Could you build an accounting program tailored for our company's workflow? The core features needed are A, B, and C," and then you leave work on Friday evening with a light heart. When you return to the office on Monday after the weekend, you find a perfectly functioning program, along with a user manual that even beginners can easily read, and a meticulous report of hundreds of error tests run over the weekend, all sitting neatly on your computer's desktop.

Does this sound like a sci-fi movie or a story from the distant future? Surprisingly, as of June 2026, AI technology has already reached this level. This is thanks to the recently released innovative AI, 'Claude Fable 5', by a company called Anthropic. Anthropic announced that this model is a top-tier 'Mythos-class' AI that even general users can safely and powerfully use in their daily lives [[GitHub - Anil-matcha/awesome-claude-fable-5: CuratedClaudeFable...]](https://github.com/Anil-matcha/awesome-claude-fable-5). People continuously asked which is the most outstanding and powerful artificial intelligence.

In the fierce battleground of coding and workflow automation, which AI will emerge as the true winner? To give you the conclusion first, the answer does not depend on "who is unconditionally smarter," but rather "what mission is given to whom."

## Why is this important?

Until now, the AI we knew was like a 'short-term part-timer' who worked very fast. If you asked something you didn't know, it would answer immediately on the spot, translate short foreign language sentences, and write a few lines of simple computer program code. However, if the user stepped away or didn't give the next instruction, the AI also stopped working right there.

But the dazzling advancement of technology has now elevated AI to a whole new level. It has entered the realm of 'Autonomous Agents' (artificial intelligence that moves towards a final goal on its own without human intervention), which can meticulously make plans over several days, self-verify the results it produces, and autonomously find and execute the next step instead of resting when finished [[Claude Fable 5 vs GPT-5.5: Benchmarks & Cost Compared]](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/claude-fable-5-vs-gpt-5-5-frontier-comparison-2026).

Simply put, Claude Fable 5 does not require the user to stay by its side and constantly nag, "Do this next, do that next." Over long periods, without losing focus or breaking its stride, it can silently and autonomously progress toward complex final goals [[Claude Fable 5 vs GPT-5.5: Which AI Model Should You Use?]](https://www.techloy.com/claude-fable-5-vs-gpt-5-5/). What is particularly noteworthy is that it is equipped with the ability to smartly delegate tasks to sub-agents (other smaller AIs that assist it) and stringently verify its own outputs with eagle eyes [[Claude Fable 5 vs GPT-5.5: Benchmarks & Cost Compared]](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/claude-fable-5-vs-gpt-5-5-frontier-comparison-2026).

This carries the tremendous implication that complex migration tasks of transferring a company's massive data to a new system, or huge projects taking days, can be reliably entrusted to AI without human intervention. We are now in an astonishing era where even underfunded, small-scale startups or solopreneurs can achieve the exact same effect as commanding a massive elite development team of dozens of people.

## The Current Situation

Looking only at these flashy numbers, Claude Fable 5 seems perfect in every way in the world, but the vivid stories from the actual workplaces where AI is used daily are a bit different.

Although Claude Fable 5 possesses remarkable genius that will go down in history, it sometimes reveals fatal and bizarre weaknesses. When given tasks that are too complex and overwhelming at once, it sometimes suffers from severe hallucinations, suddenly fabricating untrue facts as if they were real. Or, it exhibits unstable behavior where it fails to solve a difficult problem through to the end and tries to compromise by relying on the past capabilities of the older 'Opus 4.8' model [[Claude Fable 5 vs GPT-5.5: Mythos vs OpenAI's Flagship (June 2026) · CodingFleet Blog]](https://codingfleet.com/blog/claude-fable-5-vs-gpt-5-5/). 

Moreover, during peak times when users swarm in—like Monday afternoons when office workers worldwide start their tasks—critical physical infrastructure limitations are painfully pointed out, such as severe overloads resembling a server on fire, causing connections to drop or making users wait pointlessly for a long time just to receive a single line of response [[Claude Fable 5 vs. GPT-5.5: Better Planning, Similar Execution | Hacker News]](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48517973).

In contrast, our dependable site manager, GPT-5.5, has less emotional fluctuation and is highly consistent. In the specific 'realm of execution', where a well-structured plan must be translated into actual computer code and flawlessly finalized, it is highly praised for resolving a mountain of problems much more stably and directly, without variables [[Claude Fable 5 vs. GPT-5.5: Better Planning, Similar Execution | Hacker News]](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48517973).

## What Will Happen in the Future?

Amidst this situation, the advice from industry experts is becoming clearer than ever. The idea of "unconditionally insisting on only the single smartest AI in the world" has now become an obsolete mindset of the past. The most efficient, powerful, and cost-saving wise method available today is the **'Hybrid Strategy'**, which carefully cherry-picks and mixes the distinct strengths of both geniuses.

The most ideal and seamless workflow recommended by experts goes like this. First, entrust the entire project's skeleton planning and architecture to the Chief Architect 'Claude Fable 5', who excels at seeing the forest rather than the trees. Once Fable 5 completes a meticulous blueprint without a single inch of error, we carefully hand this over to 'GPT-5.5', the master of meticulous execution and site manager. Then, we have it implement the actual computer code precisely according to that drawing.

Surprisingly, according to recent test results, when both models each wrote code based on the plan carefully drafted by Fable 5, GPT-5.5 safely passed 15 demanding acceptance checks without a single failure, producing high-quality outputs perfectly equal in level to Fable 5. Furthermore, by utilizing this 'dream team' collaboration method, overall costs were significantly reduced by a whopping 59% compared to expensively insisting on Fable 5 alone [[Claude Fable 5 vs GPT-5.5: Should You Use the New Model for ...]](https://blog.kilo.ai/p/claude-fable-5-vs-gpt-5-5).

Going forward, the way we work will change dramatically like this. Rather than desperately searching for a single perfect, all-powerful magic tool, it is an era of convergence where you build your own reliable and systematic 'AI Team' consisting of a Strategic Planning Director (Fable 5) and an On-site Execution Manager (GPT-5.5). The gloomy future where artificial intelligence takes away all human jobs, as often feared, is not coming. Instead, a 'human conductor' who perfectly understands the characteristics of various artificial intelligences with different personalities and skillfully orchestrates them will take the lead in the work of the new era.

A truly great modern leader is not just someone who merely taps a keyboard and tosses out instructions. The one who can see through the strengths and weaknesses of their digital team members (AI) and explosively maximize their teamwork—now is the time for you to be that protagonist.
