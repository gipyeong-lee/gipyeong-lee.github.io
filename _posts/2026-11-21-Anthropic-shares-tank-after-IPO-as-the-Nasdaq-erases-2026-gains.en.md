Imagine this: The company behind an artificial intelligence (AI) service that you cherish and frequently use for work or creative projects finally goes public, making a glamorous debut amid the enthusiastic cheers of investors worldwide. From day one, tens of millions of shares are traded, and the company's valuation skyrockets. Yet, the very next day, a massive economic storm rolls in, and those hard-earned gains begin to be chipped away in an instant. What on earth is happening?

A similar drama recently unfolded in the global tech and financial markets surrounding the stock market debut of AI industry giant Anthropic. After executing a historic Initial Public Offering (IPO)—where a company offers its shares to the general public for the first time—Anthropic, the creator of the popular AI chatbot Claude, has captured global attention by experiencing both a stock surge and a sudden plunge [Source 14, Source 15]. In this article, we will examine what this massive economic event means for us and step-by-step explore what happened in the unforgiving capital markets.

---

## Why Does This Matter?

Behind the AI services and chatbots on our smartphones that we use every day lie massive amounts of capital and cutting-edge technology. Until now, the vast majority of major AI companies have remained private, operating with investments from big tech giants or internal funding. 

However, Anthropic has entered the stock market as the first pure-play AI company (focusing purely on AI technology) to publicly offer shares to the general public [Source 14]. As an analogy, it means that the massive tree of new technology carefully nurtured in a greenhouse has now been thrust entirely into the harsh wild forest—a full-fledged "coming-of-age" stage subject to the cold capital evaluation of the global public and institutional investors. 

If the stock price of such a high-profile AI heavyweight stumbles or the market responds coldly, it will send significant shockwaves through the financing and developmental trajectories of countless innovative AI technologies yet to come. In other words, this event goes far beyond the stock fluctuations of a single company; it serves as a critical signal shaking the future economic map of the digital world we live in.

## Understanding It Easily: The World of Stock Markets and IPOs

Let's break down this complex and massive economic situation using everyday analogies.

### Analogy 1: The Grand Opening of a Large Restaurant
An IPO (Initial Public Offering) is akin to a very famous and delicious local restaurant opening a major first branch in a large shopping mall to expand into a nationwide chain, selling "restaurant equity" (like slices of a pizza) to the general public. 
- **IPO Price (Initial offering price):** The initial price per share set by the company was **$83 per share** [Source 5, Source 16].
- **First Day Opening (Opening Day Rush):** On the day the restaurant opened its doors (November 19), customers swarmed in like clouds. As soon as trading opened, the price jumped, starting at **$111.50**, and over **180 million shares** changed hands in the blink of an eye [Source 13, Source 16]. During that day, the company's valuation temporarily fluctuated past **$2 trillion** [Source 15, Source 16]. As such, initial expectations were nothing short of explosive.

### Analogy 2: A Sudden Change in Market Weather on a Clear Day
However, good weather does not last forever. The stock market is like a massive weather system. No matter how delicious a restaurant opens, if a massive storm (market-wide selling pressure and a correction) sweeps across the entire city, people close their wallets and grow anxious. Similarly, Anthropic stock finished its first day up 17% at **$97.17** [Source 5, Source 16], but was hit hard the very next day when it encountered the storm known as macroeconomics.

---

## Current Status: A Historic Debut and the Chilling Reality That Followed

Expressed in numbers, the records set during this debut were truly historic. 

- **Record Capital Raising:** Even before going public, Anthropic astonished the world by raising an immense **$81 billion** [Source 4, Source 14, Source 16]. This was an unprecedented scale in history for a pure-play AI company, clearly demonstrating how high humanity's expectations are for AI technology.
- **First Day Cheers and Excitement:** Listed on November 19 on Nasdaq (the premier U.S. stock market primarily featuring advanced tech companies), Anthropic stock made a glamorous debut, opening at **$111.50**, well above its IPO price of $83 [Source 3, Source 13, Source 16]. Over 180 million shares were traded on the first day, proving market expectations [Source 13].
- **Sharp Volatility and Plunge:** However, the cheers did not last long. In Friday's session, Anthropic stock **plunged nearly 10%**, giving up a significant portion of its post-historic-IPO gains [Source 4]. 

This stock drop was not unique to Anthropic. Around the same time, the entire U.S. stock market faced a severe chilling wind. The Dow Jones and S&P 500 indexes entered so-called **correction territory**, plunging **over 10%** from their recent peaks, while the tech-heavy Nasdaq index **completely erased all gains recorded throughout 2026** [Source 1]. Amid the massive selling storm covering the entire market, newly public Anthropic could not be an exception.

Financial expert Walser analyzed that Anthropic's stock could continue to face intense market pressure and testing in the coming weeks [Source 8]. In addition, Bloomberg reporters Ryan Gould and Bailey Lipschultz vividly reported on how turbulent the first trading day on Nasdaq was [Source 10]. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei also watched this intense and cutthroat listing moment live at the Nasdaq MarketSite in New York [Source 15].

## What's Next?

When new technologies take root in the world, the capital markets supporting them inevitably experience rough waves. Here is a summary of the future outlook across three key points.

1. **Continued Short-Term Market Pressure:** With the broader stock market entering a correction phase and the Dow and S&P 500 dropping more than 10% from their peaks [Source 1], newly listed Anthropic is expected to undergo strict investor scrutiny and share price defense tests for the foreseeable future [Source 8]. Short-term volatility is inevitable.
2. **Re-evaluation of the Broader AI Industry:** Anthropic's listing will profoundly influence the market entry methods and investor sentiment of other major AI companies moving forward. Attention will focus on what equilibrium the market finds between bubble concerns and tangible technological profitability, as well as what strategies followers will take.
3. **Focus on the Essence of Technology:** While stock prices can fluctuate depending on market sentiment, the fundamental value of AI technologies like Claude changing how people work and live remains unchanged. Even amidst the chilling response of the investment market, innovation delivered by AI to real life will continue to march forward.
