# Is AI Handling My Work? The Transformation Brought by the OpenAI and ServiceNow Partnership

Imagine this: It’s Monday morning, you arrive at the office, and you turn on your computer. Previously, you would have spent your entire morning opening dozens of emails, organizing meeting minutes, and checking approval chains. But now, AI has pre-categorized the priority of all tasks, summarized the necessary materials, and placed them in your approval inbox. You simply need to review what the AI has organized and click the 'Approve' button.

This scene is no longer just a story from a movie. OpenAI and ServiceNow recently announced a significant expansion of their partnership, promising to embed AI models directly into core enterprise workflows (the series of procedures through which work is conducted). [OpenAI expands enterprise reach with ServiceNow partnership...](https://www.the-sourcecode.com/ai-tech/openai-servicenow-enterprise-ai-partnership) This is a significant turning point showing that AI technology is moving beyond simple fun experiments or pilot programs to become the core infrastructure upon which businesses operate.

## Why Does This Matter?

Many companies have wanted to adopt AI, but they struggled with where to actually apply it. They stopped at simply creating 'chatbots' or used AI only in areas disconnected from actual work. However, this collaboration is different. 'ServiceNow' is often called an 'AI control tower for business innovation.' [tech mahindra and servicenow expand partnership to deliver produc](https://www.forpressrelease.com/forpressrelease/676169/21/tech-mahindra-and-servicenow-expand-partnership-to-deliver-production-ready-enterprise-ai-at-scale) In other words, it is a system that manages every 'thoroughfare' through which key departments like HR, IT, and customer service operate. The fact that OpenAI's advanced models are entering this means that AI is moving beyond being a simple 'assistant' to directly performing the roles of a 'practitioner.'

## Easy to Understand: From Smart Assistant to Proficient Deputy

Shall we use a metaphor to easily understand what it means for AI to be integrated into enterprise work?

If the enterprise AI we have had until now was a 'smart dictionary,' think of it now as becoming a 'proficient office deputy.'
- **Smart Dictionary (Past):** Answers questions, but cannot directly write documents or submit approvals.
- **Proficient Office Deputy (Present):** Accurately understands company work rules and performs tasks I need to do (approvals, data classification, customer responses) in advance, showing only the conclusion.

Also, the reason ServiceNow is called an 'AI control tower' is because it acts as the 'control tower' helping a 'plane' (the complex work system) navigate safely. By mounting a powerful engine called OpenAI into this control tower system, a company can make complex administrative tasks that humans previously handled manually flow automatically.

## Current Situation: Practical Adoption is Key

Currently, many enterprise software companies are competing fiercely over 'who can best manage AI agents (AI that performs tasks on behalf of users) within their systems.' [Why on-prem AI matters. A lot of enterprise interest in AI is real.](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bitmanager-ai_why-on-prem-ai-matters-a-lot-of-enterprise-activity-7452102555704262657-dFHp) However, simply adopting a model does not solve everything. Corporate internal security, data management, and how naturally AI blends with actual work processes are the keys. The partnership between OpenAI and ServiceNow aims to help enable 'production-ready' (a state ready for immediate deployment in the field) work through this technical integration. [OpenAI expands enterprise reach with ServiceNow partnership...](https://www.the-sourcecode.com/ai-tech/openai-servicenow-enterprise-ai-partnership)

## What Will Happen in the Future?

In the future, AI will be so deeply embedded throughout work systems that you won't even feel like you are 'using AI.' It will be just like how we currently use features that automatically correct typos in word processors without consciously thinking of them as AI technology.

Companies will continue to ponder how much more they can achieve with fewer personnel and less time through AI. However, keep in mind that as technology advances, the discernment of managers—who choose 'which AI best fits our company's way of working'—will become even more important. We must prepare to adapt to a new way of working in an era where AI is not just a tool assisting your work, but a colleague.
