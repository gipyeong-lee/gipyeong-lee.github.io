# Build Apps Without Coding? The Evolution of 'Vibe,' the AI Assistant That Works for You

Imagine this: You want to create your own cool household budget app, but you don't know the first thing about complex programming languages. In the past, you would have had to study for months or hire an expensive developer. But now, we are entering a world where you can simply say, "Build me a simple budget website for my family," and the AI will write the code and finish the testing for you, just like an efficient assistant.

The recent update of **'Mistral Vibe,'** announced by European AI powerhouse Mistral AI, has brought this magical future one step closer to us. [Remote agents in Vibe. Powered by Mistral Medium 3.5. | Mistral AI](https://mistral.ai/news/vibe-remote-agents-mistral-medium-3-5)

In this guide, we'll break down the core of this update and how our daily lives might change in the future.

---

## Why Is This Important? "From Talking AI to Acting AI"

Until now, AIs like ChatGPT have mostly been friends who are very good with "words." If you asked, "What does this code mean?" it would kindly explain it, but actually going into your computer to modify code line by line and running the program was still a human's job. In other words, AI was an "advisor," not an "executor."

However, the updated **Mistral Vibe** is on a different level. This tool goes beyond a simple chat window and is a **'coding agent'** that operates directly in the 'Terminal' (the window where you type commands to the computer) that developers actually use. [Mistral Vibe - AI coding agents for enterprises](https://mistral.ai/products/vibe)

The term **'Agent'** is crucial here. An agent isn't just an assistant that answers questions; it's a proxy or representative that acts autonomously to achieve a goal set by the owner.

To use an analogy, if previous AIs were "cookbooks that tell you recipes," Vibe is like a "chef who goes grocery shopping and cooks the meal for you." For example, if you command, "Make my website's login screen look better," the AI understands the entire code structure, decides which parts to fix, actually modifies the code, and shows you the result—handling the entire process. [Mistral Vibe 2.0: The Terminal-Based AI Coding Agent | DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/mistral-vibe-2-0)

## Understanding Vibe: A New 'Giant Brain' and 'Unstoppable Hands'

Two major changes are behind why Vibe has become so smart and capable.

### 1. A Giant Brain with 128 Billion Points of Knowledge: Mistral Medium 3.5
AI performance is often determined by a number called **'Parameters.'** Think of parameters as the "neural connections" that link the vast amount of knowledge the AI has learned. The higher this number, the better the AI is at understanding complex logic and providing sophisticated answers.

Vibe's new brain, **Mistral Medium 3.5**, is a massive model with **128 billion (128B)** parameters. [Mistral 3.5 - How To Run Locally | Unsloth Documentation](https://unsloth.ai/docs/models/mistral-3.5) It's like having tens of thousands of books from a library entirely in its head. With performance far exceeding the previous specialized coding model 'Devstral 2,' it now serves as Vibe's core engine. [mistral-medium-3.5](https://ollama.com/library/mistral-medium-3.5)

Thanks to this smart brain, Vibe can now perfectly understand even vague user requests like "Build it with this kind of vibe" and solve complex programming problems. This is referred to as 'Vibe Coding,' representing an era where you can complete projects just by conveying the 'vibe' even without knowing the syntax. [Mistral AI surfs vibe-coding tailwinds with new coding models | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/09/mistral-ai-surfs-vibe-coding-tailwinds-with-new-coding-models/)

### 2. The 'Subagent' System for Autonomous Task Distribution
The highlight of this update is undoubtedly the **'Subagents'** feature. This is similar to a capable manager in a company handling work with several specialized team members.

Let's use an analogy. If you say, "Build me a house," the manager AI (Vibe) analyzes the task and gives instructions like this:
*   "Agent A, draw the blueprints."
*   "Agent B, lay the bricks."
*   "Agent C, check the electrical wiring."

These specialized team members (subagents) handle their respective fields independently without the user having to intervene or give permission at every step. [Agents & Skills | Mistral Docs](https://docs.mistral.ai/mistral-vibe/agents-skills) The user can simply wait with a cup of coffee and check the final finished product. [Agents & Skills | Mistral Docs](https://docs.mistral.ai/mistral-vibe/agents-skills)

## Current Status: The Amazing Things Vibe Can Do

Mistral Vibe currently provides powerful features for companies and individuals that go beyond simple coding assistance:

*   **Massive Code Analysis**: The AI can instantly read and analyze thousands of source code files stored on your computer. If you ask, "Why does this program keep having errors?" the AI scans the entire project like a seasoned detective to find the cause. [mistral-vibe/README.md at main · mistralai/mistral-vibe](https://github.com/mistralai/mistral-vibe/blob/main/README.md)
*   **Connection with External Services (MCP)**: It uses a standard called the 'Model Context Protocol (MCP),' which helps different AI tools talk to each other. [MCP Integration | mistralai/mistral-vibe | DeepWiki](https://deepwiki.com/mistralai/mistral-vibe/3.5-mcp-integration) Thanks to this, Vibe can connect with external services like Slack, GitHub, and Google Drive to exchange information. Just as you expand a smartphone's functionality with apps, the AI gains endless capabilities through this protocol.
*   **Robust Security System**: The most sensitive issue for companies is technology leakage. Vibe has the unique advantage of being installable on a company's **Private servers** rather than sending information to an external cloud. [Mistral Vibe 2.0: The Terminal-Based AI Coding Agent | DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/mistral-vibe-2-0) It's like a safe that only works inside your house.
*   **Safe Approval Process**: It would be problematic if an AI changed computer settings at will, wouldn't it? Vibe has a feature that requires user approval before executing important commands, so you can control it with peace of mind. [CLI Introduction | Mistral Docs](https://docs.mistral.ai/mistral-vibe/terminal)

## Future Outlook: "Coding Is Now a Domain of Communication"

Experts are confident that tools like Mistral Vibe will lead to the **'Democratization of Coding.'** Now, coding is rapidly shifting from a 'skill' of perfectly typing a specific computer language according to syntax to a domain of **'communication skills'**—how clearly and logically you can explain the problem you want to solve to the AI.

In particular, the **'Work mode' in 'Le Chat,'** announced alongside this update, helps with complex business document preparation and data analysis, raising expectations for AI agents to play a major role in general office work as well as coding.
