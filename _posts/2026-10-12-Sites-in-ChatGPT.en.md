Imagine this: You wake up in the morning and suddenly think, "It would be great to have my own web app for managing our family's household budget." In the past, you would have had to spend several sleepless nights learning difficult coding languages like HTML (the basic language for creating web pages), CSS (the language for web design), and JavaScript (the programming language that governs web behavior), or setting up complex server hosting (the space where a website is placed on the internet). But now, there’s no need for that. You just have to talk to ChatGPT.

"Build a family budget website. I'd like features to input date, category, and amount, and to show this month's total spending."

The moment you say this, your idea turns into a real-time functioning website and appears in the internet world. This is thanks to "ChatGPT Sites," introduced by OpenAI.

## Why is this important?

Until now, AI has mainly stayed in the role of an "assistant" that searches for information, writes text, or generates images. However, ChatGPT Sites goes one step further. AI has now moved beyond being a simple assistant to becoming an "all-around workshop" that turns your ideas into physical services.

The most significant meaning of this feature is the "dissolution of barriers to entry." The professional technical domain of web development has been opened to the general user. Anyone, even those without any coding knowledge, can instantly create and deploy dashboards (screens where data can be seen at a glance), personal trackers (recording tools), business tools, and even simple games, just through daily conversation. [Source 2](https://theaicareerlab.com/blog/what-is-chatgpt-sites-codex-2026) As a result, individuals can manage their daily lives more efficiently and experiment with their ideas quickly.

## Easy to understand: A magical cooking tool

The easiest way to understand ChatGPT Sites is to compare it to a "magical cooking tool."

If web development in the past was a difficult process where a chef had to prepare ingredients, control the fire, and manage kitchen utensils, ChatGPT Sites is like telling a top-notch chef, "I want to eat a dish with this kind of vibe." When you order a menu (prompt), the chef (ChatGPT) designs the recipe, cooks it directly in the kitchen (server), and places the most delicious result (website) on the table right away.

In short, Transformer (the AI architecture that understands context by grasping relationships between words in a sentence) technology has now expanded beyond simple answers to creating actual working software. [Source 4](https://gipyeong-lee.github.io/2026/04/10/Explore-ChatGPTopens-in-a-new-window/) The AI understands the sentences you input, translates them into actual computer code, and implements them in a form that can be executed immediately on the internet.

## Current status

Released as a public beta service within ChatGPT Work on July 9, 2026, ChatGPT Sites can currently be used through the ChatGPT desktop app. [Source 1](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/sites?surface=app), [Source 3](https://playcode.io/blog/chatgpt-sites-explained) Users can build and host web applications or games without having to configure complex deployment workflows themselves, and can turn them into instantly shareable URLs (internet addresses).

It is said that team members at OpenAI, including Product Staff Kath Korevec, have already been using and refining this feature internally for over a year. [Source 5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kz5Cpomk3HA) This means it is not a feature that suddenly popped up, but a tool that has had its completeness enhanced over a long period.

Of course, it currently shows excellent performance in producing light web apps like dashboards, simple business tools, or personal recording tools, rather than large-scale services that require complex databases.

## What will happen in the future?

In the future, the process of creating your own web app will become as routine as downloading a smartphone app. We have entered an era where we create the functions we need by "talking" to them. Furthermore, these features are expected to be of immense help in educational settings or small startups for quickly visualizing ideas.

The feature you can use right now is simply "website generation," but as AI's coding abilities continue to develop, your commands will produce more detailed and powerful software. When you turn on ChatGPT next time, why not throw out a small idea like, "It would be convenient to have a feature like this"?
