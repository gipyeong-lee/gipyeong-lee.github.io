Imagine this. You're scrolling through social media and find a shocking photo. Along with a frantic post saying, "A shark just appeared on our local highway!", there's a picture of a sharp shark fin rising above a flooded road. Would you share this photo with your friends right away, or would you first doubt it, thinking, "Wait, is this photoshopped?"

We are living in an era where the old adage "seeing is believing" no longer holds true. This is because changing the background of a photo with a single click or using AI to create people who don't exist in the world has become incredibly easy. In this overflowing sea of digital information, discerning what is real has become an increasingly tiring and painful task for modern people.

To solve these concerns, Google DeepMind, one of the world's leading AI laboratories, has introduced an intriguing solution. It is **Backstory**, an experimental AI tool that thoroughly tracks the 'past' and 'context' of online images. [Exploring the context of online images with Backstory](https://deepmind.google/blog/exploring-the-context-of-online-images-with-backstory/)

## Why is this important?

The numerous images we encounter on the internet are sometimes consumed in ways completely different from their original intent. Compilations made simply for fun can turn into malicious fake news used to attack someone, or photos of events that happened years ago in another country can be repackaged to cause confusion as if they happened here today. [Exploring the context of online images with Backstory](https://news-tech.io/en/news/deepmind-blog-exploring-the-context-of-online-images-with-backstory)

In an era of explosive growth in digital content, understanding the authenticity and **context** (the actual situation or intent behind the photo) of an image has become more important than ever. [Backstory: Google DeepMind's AI Tool That Reveals the Truth Behind ...](https://aicyclopedia.com/backstory-google-deepminds-ai-tool-that-reveals-the-truth-behind-online-images/) It is no longer enough to simply debate whether a photo is real or fake; we have entered an era where the ability to understand its 'journey'—where it came from (**origin**) and what changes (**manipulation**) it underwent while circulating the digital world—is essential.

This is where Backstory becomes a reliable ally. It acts as a kind of 'truth guide,' helping to clear away unnecessary noise in the flood of information and allowing us to trust digital content with peace of mind. [Exploring the context of online images with Backstory](https://news-tech.io/en/news/deepmind-blog-exploring-the-context-of-online-images-with-backstory)

## Easy understanding: A detective AI that checks a photo's 'passport'

To easily understand Backstory, think of a **'photo detective'** or a **'passport scanner'** at immigration.

Just as you can tell at a glance where a person has traveled by looking at the various country stamps in their passport, Backstory meticulously tracks the path an image has taken through the vast world of the internet. [DeepMind's Backstory AI Tracks Image Origins and Edits](https://saiwa.ai/news/deepmind-image-tracker/)

### 1. Understanding 'context' beyond simple search
The 'reverse image search' function already exists. If you upload a photo to Google Image Search, it finds websites where the same photo appears. However, Backstory goes a step further because it operates based on Google's latest AI model, **Gemini**.

Gemini is a 'multimodal AI' that understands both images and text simultaneously. In simple terms, it's an AI with both eyes (image analysis) and a mouth (language understanding). Thanks to this, Backstory doesn't just look for 'the same picture'; it can deeply analyze the 'meaning' and 'historical background' contained in the photo. [Exploring the context of online images with Backstory](https://news-tech.io/en/news/deepmind-blog-exploring-the-context-of-online-images-with-backstory)

### 2. A timeline showing the evolution of a photo at a glance
Backstory persistently digs into the process from the moment a photo first appears to how it was edited or used with a different meaning over time (**manipulation and journey**). [Backstory: Google DeepMind's AI Tool That Reveals the Truth Behind ...](https://aicyclopedia.com/backstory-google-deepminds-ai-tool-that-reveals-the-truth-behind-online-images/)

To use a metaphor:
*   **Imagine this**: You see a mysterious and beautiful photo of an aurora on social media. Using Backstory, you can immediately find out its true identity. It shows you in a timeline format: "This photo was originally taken 3 years ago as a plain night sky photo, but an artist photoshopped the aurora in. It was later misrepresented as a real aurora photo through community A and news blog B."

Thanks to this technical support, users can judge the reliability of an image themselves with unprecedented depth and convenience, without a complicated search process. ## Current Status: A shield protecting me from fake news

Currently, Backstory is in the stage of an **experimental AI tool** being developed by Google DeepMind. [Exploring the context of online images with Backstory](https://deepmind.google/blog/exploring-the-context-of-online-images-with-backstory/) While it hasn't been officially integrated into the smartphone apps or browsers we use every day, its goal is very clear.

Its biggest mission is **'strong defense against misinformation.'** Once we can understand how and why a photo has been distorted in different situations, we will no longer easily fall into traps when someone intentionally tries to manipulate public opinion or twist facts. [DeepMind's Backstory AI Tracks Image Origins and Edits](https://saiwa.ai/news/deepmind-image-tracker/)

DeepMind is confident that this tool will return **'agency'** to users in the digital world. Instead of a passive attitude of believing information provided one-sidedly, users will gain 'digital literacy' to directly check the roots of information and make their own judgments with the help of AI. [DeepMind's Backstory AI Tracks Image Origins and Edits](https://saiwa.ai/news/deepmind-image-tracker/)
