## The Explainer

Then how was this developer able to release this game to the world? Simply put, ChatGPT played the role of a "genius coding tutor and reliable development partner" sitting right next to you writing line by line of code together [Show HN: I made a PS1 style racing game](https://outpostracer.com).

It's much easier to understand with an analogy. Imagine assembling a very complex and huge Lego castle. The assembly manual is as thick as a book, and there are tens of thousands of types of parts. If you try to put this together alone from start to finish, your head will ache and you'll want to give up halfway. But what if an assembly master sits right next to you and guides you in real-time, saying, "Now, snap this square block in here, and attach the wheel part over there"? The work speed would become incomparably fast.

Instead of being deeply buried in the complex detailed grammar of a massive game engine or headache-inducing mathematical calculation formulas, the developer gave instructions to the AI in natural everyday language. For example, by requesting, "Create a bumpy track with this kind of feel," or "Apply physics rules where the tires slip slightly when the car makes a sharp turn," the AI neatly wrote programming codes that fit those requests, and the developer combined them to complete the game.
