Imagine this: You wake up in the morning, tell your AI, "Summarize today's meeting notes," and it perfectly highlights the core takeaways. Behind this convenience lies a massive "war of computing" (the calculation processes used to handle data) that we rarely see. The S-1 filing (the IPO registration document) recently released by AI leader Anthropic vividly lays bare the reality behind this war.

Anthropic is currently preparing for an IPO with a valuation estimated between $1 trillion and $2 trillion. [Source: Anthropic IPO: Anthropic's IPO prospectus shows sweeping AI vision (Economic Times)](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/anthropics-ipo-prospective-shows-sweeping-ai-vision-surging-costs/articleshow/134554130.cms), [Source: Anthropic files confidential S-1 (LinkedIn)](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jacobmann_anthropic-filed-a-confidential-draft-s-1-activity-7467554960893800448-5eJy). Revenue has jumped 12-fold in a single year. [Source: Anthropic's Revenue Jumped 12-Fold in 2025 (The Motley Fool)](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/10/10/anthropics-revenue-jumped-12-fold-in-2025-but-so-d/) Yet, behind the spectacular growth, challenges beyond the imagination of the average person await.

### Why does this matter?

AI technology is no longer just a toy in a lab. Companies are rushing to adopt AI. Anthropic's IPO will serve as a crucial test to determine whether this massive AI ecosystem is a sustainable business or just a bubble. For everyday consumers, it is a significant turning point that will decide whether this company can continue to provide smarter AI at reasonable costs, or if services will shrink due to cost constraints.

### Simplified: The $518 Billion "Infrastructure Debt"

The most eye-catching figure in Anthropic’s disclosure is the $518 billion expenditure commitment. [Source: Anthropic S-1 is leaked (One More Thing In AI)](https://www.onemorethinginai.com/p/anthropic-s1-leaked-we-got-inside-scoop-losing-lot-money) How big is that? It is enough money to give approximately $14,000 to every person in a nation of 50 million people.

Why is so much money needed? Simply put, AI is a "power-hungry beast." Metaphorically, Anthropic has hired a highly sophisticated "AI chef" to provide services, but every time this chef cooks, they consume an endless supply of incredibly expensive, top-tier ingredients (computing resources). [Source: Anthropic’s $518B Compute Bet Depends on Customers (Observer)](https://observer.com/2026/10/anthropic-dario-amodei-ipo-ai-market-customers/)

Furthermore, Anthropic announced a $42 billion net loss in 2025; fortunately, a large portion of this is not actual cash flowing out, but rather "accounting costs" generated as the company's valuation skyrocketed. [Source: If Anthropic Lost $42B... (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lVLYvvgwwaY) However, aside from this, the structural burden of needing to pay billions of dollars in computing costs every year remains a reality. [Source: Anthropic Pays xAI $15B (Epinium)](https://epinium.com/en/blog/1-25-billion-a-month-anthropics-compute-dependency-and-the-ai-supply-chain-risk-every-enterprise-is-ignoring/)

### Current Situation: The Complex Links of the AI Ecosystem

Currently, Anthropic relies on tech giants like Amazon and Google (Alphabet) as its primary providers of computing resources. [Source: Which Stocks Have the Biggest Exposure to Anthropic's Growth? (The Motley Fool)](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/10/10/which-stocks-have-the-biggest-exposure-to-anthropi/) These companies are partners helping Anthropic grow, but they are also competitors in the AI market.

Anthropic acknowledges several risks, including intense competition, high computing costs, unpredictable AI behavior, and government regulation. [Source: Anthropic IPO questions raised (LinkedIn)](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/anthropic-ipo-questions-raised-4q-pressures-grow-despite-airac) No matter how intelligent an AI model they develop, if companies eventually decide to "use a different AI that is 30% cheaper even if it's slightly less smart," Anthropic's revenue is bound to be threatened. [Source: Anthropic's $2T IPO Has a $518B Problem (DEV Community)](https://dev.to/max_quimby/anthropics-2t-ipo-has-a-518b-problem-3ml8)

### AI's Perspective: At the Crossroads of Cost and Value

Looking at Anthropic's situation, it is reminiscent of a farmer who pays massive feed costs every day to raise a goose that lays golden eggs. While the goose is laying bigger golden eggs (amazing technological prowess), the key is whether it can generate more revenue than the cost of the feed. The AI industry is now moving beyond the initial experimental stage into the cold business world of "how much profit can be made."

### What happens next?

Anthropic's destiny depends on how "fast" and "how much" actual businesses integrate AI into their operations. [Source: Anthropic’s $518B Compute Bet Depends on Customers (Observer)](https://observer.com/2026/10/anthropic-dario-amodei-ipo-ai-market-customers/) Only by proving that AI is a tool that delivers tangible profits beyond mere experimentation will they be able to create the cash flow necessary to cover that massive $518 billion expenditure commitment. Investors and customers are watching to see how Anthropic converts these astronomical costs into "real value."
