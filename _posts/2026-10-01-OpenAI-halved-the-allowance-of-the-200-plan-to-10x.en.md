Imagine you have an AI service you use steadily every month. Now, how would you feel if, one day, the price stayed the same but the number of times you could query it per month suddenly dropped by half?

OpenAI recently made significant changes to its subscription models for ChatGPT users. It has halved the usage allowance for the popular $200 'Pro plan' and introduced a new, higher-priced tier at $500 ([Source: OpenAI halves $200 plan allowance, launches $500...](https://thenewstack.io/openai-halves-200-plan/)). This is news that may come as an unpleasant surprise to those who rely heavily on AI for their work.

### Why does this matter?

This shift is a phenomenon occurring as AI services transition from 'general-purpose tools' used by everyone to 'core infrastructure' that can determine the success or failure of a business. Running AI models requires immense computing resources and astronomical costs. For OpenAI, it was a necessary strategic choice to streamline the service structure and manage costs for users who demand higher-performance features.

For the average user, the era has arrived where you must calculate your AI usage patterns more precisely. It is time to seriously consider whether the value you gain from AI outweighs a cost of $200 or $500 per month, or if there are more affordable alternatives.

### Explained Simply

Let's compare this change to an 'all-you-can-eat buffet' at a restaurant:

*   **Previous $200 Plan:** It was a "VIP card that allowed you to eat 20 times more than a regular customer (Plus plan) for $200" ([Source: OpenAI halves Pro 200's Work and Codex allowance, introduces...](https://aistartupsnews.com/news/openai-halves-pro-200-s-work-and-codex-allowance-introduces-gpt-6-1-sol/)).
*   **Current $200 Plan:** It is like cutting the limit in half, saying, "Now you can only eat up to 10 times more for the same price" ([Source: OpenAI halves Pro 200's Work and Codex allowance, introduces...](https://aistartupsnews.com/news/openai-halves-pro-200-s-work-and-codex-allowance-introduces-gpt-6-1-sol/)).
*   **New $500 Plan:** They have added a high-end policy saying, "If you want to eat more and faster, pay $500. Then you can eat up to 25 times more" ([Source: OpenAI DevDay 2026: Every Announcement and What It Costs](https://capitalandcompute.net/blog/openai-devday-2026-announcements-prices/)).

In short, they have scaled back the 'value' model and more clearly separated a 'premium' model for heavy users who are willing to pay. According to Tibo, an OpenAI insider, this pricing adjustment means the practical API usage value of the $200 plan has been adjusted to half of what it was before ([Source: OpenAI Reopens ChatGPT Pro $200 Sign-Ups With New Usage...](https://www.unite.ai/openai-reopens-chatgpt-pro-200-sign-ups-with-new-usage-calculation/)).

### Current Situation

As of September 30, 2026, OpenAI has begun accepting new sign-ups for the $200 Pro plan again ([Source: OpenAI Reopens ChatGPT Pro $200 Sign-Ups With New Usage...](https://www.unite.ai/openai-reopens-chatgpt-pro-200-sign-ups-with-new-usage-calculation/)).

Existing subscribers don't need to worry too much. Those who were already using the $200 plan can benefit from a grace period (grandfathering) that allows them to maintain their existing usage limits until the end of October ([Source: OpenAI DevDay 2026: Every Announcement and What It Costs](https://capitalandcompute.net/blog/openai-devday-2026-announcements-prices/)). The new usage limits will officially take effect starting in November ([Source: OpenAI halves Pro 200 usage and launches a $500 ChatGPT plan at...](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-devday-pro-200-usage-cut-pro-500-plan)).

The newly introduced $500 tier is branded as 'Ultrafast.' True to its name, it provides faster response times and higher processing capabilities, and is designed to target the enterprise market by allowing users to utilize these limits within partner apps, going beyond simple usage expansion ([Source: OpenAI DevDay 2026: Every Announcement and What It Costs](https://capitalandcompute.net/blog/openai-devday-2026-announcements-prices/)).

### What lies ahead?

The gap between pricing plans for individual users and enterprise users is likely to widen further. As this change demonstrates, AI models are rapidly evolving beyond simple chat tools into 'digital labor' capable of performing increasingly precise calculations and complex tasks.

It will become crucial for users to understand how many 'tokens' (the unit of data processed by AI) they are consuming. Data literacy—the ability to read and understand data—has become essential to judge whether a 10x limit is sufficient per month or if a higher premium plan is required. It remains to be seen whether OpenAI's policy will satisfy users and create a sustainable ecosystem, or if it will accelerate the migration to more affordable competitor models.
