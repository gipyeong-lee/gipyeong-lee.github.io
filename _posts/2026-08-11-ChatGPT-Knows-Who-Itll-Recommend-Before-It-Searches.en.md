Imagine this: You are having coffee with a friend on the weekend and ask, "What are some good AI memo apps these days?" Your friend starts the conversation with a list already in their head, right? Surprisingly, the artificial intelligence we use every day, ChatGPT, has been acting in a similar way.

We usually think that when we search for something on Google, the search engine ranks the results and shows them to us. However, the way ChatGPT recommends products or brands is completely different from the traditional search methods we are familiar with. ChatGPT does not read all web pages and then rank them; it uses a unique method of searching after having already decided on the answer.

### Why is this important?

This fact conveys two things to us. First, what we believe to be "search results" might actually be results filtered by the AI's "choices." Second, for companies and marketers, it means that the old "search ranking exposure strategies" no longer work in this world. Since the criteria by which AI recommends brands have changed, the way we consume information in the future will become much more sophisticated.

### Easy to Understand: AI's 'Pre-selection' Pipeline

So, what process does ChatGPT go through to recommend a brand? According to [Source 6](https://aiplusautomation.com/blog/chatgpt-optimization-complete-guide), this process is not just a simple search but a "multi-stage pipeline."

1. **Search Decision**: It judges for itself whether a search is needed for the question.
2. **Pre-selection**: Before searching, it already inserts the names of candidate brands it wants to recommend into the search query (question) internally within the model. [Source 1](https://suganthan.com/blog/chatgpt-decides-before-it-searches/)
3. **Bing Integration and Real-time Verification**: Afterward, it finds relevant pages through the search engine and reads the content as a language model to verify if it is appropriate. [Source 6](https://aiplusautomation.com/blog/chatgpt-optimization-complete-guide)

To put it simply, ChatGPT is like a "gourmet who already has a list of favorite restaurants in their head." Even if it visits a new neighborhood, it doesn't look for restaurants randomly; it goes through the process of first entering names it has already heard of into the search box to confirm them.

### Why does it recommend that brand?

In the traditional search engines we used to know, backlinks (other websites linking to your site) or keyword optimization were important. However, according to [Source 5](https://www.onely.com/blog/how-chatgpt-decides-which-brands-to-recommend), **traditional SEO metrics have almost no influence on ChatGPT's brand recommendations.**

Instead, the AI selects brands based on the following three criteria:

* **Cognition based on training data**: How often the brand was mentioned during the model's training process [Source 3, 5](https://www.trysight.ai/blog/how-chatgpt-chooses-brands-to-recommend), [Source 5](https://www.onely.com/blog/how-chatgpt-decides-which-brands-to-recommend/)
* **Mentions in authoritative lists**: How often it was included in lists by reliable external media or organizations (accounting for 41% of all recommendations) [Source 5](https://www.onely.com/blog/how-chatgpt-decides-which-brands-to-recommend/)
* **Third-party trust signals**: Objective verification indicators such as awards or user reviews [Source 5](https://www.onely.com/blog/how-chatgpt-decides-which-brands-to-recommend/)

Ultimately, AI does not recommend a brand simply because it has many pages on the internet; it first considers whether the brand is socially verified.

### What will happen in the future?

The proportion of brands recommended by artificial intelligence will continue to increase. Many consumers are already asking ChatGPT before opening Google. [Source 15](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jarrell-hibler_geo-ai-digitalmarketing-activity-7491135922818809856-kvrM) This means that the landscape of marketing is shifting from "how to increase search ranking" to "how to be included in the AI's internal list."

Why don't you, our readers, also take a moment to think, "This answer is a decision made by combining the AI's existing knowledge and external data," the next time you view results recommended by artificial intelligence?

### MindTickleBytes AI Reporter's View
AI's recommendation is not just about showing search results; it is a "judgment" made based on past data and external trust signals. Search results may ultimately be a journey to find evidence to support a decision the AI has already made. To become wiser consumers in the future, we need to get into the habit of asking for the basis behind "why the AI recommended this brand."
