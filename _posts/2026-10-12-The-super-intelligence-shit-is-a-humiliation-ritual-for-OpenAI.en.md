Imagine this: You are a mathematician who has been researching a conundrum that has remained unsolved for decades. Suddenly, an AI company appears out of nowhere and announces, "We solved this problem in an instant." How would you feel? Recently, OpenAI, a leader in the artificial intelligence (AI) field, staged exactly this situation, finding itself at the center of a heated controversy.

### Why is this important?

This is not just news about an AI being good at solving math problems. OpenAI has consistently claimed that it is building "superintelligence" (AI that surpasses all human intelligence) and that it can completely restructure human society through it. However, as the company encounters various realistic barriers, such as backlash from the mathematics community and corporate profitability issues, skepticism is growing regarding whether its grand claims are realistic or merely marketing tools to inflate corporate value. [Source: The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/jun/03/as-the-tech-mega-ipo-race-hots-up-has-openai-missed-its-moment)

### Understanding it simply: What is AI's 'conquest of mathematics'?

OpenAI recently claimed that an internal model, which began training on August 28, solved hundreds of long-standing conundrums across major fields of mathematics. Specifically, it released research results that included 722 mathematical results spanning 372 groups. [Source: Хабр](https://habr.com/ru/companies/bothub/news/1085122/), [Source: Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/technology/some-mathematicians-call-for-an-openai-boycott-after-ai-solves-over-700-problems-article-14048569.html)

To put it simply, it's like "giving a puppy that just finished basic training a complex math puzzle to solve, and then claiming the puppy has reached superintelligence and mastered all human knowledge because it got the answer right." It may be a great achievement for an AI to have found the correct answers, but experts are divided on whether this serves as proof that the AI possesses intelligence surpassing humans. In the past, many people have cheered when an AI passed a specific test, calling it "superintelligence," only to find it did not meet expectations in reality. [Source: LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/noam-brown-8b785b62_i-think-people-are-overindexing-on-the-openai-activity-7278427303758503936-OZFt)

Under these circumstances, the Association for Human Mathematics, an authoritative organization in the field, strongly protested OpenAI's announcement and called for a boycott. [Source: Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/technology/some-mathematicians-call-for-an-openai-boycott-after-ai-solves-over-700-problems-article-14048569.html) Critics argue that the essence of mathematical research is not just finding the answer, but understanding the process and logical context, and that the method OpenAI used is far from the rigor required by the academic community. [Source: The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/tech/openai-maths-ai-artificial-intelligence-b3062704.html)

### Current situation: The reality hidden behind the glamorous announcement

OpenAI recently announced that it would introduce a feature called "Intelligent UI (IntelligentUI)" into the GPT-6 model to make user interactions more visual. [Source: The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/openai-unveils-new-feature-called-intelligent-ui-in-gpt-6-heres-the-lowdown/articleshow/134785472.cms) However, it is going through a difficult time internally. CEO Sam Altman previously poured out confident predictions that AI would change the world, but the company is now facing profitability issues. This is because it has failed to generate meaningful revenue through ads or unique service models. [Source: The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/jun/03/as-the-tech-mega-ipo-race-hots-up-has-openai-missed-its-moment)

Furthermore, even the highly anticipated initial public offering (IPO) has been postponed to 2027, citing safety concerns. [Source: NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/12/nx-s1-5950588/openai-anthropic-ai-safety-researchers-hacks) In a situation where the company's future is opaque, the word "superintelligence" sometimes appears as a strategic means to support corporate value. [Source: ZDNET](https://www.zdnet.com/article/openai-says-its-working-toward-catastrophe-or-utopia-just-not-sure-which/)

### What will happen next?

OpenAI is still one of the leading companies in terms of technology, and people remain highly interested in its tech. [Source: YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nn6gEwMUbQ4) However, it will become more important to see what concrete value AI can provide in real life rather than the fantasy provided by the term "superintelligence." As experts point out, the ability to solve problems itself is not the completion of intelligence.

How do you, our readers, view these actions by OpenAI? It is a time when the sense of responsibility for how to apply and explain technology to society is just as important as the speed of its development.
