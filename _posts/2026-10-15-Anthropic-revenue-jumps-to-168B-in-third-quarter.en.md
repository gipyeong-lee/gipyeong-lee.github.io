Imagine this: This morning, you ask your AI assistant, "Summarize the core tasks of this project based on last week's meeting minutes." In an instant, the AI analyzes the materials and creates a clean report. Behind this convenience, which we now take for granted, the companies developing these technologies are seeing astronomical growth every quarter. At the center of this is Anthropic, the artificial intelligence company that recently released a surprising financial report.

Anthropic is well-known for its artificial intelligence model series, 'Claude.' According to a recent announcement, Anthropic recorded a phenomenal $16.8 billion in revenue in the third quarter of 2026. This is a rapid increase of about 45–50% in just three months, up from $11.5 billion in the second quarter [[Source 15](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/anthropic-q3-revenue-climbs-to-168-billion-report-221619202.html?fr=sycsrp_catchall), [Source 16](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-q3-revenue-jumps-46-to-17b-ahead-of-potential-november-ipo/), [Source 19](https://www.zerohedge.com/markets/anthropic-says-q3-revenue-jumped-168-billion-and-turned-adjusted-profitable-run-rate-math)].

## Why Does This Matter?

There is a deeper significance here than just the growing numbers. For a long time, many AI companies have faced the question, "The technology is great, but how will you make money?" However, with this Q3 performance, Anthropic has proven that it has achieved adjusted profitability and is in a positive cash flow state [[Source 19](https://www.zerohedge.com/markets/anthropic-says-q3-revenue-jumped-168-billion-and-turned-adjusted-profitable-run-rate-math)].

This means that AI technology has moved beyond being an experimental toy in a lab and has fully established itself as an essential business tool. Especially with the IPO scheduled for November, this performance provides investors with strong confidence that "we have perfectly built a structure for making money" [[Source 16](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-q3-revenue-jumps-46-to-17b-ahead-of-potential-november-ipo/), [Source 17](https://investinglive.com/stocks/anthropic-q3-revenue-rises-45-to-16-8-billion-ahead-of-ipo/)].

## Simple Explanation

Let’s compare Anthropic's business model to a 'chef' and a 'restaurant.'

The AI model 'Claude' developed by Anthropic is like a very smart and tireless chef. If the AI of the past knew only how to boil simple instant noodles, the current Claude can create hotel-level cuisine in an instant.

However, instead of sending this chef directly to individual households, Anthropic sends them to 'large restaurants,' which are companies. These companies hire this chef to cook up customer service, data analysis, and complex data processing. About 80% of total revenue comes from these 'corporate restaurants' (Claude API and enterprise services) [[Source 6](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/anthropics-business-model-how-the-ai-safety-company-makes-money)]. Companies use Claude to increase work efficiency and pay Anthropic a monthly fee in return. In short, Anthropic has become a 'platform that lends smart chefs (AI models) to companies.'

## Where Are We Now?

Anthropic’s momentum is currently incredible. As of mid-2026, its annual revenue run-rate has exceeded $65 billion [[Source 3](https://en.sedaily.com/international/2026/08/19/anthropic-revenue-run-rate-tops-65-billion-up-sevenfold-in), [Source 6](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/anthropics-business-model-how-the-ai-safety-company-makes-money)]. This is an astonishing speed of growth compared to the end of last year.

However, competition is also extremely fierce. The revenue race with massive AI companies like OpenAI is heating up [[Source 4](https://dev.to/sheraz046/anthropic-vs-openai-the-revenue-race-thats-reshaping-ai-in-2026-4pi9)]. Still, Anthropic is evaluated as having secured a stable foundation for growth, given that its IPO preparations are proceeding smoothly [[Source 16](https://cryptofriefing.com/anthropic-q3-revenue-jumps-46-to-17b-ahead-of-potential-november-ipo/)].

## What Changes Are Coming?

The IPO scheduled for November will be the biggest turning point in Anthropic's history. The market expects that Anthropic will use the funds secured through the listing to develop even more powerful next-generation models and expand its global services. As general users, we will encounter AI services at a level we once couldn't have imagined, alongside an even smarter Claude.

Whether Anthropic can maintain its explosive growth after going public and remain the 'standard of the AI era' is what investors and the technology industry around the world are watching closely.
