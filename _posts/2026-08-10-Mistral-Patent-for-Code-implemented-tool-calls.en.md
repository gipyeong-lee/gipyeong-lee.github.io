Imagine this: You ask an assistant, "Check today's weather and organize my schedule." The assistant opens the 'weather app' and 'schedule management app' on its own and expertly handles the tasks. In the world of Artificial Intelligence (AI), this 'Tool calling' technology—where AI uses tools on its own to perform tasks—is becoming increasingly important. Recently, however, French AI company Mistral AI has become a hot topic in the tech industry after being granted a patent related to this tool-calling method.

### Why is this important?

The AI we use in our daily lives is evolving beyond just being good at talking; it is now entering a stage where it directly controls external services. The patent Mistral AI recently acquired concerns 'how the AI gives commands' when using tools. [Source: Mistral Patent for "Code implemented tool calls" | Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49243397) While the technology itself is technical, the fact that it has been recognized as a patent carries significant weight, as it may require other companies to check for patent infringement when developing AI services in the future.

To put it simply, tool calling is the process where AI transforms from a mere 'advisor' into a 'practitioner' that takes direct action. While AI previously only conveyed information, it is now using digital tools to create tangible results. The patent issue arising from this is an important matter that could affect the development methods of the entire AI technology ecosystem.

### Understanding it simply: AI creating 'code snippets'

Easily put, if previous AI simply gave commands like "tell me the weather" when using tools, Mistral AI's method involves the AI writing a **small piece of code (code block)** itself and delivering it to the tool. [Source: patentsgazette.uspto.gov](https://patentsgazette.uspto.gov/week26/OG/html/1547-5/US12670045-20260630.html)

It is similar to a chef (AI) not just speaking when getting ingredients, but writing down a recipe card (code snippet) and delivering it. This recipe card neatly wraps complex 'tool calling' content like a capsule. [Source: 12670045 Code implemented tool calls - patentscope2.wipo.int](https://patentscope2.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=US481918455)

In particular, this method is executed within a secure fence called a 'Sandbox,' which is like making a chef cook only in a designated space so as not to clutter the rest of the kitchen. [Source: 12670045 Code implemented tool calls - patentscope2.wipo.int](https://patentscope2.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=US481918455) Just as a chef can temporarily stop cooking if a problem arises, code execution can also be temporarily paused. [Source: 12670045 Code implemented tool calls - patentscope2.wipo.int](https://patentscope2.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=US481918455)

### Current Situation: A Patent Everyone is Watching

Mistral AI, headquartered in Paris, first applied for this patent on March 4, 2026, and was officially granted patent number (US 12670045 B1) on June 30. [Source: Targeted News Service](https://targetednews.com/pt_disp.php?pt_id=2827791)

However, not everyone is welcoming the news. The tech community is taking a critical view, arguing that this patent attempts to "claim ownership of a concept that was already openly used." Many experts point out that this is essentially no different from Remote Procedure Calls (RPC, a communication method between multiple computer systems) or JSON message delivery methods that have been used in the computer industry for a long time. [Source: Mistral Patent for "Code Implemented Tool Calls"](https://memedata.com/post/138459)

Metaphorically speaking, it is like someone claiming to have invented the 'wheel' that everyone already uses and filing for a patent. There is high concern that they are trying to get the packaging method patented rather than the essence of the technology.

### What will happen next?

While patents are a core asset for companies, there is concern that patents on foundational AI technologies, like this case, could hinder technology standardization and open development. [Source: Mistral Patent for "Code implemented tool calls" | Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49243397) Whether Mistral AI will use this patent to build its own ecosystem or if it will lead to legal disputes with other companies remains to be seen. Do you think the way AI calls tools should be subject to patents? We must not forget that technological progress grows fastest when built upon shared knowledge.

## MindTickleBytes AI Reporter's Opinion

The faster the pace of technological development, the more we must be wary of attempts to lock away shared knowledge with patents. Tool calling is not the exclusive property of any specific company, but rather a 'language' that AI must naturally possess to better assist humans. Standardization and cooperation, rather than monopoly, are the fastest paths to making the AI era healthy.
