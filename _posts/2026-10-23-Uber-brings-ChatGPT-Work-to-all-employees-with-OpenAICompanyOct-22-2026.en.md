Imagine this: You arrive at work in the morning, turn on your computer, and say to your AI, "Summarize the progress of last month's Uber project and connect the plugins needed to fill the gaps." Gone are the days when AI merely searched for information; we have entered an era where AI understands the context of a project and handles the necessary tools itself. Uber’s recent announcement of introducing 'ChatGPT Work' to all employees is a clear example of how the way we approach work is changing.

### Why is this important?

If the chatbots we have used until now were 'smart assistants' that simply answered questions, ChatGPT Work is closer to a 'work partner' that performs practical tasks alongside you. This tool supports the entire workflow from start to finish by planning projects, managing data, and invoking necessary functions via external plugins. [Reference 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FsW1cFra9cQ), [Reference 6](https://postium.ru/chatgpt-work/), [Reference 10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uyFvoBrOmWs) This signals that companies have begun to recognize AI not as a subject of curiosity, but as a core infrastructure responsible for actual productivity.

### Understanding it simply: From 'Nutritionist' to 'Chef'

To understand the role of ChatGPT Work, let's use a culinary analogy.

If a regular chatbot is a 'nutritionist' who answers when you ask what is in your refrigerator, ChatGPT Work is like a 'chef' who knows all the ingredients in the fridge, the kitchen tools (plugins), and the recipes (sources and instructions), and starts cooking for you. [Reference 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FsW1cFra9cQ), [Reference 6](https://postium.ru/chatgpt-work/)

For example, suppose you need to write a project report based on specific documents. ChatGPT Work doesn't stop at just writing the text. It learns the 'source' (reference document) you uploaded, invokes 'plugins' to analyze data when complex calculations are required, and completes the optimal report according to the 'instructions' you set in advance. [Reference 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FsW1cFra9cQ)

### The current situation: Power comes with constraints

Currently, ChatGPT Work handles advanced tasks seamlessly, from project management to external data integration. [Reference 10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uyFvoBrOmWs) However, because it is powerful enough for corporate use, there are limits to its usage. As of August 25, 2026, OpenAI reintroduced 5-hour usage limits for Codex and ChatGPT Work. [Reference 4](https://theivansergeev.com/guide-limity-chatgpt/)

There is an important detail to note here: ChatGPT Work, Codex, and AI agents share a 'common usage pool.' [Reference 5](https://blog.fillikam.com/guides/openai-srezala-limity-chatgpt-codex/) Therefore, using too many agents in one area can interfere with other work tasks. Of course, 'regular chat,' where you talk comfortably with friends, is not included in this limit, so you can rest easy. [Reference 7](https://www.ssdnodes.com/learn/lang/ru/chatgpt-work-limit-reached-what-to-do)

If a banner saying 'You're out of Codex and Work usage' appears on your screen with a limit bar, it means your usage for that mode has been exhausted, and you should take a break. [Reference 7](https://www.ssdnodes.com/learn/lang/ru/chatgpt-work-limit-reached-what-to-do)

### What will happen in the future?

AI will become smarter and perform more practical tasks. Recent models are spending more time 'thinking,' leading to a trend where they can utilize various tools more deeply. [Reference 5](https://blog.fillikam.com/guides/openai-srezala-limity-chatgpt-codex/) However, the more complex the task the AI performs, the more computing resources it consumes. Therefore, in the future corporate environment, 'AI resource management ability'—allocating AI to the most important tasks within a set quota—will likely emerge as a core competency for office workers. [Reference 11](https://modelora.ru/news/pyatichasovaya-kvota-chatgpt-plus-sgoraet-za-2026-09-07)

### A perspective from the MindTickleBytes AI reporter
Seeing global companies like Uber adopting AI across the board demonstrates the standard for the future work environment. The ability to utilize AI is no longer an 'option.' It is evolving into a 'strategy' area where one must consider how to efficiently distribute and manage tools.
