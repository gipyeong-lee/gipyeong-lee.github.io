Imagine delegating complex coding tasks or lengthy development projects to an AI, only for it to independently identify problems, propose solutions, and produce results. This special experience, akin to staying up late coding with an experienced developer colleague, is the new capability showcased by 'Claude Fable 5,' the top-tier AI model recently released by Anthropic.

However, last June, many users who were boosting their productivity with this innovative tool faced an absurd situation: a sudden service suspension. Fortunately, the service is back, and Anthropic has shared the welcome news of extending the free access period until July 12 to reward users for their patience. [Source: Claude on X](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2074548242386178258)

## Why is this important?

For those who use AI as a daily work partner, this extension means an additional opportunity to fully experience Claude’s most powerful capabilities. Claude Fable 5 goes beyond being a simple conversational chatbot; it possesses 'agentic' abilities to autonomously handle complex tasks and provide solutions throughout the entire software development lifecycle. [Source: Claude Fable \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude/fable)

For paid subscribers who felt disappointed by the disruption in their workflows due to the service suspension, this decision serves as an appropriate compensation to soothe those frustrations. Above all, with the extension of the promotional period that was originally set to end in early July, more users can now test the high productivity of this model and verify whether it fits their workflow. [Source: "Claude Fable 5" trial period extended to July 12, 23:59:59 for all paid plans - Mado no Mori](https://forest.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/2123273.html)

## Simple Explanation

To use a simple analogy, Claude Fable 5 is like a 'highly intelligent new employee who has been deployed to the field immediately after graduation.' While previous AI models operated at a level of responding to conversations based on basic knowledge, Fable 5 proactively performs practical missions, such as understanding the entire context of a given long-term project and writing or modifying necessary code by itself. [Source: Claude Fable \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude/fable)

Of course, even for such a smart AI, 'Guardrails' (technical restrictions designed to prevent the AI from generating harmful responses) are essential for safety. Just as a new employee is provided with guidelines to ensure they do not violate company regulations, Fable 5 is designed to prevent risky responses in high-stakes fields such as cybersecurity or biology. [Source: Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 is a version of Mythos the public can access today](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/09/anthropics-claude-fable-5-is-a-version-of-mythos-the-public-can-access-today/)

## Current Status

Claude Fable 5 is currently back to normal operation worldwide. As mentioned, a global service suspension occurred on June 12 due to US government export control guidelines. After a 19-day standoff with the government, the service was restored to global users on July 1. [Source: Claude Fable 5: What It Is, Why It Was Suspended, and What ...](https://theaicareerlab.com/blog/claude-fable-5-for-professionals)

The extended free access period runs until 23:59:59 on July 12 (US time), and all paid subscription plan members can fully utilize the outstanding performance of Fable 5 during this period at no additional cost. [Source: "Claude Fable 5" trial period extended to July 12, 23:59:59 for all paid plans - Mado no Mori](https://forest.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/2123273.html)

## What to Expect Next

Anthropic is painting a future where developers can delegate even more ambitious projects to AI agents and trust the results. [Source: Claude Fable \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude/fable) However, among users, there is growing curiosity regarding the official pricing structure or whether it will be included in standard subscription plans once the free access period ends completely. [Source: "We're extending access to Claude Fable 5 on all paid plans through July 12." | Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48821102) A key point to watch is what kind of official service Anthropic will launch after July 12, and whether there will be any further extension policies.
