Imagine this: This morning, you were asking your AI coding assistant, 'Codex' (an AI agent specializing in coding tasks), to write a project script slightly longer than usual. Suddenly, a "Usage limit exceeded" message pops up, and your work grinds to a halt. It’s a frustrating scenario, but it's a new reality many ChatGPT Plus subscribers will face starting today.

On August 25, OpenAI announced that it is reintroducing the '5-hour usage limit' policy for ChatGPT Plus subscribers. [OpenAI Restores Five-Hour Codex Limit for ChatGPT Plus](https://www.ithinkdiff.com/openai-codex-five-hour-limit/) This is undoubtedly disappointing news for those who have been enjoying relatively free usage. We will break down why these limits are back and how they might affect your daily AI usage.

## Why is this important?

AI technology is evolving beyond a simple conversation tool into 'agents' (AI programs that act independently based on user instructions)—helping with coding or automating complex tasks in the workplace. Codex and Work (an agent mode designed to handle complex tasks in a workspace), which were released last July, are at the core of this productivity push. [ChatGPT Usage Limit Shock: Essential 5-Hour Cap Facts](https://www.progressiverobot.com/2026/08/25/chatgpt-usage-limit-5-hour-plus-pro/)

The return of these limits means that as our reliance on AI for work grows, the computing resources (computing power) OpenAI needs to manage are under significant strain. Simply put, it is a form of 'speed control' to ensure that specific users do not monopolize limited server resources, allowing for fairer access. [OpenAI Reinstates 5-Hour Usage Caps for ChatGPT Plus, Not for ...](https://xenospectrum.com/en/openai-plus-five-hour-limit-return/)

## An analogy: A 'time limit' at a buffet restaurant

To understand this situation, imagine a popular local buffet restaurant. If everyone could stay indefinitely without limits, other waiting customers would never get a chance to eat. So, the owner sets a 'maximum stay duration' to ensure everyone gets a fair chance to enjoy the food.

Here, the 'food' is the response provided by the AI, and the 'restaurant' is OpenAI's server. The '5-hour limit' is similar to a buffet time limit. However, the 5 hours mentioned here does not mean 5 hours on the clock, but rather a 'rolling limit' representing how intensively you have used the AI over a specific period. [OpenAI Restores Five-Hour Codex Limit for ChatGPT Plus](https://www.ithinkdiff.com/openai-codex-five-hour-limit/) In other words, if you cram too many tasks into a short period, your usage may be temporarily restricted.

## Current status

Currently, this limit applies only to 'ChatGPT Plus' subscribers. This announcement was confirmed by Tibo Sottiaux, OpenAI's product lead, who shared the news directly through the platform. [Codex Plus 5-Hour Limit Returns Aug 26 (Pro Exempt ...](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/codex-plus-5-hour-limit-returns-chatgpt-work-august-2026)

One fortunate aspect is that 'ChatGPT Pro' subscribers, who pay a higher fee, will be exempt from this limit for the coming months. [OpenAI quietly enabled the 5-hour limit for Team/Business accounts...](https://community.openai.com/t/openai-quietly-enabled-the-5-hour-limit-for-team-business-accounts-too-tibo-s-announcement-never-mentioned-this/1392494) This provides some breathing room for professionals who rely on high-performance AI as a primary work tool.

## What happens next?

Experts analyze this measure as an essential process to reduce strain on computing servers in the short term, thereby providing a more stable service. [OpenAI Reinstates 5-Hour Usage Caps for ChatGPT Plus, Not for ...](https://xenospectrum.com/en/openai-plus-plus-five-hour-limit-return/)

From a user's perspective, when coding or writing documents with AI, you will need to develop the habit of breaking tasks into smaller units and using them strategically rather than trying to process everything at once. OpenAI is also expected to continue efforts to ensure fairer service while managing users so they do not exhaust their quotas all at once. [Bad news: ChatGPT Plus users have annoying limits once again](https://www.androidauthority.com/chatgpt-five-hour-limits-3702542/) As AI becomes smarter, we are entering an era where we must give more thought to the 'limitations' and 'efficiency' of the tools we use.
