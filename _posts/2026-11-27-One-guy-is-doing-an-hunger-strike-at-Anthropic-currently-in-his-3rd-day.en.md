Imagine walking past the lobby of a massive, glittering glass tech giant every morning on your commute, where someone is standing completely abstaining from food, holding just a placard [Source Title](https://twitter.com/ElliotC1/status/2125689373337489419), [Source Title](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/778773/the-hunger-strike-to-end-ai-anthropic-google-deepmind-agi). Amidst an ordinary daily life where you pull out your smartphone and marvel at the latest artificial intelligence (AI) features, how would you feel if someone was staging a life-or-threatening hunger strike shouting, "Stop the speed of this technology!"?

The world we live in today is filled with news of AI getting smarter day by day. Yet behind that dazzling innovation, deep anxieties and warning alarms are ringing: "Where exactly is this speed taking us?" Through surprising protest news recently emerging in front of cutting-edge AI labs in Silicon Valley and London, let's discuss the heavy questions that the AI era poses to us.

---

## Why Is This Important?

We often focus on the convenience AI brings. We think of it as a magical tool that writes for us, analyzes complex data in an instant, and relieves countless daily hassles. However, the ultimate goal pursued by the companies building AI—namely, the development of **AGI (Artificial General Intelligence, Human-level or superior AI that can handle any intellectual task)** that surpasses human intelligence—is a completely different dimension from simply creating another convenient program.

Simply put, blindly building a massive intelligence system capable of slipping beyond human control without any braking mechanisms is like boarding a high-speed train with broken brakes. While the train is running, you might enjoy the wind and the sense of speed, but if the end of the tracks is severed, everyone faces fatal peril. Metaphorically speaking, it's like accelerating at top speed with only an engine turned on, before even learning how to steer the ship or grab life jackets while navigating a vast ocean.

The voices of anti-AI activists are a powerful warning against this brake-less sprint. The message they cast with their entire bodies is not mere technology phobia, but demands a fundamental social reflection: "Can we really push ahead with a future we cannot control?" [Source Title](https://futurism.com/ai-hunger-strike-anthropic). Because the speed at which technology transforms our lives is so rapid, the reality is that ordinary citizens are not even given time to deeply think and debate about it.

## Easy to Understand: The Brake-less Train and the Hunger Strike

To help understanding, let's take another example. Imagine there is a factory developing a new material or chemical substance that could be extremely potent and hazardous. Residents and social members have the right to demand rigorous verification of whether that substance is safe, or whether the entire city could be imperiled if a leak occurred.

However, current tech giants in Silicon Valley are waging an AI development race pouring massive resources, much like Olympic athletes who must reach the finish line before anyone else. For instance, **Anthropic**, known as a representative AI safety research company, saw its computing and infrastructure spending alone reach a staggering $7.3 billion throughout 2025, and spent a whopping $8.9 billion during the first six months of 2026 [Source Title](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E8TJMZeVtHs).

Amidst this massive capital flow where astronomical money changes hands, an individual citizen's voice can seem utterly small. Therefore, anti-AI activist Guido Reichstadter chose the most extreme and primitive method of a "hunger strike," abstaining from food [Source Title](https://twitter.com/ElliotC1/status/2125689373337489419), [Source Title](https://futurism.com/ai-hunger-strike-anthropic). Casting silent gazes toward employees commuting every morning [Source Title](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/778773/the-hunger-strike-to-end-ai-anthropic-google-deepmind-agi) and urging corporate leaders to take responsibility [Source Title](https://futurism.com/ai-hunger-strike-anthropic], his image evokes the modern-day David and Goliath story of an individual standing up against technological power amidst a forest of giant buildings.

## Current Situation: Warning Alarms Ringing in San Francisco and London

Indeed, such social resistance has not been confined to a single company, manifesting as a global movement.

Anti-AI activist Guido Reichstadter has been continuing a prolonged hunger strike in front of Anthropic's headquarters located in San Francisco, USA [Source Title](https://twitter.com/ElliotC1/status/2125689373337489419), [Source Title](https://decrypt.co/341349/ai-doomer-still-starving-anthropic-hunger-strike). He is a figure who has previously shown active engagement, participating in protests locking the doors of OpenAI's offices as a member of the civil disobedience group "Stop AI" [Source Title](https://sfgate.com/tech/article/san-francisco-anthropic-hunger-strike-21039988.php).

Across the Atlantic in London, UK, fellow activists Michael Trazzi and Denys Sheremet staged similar hunger strikes in front of Google DeepMind's headquarters [Source Title](https://www.businessinsider.com/hunger-strike-anthropic-deepmind-ai-threat-2025-9), [Source Title](https://decrypt.co/341349/ai-doomer-still-starving-anthropic-hunger-strike). Although the London protestors wrapped up their schedule [Source Title](https://decrypt.co/341349/ai-doomer-still-starving-anthropic-hunger-strike), their actions right in the heart of global tech hubs vividly demonstrated how deep and existential public fear regarding the pace of AI advancement is [Source Title](https://www.businessinsider.com/hunger-strike-anthropic-deepmind-ai-threat-2025-9).

Amidst focused media and public attention, companies targeted by the protests—such as Anthropic and DeepMind—have maintained a cautious stance without immediately releasing official comments in response to media requests [Source Title](https://www.businessinsider.com/hunger-strike-anthropic-deepmind-ai-threat-2025-9), [Source Title](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/778773/the-hunger-strike-to-end-ai-anthropic-google-deepmind-agi). In particular, given that Anthropic champions "safety-mindedness" and has emphasized relatively ethical and safe AI development compared to competitors, it is highly suggestive that such resistance occurred right outside its headquarters [Source Title](https://sfgate.com/tech/article/san-francisco-anthropic-hunger-strike-21039988.php).

Amidst these upheavals, companies are accelerating business expansion by preparing for initial public offerings (IPOs) and submitting prospectuses to enter Nasdaq under the ticker symbol "ANT" [Source Title](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W0JDfYSpjnM), [Source Title](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E8TJMZeVtHs). This is the exact point where the commercial success of technology and concerns over human safety collide head-on.

## What Happens Next?

The core message that Guido Reichstadter, who continues his hunger strike, is casting to the world is clear. In interviews with the media, he asserted strongly:

> "We do not need to build AGI. It does not exist yet—and this is entirely our choice [Source Title](https://felloai.com/anti-ai-activists-on-hunger-strike-at-anthropic-and-deepmind/)."

Furthermore, he urged Anthropic's executives, board members, and employees:

> "We are in an emergency situation. We must immediately halt reckless actions that harm our society and strive to rectify the harms already done [Source Title](https://futurism.com/ai-hunger-strike-anthropic]."

Future prospects are not straightforward. Tech companies will introduce even more powerful models to the world backed by billions of dollars in infrastructure investments, and the temptation of productivity improvements brought by AI will be hard to resist. Yet simultaneously, social monitoring and civil disobedience movements against the uncontrollability of technology are unlikely to fade away easily.

What we need to watch is not simply how much smarter AI becomes. When human dignity and social safety nets are pushed backward while intoxicated by the speed of technological development, whether social systems capable of controlling this can grow alongside is the true crux.
