Imagine you are planning a massive project. What is the first dilemma that comes to mind? Likely, "Who should do what, and how should we divide the work?" It is just like delegating roles to team members: "Person A handles research, Person B drafts the outline, and Person C manages the final review." Well, the AI models announced recently have started working exactly like such team leads.

On November 9, 2026, Anthropic unveiled its new AI models: **Claude Fable 5.2** and **Claude Mythos 5.2**. [Source: Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2) [Source: Claude Fable 5.2 benchmarks & pricing](https://benchlm.ai/models/claude-fable-5-2) The core of these models is that they have gone beyond simply being smarter—they have maximized their capabilities as 'agents' (AI that acts autonomously to achieve a goal) that decompose and solve problems on their own.

## Why does this matter?

If existing AI was closer to the role of a 'consultant' answering questions, it has now entered the realm of a 'practitioner' that directly creates deliverables.

In particular, this latest release features significantly improved performance in coding, complex reasoning, and vision (the ability to understand visual information), allowing it to directly perform high-level technical work. [Source: Claude Fable 5.2/Mythos 5.2 launched simultaneously, targeting agentic technical tasks](https://asksurf.ai/pulse/kr/claude-fable-5-2-mythos-5-2-launch-two-track-model-strategy) This means that beyond just reducing work hours, it could change the very way experts analyze data and derive results.

## In simple terms: 'Divide and Conquer' and the Orchestrator

What is the secret behind these models solving complex problems? It is the 'Divide-and-Conquer' strategy. [Source: Anthropic — Claude Fable 5.2 & Mythos 5.2 In-depth Architecture Analysis](https://kr.linkedin.com/pulse/anthropic-claude-fable-52-mythos-심층-아키텍처-분석-에이전틱-오케스트레이션과-youshin-kim-tvhjc)

To use a metaphor, instead of trying to solve a massive puzzle alone, an **Orchestrator (a conductor AI)** has appeared. When this conductor encounters a complex engineering problem, it breaks it down into pieces and delegates sub-tasks to **Sub-agents (AI specialized in specific fields)**. It is similar to an architect reading blueprints and instructing carpenters, electricians, and plasterers to build a single large building. Thanks to this, they can systematically resolve complex system engineering problems that are difficult to solve in a single attempt.

## The current landscape: Two paths for the models

Interestingly, Anthropic has released two versions of models sharing the same brain structure, divided by their intended use. [Source: Anthropic — Claude Fable 5.2 & Mythos 5.2 In-depth Architecture Analysis](https://kr.linkedin.com/pulse/anthropic-claude-fable-52-mythos-심층-아키텍처-분석-에이전틱-오케스트레이션과-youshin-kim-tvhjc) [Source: Claude Fable 5.2 benchmarks & pricing](https://benchlm.ai/models/claude-fable-5-2)

1. **Claude Fable 5.2**: Born for general-purpose agent tasks. It is optimized for complex knowledge work that requires long periods of human concentration, such as document writing, data analysis, and coding. It possesses massive context understanding capabilities, capable of processing up to 1 million tokens at once, allowing it to grasp long reports or multi-stage research processes in one go. [Source: Claude Fable 5.2 platform docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/fable-5-2/overview) [Source: Claude Fable 5.2 - API Pricing & Providers](https://openrouter.ai/anthropic/claude-fable-5.2)
2. **Claude Mythos 5.2**: This model is equipped with stricter safety guardrails. It is specially designed for fields that can be sensitive or dangerous, such as cybersecurity or life science research, and comes with a security profile provided only to verified users. [Source: Which Claude model should you build with? Fable...](https://claude.dev/blog/which-claude-model-should-you-build-with/)

## Future expectations

Before long, we will move beyond simple requests like "Summarize this" and start delegating complex tasks to AI, such as "Analyze market data, write a 10-page strategy proposal, and organize the necessary graphs in Excel." [Source: Claude Fable 5.2 platform docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/fable-5-2/overview)

AI models are growing from media that merely convey information into 'colleagues' that judge and collaborate in real working environments. Watching what changes this smart 'conductor AI' will bring to your work environment will be a very interesting experience.
