Imagine this. You're on a boat in the middle of the blue ocean, and suddenly a pod of dolphins approaches, making "whistle" sounds. In the past, you might have just been amazed, thinking, "The dolphins must be in a good mood." But what if a device in your hand analyzed these sounds in real-time and translated them like this: "Hello! I am the leader of this pod. There's a school of delicious fish ahead, so let's go together!"

Does it sound like a scene from a science fiction movie? However, the rapid advancement of artificial intelligence (AI) technology is gradually bringing this movie-like imagination into the realm of reality. The protagonist is Google's recently announced innovative AI model, **'DolphinGemma.'** [DolphinGemma: How Google AI is helping decode dolphin communication](https://research.gatech.edu/dolphingemma-how-google-ai-helping-decode-dolphin-communication)

## Why is this important?

We have known for a very long time that dolphins are highly intelligent animals. They can recognize themselves in a mirror and hunt cooperatively in groups. However, exactly how they exchange complex information and what deep emotions they convey to one another has remained a great mystery to humanity. This is because dolphins use sounds—clicks, whistles, and burst pulses (very short, intense sounds)—that have structures completely different from human language. [DolphinGemma: How Google AI is helping decode dolphin communication](https://research.gatech.edu/dolphingemma-how-google-ai-helping-decode-dolphin-communication)

Understanding dolphin conversations has value beyond simply translating fascinating animal sounds. It is a historic attempt to approach **'interspecies communication'** (conversation between different biological species), a dream humanity has held for thousands of years. Through this, we can understand and protect marine ecosystems more deeply and learn how to truly coexist with another intelligence sharing the planet with us. [Google working to decode dolphin communication using AI](https://www.foxnews.com/science/google-working-decode-dolphin-communication-using-ai)

With this project, Google has applied Large Language Model (LLM) technology, which previously processed human text or code, to the sounds of nature. This proves that AI can transcend being a mere office tool to become a modern-day 'Rosetta Stone' (the stone that was the key to deciphering ancient Egyptian hieroglyphs) for decoding the secrets hidden in nature. [DolphinGemma—GoogleDeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/gemma/dolphingemma/)

## Easy Understanding: How does AI learn dolphin language?

The process of AI learning dolphin language is similar to how we learn a foreign language, yet much more sophisticated. Let's look at the core principles through two analogies.

### 1. SoundStream: Turning Sound into 'Digital Pieces'
Just as we might hear the sound 'Apple' and first think of the letters 'A-P-P-L-E' when learning English words, AI must convert dolphin sounds into data. Google used a technology called the **'SoundStream tokenizer'** here. [DolphinGemma: How AI can decipher dolphin communication](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/dolphingemma/)

**To use an analogy:** It's like the process of finely chopping a 'cassette tape' containing complex dolphin sounds into very small 'digital puzzle pieces' that the AI can read. These pieces come together to form the 'alphabet' of the dolphin language. Sounds converted into tokens (the minimum unit of information AI can understand) are finally in a state where the AI model can begin full-scale analysis. [DolphinGemma: How AI can decipher dolphin communication](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/dolphingemma/)

### 2. Large Language Model (LLM): Predicting What Comes After the Sound
DolphinGemma was born based on Google's latest language model, 'Gemma.' You've probably seen the feature where, when you type "What are we..." on your smartphone, it automatically suggests "eating today?" or "doing?". DolphinGemma works on a similar principle.

Professor Thad Starner of Georgia Tech explains: "Once a dolphin starts to make a sound, the AI tries to predict how that sound will end." [DolphinGemma: How Google AI is helping decode dolphin communication](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/google_dolphingemma-how-google-ai-is-helping-decode-activity-7317578448380538882-Ko3i)

**In simple terms:** AI is performing an 'auto-complete function' for the dolphin language. Thanks to learning countless dolphin conversation patterns, it can predict the next sound just by hearing the beginning, thinking, "Ah, this sound usually finishes this way in this situation." This is very important evidence that AI has begun to understand the unique grammar and structure of dolphin language. [DolphinGemma: How Google AI is helping decode dolphin communication](https://research.gatech.edu/dolphingemma-how-google-ai-helping-decode-dolphin-communication)

## Current Status: How far have we come?

This project was made possible through collaboration between Google, Georgia Tech, and a specialized research institution called the **Wild Dolphin Project (WDP)**. [DolphinGemma: How AI can decipher dolphin communication](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/dolphingemma/)

### 40 Years of Records, AI's Precious Textbook
The biggest reason DolphinGemma could become so smart is its vast 'study materials.' The Wild Dolphin Project team has accumulated data over **40 long years**, recording dolphin sounds and patiently observing their behavior in the ocean. [Google Uses DolphinGemma AI to Decode Dolphin Communication - Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/google-uses-dolphingemma-ai-to-decode-dolphin-communication/490753) AI learned this massive amount of data, equivalent to a human's lifetime, in one go and identified the subtle patterns in dolphin vocalizations.

### Summer 2025, Open to Scientists Worldwide
Google has decided not to keep this amazing technology to itself. In the summer of 2025, it plans to release the DolphinGemma model as **Open Source** (making technology public so anyone can use it for free). [Google Is Training a New A.I. Model to Decode Dolphin Chatter—and ...](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/google-is-training-a-new-ai-model-to-decode-dolphin-chatter-and-potentially-talk-back-180986434/) This is expected to allow marine biologists around the world to analyze the languages of the dolphins they study much faster and more accurately, bringing about tremendous progress in the field of marine science.

## What lies ahead?

The ultimate goal of DolphinGemma doesn't stop at simply listening to and analyzing sounds. Scientists dream that one day, **'real two-way conversation between humans and dolphins'** will become possible. [Google working to decode dolphin communication using AI](https://www.foxnews.com/science/google-working-decode-dolphin-communication-using-ai)

### The Beginning of the First Conversation Underwater
While we have mostly been in a position of unilaterally observing dolphin sounds until now, models like DolphinGemma could directly generate sounds that dolphins can understand. [SETI Tech On Earth: DolphinGemma: How Google AI Is Helping Decode ...](https://astrobiology.com/2025/04/seti-tech-on-earth-dolphingemma-how-google-ai-is-helping-decode-dolphin-communication.html) Of course, we must wait and see if real dolphins will understand and respond to the messages we send, but experts believe we are closer to that miraculous stage than ever before. [Google develops AI model to help researchers decode dolphin ...](https://siliconangle.com/2025/04/14/google-develops-ai-model-help-researchers-decode-dolphin-communication/)

However, there are still many challenges to solve. It's not just about imitating sound patterns; we must perfectly understand in what 'context' and with what 'intent' those sounds are used. Just as the phrase "Have you eaten?" can sometimes be a greeting and sometimes an expression of concern in human language, dolphin language can have different meanings depending on the situation.
