Imagine this: You wake up in the morning and tell your AI, "Create the code structure I need to develop today and update the related documentation." The AI completes the task effortlessly, like a skilled colleague. This is no longer a story from a movie. It is reported that Anthropic, one of the most prominent companies in the AI industry, is transforming from a tech-focused firm into a "money-making machine."

### Why does this matter?

For a long time, many have wondered, "I know AI is smart, but is it actually making money?" This doubt existed because many companies were operating expensive AI models without proving a sustainable business model. However, the expectation that Anthropic will record an operating profit of over $1 billion in the third quarter of 2026 is highly significant. [Source 1](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/anthropic-3q26-profit-over-1b-the) [Source 4](https://www.tickrwire.tech/article/anthropic-hits-1b-profit-in-q3-2026-ahead-of-ipo-plans) It proves that AI has moved beyond the experimental phase and has fully established itself as a "profitable tool" that substantially reduces corporate costs and dramatically increases productivity. [Source 11](https://intellectia.ai/news/crypto/anthropic-on-track-for-over-1-billion-profit-in-q3-2026-amid-ipo-filing)

### In simple terms: The 'Magic Formula' of AI business

If we were to use an analogy for Anthropic's profitability, it would be this: If AI in the past was at a basic level of simply following orders, Anthropic has now transformed into a "professional business partner" that has finished specialized training.

At the center of this is a software development tool called "Claude Code." Simply put, it lets AI take over the complex and repetitive coding tasks performed by programmers. Much like how filters in photo editing apps handle complex retouching with the press of a button, Claude Code efficiently manages many simple tasks developers would otherwise have to do. [Source 7](https://bayesiansapien.github.io/cere-bro/ai-industry/2026-07-08-anthropic-ipo/) Thanks to this, companies save time, and Anthropic is recognized for that value, creating a virtuous cycle of profit generation.

### Current Status: The Anthropic Sprint

Anthropic's growth is staggering. It already generated $10.9 billion in revenue in the second quarter of 2026 alone, achieving an operating profit of $559 million—two years ahead of its internal target. [Source 6](https://aitoolsrecap.com/Blog/anthropic-first-profit-2026-revenue-breakdown) Anthropic's current annual recurring revenue (ARR) has surpassed $60 billion. [Source 5](https://www.scribd.com/document/1065181807/SemiAnalysis-Anthropic-3Q26-Profit-Over-1B-the-Anthropic-IPO-Financials-Sneak-Peak) Building on its strong foothold in the software development market, it has surpassed major competitors like OpenAI to become a leader in the enterprise (B2B) AI market. [Source 7](https://bayesiansapien.github.io/cere-bro/ai-industry/2026-07-08-anthropic-ipo/) [Source 16](https://opentools.ai/news/anthropic-revenue-surpasses-openai)

This success isn't just Anthropic's story. As global demand for AI services explodes, an ecosystem is forming where companies producing the high-performance semiconductors (GPUs and ASICs) that act as AI's brains are growing alongside them. [Source 3](https://www.partgenie.ai/insights/anthropic-3q26-profit-over-1b-the-anthropic-ipo-financials-sneak-peak-2)

### What's next?

Anthropic completed its confidential filing for an Initial Public Offering (IPO) on June 1, 2026. [Source 5](https://www.scribd.com/document/1065181807/SemiAnalysis-Anthropic-3Q26-Profit-Over-1B-the-Anthropic-IPO-Financials-Sneak-Peak) [Source 7](https://bayesiansapien.github.io/cere-bro/ai-industry/2026-07-08-anthropic-ipo/) The investment industry sees this IPO as a major turning point that will re-evaluate the value of the entire AI industry. [Source 4](https://www.tickrwire.tech/article/anthropic-hits-1b-profit-in-q3-2026-ahead-of-ipo-plans) It will be a key point of interest to watch how much deeper AI will penetrate corporate settings to innovate productivity, and how Anthropic will lead the AI ecosystem after going public.
