Imagine this: One morning, while casually watching a TV comedy program on your way to work, the CEO of the company that created the AI service you use appears as the main character. But how would you feel if he were shaking in fear, saying, "Our AI could destroy humanity"?

Recently, the popular American comedy show *Saturday Night Live (SNL)* staged exactly this scene. In the "Weekend Update" segment of its season premiere, the show satirized Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei. The message hidden within the laughter is quite heavy. Why do those who create world-changing technologies keep talking about "doomsday"?

## Why It Matters

We are already deeply using AI in our daily lives. It answers our questions and summarizes complex documents in an instant. Yet, the very leaders creating this technology warn that "if it becomes too powerful, it could be dangerous for humanity." [Source 13](https://www.metatrader.com/en/news/beincrypto/4188537-anthropic-s-amodei-fires-another-warning-this-time-to)

These warnings are not simply intended to instill fear. They are necessary because the pace of technological development must be controlled, and standards for safety and containment must be established. As consumers, we also need to understand the potential risks of AI beyond it being a convenient tool. This will become an essential skill for wisely navigating the AI era that lies ahead.

## The Explainer

Dario Amodei is a prominent American AI researcher and the co-founder of Anthropic in 2021. Before that, he worked as the Vice President of Research at OpenAI. [Source 9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dario_Amodei) Anthropic, which he leads, is well-known for its large language model, "Claude."

The AI risk he speaks of is, simply put, like a "double-edged sword of technology." Think of a well-honed **kitchen knife**. It is the best tool when cooking, but if held incorrectly, you can cut your hand, and if used with malicious intent, it can become a weapon.

Amodei has consistently warned about the potential for AI to be misused in areas such as **"cybersecurity threats"** or **"biochemical weapon manufacturing."** [Source 13](https://www.metatrader.com/en/news/beincrypto/4188537-anthropic-s-amodei-fires-another-warning-this-time-to) It is similar to hiring a smart assistant who, if they have hacking skills and bad intentions, could cause uncontrollable damage.

He says, "If we don't develop the technology at all, humanity will not enjoy the benefits of AI, or authoritarian forces might monopolize it. Conversely, rushing the development too quickly is reckless." [Source 14](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier) This means the key is finding a "safe speed" that is neither too slow nor too fast.

## Where We Stand

Fortunately, Amodei is not engaging in unconditional fear-mongering. He emphasizes, "If we walk the right path of development, the possibility of AI causing fatal harm to humanity is very low." [Source 10](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/could-ai-kill-humans-anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-responds-101789282796444.html)

However, the reason he does not let his guard down is out of a sense of responsibility. At a past UN Security Council meeting, he even used the expression, "If it is a problem on which the fate of humanity depends, no measure is ever too much." [Source 11](https://news.google.com/stories/CAAqNggKIjBDQklTSGpvSmMzUnZjbmt0TXpZd1NoRUtEd2pscXBIM0VCRUctR2g3bjVvWXFDZ0FQAQ?hl=en-KE&gl=KE&ceid=KE:en)

SNL’s comedy highlights the gap between the CEO who bears this "heavy burden" and how he sometimes appears flustered or embarrassed in front of the public. [Source 6](https://www.businessinsider.com/snl-skit-mocks-dario-amodei-ai-doomsday-predictions-2026-9)

## What's Next

AI will continue to develop without stopping. What we should pay attention to is how transparently companies share risks and how they establish safety measures. We must all become watchdogs to ensure that the heavy messages from CEOs—"the future of humanity is at stake"—do not just become empty words decorating the media, but lead to actual safety regulations and technical control devices.

## MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Perspective

SNL's satire provides food for thought on how the AI industry communicates with the public. It is time to consider whether raising fears by talking about the fate of humanity is truly a wise approach, or if we need a new language that allows the public to better feel and understand the risks of technology. True maturity in technology starts not from selling fear, but from building a solid fence so we can safely enjoy the benefits of technology.
