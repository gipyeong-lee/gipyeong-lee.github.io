# 'Good AI Hacker' Protecting Hospitals and Power Plants? Claude Mythos's Amazing Global Expansion

Let's imagine for a moment. You wake up as usual on a cold winter morning, only to find the entire city's power is out, trapping you in pitch darkness. The tap water is cut off so you can't wash, and when you turn on your smartphone to check the news, even the communication network is dead. What's even more terrifying is that the nearby large hospital's computer system is paralyzed, threatening the life support equipment of patients in the emergency room.

This is by no means an exaggerated scenario from a sci-fi movie. In a modern society where everything is tightly connected via the internet like a spider's web, this is a disaster that could become reality at any moment if a single line of a 'software flaw' is attacked by hackers. But what if there was a 'super shield' capable of finding and blocking microscopic holes at the speed of light within systems so vast that human eyes could never handle them?

Moving beyond the level of having fun conversations with people and writing poetry, Artificial Intelligence (AI) is now stepping up as a frontline defender protecting critical facilities that determine a nation's fate. Let me introduce the incredible changes being brought about by the unprecedented AI model, **'Claude Mythos'**, introduced by Anthropic, an American AI specialty company.

## Why It Matters

The power grids, water systems, hospital medical devices, and communication networks we take for granted every day are made up of incredibly massive and complex software codes. In professional terms, these facilities that form the backbone of national operations are called **'Critical Infrastructure'**.

If a general smartphone app is hacked, it might just end in personal information leaks or financial loss, but if a hole is breached in the software of a power plant or water facility, the situation is completely different. This is because it directly threatens human lives and can lead to disastrous consequences that shake the very foundation of national security [Anthropic Expands Claude Mythos To Secure Critical Infrastructure Across 15+ Countries](https://bitcoinworld.co.in/anthropic-claude-mythos-critical-infrastructure-expansion/).

For decades, defending these massive systems was strictly the job of human security experts. They have been manually analyzing millions of lines of code day and night. However, as hackers' attack methods have become so sophisticated that they now mobilize AI, it has become practically impossible to proactively block all newly emerging vulnerabilities with human power alone.

Amid this crisis, Anthropic has put forward its most powerful AI model developed to date, 'Claude Mythos Preview'. They have decided to significantly expand a program worldwide that utilizes this AI to thoroughly scan for critical security flaws within software, known as **'Project Glasswing'** [Anthropic scales Project Glasswing to 150 partners across 15...](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-scales-project-glasswing-to-150-partners-across-15-countries-to-hunt-critical-software-flaws/).

The reason this expansion is drawing particular attention is that its scale and targets are extraordinary. Previously, IT technology companies mainly participated, but the 150 newly joined organizations include a massive number of core operators in the power, water, medical, and communication sectors [Anthropic shares Mythos with 150 more organizations, including critical infrastructure operators | Cybersecurity Dive](https://www.cybersecuritydive.com/news/ai-anthropic-claude-mythos-project-glasswing-expand/821714/). In particular, organizations from over 15 countries worldwide, including **South Korea**, Japan, the UK, Canada, and India, will now fall under this AI protective net [Anthropic Expands Claude Mythos Access to Over 150 Organizations Across 15+ Countries to Defend Critical Infrastructure — BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/XnYViZ4BLfE1EzqPwKDA).

## The Explainer

Then, by what principle does Claude Mythos exactly accomplish this incredible feat? Instead of complex code talk, let's use an **analogy**.

Suppose there is a massive metropolis where 10 million people live. All buildings in this city have tens of millions of windows and doors. To prevent break-ins, the mayor orders that every building's locks be checked for sturdiness every night. No matter how excellent the police officers are, it is physically impossible to manually rattle tens of millions of doorknobs overnight. There are bound to be 'open windows' left somewhere that escape the police's attention.

Introducing Claude Mythos here is like simultaneously unleashing **'tens of millions of invisible patrol officers'** into the city who never get tired, never sleep, and boast perfect vision even in the dark. These patrol officers sweep past millions of buildings per second, pulling on every lock, and the moment they find even the slightest gap, they immediately send out a warning: "The lock in Zone A of the power plant control system is loose!"

Anthropic is the company that first introduced the 'Claude' series of AI chatbots we use daily in March 2023 [Claude (language model) - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_(language_model)). Having steadily evolved its ability to read documents and understand code, the secret weapon they created purely for 'security defense' is none other than Claude Mythos. Anthropic boasts that this model "possesses the most powerful performance of any we have introduced so far" [Anthropic scales Claude Mythos to critical infrastructure in 15+...](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/anthropic-scales-claude-mythos-to-critical-infrastructure-in-15-countries/).

What's particularly terrifying about this AI is its ability to find **'Zero-day vulnerabilities'** that even human developers didn't know about [Anthropic scales Claude Mythos to critical infrastructure in 15+...](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/technology/anthropic-scales-claude-mythos-to-critical-infrastructure-in-15-countries/ar-AA24EgnX). A 'zero-day' means there are 'zero days' to fix the flaw; it refers to the worst kind of vulnerability that nobody knew about until a hacker found the hole first and attacked. These covert holes, which would take dozens of experts months to barely discover, are completely scanned by Claude Mythos in just a few weeks.

## Where We Stand

While it's glad news that cutting-edge AI has been deployed to the front lines of security, both 'light' and 'shadow' coexist in the current situation.

First, the **'light'** represents the overwhelming achievements of Claude Mythos. According to reports from organizations participating in Project Glasswing, it has discovered over **10,000 'high-risk and critical' software flaws** since last April [Anthropic Mythos: Glasswing Adds 150 Orgs in 15+ Countries](https://www.abhs.in/blog/anthropic-claude-mythos-glasswing-150-orgs-15-countries-june-2026). This is equivalent to preemptively dismantling 10,000 large-scale digital bombs that millions of citizens could have suffered from. Not only large corporations but also parts suppliers and open-source management groups have joined this defensive net, making the line of defense extremely tight [Anthropic shares Mythos with 150 more organizations, including critical infrastructure operators | Cybersecurity Dive](https://www.cybersecuritydive.com/news/ai-anthropic-claude-mythos-project-glasswing-expand/821714/).

However, the **'shadow'** appears after 'discovering' the flaws. Just because a hospital's MRI accurately pinpoints the location of cancer cells doesn't mean the disease is immediately cured. A delicate surgical process to actually remove the tumor is required.

The same goes for software. While finding holes has become possible at the speed of light thanks to AI, the actual **'Patch'** process of safely fixing those holes remains difficult and slow [Anthropic adds 150 Glasswing cyber partners](https://www.implicator.ai/anthropic-adds-150-new-project-glasswing-partners-in-more-than-15-countries/). They've identified where the locks are broken, but there's a shortage of skilled locksmiths (human developers) to actually repair them. In particular, because power plant and hospital systems cannot be stopped for even a single second, simply shutting down the system temporarily to modify code requires tremendous resolve. The fact that the 'diagnosis' is done by AI but the 'treatment' still remains the responsibility of humans is a dilemma for the security industry.

Despite these challenges, the global trend of cybersecurity will be reorganized around powerful AI like Claude Mythos.

Anthropic announced that it has expanded its partnerships to about 150 new organizations in close cooperation with governments and the security industry [Anthropic shares Mythos with 150 more organizations, including critical infrastructure operators | Cybersecurity Dive](https://www.cybersecuritydive.com/news/ai-anthropic-claude-mythos-project-glasswing-expand/821714/). This means that adopting AI to protect national infrastructure is no longer an option, but an **'essential condition for survival'** [Expanding Project Glasswing | Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-project-glasswing).

Furthermore, this technological leap holds great economic significance as well. The success in building a massive defensive network spanning 15 countries worldwide is causing Anthropic's value to skyrocket [Anthropic Expands Project Glasswing And Claude Mythos Access...](https://creati.ai/ai-news/2026-06-03/anthropic-expands-project-glasswing-and-claude-mythos-access-worldwide/). A company that used to make eloquent chatbots is now being reborn as a security giant responsible for humanity's safety.
