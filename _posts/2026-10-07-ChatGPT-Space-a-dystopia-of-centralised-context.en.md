Imagine this: You arrive at the office in the morning and turn on your computer. Previously, you had to check emails, share files with team members via messengers, and organize project progress in separate note apps. But now, you close all those complicated windows. You only need to open one space: 'ChatGPT Space.'

Announced by OpenAI at the DevDay event held in San Francisco on September 29, 2026, **ChatGPT Space** has evolved beyond an environment for chatting with bots into an integrated work hub where humans and AI agents (intelligent programs that perform goals on behalf of users) create documents and manage projects in real-time [[Source: OpenAI launches ChatGPT Space for collaborative AI document creation](https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-chatgpt-space-collaborative-ai/), [Source: Huge AI News Week: ChatGPT Dots, Gemini 4 Argon... - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MS5VMqyuwjU)].

### Why is this important?

Until now, AI has primarily been a 'source of answers' for us. However, in a professional setting, 'context' (the flow of work or background information)—such as file sharing, history management, and coordination among team members—is far more important than just answers. ChatGPT Space is a signal that OpenAI is stepping beyond simply being a chatbot developer and going head-to-head as a **Productivity Platform** that dominates your daily life [[Source: ChatGPT Space Is a Shared Hub for Work Projects and ... - Ground News](https://ground.news/article/chatgpt-space-is-a-shared-hub-for-work-projects-and-personal-ai-agents)].

The fact that team-based collaboration is possible is particularly noteworthy. AI that was previously used only individually can now be used with colleagues and AI agents deployed within the team [[Source: OpenAI Unveils ChatGPT Space, GPT-6.1 Sol And $500 Pro Plan For... - BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/bt-tv/whats-hot/video/openai-unveils-chatgpt-space-gpt-6-1-sol-and-500-pro-plan-for-ai-powered-work-558740-2026-09-30)]. It's as if you've hired an additional 'genius assistant' who remembers everything and can write documents instantly for the team. An environment where work efficiency can rise dramatically has been created.

### Easy to understand: ChatGPT as a 'Co-working Studio'

To understand ChatGPT Space easily, think of it as a **'smart digital co-working studio.'**

1. **Central Hub**: Your documents, files, and new AI agents called 'Dots' are gathered in this one space. It’s like having all your materials neatly organized on your office desk [[Source: ChatGPT Space Explained: OpenAI’s New AI Workflow Hub](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/chatgpt/chatgpt-space-explained-a-new-way-to-organize-ai-workflows)].
2. **Co-created Documents (Pages)**: Much like Google Docs or Notion, humans and AI can simultaneously edit and develop content within a single document [[Source: ChatGPT Space launches with shared Pages and AI collaboration](https://www.androidauthority.com/chatgpt-space-shared-ai-workspace-3717305/)].
3. **Permissions Management**: It is safe because you can finely divide viewing and editing permissions to ensure only your team members can enter [[Source: ChatGPT Space is Here: Create Pages, Share Files & Work with AI](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/news/chatgpt-space-is-here-create-pages-share-files-work-with-ai)].

Simply put, whereas previously you would copy the answer obtained from asking ChatGPT, move it to another document program, and edit it again, now the document is completed right inside ChatGPT, and team members can refine it together in real-time [[Source: ChatGPT Space Overview｜npaka - note](https://note.com/npaka/n/nda332c9113a4?hl=ko)].

### Where are we now?

Currently, ChatGPT Space has taken its first steps as a productivity platform, supporting file organization, Page creation, and maintaining shared project context [[Source: ChatGPT Space is Here: Create Pages, Share Files & Work with AI](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/news/chatgpt-space-is-here-create-pages-share-files-work-with-ai)]. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman explained that this will make various tasks, such as research, coding, and data analysis, smoother through collaboration between team members and AI [[Source: OpenAI Unveils ChatGPT Space, GPT-6.1 Sol And $500 Pro Plan For... - BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/bt-tv/whats-hot/video/openai-unveils-chatgpt-space-gpt-6-1-sol-and-500-pro-plan-for-ai-powered-work-558740-2026-09-30)].

Of course, like two sides of a coin, there are limits. Concentrating all necessary context for work in one place also means there is a risk of becoming 'locked-in' to that platform. How much to trust and entrust our company's valuable data, and how flexibly security and access control will work in actual team settings, are tasks that users must experience and resolve for themselves [[Source: OpenAI adds a shared ChatGPT workspace and an AI meeting... - Runtimewire](https://runtimewire.com/article/openai-chatgpt-space-meetings-devday-2026)].

### What will happen next?

It appears OpenAI will not stop here. According to current announcements, more advanced document work tools such as Slides or Sheets are scheduled to be added to Space in the future [[Source: ChatGPT Space launches with shared Pages and AI collaboration](https://www.androidauthority.com/chatgpt-space-shared-ai-workspace-3717305/)].

This shows an ambition to evolve not just into a chatbot, but into an 'Operating System (OS)' that manages a company's entire work flow. An era is approaching where AI is no longer just helping with 'parts' of our work, but becoming our collaboration environment itself. Are you ready to entrust the core of your work to this smart and centralized studio?
