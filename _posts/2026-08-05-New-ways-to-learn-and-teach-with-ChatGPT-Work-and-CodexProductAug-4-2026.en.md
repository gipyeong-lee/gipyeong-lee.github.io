Imagine this: You arrive at the office in the morning and tell the AI, "Find the meeting materials I need to process today from Google Drive, summarize the relevant points and send them to me via Slack, and write a draft of the final report." In the past, AI would likely have responded by suggesting methods, saying, "I'll show you how to write a report." But now, an era is arriving where AI directly opens files, summarizes content, and drafts reports to place them on your desk.

OpenAI recently unveiled its latest model, 'GPT-5.6,' while introducing the 'ChatGPT Work' mode utilizing this model ([Source: The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-pairs-its-gpt-5-6-public-rollout-with-chatgpt-work-a-new-agent-that-handles-entire-workflows/)). AI is evolving beyond a search engine that simply answers questions into a 'digital assistant' that works alongside us ([Source: OpenAI Newsroom](https://openai.com/newsroom/); [Source: Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/rayravaglia/2026/08/04/openai-education-plugins-move-ai-from-answers-to-workflows/)).

## Why is this important?

Until now, the AI we used was mostly a 'knowledge transmitter.' If you asked something, it gave an answer; if you asked it to write something, it drafted an outline. However, ChatGPT Work is an 'Agent' (a program that makes decisions and performs tasks autonomously). This brings tremendous changes to educational settings and workplaces.

Teachers, researchers, and office workers can now be freed from tedious tasks like file organization, data aggregation, and repetitive report writing ([Source: OpenAI Education Newsletter](https://edunewsletter.openai.com/p/introducing-chatgpt-work-a-more-capable)). This is because AI navigates approved files, connected apps, and the web to handle complex, multi-step workflows to completion on its own ([Source: OpenAI Education Newsletter](https://edunewsletter.openai.com/p/introducing-chatgpt-work-a-more-capable)). Beyond mere time savings, this allows humans to focus exclusively on making more creative and important decisions.

## Easy to understand

Shall we compare ChatGPT Work with the existing Chat mode? It's easy to understand with this analogy:

* **Existing Chat mode**: Like an encyclopedia or a personal tutor. It is very suitable for 'brainstorming,' where it kindly explains things if you ask questions, drafts simple outlines, or generates ideas ([Source: Tistory](https://scv1218.tistory.com/216)).
* **ChatGPT Work mode**: Like an experienced office assistant. Beyond simply providing knowledge, if given a concrete goal, it performs multiple steps of work to produce a reviewable result (deliverable) ([Source: Tistory](https://scv1218.tistory.com/216)).

In short, if Chat is the realm of 'knowledge,' Work is the realm of 'practice.' Because ChatGPT Work runs on a cloud-based virtual machine (a virtual environment that operates like a real computer), the AI can silently handle tasks in the background even while the user is away from their desk ([Source: iXBT](https://www.ixbt.com/news/2026/07/14/openai-chatgpt-chatgpt-work)).

Furthermore, with this update, the 'Codex' feature (an AI model that converts natural language into programming code), which previously existed as a separate app, has been integrated into the ChatGPT desktop app ([Source: NodeMini](https://nodemini.com/en/blog/2026-chatgpt-work-codex-merged-chatgpt-2026.html); [Source: Coursiv](https://coursiv.io/blog/codex-merged-with-chatgpt-app)). Thanks to this, you can now freely switch between three modes—**Chat, Work, and Codex (Development)**—within a single desktop app ([Source: NodeMini](https://nodemini.com/en/blog/2026-chatgpt-work-codex-merged-chatgpt-2026.html)).

## Where We Stand

The current ChatGPT desktop app is highly versatile, integrated with over 1,400 plugins ([Source: NodeMini](https://nodemini.com/en/blog/2026-chatgpt-work-codex-merged-chatgpt-2026.html)). New educational plugins for K-12 teachers, university faculty, and students have also been released, supporting learning and research more efficiently ([Source: OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/learn-teach-chatgpt-work-codex/)).

However, not all users can enjoy the same features. Access to advanced features varies depending on subscription plans such as Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise, so you need to check your own subscription environment ([Source: 4PDA](https://4pda.to/2026/07/10/458691/openai_vypustila_gpt_5_6_i_predstavila_rezhim_agenta_chatgpt_work/)).

## What's Next

In the future, the way we converse with AI will change from "How do I do this?" to "You handle this entire task." Especially for non-developers, workspace-based work efficiency will be maximized by utilizing Codex features to organize files in folders in bulk or analyze complex projects ([Source: Tistory](https://scv1218.tistory.com/216)).

Moving forward, AI will grow from a smarter assistant into a colleague who thoroughly understands the workflows of our tasks. Pick the most repetitive and time-consuming task you have right now. Before long, that task will become AI's job.

## AI's Take (MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Perspective)

It is very impressive that technological advancement has moved beyond simply providing 'smart answers' to the stage of assisting human 'action.' Just as we learned how to use new tools in the past, 'collaboration ability'—effectively working with an AI colleague—will become an essential skill for modern people.
