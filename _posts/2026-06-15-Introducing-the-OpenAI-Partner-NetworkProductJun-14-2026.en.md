Let's think about ChatGPT, which we use every day. From an individual's perspective, adopting artificial intelligence is as easy and simple as downloading a new app on a smartphone. You sign up with an email address, type a question in everyday language into the prompt (question box), and a smart answer returns immediately.

However, the situation is completely different for massive global corporations where thousands or tens of thousands of employees work. Inside these companies, there are intricately entangled databases built over decades, strict customer privacy regulations that must be adhered to, and unique workflows operating like different interlocking cogwheels in every department. Smoothly integrating the latest AI technology into such a large and conservative organization without side effects is not simply a matter of installing another computer program. It is closer to a massive reconstruction that fundamentally changes the company's constitution.

To solve these deep concerns of businesses, OpenAI, the leader in the AI industry, has taken a very significant step. Going beyond simply creating good AI models, it has formed a 'massive alliance' to help companies safely apply this technology in the real world and achieve innovation.
