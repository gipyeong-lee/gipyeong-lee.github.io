Imagine this: You turn on your computer in the morning, and instead of spending your entire morning opening complex report templates, moving data from last week one by one, and adjusting designs, you simply tell the AI, "Create this week's report using last week's sales data."

How does this single sentence make it possible to automate tasks you previously did manually? It's thanks to the **"Just Ask Claude"** approach, which is gaining recognition among recent AI users.

## Why is this important?

In the past, to use AI, you had to learn complex conversational techniques called "prompt engineering." But now, AI has reached a stage where it hides technical complexity, allowing users to instruct tasks in everyday language while the AI handles the planning and execution. [Source 2](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-personal-guidance)

This shift means that even ordinary people without specialized knowledge can use AI as their own "digital assistant." Beyond just being a chatbot that answers questions, it has become possible to have "work sessions" where the AI modifies files, analyzes data, and formats reports. [Source 8](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/natalia-poda-04a07b1b3_claude-is-the-best-tool-i-have-ever-used-activity-7465458052968869888-mpVD)

## Understanding it easily: The magic tool called "Skill"

The key to mastering Claude like an expert is understanding the concept of **"Skills."**

To use an easy analogy, if general AI use is "asking for a recipe every time," creating a skill is like "making your own custom recipe book." [Source 6](https://anthropic.skilljar.com/introduction-to-claude-cowork) For example, if you teach Claude the design template for a document you create frequently once, from then on, the work is completed simply by saying, "Create a document in my brand style." [Source 13](https://decantery.com/claude-is-now-watermarking-its-writing.heres-what-that-actually-means/)

What are the benefits of building skills?
- **Automation:** No need to repeat the same tasks every time. [Source 6](https://anthropic.skilljar.com/introduction-to-claude-cowork)
- **Consistency:** The AI always produces results according to defined rules. [Source 13](https://decantery.com/claude-is-now-watermarking-its-writing.heres-what-that-actually-means/)
- **Time Saving:** No need to explain complex instructions repeatedly; just call it by name. [Source 17](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/muskaan-aulakh-8200b3213_there-is-a-claude-code-shortcut-that-most-activity-7447335126172266498-BQKo)

How easy is this process? Surprisingly, with Claude Code, you can finish by just explaining the command name and the task you want to perform in plain, everyday sentences. [Source 9](https://writemask.com/blog/is-claude-good-at-humanizing-text)

## Current situation: What can it do?

Many people are already revolutionizing the way they work by utilizing Claude.

- **Work Automation:** Cases are increasing where people use skills to resolve discrepancies between databases or automate routine report writing. [Source 14](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/muskaan-aulakh-8200b3213_there-is-a-claude-code-shortcut-that-most-activity-7447335126172266498-BQKo), [Source 13](https://decantery.com/claude-is-now-watermarking-its-writing.heres-what-that-actually-means/)
- **Cowork:** Using the "Cowork" function allows Claude to directly read and edit files and folders on the user's computer, working together in a way where the user guides the direction from the side. [Source 8](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/natalia-poda-04a07b1b3_claude-is-the-best-tool-i-have-ever-used-activity-7465458052968869888-mpVD)
- **Error Recovery:** There is no need to worry even if an error occurs during work. Claude Code provides a feature that reverts to the state of the conversation just before the error occurred by simply pressing the Esc key twice. [Source 14](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/muskaan-aulakh-8200b3213_there-is-a-claude-code-shortcut-that-most-activity-7447335126172266498-BQKo)

Of course, there are points to be careful about. While AI provides plausible answers, in fields where precision is life—such as underwriting—even a small mistake in numbers can lead to big problems. [Source 10](https://www.sefindustries.com/posts/just-ask-claude-is-not-a-strategy) Also, keep in mind that if you ask the AI to "write like a person," it may retain its unique AI-like tone, making it detectable by AI detectors. [Source 11](https://aiblewmymind.substack.com/p/how-to-create-claude-brand-skill) Additionally, since August 11, 2026, invisible watermarks have been inserted into text generated by Claude to distinguish whether it was written by AI. [Source 13](https://decantery.com/claude-is-now-watermarking-its-writing.heres-what-that-actually-means/)

## What lies ahead?

In the future, we will move from an era of explaining at length "how" to do something to an era of instructing the results of "what" you want to do.

Claude usage is becoming so advanced that dozens of high-level tips are already being shared. [Source 5](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/my-5-favorite-claude-skills-alberto-moedano-kttbc) Instead of worrying about technical barriers, you should start by lightly asking, "Can the AI do this task for me?" One small skill might just help you leave work an hour earlier. [Source 17](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/muskaan-aulakh-8200b3213_there-is-a-claude-code-shortcut-that-most-activity-7447335126172266498-BQKo)
