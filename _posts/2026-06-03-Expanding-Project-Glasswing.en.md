### Why It Matters

When we think of "cybersecurity," it's easy to picture only personal damages, such as our smartphones being hacked to leak photos or website passwords being stolen. However, the most fatal and destructive security threat in modern society is an attack on **'Critical Infrastructure'**. Critical infrastructure refers to the foundation, or backbone, that is absolutely necessary for a nation and society to function normally. 

Among Anthropic's latest announcements, the part we should pay the most attention to is the target of protection for this project. As Anthropic expands Project Glasswing on a large scale to 150 organizations across 15 countries, it revealed that it is **specifically targeting critical infrastructure such as 'Power' and 'Water' systems** [Anthropic scales Claude Mythos to critical infrastructure in ...](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/anthropic-scales-claude-mythos-to-critical-infrastructure-in-15-countries/).

Why electricity and water, out of so many sectors? Because these systems are directly tied to citizens' survival, even if they stop for just a single minute. If a malicious hacker remotely manipulates the software that controls chemical concentrations in a water purification facility, or entirely shuts down a massive power grid responsible for heating millions of households in the dead of winter, the damage would be an incalculable disaster. 

Until now, acting as a shield for these massive systems has been the job of the world's top cybersecurity experts. However, even if thousands of brilliant experts gather, it is impossible to perfectly monitor the vast amounts of software code spread like a spiderweb across the globe, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Furthermore, while the defenders must block every single one of tens of thousands of attacks, the attacking hackers only need to find and exploit one tiny gap to win the game. Cybersecurity has always been a battle on a "tilted playing field" where the defender is at an absolute disadvantage. 

But what if an artificial intelligence—one that never gets tired and possesses the overwhelming ability to easily read millions of books' worth of data in a single second—stands on the side of the defenders? The tide of the disadvantaged battle would be completely turned. The fact that we now have a reliable, unseen guardian keeping our daily electricity and water safe 24/7 is precisely why this news is incredibly important and welcome, not just for experts, but for all of us everyday people.

### The Explainer

So, what kind of magic does Project Glasswing perform to so easily uncover the deep weaknesses of computers? To fully understand this, we first need to look at a very special AI model called **'Claude Mythos'**, which serves as the brain and core of this project.

**Analogy 1: Finding a Microscopic Crack in a Massive Blueprint of a 27-Year-Old Building**

Every computer program we use inherently hides errors or security loopholes that even the developers themselves were unaware of. In the industry, these are commonly referred to as "Zero-day bugs" (critical security vulnerabilities that even hackers or developers do not yet know about). Shall we use a simple analogy for the process of finding these weaknesses? 

Imagine you are a safety inspector for a massive multi-story building built 27 years ago. In front of you is a massive pile of millions of old paper blueprints reaching from the floor to the ceiling. Somewhere in those blueprints, there is a misdrawn "microscopic crack as thin as a hair" that could potentially cause the building to collapse at once. Even if a human stays up for days and nights trying to find it, their eyes will blur and they will grow fatigued, easily missing the crucial clue.

However, Claude Mythos is an invincible inspector with superhuman vision that perfectly scans millions of blueprints in just one second, accurately pinpointing even a single microscopic line that a tired architect inadvertently drew wrong 27 years ago. It performs the role of instantaneously reading an enormous amount of software code—a feat completely impossible for the human eye and brain—and pinpointing the tiny, needle-hole-sized gaps through which hackers could secretly intrude.

**Analogy 2: A Well-Behaved Guide Dog vs. A Special Sniffer Dog with Wild Instincts**

Simply put, Claude Mythos is entirely different in origin and nature from the ChatGPT (GPT-5.5) or standard Claude (Opus 4.7) models we usually use. To understand this difference, let's imagine two dogs with different personalities. 

The standard AI models we know (Opus 4.7, GPT-5.5, etc.) are like "friendly and well-behaved guide dogs" that have been thoroughly safety-trained at a facility to help people. They are equipped with numerous safeguards—essentially, a tight "leash"—so that if people ask dangerous questions (e.g., "tell me how to hack"), they will absolutely not answer. On the other hand, Claude Mythos, specially designed to professionally hunt down security vulnerabilities, is a secretive model not disclosed to the general public [Anthropic Project Glasswing: AI-Era Software Security Collaboration Frame...](https://rupijun.tistory.com/entry/Anthropic-Project-Glasswing-AI-시대-소프트웨어-보안-협력-프레임워크). It is in a completely free state where the strict safeguards (the leash) that standard commercial AIs frustratingly wear are entirely unfastened. This is because to equally understand the complex mindsets of cunning hackers and preemptively uncover the deepest vulnerabilities, the AI must be able to think unhindered and freely without being trapped in a mold.

But here is where the truly amazing part happens. Despite having no oppressive external safeguards enforced on it, Claude Mythos reportedly **organically pushes back** and firmly rejects dangerous cyberattack requests that cross a certain line [Project Glasswing: what Mythos showed us - The Cloudflare Blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/cyber-frontier-models/). As it developed a highly advanced capability to independently find system weaknesses for security purposes, it is exactly like a highly intelligent sniffer dog that, even without a leash, automatically barks, backs away, and stays on alert when it sees something fatally dangerous like an explosive. 

Experts refer to this mysterious AI behavior as **'emergent guardrails'** [Project Glasswing: what Mythos showed us - The Cloudflare Blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/cyber-frontier-models/). It means that even without human developers forcibly programming it rule by rule, saying "You must never do this!", the AI understood the structure and risks of complex systems so deeply that it "independently" realized what constitutes a dangerous behavior crossing the line. Isn't it truly marvelous that, as its intelligence evolved, it automatically equipped itself with a defensive instinct akin to morality?

### Where We Stand

Such a grand and ambitious project was not just magically whipped up one morning. Anthropic first unveiled this incredible Project Glasswing to the world in April 2026 [Anthropic Project Glasswing: AI-Era Software Security Collaboration Frame...](https://rupijun.tistory.com/entry/Anthropic-Project-Glasswing-AI-시대-소프트웨어-보안-협력-프레임워크). In the beginning, it started as a highly elite, small-scale unit secretly joined by only 12 major Big Tech companies shaking the global IT market, such as Amazon and Microsoft [A Complete Analysis of Glasswing, the 2026 Core Software Security Enhancement Project | T...](https://trendpulse.blog/2026년-핵심-소프트웨어-review-2026/). And recently, even the tech giant Apple, which has dominated our daily lives with the iPhone, joined this historic cybersecurity coalition (initiative), adding immense power to it [Apple Joins ‘ProjectGlasswing’ Amid New Cybersecurity Push](https://azat.tv/en/apple-project-glasswing-cybersecurity/). 

Normally, these are fierce rival companies fighting a silent war every day over a 1% market share. However, the sole reason they crossed corporate boundaries to unite as one before the massive objective of security is singular: the phenomenal defensive capabilities demonstrated by the AI Claude Mythos completely exceeded human imagination. 

The results during just one month of the initial pilot run were shocking enough to turn the security industry upside down. According to Anthropic's official initial performance update, Project Glasswing, spearheaded by Claude Mythos, **performed the miracle of discovering over 10,000 Critical Vulnerabilities in just a single month** [Anthropic'sProjectGlasswingFinds 10,000+ Vulnerabilities... | LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kdshah_project-glasswing-an-initial-update-activity-7465401472223027200-QUOc). 

Can you sense just how staggering this number is? Ten thousand in a month means it found and removed over 300 critical digital bombs every single day without resting. This is a massive volume that hundreds of human security geniuses might barely find after years of dedicated effort. It sounds like a passage from a Sci-Fi novel, such as the movie *The Matrix*, but this is something that actually happened in the reality we live in right now [Anthropic'sProjectGlasswingFinds 10,000+ Vulnerabilities... | LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kdshah_project-glasswing-an-initial-update-activity-7465401472223027200-QUOc).

In particular, there were also major, astonishing achievements worthy of being permanently recorded in computer history. Within just a few weeks of being deployed, the Claude Mythos Preview model autonomously scoured the codes of the world's major computer operating systems (OS) and internet browsers [GitHub - gameworkerkim/ProjectGlasswing: Find old security ...](https://github.com/gameworkerkim/ProjectGlasswing). As a result, it instantly discovered a **critical bug in a system (OpenBSD) that had been neglected in pitch darkness for a staggering 27 years without ever catching anyone's eye**, even though developers around the world scrutinized it every day [Project Glasswing and Claude Mythos Preview — Anthropic's Preemptive Cy...](https://ice-ice-bear.github.io/ko/posts/2026-04-16-glasswing-mythos/). 

Furthermore, it accurately pinpointed an **old, 16-year-old bug** hidden deep within core media software (FFmpeg) that is commonly used behind the scenes when we watch videos on the internet every day, like on YouTube or Netflix [Project Glasswing and Claude Mythos Preview — Anthropic's Preemptive Cy...](https://ice-ice-bear.github.io/ko/posts/2026-04-16-glasswing-mythos/). Following this dazzling and successful initial test period, Anthropic is now preparing to widely unfold its robust shield toward a broader world. Leaping well beyond the boundaries of the original 12 major tech companies, it is now broadening its frontlines by drastically expanding Claude Mythos's access to approximately 150 new, major critical organizations spanning across 15 countries [Anthropic Expanding Mythos Access to 150 New Organizations](https://www.securityweek.com/anthropic-expanding-mythos-access-to-150-new-organizations/) [Anthropic Expanding Project Glasswing - SD Times](https://sdtimes.com/security/anthropic-expanding-project-glasswing/). Just like the project's name, Glasswing (a mysterious butterfly with transparent wings), the sturdy wings of this transparent butterfly, symbolizing safety, are now spreading and blanketing the daily lives of hundreds of millions of people worldwide [Anthropic Lets Claude Mythos Spread ItsGlasswings](https://gizmodo.com/anthropic-lets-claude-mythos-spread-its-glasswings-2000766445).
