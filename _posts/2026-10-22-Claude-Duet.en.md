Imagine this: You wake up this morning, and your mind is clouded with an overflow of emails and tasks to handle. But what if an AI assistant had already organized your meeting materials and prepared the necessary documents for your clients while you were asleep last night? This is the story of 'Claude Duet,' an AI that handles your work just like a compatible best friend.

### Why does this matter?

Modern professionals and small business owners are incredibly busy. There is no end to the work that needs to be done, yet there is a lack of time to focus on what really matters. Claude Duet was designed exactly for these people. [Source: Introducing Claude Duet | Claude by Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet)

Moving beyond simply answering questions, an era has opened where AI enters your workflow directly to collaborate with you. The core goal of this technology is to maximize work efficiency and return our precious asset of 'time' to us.

### Understanding it easily

'Claude Duet' has two main faces.

The first is its appearance as your **'personal AI assistant.'** Simply put, imagine having a smart intern on standby right next to you 24/7. This assistant does not just do what it is told; it identifies on its own what requires your attention. [Source: ClaudeDuet | Your personal AI assistant](https://claude.com/product/claude-duet)

For instance, even if you step away while working, the assistant does not stop and continues to perform its tasks. It only calls you when it reaches a crucial moment that truly requires your final approval or confirmation, saying, "Please check this now." [Source: ClaudeDuet | Your personal AI assistant](https://claude.com/product/claude-duet) It is similar to a diligent assistant who has perfectly organized documents and brings them to you just to sign off.

The second is its appearance as a **'digital pair programming partner'** for developers. Usually, when developing, you work while looking at one person's screen, but with Claude Duet, when one host opens a session, other developers can connect in real-time and share the AI's responses. [Source: Introducing Claude Duet for Real-Time Pair Programming | LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/elirangeffen_opensource-claudecode-ai-activity-7437542418394017793-_W9k) Two people can watch the AI's answers via real-time streaming and modify code or share thoughts as if they were one team.

### Current status

You can currently encounter Claude Duet in the following ways:

*   **Assistant Features:** You can use it as an app on iOS and Android smartphones, and it is also accessible via the website (claude.ai). [Source: Introducing Claude Duet | Claude by Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet)
*   **Tool Integration:** It exerts more powerful capabilities by connecting with hundreds of tools we use in daily life, such as GitHub (code management tool), Google Sheets (document tool), and Slack (messenger tool). [Source: Claude Code Pricing 2026: Pro $20, Max $100 & $200 + API](https://duet.so/blog/claude-code-pricing)
*   **Developer Tools:** Developers can share Claude Code sessions in real-time through an open-source CLI (Command Line Interface) tool. [Source: claude-duet- CLI Tools on GitHub | SkillsLLM](https://skillsllm.com/skill/claude-duet)

### What will happen in the future?

Claude Duet seems set to fundamentally change how humans work, beyond simple features. As the AI's ability to proactively find and handle tasks becomes more sophisticated, we will be able to focus on more creative and strategic decisions. In the future, collaborating with AI will not be an unfamiliar technical application, but a very natural part of daily life, just like exchanging emails with a colleague.
