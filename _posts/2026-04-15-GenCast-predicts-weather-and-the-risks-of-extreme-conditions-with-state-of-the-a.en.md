2. **Smart Energy Planning**: Renewable energy sources like solar and wind power fluctuate in production based on the weather's mood. ## Easy Understanding: 50 Meteorologists in Your Pocket!

The weather forecasts we've encountered so far have primarily used 'deterministic models'—a method that produces a single result from a single set of data [GenCast predicts weather and the risks of extreme conditions with state ...](https://deepmind.google/blog/gencast-predicts-weather-and-the-risks-of-extreme-conditions-with-sota-accuracy/). To put it simply, it’s a way of plugging current weather data into complex mathematical formulas to come up with one single answer: "It will rain tomorrow."

However, the atmosphere is so fickle that even the slightest orbital change can completely alter the outcome. **As an analogy**, it's like shooting a ball in a pinball machine; even a tiny adjustment in force can completely change the direction the ball bounces. It would be very difficult to guess where the ball will land based on just one prediction.

On the other hand, GenCast has adopted a **probabilistic forecasting** model—a method that calculates various possibilities as numerical values [Probabilistic weather forecasting with machine learning - Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08252-9). GenCast doesn't insist, "This is the one right answer!"

**Here is a fun analogy.**
If we really want to find a delicious restaurant, wouldn't it be much more accurate to ask 50 foodie friends rather than trusting just one person? If 45 out of 50 say, "That place is really good!" we can go to that restaurant with much more confidence.

In the same way, GenCast simultaneously generates **more than 50 different weather scenarios (ensembles)**—a technique that synthesizes multiple predictions [GenCast predicts weather and the risks of extreme conditions with state ...](https://deepmind.google/blog/gencast-predicts-weather-and-the-risks-of-extreme-conditions-with-sota-accuracy/).
- "In scenario A it rains, in scenario B it's just cloudy."
- "Since rain is expected in 40 out of 50 scenarios, the probability of rain on the day of the event is 80%."

By calculating uncertainty in advance and utilizing a 'diffusion-based model'—a technology that creates precise data by removing noise—it provides much more reliable information even in situations where prediction is extremely difficult, like extreme weather events [GenCast: Diffusion-based ensemble forecasting for medium-range weather](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.15796).

The emergence of GenCast shows that the paradigm of weather forecasting is shifting in earnest from 'complex physics engines' to 'data-driven AI.' The future we face will look like this:

- **Securing Safety from Weather Disasters**: Knowing the risk of typhoons or heavy rain two weeks in advance will allow for evacuation and recovery plans, dramatically reducing loss of life [Google's GenCast AI can predict extreme weather 15 days ahead](https://mspoweruser.com/gencast-googles-weather-forecasting-model-can-predict-extreme-weather-events-over-two-weeks-in-advance/).
- **Personalized Local Forecasts**: Instead of just 'weather in Seoul,' ultra-precise forecasting services that probabilistically calculate even minute weather changes in your specific neighborhood will become part of daily life [Inside Google's GenCast: Learn About AI in Weather Forecasting](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/gencast).
