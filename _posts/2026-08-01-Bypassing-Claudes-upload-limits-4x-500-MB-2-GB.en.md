## Why is this important?

While the role of AI in daily life is growing day by day, one of the biggest hurdles when utilizing it in practice is the 'size of data that can be input at once.' Many of you have likely experienced the frustration of seeing a "usage limit reached" or "file is too large" message while working on analysis tasks with Claude.

In fact, as of 2026, Claude traditionally maintains strict limits of 30MB per file and 20 files per conversation (chat) [Claude File Upload Limit: Size, Types & Workarounds](https://fast.io/resources/claude-file-upload-limit/). For users who want to deal with more complex and massive practical data beyond just uploading a simple memo, this limit was a major barrier. If this could be bypassed, we would be able to demand deeper data analysis and more accurate contextual understanding from Claude.

## Put simply

To use an analogy, the data Claude can read at once is like the 'size of a dining table.' Until now, Claude's table was small, so if you put one large plate on it, there was no room for anything else. That is why we had to pass information in small pieces.

The workaround shared recently has the effect of increasing the size of the table itself by four times (from 500MB to 2GB) [hckr news - Hacker News sorted by time](https://hckrnews.com/). Of course, technical limitations still exist.
