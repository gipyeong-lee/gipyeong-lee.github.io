Imagine this: you have spent decades completing a difficult puzzle and contributed significantly to the world. How would you feel if the team that completed that puzzle were suddenly disbanded? This is exactly what has recently happened at Google DeepMind, the most prestigious institution in artificial intelligence (AI). The 'AlphaFold' research team, which shocked the scientific community, is disappearing into the pages of history.

### Why does this matter?

Google DeepMind has demonstrated exceptional ability in solving specific scientific challenges. Notably, AlphaFold solved the 'protein structure prediction' problem, which scientists had failed to resolve for decades, even earning a Nobel Prize in Chemistry ([Source 4](https://news.aibase.com/news/29980)).

However, this disbandment and strategic adjustment demonstrate Google's strong will to pour all its capabilities into building 'general-purpose AI' and 'agentic AI' systems—AI that can intervene across our entire lives—rather than specialized AI systems ([Source 2](https://www.nowosci.ai/en/article/deepmind-disbands-alphafold-team-for-gemini)). It is a signal that we are rapidly entering an era where the AI we use daily evolves beyond mere question-answering machines into assistants that perform complex tasks.

### Understanding the Shift

To put it simply, if the AlphaFold team was a 'genius math tutor' who only gets full marks in 'a specific subject (protein structure),' the 'Gemini (Google’s next-generation general-purpose AI model)' that Google intends to create now is an 'all-around home tutor' who teaches all subjects well and even manages the student's schedule.

In the past, it was important to build specialized AI to solve specific problems ([Source 8](https://www.aichatdaily.com/ai-models/google-shifts-ai-science-strategy-toward-agentic-research)). Now, however, the core of technological advancement is for AI to go beyond solving problems and become an 'Agent' that judges and acts autonomously (AI that sets goals and performs tasks independently). To achieve this larger goal, Google decided to disband the previously successful team and reassign those personnel to develop new giant models like Gemini ([Source 7](https://nile1.com/en/2026/07/29/google-reassigns-nobel-winning-alphafold-team-to-gemini-and-isomorphic-labs/)).

### Current Situation

Over the past year, key researchers from AlphaFold have gradually moved to other projects. Notably, John Jumper, who led the AlphaFold team and co-received the Nobel Prize, left Google DeepMind in June 2026, after nine years, to join the competitor Anthropic ([Source 9](https://www.dexternights.com/2026/07/04/google-deepminds-leadership-shake-up-navigating-the-shifting-tides-of-ai-talent/), [Source 12](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/deepmind-loses-alphafold-nobel-laureate-and-two-co-authors)).

According to foreign media reports, the majority of the original AlphaFold paper authors have already been reassigned, and it has been confirmed that nearly one-quarter of the key researchers have left the company ([Source 6](https://finance.biggo.com/news/0fb0fe22-2f61-4db5-a898-09568120b234), [Source 11](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/google-deepmind-dismantles-nobel-winning-040005842.html?fr=sycsrp_catchall)). Currently, the personnel from the disbanded team are dispersed into fields including Genomics and nuclear fusion, taking on new challenges, including the Gemini project ([Source 1](https://aidirectory.com/news/google-deepmind-breaks-up-alphafold-team-research-shift), [Source 10](https://x.com/etnshow/status/2082360677566402802)).

### What lies ahead?

Google has now completely turned its rudder from the goal of 'scientific discovery' to the goal of 'hegemony in the AI ecosystem' ([Source 6](https://finance.biggo.com/news/0fb0fe22-2f61-4db5-a898-09568120b234)). You will increasingly see AI services released by Google become smarter and perform roles as 'agents,' such as writing your emails or creating complex travel plans, rather than simply providing information.

However, there are voices of concern regarding the disappearance of a research organization that left behind a monumental achievement in its field. As we explore the broader possibilities of AI, it is worth keeping an eye on whether specialized scientific breakthroughs like AlphaFold might be pushed to the sidelines.
