<p>
<span>
Anyone who wishes to understand what kind of person they are should ask themselves the following questions and answer them sincerely and firmly. What have you truly loved until now? What has led your soul toward a higher dimension? What has filled your heart and brought you joy? What have you been immersed in until now? When you answer these questions, your essence will become clear. That is who you are.
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span>
- Excerpt from "The Words of Nietzsche" -
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span>
<br>
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span>
<br>
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span>
1. What have you truly loved until now?
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span>
<span>
The one person who is not by my side now, but whom I will see again someday.
</span>
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span>
<br>
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span>
2. What has led your soul toward a higher dimension?
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span>
<span>
<span>
<span>
The one person who is not by my side now, but whom I will see again someday.
</span>
</span>
</span>
</span>
</p>
<p>

<span>
<br>
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span>
3. What has filled your heart and brought you joy?
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span>
<span>
<span>
The one person who is not by my side now, but whom I will see again someday.
</span>
</span>
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span>
<br>
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span>
4. What have you been immersed in until now?
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span>
A dream. The image of myself together with the one person who is not by my side now.
</span>
</p>
<div>
<span>
<br>
</span>
</div>
<div>
<span>
<br>
</span>
</div>
<div>
<span>
I can answer each question without hesitation. Even after thinking about it again and again.
</span>
</div>
<div>
<span>
Then, just as now.
</span>
</div>
<div>
<span>
I believe that someday, a day will come when I can provide different answers to these questions.
</span>
</div>
<div>
<span>
Because it was the first and last time I gave myself—a 'human'—a chance, I think the expectations and hope were that much greater.
</span>
</div>
<div>
<span>
And that is why the pain and despair were also that deep.
</span>
<br>
</div>
<p>
<span>
I do not know what kind of figure I will be painting for my future. But I will become stronger.
</span>
</p>

<p>
<b>
<span>
Let us become stronger.
</span>
</b>
