Imagine you are an employee in charge of insurance claims. In front of you is a stack of hundreds of pages of documents—medical certificates, opinions, and receipts—submitted by a customer, all jumbled together. It could take days just to read through these documents one by one to verify that the information is consistent or to check for missing details.

This situation is very common in modern business settings. Especially in fields like finance, insurance, and life sciences, where vast amounts of paperwork are handled, "fragmentation of information" is the biggest headache affecting operational efficiency. However, a technology has recently emerged that could bring a revolutionary change to this work environment: the 'Parsewise' API, an AI platform that understands the context of multiple documents on its own and converts them into verified data.

## Why is this important?

While the chatbots we use daily are excellent at answering simple questions, they have limitations when it comes to reading professional documents totaling thousands of pages and making important decisions based on them. Even if business operation teams wanted to use AI agents, they still had to rely on a lot of human labor due to 'traceability' issues—the need for humans to verify whether what the AI processed was truly accurate. [Source: Parsewise: Multi-document processing...](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/parsewise)

Parsewise was created to solve the inefficiency of this 'AI surveillance work.' It goes beyond simply reading text; it verifies that there are no contradictions between multiple documents and finds the root (lineage) of the extracted data, identifying exactly which document and which page it came from. [Source: Parsewise: API for agentic multi-document processing](https://www.producthunt.com/products/parsewise) For companies, this means reducing the manual labor of human cross-checking and enabling decision-making based on more reliable data.

## Understanding it simply

How should you think of Parsewise? It can be compared to a **'specialized analyst that automatically completes giant puzzles and finds errors.'**

A state where many documents are mixed together is like a pile of scattered puzzle pieces. If simple existing AI methods only described what was drawn on each piece, Parsewise arranges those pieces in their correct places and even reports errors, saying, "This piece doesn't fit the shape of this picture."

Furthermore, while general AI services often charge per document, Parsewise is designed for companies handling large-scale data, allowing for the processing of over 10,000 pages of documents in a single execution. [Source: Parsewise API - API for agentic multi-document processing...](https://www.productcool.com/product/parsewise-api) This is great news for technical teams that previously had to build complex pipelines by duct-taping numerous processing steps together. [Source: Parsewise: API for agentic multi-document processing](https://www.producthunt.com/products/parsewise)

## Current status

Parsewise is currently being used in areas requiring precision work, such as insurance document reception management and insurance claim portfolio risk assessment. [Source: Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/QWV-parsewise-api-for-agentic-multi-document-processing)

When a user submits multiple documents and the desired output format in a single API call, Parsewise returns a response that includes not only data extraction but also consistency checks, contradiction discovery, and even the locations (bounding boxes) of the resulting values. [Source: Parsewise: API for agentic multi-document processing](https://www.producthunt.com/products/parsewise) In other words, instead of developers writing complex parsing logic themselves, they can immediately apply verified data results to their own services.

## What will happen in the future?

It appears that companies will be able to expand AI agents into a wider range of activities in the future. This is because Parsewise guarantees 'reliability' and 'traceability,' which are the keys to complex document analysis. [Source: Launch Parsewise API for Multi-Document Processing](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gergely-csegzi_parsewise-api-launch-activity-7464986943156600832-dXkQ)

Once such technology becomes more commonplace, document review tasks that used to require professional personnel to spend days at a time will be finished in just a few minutes, allowing key company personnel to focus on higher-level strategy and customer service rather than simple document classification. [Source: Parsewise: AI-Driven Data Analysis & Extraction](https://huntscreens.com/products/parsewise)
