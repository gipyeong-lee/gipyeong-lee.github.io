Imagine how games we joyfully played with friends, such as *Assassin’s Creed* (an action-adventure game based on historical facts) or *Far Cry* (an open-world action game), are made. Behind those spectacular screens, there are not only engineers but also pioneers who have cultivated the field of the "gaming" industry for decades.

Recently, very sad news reached gamers around the world. Claude Guillemot, a titan of the gaming industry and co-founder of the global video game publisher Ubisoft (the French game company behind *Assassin’s Creed* and others), has passed away at the age of 69 [[Source: Ubisoft co-founder Claude Guillemot dies in plane crash](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/21/ubisoft-co-founder-claude-guillemot-dies-in-plane-crash/)] [[Source: Ubisoft co-founder Claude Guillemot dies in air crash at age 69](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/world/ubisoft-co-founder-claude-guillemot-dies-in-air-crash-at-age-69/ar-AA268jSl)].

## Why does this matter?

The passing of Claude Guillemot represents more than just the death of a businessman; it marks the turning of a page in the history of how the modern gaming industry was born and grew. Since founding Ubisoft with his brothers in 1986, he was a key figure who built the gaming industry from the ground up over the past 40 years [[Source: Ubisoft co-founder Claude Guillemot dies in plane crash in ...](https://www.foxbusiness.com/technology/ubisoft-co-founder-claude-guillemot-killed-plane-crash-claimed-2-lives)] [[Source: Claude Guillemot, Ubisoft Co-Founder, Has Died in a Plane ...](https://www.dualshockers.com/claude-guillemot-ubisoft-co-founder-has-died-in-a-plane-crash-at-69/)] [[Source: Ubisoft co-founder Claude Guillemot dies at 69 in La Baule ...](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/gaming/ubisoft-co-founder-claude-guillemot-dies-at-69-in-la-baule-plane-crash/articleshow/131880809.cms)].

Ubisoft has shaped today’s entertainment culture through countless blockbuster games that we all know well. The death of a company's founder poses an important challenge for us: how the "philosophy of gaming" that he upheld throughout his life will be passed on to the next generation, beyond just the management of the company.

## Understanding it simply

To use an analogy, think of Ubisoft as a massive "digital movie studio" that was founded from scratch by five brothers working together. They started in a very small space, but by gradually accumulating technology and creating unique stories, they grew it into a massive studio that people all over the world are passionate about.

However, on June 19, a tragic accident occurred near La Baule on the west coast of France [[Source: Ubisoft Co-Founder Claude Guillemot Dies In Plane Crash](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/ubisoft-co-founder-claude-guillemot-151757084.html)] [[Source: Ubisoft Co-Founder Claude Guillemot Dead In Plane Crash In France](https://www.forbes.com/sites/paultassi/2026/06/20/ubisoft-co-founder-claude-guillemot-dead-after-plane-crash-in-france/)]. The twin-engine *Cessna 421* aircraft he was piloting crashed [[Source: Ubisoft co-founder Claude Guillemot dead after plane crash](https://www.polygon.com/ubisoft-co-founder-claude-guillemot-died/)]. Sadly, the flight instructor who was on board with Claude Guillemot also lost their life in this tragic accident [[Source: Ubisoft Co-Founder Claude Guillemot Dies in Plane Crash ...](https://www.gameinformer.com/2026/06/22/ubisoft-co-founder-claude-guillemot-died-in-a-plane-crash-saturday)].

## Current situation

Ubisoft has released an official statement expressing deep sorrow regarding the accident [[Source: Ubisoft Co-Founder Claude Guillemot Dies in Plane Crash](https://www.newsweek.com/ubisoft-cofounder-claude-guillemot-dies-in-plane-crash-12098952)] [[Source: Ubisoft's co-founder Claude Guillemot dies in plane crash](https://www.reuters.com/world/ubisofts-co-founder-claude-guillemot-dies-plane-crash-2026-06-20/)]. In the statement, the company honored the dedication and contributions Claude Guillemot made as a co-founder of the Ubisoft Group and as chairman of the technology company founded by the brothers, *Guillemot Corporation* [[Source: Ubisoft co-founder Claude Guillemot dies in plane crash - MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/world/ubisoft-cofounder-claude-guillemot-dies-in-plane-crash/ar-AA267Chj)].

## What will happen next?

Although Ubisoft is in deep sorrow after losing one of its founders to this sudden passing, there is not expected to be any major immediate change in company operations or future game development direction. However, this accident reminds many once again that a great star of the gaming world, who defined an era, has set. Gamers are now watching to see how Ubisoft will carry on his legacy and how it will maintain and develop the "innovative gaming experiences" he emphasized so much.
