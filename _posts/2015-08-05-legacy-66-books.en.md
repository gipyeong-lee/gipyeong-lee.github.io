<p>
<span>
Since death is already fated, live cheerfully. Because it will end one day, face it with all your might. Because time is limited, the opportunity is always now. Leave the wailing to opera singers.
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span>
- Excerpt from Nietzsche's Words -
</span>
</p>

<p>
<span>
When you don't want to live, and you think you'd rather not live at all than live like this, we must know this: that death is the easiest path, and that the word 'suicide' is the most convenient refuge. Generally, to achieve a better environment and better experiences, one must choose the more difficult path. 'To live' is by no means an easy phrase. However, this applies equally to everyone. The only difference is what makes each person struggle. Therefore, we must avoid falling into self-pity and destroying ourselves.
</span>
</p>
<p>
<span>
<b>
Even if you don't want to die, you will die. If so, let us have the courage to live as 'myself' for the time granted to me.
</b>
</span>
</p>
