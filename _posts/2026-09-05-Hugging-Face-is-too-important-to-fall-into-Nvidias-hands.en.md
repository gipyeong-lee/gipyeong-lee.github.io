Imagine this. There is a massive "shared kitchen" where countless chefs from around the world share recipes, and anyone can freely take ingredients to create their own unique dishes. Then one day, the company that makes the world's most expensive ovens acquires the entire kitchen. While the company promised that "the kitchen will remain open to everyone," a sense of unease has begun to spread among the chefs using the kitchen: "Won't we have to use only their ovens from now on?"

What recently happened in the AI industry is exactly like this. The news has spread that Hugging Face, an indispensable "shared kitchen" for countless developers building AI models, is being acquired by Nvidia, the world's largest AI semiconductor company. [Source 12](https://www.techpowerup.com/352319/nvidia-to-buy-hugging-face-for-usd-12-9b-promises-to-keep-it-an-open-platform), [Source 17](https://www.techspot.com/news/113640-nvidia-closes-129-billion-hugging-face-acquisition-neutrality.html)

### Why is this important?

This is not just news about a large company buying a smaller one. Hugging Face is currently the backbone of the AI industry. It occupies the core of modern AI infrastructure where developers distribute artificial intelligence models, share data, and improve each other's technologies. [Source 1](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/03/hugging-face-is-too-important-to-fall-into-nvidias-hands/5294363) 

With this platform falling under the control of a specific company, Nvidia, there are concerns that the "neutrality" of the entire AI ecosystem could be shaken. If the models and tools used by developers worldwide are optimized to favor Nvidia's hardware (semiconductors) or software environments, other hardware manufacturers or institutions conducting independent research will inevitably encounter invisible barriers to entry. [Source 15](https://news.lavx.hu/article/hugging-face-is-too-important-to-fall-into-nvidia-s-hands)

### Easy Understanding: The Relationship Between 'Platform' and 'Tool'

To understand Hugging Face more easily, let's use another analogy. Think of an "app store" where we download apps on our smartphones. Hugging Face is the "app store for AI models" for AI developers. [Source 14](https://news.lavx.hu/article/hugging-face-is-too-important-to-fall-into-nvidia-s-hands) Countless AI models that anyone can use are uploaded here. [Source 8](https://huggingface.co/spaces/laruss5/Flux2-Klein-Face-Swap)

Nvidia is the maker of "ultra-high-performance servers and chips" that keep that app store running smoothly. The two companies have already collaborated so that developers can create AI services faster by utilizing Nvidia's technology (such as NIM). [Source 11](https://www.infoq.com/news/2024/08/nvidia-nim-huggingface/) However, if Nvidia, now the owner of the platform, designs it to use only its own products through this channel, it could result in something similar to an app store being designed to run best only on one company's phone.

### Current Situation: A $12.9 Billion Deal

Nvidia has agreed to pay a total of $12.93 billion for this acquisition. [Source 12](https://www.techpowerup.com/352319/nvidia-to-buy-hugging-face-for-usd-12-9b-promises-to-keep-it-an-open-platform), [Source 17](https://www.techspot.com/news/113640-nvidia-closes-129-billion-hugging-face-acquisition-neutrality.html) Of this, approximately $11.9 billion will go to existing investors, and about $1 billion has been earmarked as compensation for retaining Hugging Face employees. [Source 19](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/sep/03/nvidia-to-buy-hugging-face-in-129bn-deal)

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang stated in a blog post, "We will expand Hugging Face's platform and strengthen its infrastructure to make AI more accessible to developers worldwide," presenting an optimistic future. [Source 9](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-to-acquire-hugging-face/), [Source 12](https://www.techpowerup.com/352319/nvidia-to-buy-hugging-face-for-usd-12-9b-promises-to-keep-it-an-open-platform) Officially, they are continuing to promise the "maintenance of independence" and an "open platform." [Source 12](https://www.techpowerup.com/352319/nvidia-to-buy-hugging-face-for-usd-12-9b-promises-to-keep-it-an-open-platform), [Source 17](https://www.techspot.com/news/113640-nvidia-closes-129-billion-hugging-face-acquisition-neutrality.html)

### What will happen in the future?

Experts analyze that this deal is a strategic move for Nvidia to return to the AI cloud market in earnest, going beyond simply increasing semiconductor sales. [Source 3](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-closes-in-on-hugging-face-acquisition/) However, regulators are unlikely to sit idly by. It is expected that they will closely examine whether this acquisition could hinder market competition and deepen dependence on a specific company's hardware. [Source 15](https://news.lavx.hu/article/hugging-face-is-too-important-to-fall-into-nvidia-s-hands), [Source 7](https://tech-insider.org/nvidia-hugging-face-acquisition-12-9-billion-2026/)

Ultimately, general users like us might be able to use smarter and more powerful AI services more easily in the future. However, whether the AI ecosystem will lose its diversity and become a "walled garden of Nvidia," influenced by the whims of a single company, or whether it will truly be reborn as more open infrastructure, will be an important point to watch over the next few years.

Diversity is the lifeblood of the open-source ecosystem. It remains to be seen whether Nvidia's technological prowess and capital will serve as a driving force for expanding Hugging Face's infrastructure or if its hardware-first policy will undermine openness; this will be a crucial test.
