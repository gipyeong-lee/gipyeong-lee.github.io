Imagine this. You wake up in the morning, turn on your computer, and say to the AI, "Please plan my business trip to Jeju Island for next week, neatly organize the available flight times in Excel, and send it to my email."

Not too long ago, the AI would have merely displayed a nicely formatted text table on the screen. We would then have to open a new browser window, visit airline websites to search for flights one by one, open Excel, and manually transfer the data.

However, this will be completely different in the future. The AI will autonomously open its built-in browser to find the most reasonable flights, whip up an Excel document, and finish all the complex and tedious processes right up to the point before clicking the "send" button. All we will have to do is simply click "send" at the end.

This is exactly the hottest topic being discussed in the global tech industry right now. News has emerged that OpenAI, the AI developer that sparked a massive AI boom by first unveiling ChatGPT to the world in late 2022, is preparing for the largest full-scale overhaul in its history [[‘Chat is dead’: OpenAI plans biggest ChatGPT overhaul ahead of IPO, pivots to AI agents, says report | Company Business News](https://www.livemint.com/companies/news/chat-is-dead-openai-plans-biggest-chatgpt-overhaul-ahead-of-ipo-pivots-to-ai-agents-says-report-11780809387032.html)], [[OpenAI plots biggest ChatGPT overhaul since launch](https://oodaloop.com/briefs/technology/openai-plots-biggest-chatgpt-overhaul-since-launch/)]. 

Even somewhat radical declarations like "Chat is dead" have appeared in related reports and evaluations from industry experts, signaling that the era of traditional conversational chatbots is fading [[‘Chat is dead’: OpenAI plans biggest ChatGPT overhaul ahead of IPO, pivots to AI agents, says report | Company Business News](https://www.livemint.com/companies/news/chat-is-dead-openai-plans-biggest-chatgpt-overhaul-ahead-of-ipo-pivots-to-ai-agents-says-report-11780809387032.html)]. Just what kind of massive winds of change are blowing through the ChatGPT we use every day?

## Why It Matters

Until now, the ChatGPT we knew was the world's finest and smartest 'answer vending machine.' No matter how bizarre or difficult the question, it was a magical tool that effortlessly churned out plausible text sentences. But OpenAI's new goal is to transform this smart chatbot beyond a simple Q&A tool into a powerful 'superapp' that directly solves various problems in our work and lives [[ChatGPT will soon get 'biggest change' since its launch: AI agents, coding tools and more - The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/chatgpt-will-soon-get-biggest-change-since-its-launch-ai-agents-coding-tools-and-more/articleshow/131565147.cms)].

What exactly is a superapp in this context? Simply put, it refers to a massive 'central hub' where users can complete all their desired tasks within a single platform, eliminating the hassle of constantly switching between various computer programs or websites [[OpenAI reportedly has a major ChatGPT overhaul in store - Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2189038/openai-reportedly-has-a-major-chatgpt-overhaul-in-store/)]. 

Under the single large umbrella of ChatGPT, OpenAI plans to tightly weave together 'AI agents'—AIs that make autonomous judgments and act across multiple steps—along with coding tools for complex computer languages, web browsers, and third-party services created by external companies [[OpenAI plans ChatGPT 'superapp' overhaul ahead of listing | Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/openai-chatgpt-overhaul/)]. This grand plan reportedly includes integrating a desktop Codex app, which helps write code right from the computer desktop, seamlessly with ChatGPT [[OpenAI reportedly has a major ChatGPT overhaul in store - Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2189038/openai-reportedly-has-a-major-chatgpt-overhaul-in-store/)].

The implications of these changes for general consumers and office workers burdened with heavy daily workloads are truly tremendous. Moving forward, ChatGPT will go beyond the role of a teacher imparting knowledge; it will become a practical worker capable of directly executing and 'completing tasks' on behalf of the user [[ChatGPT on the verge of its biggest makeover since launch ...](https://basic-tutorials.com/news/chatgpt-on-the-verge-of-its-biggest-makeover-since-launch-openai-plans-to-become-an-ai-super-app/)]. The tiring modern workflow of keeping multiple windows open on a monitor and endlessly copying and pasting will fundamentally evolve into a system where everything is delegated to a highly competent, fast-working personal assistant.

## The Explainer

If the technical jargon feels a bit unfamiliar and difficult, let me explain it using two key analogies related to our daily lives.

First is the difference between the traditional **'Chatbot' and the upcoming 'AI Agent'**.
To use an analogy, the traditional chatbot was like the meticulous 'librarian' in the neighborhood who has read the most books. When we ask, "Tell me how to cook delicious Kimchi Jjigae," it scours the entire library to bring us the cookbook with the most accurate recipe. However, in the end, holding the knife, chopping the green onions, and cooking in front of the fire in the kitchen was left entirely up to us. 

On the other hand, the 'AI Agent' that OpenAI aims to create is like an experienced 'private chef' who handles everything from grocery shopping to cooking. If you just give the instruction, "I want to eat Kimchi Jjigae for dinner tonight," this chef will autonomously open the refrigerator and check the remaining ingredients. If there is a shortage of pork, it will connect to a local delivery app (a third-party service), order the meat itself, cook the stew when the delivery arrives, and place it on the dining table. It takes care of a series of tasks on our behalf. In other words, it is moving past the stage of merely understanding human commands into a dimension where it actually 'moves its hands and feet to directly solve problems' [[OpenAI plans ChatGPT 'superapp' overhaul ahead of listing | Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/openai-chatgpt-overhaul/)].

Second is the **concept of the 'Superapp'** that ChatGPT is aiming for.
Think back to when early smartphones first came out. The app for taking pictures, the app for writing notes, and the browser app for searching the internet were all separate. To do anything complex, you had to constantly switch between this app and that app. 

However, the future ChatGPT superapp envisioned by OpenAI is a massive 'omnipotent workshop' fully equipped with every tool. Inside this workshop, there is a browser for finding the latest news, tools for complex calculations, and secret passages wide open to freely connect to external services [[OpenAI plans ChatGPT 'superapp' overhaul ahead of listing | Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/openai-chatgpt-overhaul/)]. The user simply needs to sit in a comfortable chair in the middle of the workshop and issue commands. Then, the master manager known as ChatGPT becomes part of an amazing system that freely handles countless tools in the workshop and brings back only the final, perfectly neat result.

## Where We Stand

Just how far along is the current technological prowess of OpenAI and ChatGPT, which harbor such an astonishing and grand plan? In truth, OpenAI is progressing at a terrifying speed in laying the groundwork for the future. As of December 2025, OpenAI marked exactly its 10th anniversary since being founded as a pure research lab, and since the launch of ChatGPT in 2022, it has become one of the fastest-growing commercial enterprises in the world [[OpenAI intros new AI model GPT-5.2, says better at ... - CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/12/11/openai-intros-new-ai-model-gpt-5point2-says-better-at-professional-tasks.html)].

Most recently, in March 2026, it announced the 'GPT-5.3 Instant' model, which significantly reduces the mechanical feel and provides much more naturally human-like responses in everyday conversation [[OpenAI upgrades ChatGPT with GPT-5.4 Thinking, offering six ...](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/05/openai-upgrades-chatgpt-with-gpt-5-4-thinking-offering-six-key-improvements/)]. The evolution of the technology doesn't stop there; it continues to push the limits of AI performance by successively introducing the 'GPT-5.4 Thinking' model equipped with far deeper and more complex cognitive and reasoning capabilities, as well as the 'Pro' model designed for experts [[OpenAI upgrades ChatGPT 대with GPT-5.4 Thinking, offering six ...](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/05/openai-upgrades-chatgpt-with-gpt-5-4-thinking-offering-six-key-improvements/)].

The most encouraging part of this process is that the AI's 'memory' is getting frighteningly close to a human level. Recently, a completely new and innovative memory architecture called 'Dreaming V3' was massively introduced to ChatGPT. Thanks to this incredible feature, which has now been rolled out even to non-paying free users, ChatGPT's ability to accurately handle and remember time-sensitive information has taken a quantum leap from its previous 9.4% to a staggering 75.1% [[ChatGPT Memory Just Got a Major Overhaul - aitoolbriefing.com](https://aitoolbriefing.com/industry/chatgpt-memory-dreaming-v3-overhaul-2026/)]. 

What does this mean? For instance, if a user casually mentions in passing, "I have a super important presentation at work next Thursday," the AI clearly commits it to memory. Then, as the actual date approaches, it becomes genuinely possible for it to step up and offer to help prepare presentation materials in context or remind you of your schedule. To become a true 'AI agent' that finds tasks on its own and processes them step by step, a memory that doesn't forget what was said or the situation in the past is essential, and this clearly shows that the solid technological foundation for it is already nearing completion.

## What's Next

Behind any astonishing technological innovation, there is always a cold and fierce business world lurking. If so, why is OpenAI attempting such a massive pivot 'now' of all times, boldly casting aside the traditional chat format that brought them so much glory? 

The core reason lies in the highly realistic and desperate challenges of 'IPO (Initial Public Offering)' and 'revenue generation.' Currently, OpenAI is valued as a colossal group with an astronomical corporate value of a whopping $850 billion, a figure that is hard for an average person to even fathom [[OpenAI plots biggest ChatGPT overhaul since launch](https://oodaloop.com/briefs/technology/openai-plots-biggest-chatgpt-overhaul-since-launch/)]. Management is staring right at a massive stock market IPO slated to take place within this year. In order to attract massive investments through a successful IPO and take the company to the next level, they desperately need to find solid new engines of growth that will meet the strict expectations of the market [[OpenAI plots biggest ChatGPT overhaul since launch](https://oodaloop.com/briefs/technology/openai-plots-biggest-chatgpt-overhaul-since-launch/)], [[OpenAI plans ChatGPT 'superapp' overhaul ahead of listing | The Business Standard](https://www.tbsnews.net/world/global-economy/openai-plans-chatgpt-superapp-overhaul-ahead-listing-1456206)].

Of course, the Q&A-centric chatbots up until now have achieved historic success in sparking curiosity and garnering buzz among the general public. However, it is a fact that they were insufficient in securely attracting solid 'enterprise customers' who are willing to gladly pay huge sums and sign long-term contracts. To explosively boost the company's revenue and emerge as a truly profitable business, the introduction of 'superapps' and 'AI agents'—powerful weapons capable of autonomously automating and handling the practical and complex workflows of enterprise customers—has now become an essential requirement for survival, not an option [[OpenAI plans ChatGPT 'superapp' overhaul ahead of listing | Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/openai-chatgpt-overhaul/)], [[OpenAI plots biggest ChatGPT overhaul since launch](https://www.biznews.com/global-investing/ft-openai-plots-biggest-chatgpt-overhaul)].

Additionally, the blood-curdling pursuit from competitors is also hastening OpenAI's pace. Anthropic, currently considered OpenAI's strongest rival, has also recently publicly declared its strong intention to push for an IPO [[OpenAI reportedly has a major ChatGPT overhaul in store - Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2189038/openai-reportedly-has-a-major-chatgpt-overhaul-in-store/)]. In this fiercely unpredictable global war for AI hegemony, they must land successfully in the stock market even one step ahead of their competitors and capture the immense capital and hearts of investors worldwide. To do so, OpenAI stands at the most crucial crossroads, where it must prove itself not just as a 'developer that makes chat windows,' but as a 'massive platform' that will completely dominate how humanity works in the future [[OpenAI plans ChatGPT 'superapp' overhaul ahead of listing, FT reports | Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-plans-chatgpt-superapp-overhaul-ahead-listing-ft-reports-2026-06-07/)].

## AI's Take

The glamorous first act of the conversational AI era is slowly coming to a close, and the second act, centered on 'Agents' where AI receives human instructions and autonomously executes actions and processes practical tasks, is taking the stage in earnest. Moving beyond mere novelty and entertaining tech, the pace of technological evolution is accelerating the changes even more formidably as it meshes like a cogwheel with big tech companies' fight for business survival and the massive flows of capital in the stock market. 

We are currently walking right through the middle of a historic transition period where AI is evolving beyond a smart conversational partner that just speaks fluently, into the most practical and powerful set of tools placed in our hands. We should watch with thrilling anticipation to see exactly how the first screen of ChatGPT we turn on every morning will have changed in the near future, and just how drastically it will reduce the number of our mouse clicks and our overtime hours.
