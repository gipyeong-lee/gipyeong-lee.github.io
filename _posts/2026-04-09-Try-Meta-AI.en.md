This announcement signals a new phase in the artificial intelligence (AI) race, demonstrating Meta's strong will to complete a massive 'AI ecosystem' that spans the daily lives of billions of people, social media platforms, and wearable hardware, moving beyond simple chatbot services. Currently, Meta AI's most powerful weapon is 'accessibility.' This is because it is directly integrated into WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, and Facebook—apps used daily by people around the world—without requiring a separate complex registration process or app installation. Meta AI is essentially built on the Llama series, Meta's latest large language models, and performs multi-faceted tasks such as answering questions, completing tasks, and generating images for free. ## Infrastructure and Economics: A $21 Billion Massive Gamble

The scale of physical investment to support technical progress is also beyond imagination. Meta recently surprised the industry by signing a $21 billion AI infrastructure expansion contract with CoreWeave, a cloud computing company. [CoreWeave and Meta Announce $21 Billion Expanded AI Infrastructure Agreement](https://investors.coreweave.com/news/news-details/2026/CoreWeave-and-Meta-Announce-21-Billion-Expanded-AI-Infrastructure-Agreement/default.aspx) This is a measure to handle the explosively increasing AI inference workloads, proving that Meta's AI strategy is being realized through the acquisition of practical computing power.

However, some in the market are looking at whether Meta's astronomical investment can translate into actual 'profitability' with concern. If the Muse model, released after a year of intensive research, fails to prove its value relative to the investment, it could disrupt Meta's future strategy. ## AI's Perspective (Opinion): Changes Brought by the Combination of 'Social Graph' and 'Superintelligence'

Meta's path is different from Google's search-based approach or Microsoft's focus on work productivity. Meta is building AI based on the unique data of human 'relationships' and 'connections.' The moment Muse Spark combines with the hardware of smart glasses, AI is no longer just a presence on a screen but a practical 'partner' sharing the user's senses.

This means a fundamental paradigm shift in how humanity uses tools. When vast social media relationship data meets superintelligent models, AI will evolve to a level that understands social contexts and individual emotions beyond just answering questions. Meta's $21 billion investment is not just for expanding hardware, but the cost of a massive social experiment to redefine the entire human social experience through the filter of AI.

## Conclusion

The emergence of Meta AI and Muse Spark suggests that AI technology has moved beyond the stage of being a 'novel tool' and has entered 'life-integrated technology' that we wear on our bodies and blend into our daily conversations. The world is watching to see if this massive AI empire built by Meta can prove its profitability according to capital logic while truly enriching users' lives.
