Imagine standing in the middle of a deep rainforest. Before your eyes are only dense trees, but in your ears, the chirping of thousands of birds, the croaking of frogs, and the buzzing of unknown insects pour down like a waterfall. These sounds are not just mere noise; they are like 'survival reports' that nature sends to us. However, picking out the voice of a specific endangered animal from this vast 'Earth's playlist' is as difficult as finding a single person's whisper in a crowded stadium.

Now, Artificial Intelligence (AI) has transformed into a 'sound detective' that does this difficult work for us. Here is the exciting story of how AI saves endangered animals just by listening to their sounds.

## Why is this important?

In the past, for scientists to survey endangered species, they had to wander through jungles, stake out for days, or capture animals to attach tracking devices. This was naturally a huge stress for sensitive animals, and surveying was impossible in rugged terrain where humans could not easily reach. It was like trying to force one's way into a building after losing the security card.

However, the situation has completely changed as **Bioacoustics (the study of ecosystems by recording and analyzing animal sounds)** technology met AI. Scientists now only need to install microphones in forests or underwater. They can hear the stories inside without knocking on nature's door. [AI can now be our eyes and ears in the forest and beneath the waves | EurekAlert!](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1062377)

Associate Professor Elodie F. Briefer evaluated that "Detecting species without disturbing animal habitats is a breakthrough in conservation," and that it has opened a 'new era' of nature protection.
