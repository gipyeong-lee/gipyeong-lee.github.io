Imagine this: You wake up in the morning and tell your AI, "Analyze the latest market trends for today's important meeting, and based on that, create an editable presentation and a summary report." In the past, AI would have stopped at simply summarizing information or drafting text. Now, we have entered an era where AI independently designs complex steps and generates final deliverables itself.

The protagonist of this shift is **GPT-5.6 Sol**, the latest flagship model unveiled by OpenAI on July 9, 2026. [Source 1, Source 6] Today, I’d like to talk about how this intelligent model is set to change our daily lives and the special benefits you can enjoy right now.

## Why It Matters

The AI we usually use has mostly functioned as an "assistant" that answers questions or writes text. However, GPT-5.6 Sol has maximized its ability as an "agent" (an AI that judges and acts on its own) that goes beyond giving answers to create results through a long, multi-step process. [Source 8, Source 9]

Simply put, if existing AI was like a cookbook telling you a recipe, Sol is like a chef who goes grocery shopping, cooks the meal, and serves it on the table. This brings a massive change to work productivity. For example, in tasks like financial analysis or complex report writing, the AI can independently produce presentation materials and spreadsheet results from beginning to end. [Source 13] It creates an environment where you can confidently entrust even more complex work to AI, even without knowing the technical details.

## The Explainer

The GPT-5.6 series is divided into three tiers so that users can choose according to their needs. [Source 11] Think of it like choosing a "toolkit" that fits the situation.

1. **GPT-5.6 Sol**: The top-tier model introduced here. It is like a "strategist." It has the best ability to reason through multiple steps and create plans to solve complex problems. [Source 9, Source 14]
2. **GPT-5.6 Terra**: The "balanced expert." It is a model that strikes an optimal balance between performance and price, efficiently handling the majority of tasks. [Source 9, Source 11]
3. **GPT-5.6 Luna**: The "fast executor." It is the fastest and most affordable model, suitable for handling simple and repetitive tasks. [Source 9, Source 11]

In particular, the Sol model possesses a massive "context window" (the amount of information the AI can remember and process at once) of **1.05 million tokens**. [Source 1, Source 3] To give you an idea of what 1.05 million tokens means, it is vast enough that you can show the AI a massive pile of documents running into thousands of pages or an entire complex programming codebase at once and tell it, "Find the error in this and fix it." Think of it not as viewing a single page of a book with a magnifying glass, but as surveying an entire room at a glance to identify patterns.

## Where We Stand

GPT-5.6 Sol is currently available for anyone to use via ChatGPT. [Source 6] However, even more interesting news is the collaboration with **Devin**, a developer tool. Devin is an AI-powered software engineering agent, and there is a promotion currently running where you can use GPT-5.6 Sol at a **70% discount**. [Source 8, Source 10]

This discount lasts until October 3, 2026, allowing users performing complex technical work to experience the highest-performing model at an economical cost. [Source 8, Source 10] In terms of performance metrics, the Sol (max) model has recorded 61 points on the Intelligence Index, demonstrating a level of intelligence far exceeding the industry average. [Source 2]

## What's Next

Moving forward, AI models will evolve beyond simply delivering information into "long-horizon task" solvers that complete the work we assign until the very end. [Source 9] While we currently have to review the AI's results one by one, it won't be long before AI develops to the stage where it writes the draft, corrects errors, and sends the final result via email on its own.

We are now transitioning from an era where we tell AI "how to do it" step-by-step to an era where we entrust goals to AI, saying "complete this task." As technology advances, our role will increasingly resemble that of a "manager" overseeing AI.

GPT-5.6 Sol is more than just a technical achievement demonstrating how much smarter AI can get; it is the stage that proves how much "acting AI" can practically increase work productivity. The change is particularly noteworthy in software engineering or professional data analysis, where AI has begun to do more than just provide support—it is now pulling its weight as a peer.
