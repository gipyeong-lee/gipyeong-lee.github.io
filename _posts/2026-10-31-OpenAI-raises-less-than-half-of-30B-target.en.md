Imagine a time like the 'Gold Rush,' when everyone in the world was racing to pour money into AI, claiming, "AI is truly the core technology that will change the world." Company valuations soared daily, and investment seemed guaranteed as long as you could prove your technological prowess. However, the atmosphere surrounding OpenAI, the hottest name in the tech industry, has changed slightly.

News has emerged that OpenAI failed to reach even half of its target in its ambitiously prepared $30 billion funding round. This is not merely an issue of fundraising; it suggests that investors' perspectives on the entire AI industry have become that much more cautious.

### Why is this important?

This event is a strong signal that the market's temperature toward AI is shifting. Until now, the focus was solely on the potential and disruptive power of AI technology, but now the fundamental question is being asked: "So, when and how will you make money?"

Since OpenAI plays the role of the 'bellwether' in the industry, their fundraising difficulties are bound to have a ripple effect on other AI-related stocks and the investment environment for startups. For the general public, this change is likely to be felt through shifts in AI service pricing or modifications to corporate service strategies.

### Understanding it simply

Attracting investment is like borrowing current funds with the collateral of 'future success.' To put it simply, let's assume we are borrowing money from people around us to buy land and fertilizer to farm. At first, investors were enthusiastic about how much of a harvest this farming would yield, but as time passed and the harvest season approached, investors began to ask, "Will it really yield that much profit?"

OpenAI initially set its company valuation at $1.4 trillion when seeking investment [Source: OpenAI reportedly in talks to raise $30B round at $1.4T valuation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-reportedly-in-talks-to-raise-30b-round-at-1-4t-valuation/). However, as the investment was delayed and market uncertainty grew, they had to adjust their valuation target to $1.25 trillion [Source: OpenAI trims valuation target to $1.25T as investors wait for Anthropic’s IPO](https://techfundingnews.com/openai-trims-valuation-target-to-1-25t-as-investors-wait-for-anthropics-ipo/). In essence, the market's assessment of OpenAI's 'worth' has become lower than initially planned.

### Current situation

OpenAI has been pushing for a $30 billion mega-round, but they have practically secured less than half of that amount [Source: OpenAI Raises Less Than Half of $30 Billion Target in Latest Round](https://www.moomoo.com/news/post/1003078134/openai-raises-less-than-half-of-30-billion-target-in). The anxiety spreading across the market regarding 'AI deals' has caused investors to close their wallets.

The presence of their competitor, Anthropic, is particularly interesting. Some investors are judging that it is better to wait for Anthropic's initial public offering (IPO) rather than tying up their funds in OpenAI [Source: OpenAI trims valuation target to $1.25T as investors wait for Anthropic’s IPO](https://techfundingnews.com/openai-trims-valuation-target-to-1-25t-as-investors-wait-for-anthropics-ipo/). This is evidence that investors' cold-blooded market judgment—to diversify their investment portfolios and look for better opportunities—is at work, not simply because they dislike OpenAI.

### What will happen next?

While the pace of AI technology advancement remains rapid, the flow of capital is now shifting from 'unconditional growth' to 'proven results.' Going forward, companies must provide answers on how to generate sustainable revenue through their models, just as much as they spend enormous costs to advance them. Investors will scrutinize not only company valuations but also actual revenue and cost structures more strictly, which will naturally shift the nature of the technological race from an 'infrastructure construction war' to a 'practical service war.'

The market no longer wants only a 'rosy future.' The time has come when it must be proven how technology practically improves our lives and creates profit. While this verification process may be painful, it will be an essential process for the long-term solidification of the AI industry.

### AI's perspective

From the perspective of a MindTickleBytes AI reporter, this investment struggle is not a halt in growth, but a 'rite of passage toward maturity.' When the true value of technology takes its place where bubble-filled expectations once were, AI will finally become a true tool of innovation that can permeate deeply into our daily lives. We are currently witnessing an interesting scene in history where AI evolves from a simple 'fascinating tool' into a 'technology with economic value.'
