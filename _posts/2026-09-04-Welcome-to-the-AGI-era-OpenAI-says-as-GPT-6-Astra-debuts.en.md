Imagine this: As soon as you wake up in the morning, you say to your smartphone, "Summarize my meeting materials according to my schedule today, and pull out key questions for the important parts." Like an assistant who has worked by your side for years, the AI doesn't just list internet search results; it offers suggestions that get straight to the essence of the problems you've been grappling with. If the AI we have experienced so far was a smart tool following a set path, a new era where it judges for itself and solves problems has now stepped right up to our side.

OpenAI recently set a new milestone in human technological history with the release of its most powerful latest AI model, 'GPT-6 Astra'. OpenAI President Greg Brockman left a short but powerful declaration while announcing the model: "Welcome to the AGI era." [Source: OpenAI launches GPT-6 Astra and says welcome to the "AGI era"](https://thenewstack.io/openai-gpt6-astra-benchmarks/)

### Why It Matters

'AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)' refers to intelligence that can perform any intellectual task that a human can do—or even better—rather than just being good at specific fields. Until now, we have used AI only as a 'specialized tool,' such as drawing pictures or writing code. But AGI is different. Metaphorically speaking, it is like recruiting a 'genius student' who is versatile in everything from solving math problems to writing novels and establishing complex strategies as a member of your team.

How will our daily lives change when the AGI era opens? The most immediate change will be in how we search for information and process tasks. Beyond simply spending time looking for information, AI will become a 'true collaborative partner' that comprehensively judges complex situations and provides decisive advice. However, with such massive intelligence onboard, the responsibility for security and safety has also become more important than ever before.

### The Explainer

Shall we compare AI to a massive 'library'? If the existing AI acted as a librarian helping users find books they asked for, GPT-6 Astra is more like a 'genius author' who has read every book in the library, perfectly understood the content, and is capable of writing new books on its own.

GPT-6 Astra was trained at the largest scale in OpenAI's history. It mobilized over 100,000 GPUs (Graphics Processing Units, the core components responsible for the calculations needed for AI training) installed at the 'Stargate' data center in Texas. [Source: OpenAI Declared AGI & World Models Get WILD! - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/_uZTCOfaSUk) To put this in perspective, it is like reading through the books of knowledge humanity has accumulated millions of times and fully mastering them.

Additionally, Astra is the first to cross the 'critical' threshold in the 'Preparedness Framework' (an internal system that assesses and controls the dangerous capabilities of AI in advance). [Source: OpenAI Releases GPT-6 Astra: The Closest AI Model Yet to AGI](https://decrypt.co/377341/openai-releases-gpt-6-astra-agi) To put it metaphorically, rather than handing a sports car to a novice driver without a license, it is like installing top-tier safety features and mandatory rigorous training courses. It means that while it delivers such powerful performance, the attempt to control its risks is also at the highest level.

### Where We Stand

GPT-6 Astra has already proven its astonishing performance. It scored 100 out of 100 on the 'ExploitBench' AI intelligence test and recorded 99.9% on 'ARC-AGI-3,' which evaluates complex reasoning abilities. [Source: OpenAI Wows With The 'AGI Era' GPT-6 Astra, With Preliminary...](https://wccftech.com/openai-wows-with-the-agi-era-gpt-6-astra-with-preliminary-benchmarks-showing-remarkable-performance-gains-as-sam-altman-says-releases-will-now-be-paced-by-safety-considerations-and-not-capabilit/)

However, it is not immediately available for everyone to use freely. While a public release for the general public is scheduled to happen soon, features that could be dangerous, such as Astra's high-level cyber security capabilities, have initially been released only to testers and partners. [Source: Techmeme: OpenAI launches GPT-6 Astra, initially available to...](https://www.techmeme.com/260903/p33) OpenAI is placing more weight on how safely we can handle this intelligence than on the AI's intelligence itself.

### What's Next

We have now moved past the stage of asking 'what can AI do' and into an era of contemplating 'how will we coexist with AI.' AGI could be the key to solving complex problems we face, but it also requires vigilance against uncontrolled technology. The key will be to watch how the general-purpose Astra model to be released in the future changes our daily lives, and how OpenAI manages to kill two birds with one stone: technological innovation and safety.

### MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Perspective
The opening of the AGI era is a huge event that is both an extension of human intelligence and a change in the definition of tools. We must not forget that no matter how perfect the technical metrics are, humanity's ethical standards must grow in tandem with the weight of the intelligence the AI possesses. After all, technology should exist to help humans.
