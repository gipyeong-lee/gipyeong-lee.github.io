Imagine this: You wake up in the morning and ask your AI, "Organize my morning meeting materials and send them via email, then prepare the files on my PC for my afternoon schedule." Previously, when you tried to continue tasks you started on your smartphone on your PC, you had to deal with the hassle of explaining everything from scratch because the AI couldn't remember the previous context. But now, a "true personal assistant" has arrived to break down the barriers between your smartphone and computer and become your right hand. Meet **nanoMuse**, an open-source AI agent project.

## Why Does This Matter?

We live in an era where we use multiple devices simultaneously, such as smartphones, laptops, and desktops. However, each device uses different operating systems and apps, and the AI on each is often siloed, leading to a persistent lack of organic "connectivity" between devices. nanoMuse was born to quench this thirst. Its goal goes beyond a chatbot that merely provides answers through chat; it aims to directly control your devices to process real-world tasks. It is particularly noteworthy for being a fully open-source project that anyone can audit and contribute to, rather than a closed service controlled by a corporation [Source 3, Source 4].

## Simplified: What Is nanoMuse?

In short, nanoMuse is like your **"digital alter ego."**

If existing AI models were assistants with a smart brain called a Transformer (the core AI architecture that grasps the relationships between words in sentences), nanoMuse is like giving that assistant "hands and feet." While AI until now has been confined to the narrow frame of a chat window, nanoMuse moves freely across your phone and PC screens, moving the mouse and directly manipulating browsers [Source 1, Source 11].

Metaphorically, it is like the **"conductor of an orchestra."** When you instruct the orchestra (the countless apps on your smartphone and PC) on what to play, nanoMuse navigates between each instrument (app), turning the pages of the score and adjusting the tone. The most important point here is that even when you close an app, the conductor doesn't stop, preparing for the next sequence behind the stage [Source 3, Source 5]. In this way, nanoMuse remembers your context to the end and possesses the prudence to confirm your intent by asking, "Shall I proceed like this?" before performing irreversible tasks [Source 3].

## Current Status: How Far Has It Come?

nanoMuse is currently a fully open-source project distributed under the GPL-3.0 license. Anyone can check the relevant source code and contribute directly to the project via the [official website](https://nanomuse.cn/) or [GitHub](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse) [Source 3, Source 4].

- **Excellent Connectivity:** It organically links Android smartphones with desktop computers and consists of a dedicated app, a web console, and a relay system that connects them [Source 4].
- **Model Freedom:** It is not dependent on a specific company's model. Depending on the user's preference or environment, you can freely connect models like DeepSeek, OpenAI, or Ollama installed directly on your PC [Source 6].
- **Practical Functionality:** Looking at the currently available browser demo, it shows practical productivity, such as automatically filling out PDF forms or reading screens to perform complex web tasks [Source 8].

Of course, as the project is currently in active development, there may be parts that require the user to configure settings themselves or have some technical understanding. However, this is also a natural process of the powerful advantages of "openness" and "user agency" that nanoMuse possesses.

## What Will Happen in the Future?

Agent technologies like nanoMuse will fundamentally change the way we interact with our devices. Simple tasks where we repeatedly search for files, copy and paste, and check emails will gradually become the job of agents like nanoMuse.

What will the user do? Instead of spending time digging through apps and menus agonizing over "how to do it," they will be able to focus more on creative and essential work deciding "what to do." nanoMuse will become a massive hub that connects all of a user's devices into one, becoming a reliable partner that helps ensure your workflow is never interrupted, no matter where you are [Source 4, Source 6].
