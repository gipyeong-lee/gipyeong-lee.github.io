# AI Solves Cosmic Challenges and Shakes the UN Security Council: The Era of 'True' Intelligence Beyond ChatGPT

At the close of 2026, artificial intelligence (AI) is now moving far beyond simple Q&A to deeply penetrate humanity's long-standing domains of intellectual inquiry. Imagine this: the complex and abstruse problem of the existence and smoothness of the 'Navier-Stokes equations'—akin to a cosmic secret—was solved by AI in just 88 hours. This is a monumental event demonstrating that AI can transcend mere technological progress to become a powerful tool expanding human intellect. [Source 5](https://www.ixbt.com/news/2026/09/09/433421-novaia-model-openai-resila-odnu-iz-semi-zadac-tysiaceletiia-kotoruiu-ne-mogli-dokazat-90-let.html), [Source 9](https://www.securitylab.ru/news/577100.php)

At the same time, this astonishing pace of technological development poses profound questions for human society. It is highly symbolic that OpenAI CEO Sam Altman personally appeared before the UN Security Council to issue a grave warning about the potential risks of rapid AI development and the possibility of humanity losing control. [Source 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GRfIUTelPko), [Source 4](https://iz.ru/2172471/2026-09-23/glava-openai-zaiavil-o-vozmozhnoi-potere-kontrolia-chelovechestva-nad-ii) Coupled with OpenAI and Cisco unveiling a new security solution called 'Shirudo', [Source 1](https://openai.com/index/introducing-shirudo/) this clearly demonstrates how urgent it is to rigorously manage AI's risks alongside its infinite possibilities.

## Why Does This Matter?

Put simply, AI tools like ChatGPT that we commonly use in daily life are closer to assistants that respond based on language proficiency. However, this case of solving the Navier-Stokes equations suggests that AI is moving beyond mere information processing into the realm of 'true intelligence,' autonomously exploring and solving scientific challenges. By analogy, whereas previously we were conversing with a smart assistant, we have now gained a genius scientist colleague capable of solving Nobel-tier problems. This means AI can play a decisive role in resolving massive, complex issues facing humanity, such as new drug development, climate change prediction, and clean energy research.

However, this dazzling pace of progress simultaneously presents us with a heavy task. CEO Sam Altman's warning about the risks of AI development at the UN Security Council—the world's highest decision-making stage—is a clear example of how tightly technological advancement is intertwined with social, ethical, and security issues. He expressed grave concern that AI could develop faster than humanity's ability to respond or control it, potentially leading to a loss of control over AI. [Source 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GRfIUTelPko), [Source 4](https://iz.ru/2172471/2026-09-23/glava-openai-zaiavil-o-vozmozhnoi-potere-kontrolia-chelovechestva-nad-ii), [Source 7](https://ria.ru/20260923/ii-2119881231.html) This is akin to building a spacecraft with an unimaginably powerful rocket engine, only to feel the chilling sensation that it might be moving too fast for the pilot to hold the steering wheel. International discussion and cooperation are more urgent than ever to fully enjoy the benefits of AI while protecting ourselves from its latent risks.

## Easy Understanding: AI Solves the 'Dream Equation'

The advancement of AI technology is unfolding simultaneously across multiple fronts. Among them, one of the most groundbreaking achievements is the breakthrough in mathematics. OpenAI's latest AI model successfully solved the 'Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem'—a notorious challenge—in just 88 hours by collaborating with approximately 10,000 'autonomous AI agents' (AI programs that judge and act independently). [Source 5](https://www.ixbt.com/news/2026/09/09/433421-novaia-model-openai-resila-odnu-iz-semi-zadac-tysiaceletiia-kotoruiu-ne-mogli-dokazat-90-let.html), [Source 9](https://www.securitylab.ru/news/577100.php)

Why are these 'Navier-Stokes equations' so important? Simply put, they are core mathematical tools used to describe the motion of fluids (liquids or gases like water and air). They play a decisive role in understanding and predicting all complex fluid dynamics phenomena encountered in daily life, such as flowing water, wind patterns, and even blood circulation in our bodies. By analogy, this was one of science's ultimate challenges, akin to perfectly forecasting global weather patterns or accurately calculating how an airplane can glide through the air most smoothly and safely. The fact that AI has unraveled this 'cosmic challenge' clearly demonstrates that AI has moved beyond a mere information processor to acquire the ability to understand complex scientific concepts and creatively solve problems.

Approximately 10,000 autonomous AI agents were deployed in solving this problem. It is like tens of thousands of highly trained scientists scattered across the globe collaborating day and night for a single grand objective. The approach of utilizing numerous AI agents communicating and dividing roles rather than relying on a single AI serves as an important blueprint showing how AI will drive innovation across various industries in the future.

Meanwhile, alongside technological advancement, the importance of security is also growing. On November 23, 2026, OpenAI and Cisco jointly announced a new security solution called 'Shirudo'. [Source 1](https://openai.com/index/introducing-shirudo/) This well illustrates how crucial it is to proactively prepare for new security threats that may arise as AI technology advances.

The solution to the Navier-Stokes equations proves that AI possesses the potential to transcend human cognitive limits and drive new discoveries. This astonishing rate of AI development is the background behind CEO Sam Altman's warnings at the UN Security Council regarding AI's potential risks and the possibility of losing control. Such technological progress shows that AI will profoundly impact almost every aspect of our society, making social, ethical, and security preparations more crucial than ever.

## What's Next?

AI is expected to play a core role in solving increasingly complex and critical scientific and engineering challenges going forward. For instance, AI's contributions will grow in instantly identifying potential candidate substances among millions of chemicals during drug discovery or maximizing the accuracy of climate change modeling to devise more effective environmental responses. Moreover, there is boundless potential for AI to reliably complement human creativity and analytical power in fields like materials science and space exploration, driving groundbreaking discoveries.

However, as the pace of AI development accelerates, preparations for its risks will also become increasingly urgent. CEO Sam Altman's concerns regarding the 'loss of control' raised at the UN are not mere cinematic sci-fi fantasies. If AI evolves faster than humanity's response capacity or ethical judgment capabilities, it could lead to unpredictable social turmoil. Therefore, global cooperation and the establishment of regulatory frameworks to minimize risks while maximizing the benefits of AI technology are essential. Much like international control and safety agreements following the development of nuclear technology in the past, a robust and effective international governance system will be required in the AI era as well.

The emergence of advanced security solutions such as OpenAI and Cisco's 'Shirudo' [Source 1](https://openai.com/index/introducing-shirudo/) is a natural result of this trend. Future AI must be 'safe and reliable,' not merely 'smart.' The development of AI models leading to GPT-4.5 and GPT-5 poses humanity's most critical question: how we will find the balance point between technological progress and social and ethical responsibility.
