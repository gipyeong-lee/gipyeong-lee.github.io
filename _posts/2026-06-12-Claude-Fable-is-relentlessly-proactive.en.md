But this new employee is different. They draft the plan themselves, write the code, and then use their own eyes to check if the screens appear correctly. If there's an error in the output, they don't ask questions; they'll stay up all night if necessary to fix and evaluate it themselves, quietly placing a near-perfect result on your desk the next morning.

## Why It Matters

The familiar chatbot-style AIs we've used so far were essentially like 'advanced vending machines.' You insert a coin (ask a question), press a button, and the corresponding drink (answer) pops out. However, you can't ask a vending machine to "plan a week's worth of healthy meals for me, go grocery shopping, and then cook every morning according to my constitution." This is because a vending machine only responds to one-off requests and cannot move toward long-term goals on its own.

Claude Fable 5 has leaped far beyond the limitations of such simple vending machines or chatbots. This model is designed to act as an 'independent knowledge worker,' completing large and complex projects from start to finish—projects that would normally take a human hours, days, or even weeks of struggling to solve [Prompting Claude Fable 5 - Claude API Docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-fable-5) [ClaudeFable5 - API Pricing & Benchmarks | OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/anthropic/claude-fable-5).

Anthropic refers to this model as having **'Mythos-class'** capabilities [Anthropic Claude Fable 5 on AWS: Mythos-class capabilities with built-in safeguards now available | Amazon Web Services](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/anthropic-claude-fable-5-on-aws-mythos-class-capabilities-with-built-in-safeguards-now-available/) [ClaudeFable5 - API Pricing & Benchmarks | OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/anthropic/claude-fable-5). 'Mythos' is a word meaning myth or legend; a model of this class is built for your most ambitious and long-running projects. It is specialized for solving complex, ambiguous, and vast problems that previous AI models wouldn't have dared to touch [Claude Fable \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude/fable) [Prompting Claude Fable 5 - Claude API Docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-fable-5).

The implications for the average office worker or the general public are enormous. Until now, users had to painstakingly give instructions piece by piece: "Summarize this paragraph," then "Write this into code." Humans had to constantly intervene as managers of the AI. Now, however, you only need to provide a broad direction like "Achieve this grand goal." Then, an era is opening where true 'delegation' is possible—the AI sets its own detailed plans, bypasses obstacles when it encounters them, and takes responsibility for execution until the end. Simply put, it has evolved from a simple 'tool' that helps with your work into a reliable 'colleague' that does your work for you.

## The Explainer

So, how can Claude Fable 5 handle multi-day projects on its own? Let's look at three core features that make this model special.

**1. A Meticulous Student Who Checks Their Own Answers: 'Proactive Self-Verification'**

When you throw a difficult math or coding problem at existing AIs, they would quickly generate a sentence—whether it was right or wrong—and call it a day. They were like students who confidently submitted an incorrect answer. However, Claude Fable 5 is a student who thoroughly 'double-checks' before submitting the exam paper.

According to Anthropic, this model's way of working is very thorough and proactive, testing the results it creates itself [Claude Fable \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude/fable). Technically, this is called **'Proactive self-verification.'** This AI self-updates its skills if it learns anything new during a task and even develops its own set of evaluations and harnesses to assess its own outputs [Anthropic Claude Fable 5 on AWS: Mythos-class capabilities with built-in safeguards now available | Amazon Web Services](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/anthropic-claude-fable-5-on-aws-mythos-class-capabilities-with-built-in-safeguards-now-available/). It harshly evaluates itself to reach an extreme level of completion before anyone else can check its work.

**2. A Coding Wizard with Eyes: 'Critical Review Using Vision'**

One of the most amazing developments is that this AI is not just a blind wizard typing letters (code); it actually 'sees' the results it has created with its own eyes.

For example, imagine you've instructed Fable 5 to create a web screen for a computer program. Fable 5 writes the code and implements the design with high fidelity. What's surprising is what comes next. After checking the result of the code it wrote using its Vision (the ability to see and understand images) feature, it critically critiques it by comparing it to the original goal [Anthropic Claude Fable 5 on AWS: Mythos-class capabilities with built-in safeguards now available | Amazon Web Services](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/anthropic-claude-fable-5-on-aws-mythos-class-capabilities-with-built-in-safeguards-now-available/). This perfectly mirrors a chef who, after finishing a dish according to a recipe, personally checks the plating and tastes it before sending it to the customer's table. It possesses incredible strengths in coding and multi-modal reasoning (the ability to understand text and images simultaneously) [AffordableClaudeFable5 API for Coding and Mythos-Class... | Kie.ai](https://kie.ai/claude-fable-5).

**3. A Bold Colleague Who Doesn't Always Say 'Yes': 'Prompt Reflection'**

Previous AIs would mechanically try to invent plausible answers no matter how foolish a user's question was. However, those who have used Claude Fable 5 immediately discover a peculiar tendency where the model seems to voice its own opinion about the user's question (prompt) itself [Claude Fable 5 is Mythos for the masses - Techzine Global](https://www.techzine.eu/blogs/applications/141978/claude-fable-5-is-mythos-for-the-masses/).

While still within the framework of a Large Language Model (LLM), Fable 5 shows a tendency for self-reflection regarding the input instructions [Claude Fable 5 is Mythos for the masses - Techzine Global](https://www.techzine.eu/blogs/applications/141978/claude-fable-5-is-mythos-for-the-masses/). It's more like a smart and bold practitioner who gives feedback back to the user, saying, "It might be better to approach this question in this way." It's like having a dependable partner who suggests a better path instead of blindly obeying if you give a wrong instruction.

## Where We Stand

Claude Fable 5 is not a story from the distant future; it has already entered our reality. Anthropic has officially released this model to the public as its most advanced general-purpose model [Introducing Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 - Claude API Docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/introducing-claude-fable-5-and-claude-mythos-5).

Interestingly, there is a **hidden twin brother** in this announcement. The version released to the public with built-in safeguards (features that prevent harmful or dangerous outputs) so we can use it safely in daily life and business is 'Claude Fable 5.' On the other hand, there exists a dangerous and powerful version that is identical to this model but has the safeguards limiting its power intentionally removed. This mysterious model is named **'Claude Mythos 5'** and is secretly and limitedly provided only to a few, such as cybersecurity experts with verified identities and purposes, through a top-secret program called 'Project Glasswing' [Claude Fable 5 Is Here: Anthropic's First Public Mythos-Class ...](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/4523/claude-fable-5-fruitcake-eap-mythos-public-release).

Considering that the cost of other cheap everyday AI models is around $1 to $2, it's essentially paying the 'labor cost' of an ultra-premium expert.

The teams getting the best results from Fable 5 are deploying this AI to their **'hardest unsolved problems'** [Prompting Claude Fable 5 - Claude API Docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-fable-5).
