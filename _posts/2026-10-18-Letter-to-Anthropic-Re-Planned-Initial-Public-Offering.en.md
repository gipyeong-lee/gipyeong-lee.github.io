Imagine this: the voice assistant or writing tool you use on your smartphone every day suddenly becomes even smarter, and you hear news that the company behind it is debuting on the stock market at a massive scale. Anthropic, one of the most talked-about companies in the AI industry recently, is exactly that protagonist. As a leader in generative AI (AI that learns from data to create new content), Anthropic is planning a spectacular entrance onto Wall Street through an IPO later this year. However, before this grand celebration, strong warning bells are ringing to stop the listing. What exactly is the problem?

### Why is this important?

Anthropic's upcoming IPO is expected to be the first major public listing for a generative AI company. [Source: Anthropic IPO explained...](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/us-stocks/wall-street-guide/anthropic-ipo-explained-2-trillion-valuation-ballooning-net-loss-ai-risks-and-other-details-in-leaked-ipo-papers/articleshow/134588023.cms) But why are public figures like the New York City Comptroller stepping forward to demand a halt to the process? [Source: Letter to Anthropic Re: Planned Initial Public Offering](https://comptroller.nyc.gov/reports/letter-to-anthropic-re-planned-initial-public-offering/) This is not just a stock market issue; it is a question of social trust regarding how safe the AI we use is and whether the company developing that technology operates responsibly. As general investors or AI users, it is natural to question whether this technology that has become deeply embedded in our lives is truly safe.

### Easy to understand: Where is the AI's 'brake'?

The pace of AI technological development is, metaphorically, like a 'speeding sports car.' It is so fast and powerful that it surprises people. Even Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei has argued that the development speed should be slowed down, stating that AI technology is advancing much faster than the pace at which technical safety safeguards are being created. [Source: Anthropic IPO 2026 Guide...](https://www.zacks.com/featured-articles/761/anthropic-ipo)

What is a 'technical safety safeguard' here? Simply put, in the analogy of a car, it is the process of verifying whether the 'brake' to stop in an emergency works properly, rather than just having an accelerator. Investors and relevant authorities are demanding that before Anthropic raises funds through an IPO, it transparently prove that this brake is strong enough to prevent the existential risks that could befall humanity.

### Current situation: Delayed schedule and pouring warnings

Anthropic originally targeted an October IPO, but is currently adjusting its plans for November, or at the latest, the end of this year. [Source: Anthropic IPO 2026: Everything You Need to Know...](https://thebriefscript.com/anthropic-ipo-2026-s1-filing-valuation/) [Source: Anthropic could begin marketing...](https://www.indiatoday.in/business/market/story/anthropic-ipo-november-launch-filing-existential-ai-risks-report-3009557-2026-10-05)

There are two major controversies surrounding the IPO process.
First, there is the issue of technical security. In a letter sent to Anthropic, Howard Lutnick issued a strong warning not to provide its latest models, 'Fable 5' and 'Mythos 5,' to foreign nationals without approval from the U.S. Department of Commerce. [Source: Howard Lutnick's letter to Anthropic...](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/howard-lutnick-s-letter-to-anthropic-warned-of-curbs-on-top-ai-models-126061700109_1.html)
Second, there are concerns about investor protection and corporate governance. The New York City Comptroller urged that "Anthropic should halt the IPO process and fully address concerns regarding AI safety, governance, and development speed." [Source: AI Insight: Anthropic IPO, why did the NYC Comptroller demand a halt?](https://aiinsight365.blogspot.com/2026/10/ipo.html)

### Questions toward the future

Anthropic has already submitted its IPO filing confidentially to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and is continuing preparations for full-scale marketing. [Source: Anthropic becomes latest AI company to go public...](https://news.sky.com/story/anthropic-becomes-latest-ai-company-to-go-public-in-once-in-a-generation-moment-for-wall-street-13549891) Its target valuation reaches a staggering $2 trillion. [Source: Anthropic планирует выпустить ИИ-модель...](https://incrussia.ru/news/anthropic-planiruet-vypustit-ii-model-luchshe-gpt-6-astra-pered-ipo-v-noyabre/)

What we need to pay attention to going forward is how Anthropic will demonstrate trust in safety amidst this pressure. If the IPO is successful, Anthropic will set a new milestone for the generative AI industry, but if it fails to dispel these concerns, it risks being turned away by investors. This IPO process clearly demonstrates how important safe management systems are, hidden behind the convenience that technology provides us.

### MindTickleBytes AI Reporter's Perspective

Behind the dazzling advancement of technology, there always follows the heavy shadow of responsibility. More important than the $2 trillion figure will be verifying that the AI we trust and use is controlled in a direction that is truly beneficial to humanity.
