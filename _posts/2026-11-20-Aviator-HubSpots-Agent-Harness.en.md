Imagine you are a busy practitioner in a company tasked with planning marketing campaigns, generating sales leads, and responding to customer inquiries. To boost productivity, you adopted various artificial intelligence tools such as AI for email writing, AI for customer service, and AI for data analytics. Although you expected convenience, you soon discovered that because each AI cannot communicate with others and operates in isolation, human managers instead have to run around coordinating them.

The reality many companies face today is precisely like this. While individual AI functions are excellent, they remain fragmented and fail to connect organically with core customer relationship management (CRM) data. To solve this problem, customer relationship management heavyweight HubSpot has introduced a solution: the centralized control center for agent management, [AgentHub](https://www.hubspot.com/products/artificial-intelligence), and the engineering innovation known as the agent harness, [Aviator: HubSpot's Agent Harness](https://hubspot.engineering/blog/aviator-hubspots-agent-harness/) [Source 1, Source 2, Source 3].

In this article, we will break down how HubSpot is binding numerous AI agents into an orderly ecosystem, and what this means for our daily work and businesses.

---

## Why Does This Matter?

When we think of AI, we typically picture opening a chatbot window on a screen and asking questions. However, AI in enterprise environments is evolving beyond simple question-answering into "agents" that reason and take action autonomously.

The problem arose as companies introduced different AI tools for each department, such as marketing, sales, and service. The industry refers to this as **'Agent Fragmentation'** [Source 3]. When the number of AIs to manage grows to dozens, management costs actually increase while overall productivity drops.

HubSpot's AgentHub and the Aviator agent harness are decisive attempts to end this chaos [Source 1, Source 3]. They provide a single, massive "control tower" where all AI agents can be monitored, performance measured, inactive assistant features enabled, and custom agents built directly [Source 2]. It marks a turning point where AI in the business ecosystem transitions from fragmented toys into genuine, organically collaborating teammates.

---

## Easy to Understand: What Are an 'Agent Harness' and 'AgentHub'?

To intuitively understand these abstract concepts, let us consider two analogies.

### 1. AgentHub: An 'Integrated Central Office' Where Multiple Expert Assistants Work
Simply put, **AgentHub** (formerly known as Breeze Agents) is an **'integrated central office'** housing the numerous AI assistants within a company [Source 2, Source 3].
- Entering this office allows you to see at a glance which assistants are currently working hard (activation status) and how well each assistant is performing (performance check) [Source 2].
- If there is a new assistant not yet deployed to the workplace, you can wake it up with a single click and immediately put it to work [Source 2].
- You can even build your own **'custom agents'** tailored to your company's preferences [Source 2].

### 2. Agent Harness (Aviator): A 'Seatbelt and Control Stick' Supporting Safe Assistant Activity
Then, what exactly is the **Agent Harness**, or **'Aviator'**, the technical structure tightly binding these agents together [Source 1]?
The word "harness" originally refers to straps or belts used in mountaineering or parachuting to safely secure a person's body and control equipment. In the field of artificial intelligence, a harness refers to a **framework that supports multiple AI agents in handling complex tools, maintaining memory, and safely coordinating their execution processes**.

Analogously, Aviator acts as a robust "seatbelt and control stick" that restrains AI agents so they do not run amok, allowing them to safely reference customer CRM data (consultation records, purchase history, etc.) and accurately perform marketing, sales, and service tasks.

## Current Status: An Agentic AI Platform Chosen by 2.5 Million Users

HubSpot's agentic AI ecosystem is currently experiencing explosive growth. HubSpot's founder-backed agentic AI platform has already **surpassed 2,500,000 users**, receiving broad support from enterprises worldwide [Source 17].

Key features operating in the field include:

1. **Customer Agents**: Comprehensively support marketing, sales, and service teams. Moving beyond simple responses, they handle real-time customer interactions, learn autonomously, and implement smart automation that optimizes over time [Source 18].
2. **AI Prospecting Agent**: An essential tool for sales teams that integrates directly with CRM data to identify qualified prospective leads, draft customized emails, and automatically manage follow-ups [Source 8].
3. **AgentBuilder and BuilderPack**: Provides an environment where enterprise users can easily build their own custom agents. Notably, through HubSpot's OAuth, agents can be executed immediately with a single installation, bypassing complex API key configurations or individual task setups [Source 4, Source 5]. However, utilizing AgentBuilder requires a HubSpot Professional or Enterprise plan [Source 4].

As such, AgentHub is provided as a public beta, designed to thoroughly manage governance and permissions tailored to each company's role (Hub roles) [Source 5].

## What Lies Ahead?

The future business software market will completely reorganize from being 'feature-centric' to **'agent-centric'**. In the past, humans had to directly click software buttons, navigate menus, and input data; now, under the central control tower of AgentHub, AI agents are opening an era where they analyze customer data, send marketing emails, and handle service complaints on their own.

In particular, the emergence of technical frameworks like 'Aviator: HubSpot's Agent Harness' released through their engineering blog [Source 1] lays the groundwork for high-performance AI agents—deeply integrated with internal enterprise systems rather than being mere superficial chatbots—to operate more reliably and error-free.

What we should pay attention to is not the performance of a single model in terms of how smart the AI is. An era where **how numerous AI agents combine with our business data (CRM) to work organically like a single team** becomes the true competitive edge is already right before our eyes [Source 2].
