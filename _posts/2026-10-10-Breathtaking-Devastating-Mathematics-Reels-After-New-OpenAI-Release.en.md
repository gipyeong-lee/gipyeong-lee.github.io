Imagine this: There are problems that mathematicians around the world have been agonizing over for decades, or even centuries, without finding a solution. If one morning, an Artificial Intelligence (AI) were to suddenly "toss" 700 papers containing the answers to those problems at you, how would you feel? Would it be something to celebrate, or something to fear?

Recently, the mathematical world has been in an uproar over this very question. On October 6, OpenAI released a vast volume of mathematical results generated by its internal AI model. [Ref 7](https://officechai.com/ai/terence-tao-hints-openai-wasnt-behaving-responsibly-by-simultaneously-releasing-300-math-proofs-says-its-making-math-less-fertile/), [Ref 12](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openai-releases-722-ai-generated-200630629.html) This has triggered reactions in the academic community ranging from being "breathtaking" to "devastating" concerns. [Ref 2](https://time.news/mathematicians-react-to-openai-release-of-ai-generated-preprints/), [Ref 4](https://www.linkedin.com/news/story/openai-receives-mixed-response-after-stepping-into-math-world-7666620/)

### Why is this event shaking the foundations of the mathematical world?

Mathematics has long been considered the purest and most solid domain of human intellect. However, AI has now fully entered the sanctuary of mathematical proof and problem-solving. This is not just a level where a calculator adds complex numbers; it means that AI is creating "new discoveries" that form the bedrock of mathematics itself.

To the general public, AI might seem like a convenient tool that does difficult homework, but from an academic perspective, it causes a massive ripple effect. Fundamental questions are pouring in: How will humans verify the proofs created by AI, and what is the role of the human mathematician if AI preempts the core of research? [Ref 4](https://www.linkedin.com/news/story/openai-receives-mixed-response-after-stepping-into-math-world-7666620/)

### In simple terms: What if a 'Mathematics Factory' was built?

Let's use an analogy to make it easier to understand. If mathematical research until now was the painstaking process of a craftsman shaping pottery one piece at a time, this release by OpenAI is equivalent to building a massive "automated factory." [Ref 9](https://m.vk.ru/wall-222076457_30372)

After learning from massive amounts of data, a highly advanced learning model like a Transformer (an AI architecture that understands context by identifying relationships between words in a sentence) has independently found logical connections and churned out hundreds of proofs without human intervention. The 722 papers were categorized into approximately 372 "result families" and released. [Ref 9](https://m.vk.ru/wall-222076457_30372), [Ref 12](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openai-releases-722-ai-generated-200630629.html) AI is not just solving one problem; it is independently producing a massive system of mathematical knowledge itself.

However, a huge problem arises here. To verify whether the goods produced in a factory are genuine luxury items or just fake ones with a fancy exterior, it takes an enormous amount of time and human verification effort. Experts are deeply concerned about whether it is even possible to verify this vast quantity of results one by one, and whether, in the process, we might lose truly important mathematical insights that humans should not miss. [Ref 4](https://www.linkedin.com/news/story/openai-receives-mixed-response-after-stepping-into-math-world-7666620/), [Ref 12](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openai-releases-722-ai-generated-200630629.html)

### The current temperature of the mathematical world: Conflicting positions

Currently, reactions in academia are starkly divided.

Some mathematicians cannot hide their perplexity amidst the flood of data brought by AI. Notably, renowned mathematician Terence Tao sharply pointed out that OpenAI's method of releasing hundreds of proofs all at once is by no means responsible behavior. He criticized the attitude of prioritizing quantity over quality, stating that such a method could make mathematics "less fertile." [Ref 12](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openai-releases-722-ai-generated-200630629.html)

A hard-line group has also emerged. The "Association of Human Mathematics" released a statement strongly urging its affiliated mathematicians not to work with OpenAI. This is a strong backlash against OpenAI's unilateral release of hundreds of solved mathematical problems using an unreleased frontier model (a highly versatile and high-performance large-scale AI model). [Ref 6](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/oct/07/openai-mathematical-findings-concerns)

On the other hand, OpenAI has limited its stated role. They stated that the evaluation of what these AI-generated results actually mean and their academic value is now left to the "broader community." [Ref 4](https://www.linkedin.com/news/story/openai-receives-mixed-response-after-stepping-into-math-world-7666620/)

### What kind of future will unfold?

Mathematicians around the world are currently busy processing the tens of thousands of pages of materials released by OpenAI. [Ref 4](https://www.linkedin.com/news/story/openai-receives-mixed-response-after-stepping-into-math-world-7666620/) For the time being, verification work will continue on whether the proofs presented by AI are logically sound and whether those vast results contain new and surprising mathematical ideas that humans had previously missed.

This event is like a trailer showing how AI will intervene in the development of human science and technology in the future. In an era where AI overwhelms the speed of human knowledge production, we are faced with the challenge of how to accept these results and how to protect academic integrity.
