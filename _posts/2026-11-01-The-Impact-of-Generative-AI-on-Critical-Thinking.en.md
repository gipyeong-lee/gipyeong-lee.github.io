Imagine this: You arrive at the office, tell an AI, "Organize today's meeting materials," and within seconds, a perfect draft of the report is ready. We are now able to work much faster and more efficiently than ever before. But what if this convenience is slowly eroding our "power to think"?

According to a recent study by Microsoft Research, warning lights are flashing regarding the critical thinking skills of knowledge workers who utilize generative AI (AI that learns from vast amounts of data to create new text, images, etc.). [Source: Microsoft Research Report](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/lee_2025_ai_critical_thinking_survey.pdf)

### Why Should We Be Careful?

We are currently living in the era of "cognitive outsourcing." When solving complex problems or writing, we seek help from AI instead of racking our brains. However, a major risk hides behind this convenience. It is because this could diminish our core professional capabilities in the long term.

Researchers surveyed 936 real-world cases involving 319 knowledge workers. The results confirmed that excessive reliance on AI can noticeably weaken the critical thinking and practical skills necessary for job performance. [Source: Microsoft Research Report](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/lee_2025_ai_critical_thinking_survey.pdf) [Source: APA Monitor](https://www.apa.org/monitor/2026/07-08/ai-job-skills-thinking)

In simple terms, if the habit of accepting AI-provided answers without verification becomes solidified, you end up in a state where you cannot properly distinguish between facts and opinions. [Source: LinkedIn Post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/niharika-raman-510821173_microsoft-the-impact-of-generative-al-on-activity-7295706278364884992-IdTO) This is a scary warning that goes beyond a mere efficiency issue; it suggests that our perspective on the world itself could become trapped in AI's biases.

### An Analogy: Brain Muscles and Electric Assistive Devices

Critical thinking is like the "muscles" of our brain. Just as you must exercise to build muscle, intellectual ability is cultivated by thinking deeply, doubting, and reasoning logically. Generative AI is like a highly sophisticated "electric assistive device."

When used properly, it maximizes productivity—just as a wheelchair helps someone travel further—but if you stop practicing walking entirely and rely solely on the assistive device, your leg muscles will atrophy.

The study found that the higher the level of trust in AI, the clearer the tendency to reduce the effort to think critically. Conversely, those with higher self-confidence in their own abilities did not abandon their own thought processes while utilizing AI. [Source: Socratic AI Study](https://socraticai.co/studies/lee-2025) In other words, the fate of your intellectual level depends on whether you treat AI as a "master who does your thinking for you" or as a "tool that implements your thoughts."

### The Current Crisis: Reclaiming Agency in Thought

Many workers are saving time through AI, but there are not a few cases where they lose the motivation to think for themselves. [Source: LinkedIn Post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/niharika-raman-510821173_microsoft-the-impact-of-generative-al-on-activity-7295706278364884992-IdTO) While AI creates sentences in the blink of an eye, there is always a risk that they contain logical leaps or factual distortions.

Some experts have even offered sharp criticism, stating, "AI is making us dumber." [Source: Benjamin Ellis Blog](https://benjaminellis.org/2025/02/24/is-ai-making-us-stupid-the-impact-of-generative-ai-on-cognition/) Nevertheless, rejecting the technology is impossible. What matters is changing the way we use it.

### Future Changes: Wise Usage

Experts emphasize the need for strategies from the design stage of AI that make users think deeply. [Source: The Register](https://www.theregister.com/software/2025/02/11/some-workers-are-already-outsourcing-their-brains-to-ai/1094150) Instead of AI showing results immediately, alternative methods could include asking questions or providing time for users to organize their thoughts.

Remember: Those who go through the step of "organizing with my own brain first" before using AI utilize it much better. AI should not be a substitute for your thoughts, but a whetstone that sharpens them. [Source: Thinknote](https://www.thinknote.co.kr/tag/critical-thinking-en/)
