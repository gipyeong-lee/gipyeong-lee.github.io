Imagine this: You arrive at the office on a busy morning and instruct your AI, "Draft the project results report for this week and send it to me via email." Then, you immediately close your laptop and leave for a lunch meeting. Surprisingly, while you are enjoying coffee at a café, the AI is quietly finishing the report using your files and data. You check the progress on your smartphone, and upon returning to the office, you find a neatly organized document waiting for you.

It sounds like magic, but this kind of work environment is on the verge of becoming our everyday reality. AI company Anthropic has announced that it is expanding its AI task-delegation tool, 'Claude Cowork', from its existing desktop app to web and mobile platforms ([Source: Anthropic Official Blog](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-web-mobile)).

## Why is this important?

Until now, AI agents (AI that takes instructions and performs multi-step tasks autonomously) mostly operated only within apps installed on specific computers. In other words, you had to keep your laptop on to assign tasks. With this expansion, users are no longer tied to device constraints ([Source: MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/07/claude-cowork-mobile-web/)).

The biggest change is 'continuity of work.' You can now keep an eye on complex tasks started in the office from your smartphone while on the move, and provide real-time feedback if necessary. This signifies that AI has taken another step forward from being a mere 'conversation partner' to a 'digital assistant' by your side ([Source: TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/claude-cowork-mobile-web-agent-office)).

## Simplified: What is Claude Cowork?

To understand Claude Cowork, let's use the 'professional chef' analogy. If a traditional AI chatbot is an 'advisor' that tells you the recipe, Claude Cowork is a 'professional chef' who enters the kitchen and cooks the meal themselves.

1. **Instruction**: You only need to say, "Cook me a dish using these ingredients."
2. **Execution**: Claude Cowork creates a recipe (task plan), goes into the refrigerator (your files and data), gets the ingredients, and starts cooking.
3. **Progress**: Before this update, the chef was confined to the kitchen (desktop app). Now, even while the chef continues cooking in the kitchen, you can check in real-time via your smartphone screen whether the prep work is done or if the cooking is proceeding well ([Source: Claude Product Page](https://claude.com/product/cowork)).

Thanks to advanced technologies like Transformers (the core AI architecture that grasps relationships between words in sentences), Claude Cowork can handle not only simple repetitive tasks but also complex multi-step tasks that require self-judgment and decision-making ([Source: Anthropic Official Blog](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-web-mobile)).

## Current Status

On July 7, 2026, Anthropic officially announced mobile and web support for Claude Cowork ([Source: Neura Market](https://www.neura.market/news/anthropic-launches-claude-cowork-mobile-web)). This feature is currently in beta and is being provided first to 'Max' subscribers ([Source: 9to5mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/07/anthropic-expanding-claude-cowork-to-mobile-and-web-details-here/)).

An interesting point is how Claude Cowork is actually being used. We often associate AI agents with coding or complex development tasks. However, statistics show that over 90% of Claude Cowork activity is related to mundane business tasks such as report writing, contract review, and content management ([Source: Gagadget](https://gagadget.com/en/717682-claude-cowork-comes-to-mobile-and-web-and-most-users-arent-coding/)). It is strong evidence that many office workers are already utilizing AI like a colleague.

## What comes next?

Claude Cowork is scheduled to open up to more users. Anthropic has stated that it plans to sequentially expand access to users on other subscription plans after starting with Max subscribers ([Source: Neura Market](https://www.neura.market/news/anthropic-launches-claude-cowork-mobile-web)).

We are transitioning from an era where 'time spent sitting at your laptop' was directly tied to work efficiency, to an era where 'how clearly you provide direction to your AI' determines efficiency. In the near future, it will become natural to review and revise meeting materials prepared by your AI assistant on your smartphone while you are out of the office.
