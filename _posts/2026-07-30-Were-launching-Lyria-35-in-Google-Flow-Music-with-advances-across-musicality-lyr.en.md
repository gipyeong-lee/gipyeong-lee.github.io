Imagine this: on your way home from work today, you enter a melody and lyrics that suddenly popped into your head into a smartphone app, and a high-quality song is created in an instant, as if produced by a professional musician in a studio. The era where anyone can create their own music without knowing how to play complex instruments or learning difficult composition software is just around the corner.

Google DeepMind unveiled its next-generation music generation model, 'Lyria 3.5', on July 29, 2026, opening new horizons for music creation. [Source 8](https://www.aichatdaily.com/ai-models/google-deepmind-ships-lyria-3-5-flow-music), [Source 9](https://www.tickrwire.tech/article/deepminds-lyria-35-boosts-music-creativity-in-google-flow)

## Why is AI important for music creation?

Until now, many music generation AIs have appeared, but they mostly stopped at demonstrating the novelty that 'AI can make music'. However, this Lyria 3.5 has gone a step further. It is evolving beyond generating fun demos into a practical partner that actual creators can always keep open next to their workstations. [Source 8](https://www.aichatdaily.com/ai-models/google-deepmind-ships-lyria-3-5-flow-music)

It provides opportunities for the general public to create music filled with their own vibe and emotion without extensive music theory, and it serves as a powerful assistant for professional musicians to gain inspiration or quickly create drafts.

## In simple terms: What has changed with 'Lyria 3.5'?

Metaphorically, Lyria 3.5 is like giving a composer 'smarter and more delicate hands'. This update focused on four main areas: **Musicality, Lyrics, Vocals, and Creative Control.** [Source 8](https://www.aichatdaily.com/ai-models/google-deepmind-ships-lyria-3-5-flow-music), [Source 9](https://www.tickrwire.tech/article/deepminds-lyria-35-boosts-music-creativity-in-google-flow)

*   **Musicality:** Just as a chef enhances flavor with fresh ingredients, the rhythm and arrangement have become much more natural. While the previous model, 'Lyria 3', already learned rhythm and composition through collaboration with musicians, this version 3.5 refines it further to complete much smoother songs. [Source 3](https://deepmind.google/models/lyria/)
*   **Lyrics and Vocals:** While AI-sung songs previously felt somewhat mechanical and stiff, it is now possible to have vocals that feel emotional and more natural lyric delivery. The AI now understands the creator's intended feeling more accurately and captures it in the voice.
*   **Creative Control:** This is the biggest change. While previously you were in the position of simply 'receiving' the results generated by AI, composers can now fine-tune specific parts of the music and lead it in the desired direction, much like a painter adjusting their brush strokes. [Source 8](https://www.aichatdaily.com/ai-models/google-deepmind-ships-lyria-3-5-flow-music)

This process is very similar to completing your own work of art by finely adjusting brightness, contrast, and color temperature rather than just selecting a filter in a photo editing app.

## Experience it now: 'Google Flow Music'

Lyria 3.5 can currently be experienced directly through 'Google Flow Music', Google's generative AI-based music creation platform. [Source 8](https://www.aichatdaily.com/ai-models/google-deepmind-ships-lyria-3-5-flow-music), [Source 9](https://www.tickrwire.tech/article/deepminds-lyria-35-boosts-music-creativity-in-google-flow) Going beyond simple music generation, this platform acts as a comprehensive creative studio that supports remixing existing songs, creating new instruments, and even directing your own music videos. [Source 2](https://www.flowmusic.app/?ref=bunnyy.tools&from-riffusion=true)

## What will the future of music look like?

Technology is rapidly moving from a 'Novelty' to a 'Utility'. In the future, composers will actively use tools like Lyria 3.5 to break down the technical barriers to music creation and focus more on the unique 'creative choices' and 'emotional expression' that only humans can provide. The high barrier that music once had is gradually fading, and we are entering a world where anyone can express their inner self through sound, with Lyria 3.5 at the center.
