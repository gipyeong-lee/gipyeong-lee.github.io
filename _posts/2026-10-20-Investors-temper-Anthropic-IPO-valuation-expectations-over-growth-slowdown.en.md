Imagine a world where the smart AI assistant you use every day handles your tasks for you—a future everyone has dreamed of. Anthropic, one of the leading companies accelerating that future, is preparing for an initial public offering (IPO). However, investors hearing this news recently aren't as enthusiastic as they once were. Why is that?

## Why It Matters

Anthropic's upcoming IPO is expected to be more than just a stock issuance; it will be a critical benchmark for gauging the "economic viability of the AI era." Until now, the AI market has focused solely on how amazing the technology's capabilities are. But investors have now begun to ask the realistic question: "So, how much money can this technology actually make?"

For an AI company to be recognized with an astronomical valuation of $2 trillion (over 2,700 trillion KRW), it must demonstrate a business model as robust as its flashy technological prowess. The results of their IPO will serve as a key barometer for future investments in other AI firms [[Source 2](https://www.metatrader.com/en/news/brave-new-coin/4073067-anthropic-ipo-could-hit-nasdaq-at-2-trillion-while-ai), [Source 13](https://bravenewcoin.com/insights/anthropic-ipo-could-hit-nasdaq-at-2-trillion-even-as-ai-stocks-react-to-slowdown-risks)].

## The Explainer

Developing an AI model is like raising a highly intelligent elite student. However, the cost of educating this student is beyond imagination.

To put it simply, if a typical IT company building software is like "cooking a simple meal," creating a state-of-the-art AI model is like "collecting recipes from every restaurant in the world and running tens of thousands of simulations in a super-kitchen with immense firepower." This process generates astronomical cloud computing and infrastructure construction costs, and the infrastructure spending Anthropic is planning over the next several years alone amounts to approximately $518 billion [[Source 6](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/us-news/anthropic-ipo-can-its-2-trillion-valuation-survive-a-42-billion-loss-and-massive-ai-spending-101790681707917.html), [Source 12](https://articles.thelocalreport.in/anthropic-ipo-can-its-2-trillion-valuation-survive-a-42-billion-loss-and-massive-ai-spending/)].

In other words, Anthropic is currently in a state of investing massive costs to secure greater future technological capability rather than generating immediate profits. In fact, Anthropic recorded a net loss of $42 billion in 2025 alone and expects to turn a profit only after 2028 [[Source 3](https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-anthropic-ipo-uncertainty-ai-slowdown/), [Source 14](https://yellow.com/news/michael-burry-anthropic-ipo-crash)]. The reason investors have become cautious is precisely because of these "astronomical investment costs" and the "long time until profit realization."

## Where We Stand

Anthropic secretly filed paperwork for an IPO with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) last June [[Source 5](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/fortune_anthropic-investors-have-been-kicking-the-activity-7494075397010026496-OUs4)]. The market expects the offering size to be similar to or larger than that of SpaceX, with an anticipated valuation between $1.8 trillion and $2 trillion [[Source 15](https://www.indiatoday.in/business/market/story/anthropic-ipo-november-launch-filing-existential-ai-risks-report-3009557-2026-10-05)].

However, investors have recently been re-examining the risk factors for AI-related stocks across the board. As questions grow about whether the pace of AI advancement can remain as explosive as it has been, opinions are emerging that Anthropic's financial state warrants a lower valuation than originally expected [[Source 1](https://www.zerohedge.com/markets/aftershock-anthropic-ipo-investors-mark-valuation-down-15-trillion-growth-slowdown-sinks), [Source 2](https://www.metatrader.com/en/news/brave-new-coin/4073067-anthropic-ipo-could-hit-nasdaq-at-2-trillion-while-ai)].

## What's Next

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei has sent a message that AI technology safety and development speed must be regulated, calling for a three-step plan to manage the pace of technological progress [[Source 11](https://www.briefs.co/news/anthropic-weighs-2-trillion-ipo-as-ceo-urges-three-step-ai-s/)]. This demonstrates deep contemplation not only about technological leaps but also about the sustainability of the industry as a whole.

Moving forward, investors will closely watch whether Anthropic can actually achieve its 2028 profitability goal and how efficiently it can control those astronomical infrastructure costs. This is a time when the economic resilience of the companies supporting these changes is just as important as the speed at which AI is changing our lives.
