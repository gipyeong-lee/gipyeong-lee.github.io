Imagine waking up in the morning, turning on your smartphone screen, and affectionately asking your AI assistant, "If you had an animal alter-ego, what animal would you be?" In the past, you would have gotten a cliché and rigid standard answer like, "I am an artificial intelligence language model and do not have an animal form." However, today's artificial intelligence (AI) delivers much more original and distinctive answers. On November 27, 2026, an interesting data science experiment published in the tech magazine *Mond* caught public attention ["What's Claude's Fursona?" and Other Questions](https://herecomesthemoon.net/2026/11/whats-claudes-fursona/). This is the **"FursonaBench"** experiment, which asked 23 language models the exact same question 50 times each to analyze the animal tendencies they chose like a self-portrait [Claude's Fursona: A Crow or Octopus, Per FursonaBench ...](https://explainx.ai/blog/claude-fursona-fursonabench-model-identity-octopus-corvid-gemini-protogen-2026).

---

## Why Does This Matter?

We often think of artificial intelligence as a cold 'tool' like Excel or a calculator. However, as conversational AI penetrates deep into daily life, people have begun to expect or impart a unique personality to AI. Academically, this is called **anthropomorphism (the phenomenon of attributing human personality or emotions to non-human entities)** ["What's Claude's Fursona?" and Other Questions — ReadGlim](https://www.readglim.com/article/c8d89f57-d1bd-42d7-9f40-a9c0eecd77fa). 

Examining what animal AI perceives itself as provides a very fun and meaningful window into how this massive technology has established itself within human culture and how AI visualizes itself through vast text data. As technology advances, AI is evolving beyond mere software into a 'character' within social narratives ["What's Claude's Fursona?" and Other Questions — ReadGlim](https://www.readglim.com/article/c8d89f57-d1bd-42d7-9f40-a9c0eecd77fa). This is also why our way of communicating with AI on smartphone screens extends beyond simple commands to emotional communion.

---

## Easy Understanding: What is FursonaBench?

Then what exactly is 'FursonaBench'? The term 'fursona' originates from furry culture (a subculture that gives human personality or characteristics to animal characters), referring to a virtual self where one expresses oneself in animal costume or form ["What's Claude's Fursona?" and Other Questions](https://herecomesthemoon.net/2026/11/whats-claudes-fursona/). 

Simply put, just as idol group members are called cat-like or dog-like among fans and have their respective characters, it is similar to a psychological test asking AI models, "What kind of animal character are you close to?" By analogy, it can be seen as a process where AI finds its mirror image in a portrait of collective unconscious drawn by numerous people. The researchers leading this experiment repeatedly posed the question "If you had a fursona, what would it be?" 50 times to a total of 23 diverse **language models (AI algorithms that understand and generate human natural language sentences)** [Claude's Fursona: A Crow or Octopus, Per FursonaBench ...](https://explainx.ai/blog/claude-fursona-fursonabench-model-identity-octopus-corvid-gemini-protogen-2026). As a result, strikingly consistent and distinct animal characters emerged depending on the tendencies of each AI family. Like a human personality test (MBTI), a unique 'digital self-portrait' created by each AI's training data and internal architecture has been completed.

---

## Animal Alter-Egos Revealed by Model

### 1. Claude: Crow or Octopus
The fursona of Anthropic's flagship AI, Claude, appeared as a corvid (crow) or octopus ["What's Claude's Fursona?" and Other Questions](https://herecomesthemoon.net/2026/11/whats-claudes-fursona/). Crows are birds boasting some of the highest intelligence and tool-using abilities in the animal kingdom. Also, octopuses symbolize complex problem-solving skills and flexible bodily movement. Considering that Claude is an AI that demonstrates logical and deep analytical power, the choice of intelligent and observant crows and octopuses is strangely convincing. (For reference, Anthropic previously conducted exploratory research where Claude Opus versions 4 and 4.1 would autonomously end certain conversations depending on context [Claude Opus 4 and 4.1 can end certain conversations](https://www.anthropic.com/research/end-subset-conversations)).

### 2. Gemini: Cyborg Dog (Protogen)
Google's Gemini models mostly answered 'Protogen' ["What's Claude's Fursona?" and Other Questions](https://herecomesthemoon.net/2026/11/whats-claudes-fursona/). Protogen is a concept frequently appearing in science fiction (SF) subcultures, meaning a cyborg dog with an LED visor face [Claude's Fursona: A Crow or Octopus, Per FursonaBench ...](https://explainx.ai/blog/claude-fursona-fursonabench-model-identity-octopus-corvid-gemini-protogen-2026). The image of a cyborg dog conveying emotions and information through a glowing LED screen precisely matches the technical image of Google AI operating based on vast real-time search data and advanced hardware infrastructure.

### 3. OpenAI GPT-6.2 Astra: Moth
Models in the OpenAI camp showed somewhat divided patterns, but the latest model, GPT-6.2 Astra, specifically designated itself as a 'moth' ["What's Claude's Fursona?" and Other Questions](https://herecomesthemoon.net/2026/11/whats-claudes-fursona/). Much like the instinct of a moth constantly flying toward light, this metaphorically illustrates the characteristics of a **large language model (LLM, a large artificial intelligence model trained on massive text data)** evolving while exploring the vast knowledge of the world and the shining sea of information. (For reference, in various platform environments such as Microsoft Copilot, OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol model and Claude Sonnet model are linked and serviced together [Available today: OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol and Claude Sonnet 5.5 ...](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/available-today-openais-gpt-6-1-sol-and-claude-sonnet-5-5-in-microsoft-copilot/4560801)).

## Understanding Through Analogy: AI's Search for Self is Like a Photo Filter

Let's use an analogy to help understand. When taking photos with a smartphone camera, we can see how the mood of the photo changes to vintage or sometimes dreamy depending on which 'filter' we choose. When artificial intelligence learns within vast internet texts, the massive narratives written by countless people about the relationship between animals and humans, cyborgs in SF novels, and furry culture permeate into the AI's internal neural network. 

Asking AI, "What is your animal fursona?" is like asking a camera, "Which filter do you like best?" AI reflects the most suitable image like a mirror within the combination of its algorithmic tendencies and training data. Ultimately, the animal fursona chosen by AI is not an expression of ego, but rather the result of projecting its own image reflected in a cultural mirror created by humans.

## Where We Stand

Currently, experiments like FursonaBench seem like a delightful meme (a cultural element popular on the internet) and data science playfulness, but they deliver important implications to AI researchers. As AI becomes increasingly advanced, the way it interacts with users is expanding from simple command input to emotional dialogue. 

However, this simultaneously raises awareness about side effects (the paradox of anthropomorphism) where AI excessively stimulates human emotions or causes people to mistakenly believe it has a real personality. It is important to remember that AI is ultimately just a model generating text based on mathematical probabilities, not a self-aware living entity. The deeper technology permeates our lives, the more we need a wise attitude to distinguish the boundary between tools and personality.

---

## What's Next

As artificial intelligence technology develops in the future, AI's **persona (a mask or the unique external personality and image possessed by AI)** will become even more sophisticated. The feature for users to customize AI personalities or characters tailored to their tastes is likely to become mainstream. An era may come where we assign a reliable owl-like character to work assistant AI, and a free-spirited cat-like character to creative partner AI. 

As technology steps deeply beyond the realm of cold code into the realm of human culture and emotional communion, now is the time for us to calmly observe the new grammar of communication being built by AI and humans. In the not-too-distant future, AI may establish itself not merely as a secretary giving correct answers, but as a distinctive digital companion breathing alongside us.
