Imagine this: you assign a complex computer task to your usual AI assistant, and it processes it much faster and more accurately than before. What if it were even cheaper to use, too? At the end of October, OpenAI introduced a new artificial intelligence model that makes these changes possible: **GPT-6.1 Terra**. [OpenAI presenta GPT-6.1 Terra: più velocità e costi dimezzati](https://www.ilsoftware.it/openai-presenta-gpt-6-1-terra-piu-velocita-e-cose-dimezzati/) Let's take a look at how this new model will change our daily lives.

### Why does this matter?

For the everyday user, the most welcome news is "efficiency." Even using the same package, the new model can perform more tasks faster than the previous generation, GPT-5.6 Terra. [OpenAI je predstavio GPT-6.1 Terra: jeftiniji API i jače upravljanje...](https://zg2000fs.github.io/techpuls/c/6886/) This means the response speed of the AI services we use daily is improved, and even more complex requirements can be handled easily. It has become possible not just to be smarter, but to enjoy the capabilities of AI at a much more economical cost.

### Easy Understanding: Polishing AI's "Fundamentals"

Simply put, GPT-6.1 Terra has been reborn as a "more capable and reliable professional." Here, "capable" means that its ability to understand instructions—professionally known as "alignment" (the AI's ability to act according to the user's intent)—has significantly improved. For example, even if we give the AI somewhat vague instructions, this model understands the user's intent more accurately and executes it. [GPT-6.1 Terra include le stesse protezioni di GPT-6 Astra.](https://www.punto-informatico.it/openai-gpt-6-1-terra-nuovo-modello-chatgpt-codex/)

The "reliable" aspect is well-demonstrated in its security. Recently, "prompt injection attacks," which trick AI models into giving strange answers, have become a problem, but this model is equipped with much stronger defenses than the previous generation. [GPT-6.1 Terra include le stesse protezioni di GPT-6 Astra.](https://www.punto-informatico.it/openai-gpt-6-1-terra-nuovo-modello-chatgpt-codex/) Metaphorically speaking, it's like having a seasoned bodyguard by your side, a structure that tightly keeps the AI from veering off in the wrong direction.

### Current Situation: Where does it excel?

Rather than being a universal encyclopedia, GPT-6.1 Terra is closer to a "problem solver that handles specialized tasks amazingly well." It particularly stands out in the following fields:

*   **Visual Analysis and Computer Use**: It excels in the ability to see and understand, as well as the ability to perform tasks within a computer screen. [GPT-6.1 Terra | Simon Willison’s Weblog](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/26/gpt-61-terra/)
*   **Programming and Mathematics**: It is highly skilled at writing complex code or solving math problems. In fact, it proved its strong capabilities by recording 73 points in a mathematics benchmark test. [GPT-6.1 Terra benchmarks & price: $2 per 1M tokens blended](https://www.benchleader.com/models/gpt-6-1-terra/)

On the other hand, it showed a relatively lower score of 63 in the general knowledge field. [GPT-6.1 Terra benchmarks & price: $2 per 1M tokens blended](https://www.benchleader.com/models/gpt-6-1-terra/) This means it has focused on its role as a practical productivity tool rather than showing off all its knowledge.

### What happens next?

Currently, this model is actively being utilized in services like ChatGPT Work and Codex, and anyone can use it through the API (Application Programming Interface, a communication method between programs) at the reasonable price of $1.00/1M tokens. [GPT-6.1 Terra API — $1.00/1M in · Token Harbor](https://tokenharbor.ai/models/gpt-6.1-terra/)

Going forward, AI will evolve faster into "practical agents" that go beyond simply listing information to directly controlling our computers and helping with complex programming tasks in real-time. We will move beyond the stage of just talking to AI to living in an era where we assign tasks to AI and verify the results.
