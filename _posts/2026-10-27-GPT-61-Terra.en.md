Imagine this: You sit at your office chair in the morning and turn on your computer. You tell your AI assistant, "Draft a report based on the meeting screenshots and documents I organized yesterday." The AI then opens the folders on your computer screen, reads the content of the screenshots, pulls up the document files, and naturally completes the report sentences.

Scenes like this are no longer just stories from movies. This is because the recently announced **GPT-6.1 Terra** from OpenAI was born to perform the role of such an 'Agent' (AI that performs complex tasks on behalf of the user).

### Why is this important?

The AI we have known until now was closer to a 'counselor' role that mainly answers questions. But now, AI has started to 'work' with us. GPT-6.1 Terra has the ability to go beyond simply writing text and can understand and analyze computer screens.

The reason this is important is the dimension of 'productivity.' AI can now directly watch the screen and handle tedious tasks that previously required users to manually copy and paste or constantly switch between multiple apps. Thanks to faster speeds and cost-efficiency, both companies and individuals can afford to have a high-level AI work assistant by their side. [Source: GPT-6.1 Terra is now in orbit - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tTuk4pGnT0A)

### Easy to understand

**1. Eyes that see the computer, 'Visual Understanding'**
To put it simply, GPT-6.1 Terra can be likened to a 'smart intern who knows how to freely use all apps.' If previous models were like librarians who could only read text, this model is like an intern who can look directly at the user's monitor and knows how to move the mouse. [Source: OpenAI представила GPT-6.1 Terra: быстрее работает... / Хабр](https://habr.com/ru/companies/otus/news/1099780/)

**2. A brain that remembers 1 million pieces, 'Context Window'**
This AI can process 1 million tokens (the unit of data the AI understands, usually corresponding to a few words) of information at once. For example, it means it can put material equivalent to dozens of thick books into its head at once, grasp the complex relationships between them, and answer questions or write code. [Source: GPT-6.1 Terra (max) - Intelligence, Performance... | Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gpt-6-1-terra)

**3. 'Accuracy' that reduces mistakes**
The chronic problem of AI, 'Hallucination' (a phenomenon where AI speaks non-factual content as if it were true), has also been significantly improved. When measured with AA-Omniscience, an index that measures the intelligence of AI models, GPT-6.1 Terra saw its hallucination rate drop by a whopping 50 points and its accuracy increase by 2 points compared to the previous model. In other words, it has not only become smarter but also much more reliable. [Source: GPT-6.1 Terra pushes the cost efficiency frontier | Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gpt-6-1-terra-pushes-the-cost-efficiency-frontier)

### Current Situation

GPT-6.1 Terra belongs to the mid-tier of OpenAI's new 6.1 series models. While it is cheaper than the top-tier model, GPT-6.1 Sol, it is optimized for daily coding, writing, and work automation. It shows particular strength in understanding screen content and working across various apps. [Source: GPT-6.1 Terra - API Pricing & Providers | OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/openai/gpt-6.1-terra)

It can receive and analyze images as well as text, making it possible to show it a design draft or a screen containing a complex chart and ask questions. [Source: GPT-6.1 Terra (max) - Intelligence, Performance... | Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gpt-6-1-terra)

### What will happen in the future?

Moving forward, AI will rapidly evolve from a 'being that gives answers' to a 'secretary that does everything I do on the computer for me.' As models like GPT-6.1 Terra become popularized, we will be freed from repetitive and boring tasks and be able to spend time on much more creative work.

Users will be able to control how AI watches the screen and acts in a more intuitive way, and AI will develop to the level of preparing necessary files in advance even before the user asks. The tool we knew as 'technology' is now becoming our reliable 'work colleague.'

## MindTickleBytes AI Reporter's Perspective
GPT-6.1 Terra shows that we have entered the era of the practical assistant, which reduces actual work hours for people, rather than just being a grandiose research model. Depending on how well we utilize AI as an 'work partner,' the future landscape of work will change completely.
