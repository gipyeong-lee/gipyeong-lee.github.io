Imagine this: You wake up in the morning, turn on your computer, and say to the AI, "Go through the emails that arrived yesterday, summarize the key schedules, and save the related documents to my folder." These are cumbersome tasks that previously required a person to open windows, copy content, and move files manually. Now, a smart AI model that can perform this complex 'computer operation' on its own has arrived to us in a more economical form.

On October 26, 2026, OpenAI unveiled a new model, 'GPT-6.1 Terra' [Source 2, Source 5]. Although it may not be the highest-spec model, it is a practical model expected to show dazzling efficiency in the work environments we use every day.

### Why is this important?

High-performance AI models we have used until now were very smart, but also quite expensive. In particular, the cost burden was significant to fully utilize 'Agent' functions that read and understand complex screens or perform tasks across multiple apps.

GPT-6.1 Terra targeted exactly this point. It keeps performance solid while drastically lowering usage costs, opening a path for companies or individuals to utilize 'AI that works on its own' more without high cost burdens [Source 6, Source 18]. This is a significant change that accelerates the time when AI goes beyond a tool that simply writes text and becomes a 'competent worker' that actually acts as our hands and feet to perform practical tasks inside a computer [Source 6, Source 11].

### Easy to Understand: The AI Reader and the Skilled Intern

To understand GPT-6.1 Terra, I will use two metaphors.

The first is a **'bespectacled reader'**. This model has excellent 'Visual Reasoning' capabilities to instantly read what appears on the screen, what figures are written on technical drawings, or how complex app configurations are structured [Source 6, Source 7]. It's like a reader wearing glasses who meticulously reads even the smallest text.

The second is a **'skilled intern'**. Just as a new employee opens windows and moves data as instructed by a senior when learning the job, GPT-6.1 Terra directly performs 'Actions' (Computer Use) such as clicking the mouse and entering information while looking at the computer screen [Source 6, Source 18]. If the top-tier model 'GPT-6 Astra' handles very complex and creative philosophical thinking, GPT-6.1 Terra is a 'capable intern' that quickly and cheaply handles repetitive and practical tasks that make up 80% of our work [Source 15, Source 19].

### Current Situation: Harmony of Performance and Safety

OpenAI is positioning GPT-6.1 Terra as a mid-tier model [Source 12, Source 18]. Many users have already started testing it in practice, and the reaction is dominated by evaluations that it is "very practical" [Source 5].

Particularly interesting is OpenAI's thorough safety management. GPT-6.1 Terra is evaluated as 'High' or 'Critical' for potential risks in cybersecurity, biology, and chemistry, and is subject to the same powerful safety stack as the top-tier model 'GPT-6 Astra' [Source 4]. It is a safe and reliable model, far from the saying "cheap things are no good."

Performance figures are also surprising. In tasks requiring mid-level reasoning capabilities, it scored 15% higher than the previous model, GPT-5.6 Terra, while reducing costs to about one-third [Source 19]. Even when compared to the top-tier model GPT-6 Astra, it achieves comparable results in simple computer tasks while costing only one-tenth, so it is unrivaled in terms of efficiency [Source 19].

### What will happen in the future?

In the future, it is highly likely that we will encounter 'AI that controls my computer for me' at a much cheaper price [Source 6, Source 18]. For example, tasks such as looking at numerous product photos and automatically entering catalog information, or analyzing complex technical drawings to create a list of necessary parts will be easily implemented even at the individual user level [Source 7].

The moment "features that were too expensive to even attempt" turns into "with this price, I can keep it on all day," the utilization of AI agents will explode. Now, it is time to watch how GPT-6.1 Terra, a smart intern who has entered your computer, will return your precious work time.
