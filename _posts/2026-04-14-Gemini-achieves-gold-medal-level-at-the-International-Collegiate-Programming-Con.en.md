The problems presented in this contest do not simply test the ability to type code quickly. They require solving problems that can only be answered by utilizing high-level mathematical principles and complex algorithms (logical procedures or methods for solving problems) to find solutions that no one has ever taught them before.

Gemini achieving a gold-medal-level performance in this grueling competition means that AI's ability to **'reason deeply'** and **'logically solve abstract challenges'** like a human has reached a stage of completion [Gemini achieves gold-medal level at the International Collegiate ...](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-achieves-gold-medal-level-at-the-international-collegiate-programming-contest-world-finals/).
