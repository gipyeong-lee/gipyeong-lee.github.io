## Current situation: A 'Beta Service' for everyone with safety measures

New technology always comes with responsibility. Google has introduced strong safety measures to prevent AI-generated music from being used as fake news or causing copyright issues.

*   **Invisible Identification Mark, SynthID:** All music created by Gemini has **SynthID**, a digital watermark (identification mark) that is completely inaudible to the human ear. [A new way to express yourself: Gemini can now create music](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/lyria-3/). It's a 'digital fingerprint' that doesn't degrade the quality of the song at all, but clearly shows that it was created by Google AI when checked with a dedicated tool later.
*   **Where and how to use it?:** This feature can currently be found through the **'Create music'** button added within the Gemini app. [Gemini now lets you generate AI music for free - MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/technology/gemini-now-lets-you-generate-ai-music-for-free/ar-AA1WDKdQ).
*   **Available for free trial right now:** This feature is currently being provided in a 'Beta' version, which is a stage before official release, and any Gemini app user can try it for free.
