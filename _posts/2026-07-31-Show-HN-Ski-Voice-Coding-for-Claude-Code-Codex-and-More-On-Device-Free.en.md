## What's Next?

The emergence of voice coding tools like SKI will bring significant changes to development culture. Moving away from typing and staring at screens for coding, a more immersive and natural interaction may become possible. In the future, AI agents' comprehension and voice recognition technology could become much more sophisticated, accurately grasping even complex and subtle developer intentions and actively suggesting ideas like a skilled mentor.
