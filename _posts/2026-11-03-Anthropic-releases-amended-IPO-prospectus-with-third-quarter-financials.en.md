## A New Giant of the AI Era Is Coming Our Way

Imagine this: behind the smart artificial intelligence (AI) services we use every day, thousands of engineers and massive server farms are working tirelessly. Anthropic, a giant AI company that has captured global attention, is nearing an initial public offering (IPO)—the act of listing on the stock market—which is expected to be one of the largest in history.

Anthropic is famous as the developer of the AI chatbot "Claude." The company recently released an amended IPO prospectus, which included the third-quarter financial results that investors have been anxiously awaiting. Let's look at what this news means for us and why it is garnering such significant attention.

## Why Does This Matter to Us?

This is not just news about a single company listing on the stock market. This IPO is a benchmark that clearly demonstrates how deeply artificial intelligence technology has penetrated our economy and daily lives.

Anthropic is valued at up to $2 trillion (over 2,700 trillion KRW). [[Source 6](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/anthropic-unveils-ipo-filing-showing-140626541.html)] This is expected to be one of the largest debuts in technology industry history. [[Source 2](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/science-technology/anthropic-files-for-historic-ipo-targeting-valuation-of-up-to-175t/4101384)] If you are interested in the future of AI or tech stocks, this listing is perhaps the most important event for assessing the current state of the AI ecosystem.

## Simplified: The 'Growing Pains' and 'Fuel Costs' of AI

Anthropic's current situation can be compared to a "supercar racing at high speed."

1. **Staggering Revenue (Supercar Speed):** Anthropic's revenue surged by a whopping 1,050% throughout 2025. [[Source 15](https://www.fortuneindia.com/technology/anthropic-ipo-prospectus-revenue-jumps-1050-but-posts-42-billion-loss-518-billion-infrastructure-commitments-and-ai-risks/161705)] It is showing explosive growth, moving far beyond simply selling well. Recording approximately $17 billion (about 23 trillion KRW) in revenue in Q3 of 2026 alone means this supercar is currently moving at incredible speed. [[Source 11](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/02/anthropic-ant-s-1-quarter-financials.html)]

2. **Astronomical Infrastructure Costs (Supercar Fuel Costs):** But the faster a supercar goes, the more fuel it consumes, right? Anthropic has committed to spending a massive $518 billion for future cloud computing and infrastructure development. [[Source 12](https://www.investmentnews.com/equities/anthropics-landmark-ipo-filing-shows-12-fold-revenue-jump-518b-compute-bill/268391)] It is similar to a situation where fuel costs increase to an unsustainable level as one tries to make a car faster. As a result, the company recorded a loss of $42 billion in 2025. [[Source 15](https://www.fortuneindia.com/technology/anthropic-ipo-prospectus-revenue-jumps-1050-but-posts-42-billion-loss-518-billion-infrastructure-commitments-and-ai-risks/161705)]

While growth is fast, the costs are equally massive—this is the reality of the current AI industry.

## Current Status: How Long Until the Listing?

Anthropic is aiming to begin its formal IPO marketing during the week of November 9th and complete the listing before Thanksgiving. [[Source 5](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-targets-pre-thanksgiving-ipo-at-2-trillion-valuation/)]

Of course, there are certainly concerns among investors. Updating IPO documents is a standard process before listing, [[Source 8](https://www.zerohedge.com/markets/very-intentional-steve-eisman-thinks-anthropic-hiding-something)] but some are closely watching how the stock price will perform after listing, citing the "existential risks that AI could pose to humanity" that Anthropic warns of itself, or the issue of massive infrastructure costs. [[Source 15](https://www.fortuneindia.com/technology/anthropic-ipo-prospectus-revenue-jumps-1050-but-posts-42-billion-loss-518-billion-infrastructure-commitments-and-ai-risks/161705)]

## What Should We Watch Moving Forward?

Anthropic's future path will serve as a "benchmark" for other AI companies. The core questions are how Anthropic can convert its massive investment costs into efficient revenue and how it can successfully manage both technological advancement and safety. I hope readers will watch the quarterly report cards Anthropic releases to see how much tangible value AI services create in our lives.

## The Future of Anthropic Through the Eyes of AI

Anthropic's record-breaking revenue growth has proven to the market that AI is not just a fleeting trend but a "profitable industry." However, given the astronomical infrastructure costs supporting that growth, it is a time when both investors and engineers must consider a more cautious and responsible approach for a sustainable future of AI.
