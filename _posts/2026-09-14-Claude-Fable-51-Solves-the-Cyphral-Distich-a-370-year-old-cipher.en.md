Recently, however, this long-standing riddle was solved in just 44 minutes. The protagonist isn't a human, but 'Claude Fable 5.1,' a new AI model recently introduced by Anthropic. [Source 3](https://vgtimes.com/tech-and-hardware/166170-claude-fable-5.1-在仅仅-44-分钟内破解了一个-373-年未解的密码.html)

## Understanding it simply

This riddle, known as the 'Cyphral Distich' (a cipher consisting of 64 numbers left behind by 17th-century Scottish author Thomas Urquhart), first appeared in 1653. [Source 1](https://www.vals.ai/blogs/fable-solves-cyphral-distich) Simply put, this cipher is composed of two lines of 32 numbers each, totaling 64 puzzle pieces. [Source 12](https://www.crazyalchemist.com/news/claude-deciphers-17th-century-ciphers-in-urquhart-s-treatises/)

Metaphorically, it's like putting together a giant puzzle box with tens of thousands of pieces without an instruction manual. Humans tire easily, and checking every possible combination takes an enormous amount of time.

AI is different. Claude Fable 5.1, which performed this feat, processed 176,000 'tokens' (data units AI uses to understand sentences) in an instant. [Source 2](https://securityonline.info/claude-fable-decrypts-cyphral-distich/) It tirelessly tested tens of thousands of rules to find the correct answer. Renowned cryptographer Bruce Schneier noted that the AI demonstrated overwhelming efficiency compared to humans in searching through and testing vast amounts of information. [Source 6](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/09/claude-fable-solves-a-historical-cipher.html)

## Where do we stand?

So, what was the answer found by Claude Fable 5.1? Hidden behind the numbers that had been shrouded in mystery for centuries was a 'prayer by Royalists for King Charles II.' [Source 7](https://www.aroged.com/2026/09/02/claudes-fable-5-1-model-cracked-the-code-that-cryptographers-had-been-trying-to-crack-since-1653-in-44-minutes/)

This decryption result was verified by Vals AI, a company specializing in AI evaluation. [Source 13](https://el.kz/en/anthropics-ai-solves-373-year-old-cipher-in-44-minutes_400052335/) People had spent over 370 years trying to solve this problem, but in the end, cutting-edge AI technology provided the final answer.
