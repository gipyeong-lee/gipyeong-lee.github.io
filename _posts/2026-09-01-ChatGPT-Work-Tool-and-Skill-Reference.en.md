Imagine this: You sit in your office chair as usual and tell the AI, "Organize this week's project meeting materials and email them to the team." In the past, the AI would have stopped at summarizing the materials or drafting the email for you. But now, the situation has changed completely. The era has arrived where AI can open files, analyze data, and carry out the actual email sending process itself. This is the future of our work as depicted by OpenAI's newly introduced "ChatGPT Work."

## Why is this important?

Until now, the ChatGPT we have used was primarily a "conversational tool" for asking questions or getting ideas. However, the work humans do involves much more than just asking and answering. The newly launched "ChatGPT Work" signifies that AI has evolved from an "assistant" that only helps with words into a "colleague" that handles practical work directly. [OpenAI launches ChatGPT Work, an agent built to finish the job](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-chatgpt-work-agent-launch)

This mode allows us to delegate tedious daily repetitive tasks to the AI. Thanks to this, we can secure valuable time to focus on more creative and strategic thinking. However, because this feature requires high computational power and significant energy, it is currently provided only to paid subscribers of $20 or more per month. [Understanding ChatGPT Work | Simon Willison’s Weblog](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/30/understanding-chatgpt-work/), [ChatGPT Now Has a 'Work Mode'? How to Work Smarter with Intelligent AI](https://gipyeong-lee.github.io/2026/08/31/Understanding-ChatGPT-Work/)

## Easy to understand

Let's approach the concept of "ChatGPT Work" more easily. Think of the "auto-filters" in the photo editing apps we often use, or "macro" features that handle complex inputs at once.

If the existing "Chat" mode, where we chat and exchange opinions with humans, is a "conference room," then "Work" mode is a "workspace" where the AI opens apps itself, manipulates windows, and completes tasks to achieve a defined goal. [ChatGPT: Review of GPT-5.6 and the new Work mode | Zen](https://dzen.ru/a/anwwWX_ITWFP6Cee), [New GPT-5.6, GPT-Live, and ChatGPT Work released: what... / Habr](https://habr.ru/ru/companies/bothub/articles/1057664/)

Furthermore, the "Skills" feature is like recording recipes. For example, suppose you have to organize report data through a complex process every time. If you create this process as a "Skill" just once, the next time similar material comes in, the AI will pull out that "skill" we taught it earlier to automatically complete the work. It's similar to filling up a toolbox we cherish with useful tools one by one. [OpenAI launches ChatGPT Skills for Reusable ... - LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/openai-launches-chatgpt-skills-reusable-workflows-gpt-54-jon-goodey-t5nke), [I Tried ChatGPT’s “Skills” Feature. It Saved Me Hours Of ...](https://www.forbes.com/sites/rachelwells/2026/07/20/i-tried-chatgpts-skills-feature-it-saved-me-hours-of-boring-work/)

## Current Status

ChatGPT Work, which began official service on July 9, 2026, operates based on the latest GPT-5.6 model. [What Is ChatGPT Work? OpenAI's New Agent Mode](https://felloai.com/chatgpt-work/) Users can freely switch between the conversational "Chat" mode and the practical "Work" mode using the tab selector at the top of the screen. [Understanding ChatGPT Work | Simon Willison’s Weblog](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/30/understanding-chatgpt-work/)

In particular, by utilizing the Scheduled tasks feature, you can have the AI perform tasks itself at specific times or according to events. However, note that while you can upload files or utilize connected tools freely for scheduled tasks, a separate local folder or worktree maintaining the connection state between tasks is not created. [Run tasks on a schedule or from supported app events in ChatGPT](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/automations?surface=app)

## What will happen in the future?

Agent technologies like ChatGPT Work will become much smarter and more sophisticated than they are now. We are currently at the first stage of simply connecting tools and apps, but it won't be long before AI becomes a "fully autonomous colleague" that collects data overnight while we sleep, writes clean analysis reports, and prepares necessary emails by the time we arrive at work in the morning.

Why not consider which parts of your daily repetitive work processes you can turn into your own "Skills" toolbox? That will be the first step toward a wonderful future of working with AI.
