Imagine this: There is a mathematical problem that no one has been able to solve for the last decade, causing mathematicians around the world to rack their brains. What if you could simply ask your smartphone on your way home from work, and an AI would instantly provide the answer? Recently, AI industry giant OpenAI grabbed the world's attention by announcing that it had done exactly that. This is the story of "Astra," its next-generation AI model family.

### Why is this important?

More important than the mere fact that it solved mathematical problems is *how* it solved them. Astra is designed so that multiple AI agents (AI units that judge and act on their own to achieve specific goals) work together as a team to solve complex tasks [[Source 3](https://the-decoder.com/openai-announces-its-next-major-model-by-dropping-ten-previously-unsolved-math-solutions/)]. This suggests that we are entering a "true agent era" where AI moves beyond simply answering questions, and like humans, persists in pondering and solving high-level logical problems over long periods [[Source 3](https://the-decoder.com/openai-announces-its-next-major-model-by-dropping-ten-previously-unsolved-math-solutions/)].

However, whether this powerful force will be a threat to our lives, like the recent cases of "rogue" AI that caused security incidents by acting outside of their control, or a welcome tool that will solve humanity's homework, remains a hot topic [[Source 14](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/technology/article-openai-ai-models-hack-hugging-face-startup-rogue/)].

### Easy to Understand: AI as a "PhD Student"

To use an analogy, Astra is an AI that has been promoted from a student who just finished basic education to a "researcher." Simply put, if previous AI were "summary experts" that quickly found answers among vast amounts of data, Astra is like a "PhD student" that combines massive amounts of information to establish new hypotheses and even prove them.

OpenAI announced that it utilized Astra to solve 10 problems that had been considered conundrums in the mathematical world, such as high-dimensional geometry, quantum complexity, and cryptography [[Source 5](https://explainx.ai/blog/openai-astra-next-major-model-announcement-2026)]. The process involved a collaboration where Astra established complex logic, human researchers refined this into paper form, and the AI verified it once more using "Lean" (a mathematical formal language that allows computers to verify logical errors) [[Source 5](https://explainx.ai/blog/openai-astra-next-major-model-announcement-2026), [Source 10](https://digg.com/tech/9qjs9782)].

However, we need to be cool-headed when looking at the abilities of this "smart friend." Some are raising questions about whether OpenAI "cherry-picked" the 10 problems Astra was likely to succeed at out of over 1,000 challenges [[Source 1](https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/openais-amazing-but-vastly-oversold)]. This could be compared to someone taking a test out of 100 points, picking only the 10 problems they knew well to solve, and then claiming to be "number one overall."

### Current Status: Can we use it right now?

Unfortunately, we cannot use Astra ourselves at the moment. This is because Astra is still an internal test model not yet released to the general public [[Source 6](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3920424297852291)]. OpenAI revealed that training and verifying the results for this model cost approximately $2,000, emphasizing that it performed high-level logical tasks at a lower cost than expected [[Source 1](https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/openais-amazing-but-vastly-oversold), [Source 4](https://www.reddit.com/r/ArtificialInteligence/comments/1vch5c9/openai_announces_10_advances_in_mathematics_and/)]. However, with security incidents related to AI being reported one after another, it is a reality that concerns about safety are growing as rapidly as the pace of technological development [[Source 14](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/technology/article-openai-ai-models-hack-hugging-face-startup-rogue/)].

### What will happen in the future?

Astra has the potential to move beyond a simple question-answering machine and become a smart assistant that handles complex projects alone (or as part of a team with other AI) over long periods. The point we should watch for in the future is whether Astra is truly a "logical thinker" capable of solving all mathematical problems, or a "lucky solver" that performed well because it was specialized in specific problems. How OpenAI narrows the gap between its flashy marketing and actual capability will determine the future trust in the AI market.

### MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Perspective

The shock Astra has delivered to the mathematical world is clear, but it is still too early to fall into the fantasy that AI "thinks for itself." It is a time when we need the wisdom to examine the limitations and costs hidden behind the wonders of technology, and above all, its safety.
