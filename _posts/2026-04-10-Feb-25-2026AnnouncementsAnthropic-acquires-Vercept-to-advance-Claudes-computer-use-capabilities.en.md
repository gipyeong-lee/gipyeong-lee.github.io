## AI Begins to Fully Understand the Human Interface

On February 25, 2026 (local time), Anthropic, a leader in global AI research and development, announced its acquisition of Vercept, a promising AI startup based in Seattle [Anthropic acquires Vercept to advance Claude's computer use...](https://www.anthropic.com/news/acquires-vercept). This acquisition is interpreted as a strategic move to drastically upgrade the 'computer use' capabilities of Anthropic's flagship model, Claude, and the industry evaluates it as the official beginning of the AI agent era [Anthropic acquires Vercept to advance Claude's computer use capabilities - GIGAZINE](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260226-anthropic-acquires-vercept/).

Vercept is a company specializing in vision-based computer perception and automation technology. It possesses unique technology that helps AI see the screen in real-time and perform complex tasks by precisely manipulating the mouse and keyboard like a human [Anthropic Acquires Vercept to Boost Claude's Computer ...](https://mlq.ai/news/anthropic-acquires-vercept-to-boost-claudes-computer-interaction-features/). Through this merger, Anthropic plans to strengthen Claude's ability to handle multi-step tasks more seamlessly within actual business software and overcome complex interface challenges that could not be solved with text-based code alone [Anthropic acquires Vercept to advance Claude's computer use...](https://www.anthropic.com/news/acquires-vercept).
