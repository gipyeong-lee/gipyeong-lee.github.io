Imagine if the voice assistant on the smartphone you use every day suddenly became significantly smarter and started handling complex tasks for you with ease. Anthropic, the developer of the AI model 'Claude,' is one of the companies building that very future. However, interesting shifts in sentiment have recently been detected among Wall Street and investors regarding the company's upcoming listing. What is happening as Anthropic prepares for its historic IPO and undergoes a corporate valuation reassessment?

### Why does this matter?

This news carries more weight than just a standard corporate listing. As a leader in the AI industry, Anthropic began its journey onto Wall Street by submitting confidential documents for an initial public offering (IPO) to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) on June 1, 2026 [Source 3, Source 6]. Because Anthropic is one of the most closely watched companies in AI model development, their listing will serve as a sort of "litmus test" to show how much economic value AI technology can actually be recognized for in the real market [Source 9]. For retail investors, it is also a vital indicator for gauging the gap between the "AI bubble" narrative and tangible technological growth.

### Understanding the situation

To understand Anthropic's valuation reassessment, let's use a metaphor. Imagine buying a painting by a famous artist. At first, people get excited just by the artist's reputation and think, "This painting is worth $1.5 million!" However, as time passes and market conditions become unstable, people think more calmly: "How many works is this artist actually finishing and selling consistently every month?" They then adjust the price to $1.2 million or $1.3 million.

Anthropic's current situation is similar. Investors initially expected Anthropic's corporate valuation to climb to $1.5 trillion [Source 11]. However, as the prices of AI-related stocks have recently shown volatility, investors have begun to look at the company with a more conservative eye [Source 11, Source 17]. Instead of the rosy $1.5 trillion outlook, the opinion that $1.2 trillion to $1.3 trillion is an appropriate valuation is gaining traction [Source 17, Source 18]. This does not mean the company has worsened, but rather that the market has begun to demand more concrete and realistic performance from AI companies.

### Where do we stand?

On May 28 of this year, Anthropic raised $65 billion in Series H funding, achieving a massive valuation of $965 billion [Source 6]. And during the week of October 30, they released the S-1 filing, a preliminary prospectus for their listing [Source 12].

However, the November IPO plan currently looks somewhat uncertain [Source 16]. Investors are demanding concrete figures on how much revenue Anthropic actually generated in October, and there are moves to delay the listing until this information is provided [Source 16]. Some analysts are even suggesting a more rigorous assessment of Anthropic, valuing the company closer to the $1 trillion level [Source 20]. Compared to when some investors modeled the company's value up to $3 trillion, expecting revenue of $200 billion by 2028, one can see how cautious the market sentiment has become [Source 7].

### What is the next step?

What we need to watch for next are the "real numbers" that Anthropic will disclose. Beyond future expectations, how many companies are currently paying to use the Claude model will be the key factor determining its corporate valuation [Source 12]. If the IPO is successful, it has the potential to be one of the largest corporate debuts in human history, alongside SpaceX or OpenAI [Source 5]. However, the current market caution suggests that the AI industry has entered a phase where it must prove its "business substance" beyond just being a "trend" [Source 9, Source 20].

### MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Perspective

Anthropic's valuation adjustment signifies that the AI industry has passed the bubble phase and is now on the test bed of realistic profitability. Investors are now asking "how much are you earning right now" rather than just the story that "AI will change the future." This is a necessary maturation process for AI technology to take root in our lives more deeply and healthily.
