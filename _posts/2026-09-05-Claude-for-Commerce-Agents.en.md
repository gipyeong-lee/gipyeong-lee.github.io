Imagine this: You are selecting a product in an online store and ask, "I usually wear a size 95; will this fit me?" An AI clerk immediately compares your past purchase data with the clothing's dimensions and replies, "Considering your usual style, it might feel a bit small." Meanwhile, in the back of the store, an AI manager is analyzing real-time sales data and automatically ordering products that are running low on stock. This is no longer a distant future.

Anthropic's recent announcement of 'Claude Commerce Agents' is like releasing blueprints that allow you to hire capable AI clerks and AI managers for your own site ([Interesting: Investigating Claude Commerce Agents! Cart +35%·Purchase [note.com]](https://note.com/humble_bobcat51/n/n9991736aa3ee?hl=ko)).

### Why is this important?

Until now, introducing AI to an online store meant renting complex services provided by giant IT companies at a high cost. However, the blueprints Anthropic has open-sourced offer an opportunity for everyone, from small and medium-sized businesses to large corporations, to build AI agents tailored to their own environments ([Build commerce agents with Claude [claude.com]](https://claude.com/solutions/commerce)).

Simply put, whereas previously you bought off-the-shelf AI, you can now assemble an AI agent perfectly suited to your store, much like LEGO blocks. It is particularly notable for its ability to go beyond simply answering customer questions, smoothly handling the process of finding and comparing what customers want, and ultimately helping them complete their purchases ([Building Commerce Agents with Claude [claude.com]](https://claude.com/blog/claude-for-commerce-agents)). For companies, the major features are reducing simple, repetitive tasks and providing a much more personalized shopping experience to customers.

### Easy to Understand: AI Clerk and AI Manager Blueprints

The released blueprints fulfill two major roles ([Claude Shopping and Merchant Agents: Anthropic Launches AI [datastudios.org]](https://www.datastudios.org/post/claude-shopping-merchant-agents-anthropic-ai-commerce-blueprints)).

1. **AI Clerk (Shopping Agent)**: This is the conversational AI you encounter in an online store. It understands a customer's natural language and finds products or compares differences. It is just like a veteran department store clerk who understands a customer's taste and recommends products.
2. **AI Manager (Merchant Agent)**: This is a 'back-office' staff member who helps the store's operations team. It works behind the scenes of store operations—managing inventory, analyzing sales, and handling customer management—to help management make decisions.

These blueprints are like a manual for ready-to-assemble furniture ([GitHub - anthropics/commerce-agents: Reference blueprint for... [github.com]](https://github.com/anthropics/commerce-agents)). Once developers clearly define prompts (instructions given to the AI), skills, and tool settings, they can be utilized in various environments. A playbook including 18 operational scenarios is also provided to help novice operators get started easily ([The Claude Agents Playbook: 18 AI Agents for Ecommerce [intelligence.madebydas.com]](https://intelligence.madebydas.com/playbooks/claude-agents-playbook)).

### How far have we come?

Currently, these blueprints provide concrete examples that can be used in a wide range of fields, including retail, travel, telecommunications, and entertainment ticketing ([NEW: Claude Commerce Agents is now open source, offering blueprints for AI shopping and merchant agents across retail, travel, telecom, and entertainment [cryptopanic.com]](https://cryptopanic.com/news/33320790/NEW-Claude-Commerce-Agents-is-now-open-source-offering-blueprints-for-AI-shopping-and-merchant-agents-across-retail-travel-telecom-and-entertainment)).

What is particularly noteworthy is safety. From its inception, Claude was designed with reliability and safety as top priorities through a framework called 'Constitutional AI' (a method where AI learns to follow rules itself), allowing companies to use it with confidence ([Using Claude for E-Commerce: The Complete Guide (2026) [marginops.ai]](https://marginops.ai/guides/claude-for-ecommerce)).

Of course, this does not mean the AI judges and decides everything on its own. Technical 'gates' are placed on sensitive tasks like product purchases to ensure humans do not lose control ([Claude Shopping and Merchant Agents: Anthropic Launches AI [datastudios.org]](https://www.datastudios.org/post/claude-shopping-merchant-agents-anthropic-ai-commerce-blueprints)). These act as safety devices that allow human managers to correct the situation immediately, even if the AI makes a mistake.

### What happens next?

Anthropic also provides a tool called 'commerce-builder' to support developers in more easily creating new AI agents or refining existing AI ([Anthropic Released Claude Commerce Agents: An Apache 2.0 Blueprint for Shopping and Merchant Agents across retail, travel, telecom and entertainment [marktechpost.com]](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/03/anthropic-released-claude-commerce-agents-an-apache-2-0-blueprint-for-shopping-and-merchant-agents-across-retail-travel-telecom-and-entertainment/)).

To use a metaphor, an era has opened where every online store can hire a 'smart assistant called AI.' In the future, it will become a matter of course to meet an AI clerk who knows your preferences accurately, no matter which store you visit. For operators, scenes where they no longer need to organize data in Excel and instead simply say to their AI manager, "Create a strategy by category that had the best sales last month," will become part of our daily lives.
