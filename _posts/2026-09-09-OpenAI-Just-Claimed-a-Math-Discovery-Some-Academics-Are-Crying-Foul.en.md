Imagine this: there is a puzzle that brilliant mathematicians around the world have been unable to solve for the past 90 years. Then one day, someone announces, "Our AI model solved this problem in 88 hours." You might expect cheers, but instead, the academic community is pushing back strongly, claiming, "This isn't fair." What on earth happened?

### Why does this matter?

The problem OpenAI claims to have solved is related to the 'Navier–Stokes equations,' a math challenge with a prize purse of $1 million [[Source 6]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Re31EBsukng).

The Navier–Stokes equations are physical formulas that explain how fluids like air and water flow. To use an analogy, they are like a 'map' that perfectly calculates the complex flow of ocean water or the movement of air rushing past an airplane. They are used in almost every field of modern science, from weather forecasting to airplane design, but mathematically, they remain largely uncharted territory. AI solving this problem means more than just cracking a single math puzzle; it signals the start of a new era where AI leads complex scientific discoveries [[Source 6]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Re31EBsukng).

### Understanding it simply: How does AI solve math?

Mathematical proof is like solving a very complex maze. You have to find the one correct path among countless forks in the road.

Simply put, OpenAI deployed 'thousands of AI bots' for this research [[Source 7]](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cy7zygy3rl2o). It is like having thousands of mathematicians enter the maze at the same time, each exploring a different route, and then finding the one that succeeds. To achieve this, an incomparable amount of computational power was used compared to previous math-solving attempts [[Source 1]](https://www.wired.com/story/openai-navier-stokes-math-discovery-academics/).

So, why the controversy? According to OpenAI's claim, these equations 'blow up' in very rare cases. In other words, the fluid's velocity becomes infinite at a specific point—a phenomenon that almost never occurs in the natural world [[Source 9]](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/openai-claims-blockbuster-math-breakthrough-amid-swirl-of-controversy/). While this conclusion itself is shocking, the academic community is pointing out that OpenAI may have used inappropriate methods to obtain this result, suggesting allegations of misconduct [[Source 4]](https://www.techmeme.com/260908/p29).

### Current situation: A battle over the truth

OpenAI announced that it conquered this puzzle, which had remained unsolved for 80 to 90 years, in just 88 hours [[Source 5]](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/open-ai-convinces-man-he-found-a-math-formula-tells-him-to-write), [[Source 7]](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cy7zygy3rl2o). However, immediately after the announcement, some in academia expressed strong skepticism.

Sebastian Bubeck, OpenAI's head of math research, responded strongly to these allegations, calling them "false and inflammatory claims" [[Source 2]](https://www.soz6.com/news/openai-fought-dirty-on-career-making-math-problem-says-nyu-mathematician). OpenAI maintains its stance that there was no misconduct during the research process [[Source 4]](https://www.techmeme.com/260908/p29), [[Source 9]](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/openai-claims-blockbuster-math-breakthrough-amid-swirl-of-controversy/).

### What comes next?

This controversy poses a fundamental question beyond just solving a math problem: how should human mathematicians interpret 'AI-generated proofs'? The key will be watching whether mathematical discovery shifts entirely into the realm of AI, or if thorough validation by human mathematicians remains an essential process.

Verifying whether an AI's answer is truly a truth or just a plausible-sounding result spewed out by a massive computer will be the next assignment for the math community. This is because scientific discovery relies not only on the result but also on the 'transparency of the process' used to reach it.
