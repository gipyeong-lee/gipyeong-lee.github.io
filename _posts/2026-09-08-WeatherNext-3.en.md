Imagine this: You're planning a camping trip with your family this weekend, and you check your weather app just before you leave. "There is an 80% chance of showers near your current location in two hours." In the past, weather reports told you about your 'entire neighborhood,' but we have entered an era where forecasts can target the exact spot where you are standing. This is the future brought to you by Google's new AI weather model, **WeatherNext 3**.

### Why is this important?

Weather is the most difficult element to predict in human life, yet it has the greatest impact. Beyond simply deciding whether to pack an umbrella, farmers adjust crop harvest times, and solar or wind power plants predict energy output. [Source 8](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/introducing-weathernext-3/) While traditional weather forecasting involved supercomputers calculating complex laws of physics, AI has now begun to predict local weather faster and more accurately based on real-time observational data. [Source 8](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/introducing-weathernext-3/)

### Easy to understand: Photo filters and puzzle pieces

The core technology of WeatherNext 3, the 'FGN mesh transformer' (an AI structure that understands relationships between complex data such as text and images), is easily comparable to **'high-resolution photo enhancement technology'** [Source 9](https://developers.google.com/weathernext/guides/models)

If previous models showed blurry photos, WeatherNext 3 learns from real-time incoming satellite data to remove noise and increase sharpness by five times. [Source 8](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/introducing-weathernext-3/, [Source 14](https://developers.google.com/weathernext/guides/research)) It's like applying a filter in a camera app, where a blurry landscape instantly becomes sharp.

Think of it this way: In the past, you used a large 25km puzzle piece to summarize your neighborhood's weather, but now, small 5km pieces capture fine details like terrain, valleys, and coastlines. [Source 3](https://helentech.jp/news-google-announce-weathernext-3-90859/), [Source 14](https://developers.google.com/weathernext/guides/research) Thanks to this, we can predict much more precisely whether it will rain on the mountain behind our house.

### How and where will it be used?

WeatherNext 3, co-developed by Google DeepMind and Google Research, is being applied sequentially to Google Search, Gemini (Google's AI service), Maps, and cloud services. [Source 15](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/google-launches-weathernext-3-its-most-advanced-ai-weather-model-yet/articleshow/133801237.cms)

In particular, this model does not rely solely on numerical weather prediction (NWP) based on physical calculations, but learns real-time raw observational data coming directly from satellites. [Source 12](https://9to5google.com/2026/09/03/google-weathernext-3/), [Source 16](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/googles-latest-ai-weather-model-gives-you-no-excuse-to-forget-your-umbrella/) As a result, precipitation prediction accuracy has improved by 50% compared to previous models. [Source 12](https://9to5google.com/2026/09/03/google-weathernext-3/) It is also raising high expectations in the energy sector as it can directly estimate wind and solar radiation energy needed to predict solar and wind power generation. [Source 5](https://particle.news/story/google-releases-weathernext-3-an-hourly-global-ai-weather-model)

### What's next for us?

Moving forward, we will be able to respond more quickly to sudden weather changes through weather information that is updated hourly. [Source 4](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/09/05/google-weathernext-3-hourly-runs-finer-local-forecasts-xcxwbn/, [Source 8](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/introducing-weathernext-3/)) Open your smartphone's weather app right now. You will likely be able to encounter more precise forecasts soon. However, no matter how good AI forecasting becomes, unpredictable natural phenomena will still exist, so it is always wise to check weather information but remain prepared.
