The cold winter air of Vancouver, Canada, was filled with intense heat. This was due to the hosting of **NeurIPS 2024 (Neural Information Processing Systems)**, the world's largest AI conference, often referred to as the 'Fashion Week' or 'Olympics' of the artificial intelligence (AI) industry [Source 3](https://iotribune.com/deepmind-neurips2024-innovations/).

The main protagonist that drew the most attention at this event, held from December 10 to 15, 2024, was undoubtedly **Google DeepMind** [Source 5](https://research.google/conferences-and-events/google-at-neurips-2024/). How is AI, which once existed only in complex mathematical formulas and academic papers, changing our living rooms, our smartphones, and the weather above our heads? Let's take a look at the vivid scene presented by Google DeepMind, explained as if a smart friend were walking you through it.

## Why It Matters

Until now, we have been accustomed to asking AI like ChatGPT for simple things, such as "Recommend a lunch menu for today." However, what Google DeepMind showed at this conference is on a completely different level. This is because AI has now begun to step forward as a **'real-world problem solver'** [Source 2](https://www.kifinity.com/post/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024-deepmind-google-f8eed203).

**To put it in perspective**, if AI up until now was an 'all-around assistant' who spoke very well, it has now become a 'professional engineer' who directly draws blueprints and predicts the weather.

For example, technologies to predict more accurately whether it will rain tomorrow, technologies to make the semiconductor chips—the brains of our smartphones—smaller and faster, and technologies where AI cooperates with other AIs like humans to handle complex tasks were unveiled in large numbers [Source 4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2A3tiAmaq_c) [Source 13](https://aiglobaltech.blogspot.com/2025/03/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024.html). This means AI is evolving beyond simply "speaking well" into a technology that directly improves the physical world we live in. It means our daily lives are ready to become much more efficient and sophisticated.

## The Explainer

NeurIPS 2024, marking its 38th anniversary, is a historic event, and Google DeepMind demonstrated its status by participating as a 'Diamond Sponsor' of the conference [Source 7](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2024) [Source 8](https://deepmind.google/discover/events/neurips-2024/).

### 1. AI Designing Semiconductor Chips: "An Omnipotent Urban Planning Expert"
Jeff Dean, Chief Scientist at Google DeepMind, raised a very interesting topic: how machine learning technology is fundamentally changing semiconductor chip design [Source 4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2A3tiAmaq_c).

**Imagine this.** The inside of a semiconductor chip is like a massive city where trillions of circuits are intricately intertwined. Until now, humans have manually laid the roads (circuits) and placed buildings (components). However, as the number of paths for data movement increased, human capacity reached its limit. Here, AI appears like an **'omnipotent urban planning expert.'** AI can simulate millions of paths in an instant to find the most optimal, congestion-free road network. As a result, it becomes possible to create smarter chips that are faster and consume less electricity [Source 17](https://www.turing.com/blog/agi-icons-3-recap).

### 2. Practical AI Beyond the Lab: A Bomb of 100 Papers
The Google DeepMind team published **over 100 new research papers** at this conference [Source 13](https://aiglobaltech.blogspot.com/2025/03/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024.html). Including the Google Research team, the number exceeds 120 papers [Source 5](https://research.google/conferences-and-events/google-at-neurips-2024/).

It wasn't just about the quantity. The technologies Google DeepMind demonstrated on-site are closely related to our lives. **In simple terms**, AI has stepped out of the lab and come closer to us:
*   **Weather Forecasting:** An AI technology that predicts the weather in our neighborhood much more precisely than existing meteorological office models. Advice like "It's scheduled to rain briefly, so postpone your car wash" becomes possible [Source 13](https://aiglobaltech.blogspot.com/2025/03/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024.html).
*   **AI for Music Generation:** By simply entering lyrics or a mood, it creates background music at a professional composer's level in an instant. The era of having your own background music has arrived [Source 13](https://aiglobaltech.blogspot.com/2025/03/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024.html).
*   **Gemma Scope:** **By way of analogy**, this is a 'transparent microscope' for looking into AI's inner thoughts. It helps build safer AI by transparently showing how the internal structure of the AI works [Source 13](https://aiglobaltech.blogspot.com/2025/03/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024.html).

### 3. "Let's Team Up": Cooperative AI
Google DeepMind, along with world-renowned universities like MIT and UC Berkeley, held a unique competition called the 'Concordia Contest' [Source 10](https://www.cooperativeai.com/contests/concordia-2024).

This competition was a place to test how well AI could **'cooperate'** with other AIs or humans. The prize money amounted to 10,000 USD (approximately 13 million KRW). This emphasized the importance of 'social AI' that knows how to solve problems together as a member of society, like a team member performing a group project excellently, rather than just an AI that is smart on its own [Source 10](https://www.cooperativeai.com/contests/concordia-2024).

## Where We Stand

Google DeepMind's influence was also proven through its awards. It swept two **'Test of Time'** awards, which are among the most honorable awards in the academic community [Source 2](https://www.kifinity.com/post/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024-deepmind-google-f8eed203).

This award is given to papers that have had a profound impact on the AI academic community even though a long time has passed since their publication. **To put it in perspective**, it's like being inducted into a 'Hall of Fame' in the film industry. This is evidence that Google DeepMind is not just following the latest trends but is a true leader that has built the roots of AI technology firmly [Source 2](https://www.kifinity.com/post/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024-deepmind-google-f8eed203).

Numerous researchers and developers lined up at the Google booth in the Vancouver Convention Centre. People directly experienced weather prediction models and music generation technology through live demos, confirming the power of 'real-world technology' that goes beyond theory [Source 14](https://itconsultingroup.com/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024/) [Source 16](https://aigeneratorreviews.com/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024/).

## What's Next

Chief Scientist Jeff Dean predicted that AI will become more deeply involved in solving the traditional computer system problems we use [Source 4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2A3tiAmaq_c).

In the not-too-distant future, we will see a world like this:
1.  **Ultra-precise Weather Alerts:** You will receive a notification on your smartphone 10 minutes before a picnic saying, "It will rain soon, so please go inside a building."
2.  **Personalized Media:** You can enjoy movies or games where background music perfectly suited to your mood or situation is generated in real-time.
3.  **Intelligent Agents:** An era will rapidly approach where multiple AIs talk and cooperate with each other to automatically handle our complex schedules or tasks [Source 13](https://aiglobaltech.blogspot.com/2025/03/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024.html).

Leaving behind the achievements in Vancouver in 2024, Google DeepMind is already preparing for the next step. Along with the news of preparing for the 2025 conference, a blueprint for the future was shared, stating that the conference will be held in Sydney, Australia, in 2026 [Source 15](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025). The future envisioned by AI is now becoming our reality, beyond imagination.
