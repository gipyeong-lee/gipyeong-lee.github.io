Imagine this: There is a popular game you enjoy. One day, you ask an AI, "Analyze this game's code and make me an identical version of it." In an instant, the AI churns out a clone. While this might be a huge convenience for developers, from the perspective of the original creator, it can feel like their hard-earned work has been stolen in an instant.

Recently, the artificial intelligence company Anthropic has stepped in to put a stop to this "unauthorized game cloning" situation. They have applied new rules to their AI model, [Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/research), to prevent it from performing tasks that could infringe upon the copyrights of others.

### Why is this issue important?

This move clearly demonstrates to general users that "AI is no longer just a tool that blindly does whatever you ask." Game development is both an art and an industry that requires the creativity and labor of countless people. AI reconstructing (decompiling—the process of restoring compiled programs back into source code) or cloning existing games can cause serious issues under copyright law. Major AI companies like [Anthropic and OpenAI](https://en.3dmgame.com/news/8042) are strengthening intellectual property protection to ensure that technological advancement does not infringe upon the rights of legitimate creators.

### Understanding the analogy: Copying answer sheets vs. solving them yourself

A simple analogy for this measure is like **"distinguishing between study materials and notes for cheating."**

Until now, many users have utilized Claude's powerful coding capabilities to rip the logic of existing games and reassemble them. It is similar to submitting a homework assignment by copying a friend's answer sheet instead of solving it yourself.

The rules Anthropic has introduced are like giving the AI these instructions: "You can no longer help copy another student's answer sheet. Instead, you are more than welcome to create new problems yourself or propose new methods using your own knowledge."

In fact, AI now actively assists in creating games based on the unique ideas or assets (code, designs, etc.) the user brings, but will "refuse" any attempt to simply take someone else's work as it is.

### What is the current situation?

Around October 27, 2026, Anthropic applied these new rules server-side via an internal flag named 'tengu_heron_brook'. [According to reports](https://gameranx.com/updates/id/564489/article/anthropic-cracks-down-on-claude-vibe-coding-ports-and-decompilations-of-existing-games/), these rules are mandatory and cannot be disabled by users in the settings. [On platforms like X (formerly Twitter)](https://x.com/ExtraBinoss/status/2116064975122764025), users are noting that Claude has become much stricter regarding copyright protection than in the past.

Now, if a user attempts to directly clone protected software or game content, Claude will block the output or send a warning message. Repeated violations may result in measures such as account suspension. [Both Anthropic and OpenAI](https://en.3dmgame.com/news/8042) are trending toward strengthening these intellectual property restrictions.

### What will happen in the future?

The boundaries between AI and creation seem set to become clearer. AI will solidify its identity as a "creative partner" rather than a "cloner." Anthropic's new rules guide AI to build games only in ways that respect the original game's copyright—namely, through original game development or by utilizing the user's own unique designs and assets.

Creators can step back from the risk of their work being recklessly copied, and users will learn how to develop games in a more ethical and creative way through AI. While the debate over how far technology should be permitted to go will continue, this measure is clear evidence that AI is evolving in a direction that respects human creativity.

### MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Opinion

As technology advances, setting "what not to do" becomes far more important than "what can be done." Anthropic's recent move will contribute to creating a healthy ecosystem where AI can cooperate while respecting the masters of creation. This is because AI is not just a machine that churns out code, but an excellent partner that expands our creativity.
