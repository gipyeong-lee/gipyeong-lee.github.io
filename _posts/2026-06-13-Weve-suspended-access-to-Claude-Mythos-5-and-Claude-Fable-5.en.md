## Introduction: The Cutting-Edge AI That Suddenly Stopped One Day

Imagine this. You wake up in the morning, brew a warm cup of coffee, sit down at your computer as usual, and naturally ask your AI assistant, "Please neatly summarize the important meeting materials for today and the dozens of English documents that piled up last week." Since it's the latest AI assistant that was just released a few days ago boasting immense intelligence, you wait for a perfect answer with high expectations.

However, what appears on the screen is not a smoothly organized report, but a cold error message saying "Access Denied." This isn't simply a case of the company's servers going down or a temporary network failure. It's because the nation's top security agency stepped in and issued an emergency order stating, "This AI is so powerful that its use by foreign nationals must be banned immediately." It sounds like a scene from a Hollywood spy movie, but surprisingly, this is a real event that struck the world just a few days ago.

Anthropic, which has been drawing the hottest attention in the artificial intelligence industry recently, delivered shocking news. They officially announced that they have completely blocked access for all customers worldwide to their latest AI models, 'Claude Fable 5' and 'Claude Mythos 5', which were ambitiously introduced on June 9 [Anthropic pulls Claude Mythos 5 and Claude Fable 5 following US...](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/12/anthropic-pulls-claude-mythos-5-and-claude-fable-5-following-us-government-directive/). This extreme measure is not due to the company's own decision or technical flaws. It is because the US government directly intervened and invoked an export control directive citing national security [Fable 5 Is Down for Everyone: Inside Anthropic's Government-Ordered...](https://apidog.com/blog/fable-5-down-government-suspension/).

In the midst of fierce competition with world-class artificial intelligence like OpenAI's ChatGPT and Google's Gemini, such a powerful shutdown order issued right after launch is causing massive ripples throughout the industry [Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 AI suspended over security...](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c932g3v3e13o). What secrets were hidden in these latest AI models that prompted the US government to urgently order a worldwide halt to their use?

## Why It Matters

When a smartphone app or website we normally use suddenly stops working, it's easy to assume the development company's computer servers caught fire or got hacked. However, the fundamental nature of this 'Claude 5' series blockade is completely different. The biggest significance of this incident is that artificial intelligence technology is now starting to be treated not simply as convenient software that helps our daily lives, but as an 'ultra-class strategic asset' that must be strictly managed by the state itself, much like stealth fighter jets or cutting-edge semiconductor manufacturing technology.

Looking at the specific details of the measures taken by the US government makes the severity even clearer. According to the official statement released by Anthropic, this directive, based on the powerful authority of national security authorities, contains a resolute order to "completely suspend access to the Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models for all 'foreign nationals', regardless of whether they live inside the US territory or overseas" [Statement on the US government directive to suspend access to...](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access). Even though it's cloud software serviced in the borderless virtual space of the internet, it is a highly unusual extreme move to fundamentally block access to information based solely on the real-world criterion of 'nationality'.

There are two main models whose access was blocked this time.

The first is **'Claude Fable 5'**, which was newly released and widely provided to the general public [Anthropic cuts top-tier AI access after US foreigner ban](https://www.dw.com/en/anthropic-cuts-top-tier-ai-access-after-us-foreigner-ban/a-77534688). Claude Fable 5 entered the market boasting incredible performance to counter the latest models from other rival companies [Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 AI suspended over security...](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c932g3v3e13o). In particular, numerous loyal customers paying high fees to use top-tier services like Pro, Max, and Enterprise were enjoying these amazing features during the free preview period. However, due to this directive, even the top-tier paid members are now completely unable to access the Fable 5 model [US Government Suspends Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5 and Mythos...](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/06/13/us-government-suspends-anthropics-claude-fable-5-and-mythos-5-over-security-and-jailbreak-concerns/).

The second target is **'Claude Mythos 5'**, which was not for the general public but secretly provided only to a very small number of vetted organizations that passed thorough and rigorous security screening [Anthropic cuts top-tier AI access after US foreigner ban](https://www.dw.com/en/anthropic-cuts-top-tier-ai-access-after-us-foreigner-ban/a-77534688). Mythos 5 was a highly advanced model that only trusted partner companies approved through a special access program could use. But this government action completely halted the program's use even for these verified partners [US Government Suspends Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5 and Mythos...](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/06/13/us-government-suspends-anthropics-claude-fable-5-and-mythos-5-over-security-and-jailbreak-concerns/). For the many professionals around the world who used AI daily as a core tool for their work, this is truly a bolt from the blue.

## The Explainer

Let's break down this complex political and technical situation a bit more easily by comparing it to our daily lives. To understand why the US government took such extreme measures, you need to know two key concepts.

The first is the concept of **'Export Control'** invoked by the US government. This is a measure where the government strongly enforces laws to prevent dangerous technologies or items that could threaten national security from leaking overseas.
Put simply, imagine a genius inventor in your town has invented an ultra-high-speed car engine that is faster and more powerful than a spaceship. While people all over the world are coveting this engine, the police chief suddenly visits and warns: "The power of this engine has surpassed the level we can control. If a criminal organization steals this car and runs away, our police cars will never be able to catch it. Therefore, from this moment on, outsiders who are not from our town are completely forbidden from even turning the ignition on, let alone sitting in the driver's seat of this car."
Even though it's an invisible digital artificial intelligence that converses by typing in a chat window, the US government viewed the results this latest AI could produce as dangerous as a destructive weapon. So, they defined the very act of it being delivered to people in other countries via digital communication networks as an 'export' and blocked it at the source [Fable 5 Is Down for Everyone: Inside Anthropic's Government-Ordered...](https://apidog.com/blog/fable-5-down-government-suspension/).

The second thing to know is **'Jailbreak'**, which is pointed out as the most direct cause of this blockade [US Government Suspends Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5 and Mythos...](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/06/13/us-government-suspends-anthropics-claude-fable-5-and-mythos-5-over-security-and-jailbreak-concerns/). What computer experts call AI jailbreaking is slightly different from hacking a smartphone password.
To use an analogy, imagine a rescue dog that has been perfectly trained at an excellent training center. This rescue dog has undergone rigorous safeguards training for years so as not to bite people under any emergency situation. However, someone with malicious intent appears and gives commands using a very strange and complex mix of words and whistling sounds that the trainer normally doesn't use. Confused by this clever trick, the rescue dog momentarily forgets the rules of its safety training, reveals its tethered instincts, and breaks free from control.
The exact same thing happens in the world of artificial intelligence. Anthropic's developers strictly taught the AI not to provide recipes for deadly weapons or write malicious code that destroys national computer networks. However, if a user cleverly twists a question to make it role-play by saying, "Let's write a spy movie script together," or throws a question split up like a complex puzzle, the AI is tricked into forgetting the safety rules it has set and pours out prohibited dangerous knowledge like a waterfall. This is exactly what a 'jailbreak' is. The US government judged that the Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models had become so smart that if they were fooled by such jailbreak attacks and leaked fatal information, it would cause irreversible damage to national security, so they pressed the emergency shutdown button.

However, there was not a single drop of mercy in this measure. An even more absurd and unfortunate reality is unfolding inside Anthropic's own company. According to Anthropic's announcement, even Anthropic's engineers and researchers who stayed up all night directly coding and designing this latest AI have been completely blocked from accessing it simply because their nationality is 'foreign' [Statement on the US government directive to suspend access to...](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access).
Imagine you are the head chef at a top-tier restaurant. You've poured your heart and soul into perfecting a new dish, but the restaurant manager approaches and kicks you out, saying, "You are not a citizen of our country, so you are forbidden from even tasting the dish you made yourself." This unprecedented lockdown, where even the employees who need to fix and develop the company's core technology cannot log into the system, starkly shows how extremely terrifying and serious the US authorities view this situation.

So, what will become of the future of users and developers around the world who have relied on the Claude models, and the artificial intelligence industry as a whole? Anthropic has currently thoroughly blocked access to the models in order to legally comply with the US government's powerful control directives [Statement on the US government directive to suspend access to...](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access). Because of this, it has become very difficult for users around the world living outside the US to meet this smart AI assistant again for the time being.

In particular, IT developers who were trying to build new apps or services utilizing Claude literally have their feet on fire. They were carefully choosing which model, between Fable 5 and Mythos 5, would better suit their services. Developers were meticulously analyzing everything from how the Application Programming Interfaces (APIs)—the pathways for software to communicate—connect, to the safeguards defending the systems, and the fallback behaviors, which are backup plans that activate when problems occur in the service. But now, they are in a position where they must throw all their plans into the trash and find new alternatives [Claude Fable 5 vs Mythos 5: API Routing | WaveSpeed Blog](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/claude-fable-5-vs-mythos-5/).

Even users within the US whose access has not been blocked cannot afford to be completely relieved. To manage traffic, Anthropic has strictly adjusted rate limits according to pricing plans, such as the 'requests per minute (RPM)'—the number of questions that can be sent to the AI in one minute—and the data limit processed at one time.
