Imagine this: You wake up in the morning and turn on your computer to find that an AI agent (an artificial intelligence that sets its own goals and autonomously performs complex tasks) has spent the night fixing bugs in the code you wrote, adding new features, and even completing the testing. This scene is no longer just in movies. Development tools are rapidly shifting from being 'human-centric' to 'AI agent-centric.'

Recently, in the software development ecosystem, AI agents have emerged as the key protagonists of development work, going beyond mere assistants that help with a single line of code. In line with this trend, Cursor, famous for its AI coding tools, announced 'Cursor Origin,' a new Git repository platform, on June 17, 2026 [[Source 3](https://explainx.ai/blog/cursor-origin-git-hosting-github-alternative-ai-agents-2026)].

### Why is this change necessary?

The development platforms we have used until now (such as GitHub) were fundamentally built on the premise that 'humans' write and review code. If we compare them to roads, they are roads paved to be suitable for regular cars. However, as 'autonomous vehicles'—namely AI agents—have started to hit the roads in earnest, a situation has arisen where the existing road system is inefficient.

New platforms are designed so that AI agents can process the stages of understanding, modifying, and deploying code more efficiently than focusing on human readability [[Source 1](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/what-is-cursor-origin)]. This dramatically increases development productivity and allows human developers to move away from simple, repetitive tasks of manually modifying code and focus more on creative design.

### Simply put, a dedicated office for AI

To put it in easy terms, you can think of 'Cursor Origin' as a **'dedicated collaborative office for AI.'**

If the existing Git platforms we commonly use are like libraries organized for humans to easily find and read books, Cursor Origin is like a state-of-the-art data center optimized for AI agents to read, summarize, and classify vast amounts of information in the library at the speed of light, and to quickly share opinions with other AIs [[Source 1](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/what-is-cursor-origin)]. This platform was built by the Graphite team, led within Cursor; it is not simply a copy of existing services but was redesigned from the ground up for the process of AI hosting and reviewing code [[Source 6](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48558605)].

Tools like Git-Ape have a similar orientation. For example, Git-Ape reinterprets 'Platform Engineering' (creating an environment where developers can deploy efficiently) for the agent era [[Source 5](https://github.com/Azure/git-ape)]. It is like a chef who doesn't need to prep all the ingredients themselves, but just says, "Today's menu is Korean food," and the AI prepares the ingredients, finds the recipe, and produces the perfect dish (cloud deployment) [[Source 7](https://azure.github.io/git-ape/)].

### How far have we come?

Agent-centric changes have already begun across the entire spectrum of development tools. For example, the popular API testing tool Postman already converted to an AI-native platform in March 2026, starting to support AI agents throughout the entire API development process [[Source 4](https://blog.postman.com/new-postman-is-here/)].

However, not everything is rosy. Controversies and challenges still remain. Some point out that just because AI generates a lot of code, it does not necessarily mean that better PR (Pull Request) reviews will be conducted, and that the fundamental software design principles of humans remain important [[Source 6](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48558605)]. Also, the technical and financial concerns of completely replacing well-established existing infrastructure are a major task for development teams.

### How will the future landscape change?

The future development environment will be centered on the 'close collaboration between humans and AI agents.' Human developers will clearly define the intent of "what to build," AI agents will handle "how to implement it," and platforms like Cursor Origin or Git-Ape will play the role of a solid foundation that helps this process run smoothly [[Source 7](https://azure.github.io/git-ape/)].

In the near future, developers might evolve from people who write code one line at a time to conductors leading a vast orchestra called AI agents. This technological shift seems poised to fundamentally change not just the replacement of tools, but the act of software development itself.
