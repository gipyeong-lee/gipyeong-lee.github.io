# AI Doing It All? 'Digital Employees' Working for Us Just Got an Office: Claude Managed Agents

Imagine you have a highly capable personal assistant. But this assistant doesn't just add a meeting to your calendar when you ask them to "prepare for tomorrow's meeting." They independently open past emails, scan related materials, organize necessary data into a document, and even share it with attendees. Even if you step away for a moment, the assistant continues their work silently.

Until now, AIs like ChatGPT and Claude were mostly smart friends who were very good at "talking." However, AI is now evolving beyond speaking to taking direct "action." In April 2026, AI company Anthropic unveiled **"Claude Managed Agents,"** a service designed to help anyone create these "acting AIs" easily and safely [Source 12, 17, 19].

## Why is this important for us?

Previously, asking an AI to do complex tasks was like asking a "very smart person with no hands or feet" to cook. The AI could come up with a great recipe (thoughts), but a human had to manually build the devices to actually pick up the knife to chop ingredients or adjust the gas burner (tool execution). Furthermore, monitoring for fires (security) and scaling up the number of cooks when guests suddenly pour in (scalability) were all complex backend tasks left to developers.

However, "Claude Managed Agents" is a service where Anthropic leases all this "kitchen equipment" and "management system" as a package [Source 16, 18]. As a result, companies can focus on what tasks to assign to the AI instead of struggling to build complex infrastructure themselves. Consequently, it's now possible to create AI agents that can be deployed to the field **10x faster** than traditional methods [Source 4, 11].

## Easy Understanding: A "Fully Furnished Digital Office" for AI

To use a simpler analogy, Claude Managed Agents is like renting a **"fully furnished office with all appliances and furniture"** to an AI employee. This office is divided into three key areas [Source 12]:

1.  **Session (Persistent Work Desk)**: A space where everything the employee does from clock-in to clock-out is recorded. Even if the user temporarily disconnects from the internet, the AI remains at this desk to continue its work and provides a clear report on the progress when the user returns [Source 18].
2.  **Harness (Detailed Work Guidelines)**: A device that helps the AI "brain" connect properly with the company's systems. It acts as a control room to ensure the AI doesn't act on its own and uses tools correctly within the rules we've set [Source 3, 12].
3.  **Sandbox (Safe Experimental Lab)**: A secure, isolated area where the AI can write code or modify important files without risking the destruction of the entire system. Just as children play only within a sandbox, potentially dangerous tasks are performed only here [Source 12, 18].

Thanks to this perfectly equipped environment, developers can use programming languages like Python or TypeScript to assign tasks to AI agents as simply as performing "digital summoning" [Source 12].

## How does it work? The Magic of the "Agent Loop"

The most attractive feature of Claude Managed Agents is that the AI manages the **"Agent Loop"** itself [Source 5]. A "loop" refers to the process where the AI repeats thinking and acting to achieve a goal.

For example, imagine commanding, "Find anomalies in this sales data file and write a report." The AI repeats the following process autonomously:
- **Judgment**: "Hmm, first I need to read the file. What tools do I need?"
- **Execution**: Directly issues a command to read the file within the secure sandbox [Source 5].
- **Analysis**: "Looking at the data, sales last Thursday were 3x higher than usual. I should emphasize this part."
- **Reporting**: Completes the report by sending real-time progress updates to the user [Source 5].

All these complex processes occur safely within Anthropic's robust servers. Users can simply watch the AI work efficiently while enjoying a cup of coffee.

## Current Status: Digital Colleagues Already Clocking In Near Us

Fast-moving companies are already achieving results with this technology. Familiar apps like **Notion** and the Japanese retail giant **Rakuten** are leading examples [Source 11]. They are using Claude Managed Agents to build advanced systems where multiple AIs converse with each other to solve complex business problems.

The cost is also very reasonable. Beyond the standard AI model fees, you only pay a usage fee of **just $0.08 per hour** while the agent is actually performing work [Source 11, 17]. You can now hire a smart digital employee full-time for an hour at a cost lower than a pack of gum.

## What kind of future awaits?

Anthropic's engineers designed this system to go beyond just current models. If the model—the AI's "brain"—is upgraded to be smarter, the employee can be replaced with more capable talent at any time while keeping the office (infrastructure) as it is [Source 3].

Significant changes are also expected in design and planning. AI will evolve from a simple requester—"Draw me a picture"—to a true partner performing complex missions like "Analyze our brand values, design the entire website, and write the actual functional code" [Source 13].
