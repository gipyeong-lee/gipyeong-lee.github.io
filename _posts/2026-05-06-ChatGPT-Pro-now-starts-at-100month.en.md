If you are a regular user of ChatGPT, you are likely familiar with the 'Plus' plan, which costs about $20 per month. While that price can be a burden for some, OpenAI has recently introduced a plan that is five times more expensive: **'ChatGPT Pro,'** which costs $100 per month. [OpenAI Launches $100-Per-Month ChatGPT Pro Plan With Expanded Codex Access](https://creati.ai/ai-news/2026-04-10/openai-chatgpt-pro-100-month-codex-subscription/)

You might wonder, "Why should I spend the equivalent of several expensive dinners every month just to chat with an AI?" However, this plan is not just for casual conversation. OpenAI explains that this new tier is designed to be 'professional equipment' that fills the massive gap between the standard consumer plans and the enterprise plans that cost thousands of dollars. In this article, we will explore why this expensive plan has emerged and exactly what features are making professionals open their wallets.

Until now, we have viewed AI as a 'smart assistant that answers whatever you ask.' However, AI is now moving beyond being a simple helper into the realm of a **'skilled technician'** that can write complex programs, conduct vast data research, and solve problems independently.

The new Pro plan signifies three major shifts:

1.  **The AI market is starting to 'tier' its users**: Just as a hobbyist photographer might be satisfied with a smartphone camera while a professional buys lenses worth thousands of dollars, AI is now establishing a clear pricing structure as a 'professional tool.' 2.  **Unshackling usage limits**: For developers who code professionally, AI is now an essential tool. In the existing Plus plan, usage limits often interrupted the workflow at critical moments. The Pro plan dramatically increases these limits to provide a 'near-unlimited work environment.' ## Where We Stand: Which Plan is Right for You?

The ChatGPT plans are now divided into four main tiers. Consider which one fits your usage pattern:

*   **Free**: Suitable for those who occasionally ask questions or have casual conversations with AI.
*   **Plus ($20/month)**: The most popular plan for those who use AI daily and want to try the latest features (like image generation) before others. [ChatGPT finally offers $100/month Pro plan | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/09/chatgpt-pro-plan-100-month-codex/)
*   **Pro ($100/month)**: The tier for professionals who actually make money or run businesses using AI. It offers significantly more coding tool usage and access to the exclusive GPT-5.4 Pro model. [ChatGPT Pricing 2026: Free vs Go vs Plus vs Pro – Detailed Comparison, Limits, Features, and Which Plan Is Worth It](https://www.cometapi.com/chatgpt-pricing-2026-free-vs-go-vs-plus-vs-pro/)

Notably, the Pro plan bundles **'Agent'** and **'Deep Research'** features together. An 'Agent' is an AI assistant that can judge and complete tasks on its own without constant prompting, while 'Deep Research' is a feature that scours thousands of websites to write in-depth reports. Previously, you might have had to pay for multiple services to get these features, but now they can be handled all at once within ChatGPT.
