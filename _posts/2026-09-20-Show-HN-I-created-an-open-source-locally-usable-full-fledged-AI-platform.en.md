Imagine your smartphone's voice assistant suddenly connecting to 2,000 experts from different fields simultaneously. If a math-specialized AI answered math problems, a programming-specialized AI handled coding, and a warm, friendly AI engaged in daily conversation, our lives would be much more convenient. However, until now, we had to find and use these models separately, which was cumbersome. Recently, a new open-source platform named 'Enzo' has emerged, aiming to solve all these troubles. [Source: Show HN: I created an open source locally usable full fledged AI platform | Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49771118)

### Why should you pay attention?

The current AI market is in a state of 'choice overload,' with so many models being released that users are unsure what to choose. It is like going to a huge library with 2,000 books on the shelves, all written in different languages. Enzo helps resolve this confusion and allows anyone to easily experience the performance of diverse AIs. It provides users with more choice and freedom by not being dependent on a specific company's services and by realizing the democratization of technology through the power of open source (a method of making a model's source code and blueprints public so anyone can modify and improve them). [Source: What Is Open Source AI? 12 Platforms and Tools to Know. | Built In](https://builtin.com/artificial-intelligence/open-source-ai)

### In simple terms: An 'AI Gift Set'

Enzo can be metaphorically described as an **'AI model gift set.'**

Think of a common photo editing app. Just by installing one app, you can apply mood filters, retouch portraits, or convert photos to black and white, right? Just as you don't need to carry around several cameras with different functions, the same applies to Enzo.

Enzo has aggregated numerous free AI APIs (Application Programming Interfaces, channels that allow different software to communicate) scattered around the world into one place. Through this, users can freely select and use over 2,000 different models within one 'interface' (a channel where users communicate with AI). [Source: Show HN: I created an open source locally usable full fledged AI platform | Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49771118)

To use another analogy, it is like a **'food court where top-tier chefs gather.'** Originally, you would have to go to a pasta specialty restaurant to eat delicious pasta and to a pizza place to eat pizza. However, Enzo is like creating an environment where 2,000 chefs (models) are gathered in one space, and when a user orders a desired dish, the most suitable chef provides it immediately.

### Use it right now

As an open-source platform, Enzo is accessible to everyone and can primarily be utilized for the following daily and professional tasks: [Source: Show HN: I created an open source locally usable full fledged AI platform | Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49771118)

1. **Daily Chatting:** You can converse like a friend with an AI that perfectly matches your style.
2. **Complex Coding:** It helps with development tasks such as checking the grammar of programming languages or generating new code.
3. **Academic Research:** You can summarize vast amounts of data or conduct in-depth research on specific topics.

Like this, Enzo is not just an AI that performs a single function, but a multi-purpose platform designed so that users can select models according to their goals and obtain results. It will be a very useful tool for finding the perfect partner to increase your work efficiency. [Source: Show HN: I created an open source locally usable full fledged AI platform | Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49771118)

### What is the future outlook?

Moving forward, open-source platforms like Enzo will become increasingly powerful. In particular, the spirit of 'open-source AI' lies in continuously improving into smarter and more efficient models through the cooperation of developers worldwide by making model weights (numerical results of knowledge learned by AI) public. [Source: What Is Open Source AI? 12 Platforms and Tools to Know. | Built In](https://builtin.com/artificial-intelligence/open-source-ai)

Users will now be able to make smarter decisions by comparing and using performance-verified models in one place. The future Enzo presents shows a facet of an ecosystem where people around the world work together to build smarter artificial intelligence, rather than being monopolized by specific companies. In the future, more users will be able to freely use AI as a tool through Enzo and immerse themselves in creative work.
