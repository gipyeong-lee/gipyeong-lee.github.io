**Imagine.** You are sitting at your computer and you type, "Show me a rainy, cyberpunk-style city of the future." At that moment, a sophisticated city appears on the screen with flashing neon signs and pouring rain. It's not just a video playing. You can move your mouse to pass through alleys and use your keyboard to explore inside buildings. Wherever you go, the AI creates new paths and landscapes in real-time.

This is no longer a distant story from a science fiction movie. This is the landscape being brought to life by **Genie 3**, the new artificial intelligence model unveiled by Google DeepMind [Genie 3 — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/genie/).

## Why should we pay attention to this technology?

The "Generative AI" we have encountered so far has mainly been at the level of writing text, drawing pictures, or creating short videos. But Genie 3 is on a different level. This is because Genie 3 is more than a simple "video maker"; it is a **"World Model"** that understands and simulates the rules of that world itself [Genie 3: Revolutionary AI World Model | Interactive Real-Time...](https://genie3.net/).

Simply put, a "world model" refers to an **AI structure that understands and predicts the physical laws and causal relationships of the world.** It means that the AI is grasping the logic of the virtual world on its own, just as a child learns the laws of gravity by throwing a ball. What changes will occur when this technology is fully introduced into our lives?

1.  **The boundaries of gaming will collapse**: Until now, games required players to follow paths pre-made by developers. But now, you can enjoy games where infinite terrain and stories are generated in real-time according to the player's wishes. An era is coming where AI answers the question "What's over that mountain?" by creating the world on the fly.
2.  **A revolution in training and simulation**: Before self-driving cars or robots go onto actual dangerous roads, they can undergo tens of thousands of practice runs in a sophisticated virtual world created by Genie 3 [Why Genie 3 Suggests AI 'World Models' Are The Path To...](https://www.uploadvr.com/google-genie-3-ai-world-models-the-path-to-photorealistic-vr/). This is because extreme situations, such as heavy rain or sudden obstacles, can be created virtually at will to allow for safe learning.
3.  **A world where everyone becomes a creator**: You don't need to learn complex 3D graphics technology or coding. You can decorate your own virtual reality (VR) space and invite friends with just a single sentence. Imagination becomes technology itself.

## Easy to Understand: How Does Genie 3 Work?

Genie 3 can be defined in one phrase as a **"text-controlled real-time virtual world generator"** [Genie 3: A New Frontier for World Models (Google DeepMind)](https://opencv.org/genie-3/).

### 1. Creation Starting from Text (Prompt-to-World)
When a user enters a description in natural language (the everyday speech we use), the AI identifies the context and immediately builds a virtual environment [Google DeepMind unveils Genie 3, a new AI world model | LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/peerasakc_genie-3-a-new-frontier-for-world-models-activity-7361065348663578624-tHlt). To use an analogy, it's like asking an omnipotent architect named AI to "build a mysterious castle in a dense forest," and having a detailed environment—from the rough texture of the trees to the moss on the castle walls—delivered right before your eyes.

### 2. Free Interaction Like a Game (Interactivity)
The most remarkable aspect of Genie 3 is its **"interactive"** nature. While previous AI video models only allowed you to watch the footage they showed, Genie 3 lets you walk through it yourself using a mouse and keyboard [Google DeepMind's Genie 3: A New Era of Interactive AI... | LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/amit-g-7890a1141_aiforeveryong-gemini-genie3-activity-7358930914736828417-aRfe). As the user turns their head to the left or moves forward, the AI calculates and draws a new path in real-time to match those movements [Project Genie: AI world model now available for Ultra users in U.S.](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/project-genie/).

### 3. Cinematic Smoothness and an Unchanging World (Consistency)
Genie 3 continuously generates video at **720p resolution** (one of the high-definition video standards) at a speed of **24 frames per second (24 fps)** [Genie 3: A New Frontier for World Models (Google DeepMind)](https://opencv.org/genie-3/). This is the same level of smooth movement we experience when watching a movie in a theater. Furthermore, it boasts **"consistency,"** where the appearance of previously visited locations remains unchanged even after several minutes of exploration [Google DeepMind unveils Genie 3, a new AI world model | LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/peerasakc_genie-3-a-new-frontier-for-world-models-activity-7361065348663578624-tHlt). The fact that the castle wall is still standing in the same spot when you look back is a crucial factor in determining the immersion of a virtual world.

## Current Status: Where Are We Now?

Google has unveiled a prototype called **"Project Genie"** to verify the actual performance of Genie 3. Currently, "Google AI Ultra" users in the U.S. can use this feature to create and explore their own worlds directly. It is even possible to engage in creative activities such as "remixing" existing worlds to transform them into entirely different environments [Project Genie: AI world model now available for Ultra users in U.S.](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/project-genie/).

Experts believe that world models like Genie 3 will be a key step toward **Artificial General Intelligence (AGI, AI that can perform various tasks like a human)** rather than just a fun toy. This is because, while existing Large Language Models (LLMs) learned about the world through text in books and on the internet, world models learn "cause and effect" by experiencing how the world works through direct interaction in virtual environments [Amazing! Genie 3: A new frontier for world models · AI Automation...](https://www.skool.com/ai-automation-society/amazing-genie-3-a-new-frontier-for-world-models).

## The Future Ahead: New Landscapes We Will Face

Of course, Genie 3 is not yet perfect. Currently, it maintains consistency during short explorations of a few minutes, but as the technology evolves, it will be able to generate and manage entire massive worlds that last for days or months.

In addition, the world model market is heating up with the emergence of competing models such as Runway's "Gen-3 Alpha" [Runway Research | Introducing Gen-3 Alpha: A New Frontier for...](https://runwayml.com/research/introducing-gen-3-alpha). This competition will further accelerate the era where ultra-high-definition environments, indistinguishable from reality when wearing virtual reality devices, are provided in real-time [Why Genie 3 Suggests AI 'World Models' Are The Path To...](https://www.uploadvr.com/google-genie-3-ai-world-models-the-path-to-photorealistic-vr/).

In the not-so-distant future, we might wake up in the morning and say to the AI: **"I want to take a walk through the streets of 18th-century Paris today. I hope there's a cafe on that corner with the smell of the warm coffee I like."** And Genie 3 will immediately bring that world to life, just for you.

## AI's Perspective

Genie 3 symbolizes the evolution of AI from an entity that simply "observes and summarizes" the world to one that "builds and simulates" it directly. While humans have lived within physical constraints until now, the development of world models will infinitely expand human experience and creativity by materializing any space we can imagine in real-time. This is more than just technological progress; it will be a major turning point that changes the very way humans experience the world.
