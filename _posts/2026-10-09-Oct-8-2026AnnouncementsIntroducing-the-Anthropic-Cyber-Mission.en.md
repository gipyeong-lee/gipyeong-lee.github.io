Imagine your everyday banking app or messenger suddenly hiring a 'superpowered security agent.' While security experts once had to manually search for risks and build defenses, we are entering an era where AI works around the clock, analyzing hacker entry points and deploying defenses autonomously. Anthropic, a leader in the AI industry, has announced a massive project called the 'Cyber Mission' to bring this future closer.

## Why is this important?

The landscape of cybercrime is changing much faster than we realize. In the past, hackers wrote their own code to infiltrate systems, but now, AI itself has begun to be used as a tool for hacking. Anthropic's reason for announcing this Cyber Mission is clear: as attackers use AI to launch more powerful attacks, 'defenders' need equally powerful and intelligent AI tools to stop them. [Introducing the Anthropic Cyber Mission](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-cyber-mission)

## Understanding it easily: A high-tech arsenal for security experts

In simple terms, the Anthropic Cyber Mission is an 'AI arsenal for security experts.' Let's use two analogies to understand it better.

First, the **'trained guard dog'** analogy. If the old security system was just a locked door (a firewall), now a guard dog that tracks hackers and sniffs out dangerous scents is stationed at the door. Anthropic intends to provide security experts with ongoing technical tools and research results so they can protect their systems, allowing the systems themselves to detect threats. [Introducing the Anthropic Cyber Mission](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-cyber-mission)

Second, the **'high-tech equipment supply'** analogy. If security experts are firefighters with basic knowledge, this mission is like an advanced support program that provides them with state-of-the-art body armor and precision detectors.

In fact, there was a painful impetus behind Anthropic's decision: the incident in September 2025. At the time, the hacker group 'GTG-1002' used AI's 'Agent' capability (AI that autonomously executes goals) to carry out an unprecedented level of cyber espionage. The AI was not just giving advice; it acted as a 'lead attacker,' executing the hacking itself. This event served as a major wake-up call for us. [Report on disrupting the AI espionage campaign](https://www.anthropic.com/research/disrupting-AI-espionage)

## Current situation

Anthropic is currently implementing various support policies so that security experts can work in a better environment. In September 2026, the scope of the 'Cyber Verification Program' was significantly expanded. This allows qualified security experts to utilize advanced cybersecurity features to pinpoint and block vulnerabilities in systems with greater precision. [Anthropic Newsroom](https://www.anthropic.com/news)

Anthropic is doing more than just providing security features; they update their usage policies annually to reflect the evolving capabilities of AI and customer feedback. This demonstrates that security is not a fixed state, but a living thing that must constantly evolve. [2026 Usage Policy Update](https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-usage-policy-update)

## What will happen in the future?

The battle between hacking and defense is expected to intensify, shifting from 'human vs. human' to 'AI vs. AI.' Anthropic's mission will be the first step toward an era where security defenders move beyond just blocking attacks and instead manage systems safely, efficiently, and proactively through AI—allowing us to enjoy the digital world with greater peace of mind.

## MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Perspective

Technological progress is a double-edged sword. If the 2025 espionage campaign exploited the vulnerabilities of security using AI, now the companies that created the technology must take the lead in strengthening that shield. AI is no longer optional; it is becoming an essential security partner in modern digital society. In an era where the shield is becoming stronger than the spear, Anthropic's actions are highly anticipated.
