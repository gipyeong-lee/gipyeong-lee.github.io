Imagine waking up, sitting in front of your computer, and telling your AI, "Find and fix the errors in this complex code, and create a document based on the contents of this photo." Previously, you would have had to assign these two tasks to different specialized AIs or finish them yourself due to performance limitations. However, we are entering an era where 'expert' AIs can gather within a single body to collaborate more intelligently.

The news announced today by the French AI company Mistral AI signals that this future has moved one step closer. It is the arrival of a next-generation AI model: 'Mistral Large 4.' [Source: France's Mistral Announces New AI Model](https://www.msn.com/en-us/technology/artificial-intelligence/france-s-mistral-announces-new-ai-model/ar-AA2dGbU6)

## Why is this important?

The way we use AI in our daily lives is becoming increasingly sophisticated. Beyond simply answering questions, AI is now required to write complex programming code, analyze photos or videos, and perform reasoning that transcends multiple languages.

The newly unveiled Mistral Large 4 is an 'open-weight' model. This means that countless developers around the world can utilize the internal structure of this AI to modify and improve it according to their specific goals. Through this, companies can create faster and more efficient customized AI services. [Source: Mistral Large 4 - Mistral AI | Mistral Docs](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/mistral-large-4-0), [Source: Models - From Cloud to Edge | Mistral](https://mistral.ai/models/)

## Understanding Simply: '1 Trillion Puzzle Pieces' and 'Expert Collaboration'

Let's explain why Mistral Large 4 is special with two concepts.

First, the **majesty of scale**. This model consists of a staggering 1 trillion (1.05T) parameters. Parameters are the 'numeric values' that AI stores and adjusts to govern knowledge during the learning process; 1 trillion is a massive number, more than 20,000 times the entire population of South Korea. [Source: Mistral Large 4 - Mistral AI | Mistral Docs](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/mistral-large-4-0)

Second, the **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) structure**. To put it simply, instead of the entire AI struggling to solve all problems by itself, it operates like a 'general hospital' where doctors are divided by specialty.

As an analogy, when a coding question comes in, the 'coding expert' part is activated, and when analyzing a picture, the 'visual expert' part operates. While Mistral Large 4 possesses 1 trillion parameters of vast knowledge using this structure, it efficiently uses only 49 billion parameters when providing an actual answer. [Source: Mistral Large 4 - Mistral AI | Mistral Docs](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/mistral-large-4-0) Thanks to this, we can get answers that are both smarter and faster. Additionally, it is equipped with a vision encoder consisting of 1.6 billion parameters, greatly increasing its understanding of images. [Source: Mistral Large 4 - Mistral AI | Mistral Docs](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/mistral-large-4-0)

## Current Status

Currently, Mistral Large 4 is in the 'public preview' stage, where it can be used first in API form through 'Mistral Studio.' [Source: Introducing Mistral Large 4 | Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/) Initial test results indicate that it is receiving high praise for its performance, particularly in the fields of coding and image analysis (Vision). [Source: Mistral Releases Large 4, a 1 Trillion Parameter Open-Weight AI Model](https://thenextweb.com/news/mistral-releases-large-4-a-1-trillion-parameter-open-weight-ai-model)

It is not yet possible for everyone to install and use this model directly on their own computers. Mistral has stated that it plans to release the official model weights on October 27. [Source: Mistral Releases Large 4, a 1 Trillion Parameter Open-Weight AI Model](https://thenextweb.com/news/mistral-releases-large-4-a-1-trillion-parameter-open-weight-ai-model) When that day comes, countless open-source developers around the world will begin to utilize this massive AI to create creative services.

## What will happen in the future?

AI technology is now moving from the era of 'who is smarter' to 'who collaborates more efficiently.' With the increase of high-performance open models like Mistral Large 4, an environment is being created where not only giant technology companies monopolize AI, but individual developers and small to medium-sized enterprises can also combine their ideas with top-tier AI. It is a major point of interest to watch how many ingenious AI services based on this model will pour out over the next few months.
