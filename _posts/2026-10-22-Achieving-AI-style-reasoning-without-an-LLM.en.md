Imagine you need to solve a complex math problem or make an important business decision. Until now, we have naturally assumed, "If I ask a smart AI (LLM), it will give me the answer." But is that really true? Artificial intelligence does not necessarily have to be a 'Large Language Model (LLM)' trained on vast amounts of data to solve problems.

Recently, the AI industry has seen the emergence of new models that focus on the fundamental question of "how to reason intelligently" rather than just increasing their size. It is similar to how we don't blindly memorize every piece of knowledge when taking an exam, but rather identify the core of the problem and approach it logically. Today, we delve into the world of AI that reasons like a human without an LLM.

## Why It Matters

Until now, mainstream AI has relied on learning massive amounts of data to predict the most statistically probable next word [Source: Jev + Spring Boot:AIDecisionsWITHOUTanLLM?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ai7oVfbFKts). In simple terms, it's like a pro at guessing what word comes next after reading countless books. However, this method sometimes produces nonsensical answers or commits logical errors. It also has the critical downside of consuming enormous costs and electricity to train on trillions of parameters [Source: Complete Comparative Analysis of AI Language Models: LLM · SLM · sLLM · VLM · Vertical AI](https://sieos.tistory.com/entry/AI-언어-모델-완전-비교-분석-LLM-·-SLM-·-sLLM-·-VLM-·-Vertical-AI).

What if there were an AI that could reason deeply like a human without an LLM? It would enable the use of sensitive internal corporate data, AI operation on devices with limited power, and high-accuracy decision-making specialized for specific fields. This signifies that AI technology is evolving from a mere "tool for speaking well" into a "practical problem solver."

## The Explainer

'Non-LLM' reasoning AI works in several different ways. Let's use some analogies to help you understand.

First, there is the **'choice-centered' model**. Systems like 'Jev' are representative. While a traditional LLM is a writer filling an infinite number of pages from a blank slate, Jev is like a logic test-taker who chooses the most appropriate answer from a set of given options. It is a method of evaluating predefined choices based on current situational information to make the optimal decision [Source: Jev + Spring Boot:AIDecisionsWITHOUTanLLM?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ai7oVfbFKts).

Second, there is the **'human brain imitation' model**. The 'Hierarchical Reasoning Model (HRM)' follows the way our brain processes complex information. Just as a photo app filter refines an image layer by layer, it divides information into hierarchies to reason in stages [Source: Scientists just developed a new AI modeled on the human brain ...](https://www.livescience.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/scientists-just-developed-an-ai-modeled-on-the-human-brain-and-its-outperforming-llms-like-chatgpt-at-reasoning-tasks). This model outperformed existing, supposedly "smart" LLMs in high-difficulty benchmark tests.

Finally, there is the **'independent self-learning' model**. Instead of showing AI vast amounts of human training data, researchers had the AI create and solve its own challenges [Source: AI Masters Complex Reasoning Without Human Training Data](https://www.implicator.ai/ai-learns-complex-reasoning-without-human-training-data/). It's like teaching a student how a puzzle works by letting them solve it alone, rather than giving them an answer key to memorize. They are learning complex reasoning techniques on their own without human examples.

## Where We Stand

The current AI industry is diversifying into various directions based on goals and constraints, such as LLMs, SLMs (Small Language Models), and VLMs (Vision Language Models) [Source: Complete Comparative Analysis of AI Language Models: LLM · SLM · sLLM · VLM · Vertical AI](https://sieos.tistory.com/entry/AI-언어-모델-완전-비교-분석-LLM-·-SLM-·-sLLM-·-VLM-·-Vertical-AI). Among them, non-LLM reasoning models are evolving by directly implementing the "process of thinking." Ant Group has even developed a non-reasoning model that performs immediate step-by-step reasoning processes to produce a final response without a separate large model [Source: Ling-1T Leads Non-ReasoningPerformance, MCP Poses Security...](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/issue-324/).

Of course, there are limitations. The LLMs we have experienced so far are very adept at pouring out vast knowledge and continuing conversations. On the other hand, specialized reasoning models are excellent at decision-making in specific domains, but they may not show the broad, encyclopedic knowledge that LLMs do.

## What's Next

The AI ecosystem of the future will move not toward "which model is bigger," but "which model thinks more efficiently." Researchers are focusing on developing Embodied AI (AI that interacts with the physical world, like robots) that can reason about causal relationships [Source: Beyond LLMs: The Uncharted Path to Artificial General ...](https://anshadameenza.com/blog/technology/beyond-llms-path-to-agi).

In particular, self-learning AI that creates and solves its own tasks shows the possibility of a new intelligence that surpasses human data. In the future, you might meet 'your own expert AI' inside your device that makes fast and smart logical judgments without a massive server.
