# The Largest IPO in History: The Day Anthropic Surpassed SpaceX and Its Significance

Imagine a moment when a company that has quietly researched safe and reliable technology behind the AI services we use every day suddenly leaps into Nasdaq, the epicenter of global financial markets, and smashes all-time records. It is like a cinematic scene where a scientist who has long been absorbed in a laboratory steps into the center of the global stage, basking in the spotlight of the entire world.

Anthropic, an artificial intelligence safety and research company, has created an absolute massive storm in the securities market. Easily surpassing the record set just a few months ago by Elon Musk's mega-enterprise SpaceX, it has pulled off the largest initial public offering (IPO) in history [[Source 7]].

This week, the global financial markets and the entire tech industry have been heatedly abuzz with this unprecedented, mega-scale event. What exactly happened behind the scenes of Anthropic's listing stage, and what does it mean for us?

## Why Does This Matter?

Artificial intelligence technology, which has deeply penetrated our daily lives, is no longer merely a toy inside a laboratory; it has become the core of a massive real economy where capital worth trillions moves.

Anthropic's listing is not just news in the stock market. It is the definitive proof of how much capital AI technology development demands and how highly global investors value the future worth of AI.

To put it in perspective, it is similar to the moment when smartphones first emerged and changed the world, with massive global capital concentrating on the underlying infrastructure. From the perspective of the general public, this illustrates how vastly the ecosystem supporting the AI tools we use is evolving. Furthermore, the actions of these tech giants, who bypassed established Wall Street financial rules in favor of their own methods, represent a historic event of the century that could alter the very way companies raise capital going forward.

## Easy to Understand: What Is an IPO, and Why Was It Called "Unusual"?

Simply put, an **IPO (Initial Public Offering)** is a company's debut stage in the stock market where a previously privately held company sells shares to the general public and investors for the first time to raise massive amounts of capital. It is much like a famous restaurant that has long served only local regulars opening up partnership opportunities to the general public in order to expand into a nationwide chain.

Typically, when a company conducts an IPO, traditional Wall Street investment bankers take the lead, meticulously crafting stock price ranges and following customary procedures. However, this time, Anthropic pushed ahead with an **extremely unique and unusual IPO process**, much like SpaceX did before it [[Source 9]].

Rather than blindly following the long-standing practices or advice of traditional bankers, Anthropic stuck to its own approach, causing considerable consternation and chafing among traditional financial experts [[Source 9]]. This is comparable to a famous musician rejecting the control of traditional major agencies and selling out an unprecedented world tour using their own independent method.

## Current Situation: A Massive Wave of $107.3 Billion and a Spectacular Nasdaq Debut

Expressed in numbers, Anthropic's listing report card is nothing short of phenomenal.

* **Record-Breaking Capital Raising**: Anthropic successfully raised a total of $107.3 billion through this offering (over $81 billion based on issuance size, up to $93.5 billion if underwriters exercise additional options). This easily eclipsed SpaceX's $7.5 billion recorded last June, making it the largest IPO in history [[Source 3], [Source 4], [Source 7], [Source 8], [Source 17]].
* **Offering Price and First-Day Surge**: Anthropic set its final offering price at **$83**, exceeding the initial price guidance range ($70 to $78) it had initially presented to the market [[Source 4], [Source 17]]. On its first day of trading on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol **'ANT'**, the stock opened strong and surged 34% above the offering price to close at **$111.50** [[Source 7]].
* **Astronomical Valuation**: Through this listing, Anthropic achieved an astronomical market valuation ranging between approximately $1.8 trillion and $2 trillion on a fully diluted basis [[Source 8], [Source 10], [Source 17]]. As a result, it proudly secured a spot among the top 10 publicly traded companies globally, standing shoulder-to-shoulder with SpaceX and even threatening Amazon's standing [[Source 10]].
* **Public Offering Volume**: Anthropic offered a massive volume to the public, totaling approximately 98 billion shares (980 million shares), which included shares held by initial investors and shareholders alongside the company's primary issuance [[Source 8]].

However, beneath such glittering records, tension lingered. Despite immense retail trading enthusiasm, first-day price gains showed somewhat restrained movements in certain respects [[Source 3]], and amid broader market volatility, concerns and tensions regarding potential overheating in the AI sector intersected across parts of the market [[Source 12], [Source 18]].
