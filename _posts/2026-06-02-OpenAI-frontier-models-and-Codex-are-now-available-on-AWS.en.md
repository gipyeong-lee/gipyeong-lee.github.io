When we search for something in our daily lives or need a flash of inspiration, there is a specific assistant we turn to first: ChatGPT. This technology, which produces logical and rich answers as if you were talking to a human, has already completely transformed our daily routines.

However, in the important meeting rooms of companies where the most intense work happens, or in front of the monitors of developers where security is paramount, conversations like these often take place:

"If I asked ChatGPT, this would be organized in a minute... but is it safe to input our internal customer data or confidential code there?"
"Absolutely not! If company secrets leak to an external server, it will be an irreversible catastrophe."

For the many companies that had to keep the world's smartest AI at arm's length due to the massive wall of "security," long-awaited news has finally arrived. OpenAI, the creator of ChatGPT, has officially stepped into the arms of Amazon Web Services (AWS), the world's largest cloud service provider.

OpenAI's proudest cutting-edge models, **GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.4**, along with the smart AI assistant for developers, **Codex**, have been officially launched on Amazon's enterprise AI platform, "Amazon Bedrock" [Get started with OpenAI GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4 models, and Codex on Amazon Bedrock | AWS News Blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/get-started-with-openai-gpt-5-5-gpt-5-4-models-and-codex-on-amazon-bedrock/).

This is not just a simple addition of new software. We will explain in detail why this event is shaking up the global IT industry and how it will immediately change the way your company works.

## Why It Matters

The core significance is that **the "world's smartest brain" can now be safely invited into "our company's strongest and most tightly closed vault."**

Today, many companies worldwide already store their important data and core systems in the massive cloud known as Amazon Web Services (AWS). Over the years, companies have established strict security rules and built ironclad defenses within this AWS environment to prevent hacking or data leaks.

Until now, to properly use OpenAI's powerful technology in a corporate setting, companies had to either go through Microsoft's cloud or send their data to OpenAI's external servers. For companies that previously only used Amazon's cloud, this meant building and verifying an entirely new security system from scratch—a cumbersome and risky process. However, as the exclusive contract structure between Microsoft and OpenAI changed, OpenAI's technology has finally landed officially on Amazon's cloud [OpenAI'slatestAImodels,CodexavailableonAmazonBedrock...](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/openais-latest-ai-models-codex-now-available-on-amazon-bedrock/articleshow/130587138.cms).

**Let's use a simpler analogy.** Suppose you live in a luxury apartment complex (AWS) that boasts the tightest security in town. Outside the complex, there is a restaurant run by a Michelin 3-star genius chef (OpenAI). Previously, to taste the chef's fantastic dishes, you had to pack up precious ingredients (sensitive corporate data) from your refrigerator and visit the chef's restaurant outside the apartment. You were always anxious about those ingredients being stolen or lost on the way.

Now, the situation has completely changed. That genius chef has started **coming directly into your apartment complex to cook!** Companies can now utilize the world's best AI to their heart's content while maintaining the strict security, complex compliance, and meticulous management systems they have trusted for years [OpenAI makes GPT-5.5 and Codex available on Amazon ...](https://digg.com/ai/d93d3huv).

There are also practical advantages. The cost burden on companies has not increased at all. Moving to a premium platform like Amazon doesn't mean OpenAI is charging extra. The usage fees for OpenAI models provided on Amazon Bedrock are exactly the same as the "first-party rates" paid when billing directly with OpenAI [OpenAI models GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.4—and Codex—now ...](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/bedrock-openai-models). Furthermore, these fees can be paid using the "committed spend" agreements companies already have with AWS, making internal accounting and purchasing approvals surprisingly simple [OpenAIFrontierModelsandCodexNowAvailableonAWS](https://cloudninjas.ca/ai/openai-frontier-models-and-codex-now-available-on-aws/).

## The Explainer

The core OpenAI technologies that have moved into the fortress of Amazon Bedrock can be divided into two main categories. Let's look at how they can help your company's work.

### 1. Frontier Models that Break Limits (GPT-5.5 & GPT-5.4)
In the IT industry, "Frontier" refers to the edge or the front line. It is an honorable title given to the smartest and most powerful cutting-edge AI models achievable by humanity today. The newly released GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.4 are these frontier models [OpenAI models and Codex on Amazon Bedrock are now generally available | Artificial Intelligence](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/openai-models-and-codex-on-amazon-bedrock-are-now-generally-available/). These models go far beyond just writing plausible text; they have the incredible ability to reason through complex logic and analyze vast amounts of corporate data in an instant.

Notably, these smart artificial brains operate on Amazon Bedrock's "Next-generation inference engine" [OpenAI models GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.4—and Codex—now ...](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/bedrock-openai-models). An inference engine is, essentially, the "thought motor" that allows an AI to process a human's question at high speed and generate the optimal answer. Thanks to this motor specially designed for high performance by Amazon, even the most complex and massive corporate tasks can be processed quickly and smoothly without bottlenecks. It can handle the simultaneous analysis of millions of customers' data without breaking a sweat.

### 2. A Magic Wand for Developers, Codex
The other protagonist, Codex, is an "AI coding agent" specially trained to accelerate the work of software developers [Get started with OpenAI GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4 models, and Codex on Amazon Bedrock | AWS News Blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/get-started-with-openai-gpt-5-5-gpt-5-4-models-and-codex-on-amazon-bedrock/). While general chatbot AI understands human language (Korean, English, etc.), Codex is perfectly fluent in computer languages (Python, JavaScript, etc.) as if it were a native speaker.

**Let's use another fun analogy.** Codex is like a "highly experienced genius junior assistant with light-speed typing" who sits right next to a developer's monitor 24/7. A developer only needs to give instructions in plain language, such as "Create a button on our website for customers to log in with their Google IDs and build a function to securely save customer info to the database." Codex then types out hundreds of lines of complex, sophisticated computer code in the blink of an eye. The developer simply needs to review and approve the code.

## Where We Stand

So, how are developers and companies currently applying this massive technology to their daily work?

Amazon has opened various "magical doors" so that developers can call upon AI in the way that best suits their working style and company environment. First, it provides the **Bedrock API (Application Programming Interface)**. An API is a bridge that allows programs to exchange data and talk to each other. Through this bridge, companies can seamlessly connect OpenAI's intelligence to their existing software or apps.

Additionally, three customized tools are supported for handling the coding genius Codex: the "Codex CLI (Command Line Interface)" for giving expert commands by typing text on a black screen, an intuitive "Codex Desktop app" that installs like a regular program, and an extension for "Visual Studio Code," the coding-specific notepad that almost every developer in the world uses daily [OpenAI'sfrontierAImodelsandCodexnowavailableon... - Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/openais-frontier-ai-models-and-codex-now-available-on-amazon-bedrock/). Developers can call upon their AI assistant as naturally as breathing, without even needing to close their familiar tool windows or switch screens.

In a situation where Microsoft Azure cloud virtually monopolized OpenAI's latest technology, this announcement from Amazon is causing a massive seismic shift in the entire enterprise IT industry [OpenAImodelsnowavailableonAWSBedrock, reshaping...](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/revolterab_openai-models-now-available-on-aws-bedrock-activity-7455141489531101184-QSWP).
