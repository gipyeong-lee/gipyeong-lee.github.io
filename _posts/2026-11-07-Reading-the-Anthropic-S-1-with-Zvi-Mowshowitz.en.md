Imagine this: The AI services we use every day are built upon a company's specific philosophy, and the company is making massive bets to power these enormous artificial intelligence models. Recently, Zvi Mowshowitz, an influential observer in the AI field, meticulously analyzed Anthropic's 'S-1', the registration statement filed for its Initial Public Offering (IPO).

The S-1 is a key document that companies must submit to financial authorities before going public, containing everything from internal company details and risk factors to strategic plans. Through this document, we will peek behind the curtain of Anthropic.

### Why is this important?

A company going public means more than just raising capital. It means becoming subject to public scrutiny and the requirement to transparently disclose business conditions. Anthropic is currently at the forefront of AI technology, alongside OpenAI. Understanding how Anthropic has designed its governance and how much capital it is pouring into the future is not just a company-specific issue; it is a milestone that will determine the direction in which the AI technology that changes our lives will unfold [Source 9].

### Easy to Understand: Anthropic's 'Ironclad' Governance

The most striking part of this S-1 document is the company's decision-making structure. Anthropic is designed to allow its founders to exercise powerful control through special shares called 'Class F' [Source 8]. This single class of shares holds 50.1% of the total voting power [Source 8, Source 9].

In simple terms, even if external investors or shareholders oppose management policies, the founders' will becomes Anthropic's compass. By doing this, Anthropic has built an 'ironclad' structure that allows them to steadfastly push forward with the long-term technology vision and safety policies they have set, rather than chasing short-term profits [Source 9].

### The Astronomical Figure of $900 Billion

AI is ultimately a battle of 'Compute' (the computing resources required for artificial intelligence to process complex information). According to the S-1 document, Anthropic has signed contracts worth an unimaginable $900 billion to secure computing resources in the future [Source 8, Source 9].

Can you grasp this number? It is not just a matter of buying a few good servers. It is like declaring that they will build a massive 'AI power plant' at the cost of a nation's budget. This is a clear example of how vast the data being processed by current AI models is and how aggressively companies are making their moves to maintain dominance in this technology [Source 9].

### Unique Corporate Culture: 3:1 Donation Matching

Anthropic is also garnering industry attention for its employee benefits. The most notable is the 'Unlimited 3:1 Matching' program [Source 8]. If an employee donates $1 to a charitable organization, the company contributes $3, tripling the amount. This may well be one of the most expensive employee benefits in corporate history [Source 8]. Beyond mere financial support, this program is interpreted as an intention to share the company's philosophy of valuing social responsibility with its members [Source 9].

Metaphorically, it is like the company giving the employees' good intentions 'three-fold wings.' They have designed a system where the company's success becomes society's joy.

### What happens next?

Expert Zvi Mowshowitz is keeping a very close watch on Anthropic's moves [Source 16]. He is analyzing how the high-growth dynamics and enormous computing infrastructure investments currently displayed by Anthropic will impact the AI industry ecosystem in the future [Source 9].

The point we need to watch is whether this massive flow of capital will actually lead to smarter and safer AI models. Under the powerful leadership of its founders, Anthropic is currently prioritizing the value of 'AI safety,' but the key will be how they harmonize this with the demands of large-scale shareholders after the IPO [Source 9, Source 14].
