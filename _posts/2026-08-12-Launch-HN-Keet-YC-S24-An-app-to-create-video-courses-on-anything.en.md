Imagine this: on your way home from work today, you suddenly feel like learning about 'quantum mechanics' or 'French cuisine.' In the past, you would have had to buy thick books or search endlessly through ad-filled YouTube videos. But what if you could just open your smartphone, say the topic you want to learn, and have a professional course tailored just for you appear right before your eyes?

'Keet,' a startup recently selected for the S24 batch of Y Combinator (YC), a Silicon Valley startup incubator, is making that future a reality.

### Why does this service matter?

For most people, the process of learning something new is like a massive 'barrier to entry.' We often give up before we even start, making excuses like "I don't know where to begin" or "I don't have enough time."

Keet solves this problem. [Keet is a mobile application that generates a systematic educational process immediately upon receiving any topic the user wants to learn.](https://modernorange.io/item/49259309) Because you can just follow a curriculum designed by someone else—like at a school or academy—it dramatically reduces the initial burden of starting to learn. It will be a powerful tool for those who want to move beyond simple information searching and integrate learning easily into their daily lives.

### Simply put, what kind of service is it?

You can think of Keet's learning method as your 'personalized digital private tutor.'

Just as we find joy in learning languages with Duolingo (an app that makes learning foreign languages feel like a game), [Keet provides educational content by combining short explanatory videos with games to reinforce what you've learned.](https://www.trykeet.com/)

Think about it easily. You internalize core concepts through **short videos**, just like watching a movie, and then verify what you've just learned through **games**, as if solving a puzzle. This method sticks in your memory much longer than simply reading and memorizing knowledge. [Crucially, the heart of this app is that it doesn't just provide fragmented information, but is designed to allow systematic learning over a long period by mirroring actual educational curricula.](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/keet)

### What is its current status?

Currently, [Keet supports anyone in generating courses on their desired topics through its mobile app and provides a systematic learning experience.](https://www.linkedin.com/company/keetapp) It is particularly focused on [inducing continuous learning by following curricula designed like school classes.](https://modernorange.io/item/49259309)

However, keep in mind that this service acts as a 'guide' that helps users find a structured path tailored to their level within the vast ocean of knowledge. While it significantly increases learning efficiency, the user's will to open the app and participate consistently remains essential.

### How will it develop in the future?

The emergence of Keet shows that the way we consume learning content is moving from 'passive viewing' to 'active participation.' As AI becomes smarter, it has the potential to evolve into 'hyper-personalized education' that goes beyond just creating courses—real-time assessment of the user's understanding, adjusting progress, or pinpointing and supplementing weak areas.

The era is coming where a single smartphone is all you need to walk through the world's knowledge at your own pace. What do you want to learn today?
