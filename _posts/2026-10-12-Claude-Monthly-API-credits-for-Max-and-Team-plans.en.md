Imagine this: Every morning, as you organize your day while chatting with Claude, you suddenly get the thought, "I want to build my own AI assistant app." In the past, you might have hesitated due to the costs of using an API (Application Programming Interface, the path through which different software communicates), but it's a different story now if you are a Claude subscriber. Anthropic has started gifting monthly API 'credits' to Claude Max and Team plan subscribers so they can use the API freely.

## Why does this matter?

Until now, the Claude chat service and API usage were completely separate contracts. You paid a monthly subscription fee for chat, but you had to pay separately to use the API. [Source: Claude Max and Team Plans Now Get API Credits Every Month...](https://dev.to/uehara/claude-max-and-team-plans-now-get-api-credits-every-month-200-every-month-on-max-20x-4k1n)

However, with this change, subscribers can now cover not only their daily chats but also the costs of developing their own apps or agents (autonomous AI that performs specific tasks on its own) within their subscription fee. Users who simply used AI as a "conversation window" can now take a step closer to becoming "developers who build services" using AI themselves. [Source: Monthly API credits for Max and Team plans | Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17154008-monthly-api-credits-for-max-and-team-plans)

## An easy analogy

Let's use an analogy to understand this benefit. Imagine you have an "unlimited beverage subscription" at your favorite cafe. Now, imagine the cafe owner says, "Don't just drink beverages with this subscription; you can run a small baking corner inside the store and bake your own bread to sell," and supports you by covering the cost of ingredients (API credits) every month.

The API is the path that lends the "AI's brain" to your computer program. You can embed powerful AI models like Claude 3.5 Sonnet or Haiku 5.5 [Source: Claude Max and Team API credit amounts and rules | NeoTeo](https://www.neoteo.com/en/anthropic-announced-monthly-api-credits-for-claude-max-and-team) into your app to implement your own features. Just remember that these credits are strictly for the Claude Platform API and cannot be used in the 'Claude Code' environment where you chat on the screen. [Source: Claude Max and Team Get Monthly API Credits, Not Code](https://threatfrontier.com/articles/claude-max-team-plans-monthly-api-credits-not-claude-code)

## Current status

Anthropic officially implemented this policy starting October 7, 2026. [Source: Claude Max & Team API Credits (Oct 2026): $100/$200/$500](https://qcode.cc/en/claude-max-team-api-credits) The monthly credit amounts provided by plan are as follows:

*   **Max 5x Plan:** $100 provided monthly [Source: Claude API Credits (2026): Max $100-$200, Team Up to $500 a Month](https://justinmckelvey.com/blog/claude-api-credits)
*   **Max 20x Plan:** $200 provided monthly [Source: Claude API Credits (2026): Max $100-$200, Team Up to $500 a Month](https://justinmckelvey.com/blog/claude-api-credits)
*   **Team Plan:** Provides a consolidated pool (up to $500) calculated at $20 per standard seat and $100 per premium seat [Source: Claude API Credits (2026): Max $100-$200, Team Up to $500 a Month](https://justinmckelvey.com/blog/claude-api-credits)

To use the credits, you must link your Console organization in the 'Billing' section of your claude.ai settings. [Source: Free Claude API Credits on Max and Team Plans: How to Claim ...](https://aicatchup.com/news/claude-max-team-free-monthly-api-credits) One thing to note is that these credits do not roll over. They are newly issued at the beginning of every month, and any unused balance expires when the next month begins. [Source: Claude Max and Team Get Monthly API Credits, Not Code](https://threatfrontier.com/articles/claude-max-team-plans-monthly-api-credits-not-claude-code)

## What's next?

This benefit will be a great opportunity for many creators who want to go beyond simply owning AI to making it their own tool. Especially since price-efficient models like Haiku 5.5 are offered via API, an environment has been created where you can sufficiently conduct small-scale projects or experiment with ideas. [Source: Anthropic launches Claude Haiku 5.5 at lower API prices](https://www.testingcatalog.com/anthropic-launches-claude-haiku-5-5-at-lower-api-prices/)

If there was an AI service you had always wanted to implement, this month is the optimal time to bring that idea into the world. A membership you thought was a waste of subscription fees might just become the solid foundation for building your own cool service.
