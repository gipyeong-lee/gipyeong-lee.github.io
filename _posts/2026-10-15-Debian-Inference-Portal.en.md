Behind the operating systems that power the computers and servers we use every day are projects like 'Debian,' where countless developers revise code and squash bugs day and night. Recently, however, an exciting change has occurred in the Debian project—a Linux distribution famous for being meticulous and uncompromising. Debian has announced that it has recruited a large number of 'AI assistants' specifically for its developers.

### Why is this important?

Imagine a developer maintaining a complex Linux package. Finding bugs among thousands of lines of code or refactoring for efficiency is tedious work that requires significant time and focus. What if a very smart AI were by their side, suggesting, "You could make this part safer and cleaner by fixing it like this"?

The 'Debian Inference Portal' released by the Debian project makes this possible. The [Debian Inference Portal](https://inference.debian.net/) is a service that helps Debian developers freely utilize AI and Large Language Models (LLMs)—AI that understands and generates human-like language by learning from vast amounts of data—during their work. Developers can now use the powerful tool of AI to improve their Linux distribution environment without worrying about personal costs. [Debian-Linux.cz](https://www.debian-linux.cz/)

### Simply put: What is the role of AI?

In short, this portal is an 'AI-exclusive library' and 'studio' for Debian developers. The 'library' refers to the AI models that possess vast knowledge, and the 'studio' is the space where developers can fully utilize these models.

To use an analogy, it is similar to a chef asking an AI, "How can I make a healthier and tastier sauce with these ingredients?" while developing a new recipe. Debian developers can ask the AI, "How should I modify this package configuration file to optimize it further?"

The AI perfectly grasps the context of the code, helping developers handle tasks that would take tens of minutes in just a few seconds. Currently, the Qwen model is available on this portal. [ContentBuffer News](https://contentbuffer.com/news/debian-inference-portal-adds-qwen-model-tracks-budget-950295b8)

### Current Status: Who can use it and how?

The Debian project has not thrown these doors wide open to everyone. Only 'Debian Developers (DD)' and 'Package Maintainers (DM)' who substantially contribute to the Debian project can enjoy these benefits. [DebianInferencePortal](https://inference.debian.net/)

To use this service, you log in with your 'Salsa' account, which is Debian's official site for contributors. It is just like swiping an employee badge to enter an office. After logging in, it is very systematic; developers can manage individual API keys and check their real-time budget usage for AI consumption. [DebianInferencePortal](https://inference.debian.net/)

All of these services are operated with the support of a company called Scaleway. [ABCLinuxu.cz](https://www.abclinuxu.cz/zpravicky/debian-inference-portal) In other words, an external company has donated the valuable resource of AI so that the Debian ecosystem can remain healthy.

### What happens next?

The opening of this portal is a symbolic event indicating that Debian has officially begun to embrace AI technology within its development workflow. Debian developers are expected to encounter more AI models here in the future.

Most importantly, the question is what contribution AI will make to Debian's philosophy of 'stability' and 'reliability.' Watching the achievements that Debian developers—assisted by their AI counterparts—will produce in an increasingly complex modern software environment will be a very interesting point to watch for Linux users.
