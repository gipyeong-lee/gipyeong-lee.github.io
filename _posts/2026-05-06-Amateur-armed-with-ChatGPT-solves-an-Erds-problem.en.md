## Easy Understanding: 'No Multiples Club' and AI's Shortcut

The problem solved this time was a conjecture about "Primitive sets" left by Paul Erdős, one of the greatest mathematicians of the 20th century [Source 7](https://www.indiatoday.in/education-today/news/story/liam-price-solves-60-year-old-erdos-math-puzzle-with-chatgpt-2903168-2026-05-01), [Source 12](https://www.weaving.news/news/019dc7a1-8ddd-7770-81d2-d328b83864f0). The name is difficult, but the principle is surprisingly simple.

### What is a Primitive Set?
To understand this concept, let's imagine a **"No Multiples Club."** The rules of this club are very clear.
> "No number among the club members should be a multiple of another member."

For example, if `2` is a member, numbers like `4, 6, 8, 10` can never join. If `3, 5, 7` are members, they can coexist peacefully because they are not multiples of each other. Mathematicians call a collection of numbers that follow these rules a "primitive set." Paul Erdős asked a very tricky question about the unique properties of these number sets, and the answer had been lost in a labyrinth for 60 years [Source 2](https://best-ai.org/ai-news/amateur-armed-with-chatgpt-solves-a-60-year-old-erds-problem), [Source 4](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/amateur-armed-chatgpt-vibe-maths-123000665.html).

### How did the AI solve it?
Liam Price entered this problem using the latest AI model, GPT-5.4 Pro [Source 3](https://byteiota.com/amateur-solves-60-year-erdos-problem-with-chatgpt/). Without even knowing how famous the mystery was, he simply sent a single request (prompt) like, "Prove whether this problem is true or false" [Source 3](https://byteiota.com/amateur-solves-60-year-erdos-problem-with-chatgpt/), [Source 9](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47903126).

ChatGPT created a perfect proof through a reasoning process of building logic for about 80 minutes [Source 3](https://byteiota.com/amateur-solves-60-year-erdos-problem-with-chatgpt/), [Source 11](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/chatgpt-credited-with-solving-60-year-old-erdős-math-problem/gm-GM45F355EF). **To use an analogy**, until now, all mathematicians walked through a dense forest, hacking away at the undergrowth to find a path. However, the AI launched a drone to look down at the entire forest and found a shortcut in a completely different direction that no one had thought of. Professor Terence Tao marveled at the AI's innovative approach, saying, "All previous researchers had taken the wrong path from the very first step" [Source 6](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3784815604817154).

Liam Price's case will not end as a one-time happening. This is because an "AI-for-Erdős craze" is already blowing, with many amateurs using ChatGPT to challenge various mysteries [Source 1](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/amateur-armed-with-chatgpt-vibe-maths-a-60-year-old-problem/).
