Imagine this: You ask an AI, "How's the weather today?" as you usually do, but this time, an ad with a small "sponsored" label appears next to the response. Recently, ChatGPT has been making changes to the AI service that has become deeply embedded in our daily lives. OpenAI announced that it will expand the ad feature, which was being tested in the US, to major European markets by the end of this month. ([OpenAI set to expand ads to France, Germany and Ireland](https://digiday.com/marketing/openai-set-to-expand-ads-to-france-germany-and-ireland/))

### Why is this important?

This change is more than just an added feature; it is a signal that ChatGPT, with over 900 million weekly active users worldwide, is entering a full-scale "monetization model." ([OpenAI Eyes France, Germany and Ireland as European Ad Expansion Accelerates – citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/872082/openai-eyes-france-germany-and-ireland-as-european-ad-expansion-accelerates/)) Targeting the UK, France, and Germany—countries with large advertising markets—is seen as a strategy for OpenAI to stably secure the massive funds required to develop next-generation AI models. ([OpenAI extends ad pilot to Europe in a race to drive revenues](https://www.emarketer.com/content/openai-s-extends-ad-pilot-europe-race-drive-revenues))

### Simple explanation

You might be worried, "If ads are included, won't my questions get biased answers?" To use a simple analogy, think of the "articles" and "advertisements" in a newspaper. While the articles (the AI's response) are written honestly by the reporter, ads are placed in the corners of the page.

OpenAI explains that it will place these ads in a completely separate space, labeled "sponsored," so that they do not mix with the content of the AI's response. ([Google News -OpenAI's ChatGPTadstest - Overview](https://news.google.com/stories/CAAqNggKIjBDQklTSGpvSmMzUnZjbmt0TXpZd1NoRUtEd2lXaHFXLUVCRmx1LWdJYllYZ0VDZ0FQAQ?hl=en-US&gl=US&ceid=US:en)) They are also applying an "exclusion policy" that does not place ads on conversations related to sensitive topics such as politics, mental health, or personal health. ([OpenAI Allows Some Health & FinanceAdsIn ChatGPT via...](https://netzender.com/openai-allows-some-health-finance-ads-in-chatgpt-via-sejournal-mattgsouthern))

### Current status

Currently, this test is being conducted for free users and 'Go' tier subscribers, starting in the US. ([Source 1](https://theoutpost.ai/news-story/open-ai-begins-testing-chat-gpt-ads-this-month-following-meta-s-playbook-for-revenue-growth-23522/)) Conversely, users of paid subscription services like 'Plus', 'Pro', 'Business', and 'Enterprise' will continue to have an ad-free experience. ([Source 14](https://blog.lengow.com/chatgpt-ads-are-coming-to-europe/))

In Europe, a "stricter standard" slightly different from the US approach is applied. Because of Europe's stringent data privacy regulations (such as GDPR), users must explicitly choose (opt-in) to see personalized ads. ([OpenAImakes Cannes Lions debut, pitches ChatGPTadsbusiness as...](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/openai-makes-cannes-lions-debut-pitches-chatgpt-ads-business-as-it-challenges-googles-dominance-102154.htm))

### What happens next?

If OpenAI can successfully establish an advertising model, it could serve as a foundation for free users to access more advanced AI features for free or at a lower cost. However, a key point to watch moving forward is whether users' personal information remains securely protected and if the core value of the AI service—the "quality of questions and answers"—is maintained as the number of ads increases. OpenAI has officially promised not to sell users' conversation content to advertisers. ([OpenAI rolling outadstest in the US to free and... | TechNave](https://technave.com/gadget/OpenAI-rolling-out-ads-test-in-the-US-to-free-and-ChatGPT-Go-users-45477.html))

### MindTickleBytes' AI reporter's view

Seeing ads in exchange for free services is common in the internet world, but the meeting of "thinking AI" and advertising requires a more cautious approach. We will keep an eye on OpenAI's future moves to see if this European expansion can successfully catch the two rabbits of advertising profitability and user trust.
