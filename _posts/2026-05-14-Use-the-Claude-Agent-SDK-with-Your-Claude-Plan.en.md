## Have you ever thought, "I wish I had a 'smart digital twin' to do my work for me"?

Imagine this: Monday morning, the moment you arrive at work, you're faced with a mountain of emails, complex data analysis, and minor website bug fixes... instead of sweating through all this yourself, you simply say a word to the AI on your computer: "Take care of all this for me."

This isn't just an AI that gives good answers. It's a world where the AI independently sifts through folders to open files, understands the content, searches the internet for missing information, and even writes code to perfectly finish program modifications. This magical story has now become a reality right by our side.

Recently, Anthropic introduced the **'Claude Agent SDK,'** a tool that allows you to create AI that actually 'acts' on behalf of the user. In addition, they announced that from June 15, 2026, the billing system will be innovatively changed so that these smart AI workers can be used more comfortably.

Let's dive deep with MindTickleBytes into what has changed and what massive shifts will occur in the way we work.

---

## Why It Matters

Until now, AI mostly remained at the level of 'conversing' with us. It was like a kind of 'encyclopedia' that answered questions kindly and summarized long texts into readable formats. However, we are now moving into the era of **'Agents'** (AI assistants that judge and act independently).

### 1. The Arrival of 'Real-world Workers' Beyond Simple Conversation
By utilizing the newly released tools, you can make the AI step out of the chat window and actually operate your computer. It can edit code on its own, execute complex commands in the terminal (the window where you give text commands directly to the computer), and manage multi-step work processes independently [Source 7](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-agent-sdk-typescript), [Source 8](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-sdk/overview). Simply put, you now have an on-site technician who picks up tools and works, rather than just a consultant who speaks well.

### 2. A Separate Billing System: No More Worrying "Did I use up my daily questions?"
The most welcome news for users is the change in the payment method. From June 15, 2026, the number of times you chat with the AI (plan limits) and the usage of the AI agent working silently in the background will not be mixed [Source 1](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15036540-use-the-claude-agent-sdk-with-your-claude-plan).

To use an analogy, it's similar to how 'voice calls' and 'data' are managed separately in a smartphone plan. This means that even if you have many automation tasks running, you won't see that frustrating message saying, "You have exhausted your conversation limit for today," when you actually need to ask the AI a question.

---

## The Explainer

Do terms like 'SDK' and 'Agent' feel difficult? Let's explain again with a very simple analogy.

### The Agent SDK is like a 'Remote Controller'
If the existing Claude was a game character that only moved within the screen, the **Agent SDK (Software Development Kit)** is like a 'remote controller' or a 'special manual' that allows you to bring that character into your real office to perform work directly.

Developers can use this tool to give specific missions to the AI using programming languages like Python or TypeScript [Source 8](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-sdk/overview). For example, you could create a robot assistant that performs the command: "Check every link on our company website every morning, and if there's a broken link, write a report immediately."

### The New Credit System is like 'Two Wallets'
The system being introduced from June 15, 2026, gives us **two wallets** [Source 14](https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeAI/comments/1tc6nah/a_new_monthly_agent_sdk_credit_for_claude_plans/).

1.  **Chatting Wallet**: Used when we directly ask questions and get answers on the Claude website or app. (Included by default in existing paid subscriptions)
2.  **Agent-dedicated Credits**: Used when the AI assistant processes the automation tasks you've assigned in the background [Source 3](https://www.xda-developers.com/anthropics-claude-subscriptions-no-longer-include-agent-sdk-and-claude-p-usage/).

By separating the wallets this way, it strictly protects our precious 'direct conversation time' from being depleted, no matter how many tasks the AI assistant is performing [Source 1](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15036540-use-the-claude-agent-sdk-with-your-claude-plan).

## Where We Stand: What Can AI Assistants Do?

If you utilize the Claude Agent SDK right now (or use apps built on it), the AI exhibits the following remarkable capabilities:

-   **Reading and Editing Files**: It directly reads Excel or Word documents stored on your computer, corrects typos, or updates them with new figures [Source 8](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-sdk/overview).
-   **Executing Commands**: It can directly issue and perform commands like "Install this complex program for me" or "Organize the files in that folder by date" [Source 7](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-agent-sdk-typescript).
-   **Independent Web Search**: If it gets stuck while processing a task, it searches the internet on its own to find the latest information and reflects it in the work [Source 8](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-sdk/overview).
-   **Automatic Code Generation and Testing**: Even those who don't know programming can say, "Make an app with this function," and the AI will write the code and even test if it actually runs well [Source 12](https://serpapi.com/blog/build-an-ai-agent-with-claude-agent-sdk/).

This whole process is carried out through a fascinating method called the **'Agent Loop'** [Source 8](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-sdk/overview). Just as a great chef independently repeats the process of creating a recipe (Plan), preparing ingredients (Build), and tasting and refining (Run), the AI also produces perfect results through the stages of planning, execution, and verification [Source 5](https://www.kdnuggets.com/getting-started-with-the-claude-agent-sdk).

## What's Next: Things to Note and Future Outlook

Of course, such an excellent worker isn't free. From June 15, 2026, using agents through professional automation commands like 'claude -p' or external apps will consume 'dedicated credits' that you've topped up separately [Source 4](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-reinstates-openclaw-and-third-party-agent-usage-on-claude-subscriptions-with-a-catch). This change is a rule that applies commonly to all paid users, including Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise [Source 2](https://apidog.com/blog/claude-agent-sdk-with-claude-plan-setup-guide/).

There's one more piece of notable news. Anthropic recently upgraded Claude so that its answers follow set formats very strictly through a 'Structured Outputs' feature [Source 15](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/release-notes/overview). This means that the AI assistant won't ramble and will be able to perform the task reports we assigned according to exact table formats or data standards. It has become a much more reliable employee.

### Imagine: A Morning Scene in the Near Future
Your morning might soon change like this:
*"Claude, organize all the market research data that came in yesterday to create a draft report, and pick 3 key news items for me to read on my commute and send them to my messenger."*

While you leave home and get on the subway, your own digital twin, created with the Claude Agent SDK, will be processing all these tasks silently and more accurately than anyone else in the background.
