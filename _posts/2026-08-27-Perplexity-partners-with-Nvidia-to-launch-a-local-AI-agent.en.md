Imagine this: you wake up in the morning and tell your computer, "Organize the meeting materials I need for today." Until now, most generative AI we used had to pass through massive clouds (remote servers connected via the internet). But now, a future is approaching where that smart AI assistant processes tasks safely right on your desk, not on a server somewhere across the internet.

Recently, Perplexity, famous for its AI search service, partnered with the king of GPUs, Nvidia, to unveil a new AI agent platform called 'Portable Computer' ([Perplexity-Nvidia Portable Computer Launch](https://venturebeat.com/infrastructure/perplexity-partners-with-nvidia-to-launch-portable-computer-a-fully-local-ai-agent-with-zero-token-costs)). This service is an innovative attempt to shift the AI operating model from being cloud-centric to device-centric.

## Why is this important?

The biggest changes are cost and security. Until now, every time you used cloud AI, you had to pay a fee based on "tokens" (the amount of information in word units used by AI) every time it generated a response. However, because 'Portable Computer' borrows your computer's hardware performance to run AI directly, you no longer need to pay these token costs ([Perplexity Portable Computer Launch](https://www.androidauthority.com/perplexity-portable-computer-local-ai-agent-3703083/)).

It is also groundbreaking in terms of security. With the traditional method, a user's work content had to be transmitted to an external server, but now the AI model, user data, and the tasks themselves remain on the device, making it much more reliable for personal privacy ([Perplexity and Nvidia's Local Desktop AI Agent](https://news.google.com/stories/CAAqNggKIjBDQklTSGpvSmMzUnZjbmt0TXpZd1NoRUtEd2lhM3JydkVSRWVaUGZFWUJReU1pZ0FQAQ?hl=en-IN&gl=IN&ceid=IN:en)).

## Easy to understand

To use an analogy for the principle of 'Portable Computer,' it is like the difference between a **'paid library' and a 'personal study.'**

If cloud AI until now was like paying a fee every time to go to an external library to borrow books, 'Portable Computer' is like moving the library into your own room. Although there may be initial hardware costs, once you have it, you can use the AI as much as you want without additional costs.

Technically, this platform is designed to run not only the 'model' that acts as the AI's brain but also the 'orchestrator' that determines what the AI should do and the 'agent harness' (the operating environment for AI agents) inside the personal device ([Perplexity Local AI Behavior](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/08/26/now-perplexity-is-trying-to-get-into-the-local-ai-action/5292449)). In other words, even if the internet is disconnected, the AI determines for itself and solves complex tasks ([Perplexity Portable Computer Launch](https://x.com/wallstengine/status/2092262633068277776)).

## Current situation

Currently, this platform is optimized for Nvidia's hardware environment. Specifically, it can be used on Nvidia's DGX Spark system or Linux-based PCs equipped with Nvidia RTX graphics cards ([Nvidia DGX Spark and Local AI](https://www.gadgetvoize.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-pushes-local-ai-with-open-models-agents-and-perplexity-partnership/)).

At launch, it supports the 'Qwen 3.8 27B' model and the fine-tuned 'Qwen PPLX 27B' model, with plans to support Nvidia's 'Nemotron 3.5 Lightning (30B)' model soon ([Perplexity and Nvidia's Local AI Agent](https://www.howtogeek.com/perplexity-release-portable-computer-a-local-ai-agent/)). A major feature is the ability to handle everything locally, from general information searches to complex workflow processing ([Perplexity Portable Computer Launch](https://aistart.ai/ainews/perplexity-local-ai-agent-nvidia)).

## Future outlook

In the future, it is expected that we will be able to experience such 'local AI' in more general personal PC environments. As AI technology leaps beyond the massive barrier of the cloud and deep into user devices, an era is opening where we can enjoy the benefits of high-performance AI even in environments with unstable internet connections ([Perplexity Portable Computer Launch](https://basic-tutorials.com/news/perplexity-portable-computer-ai-agent-now-runs-locally-on-nvidia-dgx-spark/)). It seems it won't be long before 'how fast can it run which AI agent' becomes as important a purchasing criterion as CPU or RAM when choosing a personal PC.
