Using ChatGPT Max, I took on the challenge of developing a game—a field outside my expertise—after work.
My wife runs a bakery, so I usually end up doing the dishes when I get home. As I watched, I noticed that bread-making has a process and uses various ingredients.

I thought it would be great to create a defense game based on combining these.

(I remembered having fun playing the old game, Dota...)

I created a game that I'm quite satisfied with, especially for a first attempt.

I set the basic framework and storyline, and used GPT models to develop the rest.

It took about two weeks from development to store registration, and I think it took another day to build the website and set up the necessary operations.
(These days, it seems Apple's review process finishes within 3-4 days.)

What was possible to automate with AI:

- iOS, Android app development (planning, balancing, design, BM design)

- iOS, Android store setup

- Background music and sound effect generation

- AdMob ad setup, Firebase setup

- YouTube channel Shorts promotional videos (a bit disappointing, but still...)

- GeekNews post writing

What was impossible:

- Naver blog and cafe post writing

- Creating and uploading higher-quality YouTube videos

What I felt while experiencing this:

- Once logged in, as long as browsing isn't blocked on the site, AI can recognize and handle the service's functions itself.

- Through this game, I've developed a desire to challenge myself to see if AI-based automation is possible in the sales and marketing domains.
- Since the emergence of AI Astra, as long as tokens are available, not doing something in the digital world that you "want to do" feels like a regrettable choice.
- Try running prompts with instructions and requirements for AI like writing a diary before you go to sleep. Exciting things will be waiting for you.

- (Just a sudden thought) If traditional advertising was C2B, B2B, or C2C, it would be fun to create something in the BM form of A2A (Agent to Agent).

Conclusion,

It seems that "what you want to do" has become more important than classifications like developer, planner, or entrepreneur.

So, I keep contemplating what it is that I want to do.
