## Easy Understanding: How Does Veo 2 Work?

To put it simply, Veo 2 is like a **'genius director who reads your mind clearly and shoots a movie on the spot.'**

1.  **A Cinematic World Drawn with Text**: Suppose you enter "a golden retriever happily running on the beach at sunset." **Simply put**, Veo 2 finds the warm lighting of "sunset," the sandy texture of the "beach," and the vibrant movement characteristic of a "retriever" from tens of thousands of data points and crafts them into a vivid 8-second video [Google Rolls Out AI-Powered Video Generation for Gemini Advanced...](https://tech-ish.com/2025/04/23/google-veo-gemini-whisk-ai-generated-video/).
2.  **Breathing Life into Images (Whisk Animate)**: In Google's other experimental tool, Whisk, magic happens with just a single still photo. By using the 'Whisk Animate' feature, a fixed image transforms into a living 8-second animation clip, much like the moving picture frames in the Harry Potter movies [Generate videos in Gemini and Whisk with Veo 2... | TechNews](https://news-tech.io/ko/news/generate-videos-in-gemini-and-whisk-with-veo-2). 

The videos generated this way are produced in **720p resolution** (High-Definition HD) and are provided in a **16:9 aspect ratio**, which is a widescreen format like what you see in movie theaters [Try generating video in Gemini, powered by Veo 2](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/video-generation/). The file format is **MP4**, which can be easily opened anywhere on smartphones or computers [Google Launches Video Generation Veo 2 in Gemini](https://en.ain.ua/2025/04/16/google-launches-veo-2-generation/).

## Current Status: Who Can Use It and How?

Currently, this amazing feature is not open to all users. It is primarily being offered first to subscribers of the paid **Gemini Advanced** service or Google One AI Premium users [Google News - News about Google • Gemini - Overview](https://news.google.com/stories/CAAqNggKIjBDQklTSGpvSmMzUnZjbmt0TXpZd1NoRUtEd2ladExIUERSRnp3V0JvVkJXR25pZ0FQAQ?hl=en-IN&gl=IN&ceid=IN:en).

How to use it is as simple as an everyday conversation:
- Click on **'Veo 2'** in the model selection menu at the top of the Gemini screen [Try generating video in Gemini, powered by Veo 2](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/video-generation/).
- Describe the scene you want to create in detail, such as "A rainy street in Seoul at night, with neon signs reflecting in puddles."
- While you take a short break, the AI completes an 8-second cinematic video.

However, to prevent overcrowding by too many users at once or putting a load on the system, monthly generation limits are set. The system kindly sends a notification when you are close to the limit, allowing you to enjoy creative activities in a planned manner [Generate videos in Gemini and Whisk with Veo 2 - The Story Thailand](https://www.thestorythailand.com/en/gemini-whisk-with-veo-2/).
