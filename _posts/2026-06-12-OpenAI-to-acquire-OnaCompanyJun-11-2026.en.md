A 'Real Employee' Has Joined Inside Your Computer

What if, in the early hours of the morning when we have clocked out and fallen asleep, someone turned on our computer and perfectly finished our backlog of work? This story, like a magical helper from a fairy tale, might soon become a reality in the world of artificial intelligence. On June 11, 2026, OpenAI, the absolute powerhouse of the artificial intelligence industry, heralded yet another massive seismic shift. It was the news of their acquisition of 'Ona', a specialized startup that provides a secure cloud (virtual server) environment allowing AI agents (autonomous AI programs that set their own goals, make judgments, and act) to work without interruption for long periods [OpenAI buys Ona as it moves to own the full stack underneath its AI ...](https://startupfortune.com/openai-buys-ona-as-it-moves-to-own-the-full-stack-underneath-its-ai-agents/). Following this acquisition, Ona's core technology and development team will officially join OpenAI's coding-dedicated AI assistant division, 'Codex' [OpenAI to acquire cloud platform provider Ona to run AI agents](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4602652-openai-to-acquire-cloud-platform-provider-ona-to-run-ai-agents).

Amidst an outpouring of countless tech news, why does this particular story shine so brightly? Even from the perspective of tech media, this acquisition symbolizes a crucial moment where the technological paradigm completely shifts [TechCrunch | Startup and Technology News](https://techcrunch.com/). Simply put, artificial intelligence is now moving beyond being a 'passive consultant' that only answers when we ask something we don't know. It is evolving into a 'proactive full-time employee' that, once given an instruction, will stay up for nights on end to write code, test it, and fix errors all on its own. Let's explore how this change will transform our daily lives using the easiest language to understand.

## Why It Matters

First, let's think about ChatGPT, which we use every day. It has dramatically increased our work productivity by finding excellent answers to our questions and drafting emails for us [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/). However, the general AI models that are currently popularized have structural limitations. They are confined within the narrow space of the user's 'chat window' (web browser or app) and the short timeframe of a 'one-time response'.

Right now, if you type "Write a simple login page code in Python" into the chat window, it spits out a plausible code in just 10 seconds. But what if you gave it a massive command like, "Completely convert our company's entire legacy website into a modern mobile app, and proactively find and fix all bugs that occur over the next 3 days"? Existing AI cannot handle such enormous requests on its own. This is because it lacks the memory to remember long-term complex tasks, doesn't have a secure test server to directly execute the code it writes, and most importantly, the moment the human user closes the window, the AI's thought circuits shut down as well.

The core reason OpenAI spent a fortune to acquire Ona is to completely shatter this physical barrier. Ona provides a specialized cloud-based platform that allows AI agents to run continuously for days in the invisible background without interruption [OpenAI to acquire cloud platform provider Ona to run AI agents](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4602652-openai-to-acquire-cloud-platform-provider-ona-to-run-ai-agents). OpenAI intends to transplant Ona's infrastructure technology into Codex, transforming it from a tool that simply recommends a few lines of code into a powerful agent that autonomously handles a company's complex workflows over the long term [OpenAI to acquire Ona](https://openai.com/index/openai-to-acquire-ona/).

At this point, let's firmly establish the concept of an 'AI Agent'. While regular programs only respond to rigid instructions like "If shortcut A is pressed, do B," AI agents are different. They are given only a 'Final Goal', such as "Complete this system." Then, to achieve that goal, they formulate their own detailed plans, find and use software tools, and if a critical error occurs, they analyze the cause themselves to find a workaround.

For such a lifelike AI agent to operate properly in a fierce corporate environment where a single error can result in massive losses, a perfectly controlled virtual computer space with zero downtime 365 days a year is essential. Ona's solution is perfectly specialized in professionally providing an environment where these smart AI agents can work with peace of mind [OpenAI To Acquire Cloud Startup Ona To Power AI Agents](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/open-ai-to-acquire-cloud-startup-ona-to-power-ai-agents/cZKTwvqR72F). In conclusion, OpenAI has firmly laid the foundation for a true 'autonomous infrastructure' where Codex can complete massive development tasks without resting, even while we sleep [OpenAI to acquire Ona to support its AI coding assistant, Codex](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/11/open-ai-ona-acquisition-codex.html).

## The Explainer

Let's easily break down these somewhat unfamiliar and rigid technical terms one by one by comparing them to familiar scenes from our daily lives.

The first thing to note is the concept of **'Persistent execution environments'**. To use an analogy, the ChatGPT we currently use as a smartphone app is like **'a chef at a drive-thru fast-food restaurant off the highway'**. When you pull up in your car and place an order (prompt) saying "I'll have a combo meal," they whip up an excellent hamburger in no time. However, the moment you drive away from the store, your connection with the chef is severed. It is a system where continuous cooking only happens if you stay at the window and keep placing the next order.

What if you gave this chef an unreasonable order like, "Perfectly prepare a full-course, top-tier hotel buffet for 500 people over a 4-day, 3-night event starting tomorrow"? It would be impossible because they lack a giant refrigerator to store massive amounts of ingredients (persistent memory capacity) and large stoves to simmer food for days and nights (long-term cloud computing environment).

This is where Ona steps in as a relief pitcher. Ona effectively builds an entire **'state-of-the-art, large-scale special kitchen and customized robotic sous-chef system'** that never stops running 24/7 for OpenAI's specialized coding AI, Codex. In other words, even if we give it a heavy set of instructions and clock out after turning off our computer monitor, Codex can continue to cook (perform complex coding tasks and testing) relentlessly for days inside this giant virtual kitchen [OpenAI to acquire Ona](https://openai.com/index/openai-to-acquire-ona/).

The second thing to look at is the **'Secure orchestration'** technology that Ona boasts [OpenAI to acquire Ona - startuphub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-to-acquire-ona). Orchestration is the technology of perfectly coordinating and managing multiple servers, databases, and numerous AI models, much like a master conductor of an orchestra, ensuring they time things right and do not clash with one another. The process of Codex writing complex code and directly deploying it to a server for testing generates numerous errors. Ona helps ensure that this entire precarious process takes place in a **'Secure execution environments'** completely isolated from a company's actual data [OpenAI buys Ona as it moves to own the full stack underneath its AI ...](https://startupfortune.com/openai-buys-ona-as-it-moves-to-own-the-full-stack-underneath-its-ai-agents/).

Simply put, it is like providing a scientist studying deadly viruses with a perfect, multi-layered, underground glass isolation chamber (safe zone) designed to prevent any external leaks under any circumstances. Even if a fatal error occurs due to Codex's mistake that paralyzes the entire system, not a single hair on the client company's actual main system is harmed, thanks to Ona's thick firewall. It is only when such ironclad security and precise coordination are foundational that global companies can confidently entrust their core IT tasks to artificial intelligence.
