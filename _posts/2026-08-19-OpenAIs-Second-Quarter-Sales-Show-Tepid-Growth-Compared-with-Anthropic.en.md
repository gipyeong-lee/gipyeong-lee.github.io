Imagine this: the personal assistants in our smartphones or the AI tools that summarize our meetings every day are actually at the forefront of a massive "revenue war." Recently, the tech industry has been buzzing as the revenue scorecards for the two giants of the AI world, OpenAI and Anthropic, have been revealed. Until yesterday, it seemed like OpenAI was receiving all the world's attention, but suddenly, the name "Anthropic" has started to resonate even louder. What on earth is happening between these two companies?

## Why Does This Matter?

You might think of this simply as corporate revenue figures. However, these numbers provide the answer to the question, **"Has AI finally started to make real money?"** Until now, AI was closer to a "cool technology" that only consumed massive amounts of investment capital. But the news that Anthropic is the first company developing AI models to record a quarterly profit is a significant signal that the AI industry has moved past the research phase and is entering a full-scale business trajectory. It also means that the foundation is being laid for us to use smarter, more convenient AI services more reliably.

## Easy Explanation

Comparing the growth of Anthropic and OpenAI is like **"two race cars speeding down a highway."**

Until now, OpenAI was the front-runner, monopolizing everyone's attention in the first lane of the highway. Recently, however, a car called Anthropic has been attempting a frighteningly fast overtake from the second lane.

It’s not just about speed. Looking at the revenue numbers makes the difference clear. Anthropic's revenue for the second quarter of 2026 was approximately $11.5 billion, a massive 14-fold explosion compared to $787 million in the same period last year [[Reference: Anthropic Posts First Profitable Quarter In Frontier AI](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/08/17/anthropics-groundbreaking-second-quarter-delivers-115b-in-revenue/)][[Reference: Anthropic revenue surges to over US$11.5bil in second quarter | FMT](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/business/2026/08/17/anthropic-revenue-surges-to-over-us-11-5bil-in-second-quarter/)]. With OpenAI's booked revenue for the same period estimated at around $7 billion to $8 billion, Anthropic is clearly pulling ahead in terms of numbers [[Reference: OpenAI and Anthropic Are Racing to Become the Same Company. It's Already Public.](https://www.thestateofai.com/news/openai-anthropic-revenue-ipo-convergence)].

Simply put, Anthropic has moved beyond "the basics" and is rapidly mastering "how to turn a profit." They achieved an operating profit of approximately $559 million in the second quarter, a milestone reached two years ahead of initial expectations [[Reference: Anthropic Turns Its First Profit: AI News August 16 2026](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-august-16-2026)].

## Current Situation

The current market landscape is very intriguing. As of the end of July, Anthropic's annual revenue run rate (the current revenue annualized) has exceeded $65 billion [[Reference: Anthropic's Revenue Reportedly Soars Past $65B, Surpassing...](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/anthropics-revenue-reportedly-soars-past-221609763.html)][[Reference: Anthropic's revenue run rate reportedly surpasses $65 billion pre-IPO](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/17/anthropic-revenue-run-rate-ipo-openai)]. On the other hand, OpenAI is also showing its strength, recording a revenue run rate of over $40 billion, nearly doubling from the end of 2025 [[Reference: OpenAI annualized revenue tops $40 billion ahead of IPO](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/openai-annualized-revenue-tops-40-111122841.html?fr=sycsrp_catchall)][[Reference: OpenAI's Revenue Run Rate Tops $40 Billion Just... - Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/openais-revenue-run-rate-tops-40-billion-just-months-after-doubling/)].

While there may be differences in how the two companies calculate these figures, they clearly illustrate how enthusiastic the market is about AI technology and how actively their services are being used in corporate environments [[Reference: Anthropic revenue surges to over US$11.5bil in second quarter | FMT](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/business/2026/08/17/anthropic-revenue-surges-to-over-us-11-5bil-in-second-quarter/)].

## What Will Happen Next?

The most anticipated event is Anthropic's initial public offering (IPO). Expectations are enormous, with market investors valuing the company at over $2 trillion [[Reference: Could Anthropic really be worth $2 trillion? - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fK48rqSR8UQ)].

There are two key points we should watch. First, can Anthropic maintain this overwhelming growth momentum after its IPO? Second, can OpenAI regain the lead with new models (e.g., the GPT-5.6 series) [[Reference: OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar says Q2 ARR topped in July 2026 - Quartz](https://qz.com/openai-revenue-july-second-quarter-sarah-friar-073026)]?

What benefits will we see amidst this race for smarter, cheaper AI? As competition between companies intensifies, the pace of technological development will accelerate, which in turn will make our daily lives a little more convenient and intelligent.

## AI's Take

The revenue war between these two giants signifies that AI technology has stepped out of the lab and into a phase of creating real economic value. More important than who reaches the summit first is how this intense competition will make our daily lives more intelligent and useful. We are currently witnessing a massive wave of change where AI is becoming a part of our world.
