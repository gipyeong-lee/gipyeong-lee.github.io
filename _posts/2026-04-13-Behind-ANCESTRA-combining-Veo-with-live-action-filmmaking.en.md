Imagine. You are a film director right now. To express the profound sadness of a protagonist, you want to create a fantastic scene that merges the mystery of the vast universe with a human heartbeat. But what if it costs billions of won in CG (Computer Graphics) and takes months of time? Most people might give up on their dreams, hitting the wall of production costs. But what if there were a 'magic brush' that could turn those abstract images only in your head into reality with just a few photos and descriptions?

Recently, a work that made such a magical imagination a reality was unveiled at the Tribeca Festival, one of the world's leading film festivals, creating a buzz. It is the short film **'ANCESTRA'** by director Eliza McNitt. This work goes beyond simply watching videos made by artificial intelligence. This is because it sophisticatedly combines live-action (a method of directly filming real actors and backgrounds with a camera) footage of real actors with virtual images created by **'Veo'**, Google DeepMind's cutting-edge video generation AI. [Behind "ANCESTRA": combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://onmine.io/behind-ancestra-combining-veo-with-live-action-filmmaking/) Today, we tell you the behind-the-scenes story of this amazing project that will change the future map of filmmaking.

## Why is this important?

Until now, videos made by generative AI (AI that learns from existing data to create new content) often received evaluations like "It's interesting, but awkward to use in actual movies." This was because backgrounds would wobble and distort, or characters' movements would be unrealistically choppy. However, 'ANCESTRA' has seemingly leaped over these technical limitations.

This project is not just an experiment to show off technical prowess. Master director Darren Aronofsky, famous for the movie 'Black Swan', and his AI studio 'Primordial Soup' joined hands with Google DeepMind to directly prove how AI can be artistically integrated into actual film production sites. [Darren Aronofsky AI Studio Announces Google Pact, 'Ancestra' Project](https://variety.com/2025/digital/news/darren-aronofsky-ai-studio-primordial-soup-google-deepmind-1236403412/)

The goal of their partnership is clear: "To break down the boundaries between storytelling and technology by putting the world's best generative AI in the hands of the best filmmakers." [Behind "ANCESTRA": combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://onmine.io/behind-ancestra-combining-veo-with-live-action-filmmaking/) Now, AI is not a threat to the artist's place, but is establishing itself as the most powerful assistant that brings to life artistic imaginations that were only kept in the heart due to budget or technical constraints.

## Current Situation: Adding a 'Warm Heart' to Cold Technology

The technical achievements are amazing, but the reason 'ANCESTRA' deeply resonated with the audience's hearts is because of the truthful **'story'** contained within it.

This film was produced based on director Eliza McNitt's actual experience. Director McNitt reportedly suffered from a condition where she had a hole in her heart from birth. The movie uses this dramatic life event as a motif to depict the great love of a mother trying to protect a child with a heart condition. [Behind "ANCESTRA:" combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://article.wn.com/view/2025/06/13/Behind_ANCESTRA_combining_Veo_with_liveaction_filmmaking/)

The director wanted to describe a mother's love not just as a 'warm emotion', but as a **'cosmic force'** that sprouts and redeems life. In the process of visualizing this abstract and grand concept, the AI Veo played a decisive role. [Behind "ANCESTRA:" combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://article.wn.com/view/2025/06/13/Behind_ANCESTRA_combining_Veo_with_liveaction_filmmaking/) As the heavy realism provided by live-action video met the dreamlike beauty provided by AI video, the audience vividly witnessed the intangible emotion of a mother's love before their eyes.

Currently, this film has successfully debuted at the Tribeca Festival and is being evaluated as the most advanced example of how generative AI can realize 'personalized storytelling' and 'sophisticated technical integration' in the film industry. ## What Will Happen Next?

'ANCESTRA' is just the beginning of a great journey. This collaboration is the first result of a series of three short films planned by Google and the filmmaking team. The changes we can look forward to are largely twofold:

2. **Birth of New Genres**: A new form of 'hybrid cinema', where the boundaries between reality and virtuality are completely broken down, will establish itself as the mainstream of movies.
