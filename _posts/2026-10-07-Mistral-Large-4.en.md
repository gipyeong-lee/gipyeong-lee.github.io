Imagine this: when you need to write complex programming code or analyze a massive volume of financial reports, it feels as if dozens of highly intelligent experts are working as a team to help you. Recently, the French AI company Mistral AI unveiled a new "digital brain" with exactly those capabilities.

### Why is this news important?

The newly revealed **Mistral Large 4** is not just a simple feature update. Currently, the field of AI technology is where major companies from the US and China are locked in a fierce war for dominance. In this situation, the fact that Mistral AI, Europe's representative AI company, has brought out a super-large model with over 1 trillion parameters is a powerful signal that Europe will secure its own independent technological prowess and protect its digital sovereignty [Source 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mistral_Large).

For general users, this means opportunities to utilize more powerful and intelligent AI at a lower cost, and with greater transparency (through open weights) [Source 10](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/mistral-large-4-le-chonk-1t-open-weights-preview-2026).

### In simple terms: What is 'Le Chonk'?

Mistral AI has given this model the nickname **'Le Chonk' (a nickname meaning 'plump' or 'chunky' in French)** [Source 6](https://habr.com/ru/news/1091148/).

To use an analogy, if existing AI were a student who memorized an encyclopedia, this model is like a **'head librarian who knows how to systematically categorize and connect the knowledge of an entire library.'**

*   **Massive Scale:** It has approximately 1 trillion parameters (the numerical values an AI adjusts while learning). Simply put, it means the AI has 1 trillion "brain cells" [Source 10](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/mistral-large-4-le-chonk-1t-open-weights-preview-2026).
*   **Efficient Work Processing:** If the brain is big, wouldn't it be too slow? That is why this model uses a **'Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)'** approach. In simple terms, the AI librarian doesn't use all 1 trillion pieces of knowledge; when a question comes in, it intelligently activates only the 'field-specific expert' sections (49 billion parameters) needed, thereby increasing calculation speed [Source 9](https://www.datalearner.com/ai-models/pretrained-models/mistral-large-4), [Source 10](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/mistral-large-4-le-chonk-1t-open-weights-preview-2026).
*   **AI with Eyes and Ears:** It is a 'multimodal' model (the ability to process not just text, but also various forms of information such as images and sound) that doesn't just write text but also understands images [Source 3](https://openrouter.ai/mistralai/mistral-large-4-0), [Source 13](https://x.com/MistralAI/status/2107457414387622310).

### Where we are now

As of October 6, 2026, developers can preview this powerful model via API [Source 2](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/), [Source 10](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/mistral-large-4-le-chonk-1t-open-weights-preview-2026). Experts assess that Mistral Large 4 demonstrates the most powerful performance among all current open-weight models, particularly in the fields of complex logical reasoning, code generation, and visual information processing [Source 12](https://officechai.com/ai/mistral-large-4-big-chonk/), [Source 13](https://x.com/MistralAI/status/2107457414387622310).

Usage fees have also been set at a reasonable level compared to competing models, allowing more companies and developers to experience this high performance without a heavy burden [Source 4](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/mistral-large-4).

### What to expect in the future

Mistral AI's biggest gamble is the release of **'open weights.'** They plan to release all blueprints and weight files for the model around October 27th [Source 11](https://thenextweb.com/news/mistral-releases-large-4-a-1-trillion-parameter-open-weight-ai-model), [Source 15](https://trashexpert.ru/news/software-news/mistral-large-public-preview).

This means anyone will be able to install and use this immense intelligence on their own computer or private server. The environment where we have had to rely solely on the AI models of closed, large corporations is changing. Watching what changes this 'Le Chonk,' which started in France, brings to the global AI ecosystem will be an important indicator of how much smarter our daily technology will become.
