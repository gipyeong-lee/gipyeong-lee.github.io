Imagine if an AI solved a riddle that has stumped humanity for 167 years in just three hours. Should we be joyous, or should we be afraid? The question recently posed by OpenAI, a giant in the artificial intelligence industry, has sent shockwaves through the academic world.

### Why is this important?

This incident goes beyond the news that "AI is good at math"; it raises fundamental questions about how we accept knowledge and research findings. 771 mathematicians and 25 Fields Medalists have strongly criticized OpenAI's move, labeling it "Slop Mathematics" [[Reference 5](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-withdraws-caltech-mathathon-slop-mathematics-fields-medallists)]. It is a critical case study showing how our society should handle information when the speed of technological development outpaces our ability to verify its results [[Reference 8](https://dev.to/ashraf_chowdury09/openai-dumped-722-ai-written-math-papers-on-github-now-mathematicians-have-a-code-review-problem-4fgk)].

### Easy Explanation: 4,000 Answer Sheets Written by AI

Recently, OpenAI dumped 722 mathematics research papers onto GitHub [[Reference 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XO_5D30KeVk), [Reference 4](https://www.linkedin.com/news/story/cutting-edge-openai-model-solves-hundreds-of-open-math-problems-7666620/)]. These papers are the result of OpenAI's unreleased internal AI model solving approximately 4,000 mathematical problems [[Reference 7](https://axeetech.com/openai-math-manuscripts-722-ai-generated-papers-2026/), [Reference 9](https://vibemathed.com/problem/hodge-conjecture-products-of-k3-surfaces)].

In simple terms, **it’s like giving an AI 4,000 math problems, having it think for three hours, and then releasing those answer sheets to the world exactly as they are** [[Reference 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XO_5D30KeVk), [Reference 9](https://vibemathed.com/problem/hodge-conjecture-products-of-k3-surfaces)]. This included a paper claiming a breakthrough on the 167-year-old Riemann Hypothesis [[Reference 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XO_5D30KeVk)].

To use an analogy, it’s as if a smart student named AI solved thousands of difficult math problems in a very short time, but the volume of material was so massive that the "math teachers" (academic experts) could not verify whether the logical processes were sound or if the AI had simply cobbled together existing solutions [[Reference 15](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/openai-unleashes-hundreds-more-math-results-upon-a-field-already-in-shock/)]. While Alex Kontorovich, Chair of the Mathematics Department at Rutgers University, did comment that certain results were at a level that would earn a human a Fields Medal, the academic community expressed concerns that verifying all of it would take months [[Reference 4](https://www.linkedin.com/news/story/cutting-edge-openai-model-solves-hundreds-of-open-math-problems-7666620/), [Reference 15](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/openai-unleashes-hundreds-more-math-results-upon-a-field-already-in-shock/)].

### Current Situation: Withdrawal of Sponsorship and Confusion

Facing intense backlash from the mathematical community, OpenAI eventually withdrew its $1 million sponsorship for an AI math competition that was to be held at CalTech [[Reference 3](https://www.livemint.com/ai/openai-vs-mathematicians-why-the-ai-giant-is-facing-growing-backlash-threat-to-intellectual-work-11789202015601.html), [Reference 5](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-withdraws-caltech-mathathon-slop-mathematics-fields-medallists)]. OpenAI stated, "We recognize that rapid mathematical progress by AI can cause confusion in existing research methods" [[Reference 3](https://www.livemint.com/ai/openai-vs-mathematicians-why-the-ai-giant-is-facing-growing-backlash-threat-to-intellectual-work-11789202015601.html)]. The 722 papers remain on GitHub, and mathematicians tasked with verifying them are struggling to make sense of the massive amount of data [[Reference 8](https://dev.to/ashraf_chowdury09/openai-dumped-722-ai-written-math-papers-on-github-now-mathematicians-have-a-code-review-problem-4fgk), [Reference 11](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/Purely-Cosmetic-Surgery-on-Knots-in-the-Three-Sphere-September-23-2026/paper.pdf), [Reference 15](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/openai-unleashes-hundreds-more-math-results-upon-a-field-already-in-shock/)].

### What Happens Next?

This incident is a prologue to how we will handle "AI-generated knowledge." If artificial intelligence continues to produce more complex scientific discoveries, failing to establish the technical foundation and social protocols to verify them could risk the research field being drowned in a "flood of knowledge." This event reminds us once again that the quantity of knowledge does not guarantee its quality. It remains to be seen whether OpenAI will adopt more cautious methods for releasing research based on the lessons learned from this controversy, or if it will continue to prioritize technological speed.
