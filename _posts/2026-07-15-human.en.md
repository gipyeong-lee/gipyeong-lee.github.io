Occasionally in life, there are people who live without any sense of responsibility.
Unless times are tough, most people live with a sense of responsibility.
But when they face hardship or a difficult situation, there are those who quietly step away.

After avoiding the tasks they were responsible for by using their own illness as an excuse,
it is astonishing to see them earning money elsewhere as if they were not sick at all.

When I realized that such a person prefers easy things and only chases after what looks good on the surface,
I felt that I never wanted to be involved with them again.

When meeting people and building relationships,

one should not be deceived by their positive mindset or smiling faces.

Let us not be deceived by people's outward appearances. Always remain vigilant.
