# Works Even Without Internet! Our Home Robot Has Started 'Independent Thinking'

**Imagine** a scene from a movie for a moment. A robot helping with your chores is delicately moving dishes in the kitchen. Suddenly, lightning strikes, and your home Wi-Fi goes out or the internet signal becomes extremely weak. What would have happened to conventional robots? They would likely have stopped immediately or stood there blankly, wondering 'What should I do?' while waiting for a response from a distant giant server (the cloud). In the worst-case scenario, a split-second delay in command transmission could cause it to drop the expensive plate it was holding.

However, such worries may soon be a thing of the past. Google DeepMind has introduced an innovative technology to the world that allows robots to see, understand, and act on their own without the 'lifeline' of the internet. It is **'Gemini Robotics On-Device,'** which essentially plants an independent brain into a robot.

## Why It Matters

When we play a game on a smartphone, a brief 'lag (latency)' is just annoying, but for a robot moving heavy objects in the physical world, 'lag' can lead to fatal accidents or damage. The reason this technology has become a game-changer in robotics is clear:

1. **Response Speed Faster than Light**: Previously, when a robot saw an object, it sent that image to a distant server for analysis and then received instructions for action. The time difference of several hundred milliseconds occurring in this process was the enemy of precise work. 'Gemini Robotics On-Device' completes all processing within the robot's body, resulting in extremely short latency [Gemini Robotics On-Device — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini-robotics/gemini-robotics-on-device/).
2. **Invincible even in Internet Dead Zones**: Robots can intelligently perform missions even in harsh environments such as underground warehouses, outdoors with unstable communication, or disaster sites where networks cannot reach at all. Robots no longer need to hover only near internet routers [DeepMind's Gemini Robotics On-Device brings advanced AI to local robots](https://roboticsandautomationnews.com/2025/06/26/google-deepmind-launches-new-vision-language-action-model-to-put-ai-directly-into-local-robotic-devices/92669/).
3. **Thorough Privacy Protection**: The fact that videos taken by a domestic robot moving around the living room are not transmitted to an external server is a huge advantage. Since the home layout or family privacy information is processed and discarded only inside the robot, security is dramatically increased.

## The Explainer: Planting a 'Local Brain' in the Robot

To understand this technology more easily, let's use a **'Chef'** analogy.

- **Conventional Method (Cloud Robotics)**: It's like a chef having to call a head chef outside the restaurant every time they chop an onion ("Chef, should I chop now?") or turn on the stove ("Chef, should I turn it on now?"). If the phone disconnects or the call quality is poor, cooking stops immediately.
- **New Method (On-Device Robotics)**: It's like a chef entering the kitchen with a perfect grasp of recipes and judgment. They can see the state of the ingredients in front of them and complete the dish immediately without contacting the outside. **In other words,** the robot has discarded the 'walkie-talkie' and gained a 'real brain' that thinks for itself.

On June 24, 2025, Google DeepMind officially launched this model, optimized to run directly on robotic devices [Gemini Robotics - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_Robotics), [Google rolls out new Gemini model that can run on robots locally | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/24/google-rolls-out-new-gemini-model-that-can-run-on-robots-locally/). This model is a lighter and more powerful version of the 'Gemini Robotics' model first introduced in March 2025, designed to achieve maximum efficiency even with limited hardware resources within the device [Gemini Robotics On-Device brings AI to local robotic devices — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-on-device-brings-ai-to-local-robotic-devices/).

In particular, this model is called a **VLA (Vision-Language-Action)** model. **Simply put,** it means a single AI integrally manages the entire process of the robot **seeing (Vision)** the world through a camera, **understanding (Language)** complex commands given by humans, and actually moving its arms to **act (Action)** [Google DeepMind introduces on-device Gemini AI model for robots](https://www.therobotreport.com/google-deepmind-introduces-on-device-gemini-ai-model-robots/).

## Where We Stand: Smooth and Precise with Two Arms

Google explains that this model brings the powerful multimodal reasoning capabilities of the latest AI, Gemini 2.0, into the physical world. Here, multimodal reasoning refers to the ability to understand and judge not only text but also images, sounds, and spatial depth all at once [Gemini Robotics On-Device brings AI to local robotic devices — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-on-device-brings-ai-to-local-robotic-devices/).

The level reached by this technology is currently much further ahead than we might think.
- **Standard for Two-Armed Robots**: This model is specifically designed for robots with two arms, like humans. This enables 'dexterous manipulation,' such as holding a box with one hand and taking out the contents with the other, rather than just moving objects [Google DeepMind introduces on-device Gemini AI model for robots](https://www.therobotreport.com/google-deepmind-introduces-on-device-gemini-ai-model-robots/), [Gemini Robotics On-Device — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini-robotics/gemini-robotics-on-device/).
- **Amazing Adaptability**: The robot does not panic even when entering an unfamiliar room it has never been to or encountering an object it has never seen before. It has the ability to quickly adapt to new environments and complete missions based on existing knowledge [Deepmind Launches New Generation Robot AI Model: Gemini Robotics On-Device](https://www.aibase.com/news/19215).
- **A Gift for Developers**: Robot manufacturers can now take this powerful model and improve performance or fine-tune it to fit the characteristics of their own robots [Gemini Robotics — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini-robotics/).

## What's Next

The emergence of Gemini Robotics On-Device is like a 'Declaration of Independence' for robot technology. Robots are now ready to cut the cloud umbilical cord and head out into the harsh and uncertain environments of reality.

Google expressed confidence, calling it the "most powerful VLA model optimized to run locally on robotic devices" among its robotics models [Gemini Robotics On-Device brings AI to local robotic devices - Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/gemini-robotics-on-device-brings-ai-to-local-robotic-devices/?amp=).

**Shall we imagine our future daily life?**
Soon, we will meet smart logistics robots that tirelessly sort delivery boxes, medical assistant robots in the operating room that don't miss a single gesture from the doctor to hand over tools, and finally, domestic robots in our living rooms that fold laundry and organize children's toys. Even if the internet suddenly cuts out, you can rest assured that the robot will silently continue its work.

Robots that move nimbly by judging situations themselves without the constraints of the internet. The future we only saw in science fiction movies has taken a giant step closer to us through Gemini Robotics On-Device.
