Imagine this: today you have to handle over 100 emails. Meeting scheduling, verifying important project reports, and even promotional emails are mixed in. If you read them one by one carefully, lunch time will have already passed. What if your assistant whispered this to you from the side? "Boss, the conditions in this sentence are a bit risky. You should confirm this schedule right away."

This kind of amazing thing is no longer just a story in science fiction movies. The term 'Superhuman Attention,' which frequently appears in the IT industry and artificial intelligence research fields recently, explains this very change. What exactly is this technology, and how is it changing our lives?

### Why is this important?

'Superhuman Attention' changes our lives from two major perspectives. The first is the evolution of the productivity tools we use. In the flood of complex digital information, we often miss what is important. Tools like the AI-based assistant service 'Superhuman' underline risk factors in real-time or point out key tasks while the user is writing [Ref 2]. Metaphorically speaking, it is like having a professional librarian by your side at all times in a library filled with countless books.

The second is breaking through the intelligent limitations of machine learning. The capability of 'superhuman' is being applied to technologies that increase the resolution of complex VR (virtual reality) videos (super-resolution) or allow self-driving cars to cope more safely than humans in unexpected situations [Ref 8, 15]. In other words, an environment is being created where AI can work without mistakes even in complex and vast information areas that we cannot supervise one by one.

### Easy to Understand: What is 'Attention'?

Then, what is 'attention' technically? The 'Attention Mechanism' is a technology inspired by the human cognitive process [Ref 11]. Simply put, think about when you are having a conversation with a friend in a very noisy cafe. The cafe is full of noise, such as murmuring people and music, but your brain focuses (Attention) only on your friend's voice.

Machine learning attention is the same. When artificial intelligence processes complex sentences or photos, it does not put equal effort into every part, but uses a method of calculating by placing greater weight on the most important words or key elements of an image in context [Ref 11, 12].

Then what is the 'superhuman' mentioned here? This refers to the attitude with which the machine processes information. Just as a careful engineer goes through thousands of tests, AI does not just blindly trust data but constantly questions and verifies it [Ref 1]. The attitude of trusting and processing only the 'survived', or thoroughly verified information in this process—this is the essence of superhuman attention shown by machines [Ref 1].

### Current Situation: AI Assistants That Have Come to Our Side

We are already in the middle of these changes. Past 2025, there has been a great leap in the field of email management. Beyond simply sorting emails according to set rules, AI assistants have emerged that learn the user's communication patterns and provide customized support [Ref 10].

In addition, based on vast training data, Tesla's Full Self-Driving (FSD) beta software boasts 'superhuman' level attention, showing superior reaction capabilities even in rare unexpected situations that human drivers might miss [Ref 15]. We have entered an era where artificial intelligence looks at the road and reviews emails on our behalf.

### What will happen in the future?

The development of technology is now going beyond the scope of human supervision. Looking at the super-intelligent AI scenario predicted around 2027, there are prospects that it will become difficult for humans to perfectly understand or supervise AI's judgment processes in areas such as scientific research or strategy formulation [Ref 14].

What should we do? As 'superhuman' technology becomes more sophisticated, it is important to break away from the obsession that humans must handle everything themselves. The ability to decide wisely how to choose and trust the data provided by technology is key. The eye to discern 'real information' in the digital world—isn't that the real focus we need in an era where technology is heading toward the superhuman?
