Imagine this: there is a complex mathematical problem that mathematicians around the world have been struggling to solve for decades. How would you feel if an AI sitting on your desk told you, "Here is a clue," and proved this conundrum that hundreds of people couldn't solve, all in just a few days?

Surprisingly, this is not a story from the distant future. It is happening right now. AI technology has moved beyond simple writing and translation to become a reliable partner in scientific research. At the center of this is OpenAI's latest flagship model, 'GPT-5.6 Sol'.

## Why is this important?

If AI in the past was merely a 'talented assistant,' today's AI is evolving into a 'smart professional researcher.' The reason we should pay attention to GPT-5.6 Sol is that it goes beyond delivering vast knowledge to solving high-level logical tasks that were difficult for human intelligence to resolve.

For average users, AI won't be solving math problems for us starting tomorrow, but the algorithms and technologies designed by such an AI will permeate the invisible corners of our daily lives—smartphones, communications, security, energy, and more. As AI's reasoning capabilities grow exponentially, the efficiency and accuracy of the digital services we enjoy every day will also improve significantly. In short, the apps we use will become smarter and respond faster.

## Easy Explanation: What is GPT-5.6 Sol?

The GPT-5.6 family consists of three main models: 'Sol', 'Terra', and 'Luna'. They differ in the depth of analysis, processing speed, and the amount of computing resources used. Among them, **GPT-5.6 Sol (hereafter Sol)** is the flagship (top-spec) model of this product line [Source: ChatGPT: Overview of GPT-5.6 and the new Work mode | Zen](https://dzen.ru/a/anwwWX_ITWFP6Cee), [Source: GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra and Luna: differences and selection — TracklyAI](https://ai.trackly.one/blog/gpt-5-6-sol-terra-luna-otlichiya).

To use an analogy, Sol is like a 'veteran researcher' who scours an entire library to find the information you need. While other models might be good at finding books in specific fields, Sol has the ability to read all the books in the library, connect the contents, and derive very complex and new conclusions.

In fact, Sol recently contributed to finding a proof for a difficult mathematical proposition called the 'double cycle cover conjecture' [Source: Too simple to be true? GPT-5.6 produced... / Habr](https://habr.com/ru/articles/1058194/). This is a remarkable case proving that AI has moved beyond a simple language model to perfectly understand mathematical logic and create new knowledge based on it.

## Current Status: Where and how can it be used?

Currently, GPT-5.6 Sol is actively used not only by experts but also in general development environments. It can be used to invoke Sol's powerful reasoning capabilities in game development engines like Godot or Unity, as well as code editors like Cursor, Cline, and Codex [Source: GPT-5.6 Sol vs GPT-6 Astra: Building the Same Game in... - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZiIwyRsGnBA), [Source: Kimi K3 vs ChatGPT (GPT-5.6): 2026 Comparison — SuperIntellect](https://superintellect.ru/guides/kimi-k3-vs-chatgpt), [Source: Use GPT-6 Astra in Codex, Cursor, Cline and DSH](https://ofox.ai/blog/gpt-6-astra-coding-agent-setup-2026/).

The price is also at a level accessible to individuals and developers. As of September 2, 2026, it can be used for $4 per 1 million input tokens (the minimum language unit processed by AI) in standard usage environments [Source: OpenAI decided to lower prices for access to GPT-5.6 Sol for...](https://3dnews.ru/1147274/openai-reshila-snizit-rastsenki-na-dostup-k-gpt56-sol-dlya-razrabotchikov-na-bligayshie-tri-mesyatsa).

Of course, new competing models like the recently released GPT-6 Astra are also emerging. Astra was unveiled on September 3 and is garnering much attention, but depending on the purpose and environment, Sol's unrivaled stability and analytical power remain a key option for many developers [Source: GPT-6 Astra: how to use, pricing, and comparison with GPT-5.6 Sol](https://vibecoding.ru/models/gpt-6-astra).

## What will happen in the future?

Moving forward, AI will be used more frequently to design more sophisticated scientific experiments or interpret complex physical laws. If the performance of the apps you use every day suddenly improves significantly, it might be because an AI like Sol was optimizing complex algorithms behind the scenes.

We will soon watch AI move beyond the 'summarization' stage to become a 'smart colleague' that sets its own hypotheses, interprets experimental results, and accelerates human scientific and technological advancement.
