Imagine this. You are a developer managing servers for a critical service used by millions worldwide. You are sharing a warm and pleasant dinner with your family for once, but suddenly, the smartphone in your pocket begins to ring loudly. On the screen, an urgent message in red letters appears: "Critical server error! Immediate attention required!" The cheerful atmosphere at the dining table instantly turns cold, and you apologize as you rush to your room to open your laptop.

This is the **'On-call'** moment that every developer in the world dreads most. Whether you're eating, in a deep sleep, or even on a sweet vacation, if the server screams that it's "hurting," you must immediately turn on your computer and find the culprit. But now, a smart AI assistant has appeared to take over this tedious and painful overnight work. It's **Relvy**, the promising standout selected by Y Combinator (YC), Silicon Valley's legendary startup bootcamp. [Launch HN: Relvy (YC F24) – On-call runbooks, automated | Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47702647)

## Why is this important to our lives?

The profession of a software engineer may look like a continuous series of glamorous coding on the outside, but there is a dark side hidden behind it: an 'endless war with outages.' As services grow and become more complex, the probability of an unexpected problem occurring somewhere in the system increases exponentially. The emergence of Relvy holds three major meanings beyond simple technical progress.

1. **Developers' 'Evenings Back'**: Relvy founders Bharath Bhat and Simranjit Singh emphasize that "the painful task of engineers manually handling alerts one by one must now disappear." [Relvynow continuously monitors your production logs and metrics and...](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/simranjit2112_relvy-now-continuously-monitors-your-production-activity-7264368367396884480-yRi7) If AI takes over repetitive investigation tasks, developers can pour more energy into their original duty: 'coding to create new value.'

2. **Protecting Business Golden Time**: If an internet service stops for just one minute, a company suffers financial losses worth tens of millions of won along with an irreversible drop in trust. Relvy drastically reduces **MTTR (Mean Time To Resolution)**, the average time it takes to resolve an incident. **Simply put**, it's like an automatic sprinkler system inside a house finding the exact spot of a fire and putting it out before the fire truck even arrives. [Relvy - Your runbooks, automated](https://www.relvy.ai/)

3. **Flawless Response Without Mistakes**: When people panic, they are bound to make mistakes even with things they know. An engineer who wakes up at 3 AM in a daze might enter the wrong command and make the situation worse. However, Relvy precisely follows the 'Runbook,' the incident response manual pre-written by engineers, without a single error. [GitHub - Relvy-AI/relvyai: Relvy AI - Your Runbooks, Automated. · GitHub](https://github.com/Relvy-AI/relvyai)

## How does Relvy work? (An Analogy)

Defining Relvy in one word, it is an **"AI mechanic who has mastered the latest repair guides and finds and repairs broken parts on its own."** Let's explain this complex process by comparing it to everyday situations.

### 1. Runbook Automation: "A Robot Chef Perfectly Recreating a Master Recipe"
Just as we look at a recipe when cooking, developers create a manual that says, "If problem A occurs, check B and execute C" in preparation for an incident. This is called a **Runbook**. Relvy reads and understands this natural language manual like a human. And it doesn't stop at just reading; it actually enters the server, inputs commands, checks data, and resolves problems according to the instructions. [Relvy AI: AI powered debugging notebooks for incident response | Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/relvy-ai)

### 2. Large-scale Data Analysis: "A Security Guard Monitoring Tens of Thousands of CCTV Screens Simultaneously"
Modern computer systems leave tens of thousands of records every second. These are called **Logs (activity records)** or **Telemetry (system status measurement data)**. It takes a human dozens of minutes to find a single clue in this massive sea of data, but Relvy scans this vast information in an instant and pinpoints the root cause of the problem in just a few minutes. [Launch HN: Relvy (YC F24) - On-call runbooks, automated](https://news.mcan.sh/item/47702647)

### 3. Intelligent Reasoning: "A Detective Gathering Scattered Evidence to Catch a Culprit"
Relvy is not just at the level of finding set words. It watches data changes over time to capture 'anomalies' different from the usual and understands the relationship between complex, intertwined systems to reach logical conclusions. It applies a smart way of thinking to judge what is truly important evidence among numerous pieces of information. [Relvy - Your AI On-call Engineer | ProductCool](https://www.productcool.com/product/relvy)

## The Future Relvy Envisions

Many people worry, "Will AI take away developers' jobs?" However, the Relvy development team thinks differently. Relvy's goal is **"not to eliminate people, but to eliminate the 'drudge work' that torments people."** [Relvy AI: Automated On-Call Runbooks for Engineering Teams!](https://dev.to/mgobea/relvy-ai-automated-on-call-runbooks-for-engineering-teeth-41pd)

The future with Relvy will look like this:

- **Daily Life Without Outage Worries**: Since AI maintains airtight security 24 hours a day, the instances where we experience inconvenience due to large-scale service interruptions will be greatly reduced.
- **A Workplace Where Creativity Blooms**: Instead of staying up all night fixing the same errors, developers will spend more time dreaming up innovative features to make our lives more convenient.
- **Systems Anyone Can Easily Operate**: Even with a lack of specialized knowledge, the era where one can safely manage and operate complex computer systems with the help of AI agents is not far off.
