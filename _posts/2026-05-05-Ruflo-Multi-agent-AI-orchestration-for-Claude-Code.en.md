## Are You Chatting with Just One AI Friend? It's Time to Work with an 'AI Team'

Have you ever felt like "one pair of hands isn't enough" when dealing with complex tasks? Something similar is happening in the world of Artificial Intelligence. Even with the emergence of brilliant AIs like ChatGPT and Claude, the tasks we assign them remain incredibly complex and vast.

**Imagine this.** You've decided to build a highly complex smartphone app. But by your side, you only have one all-rounder assistant who tries to do everything. This assistant has to write code, design the UI, and perform security checks. Trying to do all of this alone takes a long time, and sometimes they get overloaded and make mistakes. This is how we have been using AI until now.

But suddenly, this assistant picks up a walkie-talkie and shouts: **"Alright, experts in every field, gather up!"**

In an instant, a team of over 60 experts appears before your eyes. One person only writes code, another only hunts for bugs, and yet another monitors for security vulnerabilities. They talk to each other, hand off tasks, and finally report back to you with a perfectly polished result.

This is the world that **Ruflo** is creating. According to [GitHub - ruvnet/ruflo: 🌊 The leading agent orchestration platform for Claude](https://github.com/ruvnet/ruflo), Ruflo is more than just an AI assistant; it is a platform that acts as a 'conductor,' making dozens of AI agents (individual units of AI that judge and act autonomously) move in perfect harmony as a team.

## Why It Matters

Until now, the way we used ChatGPT or Claude was primarily through 'one-on-one dialogue.' It was a personal assistant format where the AI answered user questions. However, real-world work often cannot be solved through a conversation with just one assistant. Here are three key points why the change Ruflo brings is significant.

### 1. It Keeps Your Wallet Full (Cost Reduction)
Every time we use an AI, we pay a cost (API usage fee) in units called 'tokens.' For smart AIs, these costs are not negligible. Ruflo is designed to let agents communicate efficiently by exchanging only the most necessary information. As a result, it allows you to **save a staggering 75% in costs** compared to traditional methods. According to [Claude Flow (Ruflo) v3.5: Complete Guide to Multi-Agent Orchestration for Claude Code](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/774/claude-flow-ruflo-multi-agent-orchestration-guide), this is a massive economic benefit for both enterprises and individual users. It's like a magic trick where a task that used to cost ten dollars now only costs $2.50.

### 2. The Power of an 'Expert Group' (Overwhelming Performance)
Isn't it much more accurate to have 'masters' of each field working together rather than one person trying to do everything? Ruflo recorded a **remarkable score of 84.8%** on 'SWE-bench,' a rigorous test that evaluates software engineering capabilities. [Claude Flow (Ruflo) v3.5: Complete Guide to Multi-Agent Orchestration for Claude Code](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/774/claude-flow-ruflo-multi-agent-orchestration-guide) This is strong evidence that AI has moved beyond mere imitation and can now diagnose and solve complex problems on its own, like an actual skilled developer.

### 3. Many are Better Than One: 'Swarm Intelligence'
A single ant has very little power, but when thousands gather, they build massive nests and even create bridges. This concept of small intelligences gathering to create a massive intelligence is called **'Swarm Intelligence.'** [ruflo: Leading Agent Orchestration Platform for Claude](https://jimmysong.io/ai/ruflo/) Ruflo has applied this theory to AI, breaking through the limitations of a single model through 'collaboration.'
