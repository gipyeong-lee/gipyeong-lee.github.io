## 1. Why It Matters

Most of the Artificial Intelligence (AI) we use daily is 'cloud-based.' **To put it simply**, it's a method where the text I write is sent to a large corporation's headquarters server to receive an answer. This process has two major barriers.

First is the **limit of ironclad security**. When asking an AI about company secrets or personal concerns that are hard to show others, a sense of unease inevitably arises: 'What if this content remains on the corporate server?' Second is the **monthly recurring cost**. A complex payment method where you have to pay a monthly subscription fee or pay as you go is a significant burden.

CyberWriter breaks through these problems with a technology called **On-Device AI**. The AI operates only within my device, the MacBook, rather than in the world of the internet.
*   **Perfect Privacy**: My writings and notes never leave my MacBook. My private diary is kept safe [CyberWriter, a Markdown editor... - SaaS Insight](https://roipad.com/saas-metrics/view/hn_47833747/show-hn-cyberwriter-a-md-editor-built-on-apple-s-barely-used-on-device-ai).
*   **Unlimited AI with No Extra Cost**: There are no monthly subscription fees or usage-based charges (token costs). This is because it uses the AI that Apple has already put into my device [cyberWriter - Native Markdown Power for macOS](https://cyberwriter.app/).
*   **OK Even on an Airplane**: You can complete your writing with the help of AI even on an airplane with no internet at all or in the deep mountains [cyberWriter - Native Markdown Power for macOS](https://cyberwriter.app/).

Apple calls this "AI for the rest of us" [AppleIntelligence -Apple](https://www.apple.com/apple-intelligence/). Now, even without professional coding knowledge, anyone can fully utilize the intelligence their device possesses.

## 2. Easy Understanding: '3 Billion Smart Cells' Inside Your MacBook

What is the secret to CyberWriter's smart writing? This app is based on Apple's **Foundation Model**, which was officially released starting with macOS version 26 [Welcome to Tolexty's Blog: Show HN: CyberWriter – a .md editor built on Apple's (barely-used) on-device AI](https://tolexty.blogspot.com/2026/04/show-hn-cyberwriter-md-editor-built-on.html). You can think of a foundation model as a 'giant brain that forms the basis of all intelligence.'

### 3 Billion Switches in the Brain (3B Parameters)
There are about 3 billion 'parameters' inside this model [CyberWriter, a Markdown editor... - SaaS Insight](https://roipad.com/saas-metrics/view/hn_47833747/show-hn-cyberwriter-a-md-editor-built-on-apple-s-barely-used-on-device-ai). **By way of analogy**, it's like having 3 billion smart cells or tiny switches in the AI's head that understand the context and predict what word will come next with amazing accuracy. While smaller than the giant AIs in the cloud (which have trillions of parameters), it has more than enough capability to help with writing quickly and efficiently inside my MacBook.

### A 'Knowledge Map' That Remembers My Notes (Embedding)
CyberWriter doesn't just write for you. It 'understands' all the numerous notes I've written so far. How is that possible? It's thanks to a technology called **Embedding**.

Embedding is a technology that converts the meaning of text into numbers and displays them like coordinates on a map [CyberWriter, a Markdown editor... - SaaS Insight](https://roipad.com/saas-metrics/view/hn_47833747/show-hn-cyberwriter-a-md-editor-built-on-apple-s-barely-used-on-device-ai). **For example**, since 'apple' and 'pear' have similar meanings, they are placed very close to each other on the map, while 'apple' and 'car' are placed very far apart. CyberWriter uses this technology to meticulously organize my notes on a map of meaning. Thanks to this, if I ask, "What was the name of the restaurant I went to on my trip to Jeju Island last year?" the AI quickly searches this map to find the relevant content and provide an answer [cyberWriter - Native Markdown Power for macOS](https://cyberwriter.app/).

## 3. Key Features: AI Dancing at Your Fingertips

CyberWriter is more than just a diary; it's a **Markdown** editor that helps with professional-level writing [GitHub - uncSoft/cyberwriter-app: cyberWriter - a native macOS Markdown editor](https://github.com/uncsoft/cyberwriter-app). Markdown is a way to style documents as easily and quickly as coding, such as creating a title with a single '#' or making text bold with '*'.

*   **Chat Directly with Your Notepad (Chat with your vault)**: Through 'Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)' technology, you can have the AI read all sorts of documents stored on your computer (.md, .pdf, .csv, etc.) [cyberWriter - Native Markdown Power for macOS](https://cyberwriter.app/). A single request like "Summarize my notes" will organize hundreds of pages of records at a glance.
*   **Sentences Filled in Real-Time (Stream-to-editor)**: You don't have to wait bored until the AI generates the entire answer. You can see the AI typing one character at a time in real-time right where the cursor is [cyberWriter - Native Markdown Power for macOS](https://cyberwriter.app/). It feels as if an invisible writer is typing for you.
*   **The Magic Shortcut (Cmd + J)**: Highlight a part where you're stuck while writing and press `Cmd + J`. You can immediately request to summarize a sentence, change the tone to be more elegant, or explain a difficult concept at an elementary school level [cyberWriter - Native Markdown Power for macOS](https://cyberwriter.app/).
*   **Professional Tools**: It also includes **Mermaid** for drawing complex organizational charts and **KaTeX** for cleanly displaying difficult mathematical formulas. It's a very useful tool for college students and researchers alike [GitHub - uncSoft/cyberwriter-app: cyberWriter - a native macOS Markdown editor](https://github.com/uncsoft/cyberwriter-app).
