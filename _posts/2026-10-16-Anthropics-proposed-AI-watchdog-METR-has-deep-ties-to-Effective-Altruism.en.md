Imagine this: you decide to establish an 'external watchdog' to verify that the AI assistant on your smartphone or the tools you use for work aren't making dangerous or biased decisions. But then you discover that the head of that watchdog is actually close friends with the company's CEO, and both share the same belief system. This is the exact situation unfolding in the AI industry with the 'METR' controversy.

### Why is this important?

AI is now permeating every aspect of our daily lives. As we move forward, 'AI safety watchdogs'—organizations that verify whether AI models built by tech giants are functioning correctly and not harboring dangerous ideas—will become increasingly critical. [Source 7](https://ae.headtopics.com/news/who-is-dario-amodei-the-anthropic-ceo-pushing-independent-87913240) Some analysts point out that if these oversight bodies aren't free from the influence of specific companies, consumers will find it difficult to trust the safety of AI, inevitably raising questions about the ethical transparency of the entire AI industry. [Source 8](https://ae.headtopics.com/news/anthropic-s-proposed-ai-safety-watchdog-faces-questions-87811053)

### Understanding the issue: What if the monitor is friends with the academy director?

'METR' is a watchdog that has stepped forward to take responsibility for the safety of the AI industry. [Source 1](https://www.breitbart.com/tech/2026/09/16/dario-amodeis-prefered-ai-watchdog-has-deep-ties-to-anthropic-effectic-altruism/) When Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei proposed that external experts should police AI companies, METR was the first organization he pointed to. [Source 7](https://ae.headtopics.com/news/who-is-dario-amodei-the-anthropic-ceo-pushing-independent-87913240)

Here is an easy analogy: suppose our school decides to hire an external inspector to prevent cheating on exams, but it turns out that the inspector follows the same religion as the director of the academy running the exam and received investment from the same people. The students would never believe the inspector's rulings are fair. [Source 2](https://nypost.com/2026/09/15/business/anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-s-handpicked-ai-watchdog-has-deep-ties-to-effective-altruism-movement-a-complete-joke/)

METR originally spun out of the 'Alignment Research Center (ARC)', an organization based on the philosophy of 'Effective Altruism' (the belief that one should do good in the most effective way possible). [Source 2](https://nypost.com/2026/09/15/business/anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-s-handpicked-ai-watchdog-has-deep-ties-to-effective-altruism-movement-a-complete-joke/) Recent reports indicate that some critics have expressed concern by describing this movement as 'bizarre, cult-like'. [Source 11](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/ai-giant-anthropic-ties-cult-213537511.html) Consequently, METR is facing criticism that it is too closely linked to the company Anthropic and the philosophical beliefs shared by its CEO. [Source 4](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/daily-ai-wire_anthropics-proposed-ai-watchdog-metr-linked-activity-7505951179219234816-b1o-)

### Current Situation: Allegations of collusion

Many critics are currently raising their voices. While METR is in the spotlight due to Anthropic's proposal, critics argue that it is deeply connected to Anthropic and its fund investors, making it difficult to expect truly 'independent oversight'. [Source 8](https://ae.headtopics.com/news/anthropic-s-proposed-ai-safety-watchdog-faces-questions-87811053) Some METR staff members are even connected to the University of Oxford, considered one of the hubs of this ideology. [Source 12](https://bifu.co/news/detail/amazon-s-190b-anthropic-stake-has-ties-to-bankman-fried) In essence, the current situation is that the organization meant to watch over AI is under suspicion of being 'in collusion' with the very companies it is supposed to be monitoring. [Source 8](https://ae.headtopics.com/news/anthropic-s-proposed-ai-safety-watchdog-faces-questions-87811053)

### What happens next?

For the AI industry to gain trust, a clearer and more independent oversight system is needed. The key is whether METR can provide a plan to prove its independence, or if a completely new third-party organization will be established. What we need to pay attention to is when a fair system will emerge that can answer the fundamental question: 'Who watches the watchmen?' This is not merely a technical issue, but one directly linked to public trust in the AI that has become deeply embedded in our lives.

### MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Perspective

If the hands of the monitor are stained with the fingerprints of the one being monitored, no report will ever convince the public. For the AI industry to mature, it desperately needs 'eyes on the outside' that are not organizations formed through partnerships by companies themselves, but entities entirely untethered from anyone's ideology.
