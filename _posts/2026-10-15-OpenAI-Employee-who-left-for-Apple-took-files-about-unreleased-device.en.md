Imagine this: You are working on a vital secret project at your company, and you suddenly discover that a colleague who just left for a competitor has copied the company's core blueprints and confidential files. How would you feel? Recently, things between the two giants leading the global IT industry, **OpenAI** and **Apple**, have been unfolding like a scene from a spy movie.

## Why Does This Matter?

While it may simply look like a fight between two companies, this incident reveals the environment in which the latest technologies we use are created. Apple possesses a massive ecosystem of devices like smartphones and tablets, while OpenAI holds powerful AI technology like ChatGPT. Originally, the two had a relationship where they could create synergy, but conflict began when OpenAI started developing its own hardware [Ref 1](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3902614523446912).

What if the ChatGPT inside the iPhone you use every day was built along with someone's "secret file" migration? When talent leaves and takes core company technology with them, it goes beyond a simple "job change"; it is a serious issue that can shake up the landscape of technological competition and even impact consumer trust.

## Making It Easy to Understand

Let's compare this situation to a "cooking contest."

Assume there is a massive, long-established restaurant called Apple, and a startup restaurant called OpenAI that serves innovative dishes. This is similar to a situation where an Apple chef is scouted by the OpenAI restaurant and steals the secret "next-generation pot" blueprints and the special sauce recipe that Apple has been secretly developing for 10 years.

According to a recent announcement by OpenAI, a former employee who left for Apple transferred over 100 confidential files about an unreleased AI device to a personal account [Ref 9](https://nypost.com/2026/10/14/business/openai-claims-ex-employee-brought-confidential-docs-about-unreleased-device-to-apple/). Conversely, Apple has already filed a lawsuit alleging that former engineers who left for OpenAI extracted confidential information regarding the company's unreleased device manufacturing processes and components [Ref 6](https://wisatahits.blog/apple-openai-macbook-schematic-hardware-lawsuit/).

Here, a "schematic" is a matter of life and death for a tech company. The methods of data leakage are also very diverse. Methods reportedly employed include not returning company-issued laptops and keeping them, or secretly checking network access privileges [Ref 3](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/what-did-openai-allegedly-take-from-apple).

## Where We Stand

The current situation is truly a "mud-slinging fight."

1. **Mutual Allegations of Trade Secret Theft**: Both OpenAI and Apple are continuing legal responses, claiming that the other's former employees took their trade secrets [Ref 2](https://www.businesstimes.com.sg/startups-tech/technology/apple-accuses-openai-destroying-evidence-exchange-barbs). Apple sued individuals like Liu and Tang Tan, and OpenAI has also reported a case of trade secret theft by an employee who moved to Apple [Ref 5](https://www.gotechtor.com/apple-openai-lawsuit-stolen-schematic-forensic-evidence/).
2. **The Shock of the Bugging Incident**: An incident even occurred at OpenAI headquarters where an employee was arrested on charges of hiding a sophisticated recording device in an executive conference room [Ref 11](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/13/openai-employee-arrested-recording-device.html). It is a case that lays bare how information is leaked inside tech companies where security is life [Ref 14](https://rt-english.com/news/647165-openai-bugging-allegations-fbi/).
3. **OpenAI's Ambition**: There are also reports that OpenAI is preparing for an Initial Public Offering (IPO) valued at $500 billion [Ref 3](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/what-did-openai-allegedly-take-from-apple). It seems these frictions are continuing as they aggressively recruit talent to produce innovative products [Ref 2](https://www.businesstimes.com.sg/startups-tech/technology/apple-accuses-openai-destroying-evidence-exchange-barbs).

## What Comes Next

While talent movement in the tech industry is a natural phenomenon, allegations of trade secret theft in this manner can degrade the credibility of the entire industry. We should watch for the following points:

- **Court Judgments**: Depending on the results of the legal battle between the two companies, it is highly likely that new "talent recruitment guidelines" between corporations will be created.
- **Enhanced Security**: Following this bugging incident and the trade secret theft cases, it is expected that all big-tech companies will drastically strengthen their internal security controls.
- **Innovation vs. Ethics**: The social debate over whether technological advancement should proceed even while risking such security threats will likely become even more heated.

OpenAI says, "Employees have the right to choose, and it is natural to move to a startup that makes innovative products" [Ref 2](https://www.businesstimes.com.sg/startups-tech/technology/apple-accuses-openai-destroying-evidence-exchange-barbs). However, where the line should be drawn in that process remains a challenge that the two giants must prove for themselves.
