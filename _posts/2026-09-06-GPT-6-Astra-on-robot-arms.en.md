Imagine this. You wake up in the morning and tell your AI, "Automate all the complex tasks I need to do today." The AI then proceeds to manipulate software to draft reports and controls the robot arm in your office to tidy up your surroundings. While we have only seen scenes like this in science fiction movies, that boundary became a little blurrier in September 2026.

OpenAI unveiled its new flagship AI model, 'GPT-6 Astra,' on September 3rd and 4th. [[Source 1](https://openai.robocurve.org/gpt-6-astra/), [Source 7](https://thenewstack.io/openai-gpt6-astra-benchmarks/), [Source 14](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/04/openai-releasing-major-upgrade-to-chatgpt-and-codex-with-gpt-6-astra-details-here/)] Moving beyond just a chatbot that writes well, it has demonstrated the ability to handle physical devices right in front of us.

## Why It Matters

If past AI was merely an 'assistant that summarizes information,' GPT-6 Astra is closer to an 'agent'—a program that acts autonomously to achieve a goal. What is particularly noteworthy is that this model has boldly challenged the physical realm of 'robot control.'

Significant changes are also expected in the business sector. We are moving from an era where humans had to manually click through complex multi-step business 'workflows' to an era where AI handles them by operating the software directly. This holds immense potential to dramatically increase work productivity. OpenAI's Greg Brockman even declared, "The era of AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) has begun today." [[Source 13](https://wccftech.com/openai-wows-with-the-agi-era-gpt-6-astra-with-preliminary-benchmarks-showing-remarkable-performance-gains-as-sam-altman-says-releases-will-now-be-paced-by-safety-considerations-and-not-capabilit/)]

## The Explainer

To illustrate how much smarter GPT-6 Astra has become, let's look at two examples.

**1. Robot Arm Control: AI with Eyes and Hands**
OpenAI tested GPT-6 Astra's capabilities using a robot arm called 'YAM.' It was tasked with the command, "Pick up the red block on the table and put it in the bowl." [[Source 1](https://openai.robocurve.org/gpt-6-astra/)] Just as a person would organize toys, the AI calculated the joints and movements of the robot arm in real-time to execute the order. In simple terms, the AI has learned how to translate its 'thoughts' into 'physical actions' outside the digital space.

**2. Complex Task Processing: An Agent Putting Together Puzzle Pieces**
Its performance in business environments is also astounding. In a test called 'AutomationBench,' GPT-6 Astra successfully performed 41.4% of complex, multi-step tasks. [[Source 6](https://kie.ai/gpt-6-astra)] Think of it this way: when solving a complex Tetris puzzle, a beginner might struggle to fit one piece at a time, but an expert sees the whole picture and fills the gaps quickly. GPT-6 Astra possesses the skills of an 'experienced expert' who rapidly grasps the flow of the entire task and completes it flawlessly.

## Where We Stand

GPT-6 Astra is currently being rolled out sequentially to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users, and is also available via API and AWS (Amazon Web Services). [[Source 14](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/04/openai-releasing-major-upgrade-to-chatgpt-and-codex-with-gpt-6-astra-details-here/)]

This model is evaluated as producing nearly perfect results in security, mathematics, and logical reasoning fields. [[Source 4](https://interestingengineering.com/ai-robotics/openai-gpt-6-astra-agi)] In particular, it recorded a phenomenal score of 100% on ExploitBench, a security-related test, and 98.6% on the ARC-AGI-3 test, which measures complex problem-solving abilities. Consequently, it has been praised as the most intelligent and aligned model released to date. [[Source 7](https://thenewstack.io/openai-gpt6-astra-benchmarks/), [Source 13](https://wccftech.com/openai-wows-with-the-agi-era-gpt-6-astra-with-preliminary-benchmarks-showing-remarkable-performance-gains-as-sam-altman-says-releases-will-now-be-paced-by-safety-considerations-and-not-capabilit/)]

## What's Next

Moving forward, 'Agent AI' that communicates with and controls the devices around us, beyond simply conversing, will become more deeply embedded in our lives. It won't be long before smart homes and various industrial machines—not just robot arms—will be directed by high-performance AI like GPT-6 Astra.

What we need now is the wisdom to deliberate on how to safely and beneficially utilize these powerful AI agents, rather than feeling vague fear. After all, AI is evolving from a 'consultant' to an 'executor.'
