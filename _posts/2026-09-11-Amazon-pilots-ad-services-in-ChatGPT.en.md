Imagine this: It’s evening, and you’re debating where to go for your upcoming vacation. You open ChatGPT and ask, “Recommend a quiet getaway destination for this autumn.” What if, at the end of the response, information about a familiar travel agency’s package was added as naturally as if a friendly travel expert were saying, “You might find this quite lovely as well”?

AI services, which previously only listed dry search results, are now transforming into the “pathway to shopping” where we choose products and weigh our purchases. News has emerged that Amazon, the world’s largest online retailer, and OpenAI, the icon of AI, have joined forces.

## Why It Matters

Until now, whenever we needed something, we had to type keywords into a search bar and spend time sifting through numerous ads and information to find an answer ourselves. But now, an era is opening where we can have a conversation with AI and get personalized recommendations perfectly suited to our situation.

The impact of this change on our daily lives is significant. First, ads are no longer random banners unrelated to what we are looking for; they come across as “essential information” perfectly tailored to the situation we are pondering. For busy modern people, this could save the trouble of searching for information, but concerns also arise that it may become difficult to distinguish where pure AI recommendations end and advertising begins. Ensuring the transparency of ads from the user’s perspective will be a major challenge we face as we interact with AI in the future.

## The Explainer

Shall we use an analogy to understand this ad introduction?

If traditional online ads were leaflets posted throughout a department store or eye-catching banners, these conversational ads in ChatGPT are like a **“wise shopping guide.”** It is similar to when we ask a shopping assistant, “Which shoes are good?” and they explain the pros and cons of the shoes while suggesting, “Ah, as a matter of fact, this item from this store I saw yesterday is just your style.”

Amazon advertisers can now extend their product campaigns into ChatGPT conversations[Source: Amazon pilots ad services in ChatGPT: What marketers need to know](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/amazon-pilots-ad-services-in-chatgpt-what-marketers-need-to-know/829945/). The AI does not just respond; it acts as a “shopping link” by providing the most appropriate product information during the “golden time” when users are exploring information and deciding on a purchase[Source: Amazon Adds ChatGPT to Shopping Cart for Ad Buyers](https://www.themeasure.net/amazon-adds-chatgpt-to-shopping-cart-for-ad-buyers/).

## Where We Stand

This service is currently in the initial stages. The pilot program, which began on September 10, 2026, is not yet open to everyone[Source: Amazon brings its DSP to OpenAI's ChatGPT ads](https://digiday.com/media-buying/amazon-brings-its-dsp-to-openais-chatgpt-ads-extending-its-supply-chasing-streak/). It is currently operating exclusively for select advertisers in the US, with brands like “Delta Vacations” participating early on[Source: Amazon pilots ad services in ChatGPT](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/10/amazon-chatgptads-open-ai.html).

It can be viewed as a testing process to see how ads harmonize with conversations in a limited environment and how readily users accept them. OpenAI, unlike its past stance of calling ads a “last resort,” has now entered a stage of commercializing touchpoints with users in more diverse ways[Source: OpenAI Officially Launches Advertising Services in ChatGPT](https://news.aibase.com/news/29785).

## What's Next

Moving forward, the AI models we use will increasingly understand our tastes and regular purchasing patterns with keen insight. Like the Amazon pilot case announced today, other platforms are also expected to continue exploring strategies that place AI at the center of shopping[Source: Amazon Ads announces new integration with ChatGPT](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/news/amazon-ads-chat-gpt-advertising-integration).

However, users may feel resistant to an increase in advertisements. The success or failure of this experiment will depend on how “intelligently” the conversational experience provided by artificial intelligence can offer us tangible benefits without causing disruptions.

## MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter View
The days of dreaming of an AI without ads are passing. Now, AI is not just an assistant that answers questions, but a smart salesman trying to understand your tastes and even help you shop. It is a very important time to determine whether users will perceive ads as useful “information” or as “noise” that disrupts the conversation.
