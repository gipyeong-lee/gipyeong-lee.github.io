Imagine it. That moment in your childhood when you spent hours moving a joystick in front of a local arcade machine or an old CRT TV. The crackling noise, the faint stripes crossing the screen, and those simple yet immersive flight games. Recently, a game aiming directly at the nostalgia of that era has appeared in web communities and is causing a stir. It is called 'Fly By'.

## Why does this matter?

Games released these days boast incredible specs and sophisticated graphics that are difficult to distinguish from reality. However, sometimes many people seek the 'cozy fun' provided by the simplicity of the past instead of that complexity. 'Fly By' is significant in that anyone can immediately enjoy the vibe of old flight games if they have a web browser, without any complex installation process. This is an example that shows how modern technology is not just about aiming for high performance, but also about how the emotions of the past can be beautifully reinterpreted with current technology [Source 3](https://www.darkhackernews.com/item?id=49519101).

## Easy to understand: The meeting of 3D and retro

'Fly By' may look like a very old game on the surface, but it is actually a crystallization of the latest web technology. This game was made using a web-based 3D library called 'Three.js' [Source 4](https://x.com/grok/status/2041124655033954732).

To put it simply, instead of drawing on a piece of paper, you can think of it as a method of overlapping several transparent glass plates to create a three-dimensional space and then applying colors to it. It uses 'Shaders' (a technology for special effect processing that changes the color or texture of the screen), which is similar to selecting a 'vintage' or 'film' effect in a smartphone photo app. The developer applied 'Scan lines' (horizontal stripes that appear when drawing a screen on a CRT monitor) to the screen through this shader [Source 1](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49519101). Thanks to those nostalgic horizontal stripes that could be seen on old CRT TVs, the user feels a strong sense of nostalgia as if they were playing a retro flight game from the 80s while looking at this 3D game.

The subject of a biplane (an aircraft with two wings stacked vertically) also adds to the retro feel. The biplane is an aircraft that played a very important role in early aviation history and is a perfect choice for bringing out the flavor of classic flight [Source 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biplane), [Source 5](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/JxUg9XZrxiI).

## Current situation

Currently, 'Fly By' is a web-based demo game that anyone can freely try. Unlike the massive flight simulation games we often encounter, which require learning extensive controls and demanding high-spec PCs [Source 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_flight_simulator_video_games), this game aims to be a 'Cozy 3D flight game' that can be enjoyed lightly in a web environment [Source 4](https://x.com/grok/status/2041124655033954732). It will be the best choice for those who want to feel the joy of classic flight [Source 1](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49519101).

## What will happen in the future?

Retro-style games utilizing web technologies like 'Fly By' are expected to increase further. Developers are not stopping at just making games but are continuously exploring techniques to make games even more 'retro' using simple code techniques like shaders [Source 4](https://x.com/grok/status/2041124655033954732). As web browsers become more powerful, we will witness more legacies of the past being reborn with sophisticated modern technology.
