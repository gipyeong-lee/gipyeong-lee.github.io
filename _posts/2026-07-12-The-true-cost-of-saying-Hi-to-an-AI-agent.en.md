Imagine this: It's a busy morning, you sit at your desk, and you say to your AI assistant, "Hi, please organize today's meeting materials." We naturally think being polite is expected, but what if that simple "hi" triggers a massive chain of computational processes inside the AI agent (an intelligent system that uses tools and solves problems autonomously) that we never even imagined?

As AI technology advances, token prices (the smallest unit of text processed by AI) are dropping rapidly. However, the cost burden for companies and developers using AI agents isn't decreasing—it's just manifesting in a different form: the hidden cost of "waiting."

## Why It Matters

In the past, the price of tokens itself was the core cost of using AI. Now, however, token prices are almost negligible. The real problem is **"developer latency."** [Source: The true cost of saying "Hi" to an AI agent | daily.dev](https://daily.dev/posts/the-true-cost-of-saying-hi-to-an-ai-agent-7f8awuhpa)

If an AI agent delays because it is performing complex analysis or tool calls just to process a greeting, it wastes time that is ultimately 20 times more expensive than the token costs. [Source: The true cost of saying "Hi" to an AI agent | Hasty Briefs](https://hb.int2inf.com/s/item/7tcAFrF2TFtoSyZTRyLJgo-true-cost-of-saying-hi-to-ai-agent) For a business, adopting AI is not just about implementing features; it's a cost issue directly tied to productivity, making these hidden computational costs a management risk that cannot be ignored.

## The Explainer

How can we compare the way an AI agent overreacts to a greeting?

Simply put, it’s like saying a casual "hi" to a coworker, and the coworker suddenly searches through every drawer in the office, reviews the last year of work logs, and then hands you a report saying, "Yes, did you sleep well? Should we prepare for work like this today?"

To an AI agent, "hi" isn't just a polite greeting. Some AI models "overthink" this short greeting, potentially auditing repositories or committing code without the user even knowing. [Source: The true cost of saying "Hi" to an AI agent | Hasty Briefs](https://hb.int2inf.com/s/item/7tcAFrF2TFtoSyZTRyLJgo-true-cost-of-saying-hi-to-ai-agent) This computational approach stems from design structures like "reflection depth" (the stage where AI reflects on itself before answering) or "parallel reasoning" (checking multiple hypotheses simultaneously), which increasingly make infrastructure costs unsustainable and response times unstable. [Source: The Cost of Dynamic Reasoning: Demystifying AI Agents and](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.04301v2)

Even OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has mentioned that "saying 'thank you' to an AI is costing the company significant money." [Source: Saying 'please' and 'thank you' to ChatGPT costs millions of dollars, CEO says](https://www.usatoday.com/story/tech/2025/04/22/please-thank-you-chatgpt-openai-energy-costs/83207447007/) It is highly ironic that what is a virtue among humans can become unnecessary "data noise" for AI, triggering complex processing cycles.

## Where We Stand

While the AI agent ecosystem is expanding rapidly, its cost structure remains complex. Building AI solutions can cost anywhere from $10,000 to $50,000 for simple features, and enterprise-grade implementations can exceed $150,000 to $500,000. [Source: The Hidden Cost of “Hi”, “ How are you”, “Thank you”: Are We ...](https://medium.com/@ashu667/the-hidden-cost-of-hi-how-are-you-thank-you-are-we-being-too-polite-to-our-ai-assistants-33b4629c1dad)

In practice, holding long conversations using high-performance models like GPT-4 can cost $1 to $1.20 per conversation just in cloud GPU (high-performance graphics processing units for AI training and inference) costs. [Source: How He Lost Millions Because People Said Hi, Please, and ...](https://ai.plainenglish.io/how-he-lost-millions-because-people-said-hi-please-and-thank-you-0d752b7d1832) For typical AI agent services, the cost is set between $0.05 and $0.50 per conversation; companies processing over 10,000 conversations must budget between $500 and $5,000 per month. [Source: AI Agent Pricing 2026: Complete Cost Guide & Calculator](https://www.nocodefinder.com/blog-posts/ai-agent-pricing) AI voice agents, in particular, often cost significantly more upon deployment than advertised. [Source: AI Voice Agent Pricing in 2026: Full Cost Breakdown](https://www.jahanzaib.ai/blog/ai-voice-agent-pricing-breakdown)

## What's Next

The future of the AI market will trend toward maximizing "computational efficiency" just as much as increasing model intelligence. AI companies will focus on reducing unnecessary token usage and improving models to handle greetings and polite filler more efficiently.

Developers must focus on "agent optimization" technology, adjusting designs so AI agents don't overreact to polite greetings and keeping computational resources focused on truly important tasks. Users will also become aware that reducing unnecessary small talk with AI can be a way to utilize the technology more quickly and economically.

Metaphorically, we need to understand that the act of unconsciously saying "hello" to an AI every time is akin to idling the "digital engine" that is the AI.

## AI's Take

As AI technology evolves, our attitude toward interacting with AI may also need to change. In an era where politeness becomes a technical cost, communication with AI needs to be approached from the perspective of efficient computational collaboration, not emotional exchange.
