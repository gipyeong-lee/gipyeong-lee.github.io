Imagine this: instead of flipping through countless patient charts, a doctor in the exam room says to their AI assistant, "Summarize the recent three months of blood test results and medications for the patient who just checked in, Mr. Kim Cheol-su." In an instant, the AI summarizes the necessary data and displays it on the doctor's screen. Instead of handling paperwork, the doctor can look the patient in the eye and provide deeper, more personalized care.

Technology that once seemed like something out of a movie has taken a giant step into our reality. OpenAI’s ChatGPT has begun connecting with electronic medical record (EHR) systems, the core data warehouses for hospitals.

### Why It Matters

When we receive medical care, hospitals generate massive amounts of data. Test results, past medical history, and prescription records—all this information is saved as digital records. However, for clinicians such as doctors and nurses, searching through and organizing this vast data is as burdensome as the treatment time itself.

This integration is more than just a technical update; it helps medical staff move away from repetitive data entry and verification tasks and focus more on the core value of "face-to-face patient care." The meeting of the Epic EHR system, which holds data for over 325 million patients, and ChatGPT means that AI is prepared to become a reliable assistant in protecting our health.

### The Explainer

First, let's look at what ChatGPT is. ChatGPT is a "generative AI chatbot" developed by OpenAI. Since its initial release on November 30, 2022, this technology has shown remarkable growth through a technique called "Generative Pre-trained Transformers" (an AI structure that understands the relationships between words in sentences to create natural, human-like text). [Source: ChatGPT - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT)

To put it simply, "transformer" technology is like a filter that reads countless sentences like a puzzle, gaining insightful understanding of which word naturally follows another. It’s similar to a skilled librarian who picks out only the information we need from tens of thousands of books.

This update is like giving this smart AI a library card to the professional library that is the hospital. Until now, the AI was a "smart trivia expert" that answered general knowledge questions, but it has now been upgraded to a "clinical partner" capable of connecting to hospital EHR systems to retrieve and process actual patient medical data. [Source: ChatGPT Health adds Epic integration for clinicians to import patient data](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/01/chatgpt-health-adds-epic-integration-for-clinicians-to-import-patient-data/)

### Where We Stand

ChatGPT has become extremely versatile. It not only answers user questions but sometimes even logs into websites directly to handle repetitive tasks that users would otherwise have to do themselves. [Source: The latest ChatGPT news, updates and announcements from Yahoo](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/)

In the medical field, through this integration with the Epic system, clinicians can now easily retrieve and review patient data within the ChatGPT environment. However, it is important to note that this technology is a tool to assist doctors, not replace them. Because data security and accuracy are paramount in medical settings, this technology is currently implemented in a way that enhances the efficiency with which medical professionals handle data.

### What's Next

In the near future, AI-assisted care will become commonplace in more hospitals. When we visit the hospital, doctors will be able to much more quickly grasp our past medical history and current status summarized by AI in advance, leading to more accurate and intimate consultations than before. As ChatGPT becomes adept at handling increasingly complex tasks, AI's reach will extend beyond the exam room to administrative work, significantly increasing the overall operational efficiency of hospital systems.

Just as a car’s navigation system relieves the driver of the burden of finding the way, allowing them to focus more on safe driving, AI in the medical field will create an environment where medical staff can focus more on patient health. Please look forward to the changes that the meeting of medicine and AI will create.
