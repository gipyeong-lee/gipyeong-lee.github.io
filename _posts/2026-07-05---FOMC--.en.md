Samsung Electronics, which makes the semiconductors that serve as the brains for the smartphones we use every day, and the U.S. Federal Reserve (Fed), which decides the direction of global interest rates while we sleep—why do these two pieces of news change the numbers in our stock accounts? Much like checking the weather forecast to decide whether to bring an umbrella, earnings announcements and economic meeting minutes serve as information that tells investors about the "weather" of the market.

### Why is this important?

The stock market is like a massive wave. While macroeconomic policies (such as FOMC minutes) create the big waves, the report cards of individual companies (such as Samsung Electronics' earnings) determine how fast the ship can travel. For retail investors, these pieces of information are important because the current market is locked in a tight tug-of-war between concerns over inflation and expectations for interest rate cuts. ### Making it easy to understand

Simply put, the **FOMC (Federal Open Market Committee) minutes** are the 'detailed meeting records of the U.S. central bank.' They gathered about three weeks ago to decide whether to raise or lower U.S. interest rates, and they are now releasing the detailed content of those conversations. [[Weekly Economic Outlook] U.S. 'Interest Rate/Tariff' direction emerging… Where is the global stock market headed? - MoneyS](https://www.moneys.co.kr/article/2025070414505457395) Metaphorically, this is like **'the teacher (Fed) finally revealing the details of a student record they secretly met about a month ago.'** Investors look at this content to figure out, "Oh, the teacher intends to raise rates," or "There is room for a cut," and adjust their investment strategies accordingly.

On the other hand, **Samsung Electronics' earnings** are like the **'paycheck of the head of our household.'** If the breadwinner earns well, the morale of the entire family (the KOSPI market) rises. In fact, experts analyze that if upward revisions to Samsung Electronics' earnings forecasts begin in earnest, it could significantly strengthen the upward momentum of the domestic stock market. [Should I regret it if I sell now… Scenarios for Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix after FOMC [Weekly Stock Market Outlook] - Financial News](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202606140852422153)

### Current situation

The market is currently a bit anxious. As inflation continues to rise, expectations that the U.S. will lower its benchmark interest rate are dwindling, and concerns are even spreading that they might raise rates further. ["Where is the market headed as it approaches '8,000 points'… Focus on FOMC minutes amidst inflation concerns"](https://v.daum.net/v/20260517060226029)

The movements of foreign investors are also sensitive. Recently, they sold over 670 billion won worth of Samsung Electronics stock in a single day. [Foreigners 'selling' for the first time in 4 trading days on KOSPI… Will they return after the U.S. FOMC? | Yonhap News](https://www.yna.co.kr/amp/view/AKR20260617129700008) However, the market is waiting for the sturdy puzzle pieces of U.S. inflation indicators, the performance of the 'Magnificent 7' (the seven major U.S. tech stocks leading the market), and Samsung Electronics' earnings to come together. [Where is the market headed this week… Relies on U.S. inflation indicators and M7 earnings - Maeil Business Newspaper](https://www.mk.co.kr/news/stock/10995785)

### What will happen in the future?

We need to keep an eye on two things moving forward. First, in what direction the U.S. Federal Reserve will take interest rates. Second, what kind of earnings guidance (future earnings forecasts) major companies, including Samsung Electronics, will provide. Looking at past cases, the U.S. benchmark interest rate has sometimes been hiked to levels much higher than expected. [U.S. raising rates, Korea raising rates… Where is the market headed in a full-blown interest rate hike era?](https://www.businesspost.co.kr/BP?command=article_view&num=275154) Because these variables are intertwined, the market will likely struggle to find a direction for the time being.
