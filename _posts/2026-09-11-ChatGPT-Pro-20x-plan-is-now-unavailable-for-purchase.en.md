Imagine you have an assistant you use for work every day, and suddenly you hear news that its capabilities have improved dramatically. So many people flocked to use this capability that new users can no longer apply for that premium assistant service for the time being. This is exactly what has been happening recently among ChatGPT users.

### Why does this matter?

New users can no longer sign up for the 'ChatGPT Pro 20x' plan, a premium service provided by OpenAI for $200 per month. [[Source: OpenAI, ChatGPT Pro 20x Subscription Suspension Notice]](https://mihata.jp/column/chatgpt-pro-20x-purchase-paused) This service has offered much higher usage limits and more powerful features than the standard 'Plus' plan for professionals and enterprise users. [[Source: ChatGPT Pro 20x Feature Guide]](https://ggsel.net/catalog/product/gpt-6-astra-chatgpt-pro-5x-20x-1m-gotovaia-podpiska-polnaia-g-arantiia-102635272)

This news does not simply mean that an "expensive product is sold out." It clearly shows that as AI technology advances, a massive amount of servers and computing resources (the computer facilities that operate AI models) are required to run it. It means that when many users flock to a service, even the most advanced technology can hit physical limits and must temporarily close its doors.

### Simple explanation

Think of it this way: consider the AI model as a restaurant kitchen. If the 'Plus' plan is a table for regular customers, the 'Pro 20x' plan is a special seat where you can use unlimited premium ingredients and have a dedicated chef.

However, after rumors spread that the newly released 'GPT-6 Astra' menu was delicious, everyone flocked to sit at that special seat. [[Source: OpenAI, Subscription Suspension Due to Surge in Astra Demand]](https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-pauses-chatgpt-20x-purchases/) Eventually, as orders came in faster than the kitchen's cooking speed, OpenAI decided to temporarily stop accepting new customers to maintain service quality.

### Current situation

Since September 10, 2026, OpenAI has temporarily paused both new registrations for the ChatGPT Pro 20x plan and upgrades from existing plans to this tier. [[Source: OpenAI Official Notice]](https://www.progressiverobot.com/2026/09/10/chatgpt-pro-subscriptions-on-hold-astra-demand/)

However, existing subscribers need not worry:

1. **Existing subscriptions remain:** Those already subscribed to the Pro 20x plan can continue to use the service without any issues, and subscriptions will continue to renew normally. [[Source: Subscription Status Guide]](https://x.com/btibor91/status/2098057726295457975)
2. **Other tiers are open:** The $100/month Pro plan is still available for new sign-ups or upgrades. [[Source: Pro Plan Availability]](https://x.com/mark_k/status/2098126050425188774)

### What happens next?

This measure is due to temporary "capacity limitations." [[Source: Reason for Service Pause]](https://forest.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/2140106.html) In the AI industry, server load spikes frequently occur whenever a new model is released. OpenAI is expected to reopen registrations as soon as infrastructure is expanded. Those who need the highest tier immediately will have to periodically check the official help page and wait.

### MindTickleBytes AI Reporter's Perspective

Technology is advancing so quickly that we have entered an era where even if we have the money ready to buy the technology, servers physically cannot keep up. This reminds us once again of how important it is for companies to secure not only the "intelligence" of AI but also the "infrastructure"—the power that supports that intelligence—as a key competitive advantage.
