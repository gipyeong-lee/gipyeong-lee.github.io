Imagine this: You asked ChatGPT this morning, "Recommend equipment for camping this weekend." But instead of camping gear, ChatGPT shows you an advertisement for 'used car sales' that has absolutely nothing to do with your request.

ChatGPT is a powerful AI assistant that makes our lives convenient. However, its appearance as an advertising platform is still in its infancy. We at MindTickleBytes have easily broken down the news that the recently introduced ad features in ChatGPT are being harshly criticized by marketers as "garbage."

## Why is this important?

Digital advertising is not simply about placing banners on a screen. For advertisers, it is key to spend ad budgets efficiently, and for users, it is about getting useful information perfectly tailored to their interests. However, current ChatGPT ads lack the 'power to accurately find customers' that marketers expect.

From an advertiser's perspective, there is a risk of it becoming a 'money pit' where you only pay costs but it does not lead to results (purchases). From a user's perspective, it can become an annoying element that disrupts the natural flow of conversation with the AI. If this problem is not solved, ChatGPT's ad revenue model will take a big hit. In fact, while ChatGPT ads set a record of $1 billion (about 1 trillion KRW) in annual revenue within 200 days of release, experts believe it will be difficult for OpenAI to achieve the $2.5 billion in ad revenue they targeted for this year [ChatGPT Ads Reach $1 Billion [Source](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/326128/20260901/chatgpt-ad-targeting-is-garbage.htm)].

## Easy to Understand: Why is targeting difficult?

Digital ad targeting is like 'finding a needle in a desert.' It can only succeed if the AI accurately reads what the user wants to buy, which is called 'intent recognition' in marketing terms.

To use an analogy, a good ad platform is like a filter in a photo app. Just as you blur the messy background and make only the subject stand out, it must filter out only the truly necessary ads from the user's vast amount of data. However, current ChatGPT ads rely only on simple data like conversation content and location information [ChatGPT Is Now Shoving Ads Into Your Prompts [Source](https://strategicmarketingtribe.com/marketing-news/b/chatgpt-ads-openai-marketing-storybrand)].

In simple terms, ChatGPT knows 'what the user just said' right now, but it does not have a deep grasp of 'what this user actually has the will to buy.' One marketer complained that their ad campaign "worked like typical display ads that just show ads to people who are just looking, rather than finding people who will convert into buyers" [Data Reveals ChatGPT Ads Not Performing [Source](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/417528/data-reveals-chatgpt-ads-not-performing.html)].

## Current Situation: How far has it come?

OpenAI is working hard to resolve marketers' complaints. In August 2026, they finally introduced geographic targeting (location-based ads) and custom audiences (specific group settings) [Data Reveals ChatGPT Ads Not Performing [Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/media-advertising/articles/advertisers-agencies-chatgpt-ads-lack-172437111.html)].

But there is still a long way to go.
- **Inaccurate targeting**: Advertisers still feel that there is a lack of granular control over targeting tailored to the flow of conversation [ChatGPT ads add exclusions but targeting still lags [Source](https://www.contentgrip.com/chatgpt-ad-targeting-controls/)].
- **Low efficiency**: According to a recent study, over 14% of ChatGPT ads appeared as inappropriate ads irrelevant to the user's intent [ChatGPT ads miss user intent over 14% of the time, study finds [Source](https://www.emarketer.com/content/chatgpt-ads-miss-user-intent-over-14--of-time--study-finds)].
- **High barrier to entry**: The minimum spend required for an advertiser to start testing is $200,000, creating an environment where many small and medium-sized businesses cannot easily test it [ChatGPT Ads: Complete Guide for Marketers and Users (2026) [Source](https://www.aitooldiscovery.com/guides/chatgpt-ads)].

In summary, ChatGPT is a great AI, but in the advertising market, it is still at the stage of a 'beginner who doesn't know how to advertise properly.'

## What will happen in the future?

For ChatGPT ads to survive, the sophistication of data analysis must be much higher than it is now. Although it is currently operated through Microsoft's advertising system (Microsoft Advertising) [ChatGPTAdTargetingOptions: Advanced Audience... [Source](https://www.gptads.com/blog/chatgpt-ad-targeting-audience-segmentation-strategies)], the key is how to naturally connect ChatGPT's unique 'conversational context' with ads.

Going forward, rather than dumping large budgets all at once, advertisers are expected to start with small-scale tests to check how well ChatGPT ads fit their brand. From the user's perspective, we should demand smarter ads from ChatGPT. Ads that do not disrupt the flow of our conversation while enriching our lives.

In the monetization process of AI business, the introduction of ads may be inevitable. However, rushing while prioritizing profit over technical completeness invites users to turn away.
