Imagine you are working on a very difficult project. If you have a design expert, a coding expert, and a documentation expert all working separately, you would certainly need a "conductor" to coordinate communication between them and direct who does what. Until now, the complex process of assembling this team and delegating tasks was entirely the responsibility of humans.

However, a system that performs this "conductor" role autonomously has recently emerged in the field of Artificial Intelligence (AI). On June 22, 2026, Tokyo-based research lab Sakana AI unveiled a new system called "Fugu" that does exactly that [[Source 6](https://lushbinary.com/blog/sakana-fugu-multi-agent-orchestration-model-guide/), [Source 13](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/22/sakana-ai-launches-sakana-fugu-an-orchestration-model-that-routes-tasks-across-a-swappable-pool-of-frontier-llms/)].

## Why is this important?

The AI chatbots we commonly use usually try to do everything with one massive model. However, for some problems, a model specialized in writing is much more accurate, while for others, a model specialized in mathematical calculation is better. Until now, when developers created complex "multi-agent" systems (where multiple AIs cooperate as a team) by combining these various models, they had to manually code how each model would converse and exchange tasks with the others. It was as cumbersome as having someone other than a conductor personally recruit each orchestra player and distribute the sheet music.

Fugu completely changes this process. Developers do not need to design complex multi-agent systems; they only need to use a single model interface [[Source 4](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/blog/2026/06/sakana-fugu-multi-agent-system-as-a-model/)]. This not only significantly lowers the barrier to entry for developers looking to leverage AI technology, but also means that the AI services we encounter in our daily lives will become much smarter and more efficient in the future.

## Understanding Fugu: Conducting a Symphony of AIs

Fugu's core functionality is "multi-agent orchestration." In simple terms, think of it as a "conducting system" for AI [[Source 2](https://sakana.ai/fugu-release/)].

To use an analogy, **Fugu is the general director of a grand concert hall.**
1. **Judgment**: When a simple question comes in, Fugu solves the problem itself.
2. **Collaboration**: When a complex problem comes in, Fugu summons the most suitable experts from its "pool of expert models."
3. **Conducting**: If necessary, it delegates appropriate tasks to the experts, coordinates their opinions, and finally synthesizes them to return a perfect answer to the user [[Source 6](https://lushbinary.com/blog/sakana-fugu-multi-agent-orchestration-model-guide/), [Source 13](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/22/sakana-ai-launches-sakana-fugu-an-orchestration-model-that-routes-tasks-across-a-swappable-pool-of-frontier-llms/)].

In other words, Fugu itself is a smart language model, but it is an "intelligent conductor" that doesn't just provide answers, but also calls other AI models, specifies paths, and merges results [[Source 6](https://lushbinary.com/blog/sakana-fugu-multi-agent-orchestration-model-guide/)]. This pool of experts can even include third-party frontier LLMs (Large Language Models) [[Source 10](https://cryptobriefing.com/sakana-fugu-multi-agent-orchestration/), [Source 11](https://news.google.com/stories/CAAqNggKIjBDQklTSGpvSmMzUnZjbmt0TXpZd1NoRUtEd2lIcHYtMkVSRzF4WkxELWpGNWxDZ0FQAQ?hl=en-IN&gl=IN&ceid=IN:en)].

## How far has it come?

The "FuguUltra" model released by Sakana AI is already evaluated as showing industry-leading performance [[Source 7](https://digg.com/tech/kcygwbvq)]. A key feature is that it provides frontier-level functionality without the risks of certain technical limitations or export controls, while boasting performance equal to existing powerful, state-of-the-art models like Fable or Mythos [[Source 7](https://digg.com/tech/kcygwbvq), [Source 8](https://digg.com/tech/93cl89cb), [Source 14](https://coursiv.io/blog/sakana-ai-fugu)].

Until now, we have tried to solve everything with a single massive AI model; now, a "system that efficiently conducts small experts" like Fugu is becoming the new standard for AI [[Source 10](https://cryptobriefing.com/sakana-fugu-multi-agent-orchestration/)].

## What will happen next?

The emergence of Fugu heralds a "pragmatic era" for AI utilization. Instead of developers just looking for the biggest models unconditionally, they will focus on combining small models optimized for specific situations to maximize efficiency.

From the user's perspective, AI services are likely to feel "smarter today than they were yesterday." This is because, behind the scenes, Fugu will be changing the optimal combination of AI experts in real-time to solve your questions based on the situation. We all have reason to keep watching the journey of the "AI conductor" that Fugu will sketch out.
