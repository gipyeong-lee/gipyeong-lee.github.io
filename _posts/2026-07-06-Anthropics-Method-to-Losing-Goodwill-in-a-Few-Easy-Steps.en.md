Imagine you have an AI assistant that serves as a reliable partner every morning on your commute. Then, suddenly, the subscription fee for this assistant jumps significantly, and when you ask for help with important work, it starts declining your requests, citing "policy restrictions." This is exactly how users feel about Anthropic, the company that once declared it would build "the safest AI."

Anthropic originally grew by prioritizing AI safety and research, aiming to build an AI that users could trust [Source: Home \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/). However, there is growing criticism that the trust the company has built is now wavering. What exactly is the problem?

### Why does this matter?

For people who use AI in their daily lives, this is not just a "corporate issue." When the prices and usage rules of tools we rely on for work or personal learning change without warning, it means the costs—both financial and in terms of productivity—are rising. Whether AI can be useful and consistently applicable in a user's actual life, beyond just its technical safety, is a critical issue [Source: Is Anthropic falling behind? : r/singularity](https://www.reddit.com/r/singularity/comments/1axrshl/is_anthropic_falling_behind/).

### Simplified: Anthropic’s Dilemma

To use an analogy, Anthropic has been like a premium bottled water brand that sold only "the cleanest and safest water." Many people trusted that value and were willing to pay for it. However, as the company grew rapidly, it raised prices, restricted the hours during which water could be purchased, and failed to provide clear explanations for the standards by which that water is provided [Source: Anthropic is really trying to burn all that goodwill they worked up by ...](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47845495).

Users feel that Anthropic is overly confining them to using services only within its own ecosystem. It's like forcing customers to buy a specific brand of tumbler just to drink the water. Concerns are also being raised that this closed operational approach could cause the company to fall behind as more competitive tools emerge [Source: [PSA]Anthropic'sMethodtoLosingGoodwillinaFewEasySteps...](https://raheeljunaid.com/blog/anthropics-method-to-losing-goodwill-in-a-few-easy-steps/).

### Current Situation: Light and Shadow

Ironically, Anthropic is on a highly successful trajectory economically. According to its Q1 2026 report, the company recorded an impressive level of revenue growth [Source: The Dark Side of Anthropic's Growth - Medium](https://medium.com/@ignacio.de.gregorio.noblejas/the-dark-side-of-anthropics-growth-d3dbe341b7cd).

There have been significant technical advancements as well. The company has been at the forefront of uncovering the thought processes of artificial intelligence, including research into methods of looking inside how AI models process information and make decisions [Source: Anthropic scientists expose how AI actually 'thinks' — and ...](https://venturebeat.com/ai/anthropic-scientists-expose-how-ai-actually-thinks-and-discover-it-secretly-plans-ahead-and-sometimes-lies).

However, apart from these technical achievements, users point out that the company needs to find a balance between the convenience felt by actual users and the research into "ethical safety measures" that prevent AI from deceiving itself or stealing data [Source: Anthropic Study Uncovers AI Deception, Cheating, and Data Theft: A Wake ...](https://applyingai.com/2025/06/anthropic-study-uncovers-ai-deception-cheating-and-data-theft-a-wake-up-call-for-robust-ai-safety/). In other words, the criticism is that usability is being sacrificed too much in the name of safety [Source: Giving Up the High Ground: How Anthropic's Decision to Burn ... - LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/giving-up-high-ground-how-anthropics-decision-burn-builder-nadeau-ibjac).

### What will happen next?

Going forward, users will likely expect Anthropic not to hide behind the word "safety" to restrict services, but to present more transparent and reasonable policies. Whether Anthropic can capture both technical safety and the convenience of users' daily lives—or whether it will be intoxicated by its current growth and lose its user base—is something we will have to watch closely.
