These days, if you look at YouTube or online communities, stories are pouring out about how artificial intelligence (AI) will completely change the future of coding. Some praise AI as if it were the "reincarnation of God," while others dismiss it as "just a flashy auto-complete feature." [How much of F-Droid is LLM generated? - vuink.com](https://vuink.com/post/gvagbgvag-d-drh/whacky-corner/f-droid_slop) Amidst this confusing flood of information, how much of the apps on 'F-Droid,' the open-source app repository we love, are actually created by AI?

Imagine this: What if the alarm app or memo app you use every day wasn't actually made by a person, but whipped up in an instant by AI? As if someone had waved a magic wand. To solve this curiosity, let's take a look at the reality of F-Droid.

## Why It Matters

Open-source apps are "open creations" that anyone can inspect and modify. If these codes were mostly generated automatically by AI, the values of "human voluntary participation and community," which are the philosophy of the open-source ecosystem we trust and use, could be shaken. Also, AI-generated code can contain bugs different from those written by humans, making it a critical issue in terms of security and stability. When we review code ourselves, it's easy for trust to crumble if we can't tell whether it was written by a human or AI.

## The Explainer

First, let's clear up some simple terminology. Large Language Models (LLMs)—AI that learns from vast amounts of data to understand and generate language like humans—are like giant "sentence puzzle solvers." When we ask, "Write code for an Android calculator app," AI takes countless snippets of source code it has learned and puts them together like a puzzle to give us an answer. [LLM vs Generative AI: Key Differences and How to Choose](https://mastra.ai/articles/llm-vs-generative-ai)

To put it simply, if traditional programming is a process where a chef prepares ingredients from scratch to complete a dish, AI coding is similar to using a "sous-chef" where an AI that has watched tens of thousands of cookbooks suggests recipes or helps prep ingredients, saying, "This recipe goes well with these ingredients." However, just as the kitchen in a restaurant (the open-source repository) still needs a human head chef for the food to be safe and delicious, apps must also ultimately be made under human responsibility to be trusted.

## Where We Stand

Fortunately, the open-source world is not filled with "fake code" created by AI as we feared. According to various surveys that have looked into the reality of [F-Droid - Free and Open Source Android App Repository](https://f-droid.org/), the proportion of AI-generated code in the F-Droid repository is negligible. [F-Droid LLM generated apps proportion – how much is AI ...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4UNifOfgZuk)

Most apps are still written, bug-fixed, and steadily managed by passionate human developers around the world. [F-Droid LLM generated apps proportion – how much is AI ...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4UNifOfgZuk) While there are cases where they occasionally use AI to write parts of the code or for debugging (the process of finding and correcting program errors), that is nothing more than an auxiliary role in a development process driven by humans. [F-Droid LLM generated apps proportion – how much is AI ...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4UNifOfgZuk) In the end, humans are still holding the reins.

## What's Next

AI will continue to play a reliable assistant role for developers in the future. [How People Are Really Using Gen AI in 2025](https://hbr.org/2025/04/how-people-are-really-using-gen-ai-in-2025) However, because the community spirit of F-Droid lies in humans directly reviewing and sharing code, it seems unlikely that AI will replace entire apps for the time being. After all, F-Droid is a place that puts user freedom first. [F-Droid - Free and Open Source Android App Repository](https://f-droid.org/)

We will continue to be able to safely use F-Droid apps containing "code that feels the warmth of humans." Just as a letter containing a person's sincerity is more resonant than a text written by AI, code also only gains vitality when it is infused with human deliberation.
