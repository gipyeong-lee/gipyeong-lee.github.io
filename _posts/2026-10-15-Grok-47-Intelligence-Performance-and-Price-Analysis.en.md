Imagine this. It’s a typical morning, and the moment you get to work, you tell your AI: "Find the error in the code I wrote last week, fix it, review the related legal documents, and draft a report."

In the past, AI would have stopped at simply finding the relevant information or suggesting code snippets. But now, we are entering an era where AI plans for itself and completes tasks through multiple steps. The star of this shift is **Grok 4.7**, the latest AI model announced by xAI on September 21, 2026. [[Source: Grok 4.7, GPT-6 Astra, Claude, and Chinese models, who... / Habr](https://habr.com/ru/articles/1086496/)]

## Why Is This Important?

Many of the AI models we have used so far were closer to assistants that "answer when asked." However, Grok 4.7 demonstrates real potential as an "Agent" (a system that autonomously performs goals). [[Source: Grok 4.7 Released / Habr](https://habr.com/ru/news/1084850/)]

This means more than just better conversation quality; it means AI can now substitute for or significantly assist with human work in fields like programming, legal review, and complex data analysis. The key is its ability to plan complex tasks that take hours and verify the intermediate steps on its own. [[Source: What Grok 4.7 can do: benchmarks, strengths, and weaknesses... — Veruna AI](https://veruna.io/blog/chto-umeet-grok-4-7)]

## Understanding It Simply

If Transformer-based models (the AI structure that understands relationships between words) are excellent at grasping context, Grok 4.7 is like hiring a "highly skilled executive assistant."

Simply put, if previous AI models were "students who memorized encyclopedias by heart," Grok 4.7 is a "new recruit who has learned project management skills."

Grok 4.7 is equipped with three major capabilities:
1. **Planning**: Instead of giving an answer immediately when told to do something, it sets a sequence of what needs to be done first.
2. **Tool Utilization**: If it needs a calculator, it finds one; if it needs to search a database, it finds the search tool and uses it independently.
3. **Result Verification**: After finishing the work, it goes through a process of asking itself, "Is this actually correct?" [[Source: Grok 4.7, GPT-6 Astra, Claude, and Chinese models, who... / Habr](https://habr.com/ru/articles/1086496/)]

These capabilities are the result of further Reinforcement Learning (a method of learning on its own through rewards) based on the Grok 4.6 architecture. [[Source: Grok 4.7, GPT-6 Astra, Claude, and Chinese models, who... / Habr](https://habr.com/ru/articles/1086496/)] It also supports a generous 500,000-token context window (the unit for data processing, meaning it can remember about 500,000 words worth of information at once), allowing it to process thick law books or long code files without issue. [[Source: Grok 4.7, GPT-6 Astra, Claude, and Chinese models, who... / Habr](https://habr.com/ru/articles/1086496/)]

## Where and How Can It Be Used?

Grok 4.7 is currently available to everyone via API and can be applied directly to work by integrating with platforms like Cursor (a coding tool favored by developers) and the GrokBuild platform. [[Source: Grok 4.7 | Cursor Docs](https://cursor.com/docs/models/grok-4-7), [Source: Grok 4.7 Released / Habr](https://habr.com/ru/news/1084850/)]

In terms of intelligence quotient, it scored 46 on the v4.3.2 Intelligence Index by the independent benchmark organization 'Artificial Analysis.' This level is competitive even when compared to the top-tier models currently on the market. [[Source: Grok 4.7 Benchmarks: One Model, Three Scores](https://capitalandcompute.net/blog/grok-4-7-benchmarks/)]

The pricing policy is also reasonable. Based on general API usage, it costs $2 per 1 million input tokens and $6 per 1 million output tokens, while frequently used data can be utilized more affordably at $0.5 per 1 million tokens using the Cache feature. [[Source: Grok 4.7 | Cursor Docs](https://cursor.com/docs/models/grok-4-7)]

## What Can We Expect Next?

Grok 4.7 clearly shows the process of AI moving beyond being a simple information provider to becoming a real "worker." From now on, reliability—how well it reduces errors and perfectly executes set processes—will become a much more important metric than just how smart (intelligence) the model is. [[Source: What Grok 4.7 can do: benchmarks, strengths, and weaknesses... — Veruna AI](https://veruna.io/blog/chto-umeet-grok-4-7)] AI technology is now moving past the "knowing" phase and entering the "doing" phase.
