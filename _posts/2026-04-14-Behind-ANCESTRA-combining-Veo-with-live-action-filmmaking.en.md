The era when you can't distinguish between what is real and what is computer-generated in the scenes you watch in a theater is fast approaching. Beyond just creating flashy explosion scenes, artificial intelligence (AI) has now reached a stage where it paints visuals directly to match an actor's emotional arc and perfectly merges them with live-action footage. At the center of this is the short film **'ANCESTRA'**, created by Google DeepMind and legendary film director Darren Aronofsky. [Darren Aronofsky AI Studio Announces Google Pact, 'Ancestra' Project](https://variety.com/2025/digital/news/darren-aronofsky-ai-studio-primordial-soup-google-deepmind-1236403412/)

## Why is this important?

**Imagine this.** In the past, to film a mother crying in the middle of space, you would have had to build a multi-million dollar set or have hundreds of graphic designers work nights for months. But now, a director can simply tell the AI, "Keep the actor's sad expression, but change the background to look like the Milky Way."

Until now, AI-generated videos we've seen have often felt awkward or lacked consistency, like looking into a dream. But 'ANCESTRA' is different. This film goes beyond the buzz of "making a movie with AI" and proves how AI can harmoniously blend with a real actor's performance (live-action) in a professional filmmaking environment. This project was born through a partnership between world-renowned maestro Darren Aronofsky's AI-based studio 'Primordial Soup'—known for 'Black Swan', 'mother!', and more—and Google DeepMind. AI is no longer replacing human creativity; it has become a powerful assistant that realizes complex emotions and cosmic landscapes that directors only imagined on screen. ## Easy Understanding: How AI Films a Movie

The film 'ANCESTRA' was produced under the direction of Eliza McNitt. [A Behind-the-Scenes Look at ANCESTRA: Eliza McNitt's AI Film from...](https://www.vp-land.com/p/a-behind-the-scens-look-at-ancestra-eliza-mcnitt-s-ai-film-from-google-deepmind-and-darren-aronofsky) The core of this film is Google's state-of-the-art video generation model, **'Veo'**. [Behind “ANCESTRA:” combining Veo with live-action...](https://www.affasocial.com/posts/20282) What magic did Veo perform?

### 1. Personalization: Teaching the AI the Protagonist
**By analogy**, if a painter drew the protagonist's face differently every time, the audience would be confused, right? Google DeepMind added a new feature called 'Personalization' to Veo. This technology trains the AI on a specific actor's face and unique characteristics, allowing it to recognize them as the same person no matter what scene is generated. [Behind “ANCESTRA”: combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/ancestra-behind-the-scenes/) Much like assigning a dedicated makeup artist who accompanies an actor 24/7, the AI maintains a consistent appearance for the protagonist.

### 2. Precise Motion Matching: A Dance of Shadow and Light
The most difficult part of merging live-action footage with AI-generated video is 'harmony of movement.' When an actor slightly turns their head, the AI-generated background or lighting effects must move precisely according to that angle. Veo's precise motion matching technology identifies every muscle movement of the actor and transforms the AI video in real-time to match. [Behind “ANCESTRA”: combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/ancestra-behind-the-scenes/) **Simply put**, it's like a dance partner responding perfectly to even the smallest footwork changes of their counterpart.

### 3. Blending: Removing the Boundary Between Reality and Fantasy
'ANCESTRA' doesn't just put live-action and AI-generated parts together; it 'blends' them into one. [Behind “ANCESTRA”: combining Veo with live-action filmmaking](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/ancestra-behind-the-scenes/) Like pouring milk into coffee to make a smooth latte, it naturally melts surreal elements created by AI into vivid real-life footage to provide a unique visual experience. [Behind ANCESTRA: combining generative AI with live-action...](https://www.kifinity.com/post/behind-ancestra-combining-veo-with-live-action-filmmaking-deepmind-google-0f021f92) Thanks to this, the audience can immerse themselves in the story without having to worry about what is real and what is virtual.
