## Understanding easily: 'Conversational Editing' with AI

The core technology of this update is **Multi-turn editing**. Sounds a bit complicated? Think of it as 'a method of modifying an image little by little over several steps, just like having a conversation' [Nano Banana! Image editing in Gemini just got a major upgrade](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/product-updates/explore-get-answers/nano-banana-image-editing-in-gemini-just-got-a-major-upgrade/).

Imagine you are ordering at a sandwich shop:

1. **Step 1**: "One basic sandwich, please." (Overall image generation)
2. **Step 2**: "Please take out the cheese and add bacon." (Specific element modification)
3. **Step 3**: "Make the sauce a bit spicy." (Detailed style change)
4. **Step 4**: "Finally, write my name on the wrapper." (Detail addition)

During this process, the clerk doesn't throw the sandwich you ordered into the trash and start over. They precisely change or add only the parts you requested based on what has already been made. Gemini works the same way. It remembers the structure and colors of the already drawn image and sophisticatedly modifies or adds only the specific parts you request [Nano Banana! Image editing in Gemini just got a major upgrade](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/product-updates/explore-get-answers/nano-banana-image-editing-in-gemini-just-got-a-major-upgrade/).
