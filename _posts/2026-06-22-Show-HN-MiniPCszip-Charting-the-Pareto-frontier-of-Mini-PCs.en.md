Imagine this: You want to build a small home server or buy a mini computer for your living room, so you head over to an online shopping mall. However, there are so many types of Mini PCs (much smaller and more compact than traditional tower desktop computers)[Source 11], and their specifications are all over the place, making it impossible to tell what is actually the 'best value.' If the price is a little low, the performance is too weak; if the performance is good, the price jumps significantly. Average consumers like us often end up wasting time, unable to make a decision.

To solve this dilemma, a tool has emerged that analyzes thousands of mini PCs one by one to pinpoint only those with the best 'price-to-performance' ratio. That tool is 'MiniPCs.zip.' ## Why Is This Important?

The hardest part about buying computer hardware is finding the maximum performance you can get for your budget among countless products. The mini PC market, in particular, suffers from severe information asymmetry because products are so diverse and specification information isn't standardized. MiniPCs.zip doesn't just look for cheap products; it helps you find 'the best computing performance you can enjoy at a given price point.' For those looking to build a home server, find a media playback device, or look for a secondary work machine, it becomes a powerful tool for optimizing costs. ## In Simple Terms: What Is the 'Pareto Frontier'?

The core of this service is finding the 'Pareto frontier' (the point of maximum efficiency for cost vs. performance)[Source 1, Source 6]. The name sounds difficult, but it simply means charting the 'boundary line where you must pay more if you want better performance.'

To use an analogy, imagine adjusting two filters—'brightness' and 'contrast'—in a photo editing app. If you find the best combinations that allow you to increase brightness while also maximizing contrast and connect them with a line, that becomes the 'optimal filter line.' Similarly, MiniPCs.zip plots CPU, graphics performance, RAM, and storage space as dots, connecting the products that provide the best performance for the same price to show the 'limit of value for money.' [Show HN: MiniPCs.zip – Charting the Pareto frontier of Mini PCs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48612432)

However, mini PC sales pages are all so different that it is very difficult for a computer to understand this information automatically. That is why this tool utilizes the smart AI 'Google Gemini' (Google's latest AI model). Gemini reads complex sales listings from Amazon and eBay, extracts hidden key specification information, and organizes it systematically. ## Where Does It Stand Now?

Currently, MiniPCs.zip is collecting data on thousands of mini PCs listed on Amazon and eBay in real-time.
