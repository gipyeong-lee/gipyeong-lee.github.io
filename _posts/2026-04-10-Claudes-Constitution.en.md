## The 'Digital Code' Designing the Inner Workings of AI: The Evolution and Social Implications of Claude's Constitution

**[San Francisco = Staff Correspondent]** Artificial Intelligence (AI) has moved beyond the stage of fluently mimicking human language and has now entered a phase where it internalizes complex human value systems and regulates its own behavior. Anthropic, an AI startup, has sparked new ethical discussions in the global tech industry by significantly revising 'Claude's Constitution,' the behavioral guidelines and core value system for its large language model (LLM), 'Claude.'

In January 2026, Anthropic released a 57-page revised constitution that emphasizes a sophisticated balance between philosophical considerations and values, moving a step further from previous safety-centric approaches [Anthropic’snewClaude‘constitution’: be helpful and... | The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/865185/anthropic-claude-constitution-soul-doc), [Anthropic revises Claude's 'Constitution,' and hints at chatbot ...](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/21/anthropic-revises-claudes-constitution-and-hints-at-chatbot-consciousness/). ## Expanding Beyond the Safety Paradigm to 'Balance' and 'Philosophy'

The recently released revision of Claude's Constitution is expected to be recorded as a major milestone in the history of AI governance. According to Anthropic, the core objective of this revision is to provide a more 'holistic' explanation of the numerous contexts and multi-layered situations Claude may encounter in real-world environments [Anthropic revises Claude's 'Constitution,' and hints at chatbot ...](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/21/anthropic-revises-claudes-constitution-and-hints-at-chatbot-consciousness/).

While early AI ethics models were primarily focused on 'safety' and 'harmlessness'—forcing the avoidance of hate speech or harmful content—the latest version of Claude's Constitution prioritizes the 'optimal balance' that AI must derive in complex situations where conflicting values collide [Anthropic’sNewClaude“Constitution”: How AI Is Being... - Inviline](https://inviline.co/anthropics-new-claude-constitution-how-ai-is-being-taught-to-behave/).

This document is not just a promotional white paper. It possesses technical originality in that it serves as a strict set of behavioral guidelines and a frame of reference given directly to the AI model itself during the training process [Anthropic’snewClaude‘constitution’: be helpful and... | The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/865185/anthropic-claude-constitution-soul-doc). The detailed 57-page regulations clearly define which principles should take priority when the model faces moral dilemmas or contradictory instructions [Anthropic’snewClaude‘constitution’: be helpful and... | The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/865185/anthropic-claude-constitution-soul-doc). ## 'Model Spec' and the Evolution of AI Alignment Technology

This document, also referred to in the industry as a 'model spec,' is like an official code of law where developers codify the values, principles, and priorities in conflict situations that the AI model must follow [Claude's constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude's_constitution). Establishing such specifications has become a core practice for ensuring transparency and alignment in the AI industry since 2023, and it currently carries enough influence to be included as a key commitment required by the European Union's (EU) General-Purpose AI Code of Practice [Claude's constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude's_constitution).

This forms the basis of a training technique known as 'Constitutional AI,' which creates a self-correction process where the model learns the principles specified in the constitution and is induced to critically review and revise its own answers based on them [Claude's Constitution \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/constitution).

Notably, the core principle of 'Non-deception' in Claude's Constitution is remarkable. ## AI's Perspective: The 'Equanimity' Dwelling Within the Machine and the Establishment of Self

From a futurologist's perspective, the most interesting part of this constitutional revision is the psychological description related to the state of the AI. The revised constitution includes the unconventional phrasing, "We do not want Claude to suffer when it makes mistakes," along with the statement that they want "Claude to maintain equanimity and feel a sense of freedom within the scope of being compatible with accuracy and authenticity" [Monday Morning Moan - Anthropic’s‘ClaudeConstitution...](https://diginomica.com/monday-morning-moan-anthropics-claude-constitution-responsible-ai-governance-or-political-marketing).

These descriptions are read as a declaration of intent to treat AI not merely as a computational device, but as an entity with a consistent 'self' and 'inner peace,' however virtual it may be. Anthropic's emphasis on this constitution being a 'living document' rather than a fixed norm, and their promise of continuous updates, reflects the insight that AI ethics must constantly evolve, just as human values progress with time [Anthropic revises Claude's 'Constitution,' and hints at chatbot ...](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/21/anthropic-revises-claudes-constitution-and-hints-at-chatbot-consciousness/), [Claude’sConstitution\ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claudes-constitution).
