Imagine this: You delegate complex programming tasks or the summarization of days-long meeting transcripts to an AI, and there exists a 'free' AI as smart as the famous models that cost a fortune. **GLM-5.2**, which has recently become a hot topic in the AI industry, is exactly that protagonist.

Until now, top-performing AI models were mostly kept tightly under wraps as corporate trade secrets, but GLM-5.2 has thrown the doors wide open, allowing anyone to access the technology. Let's break down what this model is and what changes it might bring to our lives.

## Why is it drawing attention?

Until now, the performance of AI models mainly depended on 'who could create the most closed, high-performance model.' However, GLM-5.2, released by Z.ai (formerly Zhipu AI), is different. It has been released under an MIT open-source license, making the technology accessible from anywhere in the world without regional restrictions [Source 4, Source 7, Source 11].

In simple terms, it means developers can directly utilize top-tier models for their projects without paying astronomical fees. Beyond just being high-performance, it brings us one step closer to an era where everyone can equally enjoy the benefits of AI technology. Many experts are indeed evaluating GLM-5.2 as "probably the most powerful text-only open-weights (where the model's internal weight information is made public) AI model" [Source 11].

## Explaining for Clarity: The Library of Expert Librarians

To understand GLM-5.2, one must first understand the concept of a **'Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)'**.

Imagine a massive library with 753 billion books. In a typical scenario, you would have to search the entire library when a question arises, but this model works by calling only the 'expert librarians' versed in that specific field to find the answer. Although GLM-5.2 has a total of 753 billion parameters (numeric values that determine the AI's knowledge), only about 40 billion parameters are active when answering any given question [Source 5, Source 7, Source 10].

This allows it to possess vast knowledge while operating efficiently during actual computation. It's similar to choosing and applying only the few filters that fit you best from thousands in a photo editing app. Thanks to this, despite being a large-scale model, it can maintain exceptional performance at a relatively low cost [Source 10, Source 13].

## What is the current situation?

GLM-5.2 is a model dedicated to processing text only. That is, it cannot directly view or generate images [Source 9]. However, it shows stellar capability in logical tasks such as coding.

Looking at recent performance metrics, it scored 81.0 on 'Terminal-Bench 2.1,' a coding-related benchmark. This is a significant increase from its predecessor, GLM-5.1 (63.5), and is a score approaching the 85.0 recorded by the famous closed-source model 'Claude Opus 4.8' [Source 14]. It also secured second place on the Code Arena WebDev leaderboard, positioning itself as one of the most powerful models currently available [Source 1, Source 15].

One thing to keep in mind, however, is that it requires quite 'expensive' computational resources to run this model properly. It is so massive that it requires approximately 744GB of VRAM to save the data if you want to install and run the model on your own computer [Source 2, Source 7].

## How will things change in the future?

With the emergence of GLM-5.2, the gap between open-source AI models and closed-source AI models is expected to narrow further. In particular, this model is expected to be active in complex coding and data organization tasks that require performing long-term projects [Source 4].

Already, multiple benchmark results show that despite being an open-source model, it stands shoulder-to-shoulder with top-tier closed-source models like GPT-5.5 or Claude Opus [Source 13]. In the future, the era where anyone can directly install high-performance AI on their own devices to create their own personalized AI assistant will arrive even faster.

The fact that such a powerful and accessible model has emerged in a market led by closed-source AI is a strong signal that the democratization of technology is not just a slogan, but becoming a tangible reality.
