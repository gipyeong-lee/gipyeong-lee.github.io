## Easy to Understand: What Is 'Perplexity'?

'Perplexity' covered in this article is largely divided into two meanings. One is the baffling performance measurement metric of AI models, and the other is the name of the innovative AI search service we are familiar with. To put it simply, perplexity acts as a bridge connecting the world of technology and everyday convenience. By analogy, one side has a strict examiner sitting, and the other side has a knowledgeable butler ready to kindly explain everything in the world.

### 1. Perplexity as a Scientific Metric (Cross-Entropy Loss)
Simply put, perplexity is a test score measuring **"how confused the AI is."**
* **Shall we make an analogy?** It is like observing how confident a student is when answering correctly during an exam. When an AI reads a sentence and predicts the next word, a lower perplexity score means the AI is clearly hitting the right answer, whereas a higher score means it is confused and coming up with bizarre words.
* In November 2026, OpenAI proposed that this perplexity (or Cross-Entropy Loss) be used as a standard comparison metric commonly used by all AI model developers `[Perplexity and Intelligence Research, Nov 22, 2026](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/)`. Through this, they aim to transparently share pre-training progress and establish fair safety standards `[Perplexity and Intelligence Research, Nov 22, 2026](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/)`.

### 2. Perplexity as a Real-Time Answer Engine (Perplexity AI)
Perplexity, an AI service of the same name, is like **"a smart library librarian who searches the global internet in real time when you ask questions and answers readily with accurate sources."**
* While conventional AI relied solely on memories learned in the past, Perplexity combines real-time web search to provide the latest and most accurate answers `[Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/)`.

The current AI ecosystem is rapidly evolving along two tracks: technological standardization discussions and service expansion in real life. ### OpenAI's Transparency Proposal
On November 22, 2026, OpenAI officially argued via its blog that perplexity (cross-entropy loss) should be used as a comparison metric among model developers and the basis for shared safety standards `[Perplexity and Intelligence Research, Nov 22, 2026](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/)`. As a first step, OpenAI stated that it would further increase transparency regarding its own pre-training progress `[Perplexity and Intelligence Research, Nov 22, 2026](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/)`.
