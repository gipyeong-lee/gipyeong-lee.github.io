Imagine this: you walk into your office in the morning and turn on your computer, but instead of complex business software, an AI speaks to you first: "I've prepared the three reports you need to handle today. Which one would you like to check first?" This kind of change is moving beyond mere expectation and becoming reality. Arthur Mensch, CEO of Paris-based AI startup Mistral AI, says that AI is not just a new feature, but is actually changing the structure of corporate business itself.

### Why is this important?

We are currently transitioning from the 'era of software' to the 'era of AI.' CEO Mensch predicted that more than 50% of the software currently used by companies could be replaced by AI systems ([Source: CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/18/ai-mistral-software-switch-ceo-india-ai-impact-summit.html), [Source: CEO North America](https://ceo-na.com/news/mistral-ceo-forecasts-mass-shift-from-software-to-ai/)).

For companies, this is a massive shift. Previously, the method was to purchase specific software and train employees to use it, but now the very way of working is changing to one where AI analyzes data and assists in decision-making on its own. Mistral AI is already providing customized AI tailored to real-world field requirements for major corporations like ASML, Stellantis, and Veolia, such as identifying production defects or precisely controlling robotic arms ([Source: Usearch](https://usearch.com/company/mistral-ai/overview)).

### In simple terms: AI is the 'electricity' of the modern age

When CEO Mensch explains why AI is important in our time, he brings up a very familiar term: 'electricity' ([Source: WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/mistral-is-in-the-right-place-at-the-right-time/)).

To put it in perspective, a hundred years ago, as electricity spread, factories that were centered on manual labor transformed into mass-production systems centered on machinery. AI today is just like the electricity of that time. Wherever there is data, AI enters to optimize processes and maximize productivity.

What Mistral AI particularly emphasizes is 'controllability.' It means they intend to build AI that governments or companies can control themselves, deploy directly where they want, and operate independently, rather than unconditionally borrowing AI created by U.S. Big Tech companies ([Source: Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/europe-ai-startup-mistral-edge-over-silicon-valley-not-american-2026-1), [Source: Computer Weekly](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366625256/How-Mistral-is-driving-growth-through-open-source-and-enterprise-AI)). It's like being able to manage your own electricity at home and turn it on or off whenever you want.

### Current Status: The Stature of Mistral AI

Founded in Paris, France, in 2023, Mistral AI has grown rapidly, reaching a valuation of over $14 billion as of 2025, establishing itself as a representative AI company in Europe ([Source: Wikipedia - Mistral AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mistral_AI)).

They don't just compete on model performance; they actively support open-source systems ([Source: Cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/mistral-ceo-deepseeks-win-open-source/)). CEO Mensch believes that the open-source approach solves bottlenecks in the technical development process. It is a philosophy that technology that anyone can use develops transparently and rapidly.

### What happens next?

CEO Mensch points out that because the AI market is so vast, having specific countries or companies monopolize it could actually cause geopolitical instability ([Source: WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/mistral-is-in-the-right-place-at-the-right-time/)).

In the future, an era of 'AI democratization' will arrive where companies can directly select and build various AI models tailored to their own security situations and budgets, just as we choose smartphone apps today. Metaphorically speaking, just as we choose our electricity providers, companies will be in a proactive position to choose and manage their own 'AI partners.'

### The AI's Perspective

"The value of technology is ultimately determined by how many people can handle it proactively. The 'Controllable AI' that Mistral AI pursues is highly encouraging in that it helps field practitioners move beyond technological dependency and borrow the power of AI—like electricity—to help their businesses bloom on their own terms."
