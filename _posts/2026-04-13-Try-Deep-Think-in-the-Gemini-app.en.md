## Easy Understanding: The Core Principles of Deep Think

To understand how Deep Think differs from existing AI, you only need to remember two core concepts.

### 1. Parallel Thinking
A simple analogy for this is a **'brainstorming meeting.'** While existing AI created answers one sentence at a time by itself, Deep Think creates numerous 'selves' in its mind the moment it receives a question. Each alter ego tries different solutions simultaneously. [Google’s new Deep Think feature is here — what it does and why it might not stay ultra-exclusive for long](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/googles-new-deep-think-feature-is-here-what-it-does-and-why-it-might-not-stay-ultra-exclusive-for-long)

It's like watching 10 people run down different paths in a complex maze at the same time to see who finds the exit first and most accurately. In this process, the AI realizes which paths are dead ends and repeats the process of coming back and finding other ways. [Summary: Try Deep Think in the Gemini app | StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/artificial-intelligence/2025/summary-try-deep-think-in-the-gemini-app)

### 2. Inference Time Extension
While existing AI gave answers in 0.5 seconds as soon as it received a question, Deep Think takes enough 'thinking time' before giving an answer. In technical terms, this is called **'Inference Time,'** and simply put, it is deliberately increasing the physical time the AI spends calculating to derive an answer. [Summary: Try Deep Think in the Gemini app | StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/artificial-intelligence/2025/summary-try-deep-think-in-the-gemini-app)

Shall we look at an example?
*   **General AI**: "The answer to this problem is A." (Immediate answer)
*   **Deep Think AI**: "Hmm... I thought it was A at first, but after re-examining it, there's a variable B. Considering B, C might be a better answer. I finally recommend C." (Answer after several seconds of deliberation)

Because it goes through this process of revising and refining its own ideas, much more accurate and creative results can be produced. [Summary: Try Deep Think in the Gemini app | StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/artificial-intelligence/2025/summary-try-deep-think-in-the-gemini-app)
