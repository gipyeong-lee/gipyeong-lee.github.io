## Easy Understanding: How does ChatGPT work?

ChatGPT is a 'Generative AI' created by OpenAI. [ChatGPT - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT) Technically, it is based on a complex technology called 'Transformer' (an AI structure that identifies relationships between words in a sentence), but it is much easier to understand if we explain it through analogies from our daily lives. [ChatGPT - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT)

### 1. The 'Genius Librarian' who swallowed an entire massive library
Imagine ChatGPT as a **'genius librarian'** who has memorized a huge library containing trillions of books. When we ask a question, this librarian doesn't just say, "That information is on the 5th shelf on the 3rd floor." He quickly scans the contents of countless books, grasps the intent of our question, and 'writes' a perfectly suited answer from scratch. Because it creates new results rather than just finding information, it is called 'generative.' [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/)

### 2. Clarity like a perfectly fitted pair of glasses
The latest model, **GPT-5**, is like this librarian getting much more powerful and clearer magnifying glasses. Previous models occasionally said nonsensical things (hallucinations, where the AI tells plausible lies) or tried too hard to please the user (sycophancy, where the AI agrees with a wrong answer to cater to the user). However, GPT-5 has drastically reduced these problems. [Introducing GPT‑5 - OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5/) Just as a person with blurry vision sees the world clearly after putting on the right glasses, it now provides much more accurate and logical answers. [Introducing GPT‑5 - OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5/)

### 3. A high-speed word puzzle game
Technically, ChatGPT communicates by predicting the 'most appropriate word' to follow the sentence we've given. Like fitting puzzle pieces together, it selects the next word that best matches the preceding ones in less than 0.1 seconds. These pieces come together to form perfect sentences, sometimes becoming a touching poem, and other times becoming complex computer programming code. [ChatGPT - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT)
