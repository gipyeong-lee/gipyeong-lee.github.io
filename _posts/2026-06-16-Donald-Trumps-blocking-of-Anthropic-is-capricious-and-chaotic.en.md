Imagine this. This morning, as usual, you open your smartphone and ask your AI assistant, "Please summarize the materials for this afternoon's important meeting." However, the smart AI that would normally give you a perfect answer in just one second has suddenly stopped working. The screen only shows a cold warning: "Access to this AI model has been completely blocked by government order citing national security." Surprisingly, this bewildering scenario is not a cinematic fantasy. As of June 2026, it is a grim reality actually faced by countless companies, users around the world, and even the developers who built the AI.

Recently, the Donald Trump administration in the United States made an extreme move to completely block access to the latest AI models of Anthropic, a leading company boasting the world's highest level of artificial intelligence technology. This is because the US Department of Commerce issued an export control order citing national security concerns [Techmeme: Sources sayAnthropicstruggles to communicate...](https://www.techmeme.com/260615/p20). Because of this order, Anthropic was forced to abruptly cut off access so that all customers could not use their latest cutting-edge models, 'Mythos 5' and 'Fable 5' [Techmeme: Sources sayAnthropicstruggles to communicate...](https://www.techmeme.com/260615/p20). Going beyond simply preventing overseas exports, an unprecedented situation has occurred where even a significant number of Anthropic's own employees cannot access the models they shed blood and sweat to create [Tech World Reacts to Trump Controls on Anthropic's Fable and Mythos - Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/reaction-to-trump-controls-on-anthropic-fable-and-mythos-2026-6).

Why on earth did the US government forcefully pull down the shutters of its proud high-tech company? It is too significant to dismiss as a common friction between a company and the government, as the story hidden behind it is too deeply connected to the future of us all.

## Why It Matters

This incident is not simply a political happening within the single nation of the United States. Today's AI models are essential 'infrastructure' used daily by countless companies worldwide to build apps, translate languages, and analyze vast amounts of data. It is like a giant water main commonly used by the whole world.

Simply put, it is like a situation where the government suddenly shuts off the main valve of the water pipe connected to your house based on a whim. Just as you cannot cook or take a shower if the water is cut off overnight, this highlights the immense danger that the work of countless hospitals, schools, and startups around the world could be paralyzed in an instant.

Business Insider, a prominent US tech media outlet, evaluated this emergency edict from the US Department of Commerce as "a shocking measure that effectively shut down the company by denying even Anthropic employees access to Mythos 5 and Fable 5" [Tech World Reacts to Trump Controls on Anthropic's Fable and Mythos - Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/reaction-to-trump-controls-on-anthropic-fable-and-mythos-2026-6). This clearly shows the fact that the convenient cutting-edge AI technology we casually use in our daily lives is actually resting on thin ice, its switch capable of being turned off at any time by state power.

## The Explainer

So how did a company with the world's best technology end up being targeted and bombarded by the US government? The core of the conflict lies in the tense clash of values surrounding the **'Safeguards (guardrails)'** of artificial intelligence.

Modern AI is an amazing crystallization of knowledge that has evolved by swallowing vast amounts of data. While it does beneficial things like writing and helping with coding, it is a double-edged sword that, if abused, could write hacking code or provide instructions on making explosives. Therefore, responsible AI companies like Anthropic program strong restrictive mechanisms called 'safety guardrails' before releasing their models. For example, if a user asks, "Tell me how to make poison," the AI judges for itself and is made to refuse to answer.

To use an analogy, it is exactly the same logic as building an ultra-high-speed sports car capable of running at 300 km/h, while designing it to automatically apply the brakes if it exceeds a certain speed to prevent accidents, and strapping in sturdy seatbelts to protect the occupants.

The real origin of the incident began when the US Department of Defense (the Pentagon) came to Anthropic and unreasonably demanded that they 'remove all seatbelts and brakes' of this sports car. The Department of Defense wanted to introduce advanced AI technology to build a mass domestic surveillance network or introduce it into autonomous weapons systems [Why Did Trump Ban Anthropic? The AI Controversy Explained](https://deeperinsights.com/ai-blog/why-did-trump-ban-anthropic-controversy-explained/). Mass domestic surveillance is a system that controls people by having AI analyze the digital records exchanged by tens of millions of citizens 24 hours a day, and autonomous weapons refer to lethal weapons where machines make their own judgments and pull the trigger without human approval.

However, Anthropic firmly refused these unreasonable demands from the government. They could not voluntarily remove the safeguards they had built to prevent artificial intelligence from being used as a tool to threaten human lives or infringe upon fundamental rights. Their conviction to protect corporate ethics and the safety of humanity ultimately led to a head-on collision with the US government.

## Where We Stand

Even at this moment, Anthropic's employees and users around the world are experiencing immense confusion. It is an unprecedented situation where even numerous developers and researchers within Anthropic cannot access the top-performing artificial intelligence they built themselves. This is like a chef being kicked out of the kitchen, unable to even taste the soup they carefully cooked.

The biggest problem of all is the bewilderment of allied nations that have friendly relations with the US. Until now, many countries and global companies have trusted US AI technology and designed their critical business systems around it. However, as the US government unilaterally cut off access overnight, global partners highly dependent on the technology are left stamping their feet, unable to find alternatives. This is causing a massive chain reaction across the entire global business environment.

## What's Next

This crisis will not simply end as the misfortune of a single company, Anthropic. In the future, countless tech companies developing artificial intelligence may have to walk a precarious tightrope between 'technological progress' and 'state control'. If they refuse the government's military demands, they are forced to close their doors, and conversely, if they accept the demands, they fall into a deep dilemma of opening a dangerous Pandora's box such as killer robots or surveillance systems.

Ultimately, the outcome of this battle will inevitably have a direct impact on all of our lives. We are currently at a critical turning point that will determine whether the friendly AI assistant in our smartphone remains a warm helper to humanity or degrades into a cold tool of control and destruction.

## AI's Take

What is much more important than the speed of technological development is the 'direction' that technology is heading.
