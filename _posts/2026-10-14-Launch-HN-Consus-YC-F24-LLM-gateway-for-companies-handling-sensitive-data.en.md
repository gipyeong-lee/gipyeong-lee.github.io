Imagine you are a key researcher in the aerospace sector. You want to use the latest artificial intelligence (AI) models to precisely analyze complex design data, but the information you handle is highly sensitive and directly tied to national security. Your company's security regulations are extremely strict, and adopting AI once requires overcoming massive hurdles such as security reviews and infrastructure design. Eventually, you give up on adopting AI and begin analyzing the data manually, which is time-consuming.

A groundbreaking technology has recently emerged for such companies. It is **Consus**, which was selected for the F24 batch of Y Combinator, the world's largest startup accelerator. [Source 16](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/MJa-consus-the-github-for-government-documents), [Source 18](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/consus) This technology acts as a solid bridge for companies that have been hesitant to use the latest AI due to security concerns.

## Why is this important?

While the pace of advancement in generative AI is dazzling, it is very difficult to introduce it into fields where security is critical, such as aerospace or defense. [Source 15](https://gateway.consus.io/overview), [Source 17](https://consus.io/) This is because "compliance"—perfect assurance of where data is stored and who can access it—must be guaranteed, going beyond simply calling an AI model.

Most companies pour enormous amounts of time and money into building such security systems from scratch. However, by leveraging Consus, companies can integrate the latest AI models into processing sensitive data, such as those subject to ITAR (International Traffic in Arms Regulations) or CUI (Controlled Unclassified Information), without complex infrastructure engineering or tedious paperwork. [Source 13](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/MJa-consus-the-github-for-government-documents), [Source 15](https://gateway.consus.io/overview), [Source 16](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/MJa-consus-the-github-for-government-documents) This lowers the barrier to technology adoption and directly enhances a company's competitiveness.

## Simplified Understanding

To put it simply, Consus is like a **"dedicated VIP lane that has perfectly cleared the security checkpoint."**

If a standard AI model is like a general airport gate, companies handling security-critical data must pass through security checkpoints dozens of times to access the model. In this scenario, when a company sends a request to use an AI model, the Consus Gateway routes the request to a highly safe and verified "government-grade secure cloud environment" instead. [Source 13](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/MJa-consus-the-github-for-government-documents), [Source 14](https://gateway.consus.io/overview)

This gateway is based on a robust environment certified with FedRAMP High (the highest level of US federal government cloud security certification) or DoD (US Department of Defense) IL5. [Source 14](https://gateway.consus.io/overview) Companies no longer need to go through complex security certification processes themselves; they only need to connect to this "secure link" called Consus to immediately borrow the powerful intelligence of the latest AI.

## Where is it being used?

Consus is currently being actively utilized in sectors where data security is the top priority, such as aerospace, defense, and energy industries. [Source 15](https://gateway.consus.io/overview), [Source 17](https://consus.io/) Previously, such companies had to spend months overhauling their internal IT systems or ensuring security compliance to use the latest generative AI models. However, because Consus is provided in the form of an API (Application Programming Interface)—a gateway that connects different software—that developers can immediately apply to real-world tasks, it directly and instantly improves work efficiency. [Source 13](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/MJa-consus-the-github-for-government-documents), [Source 16](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/MJa-consus-the-github-for-government-documents)

## What lies ahead?

The integration of data security and artificial intelligence will expand into all industrial sectors in the future. As there become more "compliance-focused AI gateways" like Consus, more companies that were hesitant to adopt AI due to security concerns will be able to ride the wave of innovation. In particular, the speed of AI utilization is expected to accelerate even further in fields where security is essential, such as space development and energy infrastructure management.
