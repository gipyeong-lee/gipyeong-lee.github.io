Imagine this: You wake up in the morning and tell your AI assistant, "Organize this afternoon's meeting materials and email them to the team members." A previous AI might have only summarized the content, but today's AI directly opens files, searches for information, opens the email program, and completes the transmission. We call this type of AI, which plans and uses tools on its own, 'Agentic AI' (AI that judges for itself, uses tools, and performs tasks).

However, as these agentic AIs become smarter, it is the 'brains' of the computers that struggle in the background. Recently, SpaceXAI ([SpaceXAI](https://x.ai/)) announced that it would accelerate this era of agentic AI by adopting NVIDIA's new CPU, 'Vera'. What exactly is this CPU that has caught the attention of the AI industry?

### Why is this important?

Until now, AI technology has focused mainly on increasing the intelligence of the 'model itself.' However, for agentic AI, the process of translating the model's answers into execution in the real world is essential. During this process, complex tasks that must be handled by the CPU (Central Processing Unit, the computer's brain) such as handling tools, executing code, and processing data increase rapidly.

Simply put, if an AI model is a talented chef, the CPU is an assistant chef in charge of preparing ingredients and tools in the kitchen. If the CPU cannot handle these tasks in time, those expensive GPUs (Graphics Processing Units, core components for advanced calculations) will sit idly waiting for commands. The adoption of the Vera CPU will make agentic AI run as smoothly as clockwork, dramatically increasing the response speed and work processing capability of AI experienced by users.

### It's like this analogy

Imagine you have hired a 'Chef AI' at a high-end hotel. No matter how brilliant a recipe the chef knows, if the kitchen tools cannot prepare the ingredients in time, the meal will inevitably be delayed.

Existing standard CPUs are like 'household kitchen appliances' that couldn't keep up with the processing speed of a professional Chef AI. On the other hand, the Vera CPU was designed from the ground up as a 'high-speed, large-scale restaurant-dedicated kitchen appliance.' [NVIDIA Vera is the first CPU specifically crafted for AI agents](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/spacexai-adopts-nvidia-vera-cpu-to-accelerate-agentic-ai-at-massive-scale).

In other words, when an AI model commands, "Write an email!", the Vera CPU executes code and processes data immediately without waiting, sending the email. In this process, it has become possible to be [twice as efficient and produce results 50% faster than traditional CPUs](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/janno-koger-27b424107_if-ai-data-power-nemotron-coaliton-vera-activity-7441189579472179200--8L7).

### Current Situation

Currently, AI leaders around the world including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Oracle, as well as SpaceXAI, have received and begun adopting this Vera CPU ([NVIDIA Vera CPU Shipping Begins](https://parameter.io/nvidia-nvda-begins-shipping-vera-cpus-to-ai-industry-leaders/)). [The Vera CPU is optimized for handling CPU-intensive tasks where AI agents use tools, write code, and analyze data](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/spacexai-uses-nvidia-vera-cpu-to-accelerate-agentic-ai).

SpaceXAI already has plans to utilize this processor for their next-generation AI workloads and is expected to play a major role in [solving highly technical challenges such as AI deployment in orbit](https://blockchain.news/news/nvidia-vera-cpu-agentic-ai-efficiency). It is the beginning of a movement to solve more complex real-world problems, going beyond simple chatbots.

### What will happen in the future?

In the era of Agentic AI, it is not enough for the model to simply be smart. How organically the model and the infrastructure are connected will be the key. [In the entire process of building and executing agentic systems, the CPU is on the critical path that determines response speed and learning efficiency](https://wpnews.pro/news/ai-innovators-adopt-nvidia-vera-why-max-single-threaded-cpu-at-scale-matters).

Now, when you give tasks to AI, you will experience it being handled without a hitch, just like a real assistant working beside you, much more frequently. The emergence of dedicated processors like the Vera CPU is bringing the future of 'true AI automation' we imagine one step closer.

## MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Perspective

The development of Agentic AI is now demanding a reorganization of hardware beyond software. We are entering an era where the 'efficiency of the infrastructure,' which connects that intelligence to real-world tools, is just as important as the model's intelligence, creating gaps in AI technology. In the end, the completeness of AI depends on how well the smart brain (model) and the fast and accurate hands and feet (CPU/GPU) cooperate.
