Imagine this: You wake up in the morning, turn on your smartphone, and say to your AI, "From the walking video I took of our dog yesterday, select only the running parts and make it into a 15-second short clip with background music." A moment later, a video with all the clips you wanted, connected naturally, is ready. Video editing, once the exclusive domain of experts, is set to become as easy as chatting with a friend.

Google has officially released **'Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash,'** a new AI model with vastly improved video generation and editing performance [Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash: 15-second video and lower pricing](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/). This model heralds a new era where you can create high-quality videos through natural communication with AI, without the need for complex editing tools.

### Why is this important?

It means that everyday video creation becomes something anyone can do. Until now, every time you wanted to create a product promotional video, a short advertisement, or a vlog of your daily life, you had to learn professional software or spend a long time editing. To use an analogy, if in the past you had to manually typeset every character to write, now you have an assistant by your side who can take dictation for you.

Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is incredibly useful for businesses and individuals creating routine content, such as product videos, virtual fittings, or advertisements [Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash now GA: cost-effective video - Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/gemini-omni-1-2-flash-generally-available). The key is that it is faster, more cost-efficient, and above all, enables anyone to naturally modify and refine video content.

### Understanding it simply: Dialogue between AI and video

In simple terms, Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is like a 'genius video production assistant.' According to Google's model card, this model combines the intelligence of Gemini with generative media models to demonstrate a new level of understanding in video generation and editing [Gemini Omni Flash - Model Card — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-omni-1-2-flash/).

When a user gives a command in natural language, the model generates or modifies video based on text, image, or audio information. If you say, "Make it a little brighter," or "Cut the middle scene shorter," the AI immediately reflects those changes. This creates an environment where you can modify the flow of a video in detail, as if you were conversing, rather than just getting a final product.

### The current situation: What has changed?

Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash replaces the existing 'Veo 3.1' model, positioning itself as Google's flagship video generation model [Gemini Omni – Create & edit videos as easy as having a conversation](https://gemini.google/us/overview/video-generation/?hl=en).

Key features include:
* **Conversational editing:** Provides functionality to modify video content through multiple turns of conversation [Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash: 15-second video and lower pricing](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/).
* **Improved consistency:** Follows input image or text data more precisely and maintains video consistency reliably even when generating multiple scenes [Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash now GA: cost-effective video - Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/gemini-omni-1-2-flash-generally-available).
* **Reduced costs:** The generation cost is about 30% lower than the previous version, allowing for creative activities without financial burden [Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash GA: $0.07/Sec, 15s Video [2026]](https://shattered.io/gemini-omni-1-2-flash-ga-15-second-video-0-07-per-second-2026/).

### What is possible now?

The boundary between video generation and editing is fading ever faster. While 15-second videos are currently the primary focus, as technology advances, more precise control and editing features will allow creators to realize their ideas in video form even faster [Google Debuts Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash With Longer AI Clips ...](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/330142/20261106/google-debuts-gemini-omni-12-flash-longer-ai-clips-better-editing-fewer-errors.htm). In particular, turning everyday records into artistic videos or creating immediate advertising effects with simple ideas is expected to become an everyday activity for individuals as well [Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash can help you make 15-second AI videos from images and prompts at lower costs](https://www.digit.in/news/general/gemini-omni-1-2-flash-can-help-you-make-15-second-ai-videos-from-images-and-prompts-at-lower-costs.html).
