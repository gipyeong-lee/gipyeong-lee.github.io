## "I asked the AI a question, but there's no answer"

Imagine this: You ask your go-to AI assistant, 'Claude,' to organize meeting materials first thing in the morning to handle important tasks. But the AI, which usually finishes in the blink of an eye, just shows a spinning loading icon or repeatedly spits out an unknown error message.

Recently, many Claude users had this bewildering experience. As people wondered if it was just an internet connection issue or if their computer had broken down, Anthropic announced through their status page that this phenomenon was a system-wide problem. [Source: ClaudeStatus](https://status.claude.com/)

## Why does this matter?

We live in an era where we rely on AI for many parts of our daily lives. An AI suddenly stopping is, metaphorically, similar to the navigation system that guides you every morning suddenly going dead. For those who use AI for work in particular, a service outage means more than just being "a little inconvenienced"—it signifies a situation where the entire work process becomes paralyzed. [Source: ClaudeDown:MultipleModelsThrowElevatedErrorRates](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/claude-multiple-models-disrupted-service/)

This incident reminds us once again that even if AI possesses high intelligence, it is ultimately a 'digital tool' running on physical servers and complex infrastructure, just like other web services we use. It works on the same principle as a complex, state-of-the-art car that can stop if even a small defect occurs in the engine or software.

## Easy to understand: What is a 'model'?

Many of you might be wondering, "What is a model?" upon hearing that Claude suddenly caused errors across multiple models. Simply put, think of a model as an 'AI brain that has undergone specific training.' To use an analogy, the restaurant called Claude has chefs (Mythos 5.1, Fable 5.1, Opus 5, etc.) with different areas of expertise.

This incident was like a problem in the central management system of the restaurant's kitchen, making it impossible for all the chefs to perform at their best at once. [Source: ClaudeErrorsAcross ManyModels: What To Do Now](https://www.qwe.edu.pl/tutorial/claude-elevated-errors-many-models-resolved/) Some users experienced symptoms where responses became very slow or they were unable to answer at all. [Source: IsClaudedown? Many users reported issues while accessing the AI...](https://www.digit.in/news/general/is-claude-down-many-users-reported-issues-while-accessing-the-ai-assistant.html)

## Current status: It is already resolved

Anthropic officially named this incident 'Elevated errors for multiple models.' Anthropic began their investigation at 5:06 AM (UTC) and identified the cause of the errors occurring in Claude Mythos 5, Fable 5, and Opus 5 models within an hour. [Source: ClaudeKeeps Going Down, and Anthropic's OwnStatusPage Says So](https://dev.to/theaidownside/claude-keeps-going-down-and-anthropics-own-status-page-says-so-29mc)

Fortunately, this problem is now fully resolved. [Source: ClaudeStatus](https://status.claude.com/) It affected not only the claude.ai website but also various Anthropic services overall, such as the API and Claude Code used by developers, but everything is currently operating normally. [Source: Claudedown, or are you capped until the reset?](https://stacksheriff.com/status/claude/)

## What happens next?

Experts advise that such disruptions may occur occasionally in the future. If the AI acts strangely next time, what should you do?

1. **It's not your fault:** There is no need to panic and blame your prompting style or computer, or try to edit your code. It is highly likely to be a system-wide issue. [Source: ClaudeErrorsAcross ManyModels: What To Do Now](https://www.qwe.edu.pl/tutorial/claude-elevated-errors-many-models-resolved/)
2. **Check the status page:** Bookmark Anthropic's status page (status.claude.com) and check there first to see if an incident has been reported. [Source: ClaudeErrorsAcross ManyModels: What To Do Now](https://www.qwe.edu.pl/tutorial/claude-elevated-errors-many-models-resolved/)
3. **Use a different model:** Switching to another model within the service or waiting for a while is the wisest response. [Source: ClaudeErrorsAcross ManyModels: What To Do Now](https://www.qwe.edu.pl/tutorial/claude-elevated-errors-many-models-resolved/)
