In the past, creating such a video would have required professional filming equipment, a trained cat, and months of complex editing. For most people, it was closer to an 'impossible dream.' However, we have now entered an era where you can bring this imagination into reality just by typing a few sentences.

In 2025, tech giants like Google and Adobe unveiled a new generation of 'Generative Media Models'—AI structures that independently create images, videos, and music when text is entered. ## Understanding Simply: Google's New Gifts, 'Imagen 4' and 'Veo 3'

Google recently captured the world's attention by unveiling a slew of next-generation media models at the 'Google I/O 2025' event. ### 1. Imagen 4: The Painter Who Reads Your Mind
'Imagen 4' is like a skilled painter who seems to see right through your mind. It understands user intent much more sophisticatedly than previous models, creating high-quality images that are indistinguishable from real life. ### 2. Veo 3: Breathing Life of 'Sound' into Video
An even more amazing evolution appears in the video generation model 'Veo 3.' Compared to its predecessor, Veo 2, the most decisive difference is the 'Synchronized Audio' feature—a technology where sound is generated in sync with the movements in the video. [Fuel your creativity with new generative media models and tools](https://aipulselab.tech/news/fuel-your-creativity-with-new-generative-media-models-and-tools-659f10)

Imagine again: an AI creates a video of a chef rhythmically chopping on a cutting board, and the 'tak-tak' sound of the ingredients being sliced perfectly matches the movement of the knife on the screen without a 0.1-second error. By making this possible, Veo 3 creates truly 'living video' where vision and hearing are perfectly combined. ### 3. Flow: Your Personal Film Director
Google also introduced a new tool called 'Flow.' This is an integrated AI-powered tool encompassing image, video, and audio generation, helping users control the flow of a video very finely, just like a professional filmmaker.
