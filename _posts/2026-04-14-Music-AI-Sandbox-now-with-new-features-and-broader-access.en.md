Imagine this. Have you ever had a brilliant melody pop into your head, only to let it fade away because you didn't know how to play an instrument or use complex composition software? Or perhaps you're a professional composer who loves the overall vibe of a track but spent all night struggling just to slightly tweak the sound of a specific drum part?

Now, Google's artificial intelligence is aiming to become a magical tool that solves these problems. Google DeepMind has announced a major update to **'Music AI Sandbox,'** a tool designed to help musicians and producers collaborate with AI to create studio-quality music. [Music AI Sandbox, now with new features and broader access](https://deepmind.google/blog/music-ai-sandbox-now-with-new-features-and-broader-access/)

This news represents more than just the release of a new technology. It is a significant milestone that demonstrates how AI can 'assist' an artist's creativity and revolutionizes the way we create music.

## Why is this important?

Until now, music generation AI has largely been a one-way tool where you'd enter a prompt like "create sad jazz music" and it would spit out a finished track. However, the updated Music AI Sandbox has become much more of a sophisticated 'editing tool' that allows artists to directly touch up and refine the details of a song. [Music AI Sandbox, now with new features and broader access](https://deepmind.google/blog/music-ai-sandbox-now-with-new-features-and-broader-access/)

Recently, many music experts have been incorporating generative AI (**Generative AI**, an AI that creates new content such as text, images, and music by learning from existing data) into their creative processes. [Music AI Sandbox, now with new features and broader access](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ai-topics_music-ai-sandbox-now-with-new-features-and-activity-7321322349180399617-CDAf) This isn't about making AI do everything. It's an attempt to explore new ways of composing or sound design possibilities that were previously only imaginable. [Music AI Sandbox, now with new features and broader access](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ai-topics_music-ai-sandbox-now-with-new-features-and-activity-7321322349180399617-CDAf)

**To use an analogy,** it's not about AI taking the artist's place, but rather giving the artist a highly capable and obedient 'genius assistant.' With a skilled assistant, artists can focus on the essence of trying new styles and realizing their inspiration instead of getting bogged down in tedious technical work.

Google DeepMind has newly equipped Music AI Sandbox with 'Lyria 2,' a much more advanced music generation model than its predecessor.
