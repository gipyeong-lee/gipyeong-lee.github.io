Imagine this: your team gathers in a conference room, jotting down ideas on a whiteboard, while simultaneously editing data tables on laptops next to you, as an AI assistant summarizes the meeting. Now, this scene is not just a story from a movie—it is possible right inside the AI assistant you use every day, 'Claude'.

On November 10, 2026, Anthropic announced three new tools that will dramatically increase users' work efficiency. Moving beyond simply answering questions, Claude can now participate in real-time collaboration like a member of your team [Source 3, Source 9].

## Why does this matter?

Until now, we had to repeatedly ask AI something, copy the answer, and move it to other tools (Excel, whiteboard tools, etc.). But now, the AI tool itself has entered our workspace.

Simply put, if the AI was the chef until now, it has now become a colleague sitting right at our table, cooking with us. This change means 'seamless collaboration.' There is no need to keep multiple windows open and toggle back and forth to visualize data or organize ideas with team members. Since the workflow remains uninterrupted, we can focus solely on more important 'decision-making' while Claude organizes materials in the background during complex report creation or meetings [Source 5].

## Easy to understand: The three new musketeers of tools

The tools announced this time are like reliable team members, each with their own area of expertise.

1. **Claude Whiteboards**:
   To use a metaphor, it is a 'digital idea wall.' It is a shared board where team members can brainstorm in real-time with Claude. Just like sticking Post-it notes on a whiteboard, it is very useful for freely expanding ideas [Source 1, Source 2].

2. **Claude Sheets**:
   An 'intelligent spreadsheet' that Claude creates and edits directly. When we describe data in words, Claude automatically organizes the table and adjusts the figures. The key is that you can create tables comfortably as if you were talking to Claude, without needing to know Excel functions well [Source 1, Source 10].

3. **Claude Data**:
   Previously known as 'Claude Dashboards,' this tool has now transitioned to an official service. It tells interesting stories based on data and shares them with the team in real-time, complete with visualizations. When it is difficult to find the meaning hidden within data, Claude Data acts as a guide [Source 1, Source 3, Source 11].

## Current Status: How far can you use them?

Currently, Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets are available in beta, while Claude Data has been officially launched [Source 2, Source 11]. Along with this announcement, a meeting agent feature called 'Claude Meetings' was also unveiled. This function participates in online meetings to transcribe and summarize the content [Source 6, Source 11].

All these tools are available for immediate use by all paid plan users [Source 1, Source 9]. Please note, however, that features like meeting agents and whiteboards are still in a developing beta stage.

## What will happen in the future?

Anthropic's move shows that AI is evolving from a 'machine that answers questions' into a 'colleague who works with you.' In the future, we can look forward to even more proactive AI, such as Claude analyzing data to create sheets or arranging meeting agendas on a whiteboard before we even input anything directly.

A work environment where humans leave simple tasks to AI and focus on more creative and strategic areas is approaching rapidly. With Claude, is your team ready to unfold their ideas?

## MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Perspective

The era of simple chatbots is over. Now, AI is stepping out of the screen, lending a hand directly within the documents and tools we use. Making the user feel no walls between tools will be the core of true AI productivity.
