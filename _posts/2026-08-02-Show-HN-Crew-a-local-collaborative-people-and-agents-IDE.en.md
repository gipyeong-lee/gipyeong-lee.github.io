Imagine this: you are faced with a situation where you need to write complex code. In the past, you would have stayed up all night staring at your monitor alone or asked a colleague for help. But now, you have a very smart 'AI colleague' sitting right next to you. A scene where you divide work with a single word and create results together in real-time is no longer just a story from a movie.

Recently, a new tool called 'Crew' ([Show HN: Crew, a local collaborative (people and agents) IDE](https://modernorange.io/item/49137891)) has appeared in the developer community and is attracting significant attention. This tool intends to fundamentally change the way we collaborate with AI.

## Why is this important?

Until now, many AI coding tools operated on cloud-based systems or required cumbersome server configurations. However, for many developers, maintaining the security of their own work environment (local environment) is an invaluable priority. This is because concerns about security or data leaks arise during the process of sending code to external servers.

Crew digs into exactly this point. It allows developers to work in sync with AI agents within the safe boundary of their own computers. Now, without uploading your code to external servers, you can proceed with 'team projects' by utilizing the AI agent tools you are already familiar with and have installed on your machine. This is significant not only in that it increases developer productivity but also in that it elevates AI from a mere 'entity to ask questions' to a 'true colleague sharing the workload.'

## Simply put: A shared workspace with an AI colleague

By way of analogy, how about thinking of Crew as a **'shared workspace equipped with an AI-dedicated desk'**?

Just as we share materials and refine project proposals with colleagues sitting next to us when working in an office, Crew provides a space within the computer environment that developers use for AI agents to join and work together.

Usually, developers give instructions for tasks through an Agent Command Line Interface (a tool for executing AI by typing commands). Crew acts as a connector that calls upon these tools—which the user has already installed on their computer—as if they were team members, allowing them to build projects together ([Show HN: Crew, a local collaborative (people and agents) IDE](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49137891)). It shows efficiency similar to a new colleague being deployed to work immediately upon joining, as it can utilize existing tools without complex settings.

## How far has it come?

Currently, Crew is a fully open-source IDE that helps developers create results together with AI on their own computers ([Show HN: Crew, a local collaborative (people and agents) IDE](https://modernorange.io/item/49137891)).

Because it utilizes already installed tools without complex cloud-based settings, it dramatically reduces the time and effort required for environment setup. By sharing repetitive and complex tasks that developers used to perform alone with AI, an environment is being created where human developers can focus on more creative and essential problem-solving.

## What happens next?

In the future, collaboration between developers and AI agents will become even more natural. As tools like Crew evolve, it is highly likely that developers will transform from simply being 'people who write code' into 'team leaders who direct AI colleagues.'

In particular, the concept that 'humans and AI build something together' can extend beyond the development domain into various creative fields. As the tools we possess become smarter and more tightly integrated within local environments, our creativity will be able to soar higher with AI as a powerful pair of wings.
