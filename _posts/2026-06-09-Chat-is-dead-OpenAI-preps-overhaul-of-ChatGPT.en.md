## ChatGPT Declares "Chat Is Dead"... Evolving Beyond Simple Conversation Into a 'Super App'

When ChatGPT first debuted in late autumn 2022, it shocked the world. This incredible technology, which answered questions seamlessly and naturally like a human, quickly embedded itself deep into our daily lives. At first, we thought it was just a smart chat program equipped with vast knowledge. However, technological evolution is racing ahead much faster than we can adapt. OpenAI, the developer of ChatGPT, is preparing its most massive overhaul since its initial launch in 2022, signaling a leap to a completely new dimension [`'Chat is dead': OpenAI plans biggest ChatGPT overhaul ahead of IPO, pivots to AI agents, says report | Company Business News`](https://www.livemint.com/companies/news/chat-is-dead-openai-plans-biggest-chatgpt-overhaul-ahead-of-ipo-pivots-to-ai-agents-says-report-11780809387032.html).

There is one shocking remark that bluntly illustrates the scale and direction of this massive change. According to a recent report by the Financial Times (FT), a senior OpenAI official, while explaining the process of overhauling the company's flagship chatbot model, declared in a very resolute tone: **"Chat is dead."** [`OpenAI Declares Chat Is Dead in Biggest ChatGPT Overhaul Ahead of IPO | OpenTools`](https://opentools.ai/news/openai-chatgpt-superapp-overhaul-chat-is-dead). 

What on earth does it mean when the very creators of ChatGPT—the epitome of conversational AI that spearheaded the global text-based chat craze—say "chat is dead" themselves? Simply put, it means the era of passive, text-based Q&A has come to an end. Now, a completely new 'Super App' ecosystem—a massive, integrated application offering all services in one place—where AI directly handles various tools and takes substantial actions is opening up in earnest.

### Why It Matters

Until now, we have largely considered AI as a smart 'advisor' or a 'search assistant' armed with vast knowledge. When you asked something, it provided excellent text answers, but ultimately, taking the final physical actions—like copying the answer for documentation or actually visiting a site to make a reservation—was entirely up to humans. 

However, through this full-scale overhaul, OpenAI is attempting to transform ChatGPT beyond a simple chatbot into a 'super app'—a massive, unified platform that bundles programming tools, AI agents (autonomous AI that plans and acts on its own to achieve a given goal), image generators, and various third-party services into one [`OpenAI Declares Chat Is Dead in Biggest ChatGPT Overhaul Ahead of IPO | OpenTools`](https://opentools.ai/news/openai-chatgpt-superapp-overhaul-chat-is-dead).

Why is this paradigm shift so important to our daily lives? It is because our very way of digital living will be fundamentally restructured. Think back to when we first bought smartphones. Initially, phone calls, texts, internet browsers, and map apps all existed independently on the home screen. But now, just by opening a messenger super app like KakaoTalk or WeChat, you can transfer money, call a taxi, shop, and order food delivery—solving almost all daily services at once within it. 

This is exactly what ChatGPT is aiming for. Instead of the hassle of users copying answers and pasting them into other programs or jumping between multiple windows, the ChatGPT screen itself becomes a massive workspace and an integrated control center. This goes beyond simply stringing together multiple functions; it embodies OpenAI's grand goal of providing an ultimate environment where users can literally 'get things done' in one place [`'Chat Is Dead': OpenAI Reportedly Planning Radical Changes to ChatGPT`](https://gizmodo.com/chat-is-dead-openai-reportedly-planning-radical-changes-to-chatgpt-2000768491). In the future, instead of manually operating various software with a keyboard and mouse, we will face an era where complex tasks are completed like magic simply by giving a goal to an AI.

### The Explainer: The Magic of the 'Super App' and 'Codex'

Let's break down this massive change, which might feel a bit abstract, into an everyday situation to make it easier to understand.

As an analogy, if the ChatGPT of the past was an **outstanding 'librarian'** who had memorized all the world's knowledge, the revamped ChatGPT is evolving into an **'all-around personal assistant'** who directly handles my scheduler, wallet, and countless business software to take care of things automatically. With a librarian, you ask, "Please find me a good travel book," and then you have to book the flights and accommodation yourself. But with the all-around assistant, you just leave a single command: "Please finish all my travel preparations right away."

Imagine this: On your morning commute, you turn on your smartphone and tell the AI, "Perfectly prepare our family trip to Jeju Island next month." The old ChatGPT would have stopped at kindly writing a long text with a list of 5 popular destinations and packing tips. However, in the new super app environment, the AI rolls up its sleeves and takes direct action on the user's behalf. ChatGPT directly accesses the internal system of its affiliate partner, the global accommodation platform Booking.com, searches for the optimal lodging that perfectly fits the family size and budget, and proceeds with the reservation right up to the payment stage. At the same time, it opens the design platform Canva on its own and instantly designs a stylish, pretty travel invitation image to send to the family group chat [`OpenAI says "chat is dead" and plans to rebuild ChatGPT as a full-blown agent app`](https://the-decoder.com/openai-says-chat-is-dead-and-plans-to-rebuild-chatgpt-as-a-full-blown-agent-app/). 

In other words, OpenAI's artificial intelligence is breaking out of the narrow prison of the text window and transforming into an all-weather platform that freely crosses over into the services of other partner companies, taking actual physical and digital 'actions' [`OpenAI is preparing ChatGPT in a super-application format | Dzen`](https://dzen.ru/a/aiVWXjDwMT9Ephn_). Through this, users no longer need to click multiple app icons one by one and learn how to use each one every time; they can control the entire external ecosystem through just a single gateway—ChatGPT [`OpenAI thinks 'Chat is dead' and is overhauling ChatGPT - Android Authority`](https://www.androidauthority.com/chatgpt-overhaul-openai-3675309/).

Another innovative aspect to focus on in this overhaul is the full-scale deployment of a powerful feature called **Codex**. Codex might be a bit unfamiliar to the general public, but it is OpenAI's core technology that builds complex software or writes computer programming code on your behalf, simply by giving instructions in everyday language [`OpenAI is giving ChatGPT its biggest makeover yet — with plans for...`](https://nypost.com/2026/06/08/business/openai-is-giving-chatgpt-its-biggest-makeover-yet-with-plans-for-superapp/).

Let's explore this amazing technology through an analogy as well. For an average person who knows nothing about coding or computer languages, using a Codex-integrated environment is like **moving away from the old method of just asking an interior designer for verbal advice or blueprints, to instantly summoning a highly skilled carpenter and an entire wallpaper and flooring installation team at once**. If the user simply says, "Make me a pretty webpage where it snows on the screen when I press a button," the AI assistant handles the tool called programming language as skillfully as a master craftsman wielding a hammer, instantly completing and displaying a smoothly working software right before your eyes. OpenAI is completely redesigning ChatGPT's design and interface so that users can intuitively and easily handle such powerful coding tools, incredible image generation features, and various partner apps without feeling unfamiliar [`‘Chat is dead’: OpenAI plans ChatGPT superapp overhaul, says report - Storyboard18`](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/chat-is-dead-openai-plans-chatgpt-superapp-overhaul-says-report-100419.htm).

In fact, OpenAI employee Dresser described the goal of this ultimate integrated experience in detail as "bringing together the best of the ChatGPT conversational interface, the Codex creation tool, agentic browsing (an autonomous feature where AI surfs the web on its own without human intervention and processes complex information), and broader extensibility capabilities into a single place" [`'Chat Is Dead': OpenAI Reportedly Planning Radical Changes to ChatGPT`](https://gizmodo.com/chat-is-dead-openai-reportedly-planning-radical-changes-to-chatgpt-2000768491). It is essentially a refusal to stay within the narrow confines of a simple Question-and-Answer platform—its past glory—and an evolution into a comprehensive, infrastructure-grade AI application that encompasses everything [`News - "Chat is dead": OpenAI plans biggest... | ComputerBase Forum`](https://www.computerbase.de/forum/threads/chat-is-dead-openai-plant-groesste-umgestal-tung-von-chatgpt-seit-2022.2272894/).

### Where We Stand

So, when will we be able to directly experience these magical things that will completely change our daily lives on our smartphones and computer monitors? This is by no means a science fiction novel imagining the distant future.

Under OpenAI's meticulous planning, we are expected to see a newly revamped and even more powerful ChatGPT within the next few weeks (as of June 2026). This massive overhauled version, featuring coding tools at the forefront and equipped with autonomously acting AI agents, is slated to be gradually rolled out to the public in the coming weeks [`"Chat is dead" — at least, according to a senior OpenAI employee.`](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/07/openai-is-still-working-on-that-super-app/). 

The current atmosphere within OpenAI is incredibly serious and steadfast regarding this upheaval. This move to shed the familiar and safe shell of the existing ChatGPT without hesitation and be reborn as a 'fully integrated super app'—to the point where a bold declaration of "chat is dead" came from the mouth of a top-level employee—is considered the greatest and most decisive update in OpenAI's short but impactful history [`ChatGPT is being prepared for the largest... — Eudgene Brenev on TenChat.ru`](https://tenchat.ru/media/5489786-chatgpt-gotovyat-k-krupneyshemu-obnovleniyu-v-istorii--polnotsenniy-superapp). 

Of course, it should not be misunderstood that the provocative expression "chat is dead" means the chatbot dialogue window where we type text will physically and completely disappear tomorrow morning. We will still converse with AI using natural text or voice. However, this declaration is a powerful message symbolically showing that the 'paradigm of passive AI utilization'—where we simply entered questions into a blank space and quietly waited for a text answer to be output—has completely come to an end [`The chat is dead: OpenAI moves to the... | Context Studios Blog`](https://www.contextstudios.ai/fr/blog/le-chat-est-mort-openai-passe-la-super-application).

### What's Next

It must also be understood that a bold and meticulous business strategy by OpenAI lies behind this massive and radical technological leap. According to numerous foreign media reports, this core project to completely transition ChatGPT into a massive super app is being strongly pushed forward right on the eve of a crucial event that will determine the company's fate: OpenAI's Initial Public Offering (IPO) on the stock market [`OpenAI Declares Chat Is Dead in Biggest ChatGPT Overhaul Ahead of IPO | OpenTools`](https://opentools.ai/news/openai-chatgpt-superapp-overhaul-chat-is-dead).

An information technology company about to list on the stock market must clearly prove its value to cold-headed investors worldwide. It must absolutely show that its technology is not simply a temporarily trending novelty text toy, but a massive and essential next-generation 'infrastructure' capable of completely dominating and replacing the digital daily lives of all humanity in the future [`Before the IPO: OpenAI to rebuild ChatGPT into a Super App... | t3n`](https://t3n.de/news/openai-chatgpt-super-app-1746362/). The market is already overflowing with similar chatbot services rushed out by numerous large tech companies. With just a simple conversational service alone, it is difficult to be recognized for overwhelming and permanent value amidst that competition. 

But what if ChatGPT establishes itself as the 'single super app platform through which everything in the world flows'—a place where users stay all day, handle their company's Excel tasks, enjoy shopping on their way home, and even automatically book their weekend vacation trips? The economic value of that company would skyrocket to an astronomically unfathomable degree.

Experts predict that once OpenAI's full-scale and ambitious overhaul is completely applied and goes through an advancement process, the ChatGPT of the not-too-distant future—spanning the second half of 2026 into 2027—will be completed as a completely new form of intelligent service system that is qualitatively and 'fundamentally different' from the conversational tool of 2022 that first appeared in the world and surprised us [`[June 2026 Latest] ChatGPT Becomes a Super App | A Complete...`](https://note.com/tothinks/n/nbb4f563fe619?hl=en). 

Ultimately, in the near future, instead of the familiar question, "Have you asked ChatGPT about that?", we will use the phrase, "I just had ChatGPT do all that work," much more routinely and naturally. Artificial intelligence that moves beyond passive conversation in a text window to prove itself through direct action in the physical digital world, advancing from warm advice to perfect execution. The era of the truly perfect 'AI assistant' is finally blooming right before our eyes with the grand declaration of the death of chat.
