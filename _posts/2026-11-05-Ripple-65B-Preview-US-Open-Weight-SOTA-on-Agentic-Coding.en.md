Imagine waking up, turning on your computer, and saying to your AI: "Structure the new feature I'm working on today, install the necessary modules, and write the code." While you sip your coffee, the AI analyzes the requirements, handles the tools itself, and completes the coding perfectly. While this felt like a scene from a movie in the past, an AI called an 'Agent' is now making this a reality.

Recently, [Ripple-65B-Preview](https://openrouter.ai/ricursiveai/ripple-65b-preview), developed in the US, has been stirring up the coding agent market. It has opened a path for developers to automate coding tasks more intelligently and economically.

## Why Does This Matter?

As AI automates increasingly complex coding tasks, developers no longer need to spend time on simple code writing. Instead, an environment is being created where they can focus on high-level tasks like architecture design and product requirement definition [Reference 13](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch).

However, until now, leveraging high-performance AI meant paying high costs or shying away from using external models due to security concerns. The newly released Ripple-65B-Preview uses an 'Open-weights' approach (a method where the model's internal weights are public, allowing anyone to run them), providing developers with options to proactively manage their own infrastructure or utilize external APIs at a lower cost [Reference 1](https://openrouter.ai/ricursiveai/ripple-65b-preview), [Reference 8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wBNa41OmW3o).

## In Simple Terms: What Kind of AI is 'Ripple-65B'?

Ripple-65B-Preview is a model created by 'ricursiveAI'. As the name suggests, it is a massive model with 65 billion parameters (the adjustable values the AI learns and remembers) [Reference 1](https://openrouter.ai/ricursiveai/ripple-65b-preview).

To put it simply, this model is like a **'veteran developer who knows how to handle a toolbox.'**

- **Native tool-calling:** While general AI might just engage in conversation, this model knows how to analyze code on its own, execute necessary commands, or use external tools. It is akin to a skilled engineer knowing exactly when to reach for a hammer or a screwdriver [Reference 1](https://openrouter.ai/ricursiveai/ripple-65b-preview).
- **Efficient Scale:** The 65B scale is a 'sweet spot'—neither too heavy nor too light. Because it maximizes cost-efficiency while maintaining processing performance, it is highly affordable, priced at approximately $0.20 per 1 million input tokens and $0.60 per 1 million output tokens [Reference 1](https://openrouter.ai/ricursiveai/ripple-65b-preview).

## How Far Have We Come?

As of late 2026, the coding AI market is in a state of intense competition. Models like DeepSeek use massive context (the amount of information an AI can remember at once) as their weapon to demonstrate high efficiency [Reference 3](https://kilo.ai/landing/deepseek-v4), while Alibaba's Qwen series and the GLM series lead the open-weights field by delivering outstanding performance even in local environments [Reference 5](https://atomic.chat/blog/guides/best-local-llms-for-coding), [Reference 9](https://ip-calculator.ru/blog/artificial-intelligence/glm-5-3-open-weight-lokalno/).

Amidst this competition, Ripple-65B-Preview has carved out a narrow and deep niche: 'agentic coding.' Beyond merely completing code, there are ongoing attempts for AI to control and optimize development workflows autonomously. However, the honest reality is that there remains a gap in extremely nuanced reasoning or highly sophisticated agentic tasks compared to top-tier proprietary models like GPT-5.5 [Reference 10](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/recent-open-weights-model-launches).

## The Landscape Ahead

Future AI coding will evolve into a more powerful form of 'collaboration.' Open-weights models will be deployed within corporate internal networks, leveraging their price competitiveness and security, while developers will command their own 'dedicated AI assistants' on their laptops or local servers [Reference 7](https://localaimaster.com/blog/best-ollama-models), [Reference 8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wBNa41OmW3o).

What matters is that we have entered an era where we must move beyond asking 'which model has the best performance' and instead consider 'which AI integrates best into our team's coding workflow.'
