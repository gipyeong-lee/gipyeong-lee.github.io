Imagine your kitchen only had one knife. You’d have to peel fruit, slice meat, and trim fish with that single blade—it would be incredibly inconvenient. Chefs swap knives depending on the ingredients and the cooking method. The recent flood of Artificial Intelligence (AI) models is no different. How are developers, the experts in this field, filling their own "toolboxes"?

Recently, on the technology community Hacker News, a heated debate unfolded regarding which AI models developers use as their default [[Ask HN: What default model do you use and why?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49672966)]. It is striking to see how they don’t just stick to the single newest or highest-performing model, but instead select models to suit their individual purposes.

### Why does this matter?

The frequency with which we use AI in our daily lives is growing rapidly. However, is it best to insist on only the newest or most famous models? By looking at the choices experts make, we can learn how to use technology more efficiently. You can reduce wasted costs on unnecessary paid model subscriptions, or conversely, save time that might be lost using a model that lacks the necessary performance. Finding the AI that fits your specific purpose is a key strategy directly linked to productivity.

### Simplified: How to use AI as a 'Specialized Tool'

Think of AI models as "workers with different specialties." One worker writes very smooth prose (Claude), another delivers the latest news faster than anyone (Grok), and yet another meticulously checks facts within vast amounts of data (Gemini) [[Ask HN: Which AI model do you use for what?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48783556)].

The criteria developers use to select models can be summarized into three main points:

1.  **Purpose Alignment**: They choose models skilled in writing logical code for programming, or models specialized in creative tasks for image generation [[Ask HN: Which AI model do you use for what?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48783556)].
2.  **Instruction Following**: The newest model isn't always the right answer. Some users stick to older models because they feel they are more precise and follow instructions more strictly [[Ask HN:WhatLLM areyouusing?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49600138)].
3.  **Cost-Effectiveness (Efficiency)**: Recently emerged 'agentic models' (AI that sets its own plans and performs tasks) are very intelligent, but because they perform so many calculations, there is a risk they will exhaust 'session credits' (usage fees) in an instant [[Ask HN: What default model do you use and why?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49672966), [Hacker News story: Ask HN: What default model do you use and why?](https://usaeconomy-news.blogspot.com/2026/09/hacker-news-story-ask-hn-what-default.html)].

### The Current Landscape: AI Around Us

The market is already filled with models that have distinct strengths:

*   **Complex Tasks**: Models like GPT-6 Astra demonstrate powerful performance in in-depth research, complex data analysis, and computer control tasks [[Compare AIModels: Pricing, Context & Benchmarks](https://openrouter.ai/models)].
*   **Intelligent Reasoning**: Models like Kimi K3 are configured from launch to unleash maximum reasoning capabilities, with plans to add efficient modes based on user needs [[Kimi K3 Tech Blog: Open Frontier Intelligence](https://www.kimi.com/blog/kimi-k3)].
*   **General Versatility**: Models are provided as defaults tailored to each service, allowing users to receive immediate help with daily tasks without needing much deliberation [[GPT-Image-2.5 Flare vs Sunburst: New OpenAI Image APIs](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/gpt-image-2-5-flare-sunburst)].

### What's next?

Looking ahead, we can expect AI to evolve beyond merely being "smart." It will likely advance into systems that judge the user's situation to suggest the optimal model, or operate through collaboration between multiple models. Just as developers are doing now, before long, we won't need to worry about "which AI to use." Instead, we will be met with intelligent assistants that connect us to the most efficient model the moment we say what we want to do.
