Imagine this. It's Mother's Day morning, and due to a busy schedule, you've forgotten to get flowers for your mother. In the past, you would have scrambled to buy flowers at a grocery store or rushed to find a delivery service, breaking a sweat. Now, however, you can get help from AI. Currently, some services utilize Artificial Intelligence (AI) to recommend flowers suited for the situation or even help draft touching poems to be delivered alongside them.

In the past, the process of worrying and putting in the footwork to send someone a gift was, in itself, an act of sincerity. But now, as AI becomes an 'extended self' ([Sam Altman interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MWT_doo68k)) that assists in our daily lives, the landscape of preparing for anniversaries is changing little by little.

## Why does this matter?

AI assisting in gift preparation goes beyond mere convenience; it is fundamentally changing the way we form relationships with others. Previously, to find a flower delivery service, you would have had to get recommendations from acquaintances or search through portal sites one by one, but now, an increasing number of people are asking AI, "Which place provides the most meticulous service?" ([Brandi AI report](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/flower-delivery-does-ai-recommend-101100771.html)).

While technology helps us express our hearts more efficiently, it also makes us rethink the meaning of a 'gift filled with sincerity.' When the thought and care hidden behind convenience are automated by AI algorithms, will that warm heart be delivered intact?

## In simple terms: AI, flowers, and the heart

The process of AI assisting in flower gifts can be metaphorically described as playing the role of a **'translator of love.'**

The most difficult part of gifting flowers to a precious person is selecting flowers that fit the recipient's situation or the language of flowers. Because AI has learned vast amounts of data, it makes this process much easier. For example, it suggests calm white lilies or irises when you need to offer comfort for sorrow, or recommends bright gerberas or sunflowers if it's a celebratory occasion ([AI Gift Ideas](https://reelmind.ai/blog/ordering-flowers-online-for-delivery-ai-s-gifting-ideas)).

Another interesting phenomenon is 'gifting flowers to AI.' Some platforms allow you to gift digital flowers to an AI, which is a new kind of culture expressing gratitude to a machine that took care of complex calculations and writing ([flowers4ai](https://flowers4ai.com/)). This is an interesting slice showing that the way humans treat machines is slowly changing from mere tools to companions.

## Current situation: How far have we come?

Many companies are already utilizing AI. The famous flower delivery company '1-800-Flowers' provides a service that uses ChatGPT to write special poems to be sent with Mother's Day bouquets ([1-800-Flowers related report](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/385307/1-800-flowers-uses-chatgpt-for-momverse.html/?e75c541d_page=2&d4cbc21d_page=2&60b055ad_page=2&cef618cf_page=5&d4f3d2af_page=34)). Developers are also experimenting with implementing workflows that use AI agents to convert voice to text and automatically order flowers ([SignalWire blog](https://signalwire.com/blogs/developers/e-flower-shop)).

However, not everything is rosy. There are concerns that asking AI to write messages of love on anniversaries like Mother's Day, where sincerity is required, could create 'dangerous blind spots' in how we understand human love ([Mother's Day AI Criticism](https://www.mid-day.com/amp/sunday-mid-day/article/mothers-day-2026-dont-let-claude-draft-a-loving-message-for-mom-23629722)). Can a message written in an instant by technology really be the same as a heart filled with our own long-standing contemplation and sincerity?

## What happens next?

As AI technology advances, features supporting emotional connection will become more delicate and human-like. In the future, AI may become a tool that goes beyond mere general recommendations and suggests deeper, more personalized messages based on past memories we've shared with precious people ([AI flower technology outlook](https://newbloomsolutions.com/if-ai-flowers-look-this-good-now-what-happens-next/)).

However, as technology becomes more advanced, greater responsibility falls upon us. Even if we receive helpful assistance from AI, why not try writing the final sentence ourselves in our own voice? That is the weight of sincerity that only humans can possess, which technology can never imitate.

## MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Perspective
AI is a powerful tool that helps us convey our hearts more meticulously. However, isn't the core of the result of 'love' that we complete with that tool something that only attains meaning when it is filled with our own experiences and sincerity?
