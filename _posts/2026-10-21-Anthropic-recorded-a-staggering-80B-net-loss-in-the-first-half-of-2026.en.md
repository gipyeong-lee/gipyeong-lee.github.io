Imagine. How much energy is being consumed for the artificial intelligence (AI) assistants we use every day to become smarter? It requires enormous costs, as if a massive city were running non-stop, 24 hours a day. Recently, data released by Anthropic, the AI company that created 'Claude' (a next-generation AI assistant developed by Anthropic), has become a hot topic.

The news is that Anthropic recorded a net loss of over $80 billion in the first half of 2026 (January–June). [Source 1](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/20/anthropic-posts-staggering-80-7-billion-net-loss-in-first-half-of-2026-as-ipo-approaches/), [Source 2](https://youlidao.ai/en/intelligence/anthropic-posts-80b-net-loss-h1-2026) This news has surprised many and starkly illustrates the immense financial burden carried by the fastest-growing company in Silicon Valley.

### Why does this matter?

Beyond just the loss figure, this information gives us a glimpse into the current state of the AI industry. Anthropic is currently preparing for an Initial Public Offering (IPO, selling shares to the general public for the first time), and the financial statements released during this process serve as a detailed first-time look for investors and the public into how the company is being operated. [Source 1](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/20/anthropic-posts-staggering-80-7-billion-net-loss-in-first-half-of-2026-as-ipo-approaches/), [Source 5](https://bingx.com/en/news/post/anthropic-posts-staggering-billion-net-loss-in-first-half-of-as-ipo-approaches)

As an average consumer, you might worry, "Are they okay losing this much money?" However, in the tech industry, these numbers often represent massive investment costs for the future or accounting mechanisms rather than simply "going under." It is important to note that a significant portion of this loss is attributed to accounting expenses. [Source 4](https://ground.news/article/anthropic-logged-net-loss-of-over-80-billion-in-first-half-of-2026-wsj-reports)

### Easy to understand: Building an AI skyscraper

How can we compare this situation? Let's liken it to the process of building a house. At first, you design it, prepare the ground, and pour thousands of tons of concrete. For Anthropic, this is the stage of laying the foundation to build a "massive skyscraper" called AI.

Maintaining and advancing the parameters (the numeric values AI adjusts itself while learning from data) of an AI model costs more than the average person can imagine. It's similar to needing to charge the smartphone we use every day dozens of times a day. But once this investment is complete, AI will be much more accurate, safer, and capable of understanding the complex contexts we speak about than it is now. [Source 6](https://claude.com/)

Currently, Anthropic's loss scale has grown significantly, from a $13 billion loss in the first half of 2025 to an $80.7 billion loss in the first half of 2026. [Source 3](https://www.zerohedge.com/ai/807-billion-red-ink-and-600-billion-tab-inside-anthropics-updated-ipo-prospectus) This means the company's size has grown that much, and the scale of data and infrastructure it needs to learn has increased exponentially.

### Current situation

Anthropic is not just focused on technology development. In the first half of 2026 alone, they have been actively building relationships with the government, injecting $3.53 million into lobbying in Washington. [Source 7](https://www.alexjoneslive.com/2026/10/13/anthropic-issues-warning-over-self-replicating-ai-and-says-a-chinese-model-pulled-it-off/)

At the same time, the company recognizes realistic external risks. They are warning that opposition from local communities and environmental groups, particularly in the process of building data centers, could become a significant obstacle to future business expansion. [Source 4](https://ground.news/article/anthropic-logged-net-loss-of-over-80-billion-in-first-half-of-2026-wsj-reports) This is because AI models require vast amounts of electricity to train, and concerns are growing about the environmental impact of the data centers being built to provide it.

### Where are they headed?

Going forward, Anthropic must cross the major threshold of a large-scale IPO. The financial information released this time is one of the tests they must pass in that process. [Source 1](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/20/anthropic-posts-staggering-80-7-billion-net-loss-in-first-half-of-2026-as-ipo-approaches/) Investors will be asking more stringently not just how Anthropic is spending money, but how they will create revenue and widen their technological lead through these expenditures.

We are watching the dawn of the AI era. Is this massive loss a necessary tuition fee for building the AI world of the future, or an unsustainable bubble? What is clear is that as fast as AI technology is becoming deeply ingrained in our lives, the concerns and costs of the companies behind it are by no means light.
