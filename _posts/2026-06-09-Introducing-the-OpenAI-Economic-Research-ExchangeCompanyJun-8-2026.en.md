To answer this massive question, OpenAI, the creator of ChatGPT, has taken a very interesting step. Going beyond simply making smarter AI, they have launched a massive project to objectively measure the economic waves their technology is creating in the world.

## Why It Matters

So why is this initiative by OpenAI so important to our daily lives? The reason is that AI is no longer a difficult tool used only by a handful of tech experts, but has established itself as an essential tool for people around the world. Let's take a look at some specific, tangible numbers.

Currently, the number of people who use ChatGPT every week has reached a staggering 800 million [A free version of ChatGPT built for teachers |OpenAI](https://openai-dotcom-git-main-openai.vercel.app/index/chatgpt-for-teachers/). This means that 1 in 10 people in the global population is having a conversation with this AI every week. The changes in the education sector are particularly noticeable, with teachers being one of the groups that embraced this technology earliest and use it most actively. In line with this, OpenAI recently launched a free version of ChatGPT specifically designed for teachers and announced that it would provide this completely free of charge until June 2027 [A free version of ChatGPT built for teachers |OpenAI](https://openai-dotcom-git-main-openai.vercel.app/index/chatgpt-for-teachers/). Starting from the classroom, a new era of education has already opened, breathing alongside AI and preparing for the future.

What about businesses and office workers? According to the latest statistics in collaboration with OpenAI's economic research team, 1 out of 4 Business weekly active users already works in the 'IT (Information Technology) industry,' and 1 in 5 works in 'professional, scientific, and technical services' [This morning,theOpenAIEconomicResearchteam in collaboration...](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aaron-ronnie-chatterji_this-morning-the-openai-economic-research-activity-7375956381042118656-rbqk).

What's even more interesting is exactly how they are using AI. The top 3 tasks that Technical users rely on tools like ChatGPT for the most are:
1. **Coding**: Writing computer programs and fixing complex errors.
2. **Research**: Quickly gathering massive amounts of data and analyzing it in-depth.
3. **Writing**: Drafting important emails, reports, proposals, etc.
[This morning,theOpenAIEconomicResearchteam in collaboration...](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aaron-ronnie-chatterji_this-morning-the-openai-economic-research-activity-7375956381042118656-rbqk).

Simply put, it is like transitioning from an era where carpenters sawed by hand and hammered nails, to suddenly inventing a powerful and efficient 'power tool.' Right now, IT professionals and specialists have top-tier power drills for knowledge work in their hands. They leave tasks like programming that used to require agonizing all-nighters or summarizing hundreds of pages of documents to the power tool called AI, while humans focus on creative work like planning the bigger picture and making final decisions.

This massive shift in productivity inevitably triggers a huge butterfly effect on company departmental structures, employee hiring practices, and even the economic growth of entire nations. This is the real reason why we must face reality through sophisticated and objective economic research, rather than mere guesswork.

## What is the Economic Research Exchange? (The Explainer)

At such a critical time, OpenAI officially launched a program called the **'Economic Research Exchange'** to support objective external research to understand the economic impact of AI [OpenAI Launches AI Economic Research Program | StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-launches-ai-economic-research-program). The core of this program is very simple. OpenAI itself is rolling up its sleeves to generously provide funding and research tools so that independent scholars and economic researchers around the world can deeply explore the intertwined relationship between AI and the economy [OpenAI Launches AI Economic Research Program | StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-launches-ai-economic-research-program) [OpenAI Launches OpenAI Economic Research Exchange — TradingView News](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2026:newsml_FWN42G0YO:0-openai-launches-openai-economic-research-exchange/).

Let's use an analogy. Imagine building a massive new 'dam.' Building a dam can generate useful electricity and prevent terrifying floods, but it inevitably causes tremendous changes to the downstream natural ecosystem, farmlands, and the living environments of the residents there. If the company that built the dam unilaterally says, "The dam we built is safe and beneficial" from their own perspective, people will be anxious. Instead, it is like handing massive research funds and state-of-the-art measuring equipment to independent external environmentalists and sociologists, asking them, "Please research very precisely and transparently how this dam affects the downstream ecosystem and local economy."

Here, the dam is 'AI,' and the ecosystem is 'our jobs and economy.' OpenAI wants to thoroughly verify the effects that the massive wave they have created has on the world using objective, scientific data. Researchers participating in this program will be granted the special opportunity to freely utilize the cutting-edge tools provided by OpenAI and collaborate with other outstanding researchers [OpenAI Launches AI Economic Research Program | StartupHub.ai](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-launches-ai-economic-research-program).

In summary, OpenAI has become an actual economic titan holding unprecedented massive capital and power in history, all while pushing forward the public interest cause of creating technology that helps humanity [IntroducingOpenAI’s Chat GPT: The Future of Language... | Medium](https://avighnity.medium.com/introducing-openais-chat-gpt-the-future-of-language-generation-2747b49dcfa4). ## What Should We Keep an Eye On? (What's Next)

The launch of OpenAI's Economic Research Exchange signifies that AI has been elevated beyond a smart toy in a lab to a core 'infrastructure,' like a road network or power grid that keeps our society running.

Going forward, we need to carefully observe the various statistical indicators and papers that will be newly published by external economists and researchers backed by OpenAI's funding and tools. These research findings will provide answers to very practical questions directly related to our livelihoods:
- Which jobs will be the first to be replaced by AI and face employment threats?
- Conversely, which industries will see explosive revenue growth and create new jobs by actively utilizing AI?
- How wide will the future 'economic gap' become between students who learn to fluently handle AI in school and those who do not?

India is already agilely trying to respond to the approaching wave of changes by directly linking the introduction of talent development programs like the 'OpenAI Learning Accelerator' with its own economic outlook for 2026 [GoogleNews- India -Latest](https://news.google.com/topics/CAAqIQgKIhtDQkFTRGdvSUwyMHZNRE55YXpBU0FuQmhLQUFQAQ?hl=en-US&gl=US&ceid=US:en). Major countries and corporations around the world are moving quickly so as not to be left behind.

We must find our balance amidst the magical AI news pouring in every day. Instead of being vaguely fearful or blindly enthusiastic about technology, we should focus thoroughly on objective 'facts' and 'data.' The answer to the coming future does not stop at making smarter semiconductor chips, but depends on how wisely we cultivate 'our human economic system that utilizes that powerful technology.'

## AI's Take

When a new technology sweeps the world like a massive wave, what we need first is not vague fear. We need a reliable 'map' that accurately shows where this wave is heading and how it will change the ground we stand on.

OpenAI's recent move is highly impressive. It is because they have shown a willingness to objectively measure how the massive wave of AI they built themselves will change the landscape of the global economy, using their own efforts as well as the transparent eyes of external experts.
