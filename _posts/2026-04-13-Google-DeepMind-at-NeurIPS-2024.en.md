## Attending the 'Hottest Festival' of Artificial Intelligence

Imagine world-renowned experts gathered in one place, debating all night about "What will the future of artificial intelligence (AI) look like?" If football fans have the World Cup, for AI researchers, **'NeurIPS (Neural Information Processing Systems)'** is that very stage. Every year-end, the eyes of AI experts worldwide are fixed on this conference.

From December 10 to 15, 2024, the 38th NeurIPS conference was held spectacularly at the Vancouver Convention Centre in Canada [Google DeepMind at NeurIPS 2024 — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024/), [Events - Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/discover/events/). One of the most eye-catching protagonists of this event was undoubtedly **Google DeepMind**. They showcased their overwhelming presence as a 'Diamond Sponsor,' the highest sponsorship tier of the conference [NeurIPS 2024 - Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/discover/events/neurips-2024/).

Google DeepMind poured out over **100 new research papers** at this event [Google DeepMind at NeurIPS 2024 - AI Global Tech](https://aiglobaltech.blogspot.com/2025/01/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024.html). To put that number in perspective, it is equivalent to what an entire university research lab might produce over several years, presented at a single conference. But it wasn't just about quantity. Their research targeted areas very close to our lives, from the weather we check every day to the music that pleases our ears, and even the quantum computers often called a dream of the future.

## Why Does This Matter? (Changes Coming to Our Lives)

When people think of 'AI research,' they often imagine complex equations filling a blackboard or hard-to-read computer code. However, what Google DeepMind showed this time is a clear answer to **"How AI moves from the lab into our living rooms and offices."** AI is no longer just a story above the clouds.

For example, consider 'weather forecasting,' which helps you decide whether to take an umbrella for your morning commute. Google DeepMind demonstrated how AI-powered weather prediction technology is transforming into a practical tool [Google DeepMind at NeurIPS 2024 - AI Global Tech](https://aiglobaltech.blogspot.com/2025/01/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024.html). They also proved that AI can be an excellent creative assistant in 'music generation,' an area previously considered the exclusive domain of humans.

In simple terms, AI is evolving beyond a 'smart chatbot' that merely answers questions into a **'capable expert'** that can calculate complex physical laws and create new works of art. A reliable partner has appeared to solve life's inconveniences and boost our creativity.

## Easy Understanding: Two 'Magic Tools' Brought by Google DeepMind

Let's set aside difficult technical terms for a moment and explain two core technologies announced by Google DeepMind using simple metaphors.

### 1. AlphaQubit: The 'Genius Proofreader' for Quantum Computers
Quantum computers, often called the computers of the future, are incredibly fast but extremely sensitive. They can easily experience errors due to even the smallest vibrations or temperature changes. It's like a precarious tower made of tens of thousands of glass cups.

Google DeepMind, in collaboration with Google Research, introduced **AlphaQubit** [Google Research 2024: Breakthroughs for impact at every scale](https://research.google/blog/google-research-2024-breakthroughs-for-impact-at-every-scale/). This technology uses a neural network (an AI model patterned after the structure of the human brain) to accurately identify and fix errors occurring in quantum computers.

To use an analogy, it's like a **'genius proofreader'** standing next to a student taking a complex foreign language dictation test, finding typos in real-time and correcting them into perfect sentences. Thanks to this technology, we are one step closer to a more reliable and stable era of quantum computing.

### 2. AutoML: The 'Magic Recipe' for Self-Evolving AI
Creating an AI is as tricky as a top chef developing a new recipe. A human must decide what data to input and what structure to build through tens of thousands of trial-and-error steps.

Dr. Isabelle Guyon from Google DeepMind gave a talk on the future of **AutoML (Automated Machine Learning)** at this conference [NeurIPS Invited Talk by Isabelle Guyon (Google DeepMind, Paris-Saclay University)](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/107832). AutoML is a technology where AI itself designs and optimizes other AIs.

Put simply, it's dreaming of a **'magic pot'** that finds the optimal recipe to suit your taste just by adding ingredients, even without a chef. A virtuous cycle is created where AI builds smarter AI without constant human intervention.

## Current Status: How Far Have We Come?

Google DeepMind's achievements at NeurIPS 2024 were brilliant. Notably, two papers led by DeepMind researchers received special recognition as candidates for the 'Best Paper Award' [Google DeepMind at NeurIPS 2024 — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024/).

Specifically, the areas they focused on include:
*   **AI Agents**: AI systems that don't just do what they're told but can set plans and act autonomously when given a goal [10 Groundbreaking Innovations Unveiled by Google DeepMind](https://fusionchat.ai/news/10-groundbreaking-innovations-unveiled-by-google-deepmind).
*   **3D Scene Generation**: Technology that can freely create three-dimensional virtual spaces from flat photos, which could revolutionize game and film production [10 Groundbreaking Innovations Unveiled by Google DeepMind](https://fusionchat.ai/news/10-groundbreaking-innovations-unveiled-by-google-deepmind).
*   **Generative Media**: Tools that can turn hummed melodies into great music or text into vivid videos [Google DeepMind at NeurIPS 2024 - AI Global Tech](https://aiglobaltech.blogspot.com/2025/01/google-deepmind-at-neurips-2024.html).

Furthermore, Google DeepMind hosted the **'Concordia Contest'** in collaboration with world-class universities like MIT and UC Berkeley [NeurIPS 2024 Concordia Contest](https://www.cooperativeai.com/contests/concordia-2024). This competition was a place to test the ability of multiple AIs to cooperate and solve complex problems, attracting attention with a total prize pool of $10,000. It marks the beginning of serious consideration for how AI can 'harmonize' with other AIs or humans, rather than just performing well alone.
