Imagine this: You are behind the wheel, stuck in traffic on a busy morning commute. Suddenly, you get a notification that an important meeting schedule has changed. In the past, you would have had to dangerously check your smartphone, but now, you just say, "GrokBot, adjust today's meeting schedule and summarize the relevant documents for the team." A moment later, a task completion message appears on your dashboard screen.

This is no longer a story from the distant future. With the addition of the powerful AI assistant 'GrokBot' to Tesla vehicles, our driving environment is completely changing. [Source: Tesla, GrokBot introduction in vehicles](https://driveteslacanada.ca/news/tesla-grok-bot-hands-free-ai-tasks/)

### Why is this important?

Until now, the dashboard screens of cars have mostly been limited to controlling the 'vehicle itself,' such as navigation, music playback, or vehicle settings. However, this change from Tesla is on a different level. This is the first time an in-vehicle AI assistant has functioned as a 'Commerce Surface'—an interface where commercial activities or work can be performed—beyond simply manipulating the car. [Source: Tesla GrokBot launch in vehicles](https://247wallst.com/cards/tsla-xpost-01m3512k24h95zbtgmfk9m4a83)

Simply put, the car has evolved from a tool that just takes you to your destination into a 'personal assistant on wheels' that takes care of your work for you.

### Easy to understand: An intern assistant with a master key

How exactly does 'GrokBot' do its work? To use an analogy, GrokBot is like a smart intern assistant who holds a 'master key' that can access the various apps and services we use.

This assistant uses special connection links called 'Connectors.' To help you understand, a Connector is like a 'pass' that allows the assistant to freely enter the rooms called your email, calendar, and file storage. [Source: Tesla, Work automation via GrokBot](https://www.autoguide.com/auto/manufacturers/tesla-manufacturers/tesla-integrates-grok-bot-to-do-your-tasks-for-you-44639050)

When the driver gives a command, GrokBot uses this pass to read and organize emails, neatly modify complex calendar schedules, and find and summarize existing saved documents conversationally. [Source: Tesla, In-vehicle hands-free AI tasks](https://driveteslacanada.ca/news/tesla-grok-bot-hands-free-ai-tasks/) Since all tasks are performed 'hands-free,' the driver can safely entrust work to it while keeping their eyes on the road.

### Can I use it right now?

This service is currently provided primarily to users with Tesla's highest subscription tier, 'SuperGrok Heavy.' Tesla has stated that it plans to gradually expand the available subscription tiers in the future. [Source: Tesla, GrokBot subscription tier information](https://www.notateslaapp.com/tesla-voice-commands/340/i-m-hungry)

For reference, GrokBot can engage in general conversation as well as handle work tasks. For example, you can get witty AI-typical answers to quirky questions like, "If I were to go on a first date with Tesla's humanoid robot 'Optimus,' where should we go?" Additionally, GrokBot has separate usage from existing Grok services, so those on existing plans can use it with peace of mind. [Source: Utilizing Grok in Tesla vehicles](https://sea.mashable.com/transportation/38572/grok-is-now-in-tesla-cars-but-not-in-the-way-you-think) [Source: Introducing GrokBot](https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot)

### How far will it evolve?

Although we are in the early stages, cars will become even smarter in the future. Beyond just organizing emails, the scope of 'in-vehicle commerce' will expand significantly, such as preparing for video conferences inside the vehicle before arriving at the destination or making payments directly from the car.

What we should pay attention to is the fact that AI has deeply penetrated our travel time, which accounts for a large proportion of our lives. The driver's seat is now prepared to become a 'second office' where you can utilize your time most efficiently while on the move, beyond just being a space for driving.
