Imagine this. One morning, sitting in a café, you talk to an AI via your smartphone messenger just like chatting with a friend: "Build a mini app that analyzes the menu of the highest-rated pasta restaurant in our neighborhood and picks a dish matching my taste today." A moment later, the AI quickly writes the code and hands you a link to a ready-to-use web service. Even though you don't know the first thing about programming languages, you've brought your own service into the world with just a few sentences of conversation.

This is the scene of **'vibecoding (a novel approach to building software and AI products using natural language and AI assistance)'**, which has recently emerged as a hot topic in Silicon Valley and developer communities worldwide. However, the scope of vibecoding is now expanding beyond simple website or mobile app creation into designing **AI inference chips (brain semiconductors where AI thinks and generates answers)** and high-performance engines ([Vibecoding an Inference Chip](https://ankitg.me/blog/vibecoding-inference-chip/)).

---

## Why Does This Matter?

In the past, building a single smartphone app required spending months learning programming languages and going through complex server configurations. But in the era of vibecoding, anyone with an idea can become a developer.

For example, services have emerged that allow users to build and run entire applications simply by conversing in natural language through messengers like WhatsApp ([VibeCoding- Build Apps Through WhatsApp with AI | CodeVibe](https://www.codevibe.ai/)). Platforms like BridgeMind—a cloud development environment where multiple AI agents collaborate to maximize software release speed—are also gaining momentum ([Day 234 – VibeCoding an App Until I Make $1,000,000 - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lZjSEdIrNr4)).

This shift is not just a playground for beginners. Engineers and researchers are also leveraging AI to explosively accelerate development speeds across complex infrastructure, hardware design, and high-performance engine creation. At a time when demand for AI chips and computing power is at its peak—with tech giants like SpaceX raising massive capital for AI semiconductor infrastructure ([TLDR - A Byte Sized Daily Tech Newsletter](https://tldr.tech/))—vibecoding pushes the speed of converting ideas into physical hardware and software to its absolute limits.

---

## Understanding Simply: What is Vibecoding?

To put it into perspective, vibecoding is like **"a client raising a building by instructing a top-tier AI architect in plain words rather than laying bricks themselves."**

In the old days, building a house required earning carpentry certifications and spending years learning how to handle timber. In the vibecoding era, you simply say, "Put a large window here and fill the living room with warm-toned natural wood," and the AI architect immediately draws blueprints and takes charge of construction. Simply put, complex programming syntax is handled entirely by AI, while humans focus exclusively on vision and ideas.

AI luminary Andrej Karpathy shared work logs in April 2025 when he implemented the 'VibecodingMenuGen' app via vibecoding to solve restaurant menu indecision ([Vibecoding MenuGen – karpathy](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/vibe-coding-menugen/)). Since then, he joined Anthropic’s pre-training team in 2026, further accelerating the convergence of AI models and development methodologies ([Andrej Karpathy - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrej_Karpathy)).

Additionally, environments where developers work hand in hand with AI are becoming increasingly flexible, such as OpenAI adding manual usage reset features for Codex ([OpenAI добавила в Codex ручную сброс лимитов... -vibecoding.ru](https://vibecoding.ru/news/2026/07/13/codex-manual-usage-reset)).

However, behind this dazzling technology, sober voices exist as well. Industry expert Trace Cohen pointed out on social media that *"people can churn out crudely made trash apps, prompts, and substance-free vaporware with vibecoding too,"* while also emphasizing that genuine value-driven efforts—such as the high-performance inference engine 'KORAL' he is publicly developing—are taking place alongside it ([How it feels to vibe code AI and have no idea what it’s doing](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tracecohen_how-it-feels-to-vibe-code-ai-and-have-no-idea-what-its-doing-7468271461732925440-NpR4)).

## Current Status: How Far Have We Come and What Tools Exist?

Globally, various educational programs and tools are flooding the market in step with the vibecoding craze:
- **Diverse Tools and Platforms**: Across global markets including Russia and CIS regions, over 12 vibecoding tools offering free tiers and localized UIs are being compared and gaining popularity ([Вайбкодинг 2026: 12 инструментов бесплатно и на русском](https://gomvp.ru/blog/vibe-coding)), alongside professional development support tools like 1C:Naparnik ([1С:Напарник: бесплатно до 1 декабря 2026, где... —vibecoding.ru](https://vibecoding.ru/tools/1c-naparnik)).
- **Structured Education and Conferences**: Specialized schools teaching the fundamentals of vibecoding for youth and adults using Cursor AI have emerged (e.g., Vibecoding.by) ([Vibecoding— школа ИИ программирования, вайбкодинг с нуля](https://vibecoding.by/)), and conferences like 'Vibecoding Russia 2026' are being held for corporate executives and researchers to discuss accelerating decision-making through AI ([Vibecodingrussia 2026](https://vibecoding.hse.ru/)).
- **Development's Thrill Curve**: Many feel immense immersion and achievement creating working prototypes instantly through vibecoding, though they also experience a unique gap compared to the tactile satisfaction of writing code line by line in the past ([Vibecoding isn't as fun as writing code by hand — The Autodidacts](https://www.autodidacts.io/vibecoding-isnt-as-fun-as-writing-code-by-hand/)).

Simultaneously, demonstrations of cutting-edge models like GPT-5.2 implementing arcade games entirely via vibecoding are being shared on YouTube and elsewhere, completely transforming the paradigm of production speed and iteration cycles ([New ChatGPT 5.2 Vibecoding an Arcade Game for... - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HalhWUkXkE0)).

## Where Is It Heading Next?

Vibecoding is fundamentally redefining how software is made, going far beyond a temporary fad. Looking toward the future, several key shifts deserve attention:

1. **Popularization of Multi-Agent Orchestration**: Moving beyond humans entering individual prompts, cloud IDE environments (e.g., BridgeSpace) where multiple AI agents collaborate on planning, coding, testing, and hardware integration simulation will become standard ([Day 234 – VibeCoding an App Until I Make $1,000,000 - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lZjSEdIrNr4)).
2. **Blurring Lines Between Software and Hardware**: Even in areas previously accessible only to astronomical capital and specialized semiconductor personnel—such as inference chips and custom engines—vibecoding and AI agent support will enable small teams and independent researchers to design innovative prototypes ([Vibecoding an Inference Chip](https://ankitg.me/blog/vibecoding-inference-chip/)).
3. **Growing Importance of 'Depth' and 'Validation'**: As anyone can easily churn out apps and systems, the ability to filter out superficial vaporware and rigorously validate operational reliability, security, and performance will become true expertise ([How it feels to vibe code AI and have no idea what it’s doing](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tracecohen_how-it-feels-to-vibe-code-ai-and-have-no-idea-what-its-doing-7468271461732925440-NpR4)).
