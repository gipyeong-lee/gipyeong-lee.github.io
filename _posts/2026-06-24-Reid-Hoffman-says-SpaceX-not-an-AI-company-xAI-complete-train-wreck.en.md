Imagine the world's top tech companies competing to get ahead on the massive wave of artificial intelligence (AI). Elon Musk is always at the center of it. However, Reid Hoffman, one of Silicon Valley's most respected voices, has delivered a scathing critique of Musk's AI strategy. What exactly is the problem?

### Why It Matters

We live among AI-generated results every day. The AI models created by companies like OpenAI and Anthropic have become tools in our daily lives. [Source: Reid Hoffman: SpaceX is 'Not an AI Company,' xAI is a 'Complete Train Wreck'](https://vuink.com/post/sbeghar-d-dpbz/2026/06/24/reid-hoffman-spacex-musk-openai-anthropic-gen-z-mistake) In such a critical industry, when the most influential investor uses strong language like "complete train wreck" regarding a company's direction, it is a signal that the entire tech industry must pay attention to, not just mere criticism. [Source: Reid Hoffman: SpaceX is 'Not an AI Company,' xAI is a 'Complete Train Wreck'](https://fortune.com/2026/06/24/reid-hoffman-spacex-musk-openai-anthropic-gen-z-mistake/)

### The Explainer

Reid Hoffman is a co-founder of LinkedIn and a veteran investor in both OpenAI and Anthropic. [Source: Reid Hoffman: SpaceX is 'Not an AI Company,' xAI is a 'Complete Train Wreck'](https://biztoc.com/x/69f81c2bec4c8334) Why has he criticized Musk's companies so strongly?

To use a simple analogy, building AI technology is similar to "cooking a gourmet meal." You need the best ingredients (data), the best chefs (engineers), and an excellent recipe (model architecture). Hoffman's assessment suggests that Musk's xAI is like a kitchen where chefs are constantly swapped out and the recipe is rewritten from scratch every time.

In reality, xAI has reportedly seen researchers leave since its inception, and is said to be going through its "third restart" already. [Source: Reid Hoffman: SpaceX is 'Not an AI Company,' xAI is a 'Complete Train Wreck'](https://fortune.com/2026/06/24/reid-hoffman-spacex-musk-openai-anthropic-gen-z-mistake/) Furthermore, all of the initial co-founders personally recruited by Musk have already left the company. [Source: All xAI Co-founders Departed](https://thenextweb.com/news/xai-all-cofounders-departed-musk-spacex-rebuild) Hoffman pointed out that despite SpaceX not being an AI company, it is simply trying to "buy its way into relevance" by injecting capital. [Source: Reid Hoffman: SpaceX is 'Not an AI Company,' xAI is a 'Complete Train Wreck'](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/reid-hoffman-says-spacex-not-090000334.html?fr=sycsrp_catchall)

### Where We Stand

Currently, xAI operates under Elon Musk's SpaceX, pushing an AI chatbot called "Grok." [Source: xAI - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpaceXAI) However, within Silicon Valley, there is skepticism about whether this company is advancing technology as stably as Anthropic or OpenAI. [Source: Prediction That SpaceX Lockup Period Will Be a Disaster for Retail Investors](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/06/20/spacex-lockup-period-train-wreck-retail-investors/) Despite this critical situation, Hoffman believes there is enough room in the current AI market for companies with different strengths, like OpenAI and Anthropic, to grow together. [Source: Reid Hoffman: SpaceX is 'Not an AI Company,' xAI is a 'Complete Train Wreck,' and There's Room for Both OpenAI and Anthropic](https://www.msn.com/en-ca/news/technology/reid-hoffman-says-spacex-is-not-an-ai-company-and-xai-is-a-complete-train-wreck-and-there-s-room-for-both-openai-and-anthropic/ar-AA26pRWZ)

### What's Next

Going forward, we must watch to see if Musk's AI company can wash away the stigma of being a "complete train wreck" and build a proper foundation. Frequent leadership changes and the departure of core talent are very fatal to tech companies. Reid Hoffman emphasized that the AI industry cannot succeed merely by pouring in money; building a proper technical foundation is what matters. [Source: Tech Billionaire Reid Hoffman Bashes Elon Musk's AI Ambitions as a 'Complete Train Wreck'](https://www.dagens.com/news/tech-billionaire-reid-hoffman-bashes-elon-musks-ai-ambitions-as-a-complete-train-wreck)
