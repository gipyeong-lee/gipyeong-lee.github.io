## Why is this important?

Numerous websites around us (e.g., local communities, second-hand trading platforms, flight search sites, corporate internal portals, etc.) were built for humans to access via web browsers, look with their eyes, and click with a mouse. If companies did not open official APIs (Application Programming Interfaces, communication channels between programs), AI agents literally had to grope across website screens 'blindfolded.' 

Traditional browser-based AI agents frequently malfunctioned even with minor screen changes or slow loading, and their speeds were slow [Pomerado](https://pomerado.ai/). The task of developers directly recording website communication or analyzing HAR files (HTTP Archive, communication log files between browsers and web servers) to manually open APIs was an arduous schedule requiring immense time and cost.

Pomerado completely turned this problem on its head. Even for websites without official APIs, if a user gives just a few lines of natural language instructions like "Create a tool that performs this task on this site," Pomerado automatically navigates the site, completes example tasks, and generalizes them into reusable tools [Pomerado/pomerado:YCF26.TurnanywebsiteintoanAPI /MCP...](https://github.com/Pomerado/pomerado), [Pomerado: Turn Any Website Into an API/MCP for AI Agents (2026)](https://mishalbrands.com/trends/pomerado-website-to-api-for-agents). ## Easy to Understand: The Magic of Turning Websites into Toy Blocks

Simply put, Pomerado is a **'website-dedicated interpreter and assembly robot.'** 

Let's use this analogy. Suppose you go to a foreign country where market vendors do not speak English at all and communicate only in their local dialect. If you try to bargain with vendors directly using hand gestures, it takes a long time and leads to frequent mistakes. But what if a flawless simultaneous interpreter instantly translates the language right next to you? The conversation would proceed swimmingly.

Pomerado attaches precisely this 'flawless interpreter' to the AI agent. When an AI agent uses a website, the method of directly looking at the browser screen and calculating mouse coordinates (traditional browser agents) is like a nearsighted person reading text with a magnifying glass. On the other hand, Pomerado analyzes the structure of the website and converts it into **MCP (Model Context Protocol, a standard protocol for AI models to safely communicate with external data and tools)** tools and API forms that the agent can invoke immediately [Pomerado/pomerado:YCF26.TurnanywebsiteintoanAPI /MCP...](https://github.com/Pomerado/pomerado), [Pomerado: Turn Any Website Into an API/MCP for AI Agents (2026)](https://mishalbrands.com/trends/pomerado-website-to-api-for-agents).
