Imagine this: you wake up in the morning and say to your AI, "Analyze our company's project data from the past year, find vulnerabilities in the new system design we're working on, and outline improvements."

If AI was previously at the level of summarizing fragmented information, it is now evolving into an 'expert' capable of completing complex projects from start to finish. This is the story of **Gemini 4 Argon**, the next-generation AI model announced by Google on September 30, 2026. [Source: Google pulled out the price tag first — Gemini4Argon, confirmed numbers and still...](https://news.aikoreacommunity.com/gemini-4-argon-price-cost-per-task-1m-tokens/)

### Why is this important?

While AI has developed rapidly, it has been difficult to move beyond answering short, one-off questions. However, the truly important work in the world, such as large-scale software development or complex cybersecurity defense, does not end in just a few seconds. The fact that 'long-term tasks' involving multiple steps over a long period are now possible means that AI can now be your 'work partner' rather than just a simple assistant. [Source: Gemini 4 Argon: our next era of frontier intelligence](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/company-news/technology/gemini-4-argon-our-next-era-of-frontier-intelligence/) In particular, AI productivity in corporate environments is expected to increase dramatically.

### Easy to understand: An expert analogy

To understand Gemini 4 Argon, let's use the analogy of a **'professional chess player.'** If previous AI models were players who only moved quickly one move at a time, Gemini 4 Argon is a player who maps out the strategy for the entire game, anticipates the opponent's moves dozens of steps ahead, and corrects itself even if a crisis occurs midway, leading to victory.

This model is designed to sustain **'Deep Reasoning' (the ability to solve complex problems through logical thought processes).** [Source: Gemini 4 Argon: Benchmarks, Price and Who Gets It](https://felloai.com/gemini-4-argon/) Simply put, it excels at completing the big picture without forgetting the pieces it placed earlier when solving complex puzzles. This has enabled the performance of very long workflows, such as practical software engineering or cybersecurity. [Source: Introducing Gemini 4 Argon - The Keyword](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/)

### Current situation

Google first unveiled Gemini 4 Argon on September 30, 2026, and began providing the service in earnest to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers and paid API users starting October 13. [Source: Gemini 4: Release Date, Argon Launch & Rumors](https://felloai.com/all-we-know-about-google-gemini-4/) This is the most powerful model released about a year after Gemini 3. [Source: Google finally releases Gemini 4 Argon - The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1009862/google-releases-gemini-4-argon)

Currently, this model is engaged in fierce competition, standing shoulder to shoulder with cutting-edge models like OpenAI's 'GPT-6 Astra' and Meta's 'Muse Wonder.' [Source: You Can Use Gemini 4 Argon Now: Google's New AI Is ... - CNET](https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/gemini-4-argon-general-release-google-ai-news/) However, since AI technology changes rapidly every month, Google is also constantly improving its performance to outpace other competitors.

### What happens next?

In the future, we will enter an era where AI goes beyond simply finding information to writing code, monitoring security systems, and supporting complex corporate decision-making. In particular, Google is building security defense systems utilizing this model by running the 'Fairwind Program' with trusted partners in the cybersecurity field. [Source: Gemini 4 Argon: our next era of frontier intelligence](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/company-news/technology/gemini-4-argon-our-next-era-of-frontier-intelligence/)

Furthermore, once the process of solving complex problems together with AI becomes part of our daily lives, work complexity will decrease and productivity will be maximized. Of course, 'humanity's homework'—careful inspection and review by humans—remains regarding potential errors or security issues that may arise as AI completes complex tasks on its own.
