Imagine a stunning movie scene popping into your head. Something like, "A golden-furred puppy joyfully running along the waves on a purple beach at sunset." In the past, to create even this short scene, you would have had to head to the beach with a camera or study complex graphic tools for months. But now, you just need to say one sentence to an AI. And it will provide a highly realistic video, including the sound of rolling pebbles and the cool crashing of waves.

Recently, Google and Adobe showcased a wide array of new **Generative Media (technology that automatically creates images or videos when commands are entered)** models and tools that can instantly turn our imagination into reality. ## Current Status: How Far Have We Come?

These magical technologies are already among us. In Google's case, it is prioritizing the opening of cutting-edge tools like Veo 3 and Flow to users of its most powerful **'Ultra' plan**. As technology becomes so sophisticated, there are significant concerns that **Deepfakes (fake videos or voices made with AI)**, which are indistinguishable from reality, could be misused.
