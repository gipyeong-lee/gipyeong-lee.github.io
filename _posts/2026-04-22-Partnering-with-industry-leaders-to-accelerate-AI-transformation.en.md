Imagine you need the best ingredients to make a delicious dish. But what if the chef had to do the farming and raise the cattle themselves? They would likely lack the time to focus on cooking, and their expertise might suffer. Instead, what if the master chef formed a 'strategic partnership' with a farm that supplies the finest ingredients? The chef could then focus solely on cooking to deliver 5-star meals.

This is how global companies approach AI today. Businesses are no longer just 'purchasing' AI as a clever assistant. They are joining forces with tech giants to accelerate **'AI Transformation'**—the process of putting AI at the core of business strategy to transform the entire organization. [Source 3](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2025/07/28/how-microsofts-customers-and-partners-accelerated-ai-transformation-in-fy25-to-innovate-with-purpose-and-shape-their-future-success/)

## Why is this important?

Beyond just using a chatbot that performs better than others, why are leaders around the world forming alliances centered on AI?

LinkedIn CEO Ryan Roslansky emphasized, **"AI is redefining the definition of work, and we need a new playbook."** [Source 2](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-cloud/blog/2025/04/28/accelerate-ai-innovation-and-business-transformation-scaling-ai-transformation-with-strategic-cloud-partnership/) He says that leaders who build organizations for 'agility' rather than stability and invest in upskilling their employees internally will gain a competitive advantage.

In the past, failing to adapt to change meant simply 'falling behind a bit,' but now, failing to ride the massive wave of AI means facing the 'risk of disappearing from the market' altogether. [Source 2](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-cloud/blog/2025/04/28/accelerate-ai-innovation-and-business-transformation-scaling-ai-transformation-with-strategic-cloud-partnership/)

## Easy Understanding: The History and Principles of AI Partnerships

To understand why companies don't do everything themselves and instead form partnerships, we need to go back to 2019. When Microsoft invested $1 billion in OpenAI at the time, few predicted that this would fundamentally change the partnership approach of the entire tech industry. [Source 9](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/strategic-partnerships-ai-era-lessons-from-jonathan-bunce-miodc)

You can think of it this way: AI transformation is like switching from a regular bicycle to an **'electric bicycle.'** The destination is the same, but the power pushing the pedals (the core business engine) becomes incomparably faster and more efficient with the help of an electric motor (AI). In this case, a partnership is like a technology company providing the best motor and an engineering company perfectly assembling that motor onto the bike.

Collaborating with a strategic AI partner allows you to not only access cutting-edge technology but also leverage domain expertise and a broad ecosystem (a technical and business environment that connects to create synergy), dramatically increasing the speed of development. In simple terms, instead of trying to find your way with a map alone, it's like driving on a highway with an experienced guide. [Source 14](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/979-8-8688-0796-1_10)

## Current Situation: 'Alliances' in the Industrial Field

Currently, AI transformation has gone beyond theory and is producing concrete results throughout actual industrial sites. Let's look at some interesting examples.

### 1. Changing Employee Experience and Re-captivating Customers
Microsoft has developed a powerful framework to help partners assist customers with their AI transformation. There are two core elements.
First is **'Empowering Employee Experience.'** This involves providing world-class AI tools to help employees move away from simple repetitive tasks and work more creatively and enjoyably.
Second is **'Reinventing Customer Engagement.'** This applies AI and agentic (capable of judging and acting on their own) solutions to provide personalized experiences to customers and communicate efficiently. [Source 6](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/04/21/accelerating-frontier-transformation-with-microsoft-partners/)

### 2. A Private AI Factory: 'AI Refinery'
Many companies want to use AI but hesitate due to security concerns, fearing their precious data might leak. To address this, Accenture joined forces with Dell and NVIDIA to create a solution called the **'AI Refinery.'** [Source 16](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2025/accenture-collaborates-with-dell-technologies-and-nvidia-to-accelerate-enterprise-ai-transformation-with-ai-refinery)

This is like installing a 'private water purification facility' in your own basement. Instead of using public tap water (public cloud), it allows companies to safely process data and scale AI within their own closed on-premises (internally built) environments to create new value.

### 3. AI on the Factory Floor
Siemens and NVIDIA are expanding their partnership to deploy AI solutions directly onto the manufacturing **'Shopfloor.'** [Source 18](https://investor.nvidia.com/news-release-details/2025/Siemens-and-NVIDIA-Expand-Partnership-to-Accelerate-AI-Capabilities-in-Manufacturing/default.aspx) Now, AI is driving innovation with unprecedented speed and efficiency not just on office desks, but also among the heavy machinery where products are actually made.

### 4. How to Process Data, the New Crude Oil
EY has opened a global innovation center with Snowflake to help companies fully utilize the power of data. [Source 5](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/newsroom/2026/02/ey-announces-the-launch-of-a-global-innovation-center-for-snowflake-to-power-the-next-era-of-ai-driven-enterprise-transformation) Partners like Microsoft and SAP are participating to build a massive ecosystem that unifies scattered data assets.

## What's Next?

In 2026, we are no longer living in an era where we wonder, "Should we adopt AI or not?" According to NTT DATA Group's 2026 Global AI Report, **"Your AI strategy is your business strategy."** AI is no longer a management target for the IT department but has become the core engine that determines a company's survival and growth.

Gary Miller, Executive Vice President at Oracle, noted that "the rapid evolution of AI offers significant economic opportunities" and predicted that solutions like 'Oracle AI Factory,' which provides an integrated stack from analytics to infrastructure, will accelerate innovation for companies. [Source 17](https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/ai-world-oracle-launches-new-customer-and-partner-offerings-with-oracle-ai-factory-2025-10-14/)

Some thought AI transformation was an impossible 'Moonshot.' However, for many companies, the help of AI is not a science fiction story of the distant future but a reality that has arrived **'here today.'** [Source 13](https://kpmg.com/us/en/articles/2025/ai-transformations-enterprise-power-couple.html)

In the future, we will see companies forming deeper and broader partnerships to become 'Frontier Firms' (leading companies at the forefront of technology), as seen in the collaboration between Cognizant and Microsoft. [Source 15](https://news.cognizant.com/2025-12-18-Cognizant-and-Microsoft-Expand-Partnership-to-Advance-AI-Transformation-and-Frontier-Firm-Experiences)
