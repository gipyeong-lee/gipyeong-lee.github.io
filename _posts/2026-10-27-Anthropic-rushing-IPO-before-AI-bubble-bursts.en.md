Imagine this: You wake up in the morning and tell your smartphone AI, "Summarize today's meeting materials." In that brief moment, hundreds of billions of calculations are being processed behind the scenes. The artificial intelligence we use every day has gone beyond mere convenience; it has become a "battlefield" where massive capital and expectations collide.

Recently, Anthropic, a giant in the artificial intelligence field, made its stock market debut official. [Anthropic filed for an initial public offering on Monday, October 26, 2026.](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/26/tech/anthropic-files-ipo) People are watching closely to see if this news signals the peak of AI technology or the final party before the "bubble" bursts.

## Why Does This Matter?

The listing of an AI company is not just economic news; it is directly connected to the future of the AI services we use. If AI technology fails to generate as much profit as we expect, current services could become paid-only or see slower performance improvements. [Anthropic's IPO is expected to be a major turning point for the growth of AI technology and the tech industry.](https://trenue.com/2026/09/29/anthropic-ipo-jeonmanggwa-ai-sijang-bunseok/)

Wall Street investors are urging leaders like Anthropic and OpenAI to go public as soon as possible, and there are complex reasons for this. [There are complex reasons that go beyond the risk of an AI market bubble.](https://nypost.com/2026/10/23/business/wall-street-is-urging-anthropic-openai-to-do-their-ipos-in-a-hurry-heres-why/) For the average user, whether or not these companies go public will be an indicator of how deeply—and how affordably—AI technology will permeate our lives.

## Simply Put: AI Companies Running a 'Marathon'

Should we compare the IPO process of AI companies to a "marathon"? If a typical startup runs 10km, AI giants like Anthropic are running thousands of kilometers every day. To win the competition, they consume massive amounts of computing power (the force used to calculate data) daily.

In simple terms, Anthropic's current situation is like **"an athlete taking on enormous debt to buy the best diet and training equipment to build foundational strength."** [It has been pointed out that they may need hundreds of billions of dollars in debt to fulfill the $518 billion in obligations they currently hold.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mpBKQ9b51Mw) Investors are coolly watching to see if this athlete can make it to the finish line or if they will run out of energy along the way.

## Where Are They Now?

Anthropic's growth is astounding. [In the Series H funding round on May 28 of this year, it was recognized with an astronomical valuation of $965 billion, surpassing OpenAI's $852 billion for the first time.](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/965b-valuation-anthropic-rushes-ipo-who-you-betting-tentenco-wyqmc) [The company is reportedly aiming for a listing as early as mid-November 2026.](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/anthropic-reportedly-looking-to-ipo-as-early-as-mid-november-180315768.html)

However, there are dark shadows. [The tension with the Trump administration that has persisted since early 2025 has led to controversies such as "Woke AI," acting as a variable in corporate management.](https://www.livemint.com/companies/news/damage-control-anthropic-rushes-to-washington-amid-white-house-ban-on-top-ai-models-threatens-billion-dollar-ipo-11781518580276.html) Furthermore, [the computing infrastructure Anthropic is pouring massive amounts of money into is based on the expectation that customers will adopt AI rapidly, but many companies are actually still moving beyond basic stages of AI utilization.](https://observer.com/2026/10/anthropic-dario-amodei-ipo-ai-market-customers/)

## What Happens Next?

Anthropic's IPO will be the first major test to determine whether there is an AI "bubble." [Some experts warn that this listing could be a signal that the AI market has reached its peak.](https://imfounder.com/science-tech/ai/anthropic-ipo-ai-bubble/) It is highly likely that there will be growing pains in the process of converting the potential of the technology into real-world cash.

It is important for readers to watch how Anthropic maintains the safety of its technology while generating the massive profits demanded by investors. [Their listing will be the most important testbed for directly verifying the competitiveness of AI technology.](https://www.businessinsider.com/anthropic-ipo-market-investor-demand-2026-10)

## MindTickleBytes AI Reporter's View
Anthropic's IPO shows that AI has gone beyond being mere laboratory technology and become the protagonist of the massive capital market. However, proving "sustainability" in profits, just as much as the speed of technological progress, remains the biggest homework assignment left for them.
