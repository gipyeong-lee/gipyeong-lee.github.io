## What if there was an AI that remembered every single tree in your backyard?

Imagine this. You've become curious about how much the forest in the neighborhood you lived in 10 years ago has grown. Or, what if you wanted to take a 'time machine' and check satellite photos of anywhere in the world whenever you wanted?

Until now, to get the photos we wanted, we had to wait days or even weeks for an actual satellite to pass over that area and click a picture. Furthermore, if it was a cloudy day, the photos would often turn out a mess. But now, that's no longer necessary.

**AlphaEarth Foundations**, announced by Google DeepMind, is a kind of 'digital twin' (a replica of the real world implemented in a computer) that reconstructs the entire Earth into data. [AlphaEarth Foundations helps map our planet in unprecedented detail — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaearth-foundations-helps-map-our-planet-in-unprecedented-detail/) This amazing AI scours trillions of images to capture even the most minute changes happening on our planet. [Google AI model mines trillions of images to create maps of Earth 'at any place and time'](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-025-02412-1)

As if the Earth has become one giant living dataset, it records and tracks everything from crop growth rates and coastline changes to urban expansion and ice melt. It's as if a 'dedicated diary for Earth' has been created, meticulously recording every moment the Earth breathes. [Google DeepMind's AlphaEarth Tracks Earth's Changes - IEEE Spectrum](https://spectrum.ieee.org/google-deepmind-alphaearth-foundations-ai)

## Easy Understanding: AlphaEarth is a 'Virtual Satellite'

This is where the most innovative concept emerges: the technology of the **'Virtual satellite'**. [AlphaEarth Foundations: A "virtual satellite" to map Earth in unprecedented detail | Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/alphaearth-foundations)

### Explaining Virtual Satellites through Analogy
To see the moon clearly in the night sky, there must be no clouds, and the moon must be positioned above us. Satellite photos are the same. If it's very cloudy or the satellite is passing over the other side of the Earth, you can't take a photo even if you want to.

However, AlphaEarth studies countless photos taken in the past, along with weather and terrain data, to perfectly predict: **"If a satellite were passing over there and taking a photo now, it would probably look like this."** It shows the state of the surface as if a satellite were there, even when a physical one is not. Thanks to this, researchers can observe changes on Earth anytime and anywhere without needing to wait for a satellite to pass by. [AlphaEarth Foundations helps map our planet in unprecedented detail — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaearth-foundations-helps-map-our-planet-in-unprecedented-detail/)

### AI's Secret Language: 'Embedding Field'
AlphaEarth's way of understanding the Earth is a bit special. It doesn't just look at it as 'photos' but translates and stores all surface information into the AI's own secret language called an **'Embedding field'**. [AlphaEarth Foundations: An embedding field model for ...](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/DeepMind.com/Blog/alphaearth-foundations-helps-map-our-planet-in-unprecedented-detail/alphaearth-foundations.pdf)

To put it simply, it doesn't just record "it's green here" for a specific point on the map. Instead, it records it as a complex set of numbers with **64 different characteristics**. [Google launches 'AlphaEarthFoundations,' an AI formappingthe...](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20250731-google-deepmind-alphaearth-foundations/) These numbers are packed with information such as how fresh the vegetation is, how moist the soil is, and whether new buildings have appeared. The Red, Green, and Blue (RGB) colors we see with our eyes account for only 3 of these 64 pieces of information, meaning the AI is reading deep information that we cannot see. [Google launches 'AlphaEarthFoundations,' an AI formappingthe...](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20250731-google-deepmind-alphaearth-foundations/)

## Current Status: Looking into the Heart of Antarctica

AlphaEarth is already showing its capabilities in various places. Notably, it has precisely visualized the **surface of Antarctica**, which is difficult for humans even to approach. Researchers can now sit in a warm lab and closely observe where Antarctic glaciers are melting from and what the shape of the land beneath the ice is like. [Google launches 'AlphaEarthFoundations,' an AI formappingthe...](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20250731-google-deepmind-alphaearth-foundations/)

Furthermore, this AI doesn't just look at photos (optical images):
-   **Radar (SAR)**: Sees the terrain through clouds and darkness.
-   **Laser (LiDAR)**: Measures the height of trees and the depth of forests.
-   **Climate Data and Text**: Reads everything from temperature and humidity to local information.

It's like looking at the Earth while wearing several types of special glasses simultaneously, allowing it to identify the shape of the land beyond the clouds or the height of a forest with ruler-like accuracy.
