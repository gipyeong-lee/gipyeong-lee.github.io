## Imagine: A Colleague Who Listens and Takes Action

Imagine a highly capable colleague sitting right next to your desk. You say, "Could you organize this month's sales report and email it to the team leader?" While previous AI acted like a 'ghostwriter' that only polished sentences for the report, this new colleague actually opens Excel, gathers data, creates beautiful tables, opens the email window, and hits send.

A secretary who doesn't just talk smoothly but actually gets the 'work' done has arrived. On April 23, 2026, OpenAI introduced **GPT-5.5** and **GPT-5.5 Pro**, models heralded as opening a new horizon of intelligence. [GPT-5.5 - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.5) This announcement is particularly exciting because this smart AI has been officially released as an **API (Application Programming Interface)**, allowing developers to transplant this brain directly into their own services, going beyond just a chatbot service. [Introducing GPT-5.5 | OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/)

## Why Is This Important? AI Has Started 'Acting' Instead of Just 'Speaking'

While past AI models focused on providing plausible answers to our questions, GPT-5.5 is inherently different. OpenAI defined this model as **"a new level of intelligence for driving real work and agents (AI that thinks and acts autonomously)."** [GPT-5.5 is here! Available in the API, Codex and ChatGPT today - Announcements - OpenAI Developer Community](https://community.openai.com/t/gpt-5-5-is-here-available-in-the-api-codex-and-chatgpt-today/1379630)

The term 'agent' might sound a bit technical, but here's an analogy:

*   **Existing AI (Chatbot):** A **'cookbook'** that recites a recipe in great detail when you ask, "Tell me how to make kimchi fried rice."
*   **New AI (Agent):** A **'chef'** who, when told "I want kimchi fried rice," checks the fridge for ingredients, orders missing items from the market, and actually cooks and serves it on the table.

In simple terms, GPT-5.5 has the ability to understand complex goals, use tools like web searches and file manipulation directly, and see a task through to completion while checking its own work. [GPT-5.5 is here! Available in the API, Codex and ChatGPT today - Announcements - OpenAI Developer Community](https://community.openai.com/t/gpt-5-5-is-here-available-in-the-api-codex-and-chatgpt-today/1379630) We have entered an era where AI moves beyond just writing text to directly controlling computers or conducting in-depth research. [OpenAI announces GPT-5.5, its latest artificial intelligence model - CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/23/openai-announces-latest-artificial-intelligence-model.html)

## GPT-5.5 vs GPT-5.5 Pro: Which One to Task with What?

The newly released models are largely divided into two siblings:

1.  **GPT-5.5 (Standard Model):** The most accessible model, offering standard intelligence available immediately to ChatGPT paid users (Plus, Pro, Business, etc.). [GPT-5.5: Benchmarks, Safety Classification, and ...](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/gpt-5-5)
2.  **GPT-5.5 Pro (Professional Model):** Significantly smarter and more precise than the standard model. It is specifically designed for very demanding questions or professional tasks that require zero margin for error. [GPT-5.5 pro Model | OpenAI API](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-5-5-pro) [GPT-5.5: Benchmarks, Safety Classification, and ...](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/gpt-5-5)

Using corporate ranks as an analogy, **GPT-5.5 is a highly sensible 'versatile intern,'** while **GPT-5.5 Pro is a 'veteran manager'** with over 10 years of experience in a specific field. While an intern can handle simple report summaries or idea brainstorming quite well, the 'Pro' model provides much more reliable results for sophisticated tasks like reviewing complex legal clauses or identifying errors in large-scale systems. [GPT-5.5 Model | OpenAI API](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-5-5)

Performance metrics are also staggering. GPT-5.5 recorded dominant scores across 14 major **benchmarks** (the 'SATs for AI'), narrowly beating Anthropic's latest model, 'Claude Mythos Preview,' to reclaim the world's number one spot. [OpenAI's GPT-5.5 is here, and it's no potato: narrowly beats Anthropic ...](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openais-gpt-5-5-is-here-and-its-no-potato-narrowly-beats-anthropics-claude-mythos-preview-on-terminal-bench-2-0)

## Current Status: Meticulous 'Safety' Locks and a High Price Tag

Interestingly, while the model was applied immediately to ChatGPT for general users on April 23, the API used by businesses was released a day later on April 24. [GPT-5.5 - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.5) 

Why the one-day wait? OpenAI explained that because AI interacts directly with other programs in an API environment, it was necessary to implement **"different types of safeguards."** [GPT-5.5 - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.5) [Introducing GPT-5.5 | OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/) It was a process of fastening a sturdier 'digital seatbelt' to prevent the AI from inadvertently damaging systems or sending large amounts of data to the wrong places.

However, the cost of renting this powerful brain is significant. The price list for GPT-5.5 is as follows: [OpenAI Releases GPT-5.5: Faster, Smarter—And Pricier](https://decrypt.co/365333/openai-gpt-5-5-release-agentic-coding-benchmarks)

*   **Input (Information given to AI):** $5 per 1 million tokens
*   **Output (AI's response):** $30 per 1 million tokens
    *(A token is the unit by which AI reads and writes text—think of it as a small chunk consisting of a few words.)*

This pricing is approximately **twice as expensive** as the previous GPT-5.4 model. [GPT-5.5 is here: benchmarks, pricing, and what changes for developers](https://appwrite.io/blog/post/gpt-5-5-launch) As performance improved, so did the price tag, reflecting OpenAI's confidence in the high value of the tasks the AI can now handle. [GPT-5.5 is here: benchmarks, pricing, and what changes for developers](https://appwrite.io/blog/post/gpt-5-5-launch)
