Imagine this: the voice assistant on the smartphone we use every day or the artificial intelligence (AI) that helps us with our work has moved beyond simply answering questions to becoming the flagship product of a massive corporation valued at hundreds of trillions of won in the stock market. Now that AI technology has deeply integrated into our lives, Anthropic, one of the most prominent AI companies, is ready to take the stage on the massive platform of the stock market.

Anthropic is well-known as the developer of the AI model 'Claude.' This listing is expected to be one of the largest initial public offerings (IPO—when a company lists on the stock market for the first time and sells shares to the general public) in the midst of the AI frenzy. What exactly is it about Anthropic that has captured the market's attention so intently?

## Why does this matter?

This listing is more than just a company entering the stock market. It is a 'litmus test' that will determine just how much economic value AI technology can actually generate.

While some experts describe Anthropic's debut as a "once-in-a-generation opportunity," others express concern that it is a "massive financial gamble" [Source: [Anthropic to seek $1.4 trillion valuation in blockbuster IPO](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/10/investing/weve-never-seen-anything-like-anthropic-ipo)]. For the average person, this event serves as a reminder that AI technology is no longer just a fun toy, but stands at the center of a massive capital flow that will determine the future of humanity.

## Making it easy to understand

To truly understand Anthropic's value, we must first look at the concept of 'Constitutional AI,' their core technology. Simply put, instead of just feeding the AI vast amounts of data during training, they embed rules (a constitution) into the AI’s core—much like teaching a child with a moral textbook—stating that it must be helpful to humans and cause no harm [Source: [Anthropic's Dario Amodei Ignites AI Market with $1T Valuation](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/myredgarnet_anthropication-the-storm-of-dario-amodei-activity-7468126809297985536-Cc6S)].

Shall we compare this method to a 'filter' on a photo editing app? If a typical AI model focuses on flamboyantly changing the color palette of an entire photo, Anthropic's technology acts as a filter that makes intermediate judgments to ensure the photo is not too provocative or offensive. This ethical differentiator is highly attractive to investors and pension funds that prioritize ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) factors. While others are buried in a race for technological speed, Anthropic is gaining investor trust by creating a shield called 'safe technology' [Source: [Anthropic's Dario Amodei Ignites AI Market with $1T Valuation](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/myredgarnet_anthropication-the-storm-of-dario-amodei-activity-7468126809297985536-Cc6S)].

## Current Situation

Anthropic is currently going through the procedures to list on the Nasdaq (the US stock market centered on technology) under the ticker symbol 'ANT' [Source: [Anthropic IPO Valuation Could Be Slashed in Half to... - Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/382716/anthropic-ipo-valuation-could-fall-to-1-trillion-after-ai-stock-rout-report)]. Depending on market conditions, many reports state that Anthropic is aiming for a valuation of around $1.3 trillion [Source: [Techmeme: Updated S-1: Anthropic's seven co-founders will sell...](https://www.techmeme.com/261110/p32?ref=upstract.com)].

Of course, there is a divergence in expectations. As recently as this summer, some investors predicted a valuation of over $2 trillion, but sentiment has become more cautious due to the current volatility in the AI market [Source: [Anthropic heads for lower than expected $1.3 trillion valuation in...](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/crpdgd723x2zo)]. Nevertheless, Anthropic has an ambitious plan to raise up to $100 billion in funds through this IPO [Source: [Anthropic IPO Valuation Could Be Slashed in Half to... - Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/382716/anthropic-ipo-valuation-could-fall-to-1-trillion-after-ai-stock-rout-report)].

## What happens next?

Anthropic's listing, scheduled for Thursday, November 12, will be a crucial turning point that determines the atmosphere for the entire AI industry [Source: [Anthropic seeks $1.4 trillion valuation in highly anticipated IPO...](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/anthropic-seeks-14-trillion-valuation-in-ipo-130300928.html)].

Investors will be looking to verify whether the high valuation Anthropic proposes is merely a bubble driven by the AI boom or evidence of sustainable technological innovation. As readers, we need to pay close attention to whether AI companies can go beyond just showing off their technological capabilities to demonstrate transparent financial structures and build social trust. The technology is ready; now it is the market's turn to coolly evaluate and prove the true value of that technology.
