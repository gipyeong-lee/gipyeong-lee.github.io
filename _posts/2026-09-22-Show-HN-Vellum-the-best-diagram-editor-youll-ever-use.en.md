Imagine this: every morning, you say to your AI personal assistant, "Summarize my meetings from yesterday" or "Check my important schedule for today." But what if this assistant were sending all your private conversations and work materials to an unknown external server? Convenience is nice, but it is only natural to be concerned about security.

Recently, the name "Vellum" has been gaining significant attention in the AI industry. However, if you search for it, you might find book publishing software or a popular game. What exactly is the "AI Vellum" we should know about?

## Why It Matters

As AI technology advances, our private lives and work data are becoming easy "fuel" for making AI models smarter. Vellum, however, flatly rejects this practice. For the average user, the assurance that **"the conversations I have with AI are my own"** is crucial. Knowing that your information is not being indiscriminately used for model training brings great relief to modern people who value privacy above all else.

## The Explainer

Simply put, Vellum is like **"your own independent AI workshop."**

To use an analogy, if typical AI services are a "central kitchen" operated by a restaurant's headquarters, Vellum is like setting up "your own personal kitchen" in your home where you can safely store all your recipes and ingredients. Since you do not need to send your recipes to headquarters, there is no risk of information leakage.

1. **Local execution**: Vellum's AI assistant runs directly on your computer, not in the cloud. [Source: Vellum: Your Personal Intelligence](https://www.vellum.ai/)
2. **Thorough data security**: All personal information, such as conversation history, memory, and credentials, is managed safely without leaving your device. [Source: Vellum: Your Personal Intelligence](https://www.vellum.ai/)
3. **No learning**: Most importantly, the data generated by you in Vellum is never used to retrain AI models. [Source: Vellum: Your Personal Intelligence](https://www.vellum.ai/)

Furthermore, since Vellum is an open-source platform, users with technical knowledge can open the source code themselves to verify that data is truly not leaking externally. This is a major advantage that sets it apart from restrictive, large-scale corporate AI services. [Source: Vellum: Your Personal Intelligence](https://www.vellum.ai/)

## Where We Stand

As mentioned earlier, the name "Vellum" is currently used in several fields, so caution is needed.

*   **Vellum as an AI development platform**: This open-source LLM (Large Language Model) development platform we are discussing has security and transparency as its core values. [Source: Vellum: Your Personal Intelligence](https://www.vellum.ai/), [Source: LaunchHN:Vellum(YC W23)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=35042836)
*   **Vellum as book publishing software**: A tool used by authors to write and edit books. While free trials are available, it uses a structure where you must pay to export files as final products. [Source: VellumReview: Why IUsedto Recommend It (But No Longer Do)](https://kindlepreneur.com/vellum-software-review/)
*   **Vellum as a game**: Vellum is also the title of a recently popular action roguelite game (a game genre where you grow your character through repeated play) among gamers. [Source: Busted Build Roguelike That Constantly Impressed Me! -Vellum](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tXfrEbQtMKE)
