Imagine this: as soon as you wake up in the morning, you say to your iPhone, "Organize my schedule for today and tell me the three most important tasks." In the past, Siri (Apple's voice-activated AI assistant) would have simply opened your calendar app or told you the weather, but now, the AI model you trust most understands your speech patterns and work style perfectly to provide an answer. You're still using the same iPhone, but the "thinking brain" helping you inside it has been swapped out for one of your choosing.

According to recent reports, Apple has completely redesigned Siri's architecture for its next-generation operating systems, iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate. Now, iPhone users can replace Siri's default AI with external AI models they prefer, such as ChatGPT or Claude (a high-performance AI model developed by Anthropic) [Source 1](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/14/siri-can-be-swapped-out-for-chatgpt-claude/), [Source 8](https://www.humphreytheodore.com/writing/apple-wwdc-siri-google-gemini-claude-2026).

### Why Is This Important?

Until now, AI assistants were a "default without a choice." If you used an iPhone, you had to use Siri; if you used an Android smartphone, you had to use Google Assistant. However, this change completely transforms the iPhone from a single-assistant platform into a "multi-model platform" [Source 8](https://www.humphreytheodore.com/writing/apple-wwdc-siri-google-gemini-claude-2026).

This change is significant because control over AI usage has shifted from the manufacturer to the user. We used to have to adapt to the default settings determined by the manufacturer, but now we can make choices based on our own objectives. For example, you can attach Claude to Siri's extension functions when you need creative writing, or ChatGPT or Gemini when you need logical and quick information retrieval [Source 9](https://www.aitoolsmentor.com/blog/apple-ios-27-siri-extensions-choose-ai-model-2026). Users now have true agency to select the tool best suited for their situation.

### Simply Put: Artificial Intelligence as a 'Plugin'

To understand this technology easily, compare your smartphone to a "car" and the AI to a "driver." The existing Siri was a fixed driver built into the car. Now, however, Apple has standardized the car's steering wheel and navigation connection port, opening it up to anyone. This is technically referred to as "Model Delegation" [Source 1](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/14/siri-can-be-swapped-out-for-chatgpt-claude/).

Claude or ChatGPT now connect through this standard port. When a user says, "Organize my schedule with my iPhone," Siri forwards this request to the "AI driver" you have selected. The AI driver receives your schedule information through Apple's pre-defined "planner prompts" and "tool definitions," processes the tasks, and then sends the results back to Siri [Source 1](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/14/siri-can-be-swapped-out-for-chatgpt-claude/). The amazing thing is that regardless of which AI generated the response, the voice that answers and the screen interface remain the familiar Apple Siri. In other words, while it looks like Apple on the outside, the thinking on the inside is done by the best AI you have chosen.

### Current Status: Can You Start Right Away?

Apple has already established cooperative relationships with various third-party chatbot services. Starting with iOS 27, you can install the AI app of your choice from the App Store and change Siri's AI provider with a simple setting [Source 4](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/05/ios-27-third-party-chatbots-apple-intelligence/), [Source 9](https://www.aitoolsmentor.com/blog/apple-ios-27-siri-extensions-choose-ai-model-2026).

However, there are points to note. Experts point out that this change does not mean Siri will perfectly replace top-tier AI models in every area. Nilay Patel of the IT media outlet The Verge analyzes that while Apple's new Siri is clearly a very important step forward, it is not intended to handle complex coding tasks or compete directly with specialized generative AI models [Source 3](https://www.businessinsider.com/apple-siri-ai-chatgpt-openai-nilay-patel-interview-kafka-2026-6). In other words, Siri will focus on the role of a smart "assistant" that helps with the user's daily life, and more deep, specialized tasks will be supplemented by the model the user has chosen.

### What Will Happen in the Future?

In the future, smartphones will become personalized devices that provide completely different experiences for every user depending on which AI model they use. Developers can now build AI models specialized for specific tasks and link them with Siri, and we will increasingly learn how to "choose the right AI for me" instead of just "how to use an iPhone well" [Source 9](https://www.aitoolsmentor.com/blog/apple-ios-27-siri-extensions-choose-ai-model-2026).

When iOS 27 is officially released this fall, Siri will move beyond being a machine that simply carries out commands to perform its role in earnest as an "interface" that connects users to the best brain suited for their personal tastes [Source 9](https://www.aitoolsmentor.com/blog/apple-ios-27-siri-extensions-choose-ai-model-2026).
