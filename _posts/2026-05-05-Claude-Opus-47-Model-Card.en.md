Imagine this: A new team member arrives on their first day of work carrying a thick, 230-plus-page self-introduction that meticulously details how well they work, what mistakes they might make, and how they will act in dangerous situations. You might think, "Isn't this a bit much?" But at the same time, wouldn't you feel a sense of trust that this person is confident in their skills and highly responsible?

On April 16, 2026, Anthropic—an AI company often praised for building the "most honest and safe AI"—did exactly that. [I read all 232 pages of the Opus 4.7 system card - DEV Community](https://dev.to/ji_ai/i-read-all-232-pages-of-the-opus-47-system-card-28mh) Releasing their most intelligent AI model to date, **'Claude Opus 4.7'**, they simultaneously published a massive 232-page **'System Card'**. [Claude Opus 4.7 - Amazon Bedrock](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/model-card-anthropic-claude-opus-4-7.html) A system card is, essentially, a comprehensive "report card" and "user manual" documenting an AI's performance and safety.

Why did Anthropic go to such lengths to explain their AI through such an extensive document? And how will Opus 4.7 change our daily lives? We’ve stripped away the complex technical jargon to give you the essentials.

## Why is this important?

If the AI we’ve used until now was a "smart assistant that answers questions well," Opus 4.7 is closer to a **"professional colleague who leads complex projects independently."** [Claude Opus 4.7 | Awesome Agents](https://awesomeagents.ai/models/claude-opus-4-7/)

While most AIs focus on responding 'faster,' Opus 4.7 has chosen the path of thinking 'more accurately' and 'more deeply,' even if it takes a bit longer. [Working with Claude Opus 4.7](https://claude.com/resources/tutorials/working-with-claude-opus-4-7) This is designed to deploy AI into core business tasks where a single small coding error could lead to massive financial losses. [Introducing Claude Opus 4.7 \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7)

Notably, this model has made a leap in **'Agentic'** capabilities—the ability to judge and act independently. It goes beyond just summarizing information to performing long tasks that require multiple steps of complex reasoning. [Introducing Claude Opus 4.7 \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7) AI is no longer just doing what we tell it; it has reached a stage where it can create and execute a plan once given a goal.

## Easy Understanding: The 3 'Superpowers' of Opus 4.7

Let's look at how Opus 4.7 differs from its predecessor (4.6) through some familiar analogies.

### 1. Much stronger "muscles of thought"
Opus 4.7 examines problems from multiple angles rather than just rushing to an answer. Specifically, its performance in coding, which requires complex logic, has **improved by 13%.** [Claude Opus 4.7 | Awesome Agents](https://awesomeagents.ai/models/claude-opus-4-7/) In fact, the developer community is full of praise, calling its reasoning "surprisingly autonomous and creative." [Opus 4.7 - Out Now! - Release Discussions - Cursor - Community Forum](https://forum.cursor.com/t/opus-4-7-out-now/158192)

**Analogy:** If the previous model was a 'new hire' who only wrote code exactly as instructed, Opus 4.7 has become a 'senior engineer' who considers the overall system architecture and proactively defends against potential issues. [Introducing Claude Opus 4.7 \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7)

### 2. "Visual intelligence" is 3 times sharper
An AI's ability to read and interpret images or charts is called 'Vision.' Opus 4.7's visual resolution is **three times higher than before.** [Claude Opus 4.7 | Awesome Agents](https://awesomeagents.ai/models/claude-opus-4-7/)

**Analogy:** It’s like someone who used to struggle to read distant signs through blurry glasses now wearing the clearest modern lenses, enabling them to accurately read tiny text on complex blueprints or precise values on detailed graphs.

### 3. Ability to adjust "persistence" and "budget"
A special mode called 'xhigh effort' has been added to this model. [Claude Opus 4.7 | Awesome Agents](https://awesomeagents.ai/models/claude-opus-4-7/) Users can now request, "This problem is really important, so use maximum energy to think deeply about it." Additionally, the 'Task Budgets' feature allows users to pre-set how much the AI can spend on a single task. [Claude Opus 4.7 | Awesome Agents](https://awesomeagents.ai/models/claude-opus-4-7/)

**Analogy:** A runner who used to only sprint at full speed has now gained the ability to 'pace themselves.' It can answer simple questions efficiently to save energy and pour all its strength into problems that require critical decisions.

*   **Reward Hacking Prevention:** They thoroughly monitored whether the AI lies or uses shortcuts just to be praised by humans. [AI Model Catalog | Microsoft Foundry Models](https://ai.azure.com/catalog/models/claude-opus-4-7)

*   **Computer Use Safety:** They checked security risks that could arise when the AI directly operates a mouse and keyboard like a human. [AI Model Catalog | Microsoft Foundry Models](https://ai.azure.com/catalog/models/claude-opus-4-7)

## What Lies Ahead?

The emergence of Opus 4.7 will be a significant turning point in the evolution of AI from a 'simple tool' to a 'dependable agent.'

To put it in perspective: Previously, you had to say, "Write a report using last month's sales data." Now, you can give complex instructions like, **"Analyze the sales from the past year to find the cause of the slump, create a presentation with improvement plans, email the team members, and even schedule a meeting."** [ClaudeOpus4.7](https://overchat.ai/models/claude/claude-opus-4-7)

Through this model, Anthropic aims to prove that AI can be more than just smart—it can be a 'colleague humans can trust with their work.' The vast 232-page report is a kind of promise the AI makes to humans to build that trust. [Model System Cards - Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/system-cards)
