Imagine this: You're lying in bed before sleep and turn on your smartphone. Habitually, you open 'Hacker News,' a site filled with the latest news in the IT industry, but you find yourself squinting due to the bright light of the white background pouring across the screen. Is this discomfort, which many users experience every night, something that really needs to persist until today, in 2026?

### Why It Matters

Hacker News is one of the most influential communities where developers and IT experts from around the world exchange information. However, contrary to the orientation of this community, the site's user experience (UX, the total experience a user feels while using a product) still remains in the past. Many users read Hacker News daily, but viewing a bright screen in a dark environment is harmful to eye health and causes fatigue. This is not just a matter of personal preference, but a story about the 'digital welfare' of the countless people who use this site every day. [Source 15](https://hn-next.vercel.app/s/49268396)

### The Explainer

Dark mode (a setting that reduces the amount of light by darkening the background and brightening the text color) is similar to adjusting the aperture of a camera. Simply put, just as our eyes are dazzled when we look at bright photos in a dark room, the bright background of a website transmits too much light to our eyes at night. Dark mode filters this amount of light appropriately to minimize irritation to the eyes. Metaphorically, it protects the eyes on the same principle as wearing sunglasses in bright daylight.

Currently, there is no such filter on Hacker News. Experts agree that in 2026, a 'three-way toggle' (a feature where users can manually select a mode or set it to switch automatically according to the user's operating system settings) is the standard best practice for websites. [Source 11](https://vue-hackernews-ssr-5cavbdjcta-ew.a.run.app/item/45911105) However, Hacker News still remains outside of this standard flow. [Source 11](https://vue-hackernews-ssr-5cavbdjcta-ew.a.run.app/item/45911105), [Source 15](https://hn-next.vercel.app/s/49268396)

### Where We Stand

Fortunately, users who feel the discomfort have not just sat idly by. They found solutions for themselves.

First, there is the method of using browser extensions. Extensions like 'HNDarkMode' can be used in browsers such as Chrome, allowing users to keep dark mode on as they wish or synchronize it with operating system settings. [Source 1](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/hn-dark-mode/abpjflhoidbeaacggmdjoahiakcledna), [Source 3](https://appsupports.co/1611040838/hn-dark-mode), [Source 8](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/hn-dark-mode/abpjflhoidbeaacggmdjoahiakcledna?hl=fr_ca)

Second, there is the method of using entirely different tools. By using independent, read-only apps or readers created by community developers, such as 'HN Dark,' users can obtain Hacker News information while enjoying the benefits of dark mode. [Source 6](https://hn.sysopscafe.com/) However, because this is not official support, there may be some differences from the original site experience. [Source 6](https://hn.sysopscafe.com/)

### What's Next

Despite the continued demands of the community, official changes to Hacker News still seem far off. Many users are raising their voices, saying, "Dark mode is truly urgent now," [Source 11](https://vue-hackernews-ssr-5cavbdjcta-ew.a.run.app/item/45911105) but the pace of change is very slow. More third-party tools will continue to appear, but what users ultimately desire is an official environment where they can read information comfortably without plugins. [Source 4](https://apps.apple.com/by/app/hn-dark-mode/id1611040838)

### MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter View

It is paradoxical that Hacker News, which deals with the forefront of technology, does not introduce the most basic and popular user convenience feature. Isn't thinking about a healthy environment for the people who enjoy that technology, just as much as the technology itself, a part of 'technical maturity'?
