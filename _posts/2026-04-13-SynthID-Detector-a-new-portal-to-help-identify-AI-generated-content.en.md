Today, we live in a world where it is hard to believe even what we see with our own eyes. As we scroll through social media, we often encounter amazing photos or videos that make us wonder, "Did this really happen?" Some are so sophisticated that even experts are left in awe.

As Artificial Intelligence (AI) technology advances at the speed of light, anyone can now create imaginary scenes as if they were real. However, like two sides of a coin, we are now lost between 'real' and 'fake.' We have entered a tiring era where we must doubt whether the news we see is a fact or a sophisticatedly crafted fake.

In this chaotic digital sea, Google has handed us a reliable compass. It is a new verification tool called **'SynthID Detector.'** Let's take a look at what this tool is and why it will bring revolutionary changes to our digital lives, as if a kind friend were explaining it to you.

## Why is this important to us?

Imagine this. One morning, you find a very shocking photo in the online news. It might be a photo of a celebrity you respect engaging in inappropriate behavior they would never do, or a vivid photo showing a major accident suddenly occurring in a peaceful city. What would happen if you believed this photo without knowing it was cleverly created by AI? Misinformation would spread in an instant, and social trust would crumble like a sheet of paper.

Google's ambitiously unveiled SynthID Detector was created specifically to resolve these anxieties. This tool is a **'Verification Portal'** (a website for confirmation) that identifies images, videos, and text created with Google's AI tools [Google's new SynthID Detector can help spot AI slop](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/20/googles-new-synthid-detector-can-help-spot-ai-slop/).

There is a phenomenon we often encounter online called 'AI Slop.' It refers to the indiscriminate distribution of low-value or manipulated AI-generated content. SynthID Detector acts as a shield to protect us from this 'AI trash.' By transparently disclosing how the information we see was generated, it takes on the role of a 'digital appraiser' for the digital world [SynthID Detector: Identify content made with Google's AI tools](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/google-synthid-ai-content-detector/).

## Easy Understanding: The 'Invisible Seal' of the Digital World

Then how does SynthID Detector figure out what was made by AI? The core secret lies in a magical technology called **'Watermarking.'**

A watermark originally refers to an invisible pattern that appears only when banknotes or important documents are held up to the light. The principle of Google's SynthID technology is very similar. When AI creates an image or video, it embeds tiny digital marks across the entire content that are absolutely invisible to the human eye but can be read by a computer [SynthID — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/synthid/).

Let's use some simpler analogies:

> **Analogy 1: Special Glasses and a Secret Seal**
> All Google AI stamps a very transparent 'secret seal' on the back of its creations. No matter how much we look at it, it just looks like white paper, but the principle is that when viewed through 'special glasses' called SynthID Detector, that seal stands out clearly like a fluorescent color.

> **Analogy 2: A Chef's Hidden Recipe**
> It's like a chef who makes food that looks ordinary on the outside but secretly adds a specific spice blend that only they know. Later, an appraiser with absolute taste (Detector) who tastes the food immediately realizes, "Aha, this flavor came from Google's kitchen!"

Simply put, SynthID Detector precisely finds these hidden patterns not just in pictures, but also in videos where dozens of frames pass by in a second, and even in clever writing (text) written by AI [Google announces SynthID Detector that identifies AI-generated content ...](https://www.neowin.net/news/google-announces-synthid-detector-that-identifies-ai-generated-content/). If it's content generated by Google's AI models, its hidden identity is immediately revealed the moment it is uploaded to this portal [Google Launches SynthID Detector - A Revolutionary AI Detection Tool](https://techreport.com/news/software/google-synthid-detector/).

## Current Situation: Who can use it and how?

Google first announced this technology to the world on the stage of 'Google I/O 2025' (Google's annual developer conference) held on May 20, 2025 [Google's new SynthID Detector can help spot AI slop](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/20/googles-new-synthid-detector-can-help-spot-ai-slop/). Currently, it's not at a stage where everyone can use it freely like going to a local convenience store; it has been released first to a small group of selected experts for safe verification [Google Launches SynthID for AI Content Detection](https://itbusinesstoday.com/tech/ai/google-unveils-synthid-to-detect-generative-ai-content/).

Here is a summary of the key situation to date:

1.  **Identification Target**: It can accurately pick out images, videos, and text generated through Google's own AI tools (such as Gemini) [Google announces SynthID Detector that identifies AI-generated content ...](https://www.neowin.net/news/google-announces-synthid-detector-that-identifies-ai-generated-content/).
2.  **Access Rights**: A waiting list is being operated for media professionals (reporters, etc.) fighting fake news and researchers studying AI safety, granting access in order [Google Launches SynthID for AI Content Detection](https://itbusinesstoday.com/tech/ai/google-unveils-synthid-to-detect-generative-ai-content/).
3.  **Final Goal**: To prevent AI from being used as a malicious tool like deepfakes (AI-based image synthesis technology) and to give us confidence that the information we see is 'real' [SynthID: Tools for watermarking and detecting LLM-generated Text ...](https://ai.google.dev/responsible/docs/safeguards/synthid).

Of course, this technology is not a magic wand that eliminates all lies in the world at once. As Google itself admits, it may be difficult to 100% perfectly block out very carefully prepared malicious attackers [SynthID: Tools for watermarking and detecting LLM-generated Text ...](https://ai.google.dev/responsible/docs/safeguards/synthid). However, if combined with other security technologies in layers, it is clear that it will serve as the strongest 'front-line defense' against AI generation being misused for crime or agitation.

We will face even more sophisticated AI-generated content every day. Our question is now moving beyond simply "Is this fake?" to the fundamental transparency of "Through what process did this information come to us?"

The emergence of SynthID Detector is a historic first step toward reclaiming our 'right to know' about the content we encounter online. By clearly knowing that the touching video I'm watching is actually a creation made by Google's AI, we gain the power to make wiser judgments without accepting information uncritically [SynthID Detector: Identify content made with Google's AI tools](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/google-synthid-ai-content-detector/).

In the future, Google plans to further advance this technology and apply it across the services we use daily, such as YouTube and search results [SynthID: Tools for watermarking and detecting LLM-generated Text ...](https://ai.google.dev/responsible/docs/safeguards/synthid/). These efforts to restore trust in the digital world will ultimately create an environment where we can safely enjoy the powerful tool of AI without fear [SynthID — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/synthid/).
