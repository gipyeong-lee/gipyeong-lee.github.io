Just a few years ago, we were amazed simply by asking questions to artificial intelligence (AI) and receiving plausible answers. It felt like talking to an incredibly smart encyclopedia. But now, the role of AI is fundamentally changing. Google's recently announced **Gemini 2.0** declares that AI has evolved beyond the level of simply answering our questions into a 'capable assistant' that handles complex tasks on our behalf. [Introducing Gemini 2.0: our new AI model for the agentic era](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/google-gemini-ai-update-december-2024/)

Today at MindTickleBytes, we'll break down why this new AI model ambitiously released by Google is important and what practical changes it will bring to our lives.

## Why It Matters

Until now, AI has primarily been a passive tool that 'reacts' to what we tell it to do. If you said, "Summarize this text," it would produce a summary, and if you said, "Tell me tomorrow's weather," it would find the information. But Google now says the **'Agentic'** era—where AI judges and acts on its own—has arrived. [Introducing Gemini 2.0: our new AI model for the agentic era](https://news-tech.io/en/news/introducing-gemini-20-our-new-AI-model-for-the-agentic-era)

What is **Agentic AI**? Simply put, it refers to the ability of AI to think (Reasoning) like a human, set a plan (Planning) to achieve a goal, and actually take the necessary actions (Action). [Gemini 2.0: New AI model for the agentic era](https://www.nextbuild.in/news/google-gemini-ai-update)

To use an analogy, if previous AI was a 'calculator' that only moved as instructed, Agentic AI has become a 'professional assistant' that finds and handles work on its own.

**Imagine this.** What if you ordered, "Book a good restaurant near Seoul for me to go with friends this weekend"?
- **Past AI**: Recommends a few restaurant lists and the conversation ends. Ultimately, the user has to make the reservation themselves.
- **Agentic AI**: Checks the latest reviews with Google Search, calculates travel time on Google Maps, and chooses the most suitable place considering my calendar schedule and friends' preferences. It doesn't stop there but goes as far as connecting to the reservation page or attempting to make the reservation.

This evolution of AI from a simple tool to a true collaborator is the core of Gemini 2.0. [Introducing Gemini 2.0 Revolutionizing AI for the Agentic](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/introducing-gemini-20-revolutionizing-ai-agentic-era-saurabh-anand-hcyic) Google CEO Sundar Pichai emphasized that Google is going all-in on 'Agentic AI', a system that can reason, plan, and take action based on information. [Gemini 2.0: New AI model for the agentic era](https://www.nextbuild.in/news/google-gemini-ai-update)

## The Explainer

Gemini 2.0 is being evaluated as the most capable AI model in Google's history. [Google Launches Gemini 2.0, Multimodal AI Ushering in the ‘Agentic...](https://techeconomy.ng/google-launches-gemini-2-0-multimodal-ai-ushering-in-the-agentic-era/) What kind of technologies are inside that allow it to be called a 'capable assistant'? Let's look at it divided into three main features.

### 1. AI with Real 'Eyes' and 'Ears': Native Multimodal
The first feature is **Native Multimodal (the ability to process multiple types of information simultaneously)**. [Gemini 2.0: Google's New Model for the Agentic Era](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/blog/2024/12/gemini-2-0/)

While existing AIs had to borrow separate external programs to draw pictures or speak, Gemini 2.0's brain (model) itself is designed to directly generate and understand images and audio. [Google Gemini 2.0: News and announcements - The Keyword](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/google-gemini-ai-collection-2024/)

**Here's an analogy.**
> If previous AI was at a level where it barely understood the content of a foreign movie by running a subtitle translator, Gemini 2.0 has inherently acquired the ability to see, hear, and feel like a native speaker.

Thanks to this, Gemini 2.0 can directly create image and audio outputs, showing much more natural voices and reactions when conversing with humans. [Gemini 2.0 and the agentic era - LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/gemini-20-agentic-era-google-cloud-6b9bc/)

### 2. Directly Using Google's Powerful Tools: Native Tool Use
The second is that the AI knows how to use tools like Google Search or Google Maps on its own. [Google Gemini 2.0: News and announcements - The Keyword](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/google-gemini-ai-collection-2024/)

If a user says in an unfamiliar city, "Find a good restaurant that is near me now and reachable within a 10-minute walk," Gemini 2.0 immediately opens Google Maps to check the location and checks the ratings of currently open restaurants through real-time search. [Gemini 2.0: Google's New Model for the Agentic Era](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/blog/2024/12/gemini-2-0/) This means that AI doesn't just recite data learned in the past, but directly utilizes live information from the real world. [Introducing Gemini 2.0 | Our most capable AI model yet – JohnAi](https://johnai.co.uk/2025/01/14/introducing-gemini-2-0-our-most-capable-ai-model-yet/)

### 3. Deep Thinking, Lightning Speed: Gemini 2.0 Flash
One of the first models to be released, **Gemini 2.0 Flash**, shows overwhelming performance in terms of speed. It is said to be as much as **2 times faster** than the previous generation's top model, Gemini 1.5 Pro. [Gemini 2.0 Flash: An outstanding multi-modal LLM with a sci-fi...](https://simonwillison.net/2024/Dec/11/gemini-2/)

Being 2 times faster means more than just reduced waiting time. As AI can react immediately to our voices in real-time and handle complex multi-step tasks without delay, the completeness of a 'conversational assistant' that feels like talking to a real person has finally been achieved.

## What's Next

The future envisioned by Gemini 2.0 is a world where AI handles tasks silently but very capably in the background of our lives. By having AI take over numerous management tasks, information searches, and schedule adjustments that we found tedious, we will be able to focus on more valuable work. [Google Launches Gemini 2.0, Multimodal AI Ushering in the ‘Agentic...](https://techeconomy.ng/google-launches-gemini-2-0-multimodal-ai-ushering-in-the-agentic-era/)

However, Google places **'User Oversight'** as a core value in exercising these powerful abilities. [Google Launches Gemini 2.0, Multimodal AI Ushering in the ‘Agentic...](https://techeconomy.ng/google-launches-gemini-2-0-multimodal-ai-ushering-in-the-agentic-era/) It is a promise to ensure that AI does not judge and execute arbitrarily, but always operates safely under the user's control and confirmation.

We are now moving past the era of asking AI "What do you know?" to the era of asking "Can you do this for me?" Gemini 2.0 is the model standing at the very forefront of that change. [Introducing Gemini 2.0: Ushering in the Agentic Era of AI - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UO-vTsBns6Y)
