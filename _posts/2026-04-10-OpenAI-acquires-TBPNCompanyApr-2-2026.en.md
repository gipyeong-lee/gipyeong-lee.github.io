Imagine this. You are a chef who has invented a highly innovative cooking utensil. People are enthusiastic about how convenient it is, but at the same time, they worry, asking, "Is this really safe?" or "Will all the chefs lose their jobs because of this?"

Instead of simply improving the performance of the tool, you decide to buy a famous cooking-themed broadcasting station that people enjoy watching every day. Now, as a chef yourself, you can take the microphone and directly explain to people why this tool is safe and how the kitchen of the future will become more abundant.

**OpenAI**, the world's most famous artificial intelligence (AI) company and the creator of ChatGPT, has done exactly this. On April 2, 2026, OpenAI officially announced that it had acquired the popular tech talk show **TBPN (Technology Business Programming Network)** [OpenAI News | OpenAI](https://openai.com/news/). Why did the geniuses who used to make technology suddenly transform into owners of a 'broadcasting station'? We delve into the hidden intentions.

## Why Does This Matter?

This news isn't just a story about a "wealthy company going on a shopping spree." This is because the acquisition of a media company is a first for OpenAI since its founding [OpenAI acquires TBPN, the buzzy founder-led business talk show](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/02/openai-acquires-tbpn-the-buzzy-founder-led-business-talk-show/).

The reason this is important to us is that **the dominance over the 'story of AI' is shifting**. Until now, we have encountered the risks and benefits of AI through third-party media such as news or YouTube. However, the party that directly creates AI now owns the 'channel' itself that delivers that information.

To use an analogy, it’s as if a player has stepped onto the field wearing a commentator's microphone while other commentators were busy debating the referee's decision. OpenAI stated that through this platform, it will accelerate the global conversation about AI and communicate more directly with the people (builders) and companies that actually create AI [OpenAI acquires TBPN](https://openai.com/index/openai-acquires-tbpn/).

## Understanding the Basics: What is TBPN, and Why Was It Bought?

First, let's look at what **TBPN (Technology Business Programming Network)** is. It is a daily tech talk show and podcast hosted by two individuals, John Coogan and Jordi Hays [OpenAI acquires popular tech podcast TBPN - CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/02/openai-acquires-tech-podcast-tbpn.html).

They have built a solid fan base by covering the latest tech news daily and interviewing famous Silicon Valley executives. So, why did OpenAI choose them?

1.  **Influential Listeners**: TBPN has an average of 70,000 viewers per episode [OpenAI Buys TBPN Tech Talk Show, Puts It Under Top Lobbyist](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/04/03/openai-buys-tbpn-talk-show-puts-it-under-top-lobbyist-xcxwbn/). While the number might not seem huge, the key is that most of the viewers are tech industry leaders and founders. In other words, it is a channel to speak directly to the core figures who will spread 'word of mouth.'
2.  **Proven Profitability**: It is a self-sustaining medium that earned $5 million (approx. 6.7 billion KRW) in advertising revenue alone in 2025 [OpenAI Buys TBPN Tech Talk Show, Puts It Under Top Lobbyist](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/04/03/openai-buys-tbpn-talk-show-puts-it-under-top-lobbyist-xcxwbn/).
3.  **Strategic Value**: Although the official acquisition price is secret, the industry estimates that the deal was closed in the low hundreds of millions of dollars [Google News - OpenAI acquisition of TBPN talk show - Overview](https://news.google.com/stories/CAAqNggKIjBDQklTSGpvSmMzUnZjbmt0TXpZd1NoRUtEd2pFaXMzckVCRm9saEhSbUlVZzN5Z0FQAQ?hl=en-ET&gl=ET&ceid=ET:en).

There is one very interesting point here. Usually, when a company buys a media firm, they place it under the Public Relations (PR) or product team. However, OpenAI has placed TBPN under the **'Government Affairs' department and its Chief Lobbyist** [OpenAI Buys TBPN Tech Talk Show, Puts It Under Top Lobbyist](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/04/03/openai-buys-tbpn-talk-show-puts-it-under-top-lobbyist-xcxwbn/).

Simply put, they have overtly shown their intention to use this broadcast not just for product promotion, but as a 'strategic tool' to inform government policymakers and industry leaders of the legitimacy of AI and to form favorable public opinion [OpenAI Buys TBPN in Its First Media Acquisition | Awesome Agents](https://awesomeagents.ai/news/openai-acquires-tbpn-media-deal/).

## Current Situation: OpenAI’s Unstoppable Territorial Expansion

OpenAI currently possesses more financial power than ever before. Recently, they attracted a massive investment of **$122 billion (approx. 165 trillion KRW)** [OpenAI raises $122 billion fund for... | OpenAI](https://openai.com/ms-BN/index/accelerating-the-next-phase-ai/). 165 trillion KRW is a staggering amount, equivalent to the annual budget of many small to medium-sized countries.

With this vast amount of money, OpenAI is acquiring companies in various fields in addition to technological development. For example, they acquired 'LoveFrom,' the startup of former Apple design chief Jony Ive, for $6.5 billion (approx. 8.8 trillion KRW) [OpenAI Acquisition Archives - Cosmic Meta Digital](https://thecosmicmeta.com/tag/openai-acquisition/).

In other words, this acquisition of TBPN is one part of the grand picture OpenAI is painting. On one side, they are developing next-generation AI (the brain); on another, they are designing sophisticated devices for that AI (the body); and on yet another side, they have acquired a broadcasting station (the mouth) to explain the entire process to the public in a favorable way.

## What Lies Ahead?

Experts analyze OpenAI's move as a **"battle for dominance over the AI narrative"** [OpenAI Acquires TBPN to Control Tech Narrative | Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/openai-acquires-tbpn-to-control-tech-narrative-53fa38bf). This means there is a higher possibility that the AI stories we hear on YouTube or podcasts will flow in a direction that reflects OpenAI's intentions.

In the short term, new features of ChatGPT will likely be introduced first through TBPN broadcasts, or OpenAI's stance on AI safety will be delivered in a very logical and sophisticated manner. However, in the long term, the boundary between independent media and corporate-owned media will become blurred.

To use an analogy, just as we carefully check whether a restaurant review was written by the owner or a customer, we are entering an era where we must more meticulously consider "who owns the channel providing this information" when consuming it.

## AI Perspective

OpenAI's acquisition of TBPN signifies that AI has moved beyond being a simple tool to becoming a 'culture' and a 'political issue.' Now, AI companies must learn not only how to write good code but also how to win the hearts of the public and persuade governments. We must all keep a close eye on whether this 'microphone' held by OpenAI will become a tool to make the world smarter or a megaphone that only amplifies the company's voice.
