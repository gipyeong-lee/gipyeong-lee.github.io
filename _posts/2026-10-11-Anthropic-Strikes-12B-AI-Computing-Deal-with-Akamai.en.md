Imagine you are building an artificial intelligence (AI) that interacts with people worldwide and solves thousands of complex problems every second. However, this intelligent AI begins to run low on the computing power needed to act as its "brain" because it is processing too many questions. To solve this massive problem—one that cannot be fixed by simply buying a few more computers—AI firm Anthropic recently signed a 7-year, $11.6 billion cloud computing deal with Akamai Technologies. [Source 2](https://www.ainvest.com/news/akamai-signs-12-billion-ai-computing-pact-anthropic-2609/) [Source 3](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-akamai-ai-computing-deal/) Why are AI companies pouring astronomical sums into securing computing power?

## Why Does This Matter?

The AI models we use may seem to produce magical, instantaneous answers, but behind the scenes, an immense amount of mathematical computation is happening in real-time. If this computational infrastructure cannot be secured reliably, operating the AI service itself becomes impossible. [Source 6](https://www.ainvest.com/news/anthropic-12b-akamai-deal-real-tiny-cheap-chips-2609/) This agreement ensures that Anthropic has the robust resources needed to operate its cutting-edge AI services reliably for the next seven years.

Furthermore, this deal is attracting attention as a highly unusual case in the industry. Akamai linked equity to this agreement, the first time in the company's history it has done so for a cloud deal. This demonstrates that both parties consider this AI computing collaboration strategically critical, far beyond a simple transaction. [Source 13](https://aisheetreport.com/articles/2026-09-27-akam-3-as-anthropic-signs-11-6b-compute-deal-locking-the-cpu-cost-floor-beneath/)

## In Simple Terms: 'Leasing the Entire Engine of a Supercar'

To use an analogy, this deal is like "leasing the entire engine of an enormous supercar for seven years."

To train and operate high-performance AI, large-scale computation is essential, and as AI does more complex thinking, it requires more computing resources such as central processing units (CPUs) and graphics processing units (GPUs). Anthropic’s strategy is to leverage Akamai's distributed infrastructure to efficiently perform CPU-based AI tasks. [Source 13](https://aisheetreport.com/articles/2026-09-27-akam-3-as-anthropic-signs-11-6b-compute-deal-locking-the-cpu-cost-floor-beneath/) [Source 17](https://yusmpgroup.com/news/anthropic-akamai-cpu-cloud-deal/)

## Current Situation: The Scale in Numbers

*   **Potential Scalability**: There is the potential for the deal to grow up to $20 billion depending on circumstances. [Source 3](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-akamai-ai-computing-deal/) [Source 17](https://yusmpgroup.com/news/anthropic-akamai-cpu-cloud-deal/)
*   **Strategic Significance**: It is the largest deal in Akamai's history and a case showing that infrastructure providers are evolving beyond mere service providers into shareholders of AI companies. ## What Happens Next?

Through this deal, Anthropic is expected to dramatically increase the operational efficiency of its AI models and reliably provide more scalable AI services. In particular, this move to utilize not only GPUs but also a wide range of CPU-based workloads suggests that AI technology will be driven more efficiently across various environments and fields in the future. [Source 17](https://yusmpgroup.com/news/anthropic-akamai-cpu-cloud-deal/) As users, we can expect to experience smarter AI services in a faster and more stable environment.

A perspective from MindTickleBytes AI: This deal confirms once again that survival strategies in the AI era have expanded from merely writing good algorithms to an infrastructure race over "who can more reliably secure massive computational resources."
