Imagine a teacher’s day: after class, they spend 3–4 hours every day checking assignments submitted by dozens of students and creating creative materials for tomorrow’s lessons. These overwhelming tasks, which are hard to handle with a teacher's passion alone, are now being eased by a reliable AI assistant.

Recently, there has been a significant shift in the U.S. educational landscape. News has emerged that the 'ChatGPT for Teachers' program, which goes beyond being a simple tool to help students with homework, is expanding to more regions to help teachers dramatically improve their work efficiency and design more in-depth lessons. [Source: BringingChatGPTforTeacherstomoreU.S.schooldistricts | OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/bringing-chatgpt-for-teachers-to-more-us-school-districts/)

### Why is this important?

The introduction of AI in schools is becoming an essential change, not just a passing trend. It is a reality that many teachers are overwhelmed by vast administrative tasks and lesson preparation, leaving them lacking the 'creative time' to meet and communicate with students eye-to-eye. Through this expansion, more than 100,000 educators and staff across 55 education systems in the U.S. will have access to secure AI tools, professional training, and systematic support. [Source: BringingChatGPTforTeacherstomoreU.S.schooldistricts | OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/bringing-chatgpt-for-teachers-to-more-us-school-districts/)

If teachers can secure 'creative time' for lesson preparation, the benefits will directly return to the students sitting in the classroom. Also, the fact that AI can be used for education with peace of mind without worrying about data privacy by using security tools optimized for the school environment is a very big step forward.

### In simple terms, it's like this

Shall we use an analogy for the role of 'ChatGPT for Teachers'?

It is like having a **'24-hour on-call educational research assistant'** by a teacher's side. For example, if a teacher asks, "Create a science inquiry lesson plan suitable for a 2nd-year middle school level," the AI immediately creates the framework of the curriculum and suggests examples of questions that can stimulate students' curiosity. Just like changing the atmosphere in an instant by selecting a filter in a photo app, it helps teachers implement their desired educational goals more easily and quickly through the 'filter' of vast learning data.

This program is aimed at verified teachers in the U.S. education system from kindergarten to high school (K-12) and is provided free of charge until June 2027. [Source: Pricing | ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/ru-RU/pricing/)

### Current Situation

Currently, this program is rapidly integrating into the field, expanding its scope to 55 school systems in the U.S. [Source: BringingChatGPTforTeacherstomoreU.S.schooldistricts | OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/bringing-chatgpt-for-teachers-to-more-us-school-districts/) Separately, in the university education environment, the 'ChatGPTEdu' plan supports universities in independently introducing, researching, and utilizing AI. [Source: Pricing | ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/ru-RU/pricing/) This is a very strategic approach that considers that the role of AI required depends on the students' age and educational purpose.

### What will happen in the future?

In the future, the collaboration between educators and AI will become more sophisticated. Beyond simply creating lesson plans, it is expected that the feasibility of 'Personalized Learning' will increase, such as by understanding each student's comprehension level and instantly generating supplementary learning materials tailored to them. However, education on 'digital literacy' (the ability to properly utilize AI), where teachers learn how to use technology appropriately, will become more important to help AI function safely and correctly as an assistant in education.
