Imagine you need to write a long and complex letter to a friend. Until now, AI has functioned as if it were writing non-stop, one character at a time, worrying only about the next word. If it made a mistake, it was difficult to go back and fix it. But what if we could envision the entire content of the letter in our heads at once, and refine it while observing the overall context? Inception's upcoming next-gen AI model, 'Mercury 3,' is heralding just such an era. [Source: Batalla por Inception: Microsoft vs SpaceX en la carrera de IA](https://sudonull.com/es/battle-for-inception-microsoft-vs-spacex-in-the-ai-race)

### Why is this important?

Most AI we use today are 'autoregressive' models. Simply put, they predict the next word based on the word just written. However, this method has the drawback of easily forgetting the intention of the beginning if the sentence gets long, or committing logical errors.

On the other hand, Large Language Models (dLLMs) based on 'diffusion models,' such as Mercury 3, view the entire sentence at once. This is very similar to how we conceive the overall logical structure first and then fill in the details when we write. This means AI will be able to 'think' and write much more intelligently. [Source: DiffuCoder: Understanding and Improving Masked Diffusion Models...](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.20639)

### Easy to understand: AI resembling a photo editing app

To understand diffusion models more easily, think of the 'noise reduction filter' in a photo editing app. It is similar to the process of sprinkling a lot of messy noise on a photo to make its form unrecognizable, and then gradually removing the noise to find the original clear photo again. [Source: DiffuCoder: Understanding and Improving Masked Diffusion Models...](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.20639)

Language models are the same. AI starts with a jumble of words (a state mixed with noise). Then, through repetitive processes, it refines those words to fit the context (removing the noise) to complete a perfect sentence. In this process, the AI does not just see a part of the sentence but deals with the 'entire sequence' at once. Through this, it does not miss the overall logical flow and can continuously improve the content (iterative refinement) if necessary. Its true value is revealed especially in coding or long-form writing that requires complex logic. [Source: DiffuCoder: Understanding and Improving Masked Diffusion Models...](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.20639)
