Imagine a situation where you need to write complex programming code. In the past, you would have had to type hundreds of lines of code yourself, and if an error occurred, you would have struggled all night, scouring search engines. But now, AI is right there beside you, suggesting, "This part would be more efficient if you fix it this way," or even implementing the features you want on your behalf.

In this era of convenience, we must ask ourselves one essential question: "How far should AI assist with coding?" As AI models like Anthropic's Claude show amazing abilities as coding assistants, the debate over how to use this technology 'safely' is heating up.

### Why is this important?

As the role of AI in our daily lives grows, the principles by which the AI we use operates become extremely important. This is especially true in professional fields like coding, where AI is standing in the position of a 'collaborator' rather than just a convenient tool. What if AI ignores security rules or is used to indiscriminately clone unauthorized technology? This not only infringes on the rights of creators but can also undermine trust in the entire software ecosystem. Therefore, corporations like Anthropic contemplating 'how to make AI safe' is a matter directly linked to the safety of all of us who use AI [Source: Home Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/).

### Easy to understand: AI's Compass, Constitutional AI

'Claude,' created by Anthropic, is not just a simple chatbot. They teach AI in a unique way called 'Constitutional AI' [Source: Claude by Anthropic](https://claude.com/).

It is easy to compare it this way: If a general AI is an 'obedient child who listens to everything,' Claude, with Constitutional AI, is like a 'smart friend who always carries a compass of moral and safety rules.'

1. **Compass (Constitution):** Anthropic provides AI with clear guidelines on what is safe, what is dangerous, and what is accurate.
2. **Learning (Constitutional AI):** Following this compass, the AI reviews and improves its own responses.

In other words, tools like Claude Code are not just machines that quickly write code, but partner roles that help developers code in the right direction based on these safety guidelines [Source: GitHub - anthropics/claude-code](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code).
