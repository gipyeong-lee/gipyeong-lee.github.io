Imagine waking up in the morning and saying to your AI, "Look through all my tasks for today, organize the complex meeting materials, and get them ready to send to my team." If the AI of the past was a "smart library librarian" that simply searched for information, it is now evolving into a "capable secretary" that directly reads documents, understands content, and drafts emails.

OpenAI recently announced its new AI model series, **GPT-5.6**, foreshadowing this shift ([Source: OpenAI Announcement](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/)). Their strategy is to open an era of "efficient intelligence" that goes beyond just being smart, providing practical help in our lives and work environments.

## Why is this important?

Until now, the AI race has focused on who could build the largest model. It was like being obsessed only with making bigger and flashier engines. However, for companies, even if an AI is brilliant, it is difficult to adopt if operating costs are too high.

GPT-5.6 is an attempt to solve this dilemma. OpenAI has introduced a **"Specialized AI models"** strategy, allowing users to choose according to their purpose instead of sticking to one massive model ([Source: Aditi Shashikhare Tech Blog](https://aditishashikhare.substack.com/p/gpt-56-scaling-frontier-intelligence)). This means increasing the performance-to-cost ratio—or "intelligence efficiency"—to enable more companies and users to integrate cutting-edge AI into their practical work ([Source: AI Critique](https://www.aicritique.org/us/2026/07/12/gpt-5-6-and-the-fight-over-frontier-AI-access/)).

## Easy to understand: Intelligence has "weight classes"

The GPT-5.6 series is divided into three tiers based on usage: **Sol, Terra, and Luna** ([Source: Vellum AI](https://www.vellum.ai/blog/gpt-5-6-benchmarks-explained)).

To use a simple analogy, it's like choosing the right outfit for the occasion:
- **Sol** is the "flagship model" boasting the highest performance. Like a suit worn to highly professional meetings or formal events, it is optimized for tasks requiring a high degree of expertise, such as complex scientific research or coding ([Source: Rugpt.io](https://rugpt.io/gpt-5-6-sol), [Source: Rozetked](https://rozetked.me/news/47113-openai-vypustila-modeli-serii-gpt-5-6)).
- **Terra** and **Luna** are "casual wear" suited for lighter tasks. These models are much more economical and efficient for routine document drafting or research.

If previous AI models required wearing a heavy suit in every situation, GPT-5.6 allows you to choose the right model according to the difficulty of the task ([Source: Aditi Shashikhare Tech Blog](https://aditishashikhare.substack.com/p/gpt-56-scaling-frontier-intelligence)). Furthermore, the newly announced **"ChatGPT Work"** mode provides "AI Agent" features that help the AI perform complex, multi-step tasks on its own, further enhancing its practical utility ([Source: 3DNews](https://3dnews.ru/1144874/openai-vipustila-gpt56-i-nauchila-chatgpt-vipolnyat-mnogoetapnie-rabochie-zadachi-v-regime-wor)).

## Current Situation: The era of "Intelligence cost-effectiveness"

According to the "Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index," a current AI evaluation metric, the GPT-5.6 Sol model demonstrates intelligence comparable to the industry-leading Claude Fable 5 ([Source: Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gpt-5-6-has-landed)). A particularly notable point is that while performance is similar, the price is twice as cheap as Claude Fable 5 ([Source: Rozetked](https://rozetked.me/news/47113-openai-vypustila-modeli-serii-gpt-5-6)).

Of course, using a model to its maximum performance incurs costs based on usage. For example, for the GPT-5.6 Terra (xhigh) model, fees are charged per token (the unit of text processed by the AI) depending on usage ([Source: Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gpt-5-6-terra-xhigh)). As technology advances, users' options have expanded, but for companies, having the discernment to choose the most economical model tailored to their work characteristics has become important.

## What happens next?

Moving forward, the important topic will be less about "which is the smartest AI" and more about "which AI helps my work the best." As OpenAI has shown through GPT-5.6, AI models will become more specialized and segmented. Readers, don't just use AI as a "search tool" to ask questions. The day is not far off when you will use it as a reliable "digital secretary" that handles your daily tasks step by step.

## AI's Opinion

GPT-5.6 shows that AI technology is shifting from a competition of sheer scale to one centered on practical value. Companies can now achieve higher productivity by finding the "most suitable model" rather than the "largest model." This will be a significant turning point in accelerating the democratization of AI.
