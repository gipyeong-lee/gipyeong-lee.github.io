Imagine you are tending a massive garden. You need water to grow it, but the price of water is starting to become unaffordable. At first, it was just a few small pots, but now that you have to manage the entire garden, your water bill is skyrocketing every month. To make matters worse, you are warned that you don't know if this water will make the garden beautiful or cause a flood that swallows your house.

Recently, the IPO filing for Anthropic—a giant in the artificial intelligence industry—has left Wall Street and the tech market grappling with exactly this dilemma. We take a look behind the scenes at what has made investors both anxious and expectant.

### Why is this important?

Anthropic is one of the most closely watched companies in the AI field today. This IPO filing is more than just a document showing a company's financial status; it transparently revealed just how much money it takes to complete AI technology and what kind of risks must be taken. [Anthropic IPO Filing (CNBC)](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/10/28/why-anthropics-ipo-is-risky-business.html) Investors have effectively confirmed the reality of the "risky business" that is the AI industry through this document. [Anthropic IPO Filing (The Sydney Morning Herald)](https://www.smh.com.au/business/markets/asx-set-to-edge-up-wall-street-climbs-anthropic-files-for-ipo-20261027-p618sc.html)

This news immediately shook the stock market. As Anthropic's massive infrastructure investment plan was revealed, some tech stocks fell sharply due to concerns over uncertainty. [Anthropic IPO and Tech Stock Decline (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rIh0cs4fX5c) On the other hand, the market showed a stark contrast in temperature, with related stocks rising as investors positively evaluated the immense growth potential of the AI industry. [Anthropic IPO and AI Stock Rise (Yahoo Finance)](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/us-stock-futures-edge-oil-100215774.html)

### Easy to understand: Anthropic's "Astronomical Gardening"

Let's explain Anthropic's situation with two metaphors.

**First, it's a "sand particle factory to build a supercar."**
The process of building an AI model is like painstakingly assembling tiny parts, each as small as a grain of sand, to build a high-speed supercar. However, astronomical costs are poured in every year to build the factories (data centers and computing infrastructure) to mass-produce these precise parts. Anthropic stated in its report that it would invest approximately $450 billion with technology partners over the next 10 years to secure computing power. This is an enormous scale, enough to give every person in South Korea about 8 million won. [Anthropic's Computing Costs (TheStreet)](https://pro.thestreet.com/market-commentary/ai-sees-weak-bounce-while-anthropic-filing-fails-to-resolve-concerns)

**Second, it's a "self-learning magic broom."**
Anthropic is using AI technology itself as a development tool to create smarter AI. [Anthropic's AI Development Method (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RRT3H9FUGy4) However, no one can guarantee what will happen if this magic broom (AI model) becomes uncontrollably powerful. In the filing, Anthropic itself warned that the technology it is developing could cause "catastrophic risks" to humanity. [Anthropic's AI Risk Warning (Hindustan Times)](https://www.hindustantimes.com/business/anthropics-ipo-filing-warns-of-catastrophic-ai-risks-to-humanity-101793034189794.html)

### Current situation: Huge revenue, but even larger costs

Anthropic's report card is surprising. It recorded approximately $16.3 billion in revenue in the first half of 2026 alone, which is a 14-fold growth compared to the same period of the previous year (approximately $1.1 billion). [Anthropic's Revenue Growth (The Motley Fool)](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/10/27/one-astonishing-element-in-anthropic-s-prospectus-that-investors-shouldn-t-ignore/) However, what stands out more than the revenue is the scale of the losses. It recorded a net loss of approximately $80 billion during the same period. [Anthropic's Losses (The Guardian)](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/oct/26/anthropic-stock-market-filing-takeaways) Even considering that one-off costs played a significant role, it clearly suggests that aggressive investment will continue for several years to come. [Anthropic's Investment Plan (Yahoo Finance)](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/why-anthropics-ipo-contrasts-the-cult-of-elon-musk-at-spacex-and-tesla-192424554.html)

### What will happen next?

Investors are meticulously analyzing the extensive list of over 90 pages of risk factors presented by Anthropic. [Anthropic's Risk Factors (Hindustan Times)](https://www.hindustantimes.com/ht-explainers/anthropics-ipo-filing-opens-the-books-on-what-it-costs-to-build-frontier-ai-and-who-pays-for-it-101793093926168.html) Anthropic emphasizes that it takes an "ethical approach that differs from typical companies" in dealing with AI risks.

We must watch how efficiently AI companies increase their revenue in the future, and how they safely manage the risks they have warned about while advancing their technology. This Anthropic IPO will be a milestone signaling that AI technology has moved beyond the lab-level idea stage into an adult stage accompanied by massive capital and heavy responsibility for safety.
