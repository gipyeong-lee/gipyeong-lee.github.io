## The Explainer

So what exactly is 'TapToyPia', and how was it made? To properly enjoy the true identity of this game, we first need to understand the magical concept known as a 'Demake'.

In the video game industry, 'Remakes'—where classic games with outdated graphics are repainted and recreated with the latest technology and flashy 3D graphics—are very common. However, a 'Demake' goes in the exact opposite direction. It is a fascinating approach that intentionally strips down and scales back modern, flashy, and complex games into extremely simplified systems or classic visual forms.

As an analogy: Imagine a popular dance track today filled with flashy electronic instruments and dazzling auto-tuned sounds. Now imagine someone stripping away all the flashy accompaniments and mechanical noises from this song, turning it into a calm 'acoustic cover' played with just a single worn-out acoustic guitar. Although the flashy packaging is entirely removed, the essential melody and emotion of the song strike our ears even more clearly. A demake, too, is the process of stripping away all complex tutorials and eye-straining visual effects, leaving behind only the 'essence of fun' that the game possesses.

One day, a developer using the ID 'memalign' observed someone intently playing a game called 'Pokopia'. Although it was a short observation time of just 10 minutes, a strong conviction formed in this developer's mind that, based on the unique, bouncing charm of Pokopia, they could create a demake version offering much simpler and more immediate satisfaction [ShowHN:TapToyPia| Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48255525).

The resulting creation is 'TapToyPia'. The most surprising advantage of this game is that users have absolutely no need to bother opening an app store, searching for the game, and downloading an app weighing hundreds of megabytes that eats up phone storage. Without any separate installation process, this game can be accessed and played instantly, anywhere and anytime, as long as you have a web browser [TapToyPia](https://memalign.github.io/m/taptoypia/index.html).

This offers an experience akin to skipping the hassle of buying a complex set of Lego blocks and struggling for hours with the manual to assemble it, and instead opening a magical 'pop-up book' where a three-dimensional castle springs forth simply by flipping it open. Users are instantly transported into that cute mini-world, which became a hot topic in the Hacker News [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/) community, with zero waiting time, just by clicking a single website link.
