Imagine this: when you need to write complex programming code or analyze vast amounts of data, instead of agonizing over it alone, you instruct, "Assemble a team of five smart assistants to handle this task." The assistants divide the roles among themselves and execute the work, leaving you only to verify the final output. Does this sound like a dream? No, it is the future that OpenAI’s next-generation AI model, 'GPT-5.6,' which has just been unveiled, aims to show us.

On June 26, 2026, OpenAI released its next-generation language model, GPT-5.6, in a limited capacity ([Source: OpenAI](https://openai.com/index.previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/)). Although not yet widely available to the general public, the changes this model will bring appear set to fundamentally alter how we use AI ([Source: Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6); [Source: Towards AI](https://pub.towardsai.net/tai-211-gpt-5-6-is-here-but-most-people-cannot-use-it-yet-321b6b9c0f3a)).

### Why is this change important?

Until now, we have been accustomed to a 1:1 conversation style with AI, asking questions and receiving answers—much like talking to a tutor helping with studies. However, GPT-5.6 has enabled 'team-based work.' This means it can drastically improve the quality of tasks that were difficult to resolve in a single conversation, such as drafting complex project proposals, professional software development, or large-scale data analysis. In particular, as it is scheduled to be integrated into the Codex app, a code generation tool for developers, we expect to see the most immediate impact on productivity in development environments ([Source: 9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/26/openai-upgrading-chatgpt-and-codex-with-new-gpt-5-6-models-in-limited-release/)).

### In simple terms, what are these models?

This GPT-5.6 lineup has been released in three versions, categorized by performance and purpose, much like car trim levels ([Source: APIMaster.AI](https://apimaster.ai/de/blog/gpt-56-sol-terra-luna-preview-2026)):

1.  **Sol**: The smartest 'flagship' model. It can understand a massive context window of 1 million tokens (the unit of information AI can process at once), making it suitable for complex problem-solving that requires reviewing dozens of books' worth of material at once ([Source: BenchLM.ai](https://benchlm.ai/compare/gpt-5-6-sol-vs-ternary-bonsai-4b)).
2.  **Terra**: A model that balances performance and cost. It pursues reasonable efficiency and is well-suited for performing daily tasks without difficulty ([Source: Meshlaunch](https://meshlaunch.com/fr/blog/2026-gpt-5-6-sol-terra-luna-release-guide.html)).
3.  **Luna**: The fastest and lightest model. It is optimized for tasks where speed is critical, such as simple summaries or repetitive automated workflows ([Source: Meshlaunch](https://meshlaunch.com/fr/blog/2026-gpt-5-6-sol-terra-luna-release-guide.html)).

The core feature to watch here is **'Ultra Mode.'** To use an analogy, Sol has moved beyond being a genius working alone to becoming a highly capable **'commander.'** When Ultra Mode is activated, Sol hires several small AI subagents in real-time ([Source: 9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/26/openai-upgrading-chatgpt-and-codex-with-new-gpt-5-6-models-in-limited-release/)). Just as in a company where one person handles planning, one handles coding, and another handles error checking, this collaborative system helped Sol record an astounding score of 91.9% on the 'Terminal-Bench 2.1' test, overwhelming existing models ([Source: Towards AI](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/tai-211-gpt-56-here-most-people-cannot-f9ksc); [Source: Agensi.io](https://www.agensi.io/learn/gpt-5-6-sol-terra-luna-skills-guide)).

Another interesting feature is the **'reasoning slider.'** Users can adjust the slider to directly determine how deeply the AI should "think" during the process of finding the answer—that is, the 'depth of reasoning.' You can make it respond instantly when you need a quick answer, or induce it to take its time and think more deeply when sophisticated analysis is required ([Source: TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/openai-might-be-preparing-gpt-5-6-for-next-weeks-release/); [Source: 9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/26/openai-upgrading-chatgpt-and-codex-with-new-gpt-5-6-models-in-limited-release/)).

### Where do things stand currently?

GPT-5.6 is currently only available for experience in a limited preview stage. OpenAI plans to expand access to a wider audience over the coming weeks ([Source: Towards AI](https://pub.towardsai.net/tai-211-gpt-5-6-is-here-but-most-people-cannot-use-it-yet-321b6b9c0f3a)). In particular, they are preparing for real-world deployment by prioritizing testing of Sol's powerful coding capabilities within the Codex development tool ([Source: Codex Knowledge Base](https://codex.danielvaughan.com/2026/06/26/gpt-5-6-sol-terra-luna-preview-codex-cli-model-tiers-pricing-ultra-mode-configuration/)).

### How will our lives change in the future?

Moving forward, the core of competition will shift from 'who has the smartest AI' to 'who manages AI agents most efficiently.' Developers will use Sol’s Ultra Mode to drastically reduce the time it takes to build complex systems, while general users will use models like Terra to delegate routine document organization and analysis tasks as if they were handing them to a personal assistant. Following the upcoming official release, we look forward to a future where AI becomes a dependable team member in our daily lives.
