Imagine this: every morning on your commute, and right before you fall asleep, you speak to an AI chatbot out of habit. As you converse with AI so naturally—asking, "Organize my schedule for today" or "Summarize this article"—you might suddenly wonder, 'Just how much do I talk to AI in a day?' or 'Am I really using AI productively?'

A new feature recently announced by Anthropic for Claude provides the answers to these questions. The star of the show is the 'Reflect' feature, which acts like a mirror, reflecting your AI usage habits back to you.

## Why Does This Matter?

We already manage our own digital lives by checking our smartphone usage time or verifying how much we use specific apps. The same applies to AI. While AI has become a part of our lives, ironically, it has been difficult to systematically grasp 'how' and 'how much' we are using it.

The newly released 'Reflect' feature goes beyond simply showing usage history; it allows users to objectively view the relationship they have with AI. [Source 7](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/anthropics-new-claude-feature-is-quietly-selling-you-on-ai/) It is essentially an 'AI health checkup' that helps you understand your habits and use it more healthily instead of using it indiscriminately. [Source 1](https://www.anthropic.com/news/reflect-with-claude)

## Making It Easy to Understand

Shall we use an analogy to make Claude’s Reflect feature easier to understand? It’s much like the 'credit card statement' we receive every month. Just as a credit card statement helps you plan next month's spending by showing how much you spent on food and shopping this month, Claude’s Reflect dashboard clearly shows which questions you asked AI the most and when during the day you sought out AI the most. [Source 1](https://www.anthropic.com/news/reflect-with-claude)

To use another analogy, it's like a 'smartwatch' that tracks your daily exercise. Just as you might look at your exercise logs and think, "I moved a little less today, so I should go for a walk this evening," the Reflect dashboard allows you to decide, "I used AI too much today, so I should clear my head a bit."

## Current Status

Claude's Reflect dashboard is now available to all users. [Source 3](https://www.testingcatalog.com/anthropic-adds-usage-reflection-dashboard-to-claude-for-all-users/) It is also very simple to use. Just go to the 'Settings' menu of the Claude app and select the 'Reflect' tab. [Source 3](https://www.testingcatalog.com/anthropic-adds-usage-reflection-dashboard-to-claude-for-all-users/)

In this dashboard, you can view activity statistics for the past 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, or 1 year. [Source 1](https://www.anthropic.com/news/reflect-with-claude) It provides analysis not only on overall usage but also on the main topics you discussed and how you utilized it by time of day, allowing you to grasp your usage patterns in a multidimensional way. [Source 1](https://www.anthropic.com/news/reflect-with-claude) [Source 5](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/963105/anthropic-claude-wrapped-reflection-ai-usage)

Additionally, for healthy digital living, it is equipped with features to set 'Quiet hours' or receive notifications to take regular breaks while working. [Source 3](https://www.testingcatalog.com/anthropic-adds-usage-reflection-dashboard-to-claude-for-all-users/) These are useful tools designed to prevent users from becoming overly dependent on or immersed in AI.

## What's Next?

This announcement demonstrates that 'user experience' and 'digital wellbeing' have become key topics, going beyond the performance competition of AI models. [Source 7](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/anthropics-new-claude-feature-is-quietly-selling-you-on-ai/) Moving forward, users will likely contemplate not how to use AI more, but 'how to use AI in a way that is beneficial to me.' Tools like Reflect are expected to become essential platforms that help users diagnose their AI utilization level themselves and, if necessary, take a break or adjust their learning direction.
