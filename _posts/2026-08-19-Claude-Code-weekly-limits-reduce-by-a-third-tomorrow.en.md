Imagine this: You’ve entrusted a complex coding task to your AI and are watching the screen, when suddenly a message pops up: "You have exhausted your usage for this week." It feels like your pen running out of ink right in the middle of an important document. If you’ve been a regular user of Anthropic’s coding tool, 'Claude Code,' you'll be facing a slightly different environment tomorrow morning.

## Why does this matter?

Claude Code is a tool that allows developers to delegate complex engineering tasks directly to AI within the terminal (the screen where you control the computer by typing commands). Simply put, you are working with a reliable AI assistant that deeply understands complex technical structures [Source 12].

Since May 2026, Anthropic has been running a promotion that increased weekly usage limits by 50% to allow users to utilize the AI more generously [Source 5]. However, as this benefit ends on August 19, you will reach your limits slightly faster than before if you continue working as usual [Source 3, Source 5]. This signals a necessary shift in workflow for developers who need to maintain consistent productivity.

## Simply put

Usage limits are like "communal kitchen ingredients available for the week." When there were more ingredients (usage), you could cook leisurely for several days, but starting tomorrow, it returns to the originally allocated amount.

In other words, the more work that Claude Code, your AI assistant, processes within your terminal, the faster the "opportunities to think (tokens)" allocated to us are depleted [Source 19]. To use an analogy, just as a photo editing app slows down the more complex filters you apply, in AI coding, the more you call upon specialized AI sub-agents or use tools in a complex way, the faster your usage is consumed [Source 19].

## Current situation

Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based enterprise plan users have been enjoying these benefits [Source 5]. Anthropic has extended this promotion several times, but it is now returning to normal limit ranges [Source 3].

If you are curious about your current usage, you can check the details—including your current session's 5-hour usage, remaining time, and when your weekly limit resets—via the usage limit guide provided by the Claude Help Center [Source 9].

If you feel your usage is being consumed too quickly, here is a simple tip you can apply right away: try typing the `/clear` command in Claude Code. This command cleans out the ongoing conversation and resets the context window to its initial state [Source 13]. Clearing unnecessary conversation history before starting important tasks can significantly help improve your work efficiency.

## What happens next?

As AI technology advances, models are becoming smarter, but the computing resources they consume are also increasing [Source 19]. Moving forward, it will become important for users to develop optimal AI usage habits tailored to their work style. Rather than sending a large number of requests blindly, a "smart approach" of calling upon the AI focused on essential engineering tasks will be necessary.
