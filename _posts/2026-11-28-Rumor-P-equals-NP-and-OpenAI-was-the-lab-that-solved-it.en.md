## 1. The Epicenter of the Rumor: What is P=NP and Why Were People Enthralled?

In computer science, the **P=NP** problem is fundamentally asking: **"Is finding an answer yourself (P) truly the same difficulty as verifying whether an answer provided by someone else is correct (NP)?"**

To use an analogy, finding an exit in a massive maze is incredibly difficult and takes a huge amount of time (P). However, when someone hands you a map and the correct answer beforehand saying, "Go this way," verifying whether that path actually leads to the maze exit is completed very quickly (NP). If P and NP were equal, it would mean that all difficult and complex problems in the world could actually be solved very easily. Countless foundational systems supporting modern society's security—such as cryptography, new drug development, and global logistics optimization—would be completely upended.

The rumor that "OpenAI solved this P=NP problem" spread in some circles precisely because of this explosive impact. If true, it would cause a massive tectonic shift across all of human civilization. However, the exact truth was a bit different. The achievement accomplished by OpenAI and another leading AI research lab, Anthropic, was not a proof of P=NP itself, but rather an innovative feat that shook another massive hypothesis underlying the foundation of computer science.

Simply put, the computer science community long held a near-dogmatic belief that "certain complex problems can never be solved efficiently because the computational workload increases exponentially as data scale or time grows." OpenAI made this fact public on October 26, and Anthropic subsequently published similar results, posting its own paper and a Lean proof (a tool for computer-verified mathematical proofs) [ETH Disproof by OpenAI's AI: What Changes for PvsNP... | AIPOST](https://aipost.kr/posts/2026-10-28-openai-ai-refutes-exponential-time-hypothesis/).

## 3. Understanding Simply: How Did AI Overcome Mathematical Barriers?

So how on earth was artificial intelligence able to produce such complex and esoteric mathematical achievements in such a short time? Simply put, AI acted as an infinitely patient assistant, blazing through ultra-fast searches for valid clues among millions of potential paths that humans would otherwise have had to calculate and prove by hand one by one.

According to related reports, OpenAI's project in question began in earnest on September 1, and amidst intense academic trends surrounding the resolution of conundrums, it began challenging advanced mathematical and scientific problems [OpenAI fought dirty on career-making math problem... | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/openai-fought-dirty-on-career-making-math-problem-says-nyu-mathematician/).

Indeed, in this process, OpenAI's internal model turned the mathematical world upside down by **perfectly solving the 4-dimensional Kakeya problem and subsequently solving it in all dimensions** [Dueling proofs from OpenAI and Anthropic astound scientists | Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/dueling-proofs-from-openai-and-anthropic-astound-scientists/). The Kakeya problem is an esoteric geometric problem of finding the minimum area of space required when rotating a sharp needle 360 degrees within a small space. 

In addition, research sparks were ignited in the domain of **the Navier–Stokes equations**, a fluid dynamics conundrum originating from the research of 19th-century physicists Claude-Louis Navier and George Gabriel Stokes. Ever since Jean Leray proved the existence of solutions in a general sense in 1934, whether those solutions remain smooth at all times has remained an unsolved conundrum in modern mathematics [On the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize Problem | OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution/). In uncharted territories where human intellect has long stalled, AI provides novel computational clues, establishing itself as a robust companion to the scientific community.
