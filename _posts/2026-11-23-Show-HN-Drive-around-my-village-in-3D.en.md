## Understanding It Easily: How Did My Neighborhood Enter a 3D Game?

Then how could our real neighborhood be identically implemented right inside a computer screen? The secret lies in the 'combination of ingredients.' Just as cooking requires fresh vegetables and broth, this project was completed through the meeting of government-opened public data, map data, and an exceptional chef named AI.

Put simply, this process is like **'assembling a 3D map puzzle.'**

The first ingredient is **government LIDAR (Light Detection and Ranging, a technology that measures distance to a target by firing pulsed lasers) data** [Show HN: Drive around my village in 3D](https://cherwellvalleymotorclub.app). Lidar sensors fire lasers from the sky to precisely measure ground elevation, building heights, and tree locations, creating 3D spatial coordinate information. In other words, it provides the elevation and stereoscopic depth that form the 'skeleton' of the world.

The second ingredient is **OpenStreetMap (an open-source map created together by users worldwide)** data [Show HN: Drive around my village in 3D](https://cherwellvalleymotorclub.app). This map acts as a 'road network and boundary guide' indicating where roads are located and what building outlines look like. 

After bringing in these two precise real-world datas, the developer received strong support from an AI coding agent named **Claude** [Show HN: Drive around my village in 3D](https://cherwellvalleymotorclub.app). Instead of writing tens of thousands of lines of code manually, the developer conversed with AI to smoothly render this massive geographic data in a web browser and implement the physics laws governing car movement. It was essentially like having a genius AI acting as an invaluable assistant right next to an experienced architect.
