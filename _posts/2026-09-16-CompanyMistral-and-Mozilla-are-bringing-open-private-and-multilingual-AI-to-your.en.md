Imagine this: You are browsing the web as usual, a question pops into your head, and you pose it to a panel on the side of your browser. The AI provides an immediate answer, and without having to open a separate search results page, it shows you the website links that served as the basis for the answer. Furthermore, it promises not to take your personal information. It sounds like a dream, but this is the future Firefox is envisioning.

Mozilla recently announced that it will open an AI browsing era that prioritizes privacy, centered on user choice and openness.

### Why AI from Firefox?

Most AI services we commonly use offer intelligence in exchange for collecting data. However, Mozilla strongly opposes this familiar model. Mozilla is a non-profit organization that has made it a principle not to sell user data or use it for advertising. [Source: Firefox: The fast, private browser that keeps you safe](https://www.firefox.com/en-US/)

This move reflects a commitment to protecting user personal information while simultaneously providing a smart search experience, even in the age of AI. A browser is like a "window" we use to navigate the vast sea of the internet. As this window becomes smarter through AI, it is a crucial change for all of us that it remains a safe space that does not infringe on privacy. [Source: Announcing Firefox and Exa Partnership](https://blog.mozilla.org/en/firefox/firefox-exa-partnership/)

### Simply put, these are the features

Firefox's "Smart Window" is like a smart and honest assistant living inside your browser.

**1. Real-time information with reliable sources (Exa collaboration)**
Smart Window retrieves real-time information from the web. Through its collaboration with Exa, it doesn't just have the AI rack its memory for an answer; it accurately presents the website links that served as the foundation for the answer. [Source: Smart Window, privacy-first, AI-powered browsing with Firefox](https://blog.mozilla.org/en/firefox/firefox-smart-window/) In other words, it finds the pages containing the relevant information and unfolds them right before you.

**2. Privacy protection (Zero-data-retention)**
Mozilla's approach to AI aims for "zero-data-retention." [Source: Announcing Firefox and Exa Partnership](https://blog.mozilla.org/en/firefox/firefox-exa-partnership/) Simply put, it is a promise that the AI service will not store or use your questions for training.

**3. The user is the master of technology (Openness and choice)**
Mozilla wants users to have control over technology. [Source: Introducing AI, the Firefox way](https://blog.mozilla.org/en/firefox/ai-window/) They are building an environment where users can directly control the openness and privacy of AI without being dependent on a specific company's language model.

### What is the situation now?

Mozilla is continuously updating Firefox's AI features, centered on the Smart Window. Recently, three new features have been added to the Smart Window to refine the user's browsing experience even further. [Source: Firefox Is Rolling Out Three New Features for 'Smart Window'](https://lifehacker.com/tech/firefox-is-rolling-out-three-new-features-for-smart-window/) The most important point is that Firefox does not force all these AI features on you. All AI features are designed as "optional," which users can turn on and use only when needed. [Source: Firefox: The fast, private browser that keeps you safe](https://www.firefox.com/en-US/)

### What about the future?

Mozilla is aiming for an even smarter browser in the future. In the long term, they intend to move in a direction where the browser understands the flow of what the user has done and what they have searched for, providing more personalized help based on this. [Source: Firefox Is Rolling Out Three New Features for 'Smart Window'](https://lifehacker.com/tech/firefox-is-rolling-out-three-new-features-for-smart-window/) Of course, this entire process will also take place within strict lines that do not infringe on the user's privacy.

In an era where AI technology feels like the exclusive property of giant corporations, it is a very meaningful challenge for an organization like Mozilla to infuse AI with the values of "openness" and "privacy."
