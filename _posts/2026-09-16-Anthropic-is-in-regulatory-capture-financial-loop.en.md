Imagine you have just opened a sandwich shop with great care. A giant franchise restaurant then approaches the government and demands, "Shut down all shops that do not have massive hygiene facilities like ours." Ostensibly, they use 'hygiene' and 'safety' as an excuse, but their true intention is to block smaller shops and monopolize the market. The controversy currently unfolding in the AI industry is strikingly similar to this.

Recently, AI company Anthropic has been at the forefront of advocating for strong AI regulations to the government and Congress, leading to a flood of criticism in the industry that this is a 'regulatory capture' strategy. [Source: Anthropic Regulatory Capture Strategy - keybridgewireless.com](https://keybridgewireless.com/codex/read/groque/blog/anthropic-regulatory-capture-strategy)

### Why does this matter?

This issue is not merely a turf war between companies. AI is a core technology that will transform every aspect of our lives in the future. If a few massive corporations design government regulations to suit their own interests and eliminate competitors, we may forever lose the opportunity to experience more diverse and innovative services. [Source: Anthropic Regulatory Capture Strategy - keybridgewireless.com](https://keybridgewireless.com/codex/read/groque/blog/anthropic-regulatory-capture-strategy)

In fact, experts point out that Anthropic has increased its lobbying budget by 511%. [Source: What Anthropic's Washington Pivot Reveals About the AI Cycle](https://www.ainvest.com/news/anthropic-washington-pivot-reveals-ai-cycle-2606/) This is interpreted as a move to evolve beyond a simple tech company into a 'game designer' that sets the rules in Washington. This situation is highly likely to increase the costs for consumers to enjoy the benefits of technology and harm healthy market competition. [Source: What Anthropic's Washington Pivot Reveals About the AI Cycle](https://www.ainvest.com/news/anthropic-washington-pivot-reveals-ai-cycle-2606/)

### Simple Explanation: What is Regulatory Capture?

'Regulatory capture' simply means 'changing the owner of regulation.' Originally, regulation is created by the government to protect consumers, but when regulatory capture occurs, companies design the framework of that regulation themselves to create an environment favorable to them.

In Anthropic's case, the 'AI safety' they emphasize acts like a massive protective shield. [Source: Here’s Why Anthropic Is Pushing States to Regulate AI Faster](https://www.wired.com/story/why-anthropic-is-pushing-states-to-regulate-ai-faster/) Anthropic has the capacity to maintain vast capital and a giant legal team, but small startups or open-source projects lack the resources to comply with complex regulations one by one. In other words, it is a strategy to raise the regulatory threshold so that competitors cannot even get a foothold in the market. [Source: Anthropic Regulatory Capture Strategy - keybridgewireless.com](https://keybridgewireless.com/codex/read/groque/blog/anthropic-regulatory-capture-strategy)

Furthermore, independent researcher Kevin Bass calls this the 'Anthropic Network.' [Source: Anthropic Network Claim: METR Funding, Fact-Checked (2026 ...)](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/kevin-bass-anthropic-network-metr-funding-claims-2026) He argues that while certain AI safety non-profits appear independent on the surface, in reality, a significant portion of their funding depends on Anthropic stock (valued at approximately $7 billion), so they represent the company's interests and logic. [Source: Anthropic 的监管俘获金融闭环 — Anthropic is in regulatory-capture financial loop ...](https://zeli.app/zh/story/49705630) To use an analogy, it is a structure like a 'self-spinning wheel' where they support safety research to promote themselves, and then use the results of that research to justify stricter regulations. [Source: Kevin Bass: Anthropic builds an unshuttable regulatory ...](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2289859)

### What will happen next?

It is unclear whether such a strategy by Anthropic will continue to succeed. Industry critics and the open-source community are strongly resisting these monopolistic moves. [Source: Anthropic Regulatory Capture Strategy - keybridgewireless.com](https://keybridgewireless.com/codex/read/groque/blog/anthropic-regulatory-capture-strategy) The key is how regulatory authorities will judge the market distortion hidden behind the name of 'safety.' Readers should keep a sharp eye out whenever AI-related regulatory discussions arise, asking, "Is this regulation for the safety of us all, or is it to eliminate competitors for a specific company?"

### MindTickleBytes' Perspective

It is a natural duty for companies to be concerned about the safety of technology. However, if that excuse is used as a weapon to eliminate market competition, we may face an 'age of monopoly' that is more frightening than technological progress. We must not forget that technology grows fastest when it is opened fairly to everyone.
