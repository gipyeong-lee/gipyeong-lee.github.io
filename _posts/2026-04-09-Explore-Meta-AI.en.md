### Technical Background: 'Native Multimodal' and '10x Efficiency'

The core factor that differentiates Muse Spark from existing models lies in its 'Natively Multimodal' design and overwhelming computational efficiency. This is the result of innovating the cognitive structure of AI itself, rather than simply combining multiple functions.

1. **Implementation of True Multimodality**: From the design stage, Muse Spark was integrally trained to process and understand not only text but also images, audio, and video simultaneously [Meta’s New AI Model Gives Mark Zuckerberg a Seat at the Big Kid’s Table | WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/muse-spark-meta-open-source-closed-source/). This serves as the core driving force of Meta AI, allowing it to understand user prompts from multiple angles and generate optimized answers and outputs based on machine learning models trained on vast datasets [An overview of features, uses, insights, and concerns aroundMeta'sAI.](https://d33gy59ovltp76.cloudfront.net/news/what-is-meta-ai-everything-you-need-to-know).

2. **Maximizing Efficiency**: The most notable achievement is the disruptive reduction in computational costs. According to Meta, Muse Spark achieved equivalent or superior performance while reducing the amount of computation by more than 10 times compared to the previous generation, 'Llama 4 Maverick' [Meta's Muse Spark AI cuts compute 10x with single multimodal framework](https://interestingengineering.com/ai-robotics/meta-muse-spark-ai-launch). This efficiency is analyzed as a significant technical turning point that can drastically lower the cost of developing and maintaining even larger AI systems in the future.

3. **Small but Powerful Reasoning**: Despite being optimized to be 'small and fast' by design, Muse Spark possesses sophisticated reasoning capabilities that allow it to answer highly complex questions in the fields of science, mathematics, and health [Introducing Muse Spark: Meta's Most Powerful Model Yet](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/04/introducing-muse-spark-meta-superintelligence-labs/). Currently, this model is powering the intelligent assistant features within the meta.ai site and the Meta AI app, demonstrating its performance in a real service environment [Introducing Muse Spark: Meta's Most Powerful Model Yet](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/04/introducing-muse-spark-meta-superintelligence-labs/).

### AI's Perspective (Opinion): From 'Economy of Scale' to 'Efficiency of Intelligence'

The appearance of Muse Spark suggests that the AI industry paradigm is shifting from 'larger models' to 'more efficient models.' Until now, the Large Language Model (LLM) competition has been a field of so-called 'material superiority,' pouring in massive capital and astronomical computing resources. However, Meta has laid the foundation for realizing AI operating costs and supplying high-performance services to a broader user base by providing a technical breakthrough that maintains top-tier performance while reducing computation by 10 times.

It is particularly noteworthy that this model is the first result developed by Meta's 'Superintelligence team.' This is an external declaration that Meta is on a 'trajectory' toward Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) that assists or surpasses human intelligence, going beyond simply creating chatbot-level auxiliary tools. The ability to autonomously divide and process complex tasks through a multi-agent system foreshadows that AI will evolve from a simple question-answering machine into a 'digital agent' that solves problems autonomously in the future.
