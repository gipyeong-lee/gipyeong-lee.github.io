## Lead

Imagine this: How would you feel if you heard that the core code of an artificial intelligence (AI) service you frequently use for work or study had been leaked on the internet? Many tech enthusiasts and developers would likely have been thrown into shock and chaos. In April 2026, exactly this happened. Rumors that the source code for 'Claude,' developed by the AI company Anthropic, had leaked hit the global IT industry hard. But the outcome was unexpected. We had all been fooled by a massive prank.

## Why Does This Matter?

This incident holds significant meaning beyond being just an "amusing April Fools' prank." It vividly demonstrates how sophisticatedly the AI we use every day can deceive us, and why the ability to verify information is essential in the age of AI. It served as a powerful wake-up call to both developers and the general users who rely on AI, reminding them that even 'AI-generated information' or 'AI-related news' might not be true.

## Simple Explanation

To help you understand this commotion, let’s use a metaphor. Suppose you are a chef and hear that the secret recipe of a famous restaurant has been revealed. You check the recipe with excitement, only to find out that the restaurant owner created it as a "prank menu" using fake ingredients and an entirely different cooking process.

What Anthropic did was similar. They didn't just spread a rumor; they set up an elaborate "fake stage."
1. **Fake Model Data**: They precisely fabricated data related to a non-existent model called 'Mythos' [Fact Check](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/claude/articles/fact-check-leak-anthropics-claude-024353356.html).
2. **Manipulated Performance Metrics**: They distributed fake benchmark tables that made it appear as if the actual model had overwhelming performance [Fact Check](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/claude/articles/fact-check-leak-anthropics-claude-024353356.html).
3. **Massive Fake Documents**: They intentionally planted approximately 3,000 files that looked like internal documents [Fact Check](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/claude/articles/fact-check-leak-anthropics-claude-024353356.html).

Thanks to this precise forgery, countless experts and fans had no choice but to believe it was a real security breach. Simply put, it was an unprecedented "digital hidden camera" orchestrated at the corporate level.

## Current Situation

Claude, the center of the commotion, is now Anthropic’s core AI service, serving as a smart assistant that helps with complex problem-solving, data analysis, and coding [Product Overview](https://claude.com/product/overview). During this April Fools' period, a fun feature for users was hidden: 'Claude Buddy' [Claude Buddy](https://cloudinsight.cc/en/blog/claude-buddy-april-fools). This feature granted the AI RPG-style stats like 'CHAOS', elements designed purely for fun [Claude Buddy](https://cloudinsight.cc/en/blog/claude-buddy-april-fools).

Of course, not all news about Claude is a prank. Claude continues to evolve, adding features like 'Computer Use,' which allows it to view and manipulate computers directly, and real-time web search capabilities [Claude Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_(language_model)). However, the pros and cons of the technology are clear. In June, Anthropic halted access to its latest models, 'Fable 5' and 'Mythos 5,' without notice just three days after launch, showing that technical maturity and operational issues remain challenges to be solved [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/why-the-us-government-shut-down-anthropics-latest-claude-ai-model-285223).

## What Happens Next?

As AI technology advances, distinguishing between actual technical incidents and highly engineered fake news, like this incident, will become increasingly difficult. We must remember that AI-enabled information manipulation is possible at any time. Companies are expected to build stronger security systems while further emphasizing the importance of 'fact-checking' to verify whether AI results are true.
