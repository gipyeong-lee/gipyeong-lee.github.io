## Late Night Worries in the Classroom Where the Lights Never Go Out

**Imagine.** The school bell rang at 3 PM, but Teacher Kim's 'real work' has just begun. Sitting alone in an empty classroom, they create handouts for tomorrow's lesson and write individual feedback tailored to the learning levels of 30 students. After sending dozens of announcement emails to parents, the clock already points to 9 PM. Teacher Kim heads home with a heavy body, thinking, 'I wish I had just a little more time to make eye contact and talk with the kids.'

This scene is not unique to our country. Regarding this chronic 'lack of time' problem faced by countless teachers worldwide, hopeful news has recently arrived from Northern Ireland. It's the news that Artificial Intelligence (AI) has transformed from a scary entity taking away teachers' jobs into a reliable helper that gives back the most precious asset: 'time.' [How AI is giving Northern Ireland teachers time back](https://news-tech.io/en/news/deepmind-blog-how-ai-is-giving-northern-ireland-teachers-time-back)

## Why Is This Important?

The quality of education ultimately comes from 'people.' However, today's educational field exhausts teachers' energy more with overflowing administrative work and documentation than with the essence of teaching. When teachers are exhausted, their passion for lessons and careful observation of each child inevitably becomes difficult in reality.

The reason this project, conducted by the Northern Ireland education authority (C2k) and Google, is receiving worldwide attention is clear. It's because it proved with concrete 'numbers' how AI, when introduced to the classroom, can actually change a teacher's life rather than just being a 'novel toy.' [How AI is giving Northern Ireland teachers time back](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/ai-classroom-northern-ireland/) This pilot operation is evaluated as a milestone for the positive changes AI will bring to the education world and a 'global blueprint' that education systems worldwide can refer to. [AI Teacher Productivity: Northern Ireland Pilot Reshapes Education](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-research/2025/ai-teacher-productivity-northern-ireland-pilot-reshapes-education)

## Easy Understanding: AI as the Teacher's 'Digital Sous-chef'

**As an analogy**, shall we think of the teacher as the main chef of a top-tier restaurant? For a chef to focus only on the best taste and creative new menus, they absolutely need a capable assistant nearby to prep ingredients, help with the dishes, and take care of the complicated cleanup. **Gemini (the latest generative AI model developed by Google)**, which played an active role in this project, performed exactly that role as the teacher's capable 'digital assistant.' [AI in Education: Northern Ireland Teachers Reclaim Time | Benefits ...](https://www.world-today-journal.com/ai-in-education-northern-ireland-teachers-reclaim-time-benefits-tools/)

**Simply put**, **Generative AI** refers to intelligent tools that can create new content such as text or images on their own. For example, if a teacher requests, "Create a 3rd-grade level reading material about 'the solar system,' which is the topic of this science class," the AI presents a customized draft of the learning material in just a few seconds. The teacher only needs to review and refine this draft, drastically reducing the time required.

A particularly noteworthy point is that this project followed a **'Co-creation'** model. [AI Teacher Productivity: Northern Ireland Pilot Reshapes Education](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-research/2025/ai-teacher-productivity-northern-ireland-pilot-reshapes-education) This was not a method of simply throwing a program made by developers at teachers and saying, "Try using it once." It's a way where teachers directly decide and steer how to apply the technology in the classroom field, and develop the technology together based on their actual usage experience. In other words, the core is that technology does not dominate education; rather, the teacher, an education expert, completely controls and leads the technology as a 'tool.'

## Current Status: Northern Ireland's Remarkable Changes in Numbers

One hundred teachers selected from across Northern Ireland participated in this six-month pilot project. [AI gives time back to teachers in Northern Ireland | Keryc](https://keryc.com/en/news/ai-gives-time-back-teachers-northern-ireland-30pxpddi) They extensively utilized Google's Gemini and Workspace (a collection of document, email, and collaboration tools) from lesson preparation to administrative tasks. [Northern Ireland teachers reclaim hours with AI](https://dig.watch/updates/northern-ireland-teachers-reclaim-hours-with-ai)

The results were enough to surprise everyone.

1.  **10 Hours of Freedom Every Week**: Participating teachers reported saving an average of about **10 hours** of work time per week. [How AI is giving Northern Ireland teachers time back](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/ai-classroom-northern-ireland/) **Simply put**, golden time was created for teachers to communicate more deeply with students or research lessons for two hours every day. [Northern Ireland teachers reclaim hours with AI](https://dig.watch/updates/northern-ireland-teachers-reclaim-hours-with-ai)
2.  **Over 600 Creative Uses**: Teachers did not limit AI simply to document summarization. They utilized AI in as many as **600 different ways**, such as establishing lesson plans, producing customized learning materials, and drafting announcement emails for parents. [AI gives time back to teachers in Northern Ireland | Keryc](https://keryc.com/en/news/ai-gives-time-back-teachers-northern-ireland-30pxpddi)
3.  **Eyes Turned Back to the Students**: The saved time went straight back to the children. Teachers were able to escape the swamp of administrative work and reinvest that precious time into listening to each student's concerns and improving the quality of their lessons. [Northern Ireland teachers reclaim hours with AI](https://dig.watch/updates/northern-ireland-teachers-reclaim-hours-with-ai)

Some worry that AI will eventually take away the teacher's place. However, experts who watched this project say with a tone full of conviction: "AI is not a thief stealing the teacher's role, but like Santa Claus bringing the most precious gift to teachers: 'time'."
