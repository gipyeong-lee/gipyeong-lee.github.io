ChatGPT provides a perfect answer in less than a second. You are using this amazing, smart assistant every day for free, or for just the cost of a couple of movie tickets a month. No matter how difficult the question is, even if you ask it to write complex computer code, it handles it effortlessly without a single complaint.

But doesn't this thought suddenly cross your mind? Just how much money does it cost to process the massive volume of questions pouring in daily from hundreds of millions of people worldwide and to train such a colossal artificial intelligence? You can get a hint regarding this question by looking at a recent report analyzing hiring data in the IT industry. OpenAI, having swept up the world's top minds, is pouring astronomical amounts of money into labor costs and maintenance every single day. Now, moving past years of popularization, they have finally unveiled a meticulous and disruptive blueprint to build a massive business empire. Their real target isn't general consumers like us. It's the deep pockets of global 'enterprises' that wield tens of billions of dollars in budgets.

Right now, the biggest topic of conversation in the AI industries of Silicon Valley and Wall Street is undeniably Initial Public Offerings (IPOs) on the stock market. 

Moving past the romantic era of competing solely on technological prowess, it has now become a battle for survival over who can secure a higher valuation and raise massive capital in the stock market. In this suffocating competition, OpenAI is also standing at a crucial crossroads where it must prove groundbreaking performance to pull off a successful IPO. They are now facing the harsh reality where they must move beyond boasting to investors that "our AI is the smartest in the world," and instead prove with clear numbers the fact that "we can open the wallets of countless enterprises and generate guaranteed, massive revenue."

To achieve this, top executives at OpenAI no longer view the free version of ChatGPT that we conveniently use every day as the final revenue model. Instead, they are redefining it as a sort of powerful 'gateway' [OpenAI Preps ChatGPT Superapp Overhaul Ahead of IPO | Logicity](https://logicity.in/en/blog/openai-preps-chatgpt-superapp-overhaul-ahead-of-ipo). 

Using an analogy makes this much easier to understand. Imagine a food sampling event at a crowded supermarket on a weekend. Captivated by the incredible taste of this 'one bite,' the customer eventually opens their wallet and tosses three large packs of sausages into their cart. The free version of ChatGPT that the world is currently raving about is exactly this irresistible 'sample sausage.' It is a hook strategy designed to make not only the general public but also key corporate practitioners fall completely in love with ChatGPT's performance, ultimately naturally inducing their organizations to bulk-purchase OpenAI's expensive and secure enterprise solutions. A massive paradigm shift is happening right before our eyes, where the focus of the technology, which was once open to consumers, is completely shifting toward building systems that dominate the backends of massive enterprises.

## The Explainer

So, what exactly is the 'real main product' that OpenAI wants to sell to enterprises in earnest? Recently, the global IT industry has been focusing on the stealthy moves shown by OpenAI and computer hardware giant Dell Technologies. According to reports, OpenAI has partnered with Dell to form what is known as the 'Codex Enterprise' partnership. Through this, signs have been spotted that they are ambitiously preparing an 'on-premise' product that can be installed directly on a company's own servers without needing to connect to an internet cloud [OpenAI Prepping for On-Prem Product? | Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48497260).

The computer science term 'on-premise' might feel unfamiliar to you, but simply put, it's like **'locking up the world's smartest librarian inside your own absolutely impenetrable secret vault.'**

The ChatGPT we've been using up to now was cloud-based. It's like we send a letter with a question to a massive public library that OpenAI has built far away in the US, and the librarian there writes a reply and sends it back to us. It's convenient for individuals to use, but it's a fatal flaw that global companies like Samsung Electronics, Apple, or major banks can never accept. This is due to the security anxiety of having to send out top-secret documents that determine the fate of the company, or sensitive financial information of customers, to an outside library (OpenAI's servers).

However, the on-premise approach is different. When Dell, the master of server hardware, builds a physically solid computer server (the vault), OpenAI copies their genius AI brain (the librarian) and puts it inside that server. Then, this massive steel server is delivered straight to the company's basement and installed right there. When this happens, a company can freely utilize ChatGPT within an entirely closed internal network without sending even a single byte of data out to the external internet. It acts as the perfect master key that will disarm the wallets of large corporations that had been hesitating to adopt AI due to concerns over confidentiality leaks.

OpenAI's plans do not stop here. According to insiders, OpenAI is currently executing a master plan to massively overhaul its current simple chat window format and create a colossal 'superapp' that handles all tasks integrally in one place [OpenAI Preps ChatGPT Superapp Overhaul Ahead of IPO | Logicity](https://logicity.in/en/blog/openai-preps-chatgpt-superapp-overhaul-ahead-of-ipo). 

To use an analogy, a superapp is like a 'Swiss Army Knife' that combines multiple tools into one. Right now, we alternate between different tools like Word for writing, Gmail for emails, and Excel for data organization. But when the era of the superapp arrives, you'll only need to have one massive app window open: ChatGPT. It reflects a terrifying ambition where the AI will automatically summarize emails, analyze Excel data to create charts, and even manage schedules on its own [OpenAI prepping huge ChatGPT update, plans to turn it... - India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/technology/news/story/openai-prepping-huge-chatgpt-update-plans-to-turn-it-into-a-superapp-ahead-of-ipo-2923127-2026-06-07).

It is even reported that they are thoroughly preparing a dedicated 'AI-powered web browser' to directly compete head-to-head with Google Chrome [PYMNTS | OpenAI Reportedly Prepping Browser to Take on Google...](https://www.pymnts.com/artificial-intelligence-2/2025/openai-reportedly-prepping-browser-to-take-on-google-chrome/). If we've been navigating the sea of information riding on a passenger ship (Chrome) monopolized by Google all this time, OpenAI has essentially declared that they will launch a state-of-the-art AI ship that finds its own sea routes. It is an unconventional move, kicking down the door of Google's home turf and engaging in a frontal assault.

Behind all these astonishing plans stand newly evolved ultra-large artificial intelligence models holding their ground firmly. Recently, it was revealed that an AI model with a new architecture codenamed 'Garlic' is in development. The Garlic model has established a massive breakthrough during the 'pre-training' phase, which is the most foundational stage where AI absorbs knowledge like a sponge [OpenAI Preps New "Garlic" Model - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8GJcv-kTZrs). 

Pre-training is like training to deeply ingrain the 'fundamentals,' such as perfect knife skills and heat control, before becoming a world-class chef, rather than just rote memorizing recipes. The Garlic model fundamentally overhauled this basic physical training method of AI, laying the groundwork for the AI to realize principles on its own and evolve to be much smarter even when facing complex challenges in the future.

In addition to this, CEO Sam Altman opened up about a new flagship model called 'o3-Pro,' which is equipped with deep, mathematician-level logical thinking and complex reasoning capabilities, strongly expressing confidence in his victory in the upcoming AI hegemony competition [OpenAI Preps To Blow Past AGI Straight to Super... - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OwQ_YBBZdD8).

## Where We Stand

Right now, we are standing in the calm before the storm, just before unrealistic events previously only seen in sci-fi (SF) movies pour right into our morning news. Currently, the most hotly debated topic among experts worldwide is the emergence of 'Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)' and 'Superintelligence,' which will completely and vastly surpass the human brain. How exactly do OpenAI's executives view this massive future?

The hint can be found at the 'BlackRock Infrastructure Summit,' a top-tier meeting held in Washington D.C. on March 11, 2026. CEO Sam Altman made a surprise appearance as a key speaker at this event [Exclusive: OpenAI Preps Policy Push to “Rethink the...” | Vanity Fair](https://www.vanityfair.com/news/story/openai-new-model-superintelligence-policy-push). Why did the head of an AI company show up at a gathering of financiers and energy infrastructure titans who control global capital? It is because fully operating a new artificial intelligence that leaps far beyond the human brain now urgently requires physical 'national-level infrastructure,' such as massive power grids, colossal energy equivalent to nuclear power plants, and enormous data centers spanning across continents.

What draws even more attention is the unique phrase Sam Altman used to describe the process of this AGI seeping into our daily lives. He referred to this period of massive intelligence explosion as **"The Gentle Singularity"** [OpenAI Is Prepping for Superintelligence. Should We Panic Yet?](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/openai-prepping-superintelligence-should-we-panic-yet-doug-bard-y1gbc).

Usually, the word 'Singularity' is used when warning of the fleeting moment when artificial intelligence breaks free from human control and takes over the world at a terrifying and overwhelming speed. However, Sam Altman attached the modifier 'gentle' in front of this cold word. It is his firm belief that a tragedy like the one in the movie 'Terminator,' where steel robots forcefully conquer the world overnight, will never happen in reality.

Instead, AGI will seep into the smartphone memo apps or corporate approval programs we use every day, very naturally and quietly, like a drizzling rain wetting our clothes. It means that an AI that initially just fixes awkward typos in emails will instantly write complex Excel functions next month, and by next year, it will perfectly review tricky English legal contracts, becoming an absolutely indispensable, 'oxygen'-like existence. Like water slowly heating up in a pot, humanity will suddenly realize at a certain point, "Ah, we were already living in an era coexisting with superintelligence," greeting a very soft and elegant soft landing [OpenAI Preps To Blow Past AGI Straight to Super... - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OwQ_YBBZdD8).
