Imagine this: You wake up in the morning and tell your AI, "Analyze the photos of the documents and reports I need to read today and summarize the key agenda items for this week's meeting." The AI understands not just the text documents but also the graphs in the photos you took with your smartphone, and neatly organizes your work schedule like a long-time personal assistant.

The technology we are introducing today is the protagonist that will bring this future a little closer. Europe’s AI powerhouse, Mistral AI, has unveiled its new state-of-the-art AI model, 'Mistral Large 4' ([IntroducingMistralLarge4|Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/)).

## Why Is This Important?

Mistral AI is a leading European AI company headquartered in Paris, France ([Mistral Large](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mistral_Large)). In the midst of a massive AI technology race led by the United States and China, Europe is striving to protect its own technological capabilities and 'digital sovereignty,' and Mistral AI is at the center of this effort.

The newly announced Mistral Large 4 boasts an immense scale, earning the nickname 'Le Chonk' among experts ([MistralпредставилиLarge4«Le Chonk» на 1 трлн... / Хабр](https://habr.com/ru/news/1091148/)). It is designed not only for excellent performance but also to handle general conversation, coding, logical reasoning, and complex agent tasks (tasks where the AI plans and executes on its own) ([MistralLarge4- API Pricing & Providers | OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/mistralai/mistral-large-4-0)). In short, a reliable partner has arrived to process our daily complex tasks more intelligently.

## Easy Explanation: The AI's 'Brain' Has Grown

An AI's performance is often determined by the number of 'parameters.' Parameters are adjustable numerical values used by AI to learn and judge information, functioning similarly to the 'synapses' that connect neurons in our brains.

Mistral Large 4 has a staggering **1.05 trillion parameters** ([MistralLarge4-MistralAI |MistralDocs](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/mistral-large-4-0)). To put this scale into perspective, if a lightweight AI model running on a regular smartphone solves elementary school-level math problems, Mistral Large 4 is like a 'doctor' who has read tens of thousands of books in a library and solves complex logical problems.

Furthermore, this model uses a **'Granular Mixture-of-Experts'** structure. The name sounds difficult, but think of it like managing small groups of experts in specific fields rather than having one genius do everything. If a math question comes in, it calls the math expert group; if a coding question comes in, it calls the coding expert group. Because it operates efficiently in this way, it has optimized the number of active parameters at any given time to 49 billion despite having over a trillion parameters, capturing both speed and performance ([MistralLarge4-MistralAI |MistralDocs](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/mistral-large-4-0)).

Additionally, it provides a **512K token context window** ([MistralLarge4- API Pricing & Providers | OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/mistralai/mistral-large-4-0)). Think of tokens as pieces of words that the AI reads. With 510,000 of them, it can remember several thick novels at once and find information within them. It is also multimodal, capable of receiving and analyzing images along with text, making it highly versatile ([MistralLarge4-MistralAI |MistralDocs](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/mistral-large-4-0)).

## Current Status

Mistral Large 4 was unveiled on October 6, 2026, as a preview version for the public to experience ([IntroducingMistralLarge4|Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/)). Developers can currently use it via API, and the 'open-weights' files that researchers or companies can install directly on their own servers are scheduled to be released at the end of October ([MistralLarge4: публичное превью API и планы открыть веса](https://trashexpert.ru/news/software-news/mistral-large-public-preview)).

Price competitiveness is also noteworthy. The currently announced price is around $1.36 per 1 million input tokens and $4.18 per 1 million output tokens, offering a reasonable price point compared to competing models ([MistralLarge4Preview - Intelligence, Performance... | Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/mistral-large-4)).

## What’s Next?

In the future, models like Mistral Large 4 will evolve beyond simple 'AI assistants' into 'AI agents' that plan and execute tasks on their own in our daily lives. For example, even if a user just says, "Plan my vacation trip," the AI can check flight ticket prices, analyze hotel review photos to choose highly-rated places, and create a perfect travel itinerary matched to your schedule.

When the open weights are released at the end of October, more developers around the world will use this powerful model to build their own unique services and apps. It will be an interesting point to watch how this 'Le Chonk,' nurtured by Europe for digital sovereignty, performs in the global AI market.
