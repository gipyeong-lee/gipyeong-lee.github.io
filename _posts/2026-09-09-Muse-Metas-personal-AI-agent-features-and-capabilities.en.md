Imagine this: You wake up in the morning, open your smartphone, and say to your AI, "Organize my work meeting materials for today, find a good place for lunch, and schedule it."

Until now, the AI we used was mostly an 'encyclopedia' that answered our questions. But now, a 'real assistant' has arrived that takes care of the tedious tasks we used to have to do ourselves—opening apps, navigating sites, and clicking through menus one by one. This is **'Muse,'** the personal AI agent (artificial intelligence assistant) ambitiously introduced by Meta.

### Why is this technology important?

While AI in the past was a 'gateway' to finding information, Muse is an 'executor' that takes the user's instructions and gets to work directly. For example, when planning a trip, you no longer need to waste time going back and forth between flight booking sites, hotel reservation apps, and restaurant search sites. Muse understands the user's long-term goals and handles the detailed tasks required to achieve them 24/7. [Source 1](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/), [Source 9](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/meta-launches-personal-ai-agent-muse-to-help-with-everyday-tasks-126090900039_1.html)

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg emphasized that if an agent like Muse can help users with their daily lives around the clock, users will be able to focus on more productive and creative activities, significantly improving their quality of life. [Source 5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lx8lrn-cytc)

### In simple terms: The 'action radius' of the AI assistant has expanded

Think of it this way: if the AI of the past was a 'photo app' that automatically selected a nice filter when you chose a photo, Muse is like a 'professional photographer' who goes to the shoot, sets the composition, takes the picture, and even edits it for you.

Muse goes beyond a simple chat window and has its own terminal (a computer's core command interface) and file system. This means that Muse is capable of not just combining words, but creating files and processing information directly within a computer environment. [Source 4](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2026/09/08/meta-muse-ai-agent-us-app-store-not-canada/), [Source 8](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/meta-muse-agent-launch)

Meta designed it so that users don't have to clumsily hop between multiple apps or websites. Users simply provide the 'instructions' to get the desired outcome, and Muse takes over the complex execution process that follows. [Source 2](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/meta-unveils-muse-ai-agent-1818623)

### Current status: Starting in the US, with ironclad security

Muse has officially launched for users aged 18 and older in the United States. A dedicated app for iPhone has also been released, allowing easy access in a mobile environment. [Source 3](https://9for9.com/2026/09/08/meta-ai-launches-muse-personal-agent-including-a-new-mobile-app-for-iphone/), [Source 8](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/meta-muse-agent-launch)

Meta has paid significant attention to 'trust' and 'security.' Entrusting a personal assistant with your schedule and payment information is a sensitive issue. Meta is positioning its security and privacy features as Muse's core differentiator. [Source 2](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/meta-unveils-muse-ai-agent-1818623), [Source 6](https://www.wired.com/story/meta-releases-muse-a-personal-ai-agent-with-privacy-built-into-it/)

Furthermore, purchase activities such as shopping carried out through Muse are covered by 'Link's' purchase protection service. Even if a problem arises during service use, no-fee returns are guaranteed, highlighting the effort to lower the psychological barrier to using an AI agent. [Source 6](https://www.wired.com/story/meta-releases-muse-a-personal-ai-agent-with-privacy-built-into-it/)

### What's next?

The arrival of Muse signals that we are moving into an era that has transitioned from "how well can you use AI" to "how well can you delegate work to AI." Muse is expected to dig deeper into general daily tasks such as research, document drafting, and price monitoring across a wider variety of fields. [Source 7](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49615537)

Our task now is to understand this new technology and consider how to more efficiently utilize Muse, the smart assistant by our side.
