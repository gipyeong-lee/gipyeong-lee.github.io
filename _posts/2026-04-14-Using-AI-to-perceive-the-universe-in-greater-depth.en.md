## Why is this important?

We have always wanted to know how the universe began and what happens when distant black holes collide. But the universe is so vast, and the information we can obtain on Earth consists of only the most minute signals.

In particular, signals called "gravitational waves" are a prime example. Gravitational waves refer to the ripples in spacetime caused by the movement of massive celestial bodies, much like waves on a lake caused by a thrown stone. By the time these signals reach Earth, they become incredibly faint, turning into vibrations much smaller than a single atom.

**Just imagine.** Someone drops a tiny needle in a city thousands of kilometers away, and you have to hear that sound accurately in the middle of a noisy construction site. It sounds almost impossible, doesn't it? But AI plays a key role in cleanly removing unnecessary "noise" from this complex process and finding the "voice of truth" sent by the universe. Thanks to this, we can understand the origin and formation of the universe much more deeply and clearly than before [Using AI to perceive the universe in greater depth - BAAI Community](https://hub.baai.ac.cn/view/48658).

## Easy Understanding: How to Listen to the 'Bass Notes' of the Universe

Rana Adhikari, a physics professor at the California Institute of Technology (Caltech), explained this with a wonderful analogy: "Studying the universe using gravity instead of light is like listening instead of seeing. **To put it in an analogy:** The starlight we commonly see is like a brilliant, high-pitched melody, while gravitational waves are like the heavy sound of a bass guitar resonating throughout the entire universe. To hear this deep resonance, scientists use a special device called **LIGO (Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory)**. LIGO is equipment that captures minute distortions in space using the interference of laser light [Using AI to perceive the universe in greater depth](https://deepmind.google/blog/using-ai-to-perceive-the-universe-in-greater-depth/).

### 1. AI Stabilizing the Trembling Heart: 'Deep Loop Shaping'
However, LIGO has a very sensitive problem. Because the observation equipment is so precise, the readings often fluctuate wildly due to vibrations from a truck passing on a nearby road or even a faint breeze outside the window. This is where AI steps in as a relief pitcher.

In September 2025, a research team led by Brendan Tracey and Jonas Buchli announced an innovative AI technique called **'Deep Loop Shaping'** [Using AI to perceive the universe in greater depth - ONMINE](https://onmine.io/using-ai-to-perceive-the-universe-in-greater-depth-3/). This technology precisely controls and stabilizes observation equipment in real-time, helping to capture massive cosmic events without interference [Using AI To Perceive The Universe In Greater Depth](https://aifuturethinkers.com/using-ai-to-perceive-the-universe-in-greater-depth/).

**Simply put**, it's like equipping a "cutting-edge smart gimbal" that perfectly stabilizes image blur when taking photos on a boat rocking in a stormy sea. This research gained global recognition, being published in the prestigious scientific journal *Science* [Using AI to perceive the universe in greater depth | BARD AI](https://bardai.ai/2025/12/06/using-ai-to-perceive-the-universe-in-greater-depth/).

### 2. 99.7% Accuracy: Mapping the Universe with Precision
AI's performance doesn't stop at stabilizing machinery. It also shows exceptional skill in accurately distinguishing which of the trillions of lights in the night sky are ordinary "galaxies" and which are "quasars" (extremely bright celestial bodies emitting immense energy from the center of a galaxy).

Remarkably, a recent AI identified galaxies and quasars with a stunning **99.7%** accuracy compared to existing professional astronomical survey (GAMA survey) results [A New AI Sees the Universe More Clearly Than Ever Before](https://www.sciencenewstoday.org/a-new-ai-sees-the-universe-more-clearly-than-ever-before). It also analyzes the "spectra" (bands of color from light decomposed by wavelength) emitted by stars to instantly figure out their temperature, composition, and even age. It's as if AI is reading a diary containing the history of the universe [How AI is Revolutionizing Our View of the Cosmos](https://wait.readnovelnow.com/using-ai-perceive-universe-greater-depth.html).

### 3. Clearing the Fog: Reducing Cosmic Uncertainty
One of the most important questions for cosmologists is "how densely cosmic matter is clumped (clumpiness)." According to a study recently published in *Nature Astronomy*, AI has succeeded in reducing the uncertainty of these key cosmic parameters by more than half compared to before [AI breakthrough unlocks hidden patterns in the universe's structure](https://www.thebrighterside.news/post/ai-breakthrough-unlocks-hidden-patterns-in-the-universes-structure/).

**By analogy**, it's like turning on very bright and powerful fog lights on a road where you couldn't see an inch ahead due to thick fog. Thanks to this, humanity can now view the massive structures of the universe in much higher resolution than before.
