## Easy Understanding: Gemini 2.5 Pro's 'Coding Skills'

The biggest change in this update is that **multimodal reasoning** (the ability to understand and judge various information such as images, videos, and audio all at once, not just text) and coding ability have been merged into one. [Gemini 2.5 Pro update: Coding, web apps with Gemini](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/gemini-2-5-pro-updates/)

### 1. Understanding the Update Through an Analogy
If previous coding AI was an "assistant laborer who carefully stacks bricks one by one," the updated Gemini 2.5 Pro is like a **"master carpenter who magically completes the entire castle just by glancing at the blueprints."** This is because it doesn't just generate code line by line, but simultaneously designs the overall picture (UI/UX, user interface and experience) and functions that the user wants.

### 2. "Video to App" A Magical Case
One of the most amazing cases is the function to turn 'Video to Learning App.' [Gemini 2.5 Pro Preview: even better coding performance](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/gemini-2-5-pro-io-improved-coding-performance/)

For example, you show Gemini a complex quantum mechanics lecture video uploaded to YouTube. Then Gemini watches this video from beginning to end and perfectly understands the content. But it doesn't stop there. It codes an entire **working web app** that includes buttons to review key concepts in the video, a quiz window, and even a function to show grades in a graph. [Gemini 2.5: Pushing the Frontier with Advanced Reasoning, Multimodality ...](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.06261v4)

### 3. Vast Data at a Glance: 'Infinite Memory'
Gemini 2.5 Pro has a 'long context window' (the amount of information the AI can remember and process at once). [Gemini 2.5 Pro: My Ultimate Developer Guide & FAQ (Code, Cost ... - Habr](https://habr.com/en/articles/897832/)

**To use an analogy:** If a typical AI is an assistant who can only judge by looking at a few pages of documents at a time, Gemini is like a genius assistant who **puts the contents of dozens of encyclopedias into its head at once** and understands even if you say, "Combine the contents on page 200 of volume 37 and page 10 of volume 5 to create a new business model." Thanks to this vast memory, it can understand and modify large-scale projects containing tens of thousands of lines of code all at once.
