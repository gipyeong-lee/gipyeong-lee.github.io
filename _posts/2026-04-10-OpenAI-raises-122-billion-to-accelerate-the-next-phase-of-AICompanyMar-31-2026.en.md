Imagine this. One morning, you say to the AI on your bedside smartphone: "Organize my tasks for today by urgency, and find and summarize the materials I'll need for the meeting." The AI doesn't just read a schedule. It accurately understands your work style and perfectly organizes the documents you need, placing them in your inbox. This scene, once only seen in sci-fi movies, seems likely to arrive much sooner than expected.

On March 31, 2026, news broke that OpenAI, the creator of ChatGPT, successfully raised a staggering **$122 billion (over 160 trillion KRW)** [OpenAI raises $122 billion to accelerate the next phase of AI](https://openai.com/index/accelerating-the-next-phase-ai/). It's hard to grasp the scale of $122 billion, right? To put it simply, it's an enormous amount equivalent to about a quarter of the South Korean government's annual budget, and it stands as the largest investment in the history of artificial intelligence.

Today, we’ll explore where this massive capital will be used and what kind of magical changes it will bring to the lives of ordinary people like us.

## Why is this important? "Changes in 'Life', not just technology"

You might think, "A company made a lot of money, but what does that have to do with me?" However, this investment is more than just a celebration for one company. It’s because the "intelligence" of the AI in the smartphones, computers, and home appliances we use every day is set to take a complete leap forward.

The reason OpenAI raised this massive sum is clear: to move to **'The Next Phase' of AI** [OpenAI raises $122 billion to accelerate the next phase of AI](https://openai.com/index/accelerating-the-next-phase-ai/). While the AI we use now is excellent, it still has limitations, such as occasionally giving incorrect answers (hallucinations) or struggling with very complex reasoning. OpenAI plans to use this investment to expand "Frontier AI"—cutting-edge artificial intelligence that overcomes these weaknesses—globally and to build a massive system that remains stable even when every user in the world connects simultaneously.

In short, it’s about creating an environment where you don't have to wonder, "Will it understand this?" but can instead use AI as a human-like assistant that is much faster and more accurate.

## Understanding easily: Why is so much money needed?

The process of creating AI can be compared to **'the process of educating a giant virtual brain.'** Just as a good classroom and excellent textbooks are needed to study well, AI requires immense resources.

1.  **Creating a 'Super-sized Classroom' called Compute (AI calculation devices)**: To create very smart AI, incredibly high-performance computers must run 24 hours a day without rest. This is called 'compute,' and a significant portion of this investment will be spent on building this next-generation infrastructure [OpenAI raises $122 billion to accelerate the next phase of AI](https://openai.com/index/accelerating-the-next-phase-ai/). It's like building a giant university campus where all the genius students in the world can study at once.
2.  **Data and Learning (Reading Textbooks)**: For AI to become smarter, it needs to read and learn from more books and professional materials. As demand explodes for services like ChatGPT and coding assistants, massive funding is essential to process all this training data [OpenAI raises $122 billion to accelerate the next phase of AI](https://openai.com/index/accelerating-the-next-phase-ai/).

**Think of it this way!**
Developing AI technology is similar to launching a 'space rocket.' For a rocket to break free from Earth's gravity and reach further into space (the next level of intelligence), it needs an enormous amount of fuel (investment) and a sturdy launch pad (infrastructure). The $122 billion is the 'super-powered fuel' that allows AI to spread into every area of our lives.

## Current Status: How valuable is OpenAI?

With this funding round, OpenAI's valuation has reached a staggering **$852 billion (approx. 1,100 trillion KRW)** [OpenAI closes funding round at an $852 billion valuation - CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/31/openai-funding-round-ipo.html). It has emerged as one of the most valuable companies in the world, far exceeding the market capitalization of Samsung Electronics, a leading South Korean company.

Even more surprising is that OpenAI is not just receiving investment but is actually generating massive revenue. It is currently reported to be making about **$2 billion (approx. 2.6 trillion KRW) in monthly revenue** [OpenAI, parent firm of ChatGPT, closes $122bn funding round amid AI ...](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/mar/31/openai-raises-122-billion-ai-boom). The fees we pay for the paid version of ChatGPT or the costs companies pay to lease OpenAI's technology are coming together to create these massive numbers.

One more interesting fact! This investment involved not only large professional investment firms but also a massive number of **retail investors**. About $3 billion was raised from individual investors alone, which is evidence of the public's intense anticipation for the future of AI [OpenAI, not yet public, raises $3B from retail investors in monster ...](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/31/openai-not-yet-public-raises-3b-from-retail-investors-in-monster-122b-fund-raise/). Furthermore, paths are opening for the general public to indirectly participate in OpenAI's growth through various exchange-traded funds (ETFs) [OpenAIRaises$122BilliontoAccelerateAI Efforts - Avenue Z](https://avenuez.com/blog/openai-raises-122-billion/).

## What will happen next? "A world beyond imagination"

Now, all attention is turned toward OpenAI's next move: the **Initial Public Offering (IPO)**. Experts expect OpenAI to go public on the stock market soon [OpenAI, not yet public, raises $3B from retail investors in monster ...](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/31/openai-not-yet-public-raises-3b-from-retail-investors-in-monster-122b-fund-raise/). If the listing takes place, we will be able to become shareholders of OpenAI, just like we buy shares of Apple or Google.

However, there is an essence more important than stock talk. Experts say the future that unfolds from now on will not simply be a competition of "who makes a smarter AI" [OpenAI raises record-breaking $122B, stoking IPO hype | LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/news/story/openai-raises-record-breaking-122b-stoking-ipo-hype-8604722/). Instead, **"who provides real value to our lives with this technology"** will be the key. 

We will soon see a world where AI goes beyond a search bar that simply answers questions to become an essential infrastructure that takes responsibility for education, develops new drugs, and solves complex social problems. This $122 billion investment is the signal flare that brings that world closer. 

The day your smartphone becomes truly 'smart' is not far off.
