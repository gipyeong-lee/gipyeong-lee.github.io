# Is the Probability of Rain Tomorrow Truly a 'Probability'? How Google's New AI 'WeatherNext 2' Predicts Weather in Surprising Ways

Imagine waking up before an important outdoor wedding, opening your weather app, and seeing a message: "30% chance of rain at 2 PM." Where does this 30% figure actually come from? Until now, the weather forecasts we've seen were closer to "a single most likely conclusion" obtained by giant supercomputers calculating complex physical equations for hours. Simply put, it was like a single, stubborn mathematician working all night to provide one answer.

However, **WeatherNext 2**, recently announced by Google DeepMind and Google Research, is completely overturning this approach. [Source 4: Google DeepMind model speeds up weather forecasting | LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/news/story/google-deepmind-model-speeds-up-weather-forecasting-6765700/) Now, AI draws hundreds of 'future scenarios' in just one minute, fundamentally reshaping how we understand the weather. [Source 1: WeatherNext2: Our most advanced weather forecasting model](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/weathernext-2/)

## Why is this important?

Weather isn't just about whether to bring an umbrella. According to Google, WeatherNext 2 is already driving massive changes in **Energy Trading (the market for buying and selling electricity)** and **Hurricane Forecasts**. [Source 3: Google releases new WeatherNext 2 AI forecasting model - Overview](https://news.google.com/stories/CAAqNggKIjBDQklTSGpvSmMzUnZjbmt0TXpZd1NoRUtEd2p5dDlQLUR4RlRzU1M3TFZhVV9pZ0FQAQ?hl=en-IN&gl=IN&ceid=IN:en)

**To use an analogy:** For a wind farm operator, wind speed is 'money.' This is because they need to know exactly how much the wind will blow tomorrow to sell power efficiently. If the wind is weaker than expected, power supply is disrupted; if it's too strong, facilities must be protected. Instead of saying "The wind will blow at 10m/s," WeatherNext 2 simulates hundreds of possible situations, allowing preparation for even the worst-case scenario.

Furthermore, when a hurricane approaches, if you can simulate hundreds of possible paths in advance instead of predicting just one, evacuation plans can be much more sophisticated. Securing "golden time" through information like "This path has a 10% probability, but the damage would be catastrophic if it occurs." WeatherNext 2 is a model that maximizes the ability to manage this very 'uncertainty.' [Source 8: Google launches its most advanced AI forecasting model - WeatherNext 2](https://www.meteorologicaltechnologyinternational.com/news/climate-measurement/google-launches-its-most-advanced-ai-forecasting-model-weathernext-2.html)

## Easy to Understand: Hundreds of Futures Drawn in One Minute

The most surprising aspects of WeatherNext 2 are its **speed** and **efficiency**.

### 1. With a Single Chip Instead of a Supercomputer
Existing weather prediction models required calculations for several hours using giant supercomputers connected to thousands of servers. However, WeatherNext 2 generates hundreds of weather scenarios in **less than one minute** using only a single **TPU (Tensor Processing Unit)**, Google's specialized computer chip designed for AI calculations. [Source 1: WeatherNext2: Our most advanced weather forecasting model](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/weathernext-2/)

Can you feel how amazing this speed is? It is a whopping **8 times faster** than previous methods. [Source 13: Google’s WeatherNext 2 Pushes Global Forecasting To One Hour Resolution - Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2025/11/18/googles-weathernext-2-pushes-global-forecasting-to-one-hour-resolution/) It's like shortening the time it takes to watch a two-hour movie to less than 15 minutes.

### 2. 'Functional Generative Networks' Through an Analogy
The core technology of WeatherNext 2 is a structure called **Functional Generative Networks**. [Source 10: DeepMind's WeatherNext 2: Functional Generative Networks Power Faster ...](https://www.kiadev.net/news/2025-11-17-weathernext-2-functional-generative-network-forecast)

What does this mean? Let's use an easy analogy:
- **Existing approach**: It's like building one house with very precise blueprints. It takes days just to draw the blueprints, and if there's even a small error, the entire house could be wrong.
- **WeatherNext 2**: It's like a skilled painter creating hundreds of sketches in an instant. By slightly changing variables like "What if there were more clouds here?" or "What if the wind was a bit stronger?" (technically called **Noise Injection** [Source 13](https://dataconomy.com/2025/11/18/googles-weathernext-2-pushes-global-forecasting-to-one-hour-resolution/)), it shows hundreds of possible weather landscapes simultaneously.

By gathering these hundreds of generated scenarios, "areas where rain is highly likely" and "areas where prediction is difficult" become clearly visible. This is the true identity of the 'probability' we see in apps. It's easy to understand it as looking into hundreds of parallel universes in advance and taking statistics.

## Current Situation: The AI Meteorologist in My Hand

WeatherNext 2 isn't just staying in the lab. Google has already begun applying this model to real services we use every day.

- **Google Search and Gemini**: The information that appears when you ask about the weather in the Google search bar has been upgraded through WeatherNext 2. [Source 7: WeatherNext 2: The Impact of Google’s AI Forecasting Model](https://aimagazine.com/news/weathernext-2-the-impact-of-googles-ai-forecasting-model), [Source 10](https://www.kiadev.net/news/2025-11-17-weathernext-2-functional-generative-network-forecast)
- **Pixel Weather**: This advanced model is also included in the default weather app for Google's Pixel smartphone series, providing users with more sophisticated predictions. [Source 5: WeatherNext 2 is Google’s most accurate forecasting model](https://9to5google.com/2025/11/17/google-weathernext-2/)
- **Google Maps**: It will soon be integrated with the Google Maps Platform's weather API. Now, when finding directions, you'll be able to plan your trip by checking the weather along your route with 1-hour precision. [Source 10](https://www.kiadev.net/news/2025-11-17-weathernext-2-functional-generative-network-forecast)

This model analyzes the entire Earth by dividing it into a dense **25km grid (0.25° resolution)**. In simple terms, it's like having an individual forecaster for every area the size of thousands of soccer fields. Through this, it predicts **weather changes for the next 15 days**. [Source 10](https://www.kiadev.net/news/2025-11-17-weathernext-2-functional-generative-network-forecast) In particular, by increasing the temporal resolution to **1-hour intervals**, it aims for forecasts at the level of "Rain will start at 2:15 PM and stop at 3:10 PM," rather than just "It will rain in the afternoon." [Sources 8, 13]

## What's Next?

Google is confident that WeatherNext 2 is "the most advanced and efficient forecasting model in existence." [Source 12: Google DeepMind’s WeatherNext 2: Revolutionizing Weather Forecasting for Energy Traders](https://markets.financialcontent.com/wral/article/tokenring-2025-11-17-google-deepminds-weathernext-2-revolutionizing-weather-forecasting-for-energy-traders/) The future this technology brings won't stop at just increasing the accuracy of weather information.

In the future, companies will be able to customize the WeatherNext 2 model to suit their business purposes through **Google Cloud Vertex AI**. [Source 2: WeatherNext 2 — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/science/weathernext/)

**Imagine:**
- **Shipping Companies**: Real-time analysis of waves and wind along shipping routes to find the optimal path that uses the least fuel.
- **Farmers**: Receiving precision weather information tailored to their farm's location and getting AI advice on when to water and when to harvest.
- **Insurance Companies**: Designing more rational insurance products by analyzing the risks of weather disasters like droughts or floods with hundreds of scenarios.

Weather is an area humans cannot perfectly control, but thanks to AI, we can now look into that massive uncertainty a bit more clearly. WeatherNext 2, looking into hundreds of futures in one minute. We look forward to seeing how this technology will keep our safety and daily lives smarter and more secure.
