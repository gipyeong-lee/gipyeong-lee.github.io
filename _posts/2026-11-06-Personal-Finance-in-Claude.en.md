When we wake up in the morning, we habitually check our financial apps. After reviewing tangled credit card statements and scattered balances, we often let out a sigh. Wouldn't it be great if someone could clearly answer the question, "Where did I spend so much money last month?"

The "Personal Finance in Claude" feature recently launched by Anthropic has emerged to solve just such dilemmas. An era has dawned where you can understand the flow of your money as if you were just chatting with an AI.

## Why is this important?

Until now, financial data existed only as Excel tables filled with numbers and symbols, or as graphs within complex financial apps. We had to struggle to interpret this data ourselves. Claude's new feature lowers the barrier to entry for financial apps. It goes beyond simply listing data, explaining the flow of money in everyday language that is easy for users to understand.

Another major advantage is freedom from the "advertisements" or "product recommendations" that have always been a concern when using financial apps. Unlike financial platforms that analyze your spending patterns to encourage loans or recommend other credit cards, Claude focuses solely on data analysis. [[Source: TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/claude-can-now-analyze-your-spending-with-its-new-personal-finance-tool/)]

## Easy to Understand: An 'Interpreter' for Financial Data

To use a simple analogy, Claude's financial feature is like having a **"financial interpreter"** by your side, kindly explaining complex math problems you used to have to solve every day.

Imagine this: You ask Claude, "How much did I spend on food delivery last month?" or "What are my fixed expenses on automatic transfer this month?" Based on the linked account data, Claude replies, "Your total spend on food delivery apps last month was 00 won, which is half of your total dining-out expenses."

Claude doesn't just list data; it organizes it into a context that the user can understand. In short, it turns the "foreign language" of finance into a "native language" that anyone can comprehend.

## Current Situation: What can it do?

Currently, the Claude personal finance feature is available as a beta service for Claude Pro, Max, and Ultra subscribers in the United States. [[Source: The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1027652/anthropic-claude-plaid-personal-finance)]

The most important security principle is that it is **"read-only."** While the AI can see your accounts, it cannot directly move, invest, or manage your money. It's like being able to read books in a library, but not being able to change their content or tear them up. [[Source: Claude Resources](https://claude.com/resources/articles/introducing-personal-finance-in-claude)] Additionally, users can choose to link only the specific accounts they want and can disconnect them whenever they wish. [[Source: Claude YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UTubvRLX2j4)]

## What happens next?

Claude's financial feature is just getting started. Since OpenAI's ChatGPT has already introduced similar features, the integration of AI and finance will accelerate further. [[Source: The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1027652/anthropic-claude-plaid-personal-finance)]

In the future, it is expected to evolve into a more active financial assistance tool, going beyond simply analyzing spending history to setting budgets that match your goals or establishing debt management strategies. It is moving from the stage of knowing what you did to the stage of suggesting what you should do.
