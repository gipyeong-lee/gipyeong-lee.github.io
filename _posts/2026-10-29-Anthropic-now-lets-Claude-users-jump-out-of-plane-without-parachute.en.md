# Anthropic, the synonym for AI safety, now allows users to 'jump out of a plane without a parachute'?

Imagine the scene: the doors of a plane flying high in the sky open, and a skydiver jumps into the clouds without a parachute. If a person were to fall without auxiliary equipment due to a plane defect or an emergency, the lack of safety measures would cause intense danger and panic [Man jumps out of plane without parachute and dies... - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NqW6ruKGSmA).

Recently, however, startling news akin to this 'jump without a parachute' has come out of the generative AI industry. And the news is even more attention-grabbing because the protagonist is **Anthropic**, an AI research company that has always emphasized that "AI safety and ethics are the top priority."

Anthropic has officially started providing professional developers who use its developer tool, **Claude Code**, with an option to bypass or completely disable the **safety guardrails** (safety control mechanisms that prevent AI from executing dangerous or harmful commands) that are built into AI by default [Anthropic now lets Claude users jump out of plane without parachute...](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/10/27/anthropic-now-lets-claude-users-jump-out-of-plane-without-parachute/5305807), [ClaudeWorld: Claude and Anthropic News, Every Day](https://claudeworld.news/).

Why would Anthropic, which has always called for "safe AI," hand developers such a dangerous key to disable braking systems? We will examine whether this measure is a mere deviation or a reasoned choice to extract better AI performance.

---

## Why It Matters

### 1. An Unusual Change of Course for Anthropic, the 'Synonym for Safety'
Anthropic has built a reputation in the AI market as a company that "makes the safest and most reliable artificial intelligence" [Claude](https://claude.com/). Key leaders, including Anthropic CEO **Dario Amodei** and **Amanda Askell**, who researches the personality and ethics of Claude, have emphasized through lectures and interviews that the negative impact AI might have on human society must be thoroughly controlled [Dario Amodei: Anthropic CEO on Claude, AGI & the... - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ugvHCXCOmm4).

The fact that Anthropic has opened a path to self-disable safety constraints in Claude Code, a developer tool, sends a major shockwave and signal throughout the AI industry [Anthropic now lets Claude users jump out of plane without parachute...](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/10/27/anthropic-now-lets-claude-users-jump-out-of-plane-without-parachute/5305807).

### 2. A Tense Tug-of-War Between Agent Performance and Safety
When developers perform coding tasks, AI goes beyond merely summarizing sentences; it modifies code itself, accesses file systems, and even executes commands on its own. AI that judges and acts on its own to achieve goals like this is called an **AI Agent** (an intelligent program that performs tasks without human intervention).

The problem is that if safety guardrails are set too tightly, the AI pauses and refuses to work when it encounters a command that looks even slightly dangerous (e.g., modifying specific system files, changing network ports). For developers who want to perform complex, high-performance tasks, this is inevitably frustrating. With this measure, Anthropic has chosen the path of providing complete autonomy to developers [ClaudeWorld: Claude and Anthropic News, Every Day](https://claudeworld.news/).

## The Explainer

To make it easy to understand the significance of this measure, we will highlight the key concepts with two realistic metaphors.

### Concept 1: Guardrails and Constitutional AI
Anthropic's AI model, Claude, was trained using **Constitutional AI** (a technique that makes AI learn ethical constitutional rules set by humans to induce safe and accurate responses) [Claude](https://claude.com/).

Here is a metaphor:

> **🏎️ Metaphor 1: Automatic Braking Systems in Circuit Racing Cars**
> For passenger cars driven on public roads, an "automatic emergency braking system" that intervenes when the car slams on the brakes or drifts out of a lane is essential to prevent accidents. However, when a professional race car driver is setting a record on a controlled circuit track, this automatic braking system actually hinders high-speed driving. Anthropic giving Claude Code developers the option to disable guardrails is like allowing a "professional racer (developer) to turn off the automatic braking system within the track and reach maximum speed."

### Concept 2: Top Intelligence Models and Work Efficiency
Currently, Anthropic's flagship model, **Claude Opus 5.5**, ranks first among 195 evaluated models worldwide on the Artificial Analysis LLM Leaderboard, with an **Intelligence Index score of 58** [LLM Leaderboard - Comparison of AI models from OpenAI, Anthropic...](https://artificialanalysis.ai/leaderboards/models).

If such an overwhelmingly intelligent AI is made to check for safety on every minor detail, work speed drops significantly.

> **🛠️ Metaphor 2: Veteran Engineer and Safety Guards**
> If you give a world-class mechanical engineer a tool with a beginner's saw-blade safety guard, the guard keeps getting in the way during complex and delicate tasks, slowing the work down. Removing the safety guard speeds up the work dramatically, but it also increases the risk of injuring your hands if you are not careful.

## What's Next

Anthropic's decision to hand the decision-making power of bypassing guardrails to Claude Code users is an important turning point that shows how the generative AI development environment will change in the future [Anthropic now lets Claude users jump out of plane without parachute...](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/10/27/anthropic-now-lets-claude-users-jump-out-of-plane-without-parachute/5305807).

1. **Two-track separation: 'Safety for the public' and 'Freedom for experts'**
   AI platforms will increasingly clarify a 'two-track' strategy: applying strong safety guardrails by default for services for the general public, and handing over safety control to the user in environments for professional developers or researchers (SDK, Agent CLI).

2. **Increased responsibility and competence for developers themselves**
   Just as one bears all the risk when jumping out of a plane without a parachute, the responsibility for exception cases or security issues created by Claude Code with safety guardrails turned off lies entirely with the developer. The security consciousness and sense of responsibility of developers who handle high-performance AI tools will become more important than ever.
