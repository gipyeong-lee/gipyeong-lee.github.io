Imagine this: On a busy morning, you tell your smartphone's AI, "Read yesterday's news articles and summarize the key points for me." In an instant, the AI sifts through thousands of words and highlights only the most important information for you. Older AI used a method akin to a machine mechanically reading a text line by line, often missing the context. Today's AI, however, scans the entire text like a human and understands by 'focusing' on the parts that are most important for the context.

At the center of this remarkable transformation is a key technology called 'Attention.' Is this mathematical tool really the 'essence of intelligence' that makes artificial intelligence think like a human? Or are we missing another important piece in the grand puzzle of intelligence?

## Why Does This Matter?

The chatbots we use every day, the video generation AI that creates art, and the latest models that solve abstruse math problems are all powered by this 'Attention' technology [[Reference 7](https://www.quantamagazine.org/is-attention-the-essence-of-intelligence-20261012/)]. Attention has fundamentally changed the way artificial intelligence processes data. Instead of simply reading information in order, it makes the AI decide for itself which words in a sentence are closely connected and what is most important in the overall flow [[Reference 8](https://stacker.news/items/1594595)].

If this technology is the essence of intelligence, it means that in the future, we can create artificial intelligence that is much smarter than today simply by teaching it how to 'focus' better. However, if intelligence is closer to 'predictive power' to foresee the future or 'adaptability' to respond to the environment, we may need to rethink the direction of AI design from scratch.

## Easy Understanding: What is Attention?

'Attention' is, in simple terms, the act of focusing. Biologically, humans and animals go through a cognitive process of paying attention to a specific object while ignoring others, even when surrounded by a flood of information [[Reference 10](https://www.nature.com/subjects/attention)].

This process is also crucial for AI. For example, in the sentence "The apple is on the table, and it is very delicious," a human knows immediately that 'it' refers to the 'apple.' However, older AI often failed to see the relationship between 'it' and 'apple,' leading to bizarre interpretations. To solve this, researchers introduced the 'Attention mechanism' into neural networks in 2017 [[Reference 6](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)].

Metaphorically, attention is like a **'filter camera.'** When taking a landscape photo, it is not a technology that captures the entire background with equal clarity, but one that focuses only on the main subject while making the rest softly blurred. When reading a sentence, AI puts a 'magnifying lens' on important words and applies a 'blur effect' to less important conjunctions or particles, allowing it to understand the context much more accurately.

## Current Situation: Various Perspectives on Intelligence

So, is attention all there is to intelligence? The academic community's answer is, "We don't know yet." The criteria for defining intelligence remain a fiercely debated topic among scholars.

*   **Predictive Ability:** Yann LeCun, Meta's Chief AI Scientist, emphasizes that the essence of intelligence lies in the ability to 'predict' future situations [[Reference 5](https://lawwu.github.io/transcripts/SGzMElJ11Cc.html)]. He argues that the ability to simulate the world and predict the next moment is the core of intelligence.
*   **Adaptation and Feedback:** Other experts argue that the 'feedback' process of dealing flexibly even when the environment changes and learning from one's own mistakes is the true hallmark of intelligence [[Reference 4](https://mindhacks.com/2013/03/05/the-essence-of-intelligence-is-feedback/)].
*   **Problem Solving:** The traditional view that the ability to solve problems by simply using knowledge is intelligence also continues to gain traction [[Reference 2](https://topic.alibabacloud.com/a/thoughts-on-the-essence-of-intelligence_8_8_31622508.html)].

While current AI models show tremendous performance through attention, critics also point out that this is merely intelligence based on 'memory' and 'pattern recognition,' and does not reach the insight or 'connection' in the true sense that humans possess [[Reference 1](https://www.deseret.com/opinion/2026/09/26/attention-as-the-essence-of-love/)].

## What Happens Next?

Attention is the heart driving the current AI revolution, but it likely won't be the completed form of 'intelligence' in itself [[Reference 9](https://lifeboat.com/blog/2026/10/is-attention-the-essence-of-intelligence)]. Future AI research will move toward a 'more multidimensional intelligence' that actively predicts the future and adapts to changing environments, based on the powerful weapon that is attention.

What you, the reader, should remember is this: The amazement we feel when looking at AI might not be because AI is truly thinking like us, but because AI is very efficiently finding the wisdom and context we have accumulated through language over thousands of years using the tool called attention [[Reference 11](https://julianajackson.substack.com/p/attention-is-intelligence-in-ai)].

## MindTickleBytes' AI Reporter Perspective
Attention is the compass that helps AI find its way in a flood of information. However, defining intelligence by a single technique risks overlooking deeper and more complex aspects of intelligence, much like trying to explain love simply as a hormonal reaction.
