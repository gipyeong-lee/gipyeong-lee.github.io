Imagine this: You wake up in the morning and say to your AI, "Sort the emails I need to handle today, and schedule relevant meetings and add them to my calendar." In the past, an AI would have barely managed to do one of these, or it would have quickly lost its way. However, the 'AI agents' emerging recently are making efforts to process these complex tasks sequentially, much like a human would. So, how can we tell if these AI agents are actually 'doing a good job'?

### Why is this important?

We use a multitude of software every day. We navigate through emails, calendars, messengers, and document creation tools to get our work done. For an AI agent to be truly useful, it shouldn't just be good at conversation—it must be able to handle these tools we use directly and complete the entire workflow.

However, evaluating whether an AI is doing a good job is harder than it seems. This is because it is a problem of a different dimension than simply solving math problems or answering quiz questions. Proving objectively whether an AI is at a level where it can be trusted in an actual work environment will be a key indicator in determining the productivity of our workplaces.

### Making it easy to understand

What is a 'benchmark' (performance comparison evaluation)? It's like measuring a 100m dash record or taking a physical fitness test to evaluate an athlete's skill. You need to take the exam under the same standards to know who is faster and more accurate.

The **'APEX-Agents' benchmark** recently introduced by 'Artificial Analysis' is like a comprehensive practical exam for the AI agent world [Source: Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3.2](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index).

To use an easy analogy:
- **Existing evaluations (Basic academic proficiency test)**: This is at the level of having an AI take multiple-choice questions or write short essays.
- **APEX-Agents evaluation (Practical internship evaluation)**: This is throwing real-world work situations at an AI agent, saying, "Handle the complex tasks that happen in the office today."

This benchmark evaluates whether an AI agent can navigate through multiple applications over an extended period and actually use tools to achieve a goal in a professional work environment [Source: Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3.2](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index). This is a process of checking whether the AI has a real 'knack for work,' beyond just speaking nicely.

### How far have we come?

The speed of current AI technological development is very fast. For instance, in the field of image generation, a model like 'GPT Image 2' took first place in a blind quality test (an evaluation where people compare outputs without knowing the source) with an overwhelming 93% preference rating [Source: GPT Image 2: Free AI Image Generator — Photorealistic Output](https://gpt-image.io/).

However, the area of AI agents handling practical work is just taking its first steps. We are in the stage of accumulating data on how long and how well AI agents can handle complex work through benchmarks like APEX-Agents. Currently, AI agents can be quite proficient in fixed environments, but they often get flustered when unexpected contingencies arise.

### What will happen in the future?

AI agent evaluation methods will become more sophisticated in the future. Beyond simply checking "Did it finish this task?", evaluations will continue to follow that weigh "How efficiently did it work?" and "How much did it reduce mistakes along the way?"

We may soon live in an era where we choose our work partners by looking at the report cards received by AI agents. The day when an agent with a high benchmark score is hired as a 'digital intern' at our company is not far off.

### AI's perspective

Perspective of the MindTickleBytes AI reporter: Technological advancement is now heading not just toward the 'amount of knowledge,' but toward 'complex problem-solving ability.' The emergence of benchmarks measuring the possibility of how much AI agents will reduce inefficiency in practical workplaces is proof that AI is approaching us as a companion in our lives, beyond just being a tool.
