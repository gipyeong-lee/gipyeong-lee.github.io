Imagine this. Every morning on your way to work, you turn on your smartphone and say, "Organize my schedule for today and draft replies to the important emails that came in yesterday." Then, an invisible artificial intelligence (AI) inside your phone seamlessly moves between several apps—your calendar, email, and notepad—to perfectly handle all these tasks in just a few seconds. Things that were only seen in science fiction movies just a few years ago are now rapidly becoming part of our daily lives. For such amazing technology to work smoothly at our fingertips, an invisible, sophisticated 'bridge' is needed to connect the giant AI brain with ordinary smartphone apps. The stronger and wider this bridge is, the more convenient our lives become.

Recently, a shocking event occurred where the legendary 'bridge construction startup' that built these invisible bridges faster and easier than anyone else was swallowed whole by an industry rival. The protagonist is Anthropic, an AI research company dedicated to building reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems [Anthropic acquires Stainless](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-acquires-stainless). Anthropic has abruptly acquired 'Stainless', a startup specializing in developer tools, for an astronomical sum exceeding $300 million [Anthropic Snaps Up Stainless, the Startup Behind OpenAI's ...](https://www.marketdash.com/stock-market-news/68596/anthropic-snaps-up-stainless-the-startup-behind-openais-developer-tools). To put it simply, they poured enough money to buy several large buildings in downtown Seoul into a single, relatively unknown small company.

The reason this deal is drawing such attention isn't just because of the large sum of money. It's because Stainless was the company creating the core tools that giant IT firms like OpenAI (Anthropic's biggest rival), Google, Meta, and Cloudflare all relied on [Anthropic has acquired the dev tools startup used by OpenAI ...](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/18/anthropic-has-acquired-the-dev-tools-startup-used-by-openai-google-and-cloudflare/). What kind of magic did this small company perform to make the world's greatest genius companies its regular customers? And why did Anthropic spend such a fortune to monopolize it? We explain this breathtaking chess game in the AI industry from an ordinary person's perspective.

## Why It Matters

Behind the countless smartphone apps and internet services we use every day, incredibly complex computer code is intertwined. If a company making a typical food delivery app or an English conversation app wants to put a smart AI like ChatGPT into their app, what should they do? Simply commanding the AI in text, "AI, work in our app," won't magically connect the two systems. To ensure that two computer systems speaking completely different languages can talk to each other and exchange data without errors, a highly complex piece of software acting as a translator and link is essential. In the IT industry, this is called an SDK (Software Development Kit).

To make it easier to understand, let's use a real-life analogy. Imagine you've invited a genius 3-star Michelin chef (a large AI model) from overseas to a small local restaurant in Korea (a smartphone app). The chef only speaks very difficult French, and the local restaurant staff only speak Korean. For a customer to order and for the chef to serve a dish, a very thick and detailed 'professional interpretation manual' is needed between the two. This manual is the SDK in the computer world.

The problem was that every time a new AI model was released, or even when a small new feature was updated in an existing AI, developers had to rewrite this thousands-of-pages-thick interpretation manual from scratch. This was a grueling task that drained enormous amounts of time and energy from developers in the rapidly changing IT industry. Since it was human work, even a small mistake would cause the app to stop or the translation to become a mess.

However, the game completely changed in 2022 when Alex Rattray, a genius engineer who worked at Stripe, an innovator in online payment services, founded 'Stainless' [Anthropic buys Stainless, acquiring startup behind OpenAI's ...](https://www.msn.com/en-us/money/news/anthropic-buys-stainless-acquiring-startup-behind-openais-developer-libraries/ar-AA23vr55). Rattray began building software to help AI companies connect more easily with the world [Anthropic in talks to buy AI tools startup Stainless for over ...](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-in-talks-to-buy-ai-tools-startup-stainless-for-over-300-million-the-information/articleshow/131060389.cms). Stainless introduced a breakthrough technology to the world where, instead of developers typing this complex interpretation manual (SDK) by hand all night, a machine could perfectly and automatically generate it with the push of a button.

The performance of this tool was truly phenomenal. Not only OpenAI, the world's leading AI company that created ChatGPT, but even big tech giants like Google and Meta, where geniuses gather, began paying to use Stainless's services instead of writing manuals themselves [Anthropic Buying Stainless: 300 Million Dollar SDK Deal ...](https://letsdatascience.com/blog/anthropic-buying-stainless-300-million-openai-google-sdks). Hundreds of companies came to rely entirely on Stainless to generate SDKs for providing their APIs (Application Programming Interface, access rights to connect with other programs) to external developers, command-line tools (CLI, a screen for giving commands to a computer with text), and MCP (Multi-Cloud Platform) servers that connect different systems [Anthropic acquires Stainless \ Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-acquires-stainless?type=research).

However, with Anthropic's sudden acquisition, this 'magic wand' that supported the entire AI industry has fallen entirely into Anthropic's hands. This means that Anthropic has not only taken its technical capabilities to the next level but has also gained a powerful weapon that can shake up the rules and landscape of the entire AI market.

## The Explainer

To further explain how incredible what Stainless does is, let's look at another situation different from the restaurant analogy.

Imagine you are the CEO of a huge factory that makes 'universal smart remotes' that can control all home appliances in the world. Each company's products—Samsung TVs, LG air conditioners, Sony audio systems—all have different and complex radio signal systems. Originally, technicians had to manually solder remote button circuits for each product while looking through a microscope. It was very tiring, production was slow, and most of all, errors and defective products were constantly pouring out because it went through human hands.

Then, a place called 'Stainless Workshop' appeared in the industry like a comet. This workshop declared, "CEOs, if you just bring us the basic blueprints of your appliances, we will automatically print out the perfect universal remote parts for them in just one second using a state-of-the-art 3D printer." Factory CEOs (AI companies like OpenAI and Google) cheered with joy. Without the need for tedious and headache-inducing manual soldering, perfect parts with zero margin for error poured out at the touch of a button. Even giant corporations rushed to sign contracts with this workshop and borrow these great machines. Thanks to that, the pace of technological development in the global AI industry could become dazzlingly fast.

Then, one peaceful day, Anthropic, the rising powerhouse in the industry, bought this 'Stainless Workshop' whole for the very high price of $300 million. They then put a large, firm lock on the factory door and announced to the world: "Attention everyone. From now on, this wonderful workshop will only make dedicated remotes for our Anthropic products. For other competing companies, we are sorry, but please go back to making remotes manually as you did in the old days."

This is exactly the core of the dramatic situation that has unfolded behind the scenes of the global AI industry. Anthropic has completely integrated Stainless as a core internal department and coldly stated that it plans to terminate the external hosting services (universal services that anyone could use via the internet by paying) that Stainless previously provided to companies worldwide [Anthropic Acquires Stainless, Internalizes SDK Tools - Phemex](https://phemex.com/news/article/anthropic-acquires-stainless-shifts-sdk-tools-inhouse-83297).

As a result, existing customers who had placed absolute trust in Stainless's automation technology were dealt an immediate and fatal blow, losing access to the service [Anthropic Acquires Stainless, Dev Tools Startup Used by ...](https://shiporskip.io/news/anthropic-acquires-stainless-dev-tools). From the perspective of OpenAI or Google, they were suddenly stripped of the automation tools they had found so comfortable and sweet, forced back into the swamp of hard manual labor or left in a very difficult dilemma of having to hurriedly find other, less capable alternatives. With one clever acquisition, Anthropic has heightened its own fortress walls while dramatically slowing down the march of its competitors.

## What's Next

The future competition for AI market dominance will go beyond a one-dimensional fight over "who makes a smarter AI with more vast knowledge." From now on, it will clearly turn into a fierce platform infrastructure war over "who can make it easiest and most comfortable for countless developers worldwide to bring and use their AI."

Currently, Anthropic is trying to go far beyond the level of a chatbot that plausibly generates text answers to human questions. They are focusing their entire company's capabilities on developing 'Intelligent Agents' that can actively use various software tools on their own to responsibly handle complex human tasks from start to finish [Anthropic Expands Reach with Stainless Acquisition](https://conzit.com/post/anthropic-expands-reach-with-stainless-acquisition).

For AI to function properly as such a high-level assistant (agent) rather than a simple conversation partner, it must communicate smoothly and without errors in real-time with countless databases, websites, and other software programs in the world. The powerful and automated SDK and server tooling tools owned by Stainless, which they just acquired, will act as the ones opening thousands of wide-open highways at once so that Anthropic's intelligent agents can connect with the wide external world without hesitation [Anthropic Expands Reach with Stainless Acquisition](https://conzit.com/post/anthropic-expands-reach-with-stainless-acquisition).

This aggressive absorption of infrastructure by Anthropic is not the first. Already, just a few months ago in December 2025, Anthropic abruptly acquired a famous JavaScript execution environment company named 'Bun' to strengthen its agent coding infrastructure [Anthropic Stainless Acquisition: How a $300M+ Developer Tools Deal Could Hand Anthropic Control Over Its Rivals' SDKs](https://entrepreneurloop.com/anthropic-stainless-acquisition-300m-developer-tools-deal/). This Stainless acquisition is also perfectly in line with this grand picture of infrastructure dominance. It is an ambitious aspiration to exclusively provide the world's most overwhelmingly fast, pleasant, and safe development environment when countless app developers worldwide want to create new and innovative services using Anthropic's AI.

On the other hand, for powerful competitors like OpenAI, Google, and Meta, who were humming along and developing comfortably by relying on Stainless's automation magic until yesterday, fire has literally fallen on their feet. In a situation where they have suddenly lost their most convenient core tool, they are left with the huge homework and cost of having to hurriedly rebuild their own developer tool infrastructure from the ground up to prevent system interruptions and delays. With one clever acquisition, Anthropic has seized a clear victory while forcing significant temporal and financial bleeding on its competitors.

All this complex friction between billionaire Silicon Valley companies is actually very closely connected to the lives of us ordinary people. Ultimately, the essence of this fierce battle is a furious race toward 'who will be the first to put a smarter, innovative, and convenient app that perfectly understands my words into the smartphone in my hand.' Please watch closely to see how the artificial intelligence assistants that will dramatically change your daily life will evolve at an amazing speed on top of Anthropic's thoroughly calculated and powerful infrastructure.
