Imagine you looked into the future a few years ago and invested a large sum of money into a small startup. Over time, that company grew into a global artificial intelligence (AI) powerhouse, and the value of your shares swelled to a staggering $40 billion. However, right before you could get your hands on that immense wealth, you were convicted of fraud and locked up in prison. You find yourself in a situation where piles of gold sit right before your eyes, yet you cannot touch a single cent.

The protagonist of this cinematic tragedy is Sam Bankman-Fried, the co-founder of cryptocurrency exchange FTX and once revered as the "poster boy" of the crypto industry [See source: [Sam Bankman-Fried - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Bankman-Fried)]. As artificial intelligence research firm Anthropic stands on the verge of its Nasdaq initial public offering (IPO), he is at risk of missing out on one of the largest investment gains in history [See source: [Can’t believe SBF would have been the greatest investor in history.](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/linasbeliunas_cant-believe-sbf-would-have-been-the-greatest-activity-7440133231141613568-WJOT), [SBF misses $37B Anthropic windfall as AI giant prepares for ...](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/sbf-misses-37b-anthropic-windfall-as-ai-giant-prepares-for-nasdaq-ipo/ar-AA2hpNat)]. What is the shock and lesson this story brings us? Let's examine it in detail.

## Why It Matters

We often tend to think that "investment acumen" and "human morality" are entirely separate. If someone possesses the foresight to recognize tremendous future value, it is easy to expect their life path to be smooth sailing as well. However, Sam Bankman-Fried's case shows that the world is not so simple.

Today, AI technology is shaking up our daily lives, jobs, and the entire global economic structure. The corporate valuations of AI pioneers like Anthropic are soaring endlessly skyward, and early investors are reaping astronomical wealth. If Sam Bankman-Fried had not gone to prison, he would have joined the ranks of the world's elite billionaires [See source: [Can’t believe SBF would have been the greatest investor in history.](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/linasbeliunas_cant-believe-sbf-would-have-been-the-greatest-activity-7440133231141613568-WJOT), [Sam Bankman-Fried’s Investments Could Have Made... - Comparos.in](https://www.comparos.in/news/sam-bankman-frieds-investments-could-have-made-him-one-of-the-richest-people)].

This incident warns that no matter how brilliant one's economic insight or foresight may be, all those achievements can vanish like a mirage in front of illegalities and unethical management practices. To put it simply, it is like driving a luxury sports car without a driver's license only to be thrown out of the driver's seat entirely, unable even to watch. It serves as a reminder to the general public and investors why corporate transparency and trust are absolutely crucial, just as much as the pace of technological advancement.

## The Explainer

Simply put, this situation is like "planting a seed that grew into a massive tree, but because the owner who planted it was handcuffed, they couldn't pick a single fruit."

In 2021, Sam Bankman-Fried invested a staggering $500 million into AI startup Anthropic through his cryptocurrency investment firm, Alameda Research (a crypto trading and investment firm) [See source: [Sam Bankman-Fried - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Bankman-Fried), [Sam Bankman-Fried Misses $40B Anthropic AI IPO Windfall](https://www.feedzop.com/en-us/news/business-and-economy/sam-bankman-fried-misses-40b-anthropic-ai-ipo-windfall)]. At the time, Anthropic's corporate value was estimated at around $400 million, and through this investment, he secured about a 14% initial stake [See source: [Sam Bankman-Fried Misses $40B Anthropic AI IPO Windfall](https://www.feedzop.com/en-us/news/business-and-economy/sam-bankman-fried-misses-40b-anthropic-ai-ipo-windfall)]. 

To use an analogy, it is akin to pooling loans and pouring tens of millions of dollars into a tiny seed whose value nobody else yet recognized. At the time, it was a massive gamble and a high-risk bet.

Those around him also joined this movement. During Anthropic's Series B funding round in 2022, Singh, who worked with Sam Bankman-Fried, purchased $4 million worth of shares, and Ellison purchased $10 million worth of shares [See source: [The rise and fall of Sam Bankman-Fried: an... | The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2024/mar/23/sam-bankman-fried-rise-and-fall-details)]. Later, some of their assets and shares were seized by the federal government and liquidated [See source: [The Feds Seized Anthropic Shares And Cashed... - Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/federal-government-sold-anthropic-shares-sam-bankman-fried-friends-2026-10)].

Over time, Anthropic grew into one of the absolute powerhouses in generative artificial intelligence. And as of 2026, Anthropic is pursuing an initial public offering (IPO) targeting a staggering corporate valuation of up to $1.4 trillion to make a dazzling debut on the Nasdaq market [See source: [Anthropic Targets Valuation of Up to $1.4 Trillion for Blockbuster I.P.O.](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/10/technology/anthropic-ipo-valuation-share-price.html), [SBF misses $37B Anthropic windfall as AI giant prepares for ...](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/sbf-misses-37b-anthropic-windfall-as-ai-giant-prepares-for-nasdaq-ipo/ar-AA2hpNat)]. 

If Sam Bankman-Fried had maintained the 14% stake he secured in 2021, the value of his shares would have approached nearly $40 billion [See source: [Can’t believe SBF would have been the greatest investor in history.](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/linasbeliunas_cant-believe-sbf-would-have-been-the-greatest-activity-7440133231141613568-WJOT), [Sam Bankman-Fried Misses $40B Anthropic AI IPO Windfall](https://www.feedzop.com/en-us/news/business-and-economy/sam-bankman-fried-misses-40b-anthropic-ai-ipo-windfall)]. As Linas Beliunas pointed out on LinkedIn, he could have become one of the greatest investors in human history [See source: [Can’t believe SBF would have been the greatest investor in history.](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/linasbeliunas_cant-believe-sbf-would-have-been-the-greatest-activity-7440133231141613568-WJOT)].

## Where We Stand

However, reality was harsh. As the massive fraudulent operations of cryptocurrency exchange FTX came to light, Sam Bankman-Fried's empire collapsed like a house of cards [See source: [Sam Bankman-Fried - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Bankman-Fried), [Can’t believe SBF would have been the greatest investor in history.](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/linasbeliunas_cant-believe-sbf-would-have-been-the-greatest-activity-7440133231141613568-WJOT)]. Prosecutors sought a heavy sentence of 40 to 50 years for his FTX fraud charges [See source: [The rise and fall of Sam Bankman-Fried: an... | The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2024/mar/23/sam-bankman-fried-rise-and-fall-details)], and the court brought down the hammer.

Currently, instead of making the glorious billionaire list, he spends his time in a cold prison cell bearing inmate number "37244-510" [See source: [Can’t believe SBF would have been the greatest investor in history.](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/linasbeliunas_cant-believe-sbf-would-have-been-the-greatest-activity-7440133231141613568-WJOT)]. 

This situation also contrasts with another successful investment case. Sam Bankman-Fried previously made an early investment of $58 million in Solana (a high-performance blockchain platform), a cryptocurrency that was somewhat unfamiliar at the time, which also had the potential to swell to approximately $4.95 billion (about an 86-fold return) by 2026 [See source: [Sam Bankman-Fried Misses $40B Anthropic AI IPO Windfall](https://www.feedzop.com/en-us/news/business-and-economy/sam-bankman-fried-misses-40b-anthropic-ai-ipo-windfall)]. Despite his remarkable foresight and investment skills demonstrated across Bitcoin (a decentralized digital currency) and the broader cryptocurrency ecosystem, all those financial benefits became a pie in the sky due to illegal fund management and criminal fraud.

## What's Next

Anthropic's Nasdaq listing will serve as a massive milestone for the entire artificial intelligence industry. Once a blockbuster IPO involving trillions of dollars concludes successfully, AI technology will transcend being a mere laboratory product and establish itself firmly at the center of the global economy.

So how will imprisoned Sam Bankman-Fried's shares and seized assets be handled? Just as federal agencies and relevant institutions have already seized and liquidated a significant portion of his assets [See source: [The Feds Seized Anthropic Shares And Cashed... - Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/federal-government-sold-anthropic-shares-sam-bankman-fried-friends-2026-10)], the $40 billion windfall he should have enjoyed will likely end up in the hands of the state and creditors according to victim compensation and legal procedures. 

Dear readers, this incident delivers a clear message. While having an eye for the future and reading the currents of technology is immensely important, it cannot justify immoral desires and crimes. Anthropic's epic and Sam Bankman-Fried's downfall simultaneously prove that no matter how rapidly technology evolves and astronomical wealth is generated, everything collapses in an instant when fundamental principles of trust and honesty are broken.
