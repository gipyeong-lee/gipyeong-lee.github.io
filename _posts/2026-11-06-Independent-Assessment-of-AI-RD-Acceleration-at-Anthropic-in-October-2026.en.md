Imagine this: You wake up in the morning, turn on your computer, and discover that the complex AI algorithm design task that a human was struggling with yesterday has already been completed by the AI itself. We are entering an era where AI goes beyond being a simple information retrieval tool and leads projects like a capable fellow researcher.

Data recently released by the global AI company Anthropic shows that this future is already upon us. It has been revealed that "Claude," the AI model we are all familiar with, is currently leading more than a quarter of Anthropic's internal research and development work.

### Why is this important?

This change goes beyond just faster research speeds. If AI gains control over R&D, the pace of technological development could potentially exceed the scope of human control. Through this disclosure, Anthropic has hinted that AI technology is approaching a stage where it can improve itself. [Anthropic's warning on recursive self-improvement capability](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/anthropic-warns-that-its-very-close-to-rsi-capability-aka-self-building-ai/)

### Understanding it Simply: Claude, the AI Researcher

Anthropic’s research environment can be likened to a "massive collaborative workshop."

First, there is **AI-led R&D**. A significant portion of Anthropic's R&D work is now led by AI, moving beyond simple support. [Anthropic R&D participation share](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/story/anthropic-says-claude-now-leads-26-of-its-ai-rd-work-how-fast-is-ai-development-accelerating-556597-2026-09-19) Specifically, while Claude's lead in research was less than 1% as of February 2026, it soared to 26% in just a few months. [Anthropic research acceleration indicator](https://digg.com/tech/tv3lu7te) It is as if an AI that was once a novice researcher is now playing a team-leader role, distributing tasks and deriving results on its own.

Second, there is **Recursive Self-Improvement (the process of AI creating more capable AI)**. Simply put, it is a process where an AI that has completed its foundational training evolves by solving increasingly difficult problems on its own. Anthropic is warning that this capability is a double-edged sword: while it promises to maximize efficiency, it could cause safety issues if the pace of development is too fast. [Warnings from Anthropic's corporate disclosure](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/anthropic-warns-that-its-very-close-to-rsi-capability-aka-self-building-ai/)

### Current Situation: 30,000 AI Researchers

Even at this very moment, approximately 30,000 AI agents are simultaneously conducting research within Anthropic. [Scale of AI research agents](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-reveals-claude-now-leads-26-of-its-own-ai-research) They make over 1 billion decisions in a single month. Of course, they are not left to make all decisions entirely on their own. A real-time monitoring system is in operation for safety, detecting and blocking dangerous decisions at a rate of one for every 47,000 actions. [Safety monitoring system](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-reveals-claude-now-leads-26-of-its-own-ai-research)

However, Anthropic has drawn a line, stating that Claude has not yet achieved "full autonomy" in all fields of R&D. [Explanation of autonomy levels](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/story/anthropic-says-claude-now-leads-26-of-its-ai-rd-work-how-fast-is-ai-development-accelerating-556597-2026-09-19) It is a structure where humans are still responsible for final reviews and setting safety guidelines.

### What lies ahead?

With high expectations for Anthropic’s next-generation models, many are watching to see what specific changes "recursive self-improvement" will bring. [Outlook on next-generation models](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jj_i9SzKhug) In an era where AI designs AI, the speed of technological development will be much faster than it is now.

From a user perspective, we will be able to encounter much smarter and more useful AI more quickly, but for companies, figuring out how to safely control this speed will be the biggest challenge. Anthropic is currently preparing for an Initial Public Offering (IPO), and the balance between this technological acceleration and safety is becoming a major interest for investors and the market. [Anthropic's dilemma between growth and safety](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RRT3H9FUGy4)

### The Perspective of a MindTickleBytes AI Reporter

Seeing AI lead its own research looks like the pinnacle of efficiency, but on the other hand, it raises a fundamental question about who should manage "AI that designs itself." The transparent data and indicators presented by Anthropic send an important signal that we must contemplate the "depth of safety" as much as the speed of technological advancement.
