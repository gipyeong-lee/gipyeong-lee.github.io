Think about a time when you asked someone a very difficult question. You might have seen them pause, look into the distance, and say \"Hmm...\" as they sink into thought instead of answering immediately. In that brief moment, their mind is working hard—weighing countless possibilities, checking for logical flaws, and striving to find the best possible answer. Now, Artificial Intelligence (AI) has begun to go through this same process of 'deliberation' just like a human.

Google recently introduced a powerful reasoning tool called 'DeepThink' to its AI service, the Gemini app [Gemini 2.5: DeepThink is now rolling out](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/gemini-2-5-deep-think/). This tool goes beyond simply predicting the next word statistically; it goes through a 'thinking process' of its own to solve complex problems. Today, I'll explain in very simple terms how this smart friend works and why we should pay attention to this change.

## Why It Matters

Until now, the AI we've encountered has focused on spitting out answers in a single second, like a quiz show champion. But is the world really that simple? Catching complex bugs in code or establishing scientific hypotheses to solve humanity's challenges has limits when relying solely on 'quick reflexes.' It's like asking a 100-meter sprinter to run a marathon course.

Google DeepThink was born at this very point. It is designed to solve the tricky tasks that arise in fields requiring high concentration and logic, such as coding, science, and research [Gemini 3 — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/). In particular, this model is optimized for solving real-world complex problems that require both rigorous logic and innovative creativity [Gemini 3 — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/).

In fact, its skills have already been proven. The news that a version of the DeepThink model achieved a gold-medal-level performance at the 2025 International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) surprised the world. This is powerful evidence that AI's capabilities have moved beyond simply 'speaking plausibly' to 'thinking accurately like a mathematician' [Gemini DeepThink: Built for the Hardest Problems - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-1moHqPBpjc), [Gemini DeepThink - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nmv4YxpbhU8).

## The Explainer: 'Brainstorming' Inside the AI's Head

The core technology of DeepThink is **'Parallel Thinking'** [What Happens When Google Released DeepThink In Your Gemini...](https://lightcheckup.com/google-released-deep-think-in-your-gemini-app/). Simply put, it's a method of coming up with and reviewing multiple ideas at the same time. To understand this more easily, let's use two analogies.

### 1. Chefs in a Kitchen (Analogy)
If a typical AI is a single chef who looks at a recipe and starts cooking right away, DeepThink is like having three top-tier chefs gathered in a kitchen. The first chef considers how to grill the ingredients, the second considers steaming, and the third considers frying—all at the same time. They compare each other's ideas, conclude that \"Steaming is best to preserve the flavor of this ingredient!\" and then serve you the best possible dish [Google rolls out Gemini DeepThink AI, a reasoning... | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/01/google-rolls-out-gemini-deep-think-ai-a-reasoning-model-that-tests-multiple-ideas-in-parallel/).

### 2. Internal Corporate Collaboration (Multi-agent)
DeepThink is also one of the first **'Multi-agent'** models (a method where multiple AI programs collaborate) released by Google [Google rolls out Gemini DeepThink AI, a reasoning... | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/01/google-rolls-out-gemini-deep-think-ai-a-reasoning-model-that-tests-multiple-ideas-in-parallel/). This is similar to a planning team, a design team, and a development team meeting in one place at a company. Each role (agent) carefully examines the problem from its own perspective and complements each other's opinions to create a single, complete result.

Imagine you ask the AI to \"come up with a new app idea.\" Inside DeepThink, one side conducts market research, another checks technical feasibility, and yet another considers design for user convenience—all occurring simultaneously. As a result, we get much more detailed and creative answers than before [What Happens When Google Released DeepThink In Your Gemini...](https://lightcheckup.com/google-released-deep-think-in-your-gemini-app/).

## Where We Stand: Who Can Use It and How?

This smart DeepThink isn't something anyone can use for free right now. It is currently being offered exclusively to subscribers of **'Google AI Ultra'**, the highest tier of Google Gemini [Google DeepMind: Try DeepThink in the Gemini app](https://www.thesearchenginepros.com/google-deepmind-deep-think-in-the-gemini-app/).

The subscription fee is quite high, at around $250 per month (over 300,000 KRW). Therefore, it targets researchers or corporate users performing professional work rather than general users [DeepThink in the Gemini app | Simon Willison’s Weblog](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Aug/1/deep-think-in-the-gemini-app/), [Google rolls out Gemini DeepThink AI, a reasoning... | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/01/google-rolls-out-gemini-deep-think-ai-a-reasoning-model-that-tests-multiple-ideas-in-parallel/).

Using it is surprisingly simple:
1. Access the Gemini app or website [DeepThink: Transform Your Workflow with Google Gemini](https://www.hitpaw.com/ai-model-tips/gemini-deep-think.html).
2. Select **'2.5 Pro'** from the model selection menu.
3. Simply turn on the **DeepThink toggle switch** next to the question box [Google rolls out powerful creative problem-solving AI model Deep...](https://siliconangle.com/2025/08/01/google-rolls-powerful-creative-problem-solving-ai-model-deep-think-gemini-app/).

Currently, this feature carries an 'Experimental' label [Gemini 3 DeepThink is Google's 'most advanced reasoning feature...](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/gemini/articles/gemini-3-deep-think-googles-143500673.html). This means Google is still refining the features and improving performance. However, 'experimental' doesn't mean it should be underestimated. DeepThink is fully equipped with **Multimodal** capabilities (the ability to understand various types of information such as images and documents simultaneously), making it very proficient at analyzing complex professional documents or solving difficult math problems in photos [DeepThink: Transform Your Workflow with Google Gemini](https://www.hitpaw.com/ai-model-tips/gemini-deep-think.html).

Furthermore, it is integrated with Google Search results to bring in real-time information and has tools to execute code directly, allowing it to generate much longer and more detailed high-quality answers [Google rolls out powerful creative problem-solving AI model Deep...](https://siliconangle.com/2025/08/01/google-rolls-powerful-creative-problem-solving-ai-model-deep-think-gemini-app/).
