Imagine if you could see how trillions of dollars flow daily behind the artificial intelligence (AI) assistant you use every day. The opportunity to finally get a detailed look at the financial flows of the AI industry—which have been shrouded in mystery—has arrived. This is because Anthropic, the company behind the AI model 'Claude', has submitted its S-1 filing for an IPO to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). [Anthropic IPO filing reveals rapid growth as investors weigh an $80...](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/anthropic-ipo-filing-reveals-rapid-growth-as-investors-weigh-an-80-billion-loss-202610270748)

This massive document, over 300 pages long, is more than just a company profile. It is one of the most important economic events of our time, showing how AI companies make money and how much they spend. [Anthropic IPO: The big questions its S-1 leaves unanswered](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/30/anthropic-ipo-s1-revenue-compute.html)

### Why is this important?

Until now, the AI industry has been evaluated solely on 'potential'. However, Anthropic's disclosure provides realistic indicators of whether AI companies are sustainable as real businesses and just how costly it is to maintain a massive computing infrastructure. [Anthropic IPO: The big questions its S-1 leaves unanswered](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/30/anthropic-ipo-s1-revenue-compute.html) This is the first time a company focused purely on AI research and services has disclosed its financials with such transparency. [June 1, 2026 – The Market Movement: Anthropic's Trillion-Dollar IPO...](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/june-1-2026-market-movement-anthropics-ipo-filing-iran-de-ruiter-ecsjc)

### In simple terms: A trillion-dollar electric bill and a puzzle piece

Let's use an analogy: The process of an AI company developing a high-performance model is like building a giant library. In this analogy, 'computing costs' are the library's utility bills and server maintenance costs. Anthropic's disclosed computing-related commitments amount to a staggering $895 billion (over $1.2 quadrillion KRW). [Anthropic's new IPO filing puts its computing bill near $900 billion](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/anthropics-new-ipo-filing-puts-its-computing-bill-near-900-billion-151830444.html) This is seven times the amount of their current cash and cash equivalents. [Anthropic's new IPO filing puts its computing bill near $900 billion](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/anthropics-new-ipo-filing-puts-its-computing-bill-near-900-billion-151830444.html)

Simply put, building a smarter AI is like pouring astronomical amounts of money every month to build a bigger library. More interestingly, Anthropic is using its own AI models to build even more powerful ones. While this dramatically accelerates technology development, it presents a fascinating 'paradox of technology' in that it simultaneously entails significant safety risks. [Anthropic is on a path it may not be able to stop - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RRT3H9FUGy4)

### How far have they come: The road from deficit to profit

Anthropic's filing contains both flashy numbers and the company's deep concerns. The fact that they recorded a massive loss of $80 billion in the first half of this year might be shocking. However, looking deeper into the details reveals signs of hope. [Anthropic IPO live updates: AI lab's S-1 filing - CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/anthropic-ipo-live-updates-ai-s-1-filing.html) Losses in operating expenses dropped noticeably to $693 million from $2.1 billion in the same period last year. [Anthropic's IPO prospectus shows $16.3 billion in revenue ... - Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/10/26/anthropics-s-1/)

This suggests the important possibility that AI technology can become profitable once it reaches a certain economy of scale. [Anthropic IPO Filing Reveals AI Profits and $800 Billion Compute Risk ...](https://www.businessinsider.com/anthropic-ipo-filing-ai-profit-800-billion-compute-risk-2026-10?op=1) Analysts also assess that Anthropic's revenue efficiency per employee could be higher than tech giants like Meta and Google. This serves as evidence of the powerful potential of AI technology, which can operate high-efficiency services with fewer personnel. [Anthropic IPO Filing Reveals AI Profits and $800 Billion Compute Risk ...](https://www.businessinsider.com/anthropic-ipo-filing-ai-profit-800-billion-compute-risk-2026-10?op=1)

### What lies ahead?

Dario Amodei, co-founder of Anthropic, has always been candid about the potential risks AI poses. [Will fears of AI crush Anthropic's IPO valuation prospects?](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/09/18/dario-amodei-danger-ai-anthropic-trillion-ipo/) The core challenges for Anthropic moving forward are clear: how to rationalize massive computing costs, and how to strike a balance between the safety and performance of the technology. Now, market investors will look beyond simple excitement and determine the company's true value by carefully scrutinizing concrete financial performance and safety management capabilities.
