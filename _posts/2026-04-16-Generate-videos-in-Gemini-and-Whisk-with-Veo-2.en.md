## Understanding It Easily: How Does Veo 2 Work Its Magic?

Creating videos with AI is actually much harder than creating photos. It goes beyond simply stitching together several pictures; the AI must perfectly understand the 'laws' of how the world we live in moves.

### 1. "AI That Learned Gravity" — Understanding Physical Laws and Movement
If you look at previous AI videos, you often see bizarre, dream-like scenes where a person suddenly has three arms or water gushes upward, defying gravity. However, Veo 2 is designed to have a much deeper understanding of real-world physics (the rules by which objects move) and complex human movements. [Generate videos in Gemini and Whisk with Veo 2](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/video-generation/)

To use an analogy, it's as if the AI watched millions of movies and learned a 'sense of how the world works', such as "Oh, when a person walks, their center of gravity moves like this," or "When waves crash, the water droplets should splash like this to look natural." [Generate Gemini and Whisk videos with Veo 2 - AI SCKOOL](https://aisckool.com/generate-gemini-and-whisk-videos-with-veo-2/) As a result, character movements are much smoother than before, and the screen texture is as realistic as if a cinematic video from a theater was brought directly to you.

### 2. "Breathing Life into Old Photos" — Whisk Animate
In addition to creating videos from text, there is another exciting feature: **'Whisk Animate'**. This is like a magic wand that breathes life into static photos. Imagine putting a crude sketch you drew with your child or a static landscape photo you took on a trip last summer into a tool called Whisk.
