## Farewell to Claude, Hello to a New Coding Partner: Pi

Imagine this: every morning, you sit at your computer to build programs with your AI coding agent. At first, you were satisfied with your coding life, marveling at the powerful features provided by 'Claude Code.' But one day, you start to feel that it's too complex with too many features, or that the features you actually need are missing. It’s as if a bespoke suit that once fit perfectly has suddenly become uncomfortable.

Recently, this shift has been detected among developers. A growing number are leaving Claude Code, once called the 'gold standard,' for a new tool called 'Pi.' What exactly has moved the hearts of these developers?

## Why Does This Matter?

AI coding tools are not just calculators that write code. They are 'digital assistants' that directly influence a developer's thinking and workflow. Therefore, the choice of tool is directly linked to development productivity. This shift is not merely following a trend, but a mature choice by developers who desire **'the flexibility to pick and choose only the features they need.'** We are entering an era where users don't have to adapt to tools; tools are adapting to the user's environment.

## Simply Put: What Kind of Tool is Pi?

To understand 'Pi' (created by Mario Zechner), let's use an analogy.

If Claude Code is a 'full-option luxury camper' that includes every feature, Pi is like a 'modular camper' where you can assemble parts however you like. A camper with a refrigerator, TV, and washing machine might seem convenient, but you might actually just be wasting space on a washing machine you never use.

Pi provides only the minimal interface necessary for a developer's computer system to communicate with AI (LLMs). [Pi focuses on extensibility, allowing developers to create and add their own plugins to suit their needs](https://www.danielkoller.me/en/blog/why-pi-is-my-new-coding-agent-of-choice).

Simply put, [Pi intentionally excludes pre-configured features like sub-agents, MCP (Model Context Protocol) integration, and TODO list management](https://drgore.substack.com/p/goodbye-claude-code-hello-pidev). Instead, it is designed so that developers can pick and choose only the features they need for their own workflow and add them as plugins.

## Current Landscape: Tools are Diversifying

The AI coding tool market is more dynamic than ever.

- **Movement to Pi**: Many developers are [moving from Claude Code to Pi because they prefer personalized workflows that go beyond simple coding features](https://www.danielkoller.me/en/blog/why-pi-is-my-new-coding-agent-of-choice). [Developers like Nicolas Maas are also sharing why they prefer Pi in line with this trend](https://nicolasmaas.be/).
- **Various Alternatives**: Developer tastes vary wildly. Some developers are [switching from Claude to OpenAI's Codex](https://www.hung-truong.com/blog/2025/09/24/goodbye-claude-and-gemini-hello-codex/), while others are [choosing MonkeyCode, which offers a large daily token limit (the unit of words processed by AI), as a new subject for experimentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8kuIgCnAMY).
- **Integration of Existing Tools**: Meanwhile, Anthropic is [continuing to integrate and improve features within its own services](https://basic-tutorials.com/news/goodbye-claude-cowork-anthropic-launches-docs-slides-and-design/), such as incorporating existing Claude Cowork features into standard chat.

## What's Next?

The future of the AI coding environment is likely to evolve from 'one giant model solving everything' to 'a way where small, agile agents connect with each other.' Developers will likely prefer lightweight interfaces with just a skeletal framework like Pi, combining small, powerful AI plugins containing only the features they need. The power to choose is increasingly shifting to the user.

## MindTickleBytes AI Reporter's Opinion

Just because technology advances quickly, it doesn't mean a tool with 'everything included' is the only answer. Sometimes, deciding 'what not to do' creates more value than 'what can be done,' just like with Pi. Ultimately, the best AI tool will not be the smartest model, but the one that becomes an extension of your own hands, allowing you to work without the slightest bit of discomfort.
