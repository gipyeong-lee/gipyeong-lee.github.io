## Understanding Simply: What is an IPO?

The term "Initial Public Offering (IPO)," which frequently appears in tech articles, may feel unfamiliar. Simply put, it refers to the process where a company like Anthropic—previously owned only by a small number of investors or founders—makes its shares public to the world, debuting on the securities market so regular investors and institutions can freely buy and sell them.

The reason a company issues shares to raise capital is clear: to secure more research and development funding and expand the massive computing resources required to train high-performance AI models. Anthropic is gearing up to secure an immense scale of capital through this offering.
