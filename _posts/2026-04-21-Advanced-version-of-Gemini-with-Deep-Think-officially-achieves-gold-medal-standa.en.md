## AI Challenges the Domain of Math Geniuses

**Imagine this.** Teenage geniuses from all over the world, the best in mathematics, have gathered in one place. For two days, they struggle, pencils moving so intensely they might tear the paper, just to solve six problems. This competition is the **International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO)**, the world's most prestigious youth mathematics contest held annually since 1959 [Advanced version of Gemini with Deep Think officially achieves gold ...](https://deepmind.google/blog/advanced-version-of-gemini-with-deep-think-officially-achieves-gold-medal-standard-at-the-international-mathematical-olympiad/).

Until now, AI has been proficient at complex calculations but has struggled with IMO problems that require high levels of creativity and **reasoning (the thinking process of drawing conclusions based on given information)**. However, in the summer of 2025, startling news broke that Google DeepMind's latest AI model, **'Gemini Deep Think,'** had broken through this formidable wall and achieved a gold medal-level score [Advanced version of Gemini with Deep Think officially achieves gold-medal standard at the International Mathematical Olympiad - Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/advanced-version-of-gemini-with-deep-think-officially-achieves-gold-medal-standard-at-the-international-mathematical-olympiad/).

This event officially proves that artificial intelligence has reached a pinnacle in mathematical reasoning—the very essence of human intelligence—going far beyond simply being 'good at solving problems.' The time has come for us to view AI not just as a calculation tool, but as a colleague to contemplate with.

## Why Does This Matter? "Evolution from Calculator to Thinker"

Calculators we commonly use or typical AIs focus on finding answers quickly. However, solving mathematical challenges is like finding a path through a foggy forest. You must build logical justifications for why you chose one path among many, step by step.

Gemini Deep Think's achievement at this IMO is very special for the following reasons:

1.  **Official Recognition**: This achievement marks the first time an AI system has been officially recognized by IMO coordinators in this elite-level competition [AI in Mathematics: Gemini with DeepThinking Sets New Standard at ...](https://www.promptwire.co/articles/gemini-with-deepthinking-sets-new-standard-at-imo). While previous AI challenges were closer to 'reference points,' Gemini has now been evaluated by the same standards as human geniuses and had its skills fully acknowledged.
2.  **Victory for Natural Language**: Gemini did not communicate only through specialized mathematical symbols or code. It solved complex mathematical problems using **Natural Language (the language we use in daily life)** just like a human [Google DeepMind makes AI history with gold medal win at world's ...](https://venturebeat.com/ai/google-deepmind-makes-ai-history-with-gold-medal-win-at-worlds-toughest-math-competition/). **To use an analogy**, it didn't just solve problems with formulas; it explained "why these conclusions were reached" in plain language. This shows that the AI understands mathematical concepts through human logical systems, not just as sequences of numbers.
3.  **Shift in Intelligence Metrics**: The performance of AI is now moving from 'how much data it memorizes' to 'how deeply it can think and reason.'

## Understanding Easily: The Magic of "Gemini Deep Think"

**To put it simply**, you can think of Gemini Deep Think as evolving from a **\"student who hastily provides only the answer\"** into a **\"student who thinks deeply and meticulously reviews the solution process.\"**

Usually, an AI tries to give an answer in less than a second when asked a question. In psychology, this is called 'System 1 thinking (intuitive and fast response).' On the other hand, Gemini Deep Think goes through a process of setting up various hypotheses, checking for logical contradictions, and finding other paths if blocked. This is 'System 2 thinking (slow but logical reasoning),' which Google calls **'Deep Think.'**

For example, if asked "Why did this apple fall to the ground?", a typical AI would immediately say "Because of gravity." In contrast, a Gemini with Deep Think would show a process of "calculating the gravitational pull between the object's mass and the Earth, checking air resistance, considering other variables, and then reaching a conclusion."

In this competition, Gemini **solved 5 out of 6 problems flawlessly** and earned a **total score of 35 points**, achieving a gold medal-level performance [Advanced version of Gemini with Deep Think officially achieves gold-medal standard at the International Mathematical Olympiad - Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/advanced-version-of-gemini-with-deep-think-officially-achieves-gold-medal-standard-at-the-international-mathematical-olympiad/), [AdvancedversionofGeminiwithDeepThinkofficiallyachieves...](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lmarmot_advanced-version-of-gemini-with-deep-think-activity-7357904363203215360-ctLD). This is a domain that only the top 1% of math geniuses can reach.

## What Lies Ahead? Beyond Math to Science

How will an AI that is good at math help our lives? Mathematics is the fundamental language of all sciences. The 'reasoning ability' used to solve IMO problems will be the key to solving the most difficult problems in our society in the future.

*   **New Drug Development**: Out of hundreds of millions of molecular combinations, AI can logically reason to find those that can treat diseases without side effects. It’s not just trying every possibility, but finding the most likely paths through 'thought.'
*   **Solving Climate Change**: By analyzing complex climate data, AI can suggest optimal energy policy scenarios to slow down global warming.
*   **Programming**: An improved version of Gemini Deep Think has already achieved gold medal-level performance at the International Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC) World Finals, signaling an innovation in how software is created [Gemini achieves gold-medal level at the International Collegiate Programming Contest World Finals — Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-achieves-gold-medal-level-at-the-international-collegiate-programming-contest-world-finals/).

Based on this achievement in the summer of 2025, DeepMind has been steadily improving the model, and currently, general users can experience some of this 'Deep Think' functionality through the Gemini app [Try Deep Think in the Gemini app](https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-2-5-deep-think/).
